(3) vitamin-d HELD `contested`. Jorde 2026 (Tromsø RCT, n=382, mean 25(OH)D 34 nmol/L): supplementation corrected PTH/ALP/calcium, but biochemical patterns compatible with osteomalacia were present in only 0–8.3% of this deficient cohort — narrows, does not close, the standing deficiency gap. (4) exercise-for-bone-health HELD `emerging`. Reverte-Pagola 2026 (26 controlled trials, n=1,880 women): exercise raises distal-radius bone density (SMD 1.05) but I²=91%, prediction interval crosses null, GRADE very low — new site coverage, not new certainty. (5) resistance-training-programming HELD `emerging`. Nagatani 2026 (OSF-registered Bayesian NMA, 37 studies): cluster sets and rest-redistribution both preserve movement velocity better than traditional sets, no clear winner among alternatives — an acute mechanical surrogate, a programming facet not a strength/hypertrophy claim.
