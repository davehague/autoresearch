  • 2026-09-27 — 5 ingests (all new sources), 1 map auto-coined (fracture-prevention-pharmacotherapy), 0 forks, no Status moved, no article written or refreshed, no regimen change. A supplement/exercise- programming/bone night. (1) creatine-monohydrate HELD `consolidating`. Wang 2026 (63-RCT three-level meta, n=1,489, largest RCT count yet for this comparison): creatine + resistance training beats resistance training alone on strength (g=0.54/0.55) and lean mass (g=0.30); body-fat% fell but fat mass itself did not — composition arithmetic, not fat loss. (2) Coined fracture-prevention-pharmacotherapy at `emerging` from Braten 2026 (Cochrane, 17 RCTs, n=4,132 men): bisphosphonates, PTH/PTHrP analogues, denosumab and romosozumab for fracture prevention in men — Cochrane's own verdict is "we are very uncertain" on every fracture outcome (GRADE very-low certainty); bisphosphonates probably don't raise adverse-event risk. `safety_flags: [prescription]` — never enters a Protocol slot. (3) vitamin-d HELD `contested`. Jorde 2026 (Tromsø RCT, n=382, mean 25(OH)D 34 nmol/L): supplementation corrected PTH/ALP/calcium, but biochemical patterns compatible with osteomalacia were present in only 0–8.3% of this deficient cohort — narrows, does not close, the standing deficiency gap. (4) exercise-for-bone-health HELD `emerging`. Reverte-Pagola 2026 (26 controlled trials, n=1,880 women): exercise raises distal-radius bone density (SMD 1.05) but I²=91%, prediction interval crosses null, GRADE very low — new site coverage, not new certainty. (5) resistance-training-programming HELD `emerging`. Nagatani 2026 (OSF-registered Bayesian NMA, 37 studies): cluster sets and rest-redistribution both preserve movement velocity better than traditional sets, no clear winner among alternatives — an acute mechanical surrogate, a programming facet not a strength/hypertrophy claim. Titles-scan + global ripple run on every ingest; no new `safety_flags` beyond the new map's standing `[prescription]` tag. New map flagged for human review per the auto-coin protocol; a PTH-analogue↔bisphosphonate sequencing interaction logged (not graded) in `docs/open-gaps-log.md`.
