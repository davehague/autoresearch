(a) The loop layer counted itself. A full-text-coded survey of 24 autonomous-research systems splits closed-loop autonomy into feedback on an *internal metric* versus feedback against an outside oracle — a physical measurement or a checker the system did not author. Nine reach closed loop; seven are mechanical, one is author-claimed, one is externally validated, and that one is a 2020 materials platform predating large-language-model agents. 83% release code; 38% release the seeds or traces needed to reproduce a run. The mechanism half travels furthest: a judge model prefers its own output because it assigns it lower perplexity — *the same quantity the generator was trained to produce* — so juries help only as far as they are diverse and calibration helps not at all: *"calibration audits the estimator, not the oracle."* That is this vault's correlated-blind-spot rail with the substrate changed. And it re-describes what the vault has been grading all along: an adopted Test Guideline's arithmetic threshold on an instrument reading *is* an outside oracle, because tissue either died or it did not. Toxicology holds 26 externally anchored assays and no autonomous system running them; autonomous science holds the systems and, in this sample, one anchor. (b) The same problem, built. An AI-scientist agent trained to replicate published papers is rewarded by an auto-generated rubric judge whose reliability its authors report: two draws of the judge agree 0.66, two human experts agree 0.30, and the judge agrees with humans 0.19. Low noise is what makes it trainable; it is not evidence it measures the right thing. The agent transfers to held-out scientific domains (60% win rate) — and, unasked, every agent tested gets worse the more recent the paper is (2.7–8.4 rubric points per decade), which is a warning about extrapolating any benchmark number toward work nobody has done yet. (c) The wet side said it in cells. Six fish cell lines, one adopted-guideline reference toxicant, one real chemical: the reference chemical gave the same answer in all six lines while 6PPD-quinone — the tyre chemical that kills coho salmon in urban streams — read 8.54 µg/L in coho cells, 37.8 µg/L in trout cells and nothing at all in four lines including two salmon species. A reference-chemical check certifies that the assay is performed correctly and cannot certify that its biology contains the mechanism the next chemical acts through — the wet-lab statement of the same you-cannot-audit-yourself finding, and the reason TG 249's `domain_gate` now records a mechanistic carve-out its physicochemical bounds cannot see. (d) Two quieter ones. Inflammation lowers the cytochrome-P450 activity that an adopted guideline exists to measure, in that guideline's own cell line — an open question about the baseline a fixed rule is applied to, logged as a gap, and the first candidate handle on the DILI battery's documented no-immune-component blind spot. And 1,801 simulated individuals put 15- to 18-fold on how differently the same absorbed dose lands, of which only about 5-fold is predictable from age, sex and body size.
