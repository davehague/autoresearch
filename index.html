The human thyroid microtissue assay — 3D tissue from primary human thyroid cells, measuring hormone synthesis output rather than one fragment of the pathway — transferred reliably across three laboratories, on four blinded reference chemicals (3 true positive, 1 true negative), across three independent human donors, meeting pre-set acceptance criteria for dynamic range, precision and screening quality. `evidence_tier` `peer-reviewed-method` → `formally-validated`; `regulatory_status` `none` → `under-validation`; `loop_readiness` HELD at `automatable-open-loop`. The honest size of it: this asked "does the assay give the same answer in different hands?", not "is the answer right?" — no sensitivity, no specificity, no apical concordance, four chemicals, no decision rule, no Test Guideline. What changed is the blocker's *composition*: thyroid's was doubled (no validated method AND no rule, so no rule could attach to anything) and is now single. `funding_flag: [industry]` — Bayer Crop Science and Corteva Agrisciences, agrochemical companies whose products this assay screens, were collaborators; US EPA/NICEATM coordinated with an independent validation management team. Opens a second validation jurisdiction on the endpoint, on methods that do not overlap the eight EU-NETVAL candidates.
