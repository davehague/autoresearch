Part III sorts fourteen named animal tests into three baskets by replacement maturity, each with a dated target — the boundary drawn on validation status, not science quality, which is this vault's own axis split done by a regulator in public. Basket 1 (mature): skin irritation (TG 439), eye irritation, skin sensitisation (TG 442C/D/E, TG 497 + SARA-ICE), all end-2026. Basket 2 (not validated enough): fish acute toxicity, PK in dogs/NHPs (*"there are currently no alternative methods which fully predict in vivo pharmacokinetics"* — a regulator confirming pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics's own ceiling). Basket 3 (nothing exists): one test. (1) The targets are aims, not mandates — every one reads *"we will aim to apply only validated alternative methods"* in a non-binding policy paper, while trade and campaign coverage reports them as hard requirements. The vault graded against the primary PDF; the leads-are-not-sources rail earning its keep on a live case. (2) ⚠️ THE RAIL HIT. Basket 2 targets replacing the in-vivo fish test under UK REACH by end-2028 with TG 249 (RTgill-W1 gill cells) + TG 236 (fish embryo) + QSAR — the exact pair this vault has carried as a `correlated_blind_spot` since 2026-07-11, because neither has a functioning nervous system or full biotransformation, so neurotoxicants and bioactivation-dependent compounds are missed by both *for the same structural reason*. Two tests, one vote. The strategy does not acknowledge it, and the EU is proposing the same pair in the REACH revision. This is the first time a vault blind-spot flag has been contradicted by a live policy proposal rather than by a paper. (3) Creates UKCVAM, a national validation body (filling the post-Brexit EURL-ECVAM hole) whose remit says spokes will explore "ring trials or innovative ways of agreeing regulatory readiness" — the fourth independent actor here demoting the one-off ring trial, after FDA CDER, the AI-NAM literature, and tonight's next-generation-validation framework.   • 2026-07-22 — genotoxicity-tgx-ddi-transcriptomic-biomarker → consolidating (auto-coined) · the genotoxicity column's first `closed-loop-ready` map, and it qualifies the tier on arrival (Wang & Crute et al. 2025, *Toxicological Sciences* 208(2):233–243, PMID 41031489, DOI 10.1093/toxsci/kfaf138). A 64-gene expression signature in human TK6 cells, read by digital transcript counting, classifying chemicals as DNA damage-inducing or not. Four independent laboratories, 14 blinded compounds, 47 valid tests: 100% sensitivity (95% CI 82–100%), 86% specificity (95% CI 67–96%), 91% accuracy, inter-lab Kappa 0.61–1.0 → `evidence_tier: formally-validated`, a bar few sources here clear. The specificity is the contribution, not the sensitivity: it exists to fix the in-vitro micronucleus assay's 30–65% specificity — an adopted, required test that wrongly flags a third to two-thirds of harmless chemicals, each false alarm historically buying a follow-up animal study. (1) Loop: `closed-loop-ready` — machine-readable readout and a published rule (three classifiers, positive if any fires). ⚠️ But the rule is *fixed* without being *sample-independent*: two of the three legs (PCA, hierarchical clustering) are unsupervised and batch-relative, so a compound's call can depend on what else was analysed with it. Invisible in a lab running a standard reference set; not invisible in a loop that chooses its own next compounds, which changes that context on purpose, adaptively, in a direction correlated with the last result. New distinction for the axis: *fixed* ≠ *sample-independent*. Practical fix is cheap and exactly the kind of thing an autonomous system would optimise away as redundant — pin the reference set, run it every batch. (2) Its `domain_gate` is biological, not chemical — the signature is p53-enriched, so it needs functional p53 in the cells, misses aneugens by design, and false-positives on non-genotoxic p53 modulators (kinase inhibitors, protein-synthesis inhibitors). Better than an empirical domain (you can check it in advance) but it has a compound-side half and a cell-side half that live in different places. (3) ⚠️ Rail: paired with the micronucleus assay it is complementary on aneugens and correlated on metabolism — both TK6, both rat liver S9, so a pro-genotoxicant needing a route rat S9 doesn't perform reads negative in both for the same reason. Both maps flagged. Honest brakes: 14 compounds and wide CIs ("100%" is zero misses out of six); adopted nowhere (`regulatory_status: proposed`, package submitted to FDA's Biomarker Qualification Program); and the developers are on the author list and are the party seeking qualification — a strong ring trial, not an independent ECVAM/ICCVAM one. `funding_flag: [industry]`. → tgx-ddi-ring-trial-performance, tgx-ddi-decision-rule-is-fixed-but-batch-relative, tgx-ddi-p53-dependency-defines-the-domain, tgx-ddi-micronucleus-share-the-s9-metabolism-gap
  • 2026-07-22 — regulatory-acceptance-ai-model-credibility → emerging (held) + ml-model-reporting-reproducibility → emerging (held) · the first proposal in this vault to put an AI on the *validation* side of the loop (Kopańska & Hartung 2026, *Frontiers in Toxicology* 8, DOI 10.3389/ftox.2026.1790669, peer-reviewed framework paper, no primary data). (1) A five-pillar replacement for the one-off ring trial — pre-specified reproducibility targets, mechanistic anchoring to an Adverse Outcome Pathway, external predictive performance, uncertainty quantification, data traceability. Pillar 2 is the substantive break: validating against the mechanism instead of against agreement with the animal test removes the ceiling that caps every NAM at *"as right as the rodent, including where the rodent was wrong about humans"* — a ceiling the zebrafish, DNT and thyroid maps have all hit. (2) "AI companion agents" monitoring a deployed method continuously, under Predetermined Change Control Plans borrowed from medical-device software regulation, with numeric re-review triggers (>5% absolute performance drop, covariate shift, code/library change, new intended use). Every autonomous-lab source here so far puts the AI *inside* the experiment loop; this puts one on the part that decides whether the method is still trustworthy — and continuous monitoring is the only validation regime a continuously-running loop can satisfy, making it a candidate way to pay the invoice FDA's model-influence arithmetic hands autonomy. (3) Virtual control groups (VICT3R): synthetic control cohorts built from historical control-animal data, claimed 15–20%, "in some cases even up to 30%" animal reduction, gated on *"Type I error inflation remains within a pre-specified margin."* Filed on the model-quality map, not a method map — a VCG is a model substituting for a measurement, and it *reduces* animals rather than replacing them, a distinction this vault keeps. Three brakes, stated plainly. Nothing adopted. Every acceptance number (ICC ≥0.75, CV ≤20%, sens/spec ≥0.80, the 5% drift trigger) is offered "for example" and derived from nothing — a validation framework whose criteria are placeholders is a shape, not a standard. And the headline trigger needs a ground truth deployment does not have: you cannot measure a >5% accuracy drop on field samples without knowing their right answers, which is the reason you ran the assay. Unaddressed in the paper. `evidence_tier: in-silico-mechanistic` (the fifth missing-rung instance for reviews/frameworks). Third Hartung ingest and the least independent of the three — his own *e-validation* framework, argued against the primacy of the ring trial run by the validation body he formerly headed.
