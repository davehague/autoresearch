Human corneal epithelium on a collagen vitrigel membrane; barrier integrity read as transepithelial electrical resistance (TEER) over a 3-minute exposure; a fully deterministic three-index threshold rule (time lag > 180 s AND intensity < 0.05 %/s AND plateau ≤ 5.0 % → No Category) → `closed-loop-ready`, `loop_blocker: none`. The finding is NOT another top-tier method (that makes 21). It is the `domain_gate`: TG 494's applicability domain is enforced by a machine-runnable pre-test — pH ≤ 5, rapid phase separation, absorbance shift > 0.1 — not a declared caveat. Every other domain gate in the vault is a fact a loop must be TOLD; this is one a loop can MEASURE. Read the numbers honestly: in-domain 96%/67%/81% sens/spec/accuracy, but that 96% is 83% before the pre-test removed 32% of chemicals, and the guideline's own verdict on the unfiltered configuration is that it *"would not be the method of choice because of its limited predictivity."* Both surviving false negatives were the mildest irritant class. ⚠️ Sets the vault's FIFTH correlated blind spot — and its first stated by a guideline rather than inferred by the vault: TG 494 (barrier) ↔ eye-irritation-rhce-no-category (TG 492, cell viability) read the same tissue, and TG 494's own text says barrier loss *"subsequently"* causes the cell death TG 492 measures — one mechanism sampled early and late, not two independent votes.
