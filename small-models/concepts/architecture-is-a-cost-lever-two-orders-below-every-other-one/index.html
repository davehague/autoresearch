$0.0007 per task from a 150M recurrent model — the largest term in an inference bill is a modelling choice made before any of this map's levers apply

Every cost lever on this map is a percentage off a bill whose order of magnitude was fixed elsewhere: switching to a cheaper open-model API at 40–60% (switching-to-cheaper-models-is-the-largest-named-coding-cost-lever), cross-model escalation at 74% for a 7% call fraction (cross-model-escalation-routes-7-percent-of-calls-and-cuts-74-percent-of-cost), context pruning at up to 73% of tokens (context-pruning-cuts-73-percent-of-tokens-with-cheap-heuristics), KV-cache layout at 3.3× concurrency (kv-cache-layout-buys-33x-throughput-with-no-quality-spent), quantization at ~30%, expert pruning at a third of the weights. All of them assume a general-purpose transformer generating visible tokens.

BDH-CQ reports 29.5% pass@2 on public ARC-AGI-1 at a computed $0.0007 per task from a 150M-parameter recurrent model that reasons in latent space and never verbalizes intermediate steps. The authors claim it breaks the published ARC-AGI-1 cost/accuracy Pareto frontier. The mechanism for the cost is structural rather than clever: there are no reasoning tokens to bill for, because the iteration happens in a recurrent latent state.

For scale, the cheapest per-task figure this vault holds is $0.040 — DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Preview on GDPevo's enterprise workflows, and ARC Prize's DeepSeek V4 Flash on ARC-AGI-2 (open-model-trails-frontier-29-points-on-abstract-reasoning). This is 57× below that, and roughly three thousand times below the $2.06 frontier ARC figure. Run 12's convexity result — 18.28 points of quality bought 97% of the bill, a 34× ratio end to end (open-model-trades-seven-points-for-61-percent-of-the-bill) — describes a different regime.

Three reasons this is a framing note rather than an action. The benchmarks do not connect: BDH-CQ is ARC-AGI-1 and the vault's cheap-open datapoint is ARC-AGI-2, so the two cannot be placed on one curve and the ratio above is an order-of-magnitude observation, not a measurement. The cost is computed, not billed — nobody serves this commercially, so it carries none of the endpoint, precision, GPU or time-of-day variance every other price on this map now has to carry (api-endpoint-changes-an-open-models-accuracy-by-27-points, api-pricing-now-varies-by-time-of-day). And ARC transfers to none of our work — grid-transformation puzzles are the furthest thing on this vault's ledger from tool calling and long-horizon agentic tasks, which are the first capabilities to degrade under any specialization or compression (compression-degrades-agentic-skills-more-than-reasoning).

What to keep: the cost axis has more room in it than any lever on this map exercises, and the room is unlocked by architecture, not by tuning, quantization, routing or scaffolding. That is a reason to watch non-transformer and latent-reasoning work as a live variable in a multi-year buy-vs-build decision, and not a reason to change anything this quarter.
