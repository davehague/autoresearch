A 2.6B model whose final training stage was RL run inside real agent harnesses beat a 9B by 17.5 points on multi-turn instruction-following and lost to it by 10.5 on coding

Liquid AI's LFM2.5-2.6B is 2.69B parameters, 128K context, ~34T pre-training tokens, under 2.5GB resident and ~1.67GB as Q4_K_M. Its post-training is four stages — SFT → teacher specialization → Multi-Domain On-Policy Distillation → agentic RL, the last being "multi-turn agentic RL through real agent harnesses" optimised with GRPO, over an SFT mix ~7× larger than its 8B-A1B sibling's and weighted toward agent tasks.

Against Qwen3.5-9B, ~3.5× its size (all figures vendor-reported):

  • Wins on instruction-following: Multi-IF 80.07 vs 62.55 (+17.5), IFBench 59.17 vs 56.47 (+2.7).
  • Ties on tool use: ToolSandbox 77.83 vs 76.44 (+1.4), BrowseComp+ 26.89 vs 27.23 (−0.3), BFCLv4 56.88 vs 60.13 (−3.3).
  • Loses on generation and math: LiveCodeBench v6 59.41 vs 69.86 (−10.5), AIME25 51.87 vs 56.07 (−4.2), Claw-Eval 62.85 vs 66.53 (−3.7).
  • AA Omniscience −29.50 vs −50.43: better calibrated about what it does not know, not more knowledgeable.

The split confirms the shape the vault has been assembling across finetuned-slm-matches-frontier-enterprise-labeling, subbillion-finetuned-beats-frontier-across-nine-benchmarks and purpose-built-extraction-agent-matches-frontier-at-fraction-of-cost, and sharpens where the boundary sits: not narrow-vs-broad task, but executing a specified procedure vs producing a novel artifact. Liquid says so itself — the card does not recommend the model for agentic coding.

The more consequential detail is the recipe. The vault's harness-absorption thread — compiling the scaffold into the weights rather than building or buying one — has rested on one controlled study of node-graph dialogue procedures (compiled-small-model-nears-frontier-beats-orchestrator). Here a general-purpose shipped model puts the harness inside the RL loop, and its strongest results are the harness-adjacent skills. That is a third configuration of this vault's central question: not small model + custom harness versus frontier + default SDK, but small model trained against the harness it will run in.

Two discounts, one of them structural. Everything is vendor-reported on Liquid's own choice of comparators, unreplicated at ingest. And the comparison is against base instruct models run as shipped — the same zero-shot-comparator bias flagged in parity-claims-keep-using-zeroshot-comparators, now for the sixth time, with no indication Qwen3.5-9B was given a tuned prompt or any scaffold. There is also no ablation: four post-training stages ran and nothing separates the agentic-RL stage from the 7×-larger agent-weighted SFT mix, so "training through the harness works" is a hypothesis this release makes attractive rather than one it demonstrates.
