Both halves of the pairing question appear in one table, and they answer the same way they have every previous time.

The headroom law holds for a fifth intervention class. Uplift sorts by how much room the policy had — +11.1 and +9.3 for the two lowest starters, +4.9 and +5.2 for the two highest. It has now been observed for builder-written test-time harnesses, evolved harnesses, designed meta-harnesses, harness-native RL training, and now environment-derived skills (harness-uplift-tracks-headroom-not-model-size, the-headroom-law-replicates-across-a-fifty-point-uplift-range, harness-uplift-and-base-score-are-inversely-related-across-seven-agents, training-uplift-is-largest-through-the-weakest-harness). Sonnet's +5.2 slightly exceeding Flash's +4.9 is the one non-monotonic step.

And the ceiling holds. The open 27B's best configuration — every skill the method can produce — reaches 52.1. Claude Sonnet 4.6 with no skills at all sits at 67.2. The pairing this vault exists to evaluate loses this comparison by 15.1 points, and it loses it to the frontier model in its default state, which is exactly the configuration the vault's question names. That is the third independent measurement of this shape (the-harness-uplift-does-not-close-an-eleven-point-model-gap, open-weights-in-the-best-harness-still-trail-frontier-in-a-stock-sdk), and none has yet gone the other way.

The two readings are the same fact seen from opposite ends: the weaker the checkpoint, the more the scaffolding buys, and the further it still has to go. The levers are substitutes, not complements.

One further reading from the same rows, and it is a measurement rule rather than a result. Average episode length is uninterpretable without the success rate beside it. Bare Qwen3.6-27B runs 69.8 steps, the longest of any policy, and skills nearly halve it to 37.1 — it was flailing. Bare Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite runs 36.7 steps and skills push it to ~50 while solving far more — it was quitting early. Claude Sonnet 4.6 barely moves, 29.3 → ~25 — it already knew where it was going. Two of those three step counts are close together and mean opposite things. Every token-efficiency and step-count comparison in this vault that omits the paired success rate is exposed to that confusion.
