Two readings, both load-bearing. The best open-weight entry is a 1.6-trillion-parameter checkpoint trailing the best closed model by 22 points on work shaped like the enterprise workflows this vault's decision is about. And the size class we could host and train starts near the floor: 5.41% at 9B, 32.94% at 27B.

Run 23's headline was a 9B moving 41.8% → 56.4% on SWE-bench Verified through harness-mediated RL (a-9b-gains-14-6-points-on-swe-bench-through-a-minimal-harness) — coding, with a strong pretraining prior. On multi-tool policy-conditioned business work the same size class does not clear 6%. Headroom is enormous, which by run 26's two-condition rule is necessary and not sufficient (headroom-is-necessary-and-not-sufficient-for-harness-uplift): at 5.41% a feedback signal may not distinguish candidate harnesses at all, which is exactly the NLE failure case.

Size is not the operative variable. Mistral-Large-3 at 675B/41B scores 4.66, below the 27B — almost certainly a tool-format or template mismatch rather than a capability floor, which is a live reminder that one fixed harness across twelve models is fair in intent and not in effect.
