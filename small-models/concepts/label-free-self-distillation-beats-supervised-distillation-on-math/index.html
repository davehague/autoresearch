A 4B gains 8.5% with no labels, no teacher and no reward model — and beats the supervised version of the same method by 3.2%

U-OPSD samples several completions per prompt, builds a pseudo-solution by majority vote, and distils the shortest correct completion into the incorrect ones. On Qwen3 at 4B and 8B in non-thinking mode it gains 8.5% and 10.7% over base, beats supervised OPSD by 3.2% and 2.3% on average, matches it in thinking mode (+0.9% at 4B, parity at 8B), and beats GRPO by 0.7% and 1.1% — across AIME24, AIME25, HMMT25, MATH500 and AMC23.

The reason this matters to our feasibility question is what it removes rather than what it adds. Every distillation result this vault holds assumes a teacher: frontier traces to log, an API bill to pay, and a provenance constraint that can silently flatten a run when the supervising model did not generate the SFT traces (cross-teacher-distillation-fails-silently-without-style-residualization). U-OPSD has no teacher, so it has no provenance problem by construction, and its cost is sampling rather than procurement. For a cluster with idle capacity and no purchasing path for frontier tokens, that is a materially different proposition from anything else on this map — and it sits before the run-12 build order rather than competing with it: extract the free gains from the student alone, then spend teacher budget on the residue.

It also puts a testable strain on the vault's corrected design rule. Run 12 restated it as internal referents are cheap and small; external referents are expensive and large (internal-referents-are-cheap-and-small-not-worthless). Majority voting over a model's own samples is about as internal as a referent gets, and here it beats GRPO, whose reward is external and verifiable. The magnitude is small (0.7–1.1%), consistent with the "cheap and small" half; the sign is not, since the rule predicts the external referent wins. The reconciliation available — ours, not the paper's — is that self-consistency on competition mathematics is a quasi-external signal, because a majority vote over sampled solutions to a single-answer problem approximates verification. That is falsifiable: the rule predicts this method degrades sharply on tasks where the model's modal answer is confidently wrong, which is most of our workflows.

Limits, and they are tight. Competition mathematics only — the task class with the strongest self-consistency structure and the least resemblance to our work. Two sizes, one model family. The GRPO margin (0.7–1.1%) sits inside the seed variance nobody in this literature reports (harness-optimization-gains-swing-with-task-and-seed-regime). And there is no token-matched comparison: sampling several completions per prompt is not free, and cost matching is precisely the control that reversed the self-inspection result in run 7 (self-inspection-harnesses-lose-to-repeated-sampling-at-equal-cost). The thinking-mode parity is the honest ceiling — this largely recovers what extended reasoning already supplies.
