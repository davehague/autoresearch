The lever that moved GLM-5.3 is the half of the pipeline we could actually operate

Z.ai states that GLM-5.3 runs on the same 743B base model as GLM-5.2, with every capability gain derived from scaled post-training — expanded task environments, longer training — and no new pretraining run. The self-comparison, which is the only part of the release where the base is genuinely pinned:

BenchmarkGLM-5.2GLM-5.3
Terminal-Bench 3.04.628.3
DeepSWE v1.146.266.9
Agents' Last Exam (CLI)23.828.5
CyberGym77.284.5
ExploitBench24.454.4

A 6.2× move on a terminal benchmark and +20.7 on an SWE benchmark with the weights' foundation unchanged. If it holds, the remaining separation on agentic work lives in post-training, not in the base — which is the half of the pipeline this vault has been asking whether we can run, rather than the half (pretraining a 743B MoE) we obviously cannot.

The direction is the transferable part; the recipe is not. "Expanded task environments and longer training" is a description, not a method, and the vault's own probes say the hard part is precisely what is omitted: CalibForge found training tasks must be calibrated against solver disagreement rather than merely validated as solvable (training-tasks-must-be-calibrated-to-the-solver-not-just-validated), which is a claim about how difficult environment construction is to do well. AgentOPSD's turn-level credit (turn-level-credit-adds-more-to-the-3b-than-the-7b) and Simple-OPD's warm-up finding (incorrect-teacher-rollouts-warm-up-a-student-as-well-as-correct-ones) are the vault's own entries in this column, both measured at 0.6–8B. Nothing here says the 743B result scales down; it says a vendor with the option to spend on either half spent on this one.

One row cuts against the headline and should be kept. On ExploitGym in a 6-hour window, GLM-5.3 completes 130 tasks against Mythos 5's 247 — nearly 2× behind — on the longest-running, most open-ended task in its own table, while leading the aggregate cyber benchmark reported beside it. Same boundary the vault keeps finding: near-frontier on scored procedures, far from it on producing a solution to something novel (open-model-trails-frontier-29-points-on-abstract-reasoning).
