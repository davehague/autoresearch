Quantization spares reasoning and eats long-tail factual recall — and the reasoning benchmarks you would check it with cannot see the loss

Across 58 quantizations of Qwen3.6 27B measured on a 1,400-question probe stratified into 7 tiers of obscurity, factual knowledge degrades non-linearly: flat above roughly 5-bit, dropping among 3-bit quants, sharp in the 2-bit range. The best predictor of the loss is not bit width or model size but KL divergence from the BF16 original, r = −0.981. Tier profile at full precision: T1 99.5%, T3 78.5%, T7 4% — obscure facts are the fragile margin before compression touches them.

The finding is the contrast with the same author's prior run on the same model and the same quant ladder, measured on AIME-120 and Terminal-Bench 2.1, which concluded that above 4-bit "the quality is basically the same" (BF16 70.8% on AIME, higher quants indistinguishable up to noise). Same weights, same compression, two capability probes, two answers. Reasoning survives quantization; long-tail recall does not, and the standard evals are structurally blind to it.

Two consequences. First, numerical precision is a cost lever for self-hosting, joining utilization, engineer cost, hardware fit (accelerator-memory-capacity-swings-self-hosting-economics) and software footprint (inference-engine-footprint-is-a-self-hosting-lever). Every self-hosting plan here implicitly quantizes — that is how a frontier-class checkpoint fits affordable hardware — and this prices the squeeze in a currency nobody was measuring. It qualifies the operator claim in operator-quantization-cuts-cost-at-benchmark-parity, whose "within 0.8 points across benchmarks" is measured on precisely the probes shown here to miss the defect.

Second, and more important: this extends the vault's central methodological finding one stage further down the pipeline. distillation-harms-are-invisible-in-aggregate-metrics and rubric-rl-silently-drops-signal-recoverable-by-self-distillation found the same shape in the distillation and RL objectives; finetuning-is-fragile-to-data-quality found it in curation. This is deployment: quantize, run the reasoning eval, see nothing, ship, lose the long tail silently. The tier stratification is what makes it visible — an averaged score would not have.

The KL-divergence result is the practical payoff: at r = −0.981 we can screen a candidate quantization against our own traffic without running a knowledge benchmark at all. That is the first detector in this vault for an invisible-defect class that operates at deployment time rather than training time.

Discount for source: one author, one model, one benchmark family, self-published, unreplicated — and the top-voted comment caught a real error in his size chart, which he acknowledged and corrected. Commenters also flag that imatrix calibration sets are English-heavy, so off-distribution content degrades more than English probes reveal. Direction and the KL instrument travel; the exact bit thresholds are this model's, not a law.
