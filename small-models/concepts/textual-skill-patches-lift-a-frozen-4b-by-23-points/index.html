A frozen 4B gains 23.4 average points — and 56 points on held-out ALFWorld — from 3,010 words of prompt text distilled out of a teacher's trajectories

SKILL-KD contrasts a student's failed trajectory against a teacher's successful one on the same task, distils the actionable discrepancy into a textual skill patch, and validates the patch by re-running the student. No weights are updated.

Teachers: Qwen3.7-plus for the 4B, ChatGPT-5.5 for the 35B-A3B — so cross-family transfer works. Best prior skill-induction baseline (SkillOpt) reaches 63.5 and 68.6 respectively.

This is a fourth configuration of the vault's central question, and the first one that requires no training run. The three on record are small model plus custom harness, harness compiled into weights (compiled-small-model-nears-frontier-beats-orchestrator), and model trained against its harness (small-model-trained-through-its-harness-leads-on-instruction-following). Here the teacher's advantage is extracted as prompt state, and the prompt state is the harness. Everything needed is a frontier API, our own failing traces, and a re-run loop — no cluster, no data curation, no distillation objective to get wrong.

The size direction matters for our experiment design. The 4B gains +23.3, the 35B-A3B +16.7; held-out ALFWorld moves +56.0 pp and +36.6 pp respectively. That is a fourth measurement putting harness effects largest at small scale, against the single relayed study claiming benefit peaks mid-tier (harness-benefit-peaks-at-middle-tier-models). Still nobody has plotted the curve, but the weight of direct measurement is one-sided.

Two cautions before treating this as free quality. First, the costs are unstated and they are not zero: generating patches requires teacher trajectories and repeated student re-runs, and the resulting 3,010-word skill library rides on every subsequent inference — a standing context tax that nothing here prices, on a map whose other findings turn on cost per task. Second, the baselines are other skill-induction methods, not a fine-tuned student, so this does not establish that text beats weights — only that this text beats other text.

Scope: two students, both Qwen, 4B and 35B-A3B; no frontier student arm; five benchmarks of which only held-out ALFWorld is clearly out of distribution; no intervals or repeat runs; one preprint, unreplicated. The teacher succeeds on only 46.1% of instances where the student initially failed, which is a genuine strength — the method does not need an oracle teacher.
