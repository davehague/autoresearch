Anti-distillation protection on frontier reasoning traces broke across three providers at once — the teacher side of the build order carries an access variance

Providers stopped storing chain-of-thought server-side and instead hand it to the client as an encrypted block replayed on the next turn. Panfilov et al. find the blocks are fully interchangeable across sessions, users and models within one provider's ecosystem. Injecting a strong model's encrypted trace into a weaker, less-safeguarded sibling from the same provider makes that sibling decode and emit it verbatim in plaintext — the capable model is never jailbroken. Demonstrated across Anthropic, OpenAI and Google. Responsibly disclosed, with cryptographic and system-level mitigations proposed.

Do not treat this as a lever. It is a disclosed vulnerability with a shelf life measured in weeks, the fix is straightforward binding of blob to session and model, and providers have every incentive to ship it. What outlasts the specific attack is a structural fact about the build order this vault has assembled, whose first step is log frontier traces where our small model fails (skill-text-can-supervise-training-and-be-dropped-at-inference): frontier reasoning traces are a supply we do not control, held behind protections whose strength is a vendor's implementation detail.

That completes a set. Run 12 established the buy side carries a price variance (open-model-api-prices-are-not-a-stable-planning-input); run 15 that it carries a quality variance (api-endpoint-changes-an-open-models-accuracy-by-27-points); this adds an access variance on the teacher. Every arm of buying is a dependency on someone else's decision, and the teacher arm is the one the build order's first step rests on.

The constructive response is not a cleverer extraction route. It is that the front of the build order already does not need a teacher: label-free-self-distillation-beats-supervised-distillation-on-math gains 8.5% at 4B and 10.7% at 8B with no labels, no teacher and no reward model, and incorrect-teacher-rollouts-warm-up-a-student-as-well-as-correct-ones shows warm-up needs teacher-compatible traces rather than correct ones. Take the teacher-free gains first; spend contested teacher budget on the residue.
