The "ties Opus 5.5" claim circulating about Inkling 2 Mini holds against a restricted-access Opus variant that scores 19 points above the standard one on the same leaderboard

Vals AI's CyberBench v1.1 leaderboard (updated 2026-11-19) lists Inkling 2 Mini at 74.58% accuracy, exactly matching a "Claude Opus 5.5 (CVP)" entry also at 74.58%. CVP is Anthropic's Cyber Verification Program, a restricted-access configuration — and the same leaderboard carries a separate, standard-access "Claude Opus 5.5" row scoring 55.36% (rank 41), nearly 20 points lower than the CVP entry. The LocalLLaMA sweep's framing ("Inkling-2-Mini ties Opus 5.5 on CyberBench") cites the match without naming which Opus variant, which is materially different from the headline claim: a tie against the standard model most people deploy would be a much stronger result than a tie against a restricted access tier most users cannot obtain.

Inkling 2 Mini is also far cheaper per test on this leaderboard ($0.93 vs. $4.17) but uses more than 4x the tokens (29.09M vs. 6.80M) to get there, so the cost comparison and the accuracy comparison pull in opposite directions depending on which axis a reader weights.

Discount: single benchmark, single leaderboard snapshot, no explanation from Vals AI of what the CVP access tier changes about Opus's behavior on this specific benchmark; the standard-vs-CVP gap could reflect a genuine capability or tooling difference, a different system prompt, or something specific to this one benchmark — unclear which from the leaderboard page alone.
