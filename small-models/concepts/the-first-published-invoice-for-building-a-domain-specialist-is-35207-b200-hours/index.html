1.63e23 FLOP and 35,207 B200 GPU-hours — and the artifact is licensed noncommercial

The vault has spent twenty runs asking what a domain specialist actually costs to build. Thomson Reuters published it on the model card: ~1.63 × 10²³ FLOP across 35,207 B200 GPU-hours, for a three-stage pipeline over a 35B-total / 3B-active MoE — Constitutional DPO alignment, continual pre-training on 200B curated tokens drawn from a >19T-token proprietary pool, then DPO plus RL post-training.

That bought +2.9 average points over the base checkpoint it started from (a-domain-specialist-cost-thirtyfive-thousand-b200-hours-and-moved-its-base-two-point-nine-points).

This is the first first-party invoice on the vault's ledger for the brute-force version of our own plan, and it lands against a standing position that neither half of the build is expensive (training-your-own-feasibility). At commodity B200 rental this is a multi-hundred-thousand-dollar run. Run 23 established that the hardware side of buy-vs-build had already taken an 87% price increase (the-hardware-side-of-buy-vs-build-took-an-87-percent-price-increase) and that rental rates still price a capex basis that no longer exists (rental-rates-still-price-a-capex-basis-that-no-longer-exists), so the dollar figure implied here is a floor, not a quote.

Compare the alternatives already priced in this vault: a verified RL environment at under 30,000 tokens (a-verified-rl-environment-costs-under-thirty-thousand-tokens); a skill library for a compact model at $9 (a-skill-library-for-a-compact-model-costs-nine-dollars-to-build); a harness search at $117 and 22 hours (a-harness-search-costs-117-dollars-and-22-hours). Continual pre-training is four to six orders of magnitude more expensive than every cheap lever the vault has catalogued, and on this evidence it moves the knowledge benchmarks least.

And the output cannot be shipped. The license is PolyForm Strict — noncommercial. As the release thread's sharpest comment puts it, the weights are "open enough to evaluate but not to ship a paid legal workflow." Run 18 established that open-weights status is a date rather than a property (open-weights-status-is-a-date-not-a-property); this adds that it is not a permission either. A specialist we could not ship is evidence about method, not about supply.

Discounts: single release; the FLOP figure covers the whole pipeline with no breakdown between the alignment, pre-training and post-training stages, so the marginal cost of the continual pre-train alone is unknown; no wall-clock, no cluster utilisation, no dollar figure; and the 200B-token curation from a 19T pool is itself an unpriced cost that only a publisher with that corpus could pay.
