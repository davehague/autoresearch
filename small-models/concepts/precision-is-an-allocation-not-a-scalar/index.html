Redistributing precision between tensors at a fixed file size moved reasoning 40.6 points — compression damage is steerable, not just measurable

Everything this map holds treats precision as a scalar the operator turns down: a cliff at low bit widths (quantization-damage-is-a-cliff-that-log-scale-metrics-hide), long-tail knowledge erased invisibly (quantization-erases-long-tail-knowledge-invisibly), and a per-capability decomposition where loss sorts by distance from a closed-form answer (compression-degrades-agentic-skills-more-than-reasoning).

A community build of Gemma 4 E4B says the scalar is an allocation. Building a category-oriented imatrix, measuring per-tensor damage and solving the precision distribution under a fixed byte budget takes reasoning from the stock IQ2_XXS quant's 28.906% to 69.531% — +40.625 points at identical size, and 96.74% of the BF16 source's 71.875% at roughly 24% of its bytes (3.3 GB against 14 GiB). Ten of eleven categories improved over the same-size baseline; the single regression was stability.

Two consequences.

The build-identity rule gets its sharpest instance. Run 13 established that Q4_K_M was non-monotonic in three of four independent GGUF builds of the same model (the-quant-label-is-not-a-spec). Here two files carry the same base, the same nominal IQ2_XXS label and the same size, and score 28.9 against 69.5 on the same category. A 40-point spread inside one nominal quant is larger than the 27-point endpoint spread (api-endpoint-changes-an-open-models-accuracy-by-27-points) and larger than the 18.6 points separating two open 30Bs (the-open-field-is-stratified-enough-that-open-is-not-a-capability-statement).

The KLD screen cannot see this. Run 9 established knowledge loss tracking KL divergence from the full-precision original at r = −0.981; run 13 required a linear axis; run 16 required verifying the reference bit-exact (the-lossless-baseline-was-farther-from-the-original-than-a-3-bit-quant). None of that helps here, because a single divergence number against the original is exactly the aggregate a redistribution hides: moving damage between categories need not move the mean. The instrument this vault calls its cheapest is blind to the newest lever on its own map — the averaging defect arriving at a tenth stage. Any screen we run has to be per capability, which is the same instruction run 10 already issued for evaluation and we have not applied to the screen.

For our workflows the direction of this particular build is wrong, which is what-the-allocation-does-not-target-it-spends. The transferable part is the method: build the allocation against the capability you deploy, and measure the ones you spent. It needs no training, no cluster and no vendor — an afternoon on a checkpoint we already have.

Discounts: one hobbyist, one model, one run, no replication, no KLD reported anywhere, and category scores come from a self-authored harness with small per-category sample counts. The +140.54% headline is relative to a baseline the builder calls collapsed; the number to carry is 96.74% of BF16 reasoning at 24% of the size.
