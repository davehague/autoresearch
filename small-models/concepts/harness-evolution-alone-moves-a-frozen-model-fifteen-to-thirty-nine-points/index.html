A per-task harness, rewritten by the model that runs inside it, moved a frozen backbone 15 to 39 points with reasoning switched off during execution

HSI gives each task family its own harness, hot-swaps it between iterations through a fixed task-injection seam, and lets an evolver rewrite the harness source from environment feedback. One frozen backbone — DeepSeek-V4-Flash — occupies all three scopes: it executes tasks inside the harness, it rewrites the harness, and a meta-evolver rewrites the evolver's strategy.

The control is what makes the numbers readable. Thinking is disabled during task execution and enabled only during self-modification, so nothing here can be charged to the backbone reasoning harder at run time — the confound sitting under most scaffold comparisons in this vault. Held out on BabaIsAI from a 20% unseen split, the evolved harnesses reach 0.980 on BreakStop (from 0.033) and 1.000 on GoTo (from 0.182), so the gain is not memorised dev tasks; Make, at 0.556 dev against 0.363 test, shows what selection on a small dev set costs.

This is the "build your own harness per workflow" arm run as an experiment rather than argued. Every other scaffold result here either freezes a designed harness (a-designed-harness-lifts-five-open-weight-targets-11-to-20-points) or evolves one against a fixed task distribution (evolved-harness-clears-the-harness-noise-floor-at-avg-55); this keeps one alive per task family.
