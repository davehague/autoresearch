The two layers run 29 could not separate are worth 0.2 points, and one of them turns out not to be a fine-tune at all

Run 29 ingested TielCoder's claim to tie Claude Opus 4.6 medium at 12/25 on SWE-bench Live, and flagged the unresolved attribution: three stacked changes, one number, no ablation (three-stacked-changes-one-number-and-no-ablation). The cheapest experiment it implied was to hold the model fixed and vary only the packaging.

Somebody did, on a different axis. Across 13 GGUF files, 5 seeds each, 65 runs, >300 GPU-hours, on tool-eval-bench 2.6.0 in hardmode at 50% context pressure:

  • Ornith-1.5 — 144.2, 95% CI [141.7, 146.7], n=10
  • Tiel-Coder — 144.0, 95% CI [141.8, 146.2], n=10

Tiel is Ornith plus a code-weighted dynamic imatrix quantisation plus the Qwen-Sharp chat template. The two layers that carried the entire SWE-bench Live margin are together worth 0.2 points here, with near-identical intervals.

A maintainer states the framing correction in-thread: "Tiel is an Ornith imatrix quant with different chat template instructions. There is no finetune involved with Tiel." Run 29's note said the fine-tune was Ornith's, which was accurate; the index blurb reading "a stacked fine-tune reached a frontier tier's solve count" was not. This is a packaging result, not a training result — it belongs to the harness half of the vault's question.

TielCoder's author responded and did not dispute the tie, arguing instead that the difference only appears on "genuinely hard problems outside the training data" and pointing back to the same SWE-bench Live chart. That is a testable claim and it remains untested by anyone but him.

The vault's position should move from unattributed to unreplicated: the +4 solves are not refuted — these are different tasks, and 88 tool-call tests are not 25 repository issues — but a claim whose entire margin lives in two layers that measure zero elsewhere no longer gets the benefit of the doubt. Better evidenced than run 27's community fine-tune claiming 2.7× with no baseline (five-thousand-examples-on-a-sixteen-gigabyte-card-claimed-two-point-seven-times); this is the arms being run by a disinterested party.

Discounts: one suite, one grader, one context regime; the author says first that "tool calls are not everything"; V100 hardware with q8_0 KV cache throughout; and the 27B comparators had 5 runs against the variants' 10.
