Unsloth's own GGUF-quality benchmarks against GSQ-RCO and Exllama-3 on Qwen3.8-27B draw unlabeled-chart and "more marketing than science" pushback from its own user base

Unsloth's Dynamic v3.1 GGUFs for Qwen3.8-27B claim 5-10% higher accuracy than its own v3 quants, ISTA-DASLab's GSQ-RCO, and Exllama-3 on DeepSWE/Terminal Bench at 256K context, with an explicit choice to exclude the BF16 token-embedding layer from its main accuracy-vs-size plots because Exllama-3 keeps embeddings unquantized (which Unsloth says would otherwise make Exllama-3 look worse on disk/RAM usage, though better on the chosen accuracy-vs-size axis). This is the first entry in this map's three-way GGUF dispute where the vendor's own user base, not just its silent rivals, pushes back directly on the presentation: a 44-point comment says most data-point labels are missing, including several peaks, and that scores implausibly "plummet" from Q3 to Q4; a 14-point comment says the lack of error bars and open/verifiable methodology on charts from an otherwise-trusted vendor "hurts their credibility" and looks "more marketing than science"; a third commenter disputes the embedding-exclusion choice with their own KLD numbers.

This map's GGUF-fidelity dispute (tracked since run 89-92) has so far turned on competing vendors' silence rather than their own community's scrutiny. This entry flips that: the credibility question here is raised by Unsloth's own audience against Unsloth's own chart-making choices, independent of whatever GSQ-RCO or Exllama-3's maintainers might say. That's a different, and arguably more load-bearing, kind of pushback for whether any vendor-run quant comparison in this dispute can be trusted without an independently run reproduction.
