Swapping shipped harnesses moved best-of-three by at most 0.035 and average-of-three by up to 0.055 — the effect is on consistency, and "quality-neutral" was averaging over it

Run 12 closed the shipped-harness half of this map: three independent routes, three task classes, one answer, and for the first time a published error bar — 0.079 normalized gain against per-task noise floors of ±0.035 to ±0.066 (optimizer-model-outweighs-harness-when-an-llm-builds-the-harness). Three shipped-harness comparisons before it had found quality flat while cost moved 2–3.7× (harness-swap-moves-cost-not-quality-on-real-engineering-tasks).

Comparing Claude Code against native and open-source harnesses across seven models on 36 long-horizon tasks reproduces that number and then shows what it was averaging over:

  • best@3 varies by at most 0.035 for any model — squarely inside every noise floor this vault has bracketed, and consistent with run 12.
  • avg@3 is more sensitive, with native harnesses improving stability — +0.055 on avg@3 for Kimi-K2.7-Code.

So the accurate statement is narrower and more useful than either "quality-neutral" or "buys quality": shipped-harness choice does not move the ceiling and does move the repeatability. A harness that removes half a model's bad runs raises the average without touching the best score, and every comparison reported as best-of-n or single-run is blind to it — which is most of the comparisons this vault holds.

That matters more than the size of the number, because of what the same study says about the gap it would be closing. The open-versus-frontier separation here is 0.237 on avg@3 and 0.122 on best@3 (the-open-frontier-gap-is-mostly-a-consistency-gap) — so consistency is where the gap lives, and consistency is the dimension the harness moves. The two levers this vault kept treating as unrelated are aimed at the same target.

Two revisions follow. Run 10's dichotomy — comparing shipped harnesses buys cost; designing a harness buys quality — gains a middle term: comparing shipped harnesses also buys stability, which is quality measured over repeats rather than at the peak. And any harness comparison we run has to report avg@k, not best@k, or it will reproduce the null this literature has been reporting for four runs.
