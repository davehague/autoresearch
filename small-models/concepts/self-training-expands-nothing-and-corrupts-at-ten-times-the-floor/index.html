Once the null is measured, external distillation reaches problems the base model does not and three forms of self-training do not — while self-training breaks working behaviour at ten times the floor rate

A ladder of training arms matched in data stream, volume and evaluation, on Qwen3-8B: majority-vote SFT (filtered over 8 samples), STaR (filtered by ground truth), a gpt-oss-120b external-teacher distillation positive control, a policy-gradient arm on majority-vote reward, and a frozen control. Evaluation at k = 128 samples per problem per checkpoint.

Acquisition. On the low-base pool, external distillation registers 8–11 detections; the three self-training arms register 0–2. A logistic regression rejects the obvious explanation — that distillation simply gained more overall — at β = 1.91 [1.25, 2.56], p < 10⁻⁸.

Corruption. In the 1,163-problem difficulty band, problems solved at baseline and lost after training: STaR 106, majority-vote 88, design-matched frozen control 8 (median). Self-training is not neutral on what already worked.

The direction matters more than the magnitudes. The cheapest version of "train our own" — bootstrap off our own outputs, no teacher, no labels — is exactly where the phantom gains concentrate once a control is present, and it carries a corruption bill an order of magnitude above the measurement floor. What survives the audit is the arm that needs a stronger external model, which reinstates the dependency run 16 flagged as a supply we do not control (frontier-teacher-traces-are-a-supply-we-do-not-control).

This does not overturn the vault's prior self-distillation results — run 13's label-free self-distillation beating supervised distillation on math (label-free-self-distillation-beats-supervised-distillation-on-math) and run 15's finding that incorrect teacher rollouts warm a student as well as correct ones (incorrect-teacher-rollouts-warm-up-a-student-as-well-as-correct-ones) measure different statistics on different setups. It does say that any of them reporting a transition-level ledger without a frozen control should be re-read.
