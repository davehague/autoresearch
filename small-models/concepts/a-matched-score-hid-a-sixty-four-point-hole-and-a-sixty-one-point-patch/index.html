A three-point gap between two conditions turned out to be a 64-point failure and a 61-point compensation, and the deployment path reorders the leaderboard

GPT-5.6-sol's four QuoteBench cells:

conditionpass rate
raw generation, raw path94.6%
raw generation, through the parser30.4%
disclosed generation, raw path55.4%
disclosed generation, through the parser91.1%

The matched comparison anyone would report — 94.6 against 91.1 — is a gap of −3.6 points, and it is the sum of −64.3 of transport damage and +60.7 of compensation. The aggregate is not noisy. It is composite, and the composition is the entire finding. Note the third row too: disclosure costs 39 points when the boundary it warns about is not present, so both terms are real and both are conditional.

And the ordering is not stable across paths. Kendall τ between the raw-path and nested-path rankings is 0.57. GPT-5.6-sol trails Gemini-3.5-Flash by one task under the raw path and leads by eighteen under the nested one — the authors call that reversal unambiguous — with bootstrap putting four more pairs on single-task margins.

This lands directly on how this vault reads capability comparisons. Run 15 found an open model's accuracy moving 27 points across API endpoints (api-endpoint-changes-an-open-models-accuracy-by-27-points) and concluded that self-hosting is the reference every API is measured against (self-hosting-is-the-reference-every-api-is-measured-against). This adds that even at a fixed endpoint, the harness's serialization layer reorders models. A benchmark score is a property of a model-plus-path, and the path is almost never reported — not by Artificial Analysis, not by ARC Prize, not by the Terminal-Bench numbers this vault quotes.

It is also run 25's instrument problem one layer down. There, a signal the agent could see read 96% while exact repair ran at 48% (visible-tests-saturate-while-exact-repair-is-half). Here, a signal the evaluator sees reads −3.6 while the underlying quantities are −64.3 and +60.7. Both are aggregates that survive scrutiny precisely because nothing about them looks wrong.
