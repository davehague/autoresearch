The candidate checkpoint's four reasoning levels were three, and the most expensive one truncated the harness's own conversation summary

A community Jinja chat template for Qwen 3.5/3.6/3.8 under llama.cpp documents two defects in what the model shipped with:

  • high was an alias for xhigh. The reasoning_effort dial presented four settings and had three. The template makes the four distinct, with scoped instructions: low acts one decision ahead, medium stays turn-local and bounds failed verification, high validates material alternatives without open-ended exploration, xhigh stays comprehensive.
  • The default setting destroyed harness state. In the author's controlled A/B on Qwen3.8-27B, the stock template under xhigh averaged 30,102 reasoning characters below 40k context and produced two output truncations, one of them the harness's own conversation summary. The template defaults to medium instead.

Run 21 established that this checkpoint ships with its effort dial at its most expensive position (the-effort-dial-ships-set-to-its-most-expensive-position) and runs locally at a fifth of hosted throughput (a-27b-runs-locally-at-a-fifth-of-hosted-throughput). Those priced the setting. This names the failure: at that reasoning length the output ceiling is reached, and what gets cut is the compaction artifact that carries state across the context boundary. Run 24 traced a 12.8× token spread at identical test outcomes to refetch rate (harness-token-spend-differs-12-8x-at-identical-test-outcomes), and compaction is what forces refetching. A truncated summary is the mechanism by which a model-side effort setting becomes a harness-side token bill.

The same template's release notes carry a second observation in the same family: frontier models load matching skills as a matter of course, while local Qwen models often read the published catalog and then ignore it. The author's fix, arrived at over versions v14–v16, is not a better instruction but a structural opening checkpoint that makes the load happen. That is run 25's proposed split — supply structure, do not request judgment — discovered independently by someone shipping against a local open model (the-open-weight-model-is-the-one-that-cannot-use-the-disclosure).

Two smaller conditions worth carrying into our own experiments. The template recommends temperature 0.60 for agentic use against Qwen's official 1.0 for general thinking-mode tasks. And some harnesses silently drop client-side chat_template_kwargs (OpenCode issue #26233), so a configuration can be set and never arrive; kwargs belong on the llama-server command line, in a single JSON object, because a second flag overrides rather than merges.
