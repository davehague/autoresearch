Kimi K3.1 Pro's own launch copy names a trailing gap against frontier proprietary models on its hardest benchmark, rather than leaving the gap for an independent audit to find

Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro (3T-class open-weight, 1M-token context, active-parameter count undisclosed) lands at 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 against Claude Opus 5.5's 66.4% and GPT-6 Astra's 58.2% — close enough that Moonshot calls it "narrowly behind" Opus. On xdev-bench-hard (internal cyber benchmark, pass@3), K3.1 Pro scores 81.0% against DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash's 85.7% and a third-party-sourced GPT-5.6 Sol figure of 100%, and Moonshot's own text states plainly that K3.1 Pro's cyber capability "remains behind" publicly available frontier proprietary models. On Kimi Hallu Bench Hard (claim-level hallucination, with tools), K3.1 Pro's 8.3% beats Claude Opus 5.5's 15.0% outright — its clearest lead against a current Claude model logged on this map.

This map's prior Kimi K3 entries have had their trailing gaps surfaced by independent audits (the three-months-later footprint/licensing audit, the AA Index drop traced to a scoring-methodology change rather than regression) rather than by the vendor's own copy. K3.1 Pro is the first entry in the lineage where the vendor names a specific trailing gap in its own launch material, even while headlining "narrowly behind" framing on the row where it comes closest.
