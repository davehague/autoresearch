A vendor's own table shows 1.7-bit compression retaining 99.5% of a model's competition-math score and 83.2% of its instruction-following score — the "95% mean retention" headline is carried by the tasks nobody deploys

Syzygy Research's Mach-1 Additive compresses Qwen3.6-35B-A3B to 1.7 bits per weight with multiplication-free inference, claiming ~7GB, 10× smaller, up to 120 tok/s on consumer laptops, and 95.0% mean retention across 12 benchmarks. The per-benchmark table published on the model card does not support reading that mean as a summary:

The ordering sorts by distance from a closed-form answer. Competition math and executable code sit at the top; instruction-following under pressure (IFBench −10.9pp), agentic tool use (τ²-bench −7.9pp, BFCL-v3 −6.0pp) and multi-step soft reasoning (MuSR −4.9pp) sit at the bottom. Top-to-bottom spread: 16.3 points of retention.

This extends quantization-erases-long-tail-knowledge-invisibly in the direction that matters for a deployment decision. That finding established that compression degrades long-tail factual recall non-linearly while reasoning and terminal benchmarks show no cliff. Here the same asymmetry appears inside one vendor's own table, and the casualty is not obscure facts — it is the ability to follow instructions and call tools, which is what an agent is made of. A model retaining 99.5% of AIME and 83.2% of IFBench has not lost 5% of itself; it has lost the part being deployed.

That reframes operator-quantization-cuts-cost-at-benchmark-parity. An operator reported ~30% lower cost per token at "within 0.8 points across benchmarks" — measured on precisely the benchmark class this table shows is insensitive to the loss. The blindness is now demonstrable rather than inferred, because this vendor happened to report agentic benchmarks alongside math.

Two qualifications. Retention is not fitness: 71.58 on τ²-bench and 68.97 on BFCL-v3 remain usable in absolute terms, so the compressed model is not broken, it is systematically worse at exactly the target capability. And this is a vendor model card — no independent evaluation, no error bars, no ablation separating bit width from the retraining recipe, no license clarity, no limitations section.

Operational consequence: any compression we run has to be evaluated agentically, on our own tasks, before and after. A benchmark mean is the wrong instrument, and this is the cleanest demonstration of why the vault holds. Complements distillation-harms-are-invisible-in-aggregate-metrics and rubric-rl-silently-drops-signal-recoverable-by-self-distillation — same failure of averaging, now at deployment.
