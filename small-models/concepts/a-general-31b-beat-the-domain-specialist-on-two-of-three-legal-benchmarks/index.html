The specialist lost the law exams to a general model a third smaller, and won the one benchmark that measures doing rather than knowing

On the vendor's own card, Thomson-1.0-Small (35B-A3B, legal and tax specialist) against Gemma 4-31B, a general open model:

BenchmarkThomson-1.0-SmallGemma 4-31BΔ
Stanford LegalBench79.983.1−3.2
MBE Bar Exam83.488.8−5.4
Harvey Legal Agent Bench.73.434.2+39.2
Tax Q&A86.684.5+2.1

A general model a third smaller, with no legal training, beat the specialist on both legal knowledge benchmarks — and collapsed by 48.9 points when the task changed from answering about law to executing legal work. Haiku 4.5 shows the same split at lower amplitude: 80.7 on LegalBench, 60.5 on the agentic benchmark.

This is the cleanest statement the vault has of what domain specialisation is and is not for. It is not a knowledge transfer. A strong general base already contains the domain — 200B tokens of proprietary legal corpus could not beat Gemma 4-31B on a bar exam. What separates the models is procedural competence under an agentic harness, and that is the axis run 24 isolated (the-scaffold-moved-execution-forty-points-and-strategy-zero) and run 20 found trainable rather than scale-supplied (skill-selection-is-a-trainable-capability-that-scale-does-not-supply).

The operational consequence is that this table cannot tell you whether to train. The 39.2-point agentic gap is measured against a model run with no disclosed harness, no legal system prompt and no tool scaffolding — the exact asymmetry the vault has flagged ten times, most recently in run 29 (sixteen-of-seventeen-agents-ran-in-one-vendors-harness). Gemma at 34.2 is a zero-shot generalist number, and the vault's standing position is that a comparison against an unoptimised comparator settles nothing (the-seventh-parity-claim-against-an-untuned-comparator). The honest read: the specialist demonstrably wins on agentic legal work against models nobody tuned for it.

Discounts: comparator numbers produced by the party being compared to; no harness disclosed for any arm; no seeds or intervals; LegalBench and MBE are multiple-choice-style knowledge instruments and their ceiling behaviour is not characterised here; and Gemma 4-31B is dense while Thomson is 3B-active, so the "third smaller" comparison flatters the specialist on serving cost and disfavours it on capacity.
