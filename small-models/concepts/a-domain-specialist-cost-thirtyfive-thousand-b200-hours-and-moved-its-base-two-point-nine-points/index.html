200 billion tokens of proprietary legal data bought 2.9 average points over the base, and cost a point on the field's standard benchmark

Thomson Reuters had every structural advantage the vault's core bet assumes: exclusive domain data at 19-trillion-token scale, a curation budget, B200s, and a commercial reason to be right. Thomson-1.0-Small is the result — Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, continual-pretrained on 200B curated tokens, aligned by Constitutional DPO, post-trained with DPO plus RL.

The average is doing all the work. Three of six cells move less than a point. The field's most-cited legal benchmark moves backwards. Tax Q&A — the domain where the publisher's data moat is widest — moves 0.4. The intermediate checkpoint beat the final one on LegalBench (80.9 vs 79.9), so the last stage cost a point there.

The one large move is agentic: +3.9 over base on the Harvey Legal Agent Benchmark, +12.9 over Haiku 4.5, +39.2 over Gemma 4-31B. Gemma splits 83.1 on LegalBench against 34.2 here — a 48.9-point gap between knowing law and doing law.

Read as evidence about method: domain continual pre-training on a strong open base has a low ceiling on knowledge, and what it actually buys is procedure. That is the same axis run 24 found when a scaffold moved execution forty points and strategy zero (the-scaffold-moved-execution-forty-points-and-strategy-zero), and run 28 found hiding behind structural completion (structural-completion-at-87-percent-hides-strict-pass-at-44). Procedure is also the thing the vault's harness half claims you can buy with a prompt for nothing — run 25's supplied context helped the weaker model without any training at all (supplied-context-helps-the-weaker-model-and-hurts-the-stronger-one). Nobody ran that arm here, so the 3.9 points are unattributed between weights and format.

This is the eleventh parity-shaped claim on the vault's ledger and among the most honest — the base is in the table, which run 28's 450M specialist also managed (a-450m-specialist-beat-two-larger-generalists-and-lost-to-the-third) and most vendors do not.

Discounts: vendor-published and vendor-benchmarked; no seeds, no error bars, no n; comparator scores for Gemma 4-31B and Haiku 4.5 were produced by the party being compared to, with no harness disclosed; Harvey Legal Agent Benchmark is not independently characterised; and the base is a one-generation-old checkpoint that a-generation-of-the-base-outranks-every-tune-applied-to-the-last-one shows losing to a current dense 27B by 8.4 points on tool calling.
