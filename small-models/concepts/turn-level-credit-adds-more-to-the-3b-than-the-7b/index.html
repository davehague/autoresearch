Splitting one outcome reward into turn-level credit buys 7–10 points over GRPO, and buys more on the 3B than the 7B in all three environments

AgentOPSD changes nothing about capacity, rollout budget or architecture. It aggregates token-level teacher–student log-probability gaps into turn-level evidence and recursively updates a Bayesian belief over which turns earned the trajectory's single outcome reward. Cost: one teacher forward pass per trajectory, no extra rollouts, no critic, no learned parameters.

Two claims fall out. The first is mechanical: scalar aggregation over a trajectory destroys signal the run already paid to collect, the same complaint rubric-rl-silently-drops-signal-recoverable-by-self-distillation made about rubric criteria and distillation-harms-are-invisible-in-aggregate-metrics made about condition-averaged accuracy. The remedy is the same shape as input-ablation-detects-spurious-distillation-supervision — run the teacher–student pair twice and read the difference — but applied to turns of an agent trajectory rather than tokens of a completion. Three probes now sit at different points of the pipeline and all work by giving the check an external referent; this one's referent is the outcome reward itself, unsmeared.

The second is the size direction: the delta is larger on the smaller model in all three environments. That is three more direct measurements on the accumulating pile that harness- and teaching-adjacent levers pay most at small scale, against one relayed study claiming a mid-tier peak (harness-benefit-peaks-at-middle-tier-models). Nobody has plotted the curve, and until someone does the mid-size arm stays mandatory in our own design — but the pattern is no longer attributable to one paper.

Scoping. All three environments are bounded and programmatically verifiable (ALFWorld, WebShop, Search-QA), the same property that made this vault's node-graph evidence resist generalisation. Absolute levels outside ALFWorld stay modest — 49.2% on Search-QA is a large relative move on a low base. And on WebShop the 3B ends up ahead of the 7B on score (90.4 vs 90.2) while behind on accuracy (69.5 vs 79.7), a live instance of the vault's own rule that the summary metric picks the winner.
