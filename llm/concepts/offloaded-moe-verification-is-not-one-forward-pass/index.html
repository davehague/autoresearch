The free lunch in speculative decoding was a dense-model lunch

speculation-trades-arithmetic-for-latency-without-moving-the-distribution holds that speculative decoding is a pure latency win: draft K tokens cheaply, verify them in one target forward pass, accept the longest correct prefix, and the output distribution is untouched. The verification step is free-ish because a dense transformer reuses the same FFN weights for every position in the block — the arithmetic grows, the memory traffic does not, and decode is bandwidth-bound.

Take the same block to an MoE whose routed experts live in CPU RAM or on Flash, and the second half of that sentence stops being true. Different tokens route to different experts, so verifying the block requires the union of every expert any drafted token needs. The paper measures this against a dense-FFN offload control on the same hardware, and the two curves separate immediately: the dense control sits flat at about one token-decode cost for every block length; the MoE curve reaches 5.8× at K = 16, at which point 59% of all routed layer-experts have been touched.

This does not refute the dense claim — it names the precondition the dense claim was silently resting on, and the precondition is exactly the one this vault's premise breaks. local-inference already holds that single-stream decode on consumer hardware is bandwidth-bound, and moe-sparsity-becomes-a-transfer-budget-when-weights-are-offloaded already holds that under offload the sparsity dial becomes a transfer budget rather than a FLOPs budget. Put those together and the consequence for speculation follows: the lever speculative decoding pulls is arithmetic, and arithmetic is not the currency an offloaded MoE is short of. Lengthening the block buys tokens on the numerator and pays experts on the denominator.

The parameter asymmetry is what makes offload the sane configuration in the first place and the reason this is not a niche case. On DeepSeek-V2-Lite the routed pool is 91.6% of the weights and 26.8 GiB in BF16 — far past a consumer card — while everything exercised on every forward pass is 2.4 GiB, which fits anywhere. Keeping the small part resident and streaming the rest is the obvious deployment, and it makes expert movement 87% of decode time on a 4090.

The practitioner form is a warning about a knob: on an offloaded MoE, the standard advice to lengthen the speculation window is backwards. The right unit of accounting is accepted tokens per newly loaded expert, and a controller should truncate a draft when a candidate is both low-confidence and would expand the verifier's expert set — which is what the source's own truncation rule does, taking verify-side cost from 2.35× to 1.48× before any prefetching.

Bound: single author, no venue, no released code, two models sharing the DeepSeek MoE shape, one consumer GPU and one phone. The curve is measured, not derived, and its shape depends on the router's dispersion — a model with fewer, larger experts (Mixtral's 8) would expand more slowly than one with 64. Nobody has measured this on a coarse-grained MoE, which is the configuration mixtral-offloading-moe-consumer-inference used for this vault's other offload result.
