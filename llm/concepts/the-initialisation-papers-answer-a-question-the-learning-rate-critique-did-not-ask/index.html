The initialisation papers answer a question the learning-rate critique did not ask

Two findings hold peft-methods at contested, and they point the same way. adapter-variant-gains-are-a-learning-rate-artifact found that variant advantages collapse once learning rate is swept per method. the-lora-init-papers-shipped-but-the-default-did-not-move recorded that a shelf of initialisation papers has not changed what practitioners run.

LoRA-GA² is the newest entry on that shelf, and this note exists to say plainly that it does not resolve either objection, and does not attempt to.

The margins are the ones the critique targets: +0.66 on the GLUE average, +1.03 on GSM8K, +0.87 on HumanEval, all over GoRA. The protocol is the one the critique indicts: baselines run under "the same optimizer settings as the corresponding baselines" — each method's own published configuration, which is the honest version of a fair comparison but is not a per-method learning-rate sweep. And the standard deviations on the rows being beaten are of the same order as the beating: GoRA carries ±0.76 on GSM8K and ±2.14 on HumanEval, RaLoRA ±1.61 on HumanEval, against margins of 1.03 and 0.87.

Three seeds with reported deviations is better practice than most of this line manages, and the paper should get credit for it rather than be dismissed. The point is narrower: three seeds under one learning rate measures seed variance, not tuning sensitivity, and tuning sensitivity is the axis the critique names. Nothing here rules out that GoRA under a swept learning rate closes the gap, because nobody swept it.

Two counterweights keep this from being a dismissal.

The paper contains one contrast that the critique cannot reach, because it varies nothing tunable: where-the-gradient-is-measured-beats-how-much-of-it-you-average holds the gradient budget, the method and the optimiser settings fixed and changes only whether the weights move during collection. That 1.81-point gap is real evidence about gradients, whatever one concludes about the cross-method table.

And the shape of the results is consistent across three model families and two modalities — T5-Base on GLUE, Llama-3.1-8B on reasoning and code, CLIP-ViT-B/16 best on six of seven vision tasks. A pure tuning artifact would not be expected to replicate its sign that consistently across encoder-decoder, decoder-only and vision-encoder backbones. That is suggestive, not decisive: all three arms inherit the same protocol, so a systematic protocol advantage would also replicate.

The status of the map does not move. What this adds is a dated, specific record that the line is still producing sub-point margins under the protocol the map has learned to discount, three weeks after the map learned to discount it.
