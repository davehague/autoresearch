A quantized larger model beats a from-scratch small one

distillation-small-models has held since the seed ingest that the way to get a good small model is to derive it from a big one. distilled-reasoners-beat-direct-rl established it on competition math; strong-to-weak-distillation-is-the-small-model-build-path replicated it at production scale as Qwen3's actual build process. Both are capability results, and both cost real money — a teacher, a trace corpus, a training run. Qwen3 spent flagship GPU hours to produce its 0.6B.

This confirms the same ordering on a different axis, and the cheap version of the build path is the finding. Take Qwen2.5-1.5B, run GPTQ at INT4, and it scores 63.86% against the from-scratch Qwen2.5-0.5B's 51.17% — +12.69 points, winning all four dimensions, at roughly comparable tokens per second (the authors put the quantized 1.5B within 1.2–1.5× the 0.5B's ms/token on an RTX-4090, since INT4 cuts weight memory ~4×). The whole from-scratch SLM cohort clusters at 45–52%; the compressed cohort at 63–65%. The gap is not marginal and it is not size — the 1.5B and the 0.5B are close enough in footprint after quantization to be genuine alternatives for the same slot.

Stated as a rule for the vault's premise: build path first, size second. If a 1.5B fits after quantization, a purpose-built 0.5B is the wrong artifact, and the required tooling is pip install plus a calibration corpus rather than a training pipeline.

The distillation arm points the same way and adds a detail the capability literature has also seen: KD-3B reaches 56.45% from a 54.85% baseline and lands above its 7B teacher on overall, robustness and fairness. A student exceeding its teacher on the trained axis is familiar (distillation-gain-tracks-novelty-not-teacher-rank gives one account of why), but that arm is a single 7B→3B run on Alpaca and should be read as directional only.

The consistency across three unrelated instruments is what makes this worth recording rather than filing as a safety result. Competition-math pass@1, production Pass@64 at 1/10 the GPU hours, and now a four-dimension trustworthiness benchmark all rank derived-from-large above trained-small. That is the vault's premise holding on an axis it was not selected for.

Bounds. Published venue, but the head-to-head is one family (Qwen2.5) at one size pair (1.5B vs 0.5B), and the latency claim supporting "comparable ms/token" is a single sentence about one consumer GPU with no table behind it. The from-scratch cohort is small and none of its members is a 2026 release. And the paper's own numbers show the advice has a floor — pruning-costs-trustworthiness-where-quantization-does-not records Qwen2.5-0.5B losing 4.34 points under INT4-AWQ where the 7B loses 0.37, so the model being compressed has to be big enough to absorb the compression for the argument to run.
