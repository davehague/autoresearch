The repair only works on the damage it was trained on

The most useful number in RestoreKV is one the paper files under diagnostics. Train the restoration module against SnapKV, then run it on a cache produced by KVzip at the same 5% budget, and it reads 42.0 against 73.2 matched. The transfer penalty is roughly the size of the entire headline gain.

That means the learned restore cache is not reconstructing "what a good cache looks like." It is modelling a specific compressor's specific damage — which is consistent with the method's own mechanism evidence (the restore pass reads the full cache and learns to emit whatever the evictor is about to remove) and with its ablation showing that reading the evicted cache instead costs 8.8 points. The module learns a residual against a particular selector's blind spots.

For kv-cache-management this is worse news than the headline is good news, and it is the reason this note exists separately. The map's founding complaint, restated every night since 2026-08-03, is that five or six levers act on one budget and nobody composes any two. A lever that must be re-trained per partner raises the cost of composition rather than lowering it: the number of things to train is the product of the choices, not the sum. And for a local practitioner the partner is usually not a choice at all — the eviction policy is whatever llama.cpp, vLLM or SGLang shipped, and it changes between releases.

It also sets up the obvious next question, which is cheap and unrun: is the transfer penalty a property of eviction policies being different, or of selection-based compression as a class? A restoration module trained against a method that does not select at all — approximate-every-token-instead-of-keeping-a-few keeps every token coarsely, so its damage is diffuse rather than positional — would either transfer much better (suggesting the penalty is about where information was lost) or not at all (suggesting these modules are irreducibly per-method). The two arrivals are from the same week and stack trivially, and neither group knows about the other.

Graded mixed on direction and open-question on stance deliberately: the measurement is strong and the method works, but what it establishes about the family is a cost nobody has priced.
