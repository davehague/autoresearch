Parallel generation and exactness stop competing when the generator drafts

diffusion-decoding was coined on 2026-08-10 around a clean-looking exchange rate: parallel emission is "a curve," with speculative decoding at the zero-quality-cost end and diffusion decoding at the many-tokens-per-pass end, paying for its 15–20 tokens per forward pass in measured accuracy (parallel-denoising-buys-tokens-per-pass-by-abandoning-exactness — −5.0 MMLU Pro but −19.2 AIME against the autoregressive parent the model was converted from).

That framing contains an assumption nobody stated: that the diffusion model is what produces the final text. It need not be. Put it in the drafter slot and the target model verifies its block, so its errors are rejected rather than emitted. The output distribution is the target's, exactly — the paper's mechanism is "lossless acceleration with formal guarantees."

The corner is real, and the reason it works is the same mechanism that made the diffusion regime's bill so uneven. Committing many positions in parallel is structurally a bet that they do not depend on each other, and the bet is worst on long dependency chains — which is why reasoning benchmarks fell four times harder than general knowledge. Under verification, those are precisely the tokens a target rejects. The failure mode and the rejection criterion select for the same thing, so the quality cost converts into a throughput cost: 2.1×–3.5× typical rather than the 10–15 tokens per pass a free-running canvas emits, with nothing lost.

So the map's founding trade needs restating with a scope condition. It is not that parallel denoising abandons exactness; it is that a decoder with no target distribution has none to preserve, and this literature is not obliged to run the diffusion model as the decoder. Three configurations now exist where the map recorded two: autoregressive, diffusion-sampled, and diffusion-drafted-with-verification. Graded pushes-back because it contradicts the map's stated framing of the axis rather than any of its measurements — every number in parallel-denoising-buys-tokens-per-pass-by-abandoning-exactness and reproducing-your-own-output-costs-most-of-the-speedup stands.

Two consequences for the map's open questions. The standing "diffusion versus speculation at matched quality" experiment is not a two-horse race — the practitioner-relevant configuration may be neither arm, and the right version is a three-way on one checkpoint. And the map's reporting standard, that a diffusion-decoding speedup must be quoted with the agreement it was measured at, has an exception with a bright line: under verification, agreement with the target is 1.0 by construction, which makes the drafted configuration the only one on this map whose speedup number is self-interpreting.

What does not transfer is the reason the map was coined in the first place. The drafted configuration inherits speculative-decoding's economics wholesale, including its dependence on the backbone (verification-cost-is-a-property-of-the-backbones-memory-structure) and on offload (offloaded-moe-verification-is-not-one-forward-pass). The questions unique to this map — what a free-running canvas costs on consumer hardware, whether the accuracy bill falls with more conversion compute, what the memory profile of a 256K canvas is — are all about the sampled regime and none of them are answered here. This narrows the map's claim; it does not empty it.

Graded strong on the losslessness and the speedup band (six targets, three base methods, four benchmarks, both temperatures, convergence proof), and bounded by scope: A100 only, 1K context only, and no measurement of what a diffusion drafter costs in resident memory — which on a consumer card is the number that decides whether this corner is reachable at all.
