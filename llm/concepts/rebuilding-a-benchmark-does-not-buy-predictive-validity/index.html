The repair works, and it repairs the other thing

This map's oldest failure mode is leakage, and it has been anchored since coining on a remedy as much as a diagnosis: rebuild the benchmark from scratch and watch the scores fall (benchmark-scores-can-be-memorization, GSM1k, up to 8% across families). Underneath that sits an assumption nobody stated — that reconstruction restores the instrument. Remove the memorized items and what remains measures what it always claimed to.

This tests the assumption on four benchmarks that have already been repaired by the field: WinoGrande → WinoWhat (paraphrased), HellaSwag → GoldenSwag (filtered), Social IQA → a filtered subset, Physical IQA → PIQA-RUP (paraphrased). Across 23 models from six families, the repaired versions largely preserve the originals' rankings and do not improve downstream predictive power — mean Fisher-z correlation with downstream tasks reads 1.565 for originals against .982 for the reworked (Δ = .584, 95% CI [.326, .853]).

The two findings are compatible and it is worth being precise about why, because it would be easy to read this as contradicting GSM1k. Rebuilding does remove memorized items; scores do fall for the families that memorized them. What it does not do is change what the surviving items measure or how well that predicts anything downstream. Leakage and criterion validity are independent properties of an instrument, and the standard remedy addresses exactly one.

A second number in the same study makes the point harder to dismiss as a quirk of these four benchmarks. Model rankings are unstable across benchmarks in the first place — models move roughly six to seven positions, and the top-three overlap never exceeds an average Jaccard score of .25. Whichever commonsense benchmark you pick, you get a different top-3. A repair that preserves rankings is therefore preserving something that was already not agreed upon.

The operating consequence for this map is a rule it can state plainly and could not before: a benchmark can be clean, uncontaminated, freshly rebuilt, and still not predict the capability it is named after. That belongs beside — not inside — the four existing failure modes. Leakage is about the items having been seen. Construct mismatch (low-rank-adapters-match-full-finetuning's structural divergence) is about two models reaching the same score by different means. This is about the instrument's relationship to the world, and it survives fixes to both of the others.

It also lands where the vault is most exposed. evaluation-contamination's standing complaint is that the four modes have no ordered clearing procedure at practitioner budget. This adds a fifth thing to clear that a practitioner cannot clear at any budget — establishing criterion validity requires a downstream task suite and a multi-family model sweep, which is a research programme rather than a check.

Bounded carefully. This is commonsense benchmarks specifically, and nothing here licenses extending it to GSM8K, HumanEval or MMLU, which carry most of this vault's claims. Evaluation is option log-likelihood scoring rather than free-form generation, which is not how these models are deployed. And with six families the authors state their own precision limit for analyses that account simultaneously for size, family and non-commonsense performance.
