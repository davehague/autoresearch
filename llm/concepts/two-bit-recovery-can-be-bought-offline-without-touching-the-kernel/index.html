Pay once on someone else's GPU, ship a file that runs everywhere

There are two routes into usable 2-bit territory and this vault ingested one of each within a day.

Change the runtime. QuIP#, AQLM, rotation-based methods and lookup-table kernels all reach low-bit quality by altering what happens at inference — an online Hadamard rotation, a packed-index codebook decode, a custom CUDA kernel that computes through a table instead of dequantizing. a-training-free-two-bit-method-caught-the-fine-tuned-ones and a-lookup-table-kernel-leaves-the-memory-bound-regime are that route, and this vault's standing discount on both is that they are unreachable from llama.cpp, MLX, or any runtime a reader of local-inference actually runs.

Change nothing but the offline search. This source keeps the same scalar per-row group quantizer GPTQ emits — an FP16 scale and a b-bit zero-point per group of 128, roughly b + 16/g effective bits — and spends the entire budget on a better discrete assignment plus an augmented calibration objective (the-ptq-optimizer-is-a-separate-lever-from-the-objective). Nothing downstream is aware. Every existing kernel loads the result.

The price is stated honestly and is the paper's own listed limitation: 253.7 minutes for a 4B model at 2 bits on an H100 PCIe, against GPTQ's 5.5 — a 45.8× ratio, 31.8× on Llama2-7B. For a practitioner that is close to free in the way that matters. Quantization is a once-per-checkpoint operation on rentable compute, and the artifact is a file. It is a different shape of cost from the one the-quantization-run-has-its-own-memory-budget tracks — that one binds because the run has to fit; this one only makes you wait.

What the wait buys is not marginal at the low end. Mean six-task zero-shot at 2 bits across eight models: 50.83% against 41.18% for the strongest baseline. More usefully, it removes the failure mode that makes 2-bit unusable in practice — Llama3.2-3B's WikiText-2 perplexity goes 10549.22 → 29.39, Qwen3-0.6B 12027.65 → 74.72. A method that turns "the model is destroyed" into "the model is degraded" on small checkpoints is doing something categorically different from one that shaves a point.

Two things to hold against it. There is no released code — a reproducibility statement covering the derivations, not an artifact — so this is a design anyone can reimplement and nobody has yet verified. And the advantage is a function of bit width: +9.65 points at 2 bits, +2.16 at 3, +0.68 at 4. At the 4-bit default this map still recommends, the offline hours buy essentially nothing, and tighter-layer-reconstruction-stops-paying-above-two-bits explains why.
