Bytes saved and tokens per second are not the same currency

local-inference's currency test — what did this actually buy, in the unit the user feels — gets its widest confirmation yet, and from a paper whose primary subject is something else.

cache-compression-charges-throughput-and-pays-only-under-pressure established the shape on one Apple laptop and one model: a fused compressed-cache path cost 5.3× throughput when the model already fit. This replicates the direction on an H100 and an M1 Max, across six compression configurations and multiple context lengths, on a sparse model rather than a dense one, from an unrelated group. Adding cache compression to an already-quantized model takes decode to 0.61–0.63× while barely touching prefill, and the penalty is context-sensitive in exactly the way the mechanism predicts — 0.97× at 2K, 0.61× at 32K — because long-context decode repeatedly reads and dequantizes the compressed cache. On the M1 Max the trade is one-for-one and legible: 46% less memory, 46% less decode.

Three further results push past the cache case into a general rule.

A smaller weight format can be slower. IQ1_M uses half the memory of Q4_K_M at 32K and delivers 0.41× its prefill throughput. Unpacking and dequantization overhead does not merely erode the saving; it inverts the ranking. This is decode-streams-the-whole-weight-set-at-a-constant-fraction-of-nominal-width's falling bandwidth-attainment curve continued past the point where fewer bytes stop being fewer milliseconds.

Structure beats precision for hardware acceleration. Expert pruning compresses only ~1.3× but preserves the BF16 execution path, holding 0.94–1.03× of baseline speed for a hardware score of 72–79. Q4_K_M compresses 2.8–3.1× for a 1.38–1.51× effective speedup, scoring ~48. The lever that touches the least numerically is the one whose gain survives the runtime. That sits in tension with sparsity-damages-more-than-quantization-at-half-the-compression, which prices structure as the more expensive lever on quality — the two together say structure costs accuracy and keeps its speed, precision costs speed and keeps its accuracy, and a practitioner choosing between them is choosing which of the two to pay in.

Memory savings port; throughput gains do not. The same Q4_K_M format gives 1.36× prefill on an H100 and 0.97× — parity — on an M1 Max, while cutting memory on both. So a compression result quoted as a speedup is a statement about the runtime's ability to hide dequantization and cache-format overhead on that specific hardware, and it should not be expected to transfer to a consumer machine. That is this map's standing complaint about datacenter-measured mechanisms, now measured on both sides of the line in one study.

Limits. Batch size 1 throughout (which is the right regime for this map and the wrong one for a serving claim), one H100 and one M1 Max, and the M1 Max figures are estimated total memory rather than a measured peak. Everything is one model family at 30B-A3B, so the numbers are MoE numbers — the expert-pruning arm has no dense analogue, though the KV and weight-format arms should.
