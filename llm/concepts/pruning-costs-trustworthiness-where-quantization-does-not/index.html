Pruning costs trustworthiness where quantization does not

post-training-quantization is built out of bit-allocation arguments and measures damage in perplexity, benchmark deltas and decision margins. It has never had an axis for what compression does to fairness, privacy, robustness or ethical judgment. This supplies one, and the result is not a small correction to the map's ranking — it is a 10× separation between two levers the map has been treating as one family.

The numbers sit side by side. Every bit-width step from FP16 down to INT3 on Qwen2.5-1.5B costs a combined 1.58 points (64.90 → 64.93 → 63.86 → 63.32) — and INT8 is a wash, and INT4-GPTQ raises Llama-3.2-3B from 63.19 to 66.55. One step from dense to 2:4 SparseGPT costs Gemma-1.1-7B 10.1 points (63.99 → 53.89) and Llama-3.1-8B 5.7 (59.52 → 53.79). Wanda at unstructured sparsity is worse than SparseGPT at 4:8. There is no bit-width in the tested range whose damage approaches the mildest sparsity pattern's.

The actionable form is short enough to state as a rule: at matched footprint, quantize rather than prune. That is already the local practitioner's default for unrelated reasons — GGUF and the 4-bit ecosystem simply have better tooling — and this says the default was carrying a safety property nobody had priced.

One dimension has to be carved out of the headline. Fairness swings widest and swings both ways — down 44.73 → 24.43 under 2:4 SparseGPT, but up 70.81 → 79.11 for Llama-3.2-1B under INT4-AWQ and 43.30 → 59.52 for Llama-3.2-3B under INT4-GPTQ. A score that improves substantially when you damage the model is reading refusal and hedging behaviour, not fairness, and quantization-multiplies-the-decision-margin gives the mechanism: compression shrinks decision margins, a shrunk margin near a policy boundary falls toward the safe default, and a benchmark scoring the safe default as fair reads that as a gain. Quote this result on ethics, privacy and robustness; do not quote it on fairness in either direction.

The study also replicates a size-dependence the vault found on an unrelated instrument. Qwen2.5-0.5B loses 4.34 points under INT4-AWQ where Qwen2.5-7B loses 0.37 — the same asymmetry small-models-amplify-quantization-error-large-ones-attenuate-it measured on error propagation. So the advice above has a floor it never states: it holds because the model being quantized is large enough to absorb it, and sub-1B checkpoints are on the wrong side of that line.

Bounds. Published venue (IJCNN 2026), four model families, two pruning methods at three sparsity patterns, two quantization methods at three bit-widths. But: truthfulness is dropped from the TrustLLM dimension set without justification; only instruction-tuned variants are tested; structured pruning is excluded by choice; all compression uses WikiText-v2 calibration, which calibration-corpus-choice-swings-a-quantized-models-math-score-again shows is itself a load-bearing variable this study holds fixed and never varies.
