The speedup is real, the model is not the one you asked for

speculation-trades-arithmetic-for-latency-without-moving-the-distribution is the reason local-inference treats speculative decoding as the stack's one exact lever: the rejection rule provably reproduces the target's distribution, so a bad draft costs latency and never correctness. Every stacking argument the map has made about speculation depends on that.

The lossy variants now shipping do not honour it, and they reduce to two mechanisms. Truncation-based verification (SpecCascade with min-p, Medusa's typical acceptance with η-sampling) accepts a draft token if it falls in an allowed set and rejects with no resampling — which makes the emitted distribution "the renormalized draft distribution restricted to 𝒜_Θ". Under that rule the model being sampled from is the 0.5B, filtered, wearing the 72B's name. Collaborative verification (CoS, lenience) emits a blend of draft and target.

The measured cost depends on a configuration choice that has nothing to do with the verification rule. Under plain single-draft speculation the gaps against matched truncation-sampling baselines are −0.88 to +0.76 across MATH, MBPP+, INCLUDE and BFCL — noise, and easy to conclude the relaxation is free. Under EAGLE-3 tree verification, typical acceptance loses 6.28 / 5.29 / 8.82 / 4.90 points on the same four benchmarks. The average gap widens from −0.32 to −6.32. The relaxation compounds across a tree of speculated positions where a single chain barely exercises it.

Block efficiency cannot see any of this, by construction. Every lossy configuration improves tokens-accepted-per-step and tokens-per-second — that is why they exist — while quality is measured on an axis local benchmarking almost never includes. The sharpest illustration is in the paper's own ablation: the best-looking configuration in the study by block efficiency (9.15, a 1.67× gain over lossless) is the worst model in it (50.26 Pass@1 against 75.84). Someone benchmarking their own machine — which is what this map tells people to do — would see the speedup and get no signal that the distribution moved.

This is a sixth entry on evaluation-contamination's infrastructure-non-equivalence ladder and the first on the inference side: exact by default, deliberately inexact under a flag, and inexact by an amount set by a second unrelated configuration choice. It also makes a fourth instance of recipe leakage — an undisclosed preparation step that sets the score — in a fourth literature, and the first where the undisclosed choice lives in the serving stack rather than in training or quantization.

One consequence for this map's own standing question. It has asked since 2026-07-29 whether a quantized draft still agrees with a quantized target at usable acceptance rates. That experiment now has a precondition: run it under lossless verification, or report the verification rule alongside the acceptance rate — because a quantized draft is a worse approximation of the target, and under a lossless rule that costs only acceptance rate while under a lossy rule it costs output quality. The two configurations are not comparable and neither side currently reports which it used.

Bounded: two model families at a fixed draft/target pairing, and the authors flag that magnitudes may not transfer to other architectures or scales. No open-ended generation or dialogue. Nothing measured on consumer hardware, which is this map's standing complaint. And the EAGLE-3 rows are Llama-3.1-8B while the standard-SD rows are Qwen2.5-72B, so the widening is confounded with a change of model and scale — mechanistically plausible, not isolated by an experiment holding the model fixed across both verification architectures.

Rebutted by: reuse-aware-gating-edits-the-target-model-not-the-draft — not a contradiction but a widening, and the reason "lossy verification" is the wrong name for the category. This note's two mechanisms both relax the acceptance rule. A third route leaves the rule untouched and perturbs the target model instead: an additive bias on a sparse model's gating logits during verification, applied to buy expert locality, so exact rejection sampling faithfully reproduces a model whose routing was edited. Measured top-1 agreement with the unmodified target, 89.1–93.0%, is the same order as the damage recorded here — and it is invisible to the same instruments, since task averages rose.
