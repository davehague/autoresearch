Attention splits into a random bulk and a thin learned signal

That weight spectra have heavy tails is folklore. What this establishes is stronger and testable: fit the Marchenko-Pastur law to a trained attention projection and everything inside the predicted bulk is statistically consistent with an untrained matrix of the same shape, while the outliers above the threshold are structure that random initialization would not produce. The threshold is theoretical, not fitted to the outcome.

The ablation is what converts this from a description into a finding, because it could have failed. Zeroing the 112,458 MP-identified outlier values in Mistral-7B lands all three benchmarks at chance — HellaSwag 0.814 → 0.256, MMLU 0.627 → 0.269, PIQA 0.823 → 0.508. The count-matched comparison removes 75% of the bulk on LLaMA-2-7B and costs 0.007, 0.060 and 0.002. Three quarters of the spectrum is nearly free; a small slice of it is the entire model.

For transformer-architecture this is the sharpest causal statement yet that the trained content of the 2017 block is low-dimensional. It is also the second attention-spectrum paper in five days to earn its claim with a control that could have gone the other way — head-function-is-readable-from-the-frozen-query-key-spectrum used an inverted-criterion arm (23.31 against 47.55), this uses a count-matched bulk arm. Two independent groups, same discipline, and it is the discipline that makes both credible.

It supplies the missing foundation under a-frozen-weight-spectrum-can-replace-a-calibration-set rather than a fourth instance of it. That note collects three methods — MoE bit budgets from tail exponents, data-free rotation for outlier removal, head function from query-key spectral entropy — and grades the general claim suggestive precisely because nobody had tested whether a spectrum genuinely locates the learned content or merely correlates with something useful for one allocation task. This tests it directly and separately from any allocation problem, on eleven models spanning BERT-base to LLaMA-3-8B. The first half of that note's heuristic — spectrum for role — now has a mechanism; the second half, calibration for range, is untouched and is exactly where quantization-damage-has-a-task-term-not-just-a-weight-term says the activation-space terms live.

The interaction with adapters is the awkward one, and it resolves into something sharper than either side. PiSSA initializes on principal singular components, and this says those components are where learned structure demonstrably lives — a clean justification. But adapter-variant-gains-are-a-learning-rate-artifact ran nine variants under up to 16 learning rates and found PiSSA's advantage at its own optimum is 0.22 points against a ±0.79 standard deviation. A correct account of where structure lives does not tell you where to add capacity. Read alongside the-minor-singular-directions-are-where-new-knowledge-fits, which arrived at the complementary claim from the other end, the pair is consistent and more useful than either: the outliers are what the model already knows; the bulk is where there is room to put something else. That predicts spectrum-aware adaptation should target the bulk — the opposite of what PiSSA does — and it is a cheap experiment nobody has run.

Graded strong on the decomposition and the ablation; the limits are about coverage, not rigour. The ablations run on two of the eleven models, and the two halves of the headline comparison are different models (outliers zeroed on Mistral-7B, bulk removed on LLaMA-2-7B). The benchmarks are three commonsense/knowledge tasks with no reasoning, code or long-context arm — and this vault's five-appearance pattern is that damage concentrates on multi-step derivation while single-step tasks hold (quantization-preserves-the-answer-and-changes-the-derivation), so "75% of the bulk is nearly free" is measured on exactly the tasks least able to see that failure. The authors themselves note the bulk contains "structured information that is spectrally diffuse," and their PEFT and pruning claims are outlined, not run.
