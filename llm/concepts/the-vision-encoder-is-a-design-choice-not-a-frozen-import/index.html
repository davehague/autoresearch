The vision encoder is a design choice, not a frozen import

vision-language-models carried one bullet describing late fusion as "a frozen CLIP encoder connected to an open LLM through a trainable projection." Qwen2.5-VL keeps the second half and discards the first, which means that bullet was two claims wearing one coat — and only one of them replicates.

The interface claim is now strong: an image becomes a token sequence the LLM reads, through a small learned bridge, with no cross-attention surgery. Three independent groups (a-projection-layer-is-enough-to-add-a-modality — LLaVA; Molmo; Qwen) across three years. The encoder claim is not replicated at all. LLaVA and Molmo both import a frozen CLIP ViT-L/14 at a fixed 336px; Qwen trains its own ViT from scratch at native resolution and aspect ratio (resize only to a multiple of 28, 14×14 patches, token count varying with the image), and makes that affordable with window attention in all but 4 layers — a 112×112 window, giving encoder cost that scales linearly rather than quadratically in patch count. That is the same move as sharing-kv-heads-buys-decode-speed-at-near-zero-quality-cost: buy back a cost curve with an architectural restriction instead of a kernel.

The practitioner rule is conditional, and it does not favor this vault's budget premise. The frozen CLIP is what makes VLM adaptation cost about a node-day; training a ViT from scratch through CLIP pretraining, alignment, and end-to-end fine-tuning is lab-scale. The gains it buys concentrate exactly where a fixed 336px grid hurts most — documents, charts, OCR (InfoVQA +13 over GPT-4o). So: if your target is documents at native resolution, the frozen encoder is the bottleneck and no projection will fix it; otherwise the cheap recipe still holds. The middle option — unfreezing an off-the-shelf CLIP instead of replacing it — is measured by nobody in-vault.

This does not settle the map's contest with early fusion. Chameleon (early-fusion-tokenizes-images-into-the-text-vocabulary) is still n=1, and its distinctive property is native image generation, which a third understanding-only model leaves untouched. The tension narrows to a count — three groups on late fusion, one on early — which is a reason to hold the map contested, not to resolve it.

Graded T2: a vendor technical report with open weights and standard benchmarks but no external review, no human-preference measurement, no data disclosure or decontamination statement (benchmark-scores-can-be-memorization), and no ablation separating window attention and native resolution from the 3.4× data increase. Molmo specifically published that Qwen2-VL scores better on academic suites than with human raters (open-vlms-are-mostly-distilled-closed-vlms) — a directly relevant prior about this lineage's numbers. The architecture is the durable content; the ranking is a vendor claim.
