Quantization is not one speed knob — it speeds decode and can slow prefill

The vault's oldest principle says arithmetic is nearly free and data movement is the bill (attention-is-memory-bound-not-compute-bound). This is the first in-vault measurement showing that the principle has a boundary, and that both sides of it are visible in one sweep on one machine.

Decode moves one token at a time through the whole weight matrix, so it is bandwidth-bound: a smaller file is strictly less to read, and generation throughput rises monotonically all the way down the ladder — 2.83 → 9.91 tok/s, a 3.5× span, with the most aggressive 3-bit format the fastest. Prefill processes a batch of tokens against the same weights, so arithmetic intensity is high and the kernel is compute-bound: dequantizing packed weights is real work that the smaller read no longer pays for. Prompt throughput peaks in the middle of the ladder (Q4_0 at 97.35, above F16's 79.57) and then falls, leaving Q3_K_S at 57.39 — 28% slower than full precision.

Practitioner consequence, which no in-vault source stated before: the right quant level depends on your prompt-to-generation ratio, not just your memory budget. A long-context RAG or document-summarization workload — big prefill, short answer — is penalized by exactly the format that a long-generation chat or agent workload rewards. Two users with the same GPU and the same quality tolerance should pick different files.

This also puts a caveat on every quantization speedup claim in post-training-quantization: those figures are decode or end-to-end serving numbers, and the prefill half moves the other way. The map's rule that a throughput figure carries a kernel, a card, and a precision (attention-kernels-are-tuned-to-a-gpu-generation) needs a fourth field — which phase was measured.

Scope caution: the crossover point is a property of this machine's arithmetic-to-bandwidth ratio. A 96-core Xeon with AVX-512 has far more arithmetic per byte of memory bandwidth than a laptop or a consumer GPU, so the existence of the crossover is the durable claim and its location is not.
