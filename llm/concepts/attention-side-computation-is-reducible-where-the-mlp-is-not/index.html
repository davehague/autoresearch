The block has two halves and they are not equally compressible

transformer-architecture's thesis is that the 2017 block has not changed and everything since is systems and numerics on top of it. This is a measurement of the block, taken with a numerics tool, and it finds an asymmetry the block's symmetry hides: at the same relative reduction of the contraction axis, attention-side computation loses 3.52 accuracy points and the MLP loses 18.78.

That ordering is not new as folklore — attention-is-memory-bound-not-compute-bound and ffn-activation-sparsity-is-a-training-free-decode-lever both live off the fact that the two halves have different characters — but the controlled version is. Every arm here uses the same retention ratio on the targeted product, so the comparison is compute-normalized rather than confounded by how much work each component does.

The finding inside the finding is sharper. The Up, Gate and Down projections have identical matrix dimensions and receive identical relative budgets, and they spread nearly 13 points: Down costs 3.51, Gate 7.20, Up 16.32. Nothing about shape, parameter count or retention explains that. What is left is functional role — where in the computation the tensor sits and what depends on it downstream. Down projects back into the residual stream, where the following normalization and residual addition have something to absorb an error with; Up and Gate construct the nonlinearity's argument, where an error is about to be amplified by a multiplicative gate. That is the same shape as an-error-that-reaches-the-softmax-has-nothing-to-absorb-it arrived at from the quantization side, and it suggests the two literatures are measuring one property with different instruments.

Retained activation energy is offered as the diagnostic and it only half works. It orders the individual projections correctly — Down retains 99.02% and costs least, Up retains 82.24% and costs most — but the whole-MLP arm retains 87.85%, more than Gate's 84.61%, while costing 11.6 more points. So energy captures how much magnitude survives selection and misses how errors compound across composed operations, which is exactly the gap quantization-damage-has-a-task-term-not-just-a-weight-term formalizes as the difference between a weight-space quantity and an activation-space one.

The practitioner form, which the paper states outright: component-aware retention, not a global dial. Reduce attention-side computation freely and the MLP selectively, Down before Gate before Up. That is the fourth independent partitioning in this vault to conclude a compression budget is an allocation problem rather than a global setting — after per-layer, per-tensor-class and per-modality (vlm-precision-should-be-allocated-by-modality, bit-width-can-be-assigned-by-architectural-role).

Held at suggestive: one model, one benchmark, one retention ratio for the component sweep, no seeds, and the mechanism above is this vault's reading rather than the paper's — the source reports the ordering and the energy diagnostic and does not attribute the spread to residual-stream position. It also sits in tension with the systems half of its own paper: the MLP is where most of the arithmetic is, and it is the half you cannot cut, which is why the end-to-end speedup replaces attention kernels only (a-twenty-percent-compute-cut-buys-five-percent-of-the-time).
