A third kind of lever for this map's budget strand: not compressing the cache's contents or merging it across layers, but replacing the storage mechanism itself with a unified quantized/recurrent form via weight migration

This map's strand 1 (what to keep, in what form) has so far carried levers that compress an existing cache within its per-entry structure (eviction, quantization, anchor-plus-residual, depth partitioning) or, as of vfold-aligns-then-merges-adjacent-layer-value-caches-at-near-zero-cost (2026-10-09), merge aligned caches across layers while leaving the per-entry storage model intact. RAM-Net is a different kind of move again: it replaces per-entry KV storage with linear attention's fixed-size recurrent state, using soft (rather than hard) address assignment as the bridge that lets the recurrent form locally approximate full-attention similarity. Unlike VFold's function-preserving guarantee (numerically verified, near-zero output distance), RAM-Net's theory is explicitly a local approximation for a restricted construction (fixed circular codebook, rank-at-most-2 projection blocks) — the practical system (unrestricted projections) is not covered by the proof, so this is a weaker formal guarantee than VFold's, applied to a much larger architectural change (eliminating per-entry storage entirely rather than merging two caches).

What "recovery" means here and where it breaks down. The headline 87.1% average recovery is on commonsense/knowledge tasks (ARC, PIQA, SciQ, COPA, HellaSwag) — short-context, not where a KV cache's storage economics bite hardest. On LongBench, the gap is both larger and gets worse for the strongest teacher tested (Mistral-7B: LCC code-completion falls from 70.0 to 33.4), which is close to the opposite of what this map would want from a storage-replacing lever: the long-context, large-model regime is exactly where eliminating per-entry KV storage would matter most economically, and it is exactly where this method's recovery is weakest and scales unfavorably with teacher strength.

Cross-linked to transformer-architecture, whose linear-attention/state-space coverage this method's recurrent-state mechanism is a variant of; RAM-Net's contribution to that side of the ledger is the weight-migration path (converting an already-pretrained quadratic-attention model) rather than a from-scratch linear-attention architecture, which is the more common framing on that map.

Caveats carried from the source. Theory covers a restricted construction only; practical RAM-Net uses unrestricted projections not covered by the proof. Fixed 500M-token migration budget, no budget-vs-recovery curve. The mass-aware readout variant trades closer attention-fidelity for slightly worse post-migration perplexity — a stated trade-off, not a dominant configuration. Nine models is a reasonable spread across scale and family, but the long-context weakness is measured on exactly the models (the larger, stronger ones) where it matters most, and the paper does not test whether a larger migration budget closes that specific gap.
