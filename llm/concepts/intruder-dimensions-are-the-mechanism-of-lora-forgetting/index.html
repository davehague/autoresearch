Equal scores, different solutions — and the difference is visible in the singular value spectrum

The second independent T1 push-back on low-rank-adapters-match-full-finetuning, and the one that supplies a mechanism. Shuttleworth et al. take the SVD of fine-tuned weight matrices and find that LoRA inserts new, high-ranking singular vectors approximately orthogonal to the pretrained model's — intruder dimensions — where full fine-tuning instead makes a spectrally minimal change, adjusting directions that were already there.

The claim is causal, established by intervention rather than correlation: scale the intruder dimensions down after fine-tuning and pre-training-distribution performance substantially returns, while the downstream task barely moves. Target-task capability and pre-training damage occupy separable parts of the spectrum, which means the damage is targetable.

Two practical consequences the vault had no evidence on before. (1) Benchmark parity hides it: at equal in-domain accuracy, LoRA models with intruder dimensions generalize worse out of distribution — so the matched scores that made "LoRA ≈ full FT" look settled were measuring the wrong surface. This is the same failure shape benchmark-scores-can-be-memorization names from the contamination side: a number that agrees with the claim while the underlying thing does not. (2) Adapters stack badly: intruder dimensions accumulate across sequential fine-tunes and multi-task performance degrades, which puts a compounding cost on the hot-swappable-adapter-per-task workflow that adapters-can-merge-away-inference-latency makes attractive.

Reconciling with lora-regularizes-more-than-it-adapts (LoRA forgets less than full FT): both hold at different levels. Biderman et al. measure net forgetting — LoRA's smaller effective update wins overall. Shuttleworth et al. isolate the mechanism of the forgetting LoRA does cause, and show it is structurally distinct from full FT's and removable after the fact. Together: the low-rank constraint limits total damage while introducing a specific, fixable kind.

Rebutted by: the-minor-singular-directions-are-where-new-knowledge-fits — not a contradiction but the constructive consequence nobody had drawn: if dominant-subspace adaptation is what manufactures the intruder dimensions, a minor-subspace adapter should show fewer of them at equal or better acquisition. That paper reports flat retention where full fine-tuning drops, which is consistent, and never runs the SVD check — making it the cheapest confirmation available for this claim.
