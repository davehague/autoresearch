The kernel lineage's own expiry clause fires again, on schedule, one GPU generation later

This map's 2026-07-20 entry stated the lineage plainly: FlashAttention-3 closed the FA2-era gap but is explicitly H100-tuned, and "whatever ships next resets this line." FlashAttention-4 (Blackwell- native, arXiv:2603.05451, not yet separately in-vault) is that reset, and this source is the first in-vault evidence that the reset is already being extended further — to end-to-end low precision — within the same generation rather than waiting for the next one.

The numbers, and their limits. On matched LLM shapes against cuDNN 9.24 and an internal BF16 baseline (Meta-internal Recsys GB300 GPUs): forward MXFP8 reaches 2.85 PF/s (1.43× over BF16, comparable to cuDNN's own MXFP8 at 2.82 PF/s); backward reaches 1.82–1.98 PF/s depending on dQ- reduction precision (1.21–1.32× over cuDNN BF16). At the full attention-module level, the win is sequence-length-gated: no real gain at 4,096 KV tokens (1.00×), rising to 1.30× at 16,384 — quantization/dequantization overhead dominates at short sequences, so this is a long-context lever, not a uniform one.

A correctness caveat inside the comparison itself. The authors found cuDNN 9.24's own MXFP8 backward produced mismatched dQ/dK gradients under nonuniform per-head V scales (fixed by forcing uniform scales) and flag their own cuDNN-MXFP8-backward reference number as "performance-only, not accuracy-qualified" as a result. A new low-precision kernel path can report a plausible throughput number before its gradient is verified correct — worth carrying alongside any future PF/s claim in this format.

What this does not close. This map's longest-standing open item — the task-accuracy cost of FP8/low-precision attention — remains unmeasured here too. This source reports SQNR against a BF16 reference and "neutral NE" in one production training run (Meta Ads recommendation training), not a benchmark-task accuracy delta, and the shapes measured are Ads-recommendation shapes, not an LLM workload — the LLM-shape kernel numbers above are the most transferable figures, but the module- and numerics-level results are measured on a different workload entirely.
