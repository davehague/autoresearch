This source names the training-time analogue for on-policy RL/distillation specifically: when rollout generation and gradient updates both depend on a model whose active-expert set varies per token, naively re-running full discrete routing decisions inside the training actor and running rollout/training serially is slow. Three fixes, combined: bounded-width dispatch caps how many experts a single token can route to after capacity filtering (RW32: keep the full set if ≤32, else keep only the top-32 by score) — a training-time analogue of the capacity constraints this map already knows from inference-time MoE serving; route replay reuses the rollout's own discrete expert assignments inside the training actor while letting the actor recompute continuous routing weights under its current (updated) parameters, avoiding re-running the discrete routing decision from scratch; and asynchronous overlap runs the next batch's rollout generation concurrently with the current batch's actor update, at the cost of a one-training-step policy lag.
