A four-stage audit of on-policy self-distillation finds the transmission step — teacher token targets becoming student behavior — is where the pipeline leaks, not teacher access to privileged information

TL;DR: This map's standing on-policy-distillation evidence (SPTA/CoRD/O²PD's "early rollout prefix" fragility, RDS-MOPD's teacher-scheduling fragility, OPMD's model-dependent-corpus fragility) treats OPD's reliability problem as located in when and how much to trust teacher judgment during a rollout. This paper decomposes the pipeline one step further and finds the fragile step is specifically transmission — whether better teacher token targets (produced when the teacher has the privileged solution) translate into better student behavior — not access (the teacher demonstrably uses the solution to solve harder problems and recover from errors) and not reception (the student clearly can learn from well-chosen targets, per the large Thinking-OFF gains under a Thinking-ON teacher). The paper's "reasoning arbitrage" finding — that a thinking-mode mismatch between teacher and student rollout drives most of the student-side gain, independent of whether the privileged solution was present at all — is a mechanism this map's other 2026-11 OPD entries do not test for and could be confounded by: any of them comparing a privileged-solution teacher against a problem-only teacher, where the two also differ in thinking mode, risks attributing to the solution a gain this paper traces to the mode mismatch instead.

What it says. This gives this map's T3-only "reconstructibility" hierarchy (privileged-information-helps-only-if-the-student-could-reconstruct-it, which ranks a full worked solution as the second-riskiest thing to show a self-distillation teacher, just behind the bare final answer, and reports full demonstrations costing up to −17% avg@16 on thinking models) its first T1 corroboration with a named mechanism: "Standard OPSD" — which conditions the teacher on the privileged solution — measurably degrades Step-100 accuracy by −1.92 to −3.57pp below baseline, while "Purified OPSD" (filtering the resulting training signal) recovers +0.74 to +1.86pp above baseline on the same setup. Where the T3 review could only report that full demonstrations hurt, this paper adds the why: the degradation is not generically attributable to "too much information" but to a specific transmission failure — the teacher's access to the solution improves its own solving and error-recovery without reliably improving its error recognition, correctness ranking, or branch selection, so the signal that reaches the student is noisier than the teacher's own improved performance implies. The blind audit finding that 45.7% of confirmed hard-problem improvements are self-contained (don't closely reproduce the supplied solution) is consistent with the T3 review's reconstructibility criterion from a different angle: gains the student could have reached on its own transfer more cleanly than ones that depend on content the student cannot reconstruct. Separately, the paper's "reasoning arbitrage" finding — that a thinking-mode mismatch between teacher and student rollout drives most of the student-side gain, independent of whether the privileged solution was present at all — is a mechanism neither this map's reconstructibility review nor its other 2026-11 OPD entries (SPTA/CoRD/O²PD, RDS-MOPD, OPMD) test for, and any of them comparing a privileged-solution teacher against a problem-only teacher without controlling for thinking mode risks attributing to the solution a gain this paper traces to the mode mismatch instead.

Caveats. Internship-affiliated two-author work (Meta Superintelligence Labs/UCLA), no stated peer venue, no code release. Multiple analyses are small single-seed pilots (35-111 questions, 50 recovery prefixes, 135 branch events), the 1.7B problem-only-teacher gain's interval includes zero, and the authors' own held-out critic for validating a hindsight-oracle estimate scored below chance (AUROC 0.452) — the paper is explicit that the mechanism behind "reasoning arbitrage" is unresolved. Thinking-mode comparisons use non-matched corpora across conditions, which the authors flag as descriptive rather than fully controlled. No test against this map's other named OPD fragility fixes (SPTA/CoRD/O²PD, RDS-MOPD, OPMD) on the same benchmark/model setup — whether thinking-mode mismatch explains any of their reported gains, or is orthogonal to the fragilities they each separately name, is untested in either direction.
