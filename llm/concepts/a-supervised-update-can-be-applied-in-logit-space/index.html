A supervised update can be applied in logit space

adapter-objects-and-serving exists because the sources kept changing where task knowledge lives rather than how it is configured. It holds four locations so far: merged into the weights (adapters-can-merge-away-inference-latency), in an unmergeable nonlinear branch (a-nonlinear-adapter-branch-can-never-merge), in an external positionless operator read from the residual stream, and in the prompt. Weightless Fine-Tuning adds a fifth, and it is the first that leaves the model completely untouched: the logits, at decode time.

The construction is short. The supervised residual r_s = e_ỹ(s+1) − p(·|x̃_1:s) is a vocabulary-space vector — the thing a cross-entropy gradient is computed from. WFT never propagates it. It estimates a cross-prefix transport operator M_{t←s} mapping a logit perturbation at one context to the induced change at another, then adds η Σ_s M_{t←s} Σ_j r_s to the current logits. The operator is estimated from dropout-induced cross-covariance: K=10 forward passes under different dropout masks, M̂ = Σ_{t,s}(Σ_{s,s} + λI)^{-1}. There is no backward pass in the method at any point.

The claim that it is the same adaptation, not merely a competitive one, rests on the logit-level check rather than the benchmark: cosine 0.875 between the WFT and SFT logit shifts, over the top-10 tokens carrying >95% of the probability mass. That is a mechanistic result, and it is the part of this paper most likely to survive.

What the benchmark result is worth is a separate question. The SFT arm is a fixed-recipe LoRA — rank 8, 40 epochs, batch 1, no learning-rate sweep — and this vault has a standing correction for that. a-differential-learning-rate-is-the-free-version-of-the-sweep moved a LaMP-2 score from 0.326 to 0.572 on a step-size change alone, on the same benchmark family; adapter-variant-gains-are-a-learning-rate-artifact prices tuned parameterization differences at 0.43–1.75%. A 0.014 average R-1 margin over an untuned LoRA does not clear that. Carry the mechanism, hold the head-to-head open.

Two conditions decide whether the method is available at all, and both are checkable properties of the artifact rather than open questions. It requires dropout at inference on a frozen model, since dropout is the perturbation source for the operator — the same shape as the weight-tying prerequisite weight-tying-decides-whether-vocabulary-pruning-is-free found for vocabulary pruning. And its serving cost is linear in V on every decoded token of every personalized request, which is precisely the per-token unembedding-scale term tokenization-and-vocabulary prices. Against a merged LoRA, which costs exactly zero at serving time, that is the trade: WFT buys the training budget and spends it at decode, and the paper reports no wall-clock, memory or throughput number anywhere.

Bounds. One task family — short-form stylistic personalization scored by ROUGE. Two 8B bases. 50 authors per dataset. No code link. The authors' own scope note is the sharpest one: WFT "does not reproduce the internal representation changes induced by parameter fine-tuning," and extension to reasoning-heavy, long-form or cross-task adaptation is untested. The separating experiment is cheap and unrun: run it on a task where the adaptation is a capability rather than a style.
