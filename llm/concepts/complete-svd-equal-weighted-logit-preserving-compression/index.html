Post-training low-rank compression should preserve a model's whole output distribution, not just the tokens it happened to predict or observe — equal-weighting every logit change, not just the likely ones, gives a closed-form SVD that wins on perplexity and instruction-following

TL;DR: Complete-SVD compresses an LLM's weight matrices via post-training low-rank decomposition while explicitly preserving the model's input-output behavior: it minimizes the L2 distance between the original and compressed model's logits, weighting all logit changes equally rather than conditioning on the observed next token or the model's own predicted probability, as prior "output-aware" low-rank methods do. After linearizing the compression step and applying a Kronecker-factored approximation, this reduces to a closed-form weighted SVD; the method's own output-side weighting factor, otherwise intractable, is estimated without bias using K unbiased random-probe backward passes per calibration sequence, at a cost independent of vocabulary size or sequence length. With uniform per-layer ranks and WikiText-2 calibration, Complete-SVD posts the lowest C4 and PTB perplexity in every tested setting, and also wins at a 0.7 compression ratio under non-uniform (LEMS) rank allocation. A KL-divergence variant (KL-SVD) improves calibration-corpus perplexity and downstream accuracy in most settings but generalizes worse across corpora. Complete-SVD also improves IFEval instruction-following accuracy over the strongest prior output-aware baselines throughout.

Why it matters here. This map's founding claim is that pruning (removing parameters) is a different object from re-encoding them (quantization), with pruning paying roughly an order of magnitude more per unit of footprint reduction than quantization at matched budget. Complete-SVD is this map's first low-rank decomposition source — a third compression family alongside structured pruning (depth/width removal) and quantization (bit re-encoding), closer in mechanism to quantization (the weight matrix is re-encoded in a smaller factorized form, not deleted) but reported in this map's literature because low-rank weight compression competes directly with pruning for the same "shrink the base checkpoint" use case. It is also the map's first source to report a downstream accuracy metric (IFEval) alongside perplexity rather than perplexity alone — a partial answer to evaluation-contamination's standing "perplexity is a training-health signal, not a model-selection criterion" rule, though IFEval is a narrow instruction-following check, not the GSM8K/HumanEval/MMLU family most of this vault's claims rest on. No source here or in post-training-quantization has yet run low-rank weight compression against quantization at a matched footprint, which is exactly the "one study, same models, same tasks" comparison this map's open questions already call its single most actionable missing experiment — Complete-SVD sharpens that gap rather than closing it, since it has its own distinct compression family to compare.

Caveats. No institutional affiliation or code-release link stated on the abstract page. Primary evaluation is perplexity (C4, PTB); IFEval is the only downstream-accuracy metric reported at the level of detail available here, and no reasoning benchmark (GSM8K/MMLU/HumanEval) is mentioned. No comparison against structured pruning or quantization at matched footprint. No latency, memory, or hardware-serving numbers — this is a compression-quality paper, not a systems paper. KL-SVD's cross-corpus perplexity regression is a real caveat on the method's own calibration-generalization, parallel to this vault's standing calibration-sensitivity concerns for quantization and pruning, untested against either here.
