13:T4ef, Complete-SVD compresses an LLM's weight matrices via post-training low-rank decomposition while explicitly preserving the model's input-output behavior: it minimizes the L2 distance between the original and compressed model's logits, weighting all logit changes equally rather than conditioning on the observed next token or the model's own predicted probability, as prior "output-aware" low-rank methods do. After linearizing the compression step and applying a Kronecker-factored approximation, this reduces to a closed-form weighted SVD; the method's own output-side weighting factor, otherwise intractable, is estimated without bias using K unbiased random-probe backward passes per calibration sequence, at a cost independent of vocabulary size or sequence length. With uniform per-layer ranks and WikiText-2 calibration, Complete-SVD posts the lowest C4 and PTB perplexity in every tested setting, and also wins at a 0.7 compression ratio under non-uniform (LEMS) rank allocation. A KL-divergence variant (KL-SVD) improves calibration-corpus perplexity and downstream accuracy in most settings but generalizes worse across corpora.
