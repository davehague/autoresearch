A teacher that cannot see can still write the corpus for a student that can

The vault's first evidence on fine-tuning-data synthesis — the gap data-curation names explicitly, having carried only pretraining-data evidence until now. Facing the absence of any multimodal instruction-following corpus, LLaVA's authors manufactured one: hand language-only GPT-4 the symbolic residue of an image (COCO captions plus bounding-box coordinates), never the pixels, and have it write conversations, detailed descriptions, and multi-step reasoning about a scene it cannot perceive. 158K samples, and they were enough.

Two things generalize past the multimodal setting. First, the bottleneck for a new capability is often corpus existence, not model capacity — and a corpus can be manufactured from a symbolic proxy when direct annotation is infeasible. Second, this is distillation-small-models' maneuver running sideways rather than down: strong-to-weak-distillation-is-the-small-model-build-path transfers a teacher's capability into a smaller student on the same modality, while this transfers a teacher's reasoning into a student operating in a modality the teacher never had. The leverage in both is access to teacher output, not access to the teacher's training run.

It also sharpens data-curation's central finding in a new direction. data-curation-choices-are-ablatable-and-non-monotonic establishes that data-quality decisions are engineering variables you can ablate; this shows the supply of data is likewise an engineering variable rather than a given. But the ablation discipline FineWeb models is exactly what is missing here — LLaVA reports that the synthesis worked, not which synthesis choices mattered.

Standing caveats: the teacher is proprietary, carrying licensing and reproducibility strings; the data inherits GPT-4's biases and hallucinations unmeasured (a bounding box does not stop a teacher from inventing detail); and the model trained on this data is evaluated by the same teacher that wrote it, a loop evaluation-contamination treats as uninformative about absolute capability.
