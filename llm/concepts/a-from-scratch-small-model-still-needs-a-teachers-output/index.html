A from-scratch small model still needs a teacher's output

distillation-small-models's spine is that you derive a small model from a large one rather than train it small — strong-to-weak-distillation-is-the-small-model-build-path from a production vendor, a-quantized-larger-model-beats-a-from-scratch-small-one from the deployment side, distilled-reasoners-beat-direct-rl from the reasoning side. Three unrelated instruments, one build-path claim. This is the first source to run the other arm and win.

Mimir v1 is 1B parameters, trained from scratch, with no distillation loss, no teacher logits and no larger sibling in the lineage. It beats every same-class open model in the comparison by 18–32 points on the English suite and by 20–28 on Danish. Against the identical architecture at identical scale — HRM-Text 1B, where the data mixture is the only difference — it gains +36.7% on math and code and +35.1 points on Danish. Taken at face value, that is a from-scratch 1B outperforming the distilled and the conventionally-pretrained 1B field.

It should not be taken entirely at face value, and the qualifier is in the corpus table rather than in the results. 7.81B of the 70.48B tokens — 11.08% — are synthetic data generated by a 31B model and audited before inclusion, plus a further 1.59B translated-and-audited. A strong model's output is shaping the small model's training distribution. That is distillation routed through a dataset instead of through logits, which off-policy-distillation-transfers-what-on-policy-does-not already covers as a mechanism and which synthetic-instructions-from-a-blind-teacher-bootstrap-a-corpus covers as a recipe.

So the honest reading narrows the incumbent claim rather than refuting it. The build path is teacher-free at the gradient and not teacher-free in the information, and the distinction is practical rather than pedantic: teacher-free-at-the-gradient buys you a licensing story, an infrastructure story (no teacher to host during training), and freedom from the teacher's tokenizer and context limit — see the-kl-loss-not-the-model-caps-distillation-context-length for what that last one costs when you do keep the teacher in the loop. It does not buy you independence from a larger model's capabilities. What strong-to-weak-distillation-is-the-small-model-build-path should now be read as asserting is that the information has to come from somewhere stronger, not that a distillation objective is the way to move it.

Graded single-study and deliberately: one model, one architecture (HRM-Text, which is unusual and whose scaling behaviour the authors say is unstudied), two target languages, self-reported evaluation with no independent replication, and no ablation isolating the synthetic fraction — dropping the 11.08% and retraining is the experiment that would settle how much of the result it carries, and it was not run. The English-side comparisons additionally need training-on-the-answer-format-is-most-of-a-benchmark-win applied before they are quoted; the same-architecture HRM-Text contrast and the Danish results are the parts that survive that screen. This narrows the map's strand-1 claim and does not downgrade it.
