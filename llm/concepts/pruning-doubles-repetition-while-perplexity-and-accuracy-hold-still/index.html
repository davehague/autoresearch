The metric that says pruning was cheap is not measuring the thing that broke

This map's central accounting — at matched footprint, quantizing costs several points where pruning costs tens — is measured on capability suites and perplexity. This source is not a rebuttal of that accounting. It is a statement about what those two instruments cannot see, and it lands on the map's already-recorded observation that pruning damages output discipline before it removes knowledge.

That observation came in from the deployment side as verbosity: pruned models get wordy, mean SQuAD response 29 → 61 characters, and the token-count inflation was paid N_gen times per generation (structured-pruning-breaks-the-k-quant-block-and-inflates-the-file). This is the same defect measured properly and given a mechanism. It is not wordiness. It is repetition loops — a short n-gram recurring until it dominates the output — and it roughly doubles under sampling and rises 2.4× under greedy decoding at a pruning ratio where perplexity and zero-shot accuracy are essentially unmoved.

Both directions of the blindness are demonstrated in one table, which is what makes it worth keeping. Going in: pruning wrecks generation while PPL and accuracy hold. Coming out: the repair drives CREP from 7.3 to 0.57 and MAUVE from 0.61 to 0.73 while perplexity gets 1.43 points worse and zero-shot accuracy stays flat. So perplexity does not merely fail to detect the damage; on the repair it moves in the wrong direction. Anyone selecting a recovery recipe by validation perplexity would reject the configuration that fixes the model. This is perplexity-does-not-rank-quantization-formats arriving from the pruning side, and it is the sharper version — there the metric was uninformative, here it is actively misleading.

The mechanism is a dynamical decomposition rather than a hand-wave, and it is testable in one experiment. Treating decode as a process over n-gram contexts, degeneration factors into entry risk into a repetition-prone region and persistence once inside, with P(Coverage ≥ τ) ≤ R_T · ρ̄^{ℓ_τ} — entry enters linearly, persistence exponentiated by the run length the coverage threshold requires. Prediction: a small reduction in persistence should suppress long loops even if entry is untouched. Test: at the onset of the first loop, replace only the first two tokens with an alternative from the model's own top-2 at the same prefix. CREP falls 5.9% → 0.7% under sampling and 26.6% → 10.8% greedy. Two in-distribution tokens. The failure is concentrated in a handful of loop-sensitive contexts near onset, not diffused through the model.

Why pruning specifically produces this is also named: under the capacity constraint pruning imposes, a student minimizing forward KL against a multi-modal teacher mode-averages — each token contributes proportionally to teacher probability, so the low-mass alternatives that would let a trajectory escape a loop receive almost no weight. That is a claim about recovery distillation, not about pruning alone, which is the part this map should sit with: the standard recovery stage is not neutral, and its loss function has a specific failure it induces. Read against lora-recovery-after-pruning-is-task-specific-not-restorative — where recovery redistributes capability rather than restoring it, with the sign varying per task inside one checkpoint — the picture is consistent: the recovery stage is a second, unmeasured intervention with its own damage profile, and reporting a single post-recovery number hides both.

Practitioner form. If you prune, add a generation-quality metric before you accept the result — CREP or any repeated-n-gram coverage score is cheap and needs no labels — and do not select a recovery recipe on perplexity. The vault-level generalization is the one a-compressed-model-can-hold-its-score-by-answering-one-class already argues: a compression that preserves its score can still have destroyed the behaviour the score was standing in for, and the third independent instance of that pattern now sits on this map.

What it does not settle. Repetition is decoding-sensitive and every arm here runs one configuration (p=0.9, or greedy); no sweep over temperature, p or repetition penalty is reported, so how much of the pruned excess a tuned sampler would absorb is unknown — and a practitioner's first move on seeing loops is to tune the sampler. There is no unpruned control run through the repair objectives, so whether these help any model or specifically repair a pruning-induced defect is not separated. No code link, no human evaluation, and the escape-mass quantity the theory turns on is inferred from the two-token intervention rather than measured directly.
