The cache belongs to the family, not to the model

Every other entry on this map treats the KV cache as a budget belonging to one model: what to evict, how to reconstruct what eviction removed, which axis to compress, how few bits per entry. This asks a question none of them pose — whether the cache is portable. Production stacks swap between sizes in a family for cost-quality cascading and mid-conversation escalation, and each swap makes the receiver re-prefill from scratch.

It turns out the relationship is substantially linear. On Qwen3 14B→32B, a single source layer explains 56% of the variance in the target's keys and 32% in its values; using several source layers, 79% and 65%. That is enough structure for a closed-form fit: per-head ridge regression (λ = 0.01) on 500 FineWeb-Edu sequences of 1,024 tokens, with RoPE stripped from the keys so the map lives in content space and is reusable at any context length. No training, no gradient.

Three things make it work, and the ablation ranks them unambiguously. Cross-layer source selection is the whole method — collapsing to a single source layer (k=1) costs 35.89 HellaSwag points, against 5.31 for dropping inference-time RoPE and 18.44 for dropping both cross-layer and ridge. A target layer is not a function of the corresponding source layer; it is a function of several.

The failure mode is task-shaped, and the shape is the vault's most-repeated one. Read the retention table by column. WinoGrande holds 74.0–98.5% and HellaSwag 58.7–97.6% across every pair including the collapsed ones. GSM8K runs 95.6% → 68.8% → 36.6% → 18.2% → 3.2% → 1.6%. There is a pair (Llama 3.1 8B→70B) whose HellaSwag retention is 94.4% and whose GSM8K retention is 18.2%. Multi-step arithmetic is where a slightly-wrong cache stops being slightly wrong — the same signature silent-failures-quantized-reasoning found for weight quantization, reached here by an unrelated perturbation of an unrelated tensor.

Why this pushes back. kv-reuse-must-be-exact-prefix-to-stay-lossless sorted serving levers into "exact, enable freely" and "approximate, measure before trusting," and put non-prefix reuse in the second bucket on a single characterized data point (PromptCache's 43% drop, cited in a competitor's related work). This is the first systematic measurement of inexact reuse, and the picture is bimodal, not uniformly bad: four pairs at 73–98%, two at 41–44%, and the split is largely rescuable — swapping the ridge map for an MLP lifts the two failures by +24.3 and +36.8 HellaSwag points while costing 0.3–1.5 on the pairs that already worked. The exactness ordering survives as a default; what falls is the implication that approximate reuse is a category to avoid rather than a curve to price.

Bounded, and the authors say most of it. Calibration is single-domain (FineWeb-Edu only). The layer count k is selected on the same benchmarks the results are reported on — the one hyperparameter that most affects the headline is not held out. The matched-KV precondition (shared KV head count and per-head dimension) is empirical rather than derived. Scope is within-family, dense, full-attention models. And this vault adds the structural one: the premise is a fleet holding two family members live, which is not a configuration anyone with one consumer card is in — if both models fit, the transfer is unnecessary; if they do not, there is nothing to transfer between.
