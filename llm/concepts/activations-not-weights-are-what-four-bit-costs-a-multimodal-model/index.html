The weight-only regime is cheap for a reason, and this is the measurement of the reason

This vault runs on weight-only quantization because that is what a single card serves: W4A16, memory-bound, activations left alone. The justification has been mostly structural — whether-a-rotation-helps-depends-on-the-number-format-not-the-bit-width argues the transform stage is optional in W4A16 and earns its keep in W4A4, and activation-outliers-are-the-barrier-to-w8a8 names activations as the obstacle at eight bits. What has been missing is a controlled price on the two halves separately. This supplies one, on multimodal models, and the ratio is larger than the vault has been assuming.

W4A8 costs 0.7–0.8%. W4A4 costs 7.1–7.2%. Same weight precision, same models, same 5B-token training budget, same hyperparameters — the entire difference is what happens to the activations, and it is roughly a factor of nine. MXFP8 is near-lossless (0.2–0.3%). The mechanism is measured rather than asserted: activation tensors have substantially wider dynamic ranges and more heterogeneous distributions than weights, extreme outliers expand the FP4 range, and the effective precision left to the majority of activation values collapses — small-magnitude activations underflow. Weight distributions, in the paper's phrase, "remain comparatively compact and well-behaved."

Two things follow that this map should hold onto.

Format matters enormously at four bits, and the gap is not small. HiF4's multi-level hierarchical scaling over 64-element blocks costs 2.7–3.8% where MXFP4's single shared block exponent over 32-element blocks costs 7.1–7.2% — the same nominal bit-width, between half and a third of the damage, on identical models. This is whether-a-rotation-helps-depends-on-the-number-format-not-the-bit-width's argument arriving as a direct measurement: "4-bit" is not a specification, and the metadata structure around the four bits is doing more work than the four bits.

The correction is a residual, and it is the third instance of that pattern in this vault. Residual Fallback Quantization decomposes each activation into a primary FP4 representation plus an explicit quantization error ΔX = X − X̃, re-quantizes the residual through a second low-bit pathway, and accumulates its contribution inside the GEMM — no threshold selection, no full-precision fallback, both paths FP4-compatible, differentiable so it trains rather than calibrates. It recovers most of the loss: MXFP4 7.50% → 1.14% and HiF4 2.89% → 0.61% on Wan2.2, with the same shape on Qwen3-VL, and downstream reasoning accuracy returning to within half a point of BF16 on three of four benchmarks. Coding the residual rather than raising the precision is the same move the weight-side sidecar literature makes, arriving independently on the activation side.

Where this does not reach, and it is a long way from a consumer card. Every number costs a 5-billion-token quantization-aware fine-tuning run on a 5B or 30B model. This is not a post-training recipe and the paper does not offer one — its own related-work section separates itself from SmoothQuant-style PTQ precisely because calibration statistics are static and these outliers evolve during training. The formats measured (MXFP4, HiF4) require hardware support consumer cards do not currently have. No code release, two models, no seeds or intervals. And the Wan2.2 evaluation arm is genuinely hard to read as a quality ordering — the largest single change is Dynamic Degree 43.00 → 50.00 against a 45.00 BF16 reference, i.e. the correction overshoots the baseline, which is what a video-liveliness metric does when it is not monotone in quality. The Qwen3-VL reasoning table is the load-bearing evidence; the generation table is supporting colour.

The same activation-outlier mechanism carries the source's other finding, and that one runs against this map rather than with it: the outliers are largest in vision-related layers, which is how the paper reaches the modality inversion in under-four-bit-training-the-vision-tower-is-the-fragile-half. The two notes are one measurement read on two axes — precision type and modality — and the second is why this map is now contested.

What generalizes without the method or the hardware is the accounting. The cheapness of 4-bit that this vault is built on is a property of leaving activations alone, and it is now priced: about a tenth of the cost of the full four-bit move. Anyone reading a "4-bit" claim should establish which side it applies to before comparing it with anything here.
