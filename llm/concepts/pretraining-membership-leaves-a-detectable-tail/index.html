Pretraining membership leaves a detectable tail

This map has named four ways a benchmark number lies and, until now, offered no way to test for the first of them — leakage — without owning the pretraining corpus. Min-K% Prob is that test, and its design is one asymmetric observation: a passage the model has seen should contain no very-low-probability tokens, whereas an unseen passage is likely to contain a few. So discard the sequence average, keep the worst 20% of tokens, and score those.

Two properties make it usable where the alternatives are not. It requires no corpus access — the prior state of the art fit a reference model on data similar to the target's pretraining set, which for any model you did not train is unavailable. And it requires no training: it runs against log-probs.

The benchmark is arguably the larger contribution. WIKIMIA exploits the one externally verifiable fact about pretraining data — time. Wikipedia articles predating 2017 are members; 394 articles about post-2023 events are guaranteed non-members. That construction is reusable for any model with a disclosed cutoff, which is what makes the AUC numbers mean anything. Evaluated across LLaMA 7B/13B/30B/65B, GPT-NeoX-20B, Pythia-2.8B and OPT-66B at passage lengths 32–256.

For this vault the important number is not 0.72 but the gap to 0.86: general-purpose membership inference is hard, while detecting a specific eval set you suspect entered a specific continued pretraining or fine-tuning run is substantially easier, because detectability scales with learning rate and with how often the example appeared. That is precisely the situation of a practitioner fine-tuning open weights on their own mix — so the instrument is most reliable where this vault's readers would reach for it, and least reliable when interrogating a frontier checkpoint about a public benchmark.

It also makes runnable an audit distillation-small-models has wanted since 2026-07-16. Distillation sets are teacher generations, teachers have seen the benchmarks, and "contamination audits of distill sets" has sat unsourced ever since. Nothing here runs that audit; before this, nothing made it possible without the teacher's corpus.

Caveats that bound the claim hard: paraphrasing degrades every method tested including this one, and in-the-wild contamination frequently arrives paraphrased. Detectability requires repetition and a non-trivial learning rate, so a benchmark seen once in a trillion tokens is close to invisible. WIKIMIA's members and non-members differ in era and topic as well as in membership, so some separable signal may be distribution shift. Models tested top out at 65B and are all 2022–2023 vintage, none trained at modern token counts with modern deduplication. And k=20 was chosen by validation sweep — a tuned hyperparameter on a detector sold as assumption-free.
