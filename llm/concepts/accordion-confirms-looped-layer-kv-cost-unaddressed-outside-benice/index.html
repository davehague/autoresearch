A second, independent looped architecture — one day after BeNice's fix landed — confirms the per-recurrence KV-cache tax is not a quirk of one design, and ships without the fix

benice-kv-sharing-collapses-looped-model-recurrence-memory (ingested 2026-10-24) showed that looped/recurrent-depth transformers otherwise pay for a separate KV cache at every recurrence of a reused block, and fixed it via stochastic-depth pretraining plus a broadcast-and-adapt post-training stage. Accordion (ingested 2026-10-26, submitted one day after BeNice, no cross-citation) is a different looping scheme — a fixed contiguous prefix window repeated a variable number of times, rather than one block repeated uniformly — built independently at Tsinghua/Baidu and carried to 3.6T pretraining tokens plus SFT and RL on a released multi-checkpoint model line. Its own architecture section states the same cost in the same shape: "the second attention pass maintains a separate key-value (KV) cache built from its own activations... KV storage scales as (L+k)/L relative to k=0: 1.25x at k=6 and 1.5x at k=12" — and stops there. No cache-sharing, broadcast, or reconstruction mechanism appears anywhere in the paper's design-choice ablations.

This is evidence of generality on two fronts kv-cache-management didn't have before: the KV-cache-multiplication problem recurs across at least two independently-designed looping schemes (uniform single-block repetition and prefix-window repetition), not just the one BeNice studied; and at least one looped architecture that has cleared pretraining-to-RL at real scale and shipped checkpoints does so while still paying the unfixed cost — meaning BeNice's fix has not yet propagated to even the next looped-architecture paper published, let alone practice.

What stays open. Whether BeNice's broadcast-and-adapt mechanism is even compatible with Accordion's prefix-window structure is untested either way — repeating a window of layers rather than a single block changes what "broadcast the final recurrence's cache into the first" would mean mechanically. Accordion's own KV figures are analytical, not measured memory/latency, so the real-world size of the tax this architecture is paying is not directly comparable to BeNice's measured 23.6% throughput / 83.6% p90-TTFT figures.
