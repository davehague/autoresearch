Sengupta, Wang and Brunswic measure whether that equal-step assumption matches the loss surface's actual local curvature, using held-out gradients and Hessian-vector products in a 124M-parameter decoder-only Transformer trained on FineWeb-Edu. It does not: the loss-optimal step multiplier climbs steadily from the top (highest-curvature) singular directions to the tail (flattest) ones, meaning Muon's flat step size is simultaneously too large where the loss bends sharply and too small where it barely bends at all.
