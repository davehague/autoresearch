CoS-VQ's fix has three parts: (1) multi-scale candidate construction -- build each position's candidate codeword pool from top-K codewords evaluated at several probe scales (not just the current one), so a codeword that ranks poorly now but would win after a scale shift stays available; (2) a residual-aware tangent majorizer -- a block-diagonal upper bound (via Cauchy-Schwarz) that replaces the true cross-position quadratic coupling with an independent (separable) one, turning the joint objective into a sum of per-position quadratics in the scale; (3) lower-envelope interval search -- under the separable surrogate, each candidate's cost becomes an affine line in 1/scale, so finding the best joint assignment reduces to taking the lower envelope of these lines and merging their switching points into finitely many scale intervals (growing linearly in candidate-pool size, not exponentially in combinations), each with a closed-form optimal scale.
