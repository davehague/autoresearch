The GGUF quant ladder is graded, and the damage is task-shaped

The single-model, 13-format sweep that finally puts numbers on a menu local users have been choosing by folklore. Two shapes come out of it.

Down the ladder, the loss is flat until 3-bit and then partial. Q8_0, Q6_K, Q5_x and the 4-bit K-quants all land within about half an aggregate point of FP16's 69.47 — Q4_K_M posts 69.15 at 69.4% smaller, Q4_K_S 69.17. Only at 3-bit does anything move, and even there Q3_K_L (68.78) gives back most of what Q3_K_S (65.49) loses for four percentage points of file size. "3-bit is broken" is not what the data says; "the small 3-bit variant is broken and the large one is usable" is.

Across tasks, the loss is not uniform, and the spread is the more useful finding. At Q3_K_S, GSM8K drops 9.3 points (77.63 → 68.31), IFEval 5.0, MMLU 4.2 — while HellaSwag moves 0.6 and TruthfulQA 0.7. Multi-step arithmetic and instruction compliance are where precision is load-bearing; single-step commonsense retrieval is not. So the practitioner rule is conditional on workload rather than global: a quant level that is free for a chat or classification workload can cost a tenth of a math benchmark.

Map-level consequence: this is the first in-vault evidence that post-training-quantization's 4-bit default — established via AWQ, GPTQ and QLoRA, all GPU methods with bespoke kernels — survives translation into the K-quant construction people actually download. It also reads against quantization-aware-init-extends-lora-below-the-4-bit-floor's finding from the fine-tuning side: LoftQ showed part of the sub-3-bit collapse was an artifact of how the adapter was initialized, not of the weights; this shows the inference-only 3-bit penalty is real but far smaller than the QLoRA-at-2.5-bit failure implied. Both agree the cliff is not where bit-counting puts it.

Scope: one model, one size, one CPU machine, and a preprint with no venue — the shape is the claim, not the decimals. The IQ series and MXFP4 rungs were not tested, and nothing here is long-context or KV-cache quantization.
