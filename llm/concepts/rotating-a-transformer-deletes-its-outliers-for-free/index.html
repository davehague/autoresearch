The outlier basis is arbitrary, so change it

This vault's outlier doctrine has been locate and accommodate since the seed ingest. salient-channels-carry-quantization-error says the activation distribution tells you which ~1% of channels carry the error, so protect them. activation-outliers-are-the-barrier-to-w8a8 and quantization-difficulty-is-transferable-between-weights-and-activations say the same difficulty can be divided out of the activation and multiplied into the paired weight row. Both take the outlier channels as given and work around them. A third family keeps them in higher precision outright — QUIK retains 256 outlier features, Atom 128 — at the price of a mixed-precision kernel.

Rotation says the premise is wrong. Outlier concentration is a statement about the basis the activations are expressed in, and a transformer's basis is not load-bearing. Computational invariance: in a pre-norm architecture, multiplying an input-side weight matrix on the left by an orthogonal Q and the corresponding output-side matrix on the right by Qᵀ leaves the network's function unchanged, because RMSNorm divides each row by its norm and a rotation preserves norms — RMSNorm(X) = RMSNorm(XQᵀ)Q. (The per-channel scale RMSNorm normally applies is first absorbed into the neighbouring weights so the normalization is pure.) Pick Q to be a randomized Hadamard matrix and the rotation is also incoherence processing: it mixes every coordinate into every other, so a spike in one channel becomes a small contribution to all of them. The activations between blocks come out with no outliers to protect, and the rotation costs nothing at runtime because it lives inside the weights.

Some of it does not fuse and must run online — one Hadamard before the FFN down-projection, one head-wise transform on values, and one on queries and keys after RoPE, since a positional rotation sits between W_k and the attention score and blocks fusion there. The total is 1.5 Hadamard transforms per layer at O(d log₂ d) each.

The evidence that removal beats accommodation is a single table read across its rows. At 4-bit weights, activations and KV cache on LLaMA-2-7B: the scaling method collapses to perplexity 83.12 from a 5.47 baseline, the reconstruction-plus-outlier-retention method reaches 8.87 while holding 256 channels in high precision, and rotation reaches 6.10 while holding none. Per-channel scaling was engineered for 8 bits and does not extend downward; rotation does.

Three things follow for the map. The general statement replaces two narrower ones: the enemy is outlier concentration, and concentration is basis-dependent — which subsumes both the protect-the-salient-channels and the migrate-the-difficulty findings as basis-preserving special cases. Second, it retro-explains a coincidence the vault recorded on 2026-07-20, when FlashAttention-3 was found reaching for the same random orthogonal rotation to quantize attention activations in-kernel: that is the same theorem applied transiently rather than fused, and the trick now appears in three unrelated places — weight quantization, activation quantization, and attention kernels. Third, it has structural preconditions worth stating, since they are architectural rather than incidental: pre-norm RMSNorm (a rescaling normalization breaks the commutation) and power-of-two head counts and head dimensions for the Kronecker construction that spans heads.
