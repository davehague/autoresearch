A stage that selects among behaviours gets nothing from a base that has none

The interesting half of this result is not the threshold, it is the variable. Parameter count does not predict whether PPO helps: Pythia-410M (405M parameters) improves markedly on TinyStories and regresses significantly on Wikitext-103, while SmolLM2-360M — fewer parameters — improves on both. What separates the cells is the perplexity of the supervised prior on that corpus. Pythia-410M reads 6.5 on TinyStories and 25.4 on Wikitext-103; SmolLM2-360M reads 5.3 and 16.7. The regression sits above the threshold and the gains sit below it.

The stated mechanism is distributional: a fluent prior keeps sampled rollouts inside the reward model's training distribution, so the reward signal is informative; a weak prior produces samples the reward model has no opinion about, and the resulting gradient is indistinguishable from noise.

This is the same claim alignment-is-format-selection-not-knowledge-acquisition makes from the data side, and neither source cites the other. LIMA argued that capabilities and knowledge are acquired almost entirely in pretraining and that alignment merely selects which subdistribution of response formats to use — evidenced by 1,000 curated examples on a 65B base beating the same base trained on 52× more distilled data. If post-training selects among behaviours the base already has, then a base with no fluent behaviours to select among gains nothing, and the amount of gain available should track how much the base already does. That is exactly the measured relationship, at 70M–500M instead of 65B, from the RL side instead of the SFT side. Two findings, opposite ends of the scale range, one claim about where capability lives.

It is also a rare legitimate use of perplexity, and this vault should say so out loud. The standing rule here is that perplexity is valid evidence for training health and invalid for model selection — stated when rsLoRA offered only fine-tuning perplexity for a better-model claim, and again when four llama.cpp formats spanned 0.03 PPL and 3.4 GSM8K points (perplexity-does-not-rank-quantization-formats). PPL_SFT < 20 is not ranking models. It is predicting whether a subsequent training stage will work, which is training health precisely. The vault has spent two weeks discounting perplexity claims; this is the case where the proxy is the right instrument, and recording it keeps the rule a rule rather than a reflex.

Practitioner form, and it costs minutes on one card: before spending anything on preference optimization, measure your SFT checkpoint's perplexity on the target corpus. If it is high, the budget belongs in SFT data, not in a reward model.

Graded suggestive rather than strong: the threshold is a landmark on one plot over fifteen points, the preference pairs are synthetic (which is the most likely explanation for the Wikitext regression and caps how discriminative the reward model can be), the PPO budget is 250 steps, and Bradley–Terry additive invariance means the reward deltas are comparable only within a configuration. The variable is the durable finding; the constant 20 is not.
