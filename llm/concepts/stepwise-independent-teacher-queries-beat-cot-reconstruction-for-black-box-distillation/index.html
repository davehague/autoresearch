This map's teacher-selection and teacher-switching threads assume the teacher's internal chain-of-thought is at least inspectable even when its logits aren't -- this source is the first to build and validate a distillation method for the harder, commercially realistic case where even the CoT is hidden, and shows final-answer correctness and intermediate-step validity are separable supervision signals a final-answer-filtered dataset throws away

TL;DR: This map's recent arrivals (Entropy Shape Correlation teacher selection, farthest-KL mid-rollout teacher switching, on-policy-distillation calibration across merged specialists) all presuppose some access to the teacher beyond a bare final answer -- entropy shapes, KL divergences, or per-teacher loss scales. Stepwise-Querying targets the case directly below that: a commercial teacher API that returns only a candidate answer and a brief numbered solution sketch, with no logits and no observable internal reasoning. Rather than trying to reconstruct the hidden CoT holistically (the field's prior approach, represented here by the Trace Inversion baseline), it queries the teacher once per claimed step to re-derive, validate and correct that step independently -- and the resulting step-assembled supervision beats three existing baselines in 14 of 16 student-teacher-benchmark cells and beats training on the teacher's own exposed CoT/answer-explanation in all 16. The more structurally interesting result for this map is narrower: on GSM8K, trajectories whose final answer is wrong but whose intermediate steps validate are worth keeping (+0.6-1.5 points over correct-answer-only training), while trajectories with invalid intermediate steps hurt even when correct-labeled -- step validity and final-answer correctness are shown to be separable axes of supervisory value, which is new information for a map whose contamination-audit backlog item has so far reasoned about final-answer correctness and sampling-temperature brittleness, not step-level validity.

What it says. The teacher first emits a candidate final answer and a short numbered solution. Each numbered step is then independently re-queried -- shown the problem and the initial brief solution, asked to derive that specific step on its own, and to validate/correct it -- without seeing how other steps' queries were answered. The assembled, consistency-checked, filtered result becomes the SFT target; a step-valid trajectory with a wrong final-answer label can still be retained rather than discarded. Tested on two students (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct), two teachers (DeepSeek-V4-Pro, GLM-5.2), and four benchmarks (MATH-500, GSM8K, HumanEval, LiveCodeBench) against Trace Inversion (reconstructs a trace from question/answer/optional summary), Answer-Conditioned CoT (generation conditioned on the answer), and GLOW (sample reweighting by loss reduction). The companion diagnostic, SEIG (Stepwise Explanation Information Gain -- a frozen student's own completion-probability log-ratio under stepwise vs. global explanations, needing no teacher logits), is positive in 25/32 teacher-student-dataset combinations at a modest sampling budget (K=16) and in all 32 at a larger one (K>=24), offering this map a cheap, teacher-logit-free way to check whether a given stepwise-supervision scheme is actually adding information before training on it -- a check this map's other teacher-selection metrics (Entropy Shape Correlation) perform with access to teacher internals SEIG does not require.

Caveats. The paper itself states the SEIG-to-downstream-gain relationship needs separate validation -- unlike Entropy Shape Correlation elsewhere in this map, which was validated against actual training outcomes (rank agreement 1.00), SEIG here is reported as suggestive. The step-validity/final-answer-correctness separation is demonstrated on GSM8K only, not generalized across the other three benchmarks in this paper. Only two teachers, two students and four math/code benchmarks are tested -- no code-only or non-math-reasoning domain, and no test of whether this composes with this map's teacher-selection or teacher-switching findings (an untested composition, in the pattern this map has flagged repeatedly for new distillation arrivals). Per-step teacher querying multiplies API calls per training problem; the authors flag the resulting cost as open rather than measured. Code/dataset release is not stated.
