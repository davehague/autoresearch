The map's transfer-mechanism strand gets a decomposition of what the teacher signal actually carries — separable correctness and behavior components — sitting alongside, not resolving, its entropy-suppression and routed-transport findings

This map's transfer-mechanism strand (strand 2) has been accumulating accounts of what moves from teacher to student in on-policy self-distillation: distributional novelty over teacher rank (distillation-gain-tracks-novelty-not-teacher-rank), an entropy-suppression account that traces most of OPD's gain to suppressing low-probability tokens regardless of signal accuracy (on-policy-distillation-gains-are-mostly-entropy-suppression-not-transfer), and a routed-transport account that the destination of moved probability mass matters (routed-transport-of-teacher-student-disagreement-beats-sampled-reverse-kl-in-on-policy-distillation). This source adds a different cut on the same question: rather than asking what content moves, it asks what a privileged-conditioning teacher signal actually contains, and argues it is two entangled things — a correctness-relevant component and a behavioral-shift component (response length, thinking-mode switching) — that standard self-distillation does not separate. Isolating each (attractive-only, repulsive-only) shows each imports its own behavioral cost; contrasting the two is proposed as a way to cancel the shared behavioral part while keeping the correctness-relevant difference.

This sits in the same unresolved-tension space the map already carries between the entropy-suppression account and RouteOPD's destination-specificity findings (recorded 2026-09-10): neither of those papers ran this paper's attractive-vs-repulsive manipulation, and this paper does not run either of theirs, so the three accounts of "what the teacher signal is doing" are recorded side by side rather than reconciled. Worth noting for a reader connecting strands: this source's mechanism (behavioral drift as an entanglement problem) is a different question from yesterday's self-retiring-teacher finding (self-retiring-teacher-outperforms-continuous-opd-supervision, 2026-09-19), which addresses when to stop supervision rather than what the supervision signal conflates — the two could interact (a teacher's behavioral-drift contribution might change over training the way its correctness contribution does) but neither paper tests the other's axis.

Graded extends: adds a new account of teacher-signal composition without contesting the map's existing entropy-suppression or routed-transport findings. Graded suggestive: results are reported as training/evaluation curves rather than quantified deltas with confidence intervals, tested at a single model size (Qwen3-4B) on reasoning/math tasks only, and the authors themselves frame the balancing of the two teacher signals and scaling to larger models as future work rather than a settled recipe.

Rebutted by: none — this extends the map's transfer-mechanism strand with a new account of signal composition rather than contesting a standing claim.
