The number goes up while the model gets narrower, and entropy will not tell you either

This map's transfer strand already holds pass@K collapse as a contested question, established from primary sources: on-policy-distillation-buys-sampling-efficiency-not-capability, on-policy-distillation-degenerates-without-a-leash-to-the-initial-policy, self-training-corrupts-what-it-already-solved. What a review adds — and the only thing it can add, having run nothing — is the observation that these are the same phenomenon under different names, and a statement of what instrument detects it.

The instrument claim is the portable part. Mean score is not merely insensitive to collapse; it moves the wrong way while collapse happens — pass@1 71.9 → 73.4 as pass@16 falls 83.6 → 78.5. And the obvious backstop fails too: the same collapsed model registers higher token entropy than a reinforcement-learning comparison while showing lower functional diversity. So the cheap proxy a practitioner would reach for is not just noisy, it is anti-correlated in at least one documented case. Pass@k over a fixed sampling budget is the measurement, and it is the one nobody reports.

The three-level decomposition is a useful vocabulary rather than a finding: behavioural (pass@k flattens, out-of-domain drops), token-level (entropy → 0), geometric (one mode per problem). Its value is that papers naming only one level have been read as describing different failures.

The causal split is worth keeping because it bounds the blame. One family predates self-distillation entirely — the reinforcement-learning gradient erodes entropy, and finite sampling removes distribution tails before it removes modes — so collapse is not an artifact of removing the teacher. The family that is specific is mechanical and specific: conditioning the teacher on the reference solution makes it an oracle, an oracle suppresses deliberation tokens, and the student inherits the missing uncertainty. That is a sharper statement of the same shape as distillation-gain-tracks-novelty-not-teacher-rank — what the student gets is set by what is new in the signal, and an over-informed teacher emits a signal with the deliberation removed.

It is included because the instrument claim is decision-relevant and independently checkable, not because a review is evidence. The scope is mathematical reasoning only, the underlying work is nearly all preprints under six months old, and the experiments beneath it are mostly one model family at ≤8B — a confound the review itself flags in its benchmark section and then builds on anyway.
