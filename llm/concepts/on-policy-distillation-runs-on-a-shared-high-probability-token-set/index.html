Ninety-nine percent of the mass sits on tokens they already agree about

On-policy distillation matches the student's logits to the teacher's at states the student itself visits. The intuition is that the teacher supplies a dense per-token reward where reinforcement learning supplies one scalar at the end, and that this density is where the efficiency comes from. This measures what the density actually consists of.

Across training in successful runs, the token-overlap ratio — the fraction of each model's top-k that the other also ranks in its top-k — climbs from about 72% to 91%, the entropy gap narrows, and those overlapping tokens carry 97–99% of the total probability mass for both models. In failing runs the overlap is stagnant from initialization and never moves.

The ablation makes the causal claim. Restrict optimization to the overlap set and the run matches standard on-policy distillation; restrict it to the symmetric difference — the tokens where the two models disagree about what is even plausible — and it is substantially weaker. "Optimizing only the overlap region is sufficient to recover nearly the full benefit."

Two things follow, and both are cheap.

The overlap ratio is a free early-stopping diagnostic. It is computable from quantities the training loop already has, it separates successful from failing runs at initialization, and the paper's companion finding is that early mismatch cannot be recovered later (distillation-gain-tracks-novelty-not-teacher-rank). So a run whose overlap is flat after the first phase should be killed rather than continued — which for a practitioner distilling on one or two cards is the difference between a wasted afternoon and a wasted week.

And the tail is mostly wasted compute. Divergence computed over the full top-k spends most of its budget on tokens holding 1–3% of the mass and contributing little. Nothing in the paper builds a system around that, and it is a straightforward efficiency lever nobody has taken.

The repair the paper recommends operates directly on this quantity: off-policy SFT on teacher rollouts before on-policy distillation begins raises the initial overlap ratio by 15–20 percentage points, and the advantage "persists throughout training, indicating that the off-policy cold start improves not only early optimization, but also the final performance ceiling." That is the two-stage ordering strong-to-weak-distillation-is-the-small-model-build-path already records as shipped practice, arrived at here from mechanism by a group with no stake in it.

Bounded to mathematical reasoning and 1.5B–7B students, like everything else in this source.
