Vision-language models (architecture & adaptation) — map

Coined 2026-07-18 (auto run) at emerging — the first evidence in the "Multi-modal & edge" group, which had been empty since the vault was seeded. Anchored on one T1 (LLaVA / visual instruction tuning). Auto-coined; flagged for David's review.

Status: emerging

What we believe now:

  • Adding a modality is an adapter-shaped problem, not a pretraining-shaped one. LLaVA connects a frozen CLIP ViT-L/14 to an open LLM (Vicuna) through a single trainable linear projection that maps image patch features into the LLM's token embedding space — the image becomes a sequence of tokens the model reads. No cross-attention surgery, no joint pretraining. Both heavy components are downloaded, not trained. [T1]
  • The training recipe is two staged unfreezes. (1) Feature alignment: freeze the vision encoder and the LLM, train only the projection on image-caption pairs. (2) Instruction tuning: unfreeze the projection and the LLM together on multimodal instruction data. [T1]
  • The cost lands inside fine-tuning economics, not cluster economics. LLaVA-1.5 reports full training on ~1.2M public pairs in ~1 day on a single 8×A100 node — the same order of resource as the text fine-tunes in peft-methods, which is what makes VLM adaptation reachable for a practitioner rather than a lab. [T1]
  • The corpus can be manufactured by a teacher that cannot perceive the modality. There was no multimodal instruction corpus, so language-only GPT-4 was fed symbolic proxies for images (COCO captions + bounding boxes, never pixels) and wrote 158K conversations, descriptions, and reasoning chains. Reported results: 85.1% relative score vs GPT-4 on the authors' multimodal chat benchmark; 92.53% on ScienceQA (SOTA at publication). [T1, but see the eval caveat]
  • Discount the headline numbers. The 85.1% is GPT-4 judging a model trained on GPT-4-authored data against a GPT-4-authored benchmark — a closed loop of exactly the kind evaluation-contamination exists to flag. Read the result as "the pipeline works," not as a calibrated capability level. [T1, downgraded read]

Open questions:

  • n=1, and dated. One T1 from April 2023. LLaVA-1.5 (MLP projection, academic VQA data) and LLaVA-NeXT (higher input resolution) are cited by the source but not ingested, and the open-weights VLM field has moved substantially since (Qwen-VL, InternVL, Llama-Vision, Molmo-class releases with open data). The pattern is what this map currently asserts; the configuration is folklore until graded sources land.
  • Does PEFT transfer cleanly to the vision half? weight-decomposition-recovers-lora-gap validated DoRA on LLaVA, so adapters work on the LLM side — but whether the projection and the vision encoder want the same treatment, and whether the contested rank findings in peft-methods apply to cross-modal alignment (plausibly a high-rank update), is unmeasured.
  • Honest VLM evaluation has no in-vault source. Every number here is teacher-judged. What a decontaminated, task-specific VLM harness looks like — the multimodal version of evaluation-contamination's operating rule — is the gap that blocks any "beats frontier on X" claim in this modality.
  • The edge half of this group is still empty. Quantizing and deploying a VLM (a vision encoder plus an LLM, two very different weight distributions), on-device constraints, and the compress→convert→deploy pipeline have no sources. Whether the GGUF/llama.cpp path in local-inference handles the vision tower well is unknown here.
  • Synthetic-data quality control is unablated. LLaVA reports that the corpus worked, not which generation choices mattered — the discipline data-curation establishes for pretraining data has no counterpart here, and the data inherits an unmeasured share of the teacher's hallucinations.
