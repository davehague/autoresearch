Data curation (filtering, dedup, decontamination, selection) — map

Coined 2026-07-17 (auto run) at emerging — first evidence in the data-quality thread the vault had flagged as an open gap across two groups ("pretraining data & objectives"; "data curation & synthetic data & decontamination"). Anchored on one strong, reproducible, method-legible T1 (FineWeb); the fine-tuning-data and synthetic-data halves are still unsourced. Auto-coined; flagged for David's review.

Status: consolidating (emerging → consolidating 2026-07-25) Status note (2026-07-25): the map tips on lima-less-is-more-for-alignment (Meta AI et al., NeurIPS 2023, T1), which closes the gap this map has named since coining — "fine-tuning / instruction-data curation proper still has no dedicated in-vault source" — and closes it with the same instrument FineWeb used one stage earlier: controlled ablations over data variants with the models released. What tips the status is not a second method but a principle-level replication. Two T1s, two independent groups that do not cite each other, two different stages of the pipeline (15T pretraining tokens; 1,000 instruction examples), both concluding that selection dominates scale — and both inverting the intuitive lever, FineWeb by showing aggressive global dedup hurts, LIMA by showing a 16× increase in example count buys nothing measurable. That is a stronger form of agreement than two papers replicating one technique. Honest qualifier: the two share no methodology beyond "train the variants and measure," which is what makes the agreement meaningful and also means nothing here is a direct reproduction — and LIMA's ablations are 7B models graded by GPT-3.5-turbo, so this map's newest numbers carry evaluation-contamination's judge discount.

What we believe now:

  • Quality and diversity are the levers; quantity saturates — and this now holds at both ends of the pipeline (new 2026-07-25). At fine-tuning scale, with everything else matched, the three variables separate cleanly: response-quality filtering is worth +0.50 (2,000 filtered Stack Exchange examples 3.83 vs 2,000 unfiltered 3.33), prompt diversity is worth +0.34 (heterogeneous Stack Exchange 3.83 vs equally-well-answered but homogeneous wikiHow 3.49), and scaling the example count 16× from 2K to 32K moves nothing at all (flat at ~3.8). The filters that earn the half point are unglamorous and specific — answer score ≥10, length between 1200 and 4096 characters, drop first-person or cross-referencing answers, strip links and images. Practitioner consequence, and the most directly actionable claim on this map: given a fixed curation budget, spend it filtering what you have and broadening the prompt distribution, not collecting more of the same. [T1]
  • Alignment is format selection, not knowledge acquisition — which is why the small-data result is possible at all (new 2026-07-25). The Superficial Alignment Hypothesis: capabilities and knowledge are learned almost entirely in pretraining, and alignment teaches only which subdistribution of response formats to use. Its corollary survives testing — LLaMa 65B on 1,000 curated examples (~750,000 tokens), no RLHF and no reward model, is equal-or-preferred to GPT-4 in 43% of 300 head-to-heads, to Bard in 58%, to RLHF-trained DaVinci003 in 65%, and beats Alpaca-65B — the same base model trained on 52× more distilled data. The sharpest demonstration is multi-turn: a model trained on zero dialogue examples can converse at all, and 30 hand-written chains take excellent responses from 45.2% to 76.1%. Those are the numbers of a capability being invoked, not installed. [T1]
  • Data curation is an engineering variable you can ablate — not a trade secret. The way to know a filtering or dedup choice helps is to train identical proxy models on each data variant and read the downstream benchmark. FineWeb releases the corpus (15T tokens from 96 Common Crawl dumps), the processing code (datatrove), and the ablation models — the method made legible, not just the artifact. It beats other open pretraining corpora on downstream evals. [T1]
  • The intuitive moves are non-monotonic — more filtering/dedup is not always better. Aggressive global (cross-dump) deduplication can hurt: it strips so much that quality drops, and per-dump dedup wins because surviving across dumps is itself a weak quality signal. A default ("dedup as hard as possible") inverted by controlled evidence — exactly the shape this vault's status-moves-both-ways rule exists to catch. [T1]
  • A cheap learned quality filter can beat a much larger unfiltered corpus. A small classifier trained on LLM-rated "educational value" yields FineWeb-Edu (1.3T tokens) whose models show large gains on knowledge/reasoning benchmarks (MMLU, ARC) vs the unfiltered 15T set. Selection can matter more than scale. [T1]

Through-line (newest first):

  • 2026-07-25 — ingest of lima-less-is-more-for-alignment (Zhou, Liu, Xu, … Levy, Meta AI / CMU / USC / Tel Aviv, NeurIPS 2023, T1): the map tips, on the fine-tuning-data ablation it has wanted since coining. The headline everyone remembers — 1,000 examples, no RL, competitive with flagship assistants — is the demonstration; the ablations are the contribution, and they are what makes this a data-curation source rather than an alignment one. Quality +0.50, diversity +0.34, quantity flat over 16×. Read beside data-curation-choices-are-ablatable-and-non-monotonic, the agreement is structural: the same qualitative ranking — selection dominates, volume saturates — at 15T pretraining tokens and at a thousand instruction examples, from groups with nothing in common but the method of asking. Three edges out. To peft-methods, the Superficial Alignment Hypothesis supplies the missing mechanism for that map's live contest: if alignment selects among behaviours already present, a low-rank update is the right shape of intervention, and if the target needs new knowledge neither adapter rank nor curated instruction data is the lever — which is low-rank-capacity-binds-when-the-domain-is-new arriving from the data side. To post-training-rl, a premise-level push-back rather than a mechanism one. To evaluation-contamination, two items: the authors' report that validation perplexity does not track generation quality, and a crowd–GPT-4 agreement of 78–79% against crowd–crowd 82% that independently corroborates MT-Bench. Caveats this map should carry: 2023-vintage baselines, all retired; the quantity ablation samples only Stack Exchange, so it tests "more of the same" and not "a new distribution" — the one case the diversity result predicts would help; ablations at 7B graded by GPT-3.5-turbo; and 230 of 300 test prompts written by the same authors who wrote 200 training examples, with an acknowledged shared-prior leak. See finetuning-data-quality-and-diversity-beat-quantity, alignment-is-format-selection-not-knowledge-acquisition.
  • 2026-07-24 — counterpart edge from min-k-prob-detecting-pretraining-data (T1, primary map evaluation-contamination): decontamination has a supply side and a demand side, and this map only has the supply side. FineWeb removes benchmark text before release and can, because it holds the corpus — the strong position, available to roughly the dozen organizations that build corpora. The demand side is a model consumer interrogating a finished checkpoint they did not build, and it is now sourced: Min-K% Prob recovers membership from the model alone at AUC 0.72, no corpus and no reference model required. The asymmetry is the point and it is structural, not a shortcoming of either method — almost every model this vault's readers fine-tune is one they did not pretrain, so the applicable tool is the weaker one, and a base model's "decontaminated" claim is an assertion its user cannot independently verify. Practical consequence for anyone assembling a fine-tuning mix: the detector works markedly better on your own data (AUC 0.86 when the examples were deliberately inserted, since detectability scales with learning rate and repetition), so the supply-side discipline this map documents is reachable at practitioner scale even though the audit of the base is not. See contamination-detection-is-a-prior-not-a-verdict, pretraining-membership-leaves-a-detectable-tail.
  • 2026-07-19 — interaction edge from molmo-pixmo-open-vlm (T1, primary map vision-language-models): the supply-side counterpart to this map's filtering discipline, and a direct rebuttal of the 2026-07-18 edge below. Where LLaVA manufactured a corpus with a blind teacher, Molmo argues the whole teacher-distillation lineage costs the community its foundational knowledge, and routes around it: annotators speak for 60–90 seconds per image, the audio is transcribed and cleaned by a language-only LLM that never sees the image (712k images, ~1.3M captions). Also PixMo-Docs, where charts are generated from code and the Q&A is derived from the generating code rather than the rendered image. The generalizable move is the one this map should carry forward: before generating synthetic data, ask whether there is a channel where the ground truth already exists — a different modality (speech), a different direction (code → render), or a human act that is cheap in the right format (pointing). Curation starts before the corpus does. See speech-sourced-captions-replace-the-teacher-vlm.
  • 2026-07-18 — interaction edge from llava-visual-instruction-tuning (T1, primary map vision-language-models): the first fine-tuning-data synthesis evidence in-vault, filling half of this map's loudest gap. A corpus that did not exist was manufactured by a teacher that could not perceive the modality — language-only GPT-4 given COCO captions + bounding boxes produced 158K multimodal instruction samples sufficient to train a working assistant. The generalizable claim: data supply is an engineering variable, not a given, and a symbolic proxy can substitute for direct annotation. The discipline this map exists to enforce is what it lacks — LLaVA ablates none of its synthesis choices. See synthetic-instructions-from-a-blind-teacher-bootstrap-a-corpus.
  • 2026-07-17 — coining ingest of fineweb-datasets (Penedo et al., Hugging Face, NeurIPS 2024 D&B, CC-BY-4.0, T1): the data-curation recipe ablated in the open; global-dedup-can-hurt and educational-filter-beats-scale are the two load-bearing, default- inverting results; built-in benchmark-n-gram decontamination ties it to evaluation-contamination. See data-curation-choices-are-ablatable-and-non-monotonic.

Open questions:

  • Two sources now disagree about where fine-tuning data should come from, and neither settles it (new 2026-07-19). LLaVA manufactures it with a teacher; Molmo collects it from humans and reaches the top of an 11-benchmark suite doing so. But no source compares the two at matched cost, which is the only form the question takes for a practitioner: given a fixed budget, is teacher synthesis or collection design the better spend? Molmo proves feasibility, not superiority — and its protocol needs a 712k-image annotation operation most readers cannot run.
  • Synthetic-data generation now has a first source; synthetic-data filtering does not. LLaVA shows a corpus can be manufactured (2026-07-18) but reports only that it worked — no ablation of generation choices, no measurement of what share of the teacher's hallucinations the student inherits. The FineWeb treatment applied to a synthetic set is the obvious missing study, and it is now more conspicuous rather than less: fine-tuning / instruction-data curation proper landed 2026-07-25 (lima-less-is-more-for-alignment) on human-sourced and human-authored data, so the ablation instrument exists and has simply never been pointed at a generated corpus.
  • "More data doesn't help" is not yet established in the form a practitioner needs (new 2026-07-25). LIMA's quantity ablation scales 2K→32K examples sampled from Stack Exchange under one filter, so what it demonstrates is that more of the same distribution saturates. The move a practitioner actually makes is adding a different distribution — and this map's own diversity result predicts that would help. Those two findings are in tension at the boundary and nobody has run the experiment that separates them: hold example count fixed and vary the number of distinct sources. That is cheap, and it is now this map's sharpest missing datum.
  • The data lever just got more load-bearing. peft-methods went contested 2026-07-18: low-rank adaptation underperforms on genuinely new domains (low-rank-capacity-binds-when-the-domain-is-new). When the cheap adaptation lever binds, data quality is the remaining lever a practitioner controls — which raises the value of every unanswered question on this map.
  • Ablations are validated at small proxy scale; whether the ranking of curation choices holds at large-model training is assumed, not shown in-vault.
  • "Educational value" is an LLM-labeled proxy with its own biases; what it systematically excludes (and whether that narrows the model) is unmeasured.
  • Multilingual, code, and math data curation (FineWeb-2 and code-corpus work) are separate and not yet ingested.
  • The practical decontamination workflow for the craft loop (dedup your training set against your eval set, prove it) — FineWeb shows the supply side at pretraining scale; the fine-tuning- loop tool-doc version is still open (evaluation-contamination).
