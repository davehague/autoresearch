Transformer architecture (attention & the block) — map

Coined 2026-07-16 during the seed ingest — first evidence in the "Transformer internals" group. Upgraded emerging → consolidating 2026-07-19 (auto run): five T1s from four independent groups (Google 2017; Stanford/SUNY Buffalo 2022; Google Research 2023; Zhuiyi 2021; Princeton/Stanford 2023) now converge, and this run lands the first genuine replication rather than extension — FlashAttention-2 re-derives the memory-bound thesis by finding a second bottleneck underneath it. The map's two load-bearing claims (the component vocabulary; systems-progress-on-an-unchanged- architecture) each rest on multiple independent sources. Status reflects in-vault evidence only. 2026-07-20 (auto run): stays consolidating and firms — FlashAttention-3 confirms, by measurement, the expiry rider this map added on 2026-07-19 by inference, and the position sub-thread spun off its own child map (context-extension) rather than growing further here.

Status: consolidating

Sub-threads: none — one thesis (systems/numerics progress on an unchanged 2017 block) illustrated by kernels, KV-budget levers and stability fixes; the one genuinely separable strand (position encoding) already forked to maps/context-extension [reviewed 2026-08-01 @ 21 bullets]

What we believe now:

  • Attention alone suffices for sequence modeling, and dropping recurrence bought the parallelism that made pretraining scale. The original result is translation (WMT14 En→De 28.4 BLEU, >2 over prior best including ensembles; En→Fr 41.8 single-model SOTA) at a fraction of prior training cost (3.5 days × 8 GPUs) — but the durable consequence is that training computes over all positions at once. [T1]
  • The component set defined here is the vocabulary of the whole craft: scaled dot-product attention over Q/K/V, multi-head attention, the block (attention + position-wise MLP + residuals + layernorm), and explicit positional encoding (attention is permutation-invariant — order must be injected). The projections LoRA adapts, the K/V tensors the cache stores, the matrices quantization compresses are all named in this paper. [T1]
  • Two of those components have since changed form, and both changes were about the KV budget or the position budget rather than the core computation. Position is no longer added to the embedding: RoPE rotates the Q/K projections by an angle scaled to the absolute index, and because rotations compose (R_mᵀR_n = R_{n-m}), the attention score depends only on the offset. That buys three things — no fixed-size position table, distance decay by construction, and compatibility with linear attention that additive relative schemes break. The evidence is 2021/BERT-scale and modest (WMT14 27.3→27.5 BLEU; GLUE a 3–3 split vs BERT; long-text CAIL2019-SCM 68.10→69.79 with the extra context), so the mechanism is what consolidates here, not the margin. [T1]
  • The block's cost is memory movement, not arithmetic — and that is fixable without changing the math. FlashAttention re-schedules exact attention against the GPU memory hierarchy (tile Q/K/V into SRAM, never materialize the n×n matrix in HBM, recompute it in the backward pass from softmax statistics): memory goes quadratic → linear in sequence length, with 3× on GPT-2 (seq 1K), 2.4× on long-range arena, +15% on BERT-large, and the longer contexts it made affordable bought quality too (0.7 perplexity on GPT-2, +6.4 points long-document classification; first Transformers above chance on Path-X 61.4% at seq 16K and Path-256 63.1% at seq 64K). The output is bit-identical. [T1]
  • Below that memory wall sits a second one — work partitioning — and clearing it is hardware-generation-specific. FlashAttention-1 still reached only 25–40% of an A100's peak while optimized GEMM reaches 80–90%. FlashAttention-2 recovers it to up to 73% (230 TFLOPs/s) by deferring the online-softmax rescale (non-matmul FLOPs cost ~16× matmul FLOPs on an A100: 19.5 vs 312 TFLOPs/s), parallelizing over sequence-length blocks rather than only batch × heads, and splitting Q across warps instead of K/V to delete a shared-memory round trip. End-to-end GPT3-2.7B at 8k context: 80 → 175 → 225 TFLOPs/s per A100 (72% MFU). Still bit-exact. [T1]
  • A large share of what reads as architecture progress is systems progress on an unchanged architecture — and that progress expires. This is now measured, not inferred. Neither FlashAttention generation nor the KV-cache work altered the 2017 block's computation. FlashAttention-2 predicted its own expiry (its A100-tuned kernel on an H100 hits 335 TFLOPs/s while the author states Hopper's TMA, 4th-gen Tensor Cores, and fp8 are worth another 1.5–2× and are not implemented); FlashAttention-3 confirms it — that same kernel reaches only 35% of an H100, and a Hopper-native rebuild (warp-specialized producer/consumer asynchrony, matmul-softmax interleaving, FP8 with block quantization) restores 75% utilization / 740 TFLOPs/s at FP16 and ~1.2 PFLOPs/s at FP8, at 1.5–2.0×. A vault prediction, confirmed by a later source. Every throughput number in this vault carries an implicit card, date, and precision. [T1, three sources]
  • Precision is becoming a compute-time decision, not only a storage one. FlashAttention-3's FP8 path uses incoherent processing — multiplying by a random orthogonal matrix to spread outliers before quantizing — for 2.6× lower numerical error than baseline FP8 attention. That is the same outlier-spreading move the weight-quantization literature applies offline (gptq-post-training-quantization), here applied to activations inside a kernel at runtime. The quantization question is migrating into the kernel. [T1]
  • Where normalization sits in the block is a scaling gate, not a detail — and each new stressor demands a new normalization tweak. Two independent sources this run (2026-07-21) land the same lesson from different regimes: Megatron found post-LN BERT degrades as it grows past a few hundred M params, and moving normalization onto the identity path (pre-LN-style) is what lets a 3.9B model keep improving; Chameleon found that mixed image+text training diverges past ~7B with a stock LLaMA block until QK-normalization + reordered layernorm are added. So beneath the headline components sits a stability layer — normalization placement — that gates whether a large model trains at all, and it is re-tuned for each new regime (pure scale, then modality mixing). The evidence is suggestive (no isolating ablation in either), which is why the specific RMSNorm/SwiGLU refinements remain their own open gap. [T1, two sources, suggestive]
  • The KV cache is an architectural choice, not a fixed cost. Grouped-query attention shares g key/value heads across groups of query heads (MHA and MQA as endpoints). On T5-XXL, GQA-8 reaches 47.1 average vs MHA's 47.2 at 0.28 s/sample vs 1.51 s — 5.4× decode speed for 0.1 points — and an existing multi-head checkpoint converts by uptraining at 5% of original pretraining compute. This is why nearly every open-weights model in current use ships with GQA. [T1]

Through-line (newest first):

  • 2026-08-01 — two more instances of this map's systems-not-architecture through-line, both from sources whose primary map is local-inference. (a) The causal mask is a parameter, and transposing it buys a new capability from an unchanged block. counter-causal-surprise-kv-eviction (T1) obtains an estimate of P(x_i | x_{i+1:t}) — a backward probability — from a forward-trained model by running it over the cached tokens in their original order with the causal mask upper-triangular instead of lower, reusing the stored K and V and computing fresh queries at the original positions. No weights change, no training, and every representation stays in the distribution the model was trained on. That is the same shape as attention-is-the-one-op-that-cannot-be-batched's observation from the serving side — attention is the block's only stateful, parameter-free operator, which is exactly why it is the one whose behaviour can be edited at inference by changing what it is allowed to see. (b) The SwiGLU feed-forward network is the block's dominant weight traffic, and it is separable per token. prox-training-free-ffn-sparsity (T1) executes only 30–50% of the intermediate channels per token on ten unmodified checkpoints across six families, at a 1.5-point aggregate cost at 50% sparsity — which says the FFN's per-token effective width is far below its declared width in every model tested. Neither result changes a single design decision in the block described here; both change what the block costs to run. See ffn-activation-sparsity-is-a-training-free-decode-lever, predictable-past-tokens-are-the-redundant-ones.
  • 2026-07-30 — a second knob on the position encoding, and it is the index rather than the frequency, from jet-long-dynamic-bifocal-rope (T1, primary map context-extension): this map established that position is a rotation, not an addition (position-can-be-a-rotation-not-an-addition) — R_mᵀR_n = R_{n-m}, so the attention score depends only on the offset. Everything the vault has since read about extending a context window manipulated the frequencies of that rotation: rescale them and old angles cover new distances. This is the other knob. Leave the frequencies exactly as trained and remap the indices — a local window where RoPE is untouched, and a remote window where position becomes f(x) = ⌊x/G⌋ with G = max(1, ⌈L/w_pretrained⌉) recomputed from the current sequence length, so remote rotation angles always land inside the trained distribution. Two things this map should carry from it. The offset-only property is what makes the remapping legal at all: because the score depends only on n − m, compressing both indices by a shared factor is a well-defined operation on the same rotation the block already computes, needing no retraining and no fitted constant — a consequence of this map's own component definition that nobody had exploited. And the two knobs are not equally safe: on RULER across Qwen3 1.7B/4B/8B, frequency rescaling (YaRN) reads 52.99 / 70.11 / 78.49 against unextended bases of 60.93 / 69.94 / 73.13 — worse than doing nothing at 1.7B — while index remapping reads 73.93 / 83.02 / 86.74. So the map's position component has a documented fragile edit and a documented robust one. Third, and the recurring lesson again: the method as written is a 7–11% prefill slowdown against FlashAttention-2 and becomes 1.28–1.39× once three FlashAttention calls and the LogSumExp merge are fused into one CuTe kernel — the mathematics unchanged between those two measurements, which is attention-is-memory-bound-not-compute-bound and kernel-performance-expires-with-the-gpu-generation restated on an H100. Scope: one model family, a 4× extension, and RULER is synthetic (the natural-benchmark gap is a wash). See remapping-positions-beats-rescaling-frequencies-for-context-extension.
  • 2026-07-28 — a symmetry in the block that nobody had used, and it turns out to be worth 4-bit inference, from quarot-outlier-free-4bit-rotated-llms (T1, primary map post-training-quantization): this map catalogues the block's components and what each one buys. Here is a property of how two of them compose. Because RMSNorm divides each row by its norm, and a rotation preserves norms, the whole network is invariant to an orthogonal change of basis: multiply an input-side weight matrix on the left by Q and the paired output-side matrix on the right by Qᵀ and the output is unchanged — RMSNorm(X) = RMSNorm(XQᵀ)Q. The block has a continuous symmetry group, and the coordinate system its activations are written in carries no information. That is a fact about the architecture, discovered late, with a large practical payoff: the outlier channels that make activations hard to quantize are an artifact of the basis, so rotating them away costs nothing and enables end-to-end 4-bit inference. Two component-level details worth recording here. The symmetry is broken by normalization that rescales — the per-channel scale must first be absorbed into the neighbouring weights for the commutation to hold, so this is a property of pre-norm RMSNorm specifically and not of normalization in general. And RoPE breaks fusion where LayerNorm does not: because the positional rotation sits between W_k and the attention score, the key rotation cannot be folded into the weights and must run online — the third time this map has recorded that position-can-be-a-rotation-not-an-addition has consequences beyond position encoding. See rotating-a-transformer-deletes-its-outliers-for-free.
  • 2026-07-28 — a fourth answer to the KV-cache budget, and it is architectural, from deepseek-v3-technical-report (T1, primary map mixture-of-experts): this map holds that sharing-kv-heads-buys-decode-speed-at-near-zero-quality-cost — GQA shrinks the cache by storing fewer key/value heads, an architectural rather than a precision or placement lever. Multi-head Latent Attention attacks the same budget by a different construction: compress keys and values jointly into a low-rank latent vector, cache only that plus one small decoupled key carrying RoPE, and reconstruct per-head K and V by up-projection at use time. Where GQA shares heads, MLA factorizes them — and the same trick is applied to queries purely to cut training activation memory, which is a use GQA has no analogue for. The RoPE detail is the interesting one and it is the same obstruction as in the rotation edge above: a decoupled key path exists precisely because the positional rotation cannot commute through the low-rank factorization, so the architecture carries a small uncompressed side-channel to hold it. The vault now has three distinct ways to shrink the same cache — fewer heads, fewer bits, fewer dimensions — and, as this map has noted since 2026-07-18, no source measuring more than one of them at a time.
  • 2026-07-27 — the strongest instance of this map's through-line yet, from emu3-next-token-prediction-multimodal (T1, primary map vision-language-models): an 8B model that generates images, generates video, and answers questions about both, using the block this map already documents and nothing else — 32 layers, grouped-query attention at 32 query / 8 KV heads, RMSNorm, SwiGLU, RoPE at base 1,000,000, biases removed. Every one of those has a graded note here already. The only architectural change is the embedding table: 184,622 entries, being the text vocabulary plus a 32,768-entry vision codebook. Everything modality-specific lives outside the transformer, in a vector-quantized autoencoder (4× temporal, 8×8 spatial) and in a document format that writes resolution and frame rate in as plain text — move the caption after the end-of-vision token and a generation example becomes an understanding one. The entire architectural apparatus of the VLM literature (encoder, projection, fusion scheme, diffusion decoder, external text encoder for conditioning) collapses into a vocabulary extension and a [SOV] token. Two riders keep this from being free and both are budget rather than architecture: one 512×512 image is 4,096 tokens, which forces a 131,072-token context, and vision tokens need an explicit loss weight of 0.5 plus dropout to keep training stable — a second group after Chameleon finding that one loss over two modalities needs a thumb on the scale, alongside mixed-modal-training-needs-qk-norm-to-stay-stable. So the block didn't change; the sequence length did. See a-discrete-codebook-turns-pixels-into-vocabulary.
  • 2026-07-27 — the memory-movement thesis derived rather than observed, from sarathi-serve-chunked-prefill-stall-free-batching (T1, primary map local-inference): attention-is-memory-bound-not-compute-bound is this map's clean statement that arithmetic is nearly free and data movement is the bill. This prices the rest of the block on the same axis and finds the split is between phases, not operators. Running the linear layers for one decode token costs about what running them for 128 prefill tokens costs, because a decode iteration amortizes each weight matrix's trip out of memory over a handful of tokens and a prefill iteration amortizes it over thousands. Two consequences worth carrying here: prefill throughput saturates around 512 tokens (so a prompt is not an indivisible unit of compute), and linear operators — not attention — account for more than 80% of iteration time even at long sequence lengths. That last figure is a useful corrective to a map whose kernel-level sources are all about attention. Also a fourth instance of the expiry rider's cousin: the interconnect threshold. Cross-node tensor parallelism costs >2× the median inter-token latency of node-local TP, which is Megatron's node-locality and Ring Attention's c ≥ F/B reappearing in serving.
  • 2026-07-26 — the expiry rider gains a third literature, and the earliest instance of it — from pipedream-asynchronous-pipeline-parallelism (T1, primary map distributed-training). This map has carried since 2026-07-19, and confirmed by measurement on 2026-07-20, that much of what reads as architecture progress is systems progress tuned to a hardware ratio, and that it expires with the GPU generation (kernel-performance-expires-with-the-gpu-generation). Both instances so far were about the kernel and HBM. PipeDream measures the same shape on the interconnect, in 2018: across five models, moving K80 → Titan X → V100 raises communication's share of total training time, and the advantage of the communication-avoiding system grows 2.99× → 5.12× on newer cards behind a slower network. Compute got cheaper; the wire did not. The generalization the vault can now state across three unrelated literatures — FlashAttention about HBM, PipeDream about Ethernet, ZeRO-Infinity about PCIe and NVMe — is that the optimizations that keep their value are the ones about movement, and they get more valuable as silicon improves, which is the opposite of how "this will be obsolete next generation" usually reads.
  • 2026-07-26 — structural edge from sglang-radixattention-prefix-caching (T1, primary map local-inference): a refinement of the 2026-07-25 note below. Attention is not only the block's one stateful, parameter-free operator — because its state for position i depends on tokens 0..i and nothing else, that state is shareable across requests and bit-identical when the prefix matches. So the KV cache is not per-request scratch; it is a content-addressable structure, and a radix tree over it with LRU eviction is the natural data structure. The same causal property that makes the cache possible at all is what makes reuse exact — and it is also where exactness stops: reusing a block that was not a prefix means its keys and values were computed against a different left context, which the source characterizes as costing up to a 43% accuracy drop. See kv-reuse-must-be-exact-prefix-to-stay-lossless.
  • 2026-07-25 — structural edge from orca-iteration-level-scheduling (T1, primary map local-inference): the block has exactly one operator that carries per-request state and holds no parameters, and naming that asymmetry explains a surprising amount of the serving stack. Batching requests requires identically-shaped tensors, which generative serving routinely violates — but only attention actually needs a request dimension. Linear, LayerNorm, Add and GeLU are token-wise and do not care which request a token belongs to, so a batch flattens to a single [Σ L, H] tensor with no batch dimension at all, and only attention gets wrapped in split/merge. The sharper half is why that costs nothing: attention reads no weights, only the per-request KV cache, so batching it was never going to amortize a parameter load — the operator that cannot be batched is precisely the one that gains nothing from being batched. This is the same asymmetry attention-is-memory-bound-not-compute-bound states in memory-traffic terms and sharing-kv-heads-buys-decode-speed-at-near-zero-quality-cost exploits architecturally, and it generalizes into a sentence worth keeping: attention's cost is state movement; everything else's cost is weight movement. It retroactively explains a result the vault holds but had not connected — routing-is-not-what-makes-moe-slow-on-device, where routing is under 9% of MoE-block time because parameter-free bookkeeping is cheap and resident weight reads are the bill. Bound: an engineering property demonstrated within one system rather than an isolated ablation, on 2022-era fused kernels; FlashAttention-class kernels have shifted the attention/FFN cost ratio since. See attention-is-the-one-op-that-cannot-be-batched.
  • 2026-07-25 — lineage edge from ring-attention-blockwise-near-infinite-context (T1, primary map distributed-training): a third instance of this map's central thesis, and the largest capability number yet attached to it. The block did not change. Ring Attention takes the identical tiling insight FlashAttention used to stage attention through SRAM (attention-is-memory-bound-not-compute-bound) and distributes the tiles across hosts instead, exploiting the fact that attention between a query block and a group of KV blocks is permutation-invariant so long as the softmax rescaling statistics are recombined correctly. No approximation, no architectural change, exact same math — and trainable context goes from 32K to 16M tokens. The map's recurring lesson has now been demonstrated at three scopes: a kernel rewrite (FlashAttention-2, bit-exact, 25–40% → 73% of A100 peak), a hardware-generation rebuild (FlashAttention-3), and now a distribution strategy that multiplies a headline capability by 512 without touching a single equation. What changes is where the tiles live. See context-length-scales-linearly-with-device-count.
  • 2026-07-24 — stability-thread edge from gpipe-micro-batch-pipeline-parallelism (T1, primary map distributed-training): a fourth independent instance of this map's recurring finding that scaling a transformer is gated by numerical stability rather than by representational capacity. Training a 128-layer, 6B multilingual Transformer failed outright — sharp activations (positive kurtosis) plus data noise producing non-finite gradients that "destroyed the learning progress" — and was rescued by two edits that touch no architecture: scale down the initialization of every feed-forward layer by the number of layers, and clip the logits before the softmax. Put beside Megatron's normalization-placement result, ST-MoE's router z-loss, and Chameleon's QK-norm, the pattern is now hard to dismiss: at every scale jump in this vault's evidence, the blocker was a variance or an outlier, and the fix was a normalization, an init scale, or a clip. GPipe's contribution to the pattern is that it is the earliest and the one about depth specifically, and that the remedy is placed at initialization rather than in the forward pass — the cheapest possible location. Same reading as the map's standing thesis that much of what looks like architecture progress is systems and numerics progress on an unchanged block. See pipeline-parallelism-splits-depth-and-pays-a-bubble.
  • 2026-07-22 — generalization edge from qwen25-vl-technical-report (T2, primary map vision-language-models): rotary position generalizes to axes that are not sequence position at all. RoPE now appears in-vault in three forms — 1D over text (position-can-be-a-rotation-not-an-addition), 2D over image patches (spatial relationships in a grid), and MRoPE keyed to absolute wall-clock time over video, which is what buys second-level event localization across hours of input. The original paper's argument was that a rotation makes the score depend only on offset; the multimodal extensions show the trick works for any axis with a metric, which is a stronger statement about the mechanism than its authors made. The same report also imports the LLM block wholesale into the vision tower — SwiGLU FFNs and RMSNorm in the ViT — which is a small piece of evidence for this map's standing thesis that the block converged and the progress is elsewhere, and a first in-vault sighting of two components the map lists as un-sourced block refinements (sighting only, not a graded result — no ablation). And window attention (only 4 of 32 ViT layers full-attention, the rest 112×112 windows, giving linear rather than quadratic cost in patch count) is the architectural rather than kernel-side answer to the same cost problem FlashAttention solves in software — worth noting as the other branch of attention-is-memory-bound-not-compute-bound. Open questions:

  • Context extension is the lever RoPE created and the vault has not sourced. Resolved 2026-07-20 — YaRN landed and the thread was large enough to carry its own map, context-extension. What remains open there is the part that matters most: every in-vault number is perplexity, which does not establish usable retrieval or reasoning across a long window.
  • The decoder-only lineage (GPT-line) and the remaining modern block refinements — RMSNorm and SwiGLU/gated MLPs — still have no dedicated in-vault sources (RoPE landed 2026-07-19, GQA 2026-07-18). Normalization placement now has a first, suggestive purchase (2026-07-21, Megatron + Chameleon), but the specific modern norm/activation variants and why they replaced LayerNorm/GeLU are unserved; tokenization and generation/sampling ends of Track A are similarly unserved.
  • Where knowledge lives (params vs activations; what fine-tuning actually moves) needs its own evidence thread — it is the internals question the fine-tuning craft leans on hardest. The intruder-dimension result (intruder-dimensions-are-the-mechanism-of-lora-forgetting) is the vault's first spectral purchase on it and belongs to this question as much as to peft-methods.
  • The kernel lineage is current through Hopper as of 2026-07-20 — and by its own thesis will not stay that way. FlashAttention-3 closed the FA2-era gap, but it is explicitly H100-tuned; whatever ships next resets this line, and the map should expect to re-open this item rather than treat it as settled.
