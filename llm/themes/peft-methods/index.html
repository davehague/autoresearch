PEFT methods (LoRA/QLoRA vs full fine-tuning) — map

Coined 2026-07-16 during the seed ingest, directly at consolidating: two independent T1s from different groups (Microsoft 2021; UW 2023) converge on "low-rank adaptation preserves fine-tune quality," and the second extends it through a 4-bit base. Flagged for David's review.

Status: consolidating

What we believe now:

  • Low-rank adaptation matches full fine-tuning on task adaptation, at a vanishing fraction of the cost. LoRA (ΔW = BA on frozen weights): trainable params ↓ ~10,000×, GPU memory ↓ ~3× on GPT-3 175B, quality on-par or better across RoBERTa/DeBERTa/GPT-2/GPT-3. Mechanism: measured task-adaptation updates are rank-deficient. [T1]
  • The base doesn't even need to be 16-bit. QLoRA backprops through a frozen 4-bit base (NF4 + double quantization + paged optimizers) into LoRA adapters and preserves full 16-bit fine-tune performance on the tasks tested — 65B on a single 48GB GPU, Guanaco at 99.3% of ChatGPT's level (LLM-judged) after 24h on one card. [T1]
  • Adapters add no inference tax: ΔW merges into W at deploy time (zero added latency), or stays separate as small hot-swappable task artifacts — the merged form is what feeds the quantize-and-ship-locally pipeline. [T1]
  • Practical shape, therefore: 4-bit-base LoRA on one card is the baseline every bigger plan must beat — full FT or multi-node runs need a stated reason (new-domain knowledge injection, measured LoRA shortfall), not habit. [T1, two groups]
  • The LoRA parameterization itself is improvable at no inference cost. DoRA splits each weight into a magnitude vector + a directional matrix and LoRA-adapts only the direction, recovering much of the LoRA→full-FT gap at +0.01% trainable params and zero merged-inference overhead (commonsense-reasoning avg +1.0 to +4.4 over LoRA across LLaMA-7B/13B/2-7B/3-8B). So "LoRA at rank r" is no longer automatically the parameter-matched frontier — but the edge is untested over a 4-bit QLoRA base, this map's actual baseline. [T1]

Open questions:

  • Where does LoRA stop sufficing? Both T1s are task-adaptation evidence; injecting large amounts of new domain knowledge (continued-pretraining-shaped problems) is untested here. The craft's most decision-relevant missing source is a controlled LoRA-vs-full-FT comparison on knowledge-heavy targets.
  • Rank/placement/hyperparameter practice has moved since 2021 (which modules, what rank). DoRA now lands the first variant source; the rest of the zoo (rsLoRA, AdaLoRA, PiSSA-class inits) and the sharp open question — does DoRA's edge survive over a 4-bit quantized base — remain unsourced.
  • Catastrophic forgetting: does the frozen-base property actually protect general capability, and by how much vs full FT? Needs its own evidence thread (Track C's forgetting rung).
  • Eval-hygiene ripple (2026-07-16): this map's headline claims are benchmark deltas ("matches full FT"; Guanaco at 99.3% of ChatGPT, LLM-judged) — GSM1k showed exactly that class of number can be inflated by contamination, and a fine-tune is deliberate exposure to task-adjacent data. The claims stand (different benchmarks, pre-2024 evals), but future fine-tune-beats-X sources get graded through evaluation-contamination.
