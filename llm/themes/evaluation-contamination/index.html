Evaluation & contamination (honest measurement) — map

Coined 2026-07-16 (auto run) — first evidence in the "Honest evaluation" group. This map is the integrity gate for every benchmark-delta claim the vault tracks: the premise ("open-weights + your data beats frontier on task X") is only as good as the eval behind it. Auto-coined; flagged for David's review. Upgraded emerging → consolidating 2026-07-20 (auto run). The judge gap this map escalated on 2026-07-18 and again on 2026-07-19 is now closed, and closing it completes the map's real thesis: a benchmark number has several independent ways of being wrong, and the vault can now enumerate all four with a graded T1 behind each — leakage (GSM1k), construct mismatch (LoRA-vs-full-FT), infrastructure non-equivalence (FSDP), and judge bias (MT-Bench). Add Molmo as a positive exemplar of what honest measurement looks like, and this map has stopped being a warning and become a checklist. Five T1s from five independent groups.

Status: consolidating

What we believe now:

  • Benchmark scores can be memorization, and the parallel-benchmark design measures how much. GSM1k (from-scratch, difficulty-matched to GSM8k): several model families drop up to 8%, and the drop correlates (Spearman's r² = 0.36) with how readily the model generates the original benchmark items verbatim — leakage, mechanistically tied. [T1]
  • Contamination is unevenly distributed — frontier models showed minimal overfitting and generalized to the novel items. "Everyone's cheating" and "leaderboards are fine" are both wrong; per-family checking is the honest posture. [T1]
  • Operating rule, therefore: a fine-tune is deliberate exposure to task-adjacent data, so a fine-tuned/distilled model's win only counts on items it demonstrably hasn't seen — freshly-authored, held-out, or post-cutoff evals. [T1-derived practice]
  • An LLM judge is a legitimate instrument with three biases that do not average out. GPT-4's agreement with human preference exceeds 80% — the same rate humans agree with each other — which is the finding that stops "LLM judges are unreliable" from being the right conclusion. There is no clean ground truth here, only a noisy human signal at the same noise floor. But the judge also carries position bias (which answer is shown first), verbosity bias (longer preferred regardless of quality), and self-enhancement bias (GPT-4 favors its own outputs by ~10% win rate; Claude-v1 by ~25%), plus weak math/logic grading. These are systematic and directional, so sampling more does not wash them out. Position bias is mitigable — swap the order and require consistency; the other two are not cleanly fixable. [T1]
  • The four failure modes, as a checklist. Any benchmark delta in this vault can be wrong by leakage (the model saw the items), construct mismatch (the score is honest but measures a narrower surface than the claim), infrastructure non-equivalence (two runs post identical scores having optimized differently), or judge bias (the ranking instrument has a preference of its own). They are independent — clearing one says nothing about the others — and each now has a graded T1 behind it. [T1, four sources]

Through-line (newest first):

  • 2026-07-24 — ingest of min-k-prob-detecting-pretraining-data (Shi, Ajith, Xia et al., UW / Princeton / Berkeley / AI2, ICLR 2024, T1): closes the "cheap contamination detectors" gap this map has carried since coining — the membership-inference branch, and the only branch a practitioner can run, since the others need the corpus. Min-K% Prob scores a passage by averaging the log-probs of only its lowest-probability 20% of tokens, on the asymmetric hypothesis that seen text has no surprising-token tail. No corpus access, no reference model, no training. The reusable contribution is arguably WIKIMIA rather than the detector: it exploits the one externally verifiable fact about pretraining data — time — with pre-2017 Wikipedia as members and 394 post-2023 event articles as guaranteed non-members, a construction that transfers to any model with a disclosed cutoff. The number is the finding, and the number is AUC 0.72 (vs 0.67 for perplexity, 0.65–0.66 for Zlib/Neighbor/reference baselines): better than everything available and nowhere near a gate. So the rule this map adopts is narrower than "leakage is checkable" — it prioritizes what to rebuild; it does not certify what to keep, and the conclusive move remains benchmark-scores-can-be-memorization's rebuild-and-measure. The gap to 0.86 in the controlled case is the useful part for this vault: detecting your own eval set in your own fine-tuning mix is far easier than interrogating a frontier checkpoint, because detectability scales with learning rate and repetition — so the instrument is most reliable exactly where this vault's readers would reach for it. Ripples: makes runnable the distill-set contamination audit distillation-small-models has wanted since 2026-07-16, and pairs with data-curation as the demand-side counterpart to FineWeb's supply-side decontamination — the weaker of the two, and the one that applies to almost every model this vault's readers actually fine-tune. Caveats that bound it hard: paraphrasing degrades every method tested; a benchmark seen once in a trillion tokens is near-invisible; WIKIMIA's members and non-members differ in era and topic as well as membership; models tested top out at 65B and are all 2022–2023 vintage; and k=20 was chosen by validation sweep. See pretraining-membership-leaves-a-detectable-tail, contamination-detection-is-a-prior-not-a-verdict.
  • 2026-07-24 — fifth construct-mismatch instance, from llama-cpp-quantization-unified-evaluation (T1, primary map local-inference): the failure mode lands inside the compression stack for the first time. Perplexity is the default reporting metric for quantization work — cheap, harness-free, and what llama.cpp prints — and it does not rank the formats: four 5-bit variants span 0.03 PPL and 3.4 GSM8K points, ordering the menu differently from the thing it is deployed for. The mechanism is the familiar one: a token-averaged likelihood is dominated by easy tokens and blind to the few hard ones, while multi-step reasoning fails precisely on the hard ones. General form of the rule: a compression method's quality claim must be measured on the capability being preserved, not on a likelihood proxy — the inference-side twin of intruder-dimensions-are-the-mechanism-of-lora-forgetting's training-side result that equal in-domain scores can conceal structurally different solutions. It also upgrades an advisory this map issued at the context-extension coining from suspicion to measurement: that map's entire evidence base is perplexity, and this is a demonstration of the proxy failing on a neighbouring task. See perplexity-does-not-rank-quantization-formats.
  • 2026-07-20 — ingest of judging-llm-as-a-judge-mt-bench (Zheng et al., LMSYS — Berkeley/UCSD/CMU/Stanford, NeurIPS 2023 D&B, T1): the gap this map escalated twice, closed, and the source that tips it to consolidating. It cuts both ways deliberately: judges hit >80% human agreement — equal to human-human agreement, so the instrument is not junk and there is no clean ground truth being approximated — while carrying position, verbosity, and self-enhancement biases that are directional and do not average out. The retroactive reach is the largest of any single source in the vault: it grades an instrument three maps had already been ranking on. Sharpest consequence — GPT-4 favoring GPT-4-family outputs by ~10% is the measurement behind the LLaVA "closed teacher loop" this map flagged on suspicion 2026-07-18. Names the fourth way an in-vault number misleads. See llm-judges-match-human-agreement-and-carry-systematic-bias.
  • 2026-07-20 — advisory edge from simpo-simple-preference-optimization (T1, primary map post-training-rl): the same-run instance that makes the judge finding concrete rather than abstract. SimPO's distinguishing mechanism is length normalization; its headline win is scored on judges with a documented verbosity/length preference. "Length-controlled win rate" is a correction applied by the harness, not independence from the judge. The pattern this map has been tracking — where the eval is honest, the margin shrinks — now has a third instance and should be treated as a prior, not a coincidence. See preference-training-should-optimize-the-metric-you-decode-with.
  • 2026-07-20 — advisory edge from yarn-context-window-extension (T1, primary map context-extension): a construct-mismatch case in a brand-new map, caught at coining. "128k context" rests on perplexity at 131,072 tokens — which shows the model is not confused, and says nothing about retrieval, reasoning, or instruction-following across that window. A headline capability number measuring a much narrower surface than the claim it is quoted for. See context-length-is-a-cheap-post-hoc-edit-not-a-pretraining-property.
  • 2026-07-19 — interaction edge from molmo-pixmo-open-vlm (T1, primary map vision-language-models): the vault's first positive example — what an honest eval looks like when someone bothers. Against the closed GPT-4 loop this map flagged 2026-07-18, Molmo ranks models by >325k human pairwise ratings (~450 matches per pair, Bradley-Terry), reports the automated 11-benchmark suite separately, and then publishes where the two rankings disagree (Qwen2-VL strong on benchmarks, weaker with humans) rather than quoting whichever axis flatters it. Two lessons this map can now state affirmatively: report the disagreement between your measurement methods, and treat a suite average as a compression that hides shape — Molmo-72B tops the average at 81.2 while sitting 15 points below GPT-4o on MMMU (54.1 vs 69.1). See open-vlms-are-mostly-distilled-closed-vlms.
  • 2026-07-19 — advisory edge from orpo-monolithic-preference-optimization (T1, primary map post-training-rl): the LLM-as-judge thread escalates again — three of ORPO's four headline benchmarks are GPT-4-family judged (AlpacaEval 1.0, AlpacaEval 2.0, MT-Bench), only IFEval is judge-free, and the comparison figures for Zephyr-β and Llama-2-Chat are lifted from public leaderboards rather than re-run alongside — stacking judge-family bias on top of eval-condition mismatch. Notably, the paper's own apples-to-apples small-scale table is where its method looks weakest. Where the eval is honest, the margin shrinks is now a pattern worth watching, not a one-off. See preference-alignment-collapses-into-a-single-stage.
  • 2026-07-19 — advisory edge from pytorch-fsdp-fully-sharded-data-parallel (T1, primary map distributed-training): a third way a number misleads, and the quietest. FSDP states plainly that sharded training "cannot ensure… the same mathematical equivalence as local training" for the optimizer step — so two runs can post identical scores having optimized differently, with nothing in the output signalling it. Construct mismatch came from the method; this comes from the infrastructure. See sharded-optimizer-math-is-not-locally-equivalent.
  • 2026-07-18 — interaction edge from lora-vs-full-ft-illusion-of-equivalence (T1, primary map peft-methods): a second way a benchmark number misleads, orthogonal to contamination. Not leakage but construct mismatch — LoRA and full fine-tuning post equal in-domain accuracy while having learned structurally different solutions, and the LoRA model generalizes worse out of distribution. The score was honest and still wrong about the thing it was taken to measure. Widens this map's remit: an eval can fail by measuring memorization or by measuring too narrow a surface. See intruder-dimensions-are-the-mechanism-of-lora-forgetting.
  • 2026-07-18 — advisory edge from llava-visual-instruction-tuning (T1, primary map vision-language-models): the vault's cleanest example of a closed teacher loop — GPT-4 writes the training data, GPT-4 writes the benchmark, GPT-4 scores the output, result reported as "85.1% relative to GPT-4." Every headline number in the new multi-modal map carries this shape, and the LLM-as-judge thread below is no longer hypothetical. See synthetic-instructions-from-a-blind-teacher-bootstrap-a-corpus.
  • 2026-07-16 — ingest of gsm1k-careful-examination (Zhang et al., Scale AI, NeurIPS 2024 D&B, T1): the parallel-benchmark experiment; up-to-8% drops; generation-likelihood correlation. Interaction noted with peft-methods and distillation-small-models — their headline deltas are the kind this map's finding inflates. See benchmark-scores-can-be-memorization.

Open questions:

  • Detection without rebuilding — partly resolved 2026-07-24. The membership-inference family now has a graded source (min-k-prob-detecting-pretraining-data) and the first rung of an ordered procedure exists: screen cheaply, then rebuild what the screen flags. Still unserved: n-gram / substring overlap at corpus scale, and dynamic or perturbed evals as a family. What replaces the closed part is sharper — at AUC 0.72 the screen is a prior, not a verdict, and the vault should expect over-reading of exactly this result in the wild, since "we ran a contamination check" reads as clearance.
  • Does the detector survive the models it would be used on? (new 2026-07-24). Every model in the membership-inference evidence is 2022–2023 vintage and ≤65B, trained before the deduplication and decontamination pipelines that data-curation now documents as standard. A modern 15T-token run with aggressive dedup plausibly has a weaker memorization tail — which would make detection harder without making contamination rarer. Untested, and it is the same 2023-vintage-numbers problem this map already flags for judge bias.
  • Nobody has measured whether the four failure modes compound, though the modal in-vault case carries all four at once — and the vault still has no source giving a practitioner an ordered, budgeted procedure for clearing them. Leakage now has its first two rungs; construct mismatch, infrastructure non-equivalence, and judge bias have named modes and no protocol.
  • Domain reach: GSM1k is grade-school math — whether code, reasoning, and instruction-following suites show the same overfitting pattern is unmeasured in-vault.
  • LLM-as-judge has failure modes orthogonal to contamination and needs its own thread. RESOLVED 2026-07-20 after two escalations (2026-07-18, 2026-07-19) — the biases are now graded. What replaces it is narrower and sharper: the in-vault magnitudes are 2023-vintage. GPT-4-0314 and Claude-v1 are retired models; the failure modes are structural but nobody has re-measured their size on the judges scoring today's papers, and the vault is applying 2023 numbers as a discount on 2024–2026 results. Also unresolved: judge reliability on technical, long-form, and domain-specific outputs — the 80% agreement figure is on open-ended chat, which is not the distribution this vault's premise targets. And reward-model failure (a different object from an eval judge) is still ungraded; see post-training-rl.
  • What replaces the judge, when you can afford it (new 2026-07-19): Molmo demonstrates human pairwise preference at 325k ratings cross-checked against automated benchmarks. That is a lab-scale answer. The practitioner-scale version — how to evaluate a fine-tune honestly without a judge model or an annotation budget — has no source, and it is the question this vault's premise most needs answered.
  • Suite averages hide shape (new 2026-07-19): Molmo-72B tops an 11-benchmark average while trailing GPT-4o by 15 points on MMMU. Any "beats frontier on task X" claim — the vault's own premise — needs the per-task breakdown, not the mean.
  • Construct validity is now a named second failure mode (2026-07-18): a score can be uncontaminated and still misleading if the benchmark surface is narrower than the claim. Detecting that is a different problem from detecting leakage — OOD generalization gaps and spectral checks on the fine-tuned weights are the two probes in-vault, neither of them a workflow. What an honest fine-tune evaluation protocol looks like, covering both failure modes, is unsourced.
  • All four failure modes are named; the protocol that covers them is still not written (sharpened 2026-07-20). The vault can now enumerate leakage, construct mismatch, infrastructure non-equivalence, and judge bias, each with a T1 — but no in-vault source gives a practitioner an ordered procedure for clearing all four on their own fine-tune, at their own budget. That synthesis is now the map's highest-value missing artifact, and it is the one thing standing between this map and consensus.
  • Nobody has measured whether the failure modes compound (new 2026-07-20). A distilled model evaluated by the teacher's own family on a contaminated suite trained under a sharded optimizer is carrying all four at once — plausibly the modal case in this vault — and every source isolates one.
  • The practical decontamination workflow for the craft loop (dedup your fine-tuning set against your eval set, prove it) has no tool-doc source yet — though FineWeb now shows the supply-side version at pretraining scale (benchmark-n-gram removal in the pipeline; data-curation).
