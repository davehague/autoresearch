What we believe now:

  • The control has a variance nobody measures, and this run put two independent numbers on it: 8% of verdicts from a frozen model under batching, 4.7% of positions from a kernel swap (new 2026-08-23, and it is the layer above everything else on this map). Every entry here says rule that failure out with the appropriate control. The structural defect is that a null is an estimate: the standard repair for a false-positive- prone expansion statistic reads as a zero null at one realization and is 0.058 [0.038, 0.078] across replicates, so a correction validated against one measurement of its null inherits that measurement's noise as a claim. The base rate it was repairing is worse — the m=1 expansion criterion fires on an untrained, frozen model 28% of the time (0.176 pooled over 110 frozen comparisons). Two mechanisms, arriving from opposite ends of the stack, now supply the noise. Batching: temperature-zero decoding is not reproducible — a frozen model re-scored twice flips 16/200 verdicts (8%) at 64-way concurrency and 4/200 (2%) serialized, and a single-greedy-decode ledger consequently assigns that unchanged model a 1.5× corruption-to-learning ratio. Kernel selection: holding the container digest fixed and swapping one INT8 GEMM flips 4.7% of teacher-forced positions, with logit margin predicting which ones at ROC-AUC 0.942 — flip rates falling monotonically from 34% in the lowest-margin decile to zero above ~9 margin units. The unifying statement is the useful one: numerical perturbation from anywhere in the stack acts on the same population of near-tied decisions, so a benchmark's margin distribution determines how much of its score is available to be moved by things that are not the model. The working replacement is cheap — pool the baseline replicates a multi-arm study already owns into one denominator (1,408 draws per problem here) and run a per-problem exact test under FDR control, which returns zero detections on 11 held-out replicates and is stable under Bonferroni, error rate and pool size. Five other named failures travel with it: fixed token caps scoring verbose methods as destructive; transition statistics needing ~10× the data of accuracy statistics; results flipping across training seeds (CLR 0.55–1.53); underpowered safety probes producing effects replication does not confirm. The operational rule: before reporting that anything changed, evaluate the unchanged thing twice through the same pipeline and report that number first.   • A reported score is one phrasing of many, the spread is enormous, and the strongest model on a benchmark is the one whose score depends most on the wording it happened to get (new 2026-08-16, and it clears a limit this map has carried since 2026-07-28).   • The contamination detector is itself a measurement with a preparation regime, and the regime it was validated in is not the one anybody audits (new 2026-08-12, and it discounts this map's own instrument rather than a benchmark). Feature-based detectors — the family carrying the strong numbers — derive membership features from the model's output. Post-training changes model outputs, and instruction tuning, preference optimization and reasoning-oriented training each "alter model outputs and shift the corresponding membership features, thereby reducing the separability between members and non-members." Every checkpoint a practitioner would audit has been through at least one; the base checkpoints these detectors were validated on are a regime nobody deploys. The damage is not measured directly — what is measured is that a calibration wrapper wins back up to 7.0% AUC and 15.0% TPR@5%FPR across two detectors, four benchmarks and three targets spanning the three post-training types — so recovery is a lower bound on damage. The operational consequence is about how this map quotes itself: pretraining-membership-leaves-a-detectable-tail's AUC 0.72 is a base-model figure, and the post-trained figure is lower by an unknown margin. And the branch splits, which inverts this map's reason for privileging it. The argument here has been structural — leakage checks need the corpus, membership inference "needs only the model." True for the unsupervised likelihood screen; false for the feature-based detectors now holding the strong numbers, which are supervised (50 labeled members and 50 labeled non-members) and whose calibration needs a further held-out known-non-member set. That is precisely the corpus knowledge the other branch was faulted for requiring, moved into the labels. So: a cheap unsupervised screen at ~0.72 that anyone can run, and a strong supervised detector at 0.54–0.92 that needs membership ground truth you probably cannot get — this map has been quoting the accessibility of the first and the strength of the second as one tool. Practitioner form: treat membership inference as a cheap screen with a weak signal, not as an audit; a negative result from a 0.72-AUC screen on a post-trained model is close to uninformative.   • A benchmark can be clean, uncontaminated, freshly rebuilt — and still not predict the capability it is named after (new 2026-08-05, and it lands on this map's oldest mode from underneath). Leakage has been anchored since coining on a remedy as much as a diagnosis: rebuild the benchmark and watch the scores fall (benchmark-scores-can-be-memorization, GSM1k, up to 8%). Underneath sat an unexamined assumption — that reconstruction restores the instrument. Tested on four commonsense benchmarks the field has already repaired (WinoGrande→WinoWhat and Physical IQA→PIQA-RUP by paraphrase, HellaSwag→GoldenSwag and Social IQA by filtering), across 23 open-weight models from six families at 1B–72B, the repaired versions preserve the originals' rankings and do not improve downstream predictive power (mean Fisher-z with downstream: originals 1.565, reworked .982, Δ .584, 95% CI [.326, .853]). The validity itself is narrow and mislabelled: under leave-one-family-out cross-validation, False Beliefs reaches R² .483/.695 and TRIP .451/.397, while CEI and Sarcasm are negative for every model and no positive correlation survives FDR correction for CEI, Presupposition, TimeDial or Indirect Requests — and "transfer to particular tasks does not reliably follow the domains implied by benchmark labels," so a social benchmark does not preferentially predict social competence.   • The dose saturates at one copy, and three of the handles are on the demand side (new 2026-07-30). Five model sizes (34M–344M) pretrained from scratch on web text mixed with a swept number of MATH test set replicas. One replica is enough to break through the clean corpus's irreducible-error floor — so the hope implicit in "RedPajama contains 8.53% of HumanEval" (that a low dose might be harmless) is gone, and it composes badly with the bullet below: the detector is blind to paraphrase and the effect saturates after the first instance, meaning the quantity a practitioner can neither measure nor bound is also the quantity that stopped mattering. What is new and useful is that contamination behaves like a dose with handles a model consumer can reach. Fresh tokens dilute it — above ~10 replicas, overtraining on clean data raises test loss, with the threshold falling as models grow (32 replicas at 34M, 10 at 63M, 1 at 93M).   • The standard defense against leakage detects verbatim copies and nothing else (new 2026-07-27). Every corpus builder in this vault decontaminates by n-gram overlap — strip training documents sharing a 13-gram (GPT-3's convention) or a 50-character span (GPT-4's) with a test item. Measured against paraphrase and translation, that detector scores an F1 of exactly 0.000, in every subject and every language tested, against a random baseline of 0.500 — while scoring a perfect 1.000 on unrephrased items, so it is working as designed and simply has no signal on a reworded sentence. The consequence is demonstrated, not argued: Llama-2 13B fine-tuned on rephrased test items takes MMLU from 54.8 to 89.9 (GPT-4 scores 86.4) and GSM-8K from 28.7 to 95.3, and CodeLlama-13B takes HumanEval from 36.0 to 81.1 (GPT-4: 67.0) — all passing n-gram decontamination clean. The obvious upgrade does not hold either: embedding similarity works within a language (0.903–0.985) and collapses across one (0.179 on a Chinese translation, 0.438 on the same problem in C), and no single threshold works across MMLU subjects because their within-subject similarity distributions differ too much. Only an LLM-judged detector (retrieve top-k by embedding, then ask a strong model pairwise "is this a rephrasing?") is reliable everywhere — and it needs the training corpus, so it is unavailable to anyone evaluating a checkpoint they did not build. Operating rule: a decontamination claim without a stated method is uninformative, and since the standard method is n-gram, the modal claim in the field covers verbatim copies only. [T1]

  • A benchmark score is a point estimate of a distribution, and the field reports the point (new 2026-07-28). Turn a benchmark's questions into symbolic templates — names, entities and numeric values become variables with sampling domains and correctness conditions — and regenerate them, and a model can be scored many times on the same reasoning problems with different surface content. Across 50 draws of 100 templated GSM8K items, one 9B model spans more than 12 points and another about 15. Two papers can honestly report very different numbers for the same model on the same task, and no source in this vault reports a spread. The contamination signal falls out of the same construction: the original items are just one more sample, so their score should sit near the centre — and for 21 of 25 models it sits on the right, often beyond one standard deviation. Drops from the published score run −9.2 to −0.3, largest in the Mistral, Gemma and Mathstral families and smallest for the frontier models, which is the same uneven-distribution shape benchmark-scores-can-be-memorization found by authoring a parallel benchmark — arrived at far more cheaply, by templating an existing one. This is the third rung of the detection ladder and the first a model consumer can climb: it needs neither the training corpus (which the strong rephrasing detector requires) nor a model-internal likelihood (which the membership screen requires) — only the benchmark and the ability to rewrite it. Two hard limits. The resolution is population-level, not per-item: it prices a reported number, it does not identify which items leaked.   • Infrastructure non-equivalence now has a scale, and one end of it is safe (new 2026-07-26). The mode has been carried as a uniform warning; it is better read as a ladder, because the training systems in this vault sit at very different points on it. Exact: activation recomputation — "all the memory optimizations proposed in this paper give equivalent weight gradient for training and can always be safely applied." It changes when a value is computed, never what it is, and is the only lever here that never belongs in a results footnote. Silently inexact: sharded optimizer math, which the FSDP authors state is not guaranteed equivalent to local training and measure nothing about. Deliberately inexact: asynchronous pipelining, where without weight stashing "the weight update is not a valid gradient of the loss function for any weight vector," where stashing restores only intra-stage consistency, and where the mechanism that would restore data-parallel equivalence is measured as negligible and ships disabled by default. That last one is the sharpest case in-vault precisely because it is documented and defensible — a model trained by an optimizer nobody would write down, whose weights get compared against models that were. Operating rule: the training system is part of the experimental configuration, almost no result reports it, and the honest ordering when you are out of memory is to exhaust the exact levers first.   • Perplexity is the vault's most-repeated bad proxy, and it now fails in three unrelated literatures (new 2026-07-25).   • 2026-07-24 — fifth construct-mismatch instance, from llama-cpp-quantization-unified-evaluation (T1, primary map local-inference): the failure mode lands inside the compression stack for the first time. Perplexity is the default reporting metric for quantization work — cheap, harness-free, and what llama.cpp prints — and it does not rank the formats: four 5-bit variants span 0.03 PPL and 3.4 GSM8K points, ordering the menu differently from the thing it is deployed for. The mechanism is the familiar one: a token-averaged likelihood is dominated by easy tokens and blind to the few hard ones, while multi-step reasoning fails precisely on the hard ones. General form of the rule: a compression method's quality claim must be measured on the capability being preserved, not on a likelihood proxy — the inference-side twin of intruder-dimensions-are-the-mechanism-of-lora-forgetting's training-side result that equal in-domain scores can conceal structurally different solutions. It also upgrades an advisory this map issued at the context-extension coining from suspicion to measurement: that map's entire evidence base is perplexity, and this is a demonstration of the proxy failing on a neighbouring task. See perplexity-does-not-rank-quantization-formats.

  • 2026-07-20 — ingest of judging-llm-as-a-judge-mt-bench (Zheng et al., LMSYS — Berkeley/UCSD/CMU/Stanford, NeurIPS 2023 D&B, T1): the gap this map escalated twice, closed, and the source that tips it to consolidating. The retroactive reach is the largest of any single source in the vault: it grades an instrument three maps had already been ranking on. Open questions:

  • Does phrasing drift explain any of what this map currently attributes to contamination? (new 2026-08-16, and it is the cheapest reanalysis available to anyone holding both instruments.) Templated perturbation finds a 12–15-point spread and reads the original score's position on the high side as a leakage prior for 21 of 25 models (a-benchmark-score-is-one-draw-from-an-unreported-distribution). Answer-fixed rephrasing finds a much larger spread with no leakage component at all. If the published-score-sits-high signal survives when the comparison distribution is generated by answer-preserving rephrasings rather than by re-templating, the leakage prior is real; if it does not, part of that signal was brittleness wearing contamination's clothes. The run is a reanalysis, not an experiment: the same 25 models, the same GSM8K items, both perturbation families, one plot. Both toolchains are public.
  • Fifth mode or meta-rule — and the test is now runnable (revised 2026-08-02). Recipe leakage has four instances across four literatures and, as of tonight, one direct replication, which is what it needed to stop being a candidate. The remaining question is structural: is "a comparison absent its preparation protocol is uninformative" a fifth item on the checklist, or a statement that sits above leakage, construct mismatch, infrastructure non-equivalence and judge bias? There is a discriminating test and it is cheap. If it is a mode, it should have its own detector, its own dose curve and its own positive exemplar, like the other four. If it is a meta-rule, then each of the four should have an undisclosed-preparation instance of its own — and three already do (a quantization calibration corpus, an ablation's compute budget, a baseline's learning rate). Go looking for the fourth: an undisclosed preparation step that changes a judge result. Judge temperature, system prompt and reference-answer formatting are all unreported in most LLM-judge papers, and if one of them swings agreement the way a calibration corpus swings GSM8K, the meta-rule reading wins.
  • Can a structural probe catch hollow reasoning where a lexical one cannot? (new 2026-08-02.) The four probes that fail at F1 0.530 are simple by design — eight hand-crafted surface features, TF-IDF, and a frozen BERT — and the paper's own diagnosis is that the distinction is structural rather than semantic, which none of the four represents. The obvious next attempt is a probe over features the diagnosis implies: step count, presence of arithmetic operations, whether the quantities given in the question appear in the chain, whether each stated intermediate follows from a prior one. If a cheap structural classifier clears 0.65, this map's newest failure mode becomes affordable to check and the cost asymmetry noted above disappears; if it does not, "you need a large judge" is established rather than assumed. One labelled dataset of 30,000 already exists.
  • The dose is measured at 344M and the trend points the wrong way for extrapolation (new 2026-07-30). The saturate-at-one-copy result is established two orders of magnitude below any model this vault runs, and the authors decline to extrapolate. The vault cannot either — but the direction is the alarming one, since the memorization threshold falls with scale (32 → 10 → 1 replicas across 34M → 93M). The missing measurement is the one that would let a percentage become a discount: the same replica sweep at 1B–7B, on HumanEval rather than MATH, with paraphrased rather than verbatim insertions — since paraphrase is the map's actual live problem and is presumably a weaker dose per copy. Until then "The Stack contains 18.9% of HumanEval" still cannot be priced.
  • A fourth detector exists and no protocol places it (new 2026-07-30). Temperature sensitivity is now the cheapest demand-side probe on this map — two sampling runs, no corpus, no log-probs — which makes the ordered-protocol gap below more embarrassing rather than less: there are now four instruments with four different failure modes and still no published procedure saying which to run, in what order, at what budget, and what a positive licenses. Concretely unrun and cheap: score a distilled small model and its teacher on the same benchmark at τ=0 and τ=1.0 and read the gap — the audit distillation-small-models has wanted since coining, now costing two eval runs.
  • Is the fifth mode one mode or a family? (sharpened 2026-07-30.) Recipe leakage was flagged 2026-07-25 on one paper in one setting (the quantization calibration corpus). It now has three instances in three unrelated literatures — calibration corpus, ablation compute budget, baseline learning rate — which is enough to state the general form (a comparison absent its preparation protocol is uninformative) and not enough to know whether the map should adopt it as a fifth failure mode alongside the four, or as a meta-rule that sits above all of them. It is a property of model preparation rather than of evaluation, which is the argument for the latter. Unresolved deliberately; the article carries it as its own flagged section.
  • Detection without rebuilding — all three families now sourced, as of 2026-07-28. Membership inference landed 2026-07-24 (min-k-prob-detecting-pretraining-data, AUC 0.72 — a prior, not a verdict). N-gram / substring overlap landed 2026-07-27 and landed negative (F1 of zero against paraphrase). Dynamic / perturbed evals landed 2026-07-28 (gsm-symbolic-limitations-mathematical-reasoning) — template the benchmark, regenerate it, and read where the published score falls in its own distribution. The branch is no longer a list of absences; it is a set of three instruments with known and different failure modes, which is what the ordered protocol below has been waiting for. What replaces the gap is the composition problem, restated with the third tool in hand: the corpus-side detector is reliable and unavailable to model consumers, the model-side screen is available and weak, and the benchmark-side perturbation is available and confounded with brittleness. Nobody has written the procedure that says which to run, in what order, at what budget, and what a positive on each licenses you to conclude.
  • The two contamination tools in-vault do not compose, and the gap between them is where every reader of this vault stands (new 2026-07-27). The reliable rephrasing detector needs the training corpus; the membership-inference screen needs only the model and runs at AUC 0.72. A model builder can use the strong one and does not need the weak one; a model consumer — which is everyone fine-tuning an open-weights checkpoint they did not pretrain — can only use the weak one, on a corpus they cannot see, against a contamination class the builder's own pipeline probably missed. Nobody has published a demand-side detector for paraphrased contamination specifically, and it is not obvious one is possible: membership inference scores a passage you supply, and the whole problem is that you do not know which passage to supply.
  • Nobody has measured the dose (revised 2026-07-28). The rephrasing result trains for 16 epochs on nothing but rephrased test items — a maximal-overfit demonstration that establishes the failure exists and says nothing about its size at realistic exposure. The number a practitioner needs is a response curve: given x% of a fine-tuning set as paraphrased benchmark items at n epochs, how many points of inflation? Without it, "8–18% of HumanEval is in RedPajama" cannot be converted into a discount on any published HumanEval score, which is exactly the operation this map exists to support. 2026-07-28 supplies the other end of the same missing curve and shows why it is hard to close. Perturbation gives an observed inflation at realistic exposure — up to 9.2 points between a published GSM8K score and its templated variants — which is the outcome measurement the dose-response curve was wanted for. But it is unattributed: some of those 9.2 points are leakage and some are brittleness, and nothing in-vault separates them. So the vault now has a controlled-exposure experiment with an unrealistic dose and an uncontrolled-exposure measurement with an unattributed effect, and the missing artifact is the one that would join them — contaminate a known fraction of a fine-tuning set with paraphrased items, then measure both the raw score and the perturbation gap.
  • Does the detector survive the models it would be used on? (new 2026-07-24). Every model in the membership-inference evidence is 2022–2023 vintage and ≤65B, trained before the deduplication and decontamination pipelines that data-curation now documents as standard. A modern 15T-token run with aggressive dedup plausibly has a weaker memorization tail — which would make detection harder without making contamination rarer. Untested, and it is the same 2023-vintage-numbers problem this map already flags for judge bias.
  • Nobody has measured whether the four failure modes compound, though the modal in-vault case carries all four at once — and the vault still has no source giving a practitioner an ordered, budgeted procedure for clearing them. Leakage now has its first two rungs; construct mismatch, infrastructure non-equivalence, and judge bias have named modes and no protocol.
  • Domain reach: GSM1k is grade-school math — whether code, reasoning, and instruction-following suites show the same overfitting pattern is unmeasured in-vault.
  • LLM-as-judge has failure modes orthogonal to contamination and needs its own thread. RESOLVED 2026-07-20 after two escalations (2026-07-18, 2026-07-19) — the biases are now graded. What replaces it is narrower and sharper: the in-vault magnitudes are 2023-vintage. GPT-4-0314 and Claude-v1 are retired models; the failure modes are structural but nobody has re-measured their size on the judges scoring today's papers, and the vault is applying 2023 numbers as a discount on 2024–2026 results. Also unresolved: judge reliability on technical, long-form, and domain-specific outputs — the 80% agreement figure is on open-ended chat, which is not the distribution this vault's premise targets. And reward-model failure (a different object from an eval judge) is still ungraded; see post-training-rl.
  • What replaces the judge, when you can afford it (new 2026-07-19): Molmo demonstrates human pairwise preference at 325k ratings cross-checked against automated benchmarks. That is a lab-scale answer. The practitioner-scale version — how to evaluate a fine-tune honestly without a judge model or an annotation budget — has no source, and it is the question this vault's premise most needs answered.
  • Suite averages hide shape (new 2026-07-19): Molmo-72B tops an 11-benchmark average while trailing GPT-4o by 15 points on MMMU. RESOLVED 2026-08-07 — and worse than the question implied. Re-ranking HELM Lite (54 models, 10 scenarios) by weakest-link instead of the mean leaves the two top-five sets completely disjoint, with displacements up to 21 positions, while the overall Spearman stays at 0.89; the effect grows with the number of scenarios averaged. Any "beats frontier on task X" claim — the vault's own premise — needs the per-task breakdown, and the operating form is now sharper: ask for the minimum, not the mean. What replaces the gap is narrower and unrun: nobody has re-ranked a domain-specific leaderboard this way, and nobody has checked whether the disjointness survives when the scenarios are correlated rather than deliberately diverse — HELM is built for coverage, which is the case most favourable to the effect. Also unrun and cheaper: apply the same re-ranking to this vault's own cited suite averages, several of which are the sole support for a graded claim.
  • Construct validity is now a named second failure mode (2026-07-18): a score can be uncontaminated and still misleading if the benchmark surface is narrower than the claim. Detecting that is a different problem from detecting leakage — OOD generalization gaps and spectral checks on the fine-tuned weights are the two probes in-vault, neither of them a workflow. What an honest fine-tune evaluation protocol looks like, covering both failure modes, is unsourced.
  • All four failure modes are named; the protocol that covers them is still not written (sharpened 2026-07-20). The vault can now enumerate leakage, construct mismatch, infrastructure non-equivalence, and judge bias, each with a T1 — but no in-vault source gives a practitioner an ordered procedure for clearing all four on their own fine-tune, at their own budget. That synthesis is now the map's highest-value missing artifact, and it is the one thing standing between this map and consensus.
  • Nobody has measured whether the failure modes compound (new 2026-07-20). A distilled model evaluated by the teacher's own family on a contaminated suite trained under a sharded optimizer is carrying all four at once — plausibly the modal case in this vault — and every source isolates one.
  • The practical decontamination workflow for the craft loop (dedup your fine-tuning set against your eval set, prove it) has no tool-doc source yet — though FineWeb now shows the supply-side version at pretraining scale (benchmark-n-gram removal in the pipeline; data-curation).
