Distillation & small models (students beating bigger generalists) — map

Coined 2026-07-16 (auto run) — first evidence in the "Distillation & small models" group, and the closest in-vault result to the vault's premise: a small open-weights student, fine-tuned on the right data, beating a frontier generalist on a specific domain. Auto-coined; flagged for David's review. Moved emerging → consolidating 2026-07-17 when Qwen3 independently replicated the distillation-beats-direct-training result at production scale (second lab, different base, outside competition-math).

Status: consolidating

What we believe now:

  • What distillation transfers is distributional novelty, not teacher quality — and the recipe's two-stage ordering is load-bearing (new 2026-07-31, and it is this map's first mechanism-level account of its own play). A student already improved by RL, distilled toward a higher-scoring same-family 7B, lands at nearly the same place as one distilled toward its own weaker pre-RL checkpoint: the two teachers are "distributionally indistinguishable from the student's perspective." Post-trained teachers transfer far more than same-pipeline base ones, and a teacher whose thinking pattern mismatches the student's loses benefit that "cannot be recovered later." So the natural rule — pick the strongest teacher you can serve — is unsupported, and it fails silently. The mechanism is narrow and cheap to instrument: the reward is carried by a shared token set holding 97–99% of both models' probability mass, with overlap rising 72% → 91% in successful runs and flat from initialization in failing ones, which makes the overlap ratio a free early-kill diagnostic. And the repair — off-policy SFT cold start before on-policy distillation — is the ordering Qwen3 already ships, derived here independently from mechanism. Bounded: mathematics only, 1.5B–7B, and the novelty condition is confounded by construction. [T1, on-policy distillation specifically]
  • The on-policy signal has a horizon, and it arrives where reasoning traces are heading (new 2026-07-31). Teacher continuation advantage falls from +0.37 at a 1K-token prefix to +0.02 at 16K; the usable response-length window is 3K–7K, with performance plateauing or declining by 10K–15K and instability propagating back-to-front from the end of the response. The authors say the technique "may not extend cleanly to longer-horizon settings such as extended chain-of-thought or agentic multi-turn interaction." Their proposed explanation is explicitly unverified, so the decay is a measurement and its mechanism a conjecture. Note the direction for this vault's premise: 3K–7K is also roughly where a local fine-tune's activation memory is still tractable, so a practitioner distilling on one or two cards is unlikely to reach the ceiling — and equally unable to observe it. [T1, suggestive]
  • Reasoning distills: teacher traces → student SFT transfers capability that direct RL at small scale cannot reach. Same Qwen-32B base: R1-trace SFT hits 72.6% AIME 2024 vs 47.0% for the RL recipe run directly on it. Exploration is expensive and scale-dependent; imitation is cheap at any scale. [T1]
  • Independent replication, now at production scale: strong-to-weak distillation is how a shipping open-weights family builds its small models. Qwen3 distills fully-trained flagship teachers (235B-A22B, 32B) into its 0.6B–14B dense students — off-policy (teacher outputs across both thinking modes) + on-policy (logit matching) — reporting higher Pass@1 and Pass@64 than running the full four-stage recipe per size, at ~1/10 the GPU hours. Same direction as R1, a different lab and base, outside competition-math — this is what moves the map to consolidating. [T2, second group]
  • A distilled 7B beats frontier generalists on the verifiable domain it was distilled for — Distill-Qwen-7B: 55.5% AIME (GPT-4o-0513: 9.3%), 92.8% MATH-500 — and outruns a 32B reasoner (QwQ-32B-Preview: 50.0%). Specificity, not size, is doing the work. [T1]
  • Cost structure of the play: the leverage is access to teacher traces (~800k of them here), not reproducing the teacher's RL run — which is why open releases of reasoning models move this map more than closed capability jumps. [T1]

Through-line (newest first):

  • 2026-07-31 — ingest of on-policy-distillation-dynamics (T1): the on-policy half of this map's production recipe finally has a controlled study behind it, and it is the first source here that measures when the technique fails rather than reporting that it worked. The map holds consolidating and the vault should say why it considered a downgrade. Three findings. (1) The teacher's benchmark score does not predict the student's gain. Reverse distillation: take JustRL-1.5B — a model RL has already improved — and distill it toward its own pre-RL checkpoint, and it regresses to roughly pre-RL performance; distill it toward a higher-scoring same-family 7B and it regresses to nearly the identical level, because "same-family 1.5B and 7B teachers are distributionally indistinguishable from the student's perspective." Paired with the condition that post-trained teachers transfer far more than same-pipeline base teachers, the unifying statement is that distillation's currency is distributional novelty, not teacher rank — which retro-explains R1's traces (a capability the family had never expressed), the 2026-07-25 LIMA scope edge (an only-aligned teacher has thin advantage to transfer), and this map's own suspicion about non-reasoning targets. (2) The dense reward is carried by a token set the two models already share — overlap climbing 72% → 91%, holding 97–99% of both models' probability mass, with optimization restricted to the overlap matching full on-policy distillation and the symmetric difference much weaker. Failing runs show overlap stagnant from initialization, and early mismatch "cannot be recovered later" — so the overlap ratio is a free kill-the-run diagnostic and the non-overlap tail is mostly wasted compute. (3) The dense reward decays with depth: teacher continuation advantage +0.37 at a 1K prefix → +0.02 at 16K, sweet spot 3K–7K, plateau or decline by 10K–15K, instability propagating back-to-front. Why not contested. The map's headlines rest on off-policy teacher-trace SFT (distilled-reasoners-beat-direct-rl), untouched here — and the paper's recommended repair, off-policy cold start before on-policy distillation (worth 15–20 points of initial overlap and a higher final ceiling), is the exact ordering strong-to-weak-distillation-is-the-small-model-build-path records Qwen3 as shipping. That is a third-party derivation of the production recipe's sequencing from mechanism, which is the opposite of a downgrade; what changes is that the ordering turns out to be load-bearing rather than conventional. What is a real narrowing is the practitioner rule this map implied and never stated: "pick the strongest teacher you can serve" is unsupported, and the failure is silent. Bounded: mathematics only by the authors' own scoping — so it sits entirely inside the easy half of this map's biggest open question and cannot help resolve it — 1.5B–7B, the novelty condition is confounded by construction (cross-family comparisons mix data, tokenizer and architecture), and the offered explanation for the depth decay is explicitly unverified by its authors. Every benchmark is competition math, so evaluation-contamination's standing discount applies unchanged. See distillation-gain-tracks-novelty-not-teacher-rank, on-policy-distillation-runs-on-a-shared-high-probability-token-set, dense-token-rewards-decay-with-trajectory-depth.
  • 2026-07-30 — the distill-set audit this map has wanted since coining now costs two eval runs, from test-set-contamination-generative-evaluations (T1, primary map evaluation-contamination): the concern has been on this map since 2026-07-16 — distillation sets are teacher generations, teachers have seen the benchmarks, and a student scoring well on a suite its teacher memorized is measuring the wrong thing. On 2026-07-24 Min-K% Prob removed the corpus-access precondition and made it cost API calls. This removes almost all of the remaining cost. Memorized answers are brittle to sampling temperature and honest ones are not: scores hold flat from greedy through τ ≤ 0.56 then fall sharply, and at high contamination going to τ=1.0 costs ~40×, collapsing to roughly uncontaminated performance. So the audit is now: score a distilled student and its teacher on the same benchmark at τ=0 and τ=1.0 and read the gap. No corpus, no log-probs, no reference model — two eval runs. And the benchmark family measured here is MATH, which is precisely what this map's headline rests on (distilled-reasoners-beat-direct-rl: 72.6% vs 47.0% AIME, 92.8% MATH-500). A second finding lands as a warning about how this map reads its own numbers: above ~10 test-set replicas, supervised fine-tuning on the training split makes test performance worse, because the model forgets what it memorized faster than it learns to generalize. A distillation SFT run on a contaminated base could therefore show a benchmark regression that means the fine-tune is working — the opposite of the natural reading, and a second mechanism (beside narrow-distribution forgetting and the chosen-only-data problem this map already carries) for a regression this map flags. Bounds worth stating before anyone acts on it: the temperature effect is measured at 34M–344M parameters on verbatim insertions, and a long chain of thought is exactly the case the source says is hardest to memorize — so a reasoning-distilled student may be the least exposed case rather than the most, which is an argument the audit would settle rather than assume. See contamination-dose-is-diluted-by-fresh-tokens-and-exposed-by-temperature, one-test-set-replica-beats-infinite-clean-compute.
  • 2026-07-28 — the predicted symptom, on the family this map is built around, from gsm-symbolic-limitations-mathematical-reasoning (T1, primary map evaluation-contamination): this map has wanted contamination audits of distill sets since 2026-07-16. This is not an audit, but it is what one would be looking for. Append a single grammatical, on-topic, operationally irrelevant clause to grade-school math problems and the Phi family takes four of the five worst positions across 25 models — Phi-3-mini −65.7, Phi-3-small −64.0, Phi-3.5-mini −62.5, Phi-3-medium −57.8 — against −32.0 for GPT-4o and −17.5 for a reasoning-trained model. Phi is the canonical case of a small model made strong by curated and synthetic training data, which is this map's central play; and data-curation established on 2026-07-27 that a generate-from-public-seeds pipeline produces paraphrased benchmark items by construction (CodeAlpaca carrying 12.8% of HumanEval). High benchmark scores plus extreme sensitivity to an inert distractor is exactly the profile that pairing predicts. The vault cannot conclude it from this evidence — those models also start highest and so have the most to lose, and the same source's separate contamination signal (published score sitting right of its own perturbation distribution) is smaller for Phi than for the Mistral and Gemma families, which cuts against the simple story. What the map gains is a cheap, specific, runnable test in place of a vague want: score a distilled small model and its teacher on a templated variant of the benchmark the distillation claim rests on, and read the gap.   • 2026-07-28 — a size floor on one capability, from ruler-real-context-size-long-context (T1, primary map context-extension): this map's premise is that a small model can be made to beat a bigger generalist on a chosen task. Long context appears to be a task where that is hard, and the evidence is a controlled ablation rather than a leaderboard — three models from one family, 34B / 9B / 6B, all trained to a 200K window on the same data blend, with the 34B "significantly better than the 6B for both performance at length 4K and relative degradation" as context grows. Across the full 17-model study, every model holding an effective 32K or more is large or frontier-closed. One counterweight the map should keep in view before over-reading: Llama-3.1-8B reaches an effective 32K, so this is not a clean parameter-count law — what fails is small and cheaply extended. Still, it is the sharpest measured limit on the small-model play in the vault, and it lands on a capability (strong-to-weak-distillation-is-the-small-model-build-path says nothing about) that no distillation source here has ever evaluated.
  • 2026-07-25 — scope edge from lima-less-is-more-for-alignment (T1, primary map data-curation): a clean loss for teacher-trace distillation, on a target this map does not actually claim — which is why it sharpens the map rather than denting it. Alpaca-65B is the same LLaMa 65B base fine-tuned on 52,000 examples distilled from text-davinci-003; LIMA is that base fine-tuned on 1,000 human-curated examples, and it wins 53% / ties 21% / loses 26% in human preference. Fifty-two times the data, from a teacher, beaten by curation. Set against distilled-reasoners-beat-direct-rl — 72.6% vs 47.0% AIME at 32B, distillation winning decisively — the two look contradictory and are not, and stating the difference precisely is the useful move: this map's strong result is about reasoning traces whose correctness a verifiable checker can confirm, where the teacher supplies something the student cannot generate and the ground truth is cheap. Alignment style has no verifiable checker, the teacher's advantage is much thinner, and the trace quality is bounded by a teacher that was itself only aligned. So the operating rule this map should carry becomes conditional: distill when the target has a checker; curate when it does not. That also re-frames the 2026-07-19 Molmo push-back (teacher distillation costs the commons) and the 2026-07-20 judge advisory as instances of one boundary rather than three separate warnings. Bound: one distilled dataset (Alpaca) of 2023 vintage, from a now-retired teacher, and Alpaca was never optimized for quality — so this bounds casual teacher synthesis, not the best version of it. Nobody compares distillation and human curation at matched cost, which remains the open question on both this map and data-curation. See alignment-is-format-selection-not-knowledge-acquisition.
  • 2026-07-24 — method edge from min-k-prob-detecting-pretraining-data (T1, primary map evaluation-contamination): the distill-set contamination audit this map has wanted since coining is now runnable. The concern has been stated since 2026-07-16 and never actionable: distillation sets are teacher generations, teachers have seen the benchmarks, and a student scoring well on a suite its teacher memorized is measuring the wrong thing — but auditing it required the teacher's pretraining corpus, which for R1 or Qwen3's teacher nobody outside the lab has.   • 2026-07-22 — reframing edge from speculative-decoding-fast-inference (T1, primary map local-inference): a small model has a second use, and in that use its quality gap is priced automatically. This map's whole play is "train a small model to replace a big one," which makes the student's residual gap a permanent quality cost. Speculative decoding uses the student as a draft model for the teacher instead: it proposes tokens, the teacher verifies them in one parallel pass, and the output distribution is provably the teacher's — so disagreement costs latency, not correctness. A student that agrees with its teacher 75% of the time is a 3× accelerator for the teacher. Two consequences worth carrying: the small-model artifact can be a component of the large model's serving stack rather than a substitute for it; and it implies a distillation objective this map has never optimized for — maximize α (expected per-token agreement with the teacher) rather than downstream benchmark score. Note the counter-intuitive corollary from that source's own table: a better student is not automatically a better draft, since its inference cost eats the winnings (T5-large, α=0.82, is the slowest configuration at 1.7×, while T5-small at α=0.75 gives 3.4×). Unmeasured in-vault: whether reasoning-distilled students (distilled-reasoners-beat-direct-rl) make good drafts, given that their long chains of thought diverge from a teacher's token-by-token. See speculation-trades-arithmetic-for-latency-without-moving-the-distribution.
  • 2026-07-21 — interaction edge from switch-transformers-sparsity (T1, primary map mixture-of-experts, coined this run): the compress-the-architecture direction of this map's play, four years before reasoning traces. A sparse MoE teacher distills into a dense student — initialize the student from the teacher's non-expert weights, 0.75/0.25 hard/soft loss — keeping ~30% of the sparse quality gain at 95% compression (28% at 99%; 14.7B → 223M), and the trick works on fine-tuned teachers too (SuperGLUE 74.6 → 76.6 of the teacher's 81.3). Where this map's headline compresses a capability (reasoning traces → SFT), Switch compresses an architecture (routed experts → one dense FFN) — and the 30%-of-the-gain number is a usefully honest ceiling: most of what sparsity bought does not survive densification. Deploy-relevant because MoE serving memory tracks total params (mixture-of-experts): distill-to-dense is one exit from that bill. See one-expert-per-token-is-enough-to-scale.

The claim "the distilled student beats a bigger generalist" is usually established by a judge — and when the student was distilled from a teacher in the judge's own family, the self-enhancement bias (~10% win-rate inflation for GPT-4 on GPT-4-family outputs) points the wrong way for this map's comfort: it inflates teacher-family outputs, so a student carrying its teacher's style may inherit judge favour it did not earn on substance.   • 2026-07-19 — push-back edge from molmo-pixmo-open-vlm (T1, primary map vision-language-models): the first in-vault argument that teacher distillation carries a cost to the commons, not merely a quality ceiling. Molmo's charge is that the strongest "open" VLMs are proprietary models distilled into open weights, leaving the community "missing foundational knowledge about how to build performant VLMs from scratch" — capability transferred, understanding not. It then builds a top-of-suite model with zero teacher data. This does not contest this map's efficacy claim (distillation works, and works cheaply), but it sharpens what the leverage costs: a distilled artifact inherits its teacher's ceiling, its blind spots, and its license, and teaches you nothing about how to get there another way. Relevant to this vault's premise, where the goal is to understand models well enough to modify them. See open-vlms-are-mostly-distilled-closed-vlms.
  • 2026-07-19 — advisory edge from orpo-monolithic-preference-optimization (T1, primary map post-training-rl): an unasked question about this map's method. Cross-entropy SFT has no term that pushes anything down, and raises the likelihood of rejected responses alongside chosen ones (cross-entropy-sft-raises-the-odds-of-rejected-text). Distilled teacher traces are chosen-only data with no rejected side at all — so a distillation SFT run has, by construction, zero downward pressure on anything the traces do not contain. What that does to a student's refusal behaviour, format discipline, and general capability is unmeasured, and it is a second mechanism (beside narrow-distribution forgetting) for the regression this map already flags below.

Open questions:

  • Is there a measurable teacher-selection criterion, now that benchmark rank is ruled out? (new 2026-07-31.) distillation-gain-tracks-novelty-not-teacher-rank says the gain tracks distributional novelty and that a stronger same-family teacher buys nothing, but leaves novelty as a description rather than a quantity — the authors flag their cross-family comparisons as confounded by data, tokenizer and architecture all at once. The obvious candidate is sitting in the same paper: initial token-overlap ratio, which separates aligned from misaligned pairs at initialization and is computable before any training. Nobody has tested whether it ranks candidate teachers, which would turn a post-hoc explanation into a cheap pre-flight check — score three candidate teachers' overlap with the student on a few hundred prompts and pick by it. That is hours of inference, and it is the most decision-relevant unrun thing on this map.
  • Does the horizon ceiling bind before or after the memory ceiling? (new 2026-07-31.) On-policy distillation's reward decays to near-nothing by 16K tokens (dense-token-rewards-decay-with-trajectory-depth) and a 4-bit local fine-tune's activations get expensive in roughly the same range (distributed-training). Which binds first on one or two consumer cards is unmeasured and decides whether this is a limitation a local practitioner will ever meet. It also means the local case cannot observe the failure, so anyone extrapolating a small local result to a long-horizon run is extrapolating past where the evidence stops.
  • Both in-vault sources are still vendor/authors' own evals (R1 T1, Qwen3 T2); the direction now replicates across two labs, but a neutral third-party reproduction and contamination audits of the distill sets are the map's next sources.
  • Does the pattern hold outside verifiable domains — style, tool use, agentic behavior — where no checker filtered the teacher traces?
  • Task-specific distillation for non-reasoning targets (the classic student-beats-bigger-generalist play on classification/extraction/domain tasks) predates R1 and has no in-vault source yet.
  • What the student loses: general-capability regression from narrow distillation (the catastrophic-forgetting question, shared with peft-methods) is unmeasured here. Sharpened 2026-07-18: that map now carries quantified forgetting evidence (lora-regularizes-more-than-it-adapts) and a mechanism (intruder-dimensions-are-the-mechanism-of-lora-forgetting) — neither applied to distillation students, which are trained on a narrow trace distribution and should be the most exposed case.
  • How is the distillation SFT actually run? peft-methods went contested 2026-07-18: low-rank adaptation underperforms full fine-tuning when the target domain is far from pretraining. Reasoning traces plausibly are such a domain — so whether these students were distilled with full fine-tuning or PEFT, and whether the rank budget binds here, is a question this map cannot currently answer about its own headline results.
