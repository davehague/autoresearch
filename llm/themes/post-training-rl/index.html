Post-training RL (preference tuning & reasoning RL) — map

Coined 2026-07-16 (auto run) — first evidence in "The training pipeline" group. Two reward sources land on day one as distinct sub-threads: human preference pairs (DPO) and verifiable outcome checkers (R1-Zero). Auto-coined; flagged for David's review. 2026-07-20 (auto run): held at emerging, deliberately. SimPO is a third T1 in thread (1) and on method grounds would have firmed this map. It is offset the same run by judging-llm-as-a-judge-mt-bench, which supplies measured bias in the only instrument thread (1) has ever been scored on. The methods are not contested; their measurement is. Status should not rise on evidence whose evaluation the vault just learned to distrust — this is the status-moves-both-ways rule applied to an instrument rather than a claim.

Status: emerging

What we believe now:

  • (1) Preference thread — preference tuning reduces to classification. The KL-constrained RLHF objective has a closed-form optimal policy; DPO reparameterizes it so preference pairs train with a single classification loss — no reward model, no sampling loop — matching or exceeding PPO-based RLHF on sentiment control, summarization, and single-turn dialogue. The affordable alignment lever: operationally the same class as SFT. [T1]
  • (2) Verifiable-reward thread — pure RL elicits reasoning where a checker exists. R1-Zero: no SFT, no human traces, outcome-only rewards → AIME 2024 pass@1 15.6% → 71.0%, with self-reflection and verification behaviors emerging from the incentive. Reach is bounded by what can be automatically verified. [T1]
  • (1a) The preference thread's trajectory is successive deletion — and the reason the stage exists at all is a property of the SFT loss. Cross-entropy has no term that pushes anything down, so fine-tuning on chosen responses alone raises log P(chosen) and log P(rejected) together (OPT-350M on HH-RLHF). Preference tuning is not a polish pass on an already-good model; it supplies the only downward pressure in the pipeline. That explains a common practitioner failure: curating clean good examples reliably underdelivers at suppressing a behaviour, because "absent from the training data" is not a signal cross-entropy can act on. RLHF deleted nothing; DPO deleted the reward model and the sampling loop; ORPO deletes the reference model and the stage boundary as well, folding a small odds-ratio penalty into the SFT loss itself (L_SFT + λ·L_OR, λ ≈ 0.1–0.25) for half the forward passes per batch. [T1]
  • (1b) Two independent groups now agree the reference model is optional — and SimPO names a mismatch none of the others did. DPO's implicit reward is a summed log-ratio against a frozen reference; decoding is governed by average token log-probability. Those are different quantities, and SimPO shows the DPO reward often disagrees with the actual likelihood ranking of the pair it trained on. Making the training reward be the decoding metric — length-normalized average log-prob, no reference model, plus a target margin γ — yields +6.4 over DPO on AlpacaEval 2 and +7.5 on Arena-Hard (Gemma-2-9B-it: 72.4% LC win rate, 59.1% Arena-Hard, 1st on Chatbot Arena under 10B). ORPO (2026-07-19) and SimPO (2026-07-20) reached "drop π_ref" by different arguments within months — that convergence is what firms the successive-deletion trajectory, even though neither validates the other's specific objective. [T1]
  • Hold ORPO's margin well below DPO's, though — the direction is credible, the win is not established. Where the comparison is apples-to-apples (same bases, same data, small scale), ORPO's win rate against DPO is 41.7% at OPT-125M, 49.4% at 350M, 70.9% at 1.3B — a loss, a tie, then a win. The headline 7B results (Mistral-ORPO-β: AlpacaEval 2.0 12.20%, MT-Bench 7.32, vs Zephyr-β 10.99) rest on one dataset for one epoch, nothing above 7B, and three of four headline benchmarks GPT-4-family judged, with the comparison models' numbers lifted from leaderboards rather than re-run. [T1, single-study]
  • The two threads differ in what they buy: preference tuning fits subjective judgments cheaply; verifiable-reward RL needs no labeler but needs a checker — and its product compresses into SFT-able traces (distillation-small-models). [T1, two groups]

Through-line (newest first):

  • 2026-07-20 — push-back edge from judging-llm-as-a-judge-mt-bench (T1, primary map evaluation-contamination): the source behind the caveat this map has been repeating without support since it was coined. Every method in thread (1) is ranked by a GPT-4-family judge, and those judges carry verbosity, position, and self-enhancement biases that are systematic and directional — they do not average out with more samples. The sharpest instance is same-run: SimPO's distinguishing feature is length normalization, and its headline win is measured on judges with a documented length preference. The counterweight the map should also carry: judge/human agreement exceeds 80%, which is what humans achieve with each other — so the instrument is noisy and biased, not worthless. See llm-judges-match-human-agreement-and-carry-systematic-bias.
  • 2026-07-20 — ingest of simpo-simple-preference-optimization (Meng, Xia & Chen, Princeton/UVA, NeurIPS 2024, T1): the second post-DPO variant in-vault and the strongest reported margin in thread (1); partially answers the "ORPO's margin is unestablished" question for the reference-free family if not for ORPO. Its durable contribution is the objective mismatch framing — train the metric you decode with — which outlives the specific loss. See preference-training-should-optimize-the-metric-you-decode-with.
  • 2026-07-19 — ingest of orpo-monolithic-preference-optimization (Hong, Lee & Thorne, KAIST AI, EMNLP 2024, T1): the first post-DPO variant in-vault, resolving the "IPO/KTO/ORPO zoo is unrepresented" gap flagged at coining.   • 2026-07-16 — ingest of deepseek-r1-incentivizing-reasoning (DeepSeek-AI, arXiv 2025-01-22 / Nature 2025, weights MIT, T1): the verifiable-reward thread (2); the distillation half of the paper coins distillation-small-models. See pure-rl-elicits-reasoning.

Open questions:

  • Does DPO's simplicity cost capability at the top end? Frontier labs kept PPO-family pipelines; DPO's wins here are narrow tasks with GPT-4-judged evals. A neutral DPO-vs-PPO-at-scale source remains the thread's missing piece — and note that the same gap now applies one level down: ORPO's DPO comparison is the authors' own, at ≤1.3B where it is APIs-and-all a wash. The judged-eval problem is now this map's structural weakness, not an incidental caveat: every method in thread (1) is ranked by a GPT-4-family judge.
  • The variant zoo is half-mapped. ORPO landed 2026-07-19 and SimPO 2026-07-20; IPO and KTO still have no in-vault sources. The bigger absence is unchanged: no neutral head-to-head of the reference-free family at a scale where differences would show. Both in-vault variants are their authors' own evaluations of their own method, and — per 2026-07-20 — on biased instruments. A third-party bake-off is now the thread's single most valuable missing source.
  • Reference-free methods drop the only anchor to the base model, and nobody in-vault measures the cost (new 2026-07-20). DPO's π_ref is a leash; ORPO and SimPO both cut it. Off-distribution degradation, and whether the gains are narrower than the judged benchmarks suggest, is unmeasured — and it rhymes with the forgetting result in peft-methods (intruder-dimensions-are-the-mechanism-of-lora-forgetting): what a cheap update quietly costs elsewhere is the question this vault keeps finding under the headline.
  • SFT itself — the stage both threads sit on top of — has no dedicated in-vault source, though this run put a sharp dent in the gap: cross-entropy-sft-raises-the-odds-of-rejected-text is the vault's first graded claim about what the SFT objective actually does. (QLoRA's data-quality finding gestures at the data side; see peft-methods.) It also raises a question for distillation-small-models that nobody in-vault has asked: distilled teacher traces are chosen-only data with no rejected side at all — so what does a distillation SFT run do to the behaviours it was never shown?
  • How post-training RL composes with PEFT: DPO/GRPO-class runs through LoRA adapters on quantized bases is common practice with no graded in-vault evidence yet.
  • Reward hacking and eval-judge bias: where those judges fail is unmeasured here. Half-resolved 2026-07-20 — llm-judges-match-human-agreement-and-carry-systematic-bias measures where the eval judge fails (position, verbosity, self-enhancement). Still open: reward hacking proper, and where a learned reward model fails, which is a different object from an eval judge. Also unmeasured — whether those 2023-vintage bias magnitudes still hold for the judges scoring today's papers.
  • Test-time compute as a controllable lever: Qwen3 (distillation-small-models) fuses the verifiable-reward reasoning product and a fast non-thinking mode into one model with an adaptive "thinking budget" — reasoning RL's output made a runtime dial. Unmapped in-vault; a thread to open as sources land.
