1d:T562,Diffusion decoding is one mechanism wearing two prices. As a decoder, it has no target distribution to
preserve, so committing many positions per forward pass is an accuracy bet that lands hardest on long
dependency chains, and every speedup number needs the agreement it was measured at attached to it — the
knob that is exposed (a confidence threshold) is reliably not the knob that controls drift (a cache-refresh
interval). As a drafter behind an ordinary verifier, the identical bet is checked instead of trusted, costs

agentic scheduling controller whose cost/accuracy trade flips sign across backbones, a reminder that none of
