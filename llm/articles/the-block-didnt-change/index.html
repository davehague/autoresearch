The block didn't change: what actually moved in transformer internals

Synthesized 2026-07-19 from transformer-architecture (status: consolidating — moved there this run). Five T1s from four independent groups. As of 2026-07 the architecture claims below are stable; the performance numbers are not — every one of them is attached to a specific GPU, and the kernel work that produced them expires. Check the map's through-line before trusting the numbers in 2027.

The claim

The 2017 block is still the block. Nine years of open-weights models are decoder-only stacks of scaled dot-product attention over Q/K/V, multi-head attention, a position-wise MLP, residuals, and normalization — the component set Vaswani et al. named. Everything a practitioner touches is named there too: the projections LoRA adapts, the K/V tensors the cache stores, the matrices the quantizer compresses.

What moved was mostly not the architecture. As of 2026-07 the vault holds two categories of change, and they are worth keeping separate because they age differently.

Two components genuinely changed form. Both are about a budget, not about capability.

  • Position stopped being additive. RoPE (Zhuiyi Technology, 2021) multiplies the Q and K projections by a block-diagonal rotation matrix scaled to the token's absolute index. Rotations compose — R_mᵀ R_n = R_{n-m} — so the attention score depends only on the offset. You encode absolute position and relative dependency falls out of the algebra. Three consequences: no position table sized to a maximum length, distance decay by construction rather than by training, and compatibility with linear attention that additive relative schemes break. [T1]
  • The KV cache became a design parameter. GQA (Google Research, EMNLP 2023) shares g key/value heads across groups of query heads. On T5-XXL, GQA-8 scores 47.1 average against MHA's 47.2 at 0.28 s/sample vs 1.51 s — 5.4× decode speed for 0.1 points — and an existing multi-head checkpoint converts by uptraining at 5% of original pretraining compute. [T1]

Everything else was systems work on unchanged math. This is the load-bearing observation, and it now has two independent sources rather than one.

  • FlashAttention (Stanford/SUNY Buffalo, NeurIPS 2022) re-scheduled exact attention against the GPU memory hierarchy — tile Q/K/V into SRAM, never materialize the n×n matrix in HBM, recompute it backward from softmax statistics. Memory goes quadratic → linear, 3× on GPT-2 at seq 1K, output bit-identical. The reframing: attention's cost was memory movement, not arithmetic. [T1]
  • FlashAttention-2 (Dao, 2023) found a second bottleneck underneath the first. FlashAttention-1 still hit only 25–40% of an A100's peak while optimized GEMM reaches 80–90% — the gap was work partitioning, not memory traffic. Three fixes: defer the online-softmax rescale to the last inner-loop step (non-matmul FLOPs cost ~16× matmul FLOPs on an A100 — 19.5 vs 312 TFLOPs/s — so that is where the time went), parallelize over sequence-length blocks rather than only batch × heads, and split Q across warps instead of K/V to delete a shared-memory round trip. Result: up to 73% of peak (230 TFLOPs/s), and end-to-end GPT3-2.7B at 8k context moving 80 → 175 → 225 TFLOPs/s per A100. Still bit-exact. [T1]

That last pair is why the map moved to consolidating this run. FlashAttention-2 is not a restatement of FlashAttention-1 — it is an independent re-derivation that finds a different bottleneck under the same unchanged math, which is what a real replication of "systems progress, not architecture progress" looks like.

What to do

  • Read architecture claims and performance claims on different clocks. The block is stable enough to learn once. Any tok/s, TFLOPs/s, or speedup figure is a claim about a kernel-and-card pair on a date. [T1, two sources]
  • Never compare throughput numbers benchmarked on different silicon. FlashAttention-2 demonstrates the trap against itself: its unmodified A100-tuned kernel on an H100 reaches 335 TFLOPs/s while the author states Hopper's TMA, 4th-gen Tensor Cores, and fp8 leave another 1.5–2× unclaimed. Block sizes are hand-tuned per head dimension. The tuning does not travel — that is why FlashAttention-3 had to be a separate paper. [T1]
  • Assume GQA when reasoning about the KV budget. Nearly every open-weights model you would fine-tune or serve already ships with it, which means KV-quantization and paging gains measured on MHA models are upper bounds on what they add to what you actually run. [T1]
  • Know that RoPE is why context length is negotiable after training. Because position lives in a rotation applied at attention time and not in a learned table, there is nothing to resize — which is the structural precondition for the whole position-interpolation / NTK-scaling / YaRN line. Flagged as inference, not as a claim of the RoPE paper: see below. [T1 for the mechanism]
  • The trade each of these buys: GQA spends a little quality for a lot of decode speed; FlashAttention spends recomputation for memory; FlashAttention-2 spends portability for occupancy; RoPE spends nothing measurable and buys structure. Only the first is a quality trade, and it is small enough that the field took it universally.

Not settled

  • RoPE makes no length-extrapolation claim, and the vault has no source for the line that does. RoFormer-1024 was trained at 1024, not extrapolated from a 512-trained checkpoint. Position interpolation, NTK-aware scaling, and YaRN are later work built onto RoPE and are entirely unsourced here — which is awkward, because context extension is the most fine-tuning-adjacent lever in this whole area.
  • The RoPE evidence is thin where it matters most to us. WMT14 En→De moves 27.3 → 27.5 BLEU; GLUE against BERT is a 3–3 split; the long-text win (CAIL2019-SCM 68.10 → 69.79) comes from the extra context, not the encoding per se. It is 2021, BERT-scale, encoder-era. Universal adoption by LLaMA / PaLM / Qwen is real but is not evidence from this paper — all of it postdates the paper. The mechanism consolidates; the margin does not.
  • The kernel lineage is one generation stale again. FlashAttention-2 is explicitly A100-tuned and explicitly leaves Hopper unoptimized. FlashAttention-3 is not ingested.
  • GQA's evidence is encoder-decoder. T5-XXL, from Google. The decoder-only Llama-class replication is universal in practice and unmeasured in this vault.
  • Nobody has stacked the levers. GQA (fewer KV heads), KV quantization (fewer bits), and paging (less waste) all attack the same memory budget, and every in-vault number isolates one of them on its own hardware. A single-runtime, single-card measurement of all three is the sharpest missing datum across this map and local-inference.
  • RMSNorm and SwiGLU still have no in-vault source, nor do tokenization, generation/sampling, or the question the fine-tuning craft leans on hardest — where knowledge actually lives in the weights.
