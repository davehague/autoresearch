21:T416,If you can afford a training pass at all, spend it on distribution, not just on bits: fixed-corpus
recovery leaves reasoning broken well below three bits in a way short-form benchmarks will not show you,
and on-policy recovery against your own deployment path is the cheapest lever this map has for closing
that gap, at least down to roughly 3 bits. Below that, loss design (weight the divergence per token

compute at a plain reverse-KL objective. Freeze your norms and embeddings during healing regardless of
which objective you pick, and if you're using a task loss rather than a KL objective, instrument the
curve past its peak before you ship a checkpoint from early in training. And the format choice itself is

into, is now a demonstrated option at a training-time cost that no longer requires a research budget of
