The artifact grows to fill the capability gap, and the growth is all in the appendix

This map treats the window as a finite decaying resource (context-is-a-finite-decaying-resource) and has accumulated a set of results about artifacts that cost more than they return — LLM-written context files (generated-context-files-cost-more-than-they-help), generic memory scaffolds (memory-scaffolds-can-degrade-long-horizon-agents), evolved skill sets that regress about as much as they gain (skills-regress-as-much-as-they-gain). All of them measure the artifact's effect. None measures what determines its size.

Two models, one task distribution, one evolution procedure. The 4B backbone converges on 40.4k characters of skill; the 27B on 27.9k. The decomposition is the finding: the main instruction file is the same size in both (14.8k against 15.4k, indistinguishable), and the entire difference is in reference material — 25.6k against 12.6k, roughly double. The weaker model does not write longer instructions; it writes more worked examples, more cases, more appendix.

Read as a design signal rather than a curiosity, that is a useful thing to know about a skill bank. A skill's instructional content appears to have a natural length set by the task, and the reference tail is capability compensation — which makes the tail the right place to look first when a bank starts costing more than it returns, and predicts that a bank evolved on a weak backbone and deployed on a strong one is carrying material the strong model does not need. That is the same separation separate-durable-scaffolding-from-model-compensation draws for harnesses, appearing inside a single artifact.

And a quarter of it is free to lose. The proximal audit compresses these skills 19–29% while raising accuracy, and the offline sweep says the artifact stays intact far past that: 52.3% at 25.7% compression, 52.0% at 41.5%, 51.0% at 74.9%, with degradation starting only above roughly 80%. Against a 50.3% uncompressed baseline, cutting three quarters of a mature skill costs less than a point. Whatever a self-evolving bank is accumulating, most of it is inert rather than actively harmful — which is a different diagnosis from the contamination story (an-unguarded-skill-pool-peaks-then-degrades) and coexists with it: some of the accumulation poisons, most of it just occupies the window.

The corollary for anyone running an evolution loop: the loop will not shrink its own output. The final forward-stage skill here is +12.1% longer than the first even under a gate that rejects regressions, because an accuracy gate has no complexity term in it. If the artifact is going to be bounded, something other than the acceptance test has to do it.

Carried as suggestive: two model sizes from one family on one benchmark is a two-point trend, not a scaling law, and the character-count variances are wide (±8.6k and ±12.9k). The compression curve is measured offline on three skills from a third backbone. And "reference material" is a structural label from the skill format rather than a semantic judgment about what the content does.
