The traces were on the filesystem and nobody read them

On 2026-07-29 this map took its strongest positive mechanism: an information ablation holding proposer, search procedure and budget fixed and varying only what the proposer may read found scores-only at 34.6 median, scores-plus-summary at 34.9, and full raw-trace access at 50.0 — with the summarized condition scoring worse than raw scores on best-candidate accuracy. The rule the vault drew was compress what the actor carries forward, preserve what the diagnostician can reach back for (search-the-harness-with-raw-traces-not-summaries).

Here, five frontier models are handed exactly that affordance — a development partition whose disclosure policy includes per-case outcomes and full execution traces — and across 111 runs they request detailed trace spans 16 times, from 7 cells. The share of actions spent reading evaluation output is negatively associated with held-out gain on all four tasks, ρ −0.31 to −0.64. The measure that is positively associated everywhere is breadth: how much of the harness the optimizer touched, ρ +0.34 to +0.88.

What this does and does not contradict. It does not refute the ablation. Meta-Harness intervened on availability, this observes voluntary usage, and the two can both be true in a way that is unflattering to the practice rather than to the finding: the evidence is worth 15 points when you are forced to have it, and optimizers left to themselves do not go and get it. That is a familiar shape in this vault — source-code-access-lifts-root-cause-analysis-the-agents-underuse-it found the same gap between an affordance's value and its uptake, and the-agents-final-report-is-its-least-reliable-artifact is the same distrust pointed at what an agent says it did.

Three readings compete and the source declines to choose, which is the correct call at this evidence strength:

  1. Summaries were sufficient here. The authors' own preferred reading: on these four tasks per-case score summaries may localize failures well enough that reading full traces does not repay its context cost. Note that the disclosure policy already gives per-case outcomes, which is materially richer than the "scores-only" arm of the ablation — so this grid never tested the condition the ablation refuted.
  2. Trace reading is a symptom, not a cause. Both correlations are shares of a fixed action budget, so an optimizer spending its actions reading is an optimizer not spending them editing, and breadth correlates with total modification volume. Confounded by construction.
  3. Frontier optimizers are leaving the gain on the table. If reading 82 files an iteration is what made harness search work in the ablation, then 7 of 111 cells doing anything like it is a capability gap, and the negative correlation is selection: the models that read are the ones already struggling.

The engineering consequence does not depend on picking one. If you are building a harness-improvement loop, provisioning trace access is necessary and not sufficient — the ablation says the signal is there, and this says the model will not go looking for it. Either make diagnosis a required step in the loop rather than an available one, or pre-localize the evidence into what the optimizer already reads. That is the same lesson restate-the-checks-externally-at-completion draws for obligations and fail-loud-instrumentation-over-plausible-substitutes draws for signals: an affordance that must be elected is an affordance that will not be.

Bounds, and they are heavy. Both measures are trajectory-derived and descriptive of process, explicitly not independent measures of held-out capability; the authors say so before reporting them. Spearman ρ over per-configuration means with two replicates each. No causal arm anywhere — nobody ran a forced-trace-reading condition, which is the cheap experiment this note is asking for. Four tasks, Python candidates, one pinned target model per task.

Rebuts: search-the-harness-with-raw-traces-not-summaries — not its ablation, which stands, but the practical corollary that provisioning raw traces is what makes an optimizer improve.
