σ_eval ≈ 0, σ_seed ≤ 10%, σ_data dominant

On 2026-07-14 this map gained the finding that architecture deltas may not be separable from run-to-run variance (coordination-deltas-need-a-measured-noise-floor) — that a floor exists, and roughly how wide. This decomposes it. A crossed grid of three independent data draws × eight training seeds, every checkpoint re-evaluated, fitted with a crossed-ANOVA and bootstrap CIs, across three cells. Two invariants hold everywhere: σ_eval ≈ 0 — re-evaluating a fixed checkpoint is deterministic, so the noise is never in evaluation — and σ_train ≤ 10%, so reseeding is never the main source either. The authors say it plainly: the naive "training-run nondeterminism dominates" story is wrong in every cell.

What remains is the data draw and a run-to-run residual, and the data draw's share grows with difficulty: ~16–22% on cells where the intervention works reliably, 48% and dominant on the hardest, where σ_data = 0.26 exceeds every other component. Which fresh batch you happened to train on — chiefly how much corrective signal it contains — matters most exactly where the effect is marginal, which is where every interesting agent result lives. The decisive control is one line: same trainer, same data, same config, run twice → 0.18 vs 0.68, while re-scoring a frozen checkpoint gave 0.176 both times.

The corollary is the part that indicts current practice. Measuring σ on subsamples of one batch rather than on independent draws halves it (0.18 vs 0.36) and erases the catastrophic-failure mode completely — 0 of 24 runs below 0.10, against 3 of 10 with real draws. A team that believes it is doing variance reporting can be doing the opposite: the cheap version of the discipline hides the instability the discipline exists to expose. This is the map's first instance of a methodological shortcut that manufactures a clean number, distinct from a bad proxy (llm-judges-have-exploitable-biases), a broken grader (fix-the-eval-before-trusting-the-score) or a skewed base rate (skewed-label-bases-inflate-classifier-headlines).

The resulting priority order is short and actionable: control the data draw first, the seed second, average out the residual — and never estimate σ from one batch. Read with agent-improvement-distributions-can-be-bimodal for what to report once you have it, and with scoreable-runs-are-a-separate-denominator (same run, different domain) for the attrition analogue.

Caveats: T3, and the domain is computer-use agents rather than coding — carried for the decomposition method, which is domain-general, explicitly not for the magnitudes, which are properties of one 35B policy in one environment family. Two authors, no venue; the training/serving backend and the oracle-graded environments are proprietary and unreleased, so the numbers are not reproducible. Three cells, one policy for the decomposition. The measured quantity is a frame-level corrective-action emission rate that is verifier-circular by construction; the authors say so and break the loop only on their DB-oracle end-to-end results.
