The lease expired, and the worker holding it is still typing

This map has established that unattended fleets need externalized durable state and that a resume contract is a real contract rather than a checkpoint (the-durable-unit-needs-a-contract-not-a-checkpoint, durable-execution-engines-do-not-supply-barrier-semantics, build-the-work-queue-from-disk-state). The mechanism it has not carried is the one that decides who is allowed to write when two workers both believe they own the job.

The near-universal implementation is a lease: a worker claims the work, renews while making progress, and the control plane reassigns when renewals stop. That is correct and it answers exactly one question — liveness and allocation. It does not establish that the previous worker is dead. A process paused under memory pressure resumes after its lease expires. A partition heals after reassignment already happened. A mutation issued before expiry arrives after it. A lease does not guarantee a single writer, and any system that treats lease expiry as permission to reassign and as proof the old worker is gone will eventually let a corpse commit.

The separable mechanism is a monotonic ownership epoch, incremented on every reassignment, carried on every mutation, and — this is the load-bearing detail — validated at the boundary being protected: the branch head, the work ledger, the external-effect adapter. Checking at the scheduler before dispatch is worthless, because the scheduler's belief about which workers are alive is precisely the information the failure invalidated; by the time the stale mutation arrives, that check has long passed. Holding the current epoch confers the right to be evaluated, not a presumption of correctness — which is gate-the-edit-on-evidence-observed-not-claimed restated for concurrency.

Two corollaries the monograph attaches:

  • A retry is a new attempt on the same logical work, not a second independent task. Unless repeated execution is established as idempotent or its side effects are isolated, a timeout produces duplicate comments, partial writes, and two workers claiming one resource — the shape gate-irreversible-actions-on-independent-confirmation guards at the action layer, arriving at the scheduler.
  • A worker's self-report can move an attempt to outcome-ready and no further. Only independently observed evidence completes logical work (ground-completion-in-a-rerun-not-a-self-report, treat-agent-output-as-a-claim-not-lifecycle-state).

The lineage is deliberately old — Chubby's sequencers pass a generation number that servers validate rather than trusting the lock still holds; ZooKeeper's premise is that coordination state belongs somewhere that survives the processes it coordinates. The monograph transfers the principles and not the deployments, and says plainly that a single durable database recording epochs transactionally is enough for a modest fleet. That is the version that matters for a personal fleet of scheduled agents: the record of who currently holds authority must survive the failure of the workers it governs, and a lockfile written by the worker does not.

Bounds and grade. T3 — an engineering contract from a single-author review, supported by distributed-systems literature none of which was measured on an agent workload, and by author-operated cases that the monograph explicitly bars from carrying evidentiary weight. Carried as a design to adopt rather than a number to cite.
