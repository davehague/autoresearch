Two of every three points of backend noise are a disclosure failure, not a fact about the stack

The finding in the-inference-backend-is-a-term-in-the-score is easy to read as fatalism: the plumbing moves the number, nothing to be done. The variance decomposition says the opposite about most of it. Stacked in order — structural effect, then sampling, then each framework's own default hyper-parameters — the variance runs 0.00074 → 0.00112 → 0.00190, so the part a practitioner actually encounters is dominated by two terms that cost nothing to remove: decode greedily and state the generation configuration.

The size of the default-configuration term is not an accident of sloppy frameworks; it is what happens when each serving stack picks a reasonable fallback in isolation. vLLM discards the model's own tuned generation_config and samples at temperature 1.0 with a 16-token output cap. Ollama reads the Modelfile and otherwise falls back to temperature 0.8 with unbounded output. The HuggingFace family honours the model's config with a 20-token cap. Nobody is wrong and no two agree, and a benchmark harness that supplies no parameters inherits whichever one it happened to import.

The practice this licenses is small enough to adopt tonight and is the direct sibling of agent-benchmarks-underdisclose-their-harness one layer down: publish the backend, its version, and the complete generation configuration with every score, and use deterministic decoding as the only regime in which a cross-backend comparison means anything. Where the comparison is between two of your own configurations rather than between published numbers, the same rule is what makes a paired delta legible at all — coordination-deltas-need-a-measured-noise-floor measures a floor, and this removes an avoidable floor before you pay to measure it.

The residual is real and is the part to be honest about. hf_pipeline matching its own reference exactly proves the instrument reads zero when there is nothing to read; vLLM diverging on one model of three under greedy decoding, on numerically identical weights, proves that what is left after configuration matching is kernel arithmetic and template handling rather than measurement error. That residual is disclosable and not correctable, because it is model-dependent: there is no table of per-backend offsets to publish.

Bounds as in the parent note — ~1B models, English multiple-choice and short-form generation, no coding, mathematical reasoning or agentic tasks, one hardware configuration, five generations per prompt in the stochastic arms. The reporting rule is what transfers; the magnitudes are not portable.
