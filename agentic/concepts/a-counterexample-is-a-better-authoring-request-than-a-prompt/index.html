The loop's problem was never the sampler — it was that "write me a better X" is the same request every time

This map's search loops mostly vary what gets edited and what the acceptance test is. This result attacks a third thing, and it is the one nobody in the vault has been treating as a design variable: the form of the authoring request.

Two obstacles are measured before any method appears. First, the space of possible artifacts is enormous and a model asked for "an evaluation operator" samples a tiny fixed region of it — and resampling does not move that region. Scoring every one of the 295 operators the study authored at that narrow interface yields 9 hits at p<0.05 against 14.75 expected by chance, so a false-discovery correction returns no discoveries at all. Second, what it does write repeats itself behaviourally rather than textually: no source-text pair among 187 candidates reaches Jaccard 0.8, yet the 183 that flag anything realise only 96 distinct flag sets. And a rejection teaches the loop nothing — it learns that a candidate failed, not which distinction it failed to draw.

The borrowed discipline is counterexample-guided abstraction refinement from program verification. Read the current artifact pool as an abstraction mapping each input to a signature (the vector of verdicts the pool returns). A signature class containing both a correct and an incorrect labelled item is proof that the pool cannot express that distinction, and that pair is handed over as the specification — not "write a metric" but "catch faults like this one". Each admission re-partitions the signatures, so the next request has moved by construction: the target supplies the diversity pressure that the sampler could not.

The escalation rule is the transferable half, and it is counterintuitive. When three attempts fail on a target, the loop does not resample — it moves the same target to a wider interface, letting the authored artifact read the candidate's peers and run them under a budget. The gate ledger locates why exactly: across 124 narrow-interface consultations, none ever cleared three helped tasks (it reaches two and stops there 55 times, hurting nothing), while the wider interface cleared it 6 times in 27 and every one was admitted. The extension does not buy the direction — the narrow interface finds that easily — it buys generality, the third distinct case. Removing escalation stops admission outright (0/0/0 against 1/1/1). And a completely independent search sharing nothing with this one but the interface reproduces the wall: 2 admissions of 48 at the narrow interface with a delta its own null does not reject, 6 of 16 at the wider one. Two searches failing at the same place is what makes this a property of the interface rather than of the method.

The negative result the loop can return. When every target has been abandoned at both interfaces, the loop emits an insufficiency certificate: the vocabulary, not the author, is the limit, and it names the distinction that could not be expressed. That is the first mechanism in this map by which a failed search produces information rather than a shrug — compare harness-optimizers-fabricate-failures-to-fix, where a search with no such stopping condition invents work.

Two honest limits inside the loop's own results. Targeting was expected to fix behavioural duplication and made it worse — the directed arm was the least diverse of three, and a novelty term at admission recovers a genuinely new artifact in only 2 of the 11 candidates it blocks; handed the incumbent it duplicated, the model duplicates it again, so the restriction sits in the author's prior rather than at the gate. And the loop is wasteful without being exhausted: 9 of 14 admissions on fresh seeds rediscovered an already-selected artifact, one composition headline did not survive the rediscovery screen the authors then added, and rarefaction over behavioural classes still yields 2.00 new classes at the sixth seed.
