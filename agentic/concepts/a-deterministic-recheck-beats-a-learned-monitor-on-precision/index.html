Recompute the number from the receipts

This map's thesis is that agents should be audited from trajectory-level evidence rather than from outcomes, and its recurring practical problem is cost: audit-agent-claims-against-trajectory-evidence and localize-first-wrong-step-not-final-answer both work, and the standard implementation puts a second model on every step. This source prices the alternatives properly, and its most useful contribution is not the cheap monitor it builds but the comparison it runs against a check that needs no model, no null and no threshold.

The three checks are unglamorous. total_consistency recomputes the run's stated total from the tool results the run actually received. required_coverage confirms every call the task required was made. tool_contract asks whether a returned result matched any shape its tool can produce. None reads the hidden world the task was generated from, so all three are deployable as-is.

Head to head on the same episodes with the same labels, recall is comparable and precision is not: 60% (96% with coverage) at 0 of 63 false positives against the monitor's 54% at 17%. At the higher temperature the gap widens on both axes. And the two properties that make a detector shippable both belong to the checks. They transfer unchanged across model families — 110 of 110 failures at 0 of 10 on llama3.1:8b with nothing retuned — where the monitor calibrated on qwen2.5:7b and deployed on llama3.1:8b sits at chance (AUROC 0.527, healthy false-alarm 0.75) until recalibrated on itself (0.885). And they score the class the monitor structurally cannot: 26 of 26 provoked fabrications, on a corpus where provoking enough fabrication leaves too few healthy episodes to fit a one-class null at all.

tool_contract deserves separate note because it is the cheapest and the most immediately portable: 0 of 1,825 healthy episodes flagged across every labelled corpus, catching 46% of context corruption and 44% of looping, and firing 215 of 218 times within one step of onset. A shape check on tool returns is perhaps fifty lines and it is the earliest signal in the study.

This is the observability-layer instance of a pattern the vault now has in four places. total_consistency is measure-whether-evidence-reconstructs-the-claim made arithmetic; the coverage check is an-output-check-cannot-see-a-skipped-step answered directly; the shape check is fail-loud-instrumentation-over-plausible-substitutes applied to tool returns. What is new is the measured comparison against the learned alternative on the same episodes — the field's default reflex when an agent might be going wrong is to add an observer, and here the observer is beaten on precision, on portability and on the failure class that matters most, by code that does arithmetic.

The monitor is not thereby worthless, and the paper is careful about where it earns its place. It covers looping (0.48–1.00) and goal drift (0.66–0.86) — classes with no deterministic invariant to recompute — at ~200 µs per step, three orders of magnitude below a judge call, and it fires before the answer is delivered rather than at it. The honest architecture is the layered one the results support: a shape check at every tool return, deterministic recomputation at every asserted figure, a cheap behavioural monitor for the classes with no invariant, and an LLM judge as escalation rather than default.

The design rule to carry: for every quantity your agent states, ask whether it can be recomputed from what the agent actually received. Where it can, that check will out-precision anything learned, cost nothing, and survive a model swap. Where it cannot — drift, looping, plausible-value corruption — you are in statistical or external-reference territory and should expect a per-deployment calibration burden and a real false-alarm rate.

Bounds. Single independent author; agent models are 3B–8B locally served plus one commercial API, with no frontier model and no frontier coding harness in the corpus. The checks were authored by inspecting failures in the serving arm, which is why the frozen 120-episode disjoint-seed replication matters — and it does cost the expected margin (60% → 54% on totals, driven by a small hallucinated class at 4/8). The tasks carry a recomputable total by construction, which is exactly the condition the checks exploit; how much of an arbitrary agent workload admits such an invariant is not measured here.
