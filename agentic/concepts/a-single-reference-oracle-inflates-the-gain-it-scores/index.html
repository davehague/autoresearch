The oracle's expected value came from inside the population it judges, and nine to fifteen points of the reported improvement is that fact

This map's core claim is that independence is engineered by controlling what the checker is denied, and its third provenance term, added 2026-08-19, is where the checker's expected value comes from. an-oracle-anchored-on-the-code-it-judges-cannot-fail established that term as exact arithmetic on one deterministic assertion in one system by one author. This is the same term measured as a quantity, on 394 tasks, two models, three seeds, with the prediction registered before the external cohort was opened — and it is the independent replication that note was missing.

The mechanism is one step removed from anchoring and lands in the same place. The oracle here does not draw its expectation from the code under test; it draws it from one accepted program in the same population the tests are supposed to discriminate. That removes output hallucination — the model's own answer is discarded — and leaves the harder half untouched: nothing validates the input. An input outside the natural-language contract still executes. The reference produces some output. Historical faulty submissions disagree with it, for reasons having nothing to do with the fault, and the disagreement is scored as detection. Optimisation then finds more such inputs, and more search reads as more learning.

The upstream number tells you how bad the raw material is. Even restricted to inputs where every accepted implementation completes and agrees — the most favourable conditioning available — the model's own expected output matches the panel 27.79% of the time on the locked external cohort. A call in which all the emitted tests are panel-confirmed happens 2.53% of the time. The stronger model roughly doubles the first figure and is still nowhere near usable as a reward signal, which the authors are careful to call replication under a different checkpoint rather than a scaling law.

The remedy is cheap, and that is the finding a practitioner uses. A second independent accepted implementation cuts the apparent gain from ~11 pp to ~4; a third takes it to ~1–2. The audit runs after selection is frozen, offline, so it is an evaluation standard rather than a deployment-time cost. Compare an-evaluator-sharing-its-reference-solver-cannot-be-self-checked, which is the same disease diagnosed from the evaluator side, and online-judge-verdicts-are-an-unaudited-ground-truth, which is the same population — platform verdicts standing in for proof. This map's version of the rule: differential agreement across independently authored implementations is the weakest usable substitute for a specification, and one implementation is not a substitute at all.

The paper's most disciplined move is refusing to call panel disagreement truth. Two blinded doctoral reviewers, denied task identity, generation method, archived output, program behaviour, panel outcome and each other's judgments, label 94.41% of disconfirmed inputs invalid — and 3.60% valid, with one valid case supporting an auditor-side output rather than the primary reference. So panel execution is a conservative consistency filter, not semantic ground truth, and the authors keep a 67-case adjudication packet open rather than forcing consensus. That distinction is exactly what the-referent-not-the-reviewer-carries-the-separation is about: agreement among accepted programs is a referent, and its quality is a measurable property rather than an assumption.

Bounds. Accepted-program agreement is a benchmark-specific empirical oracle, not proof of specification conformance — accepted programs can carry or share defects, and legal outputs can have multiple textual forms. Scope is Python exact-output competitive-programming tasks with normalised whitespace; no special checkers, no floating-point tolerance, and no claim about repository test suites where a trusted project suite exists. Two human reviewers who may share training and interpretation biases, neither of whom used the available ambiguous label. Development tasks were exposed during method development and the fault-family analysis is post-hoc; the external cohort is the prospectively locked one, and its inflation figure (9.46–10.39 pp) is the one to quote. Two model conditions, three seeds, temperature 0.7 — replication, not a scale effect.
