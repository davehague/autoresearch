The score moved and only the serving stack changed

This map's existing claim is that the agent harness — scaffold, tools, container, retry policy — is an unreported term of the same order as the effect being reported. This puts a floor under that claim one layer lower, at the software that loads the weights and emits the tokens. Three instruction-tuned models were run across five inference frameworks on six benchmarks under four generation regimes, with hardware, precision, prompts and evaluation protocol fixed. The comparison is honest by construction at the top rung: hf_pipeline wraps the same in-process model object as the reference hf_raw, and under greedy decoding it diverges by 0.000 on every metric — so the instrument can register zero when zero is the truth.

Under greedy decoding, where there is no sampling noise to blame, the backend still changes the answers. Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct diverges from the reference by 0.055 under Ollama (significant on 9 of 11 per-item metrics) and 0.049 under LangChain — and LangChain is running the same weights on the same engine, so the mechanism there is prompt-template rendering alone. vLLM is numerically identical to the reference on two of three models and diverges 0.025 on the third, significant on 10 of 11 metrics, from nothing but a different attention kernel under non-associative floating-point arithmetic.

The practical partition is the useful part. Decomposing by generation mode: structural backend effect 0.00074, plus sampling noise 0.00112, plus each framework's own default hyper-parameters 0.00190. So roughly 39% of the spread a practitioner sees out of the box is structural and the other 61% is avoidable by disclosing and matching the generation configuration — which is a cheap instruction, not a research programme. The default configurations are not close to each other: vLLM ignores the model's own generation_config and samples at temperature 1.0 with max_new_tokens=16, Ollama runs unbounded output at temperature 0.8, the HuggingFace family inherits the model's tuned config with a 20-token cap.

Two reading rules fall out. First, the effect is an interaction, not an offset — Gemma-3-1B-IT is almost immune (no divergence above 0.010, at most 4 of 16 severe configurations on any task) while Llama is severe on 14 of 16 configurations across four benchmarks, so there is no per-backend correction to subtract, exactly as no-harness-ordering-holds-across-models found one layer up. Second, the mean understates the churn: almost every severe cell sits above the y = x diagonal relating |delta| to per-item disagreement, and disagreement reaches 48% where the aggregate barely moves. A score that is stable in the mean can be composed of different correct answers, which is what kills a paired comparison.

Bounds. This is ~1B-scale instruction-tuned models on English multiple-choice and short-form generation — the authors state explicitly that they do not cover code, mathematical reasoning, multi-turn dialogue or agentic tasks, which is where every claim in this vault lives. One GPU, one CUDA version, one seed, five generations per prompt in the stochastic modes, and hf_raw is a reference rather than ground truth, so all divergences are relative. The Ollama arm mixes serving-stack behaviour with GGUF weight conversion and the authors decline to separate them, arguing correctly that a practitioner choosing Ollama incurs both. A concurrent group (Pape et al., 2026) reports the same phenomenon at larger scale; this study's contribution is the crossed decomposition, not the discovery. Carried as strong for the existence and the avoidable/structural split, suggestive for anything about frontier-scale agentic harnesses.
