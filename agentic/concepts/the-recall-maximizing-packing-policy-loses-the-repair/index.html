The flag was adopted for the metric it was tuned to, and it cost seven and a half points of resolution

This map's newest clause, from skill-use-precision-collapses-while-success-does-not, is that a retrieval score and a downstream outcome are independent measurements rather than consecutive stages. That result was observational — grow the candidate pool, watch precision collapse ninefold and success hold. This is the causal version, and it is worse than independence: at a tight fixed budget the two move in opposite directions, and the configuration a practitioner would rationally adopt is the one that loses.

The premise is not an artifact. File-level recall@k asks whether the gold file appears anywhere in the k slots, so a second slot inside a file already present cannot help it — dedup-ON is near-optimal for that metric by construction, and the recall advantage reproduces on the packs the models actually saw (0.878 against 0.806), not just in the offline index eval. This is a hard, file-keyed instance of the maximal-marginal-relevance family: exactly the diversification heuristic the retrieval literature recommends.

The evidence is unusually disciplined about which arms carry weight. The headline is +7.6pp at n=500 with McNemar exact p=0.0003, surviving repository-clustered inference with all twelve leave-one-repository-out folds positive and no single repository driving it. The open-weights replication is pre-registered and independently re-runnable, survives the same clustering, and holds under complete-case and Manski bounds. DeepSeek is demoted to supportive rather than confirmatory because its conditional estimand is null — patch production is a post-treatment mediator, so conditioning on "both arms non-empty" removes a channel the treatment acts through, and the authors report both estimands and say which is primary. The dense control is called a directional replication because its clustered interval includes zero, even though it is instance-level significant. That is report-a-null-as-a-bounded-interval-not-a-failed-test applied to one's own supporting arms.

And the alternative explanations are killed by design, not by argument. If the harm were that dedup keeps the wrong within-file chunk, then replacing the argmax with a random non-argmax chunk from the same files should recover toward depth. It does the opposite: 3.0% against dedup-ON's 6.6% against depth's 12.4%. The argmax is beneficial; argmax-selection bias is refuted. A parse-apply-test funnel kills the edit-format artifact — among patches that actually applied, depth still wins on test-pass by +5.2 to +8.0pp across three arms. A contamination check finds the per-repository effect essentially uncorrelated with GitHub stars (ρ ≈ +0.12), with the effect substantial in the low-popularity stratum. And a token explanation is ruled out: both arms fill the same twelve slots, the depth pack is only 5–6% larger, and the effect survives in the stratum where the depth pack is the smaller of the two.

The dose-response is the part that changes what you do. The advantage does not wash out as budgets grow — it grows, +4.80pp at four slots to +9.20pp at forty, tested as a difference-in-differences rather than eyeballed. dedup-ON is flat across K while dedup-OFF climbs. Extra slots spent on within-file depth keep paying; extra slots spent on file breadth do not.

This is proxy-metrics-rank-models-not-suites and retrieval-benchmarks-omit-the-quality-axis arriving with a causal toggle and an execution-graded outcome, and it makes the map's operational rule concrete: at a tight fixed budget, do not hard-deduplicate by file. The scope conditions are severe and mapped in the-recall-trap-is-a-property-of-the-regime-not-of-retrieval — this is a claim about pack-as-sole-context serving, not about tool-using agents.
