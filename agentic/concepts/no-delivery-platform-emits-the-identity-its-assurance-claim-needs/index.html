The record cannot say what ran, so no gate downstream of it can refuse a promotion on that ground

The paper's framing earns the finding. Provenance asks where a thing came from; traceability asks how things relate; justifiability asks whether the evidence available at the time licensed the transition — and it is the only one of the three that can return no. The distinction is quotable from the lower levels' own primary texts: in-toto states that judging whether a supply-chain layout is sound is not its role, PROV imposes "no prescriptive requirements on the nature of plans," GUAC returns answers and never refusals. What this measures is whether the field's records can even express the claim a promotion gate would have to refuse on.

The dependency this exposes is the one that matters to anyone shipping agents. Behavior depends on a tuple — source, model version, instructions, retrieval configuration, tool definitions, runtime configuration, environment — whose components vary independently of the source. So artifact continuity is necessary but not sufficient: identical code under a different model version breaks behavioral continuity while preserving artifact continuity. The paper is careful about its own demarcation: where the components cannot vary independently (models pinned, prompts in-tree, no runtime retrieval, static tools), the tuple collapses onto the source digest and conventional artifact provenance is sufficient. That is the escape hatch, and it is narrow.

Two design details are worth stealing regardless of the survey. First, behavioral continuity is undecidable by digest equality over a whole record, because a promotion crosses environments and the environment is supposed to differ — so it needs an explicit invariant-by-contract / variant-by-design partition declared as constants rather than inferred. Source, dependency-set, instruction and interface-contract digests, runtime library versions and behavior keys naming models, tenant and guardrail policy block on mismatch; environment label, config-file digest and per-environment keys are reported and never block. A framework that omits the partition either reports false breaks on every promotion or, comparing nothing, reports none. Second, "bound" counts only when populated with a content-addressed value — a rule that makes the difference between a rung and a label.

The reliability argument runs against the author's own interest, which is why this grades strong on a documentation survey. All four divergences on the load-bearing column had the hypothesis-aware pass grading default — the one label that would refute the headline — and the blind pass grading stricter. A grader captured by their hypothesis errs the other way.

Bounds. Documentation is not behavior: the survey grades what vendors state. That asymmetry insulates the negative cells (where documentation overstates emission, the survey errs generous) and inflates every affirmative count, which the author states plainly as an upper bound. Grading is judgment, mitigated structurally but with raw agreement over a skewed label space and no chance-corrected statistic claimed. Single author adjudicating, AI-assisted grading disclosed, raw pass files archived unmodified. The finding is falsified by exhibiting one platform that emits a content-addressed tuple identity by default, and expires rather than fails when the standardization efforts ship such a default — with a stated re-run no later than 2027-02.
