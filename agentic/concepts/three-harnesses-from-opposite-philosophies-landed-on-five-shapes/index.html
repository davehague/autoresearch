Two harnesses walked toward each other from opposite ends and met in the middle

This map already holds that the harness is the size of the model term and that no harness ordering is portable (no-harness-ordering-holds-across-models, attribute-performance-to-the-stack-not-the-model). What it has not had is a statement of where harness architectures are going, read from source rather than inferred from scores. This supplies one.

The design is worth as much as the result. Two cases ground a candidate model of the convergent form; a third is read afterward as a held-out check. Comparison dimensions were fixed a priori, before close reading, precisely so a framework could not be fitted to observations. Claims are anchored to file:line at pinned revisions, two defects were re-derived in a sandbox, two plugins were written against one runtime, and one documentation fix was merged upstream — so a reader can re-check the reading rather than take it.

The five shapes. A commoditised loop: none of the three competes on the loop, and the author states the falsifier — a harness built around a large proprietary loop would refute this. An append-only replayable session record. Model quirks kept as data rather than conditionals. Progressive disclosure of context (name, description, path in the prompt; body read on demand). Explicit extension seams instead of a monolith, where the disagreement is composition style — ordered middleware versus event bus versus service locator — never whether seams should exist.

Two readings make this more than a taxonomy. First, no element recurs as the same code: quirks-as-data is a class hierarchy in one, a data directory in another, a runtime resolver in the third. That is convergence of idea under shared pressure, not copying. Second, only the session record forms a strength ladder rather than a cluster — a view over history, an append-only entry log, an event-sourced log with a runtime assertion that anything the model saw is reconstructable — and the ladder points directly at the dimension where nobody has climbed at all (no-harness-produces-a-record-an-outsider-can-check).

The practical instruction is unusually specific: treat the five shapes as settled, adopt the strongest available form of each, and spend novelty on the unsettled dimensions. If you are hand-rolling a loop, mutating session history, coating quirks in conditionals, front-loading context, or fusing extensions into a monolith, you are re-deriving a lesson three teams already paid for.

It also qualifies the "less harness" thesis this vault has been tracking. deepagents is indeed subtracting, which fits "stronger models need less scaffolding." But pi is simultaneously accreting durable infrastructure — session records, crash recovery, quirk catalogues — that has nothing to do with coaching the model and everything to do with surviving long autonomous runs. The lesson is not thinner everywhere but thinner in coaching, thicker in durability, and how thin is set by audience: the residual disagreement between the three is whether the user is a product embedding an agent or a person at a terminal who wants to see everything.

Bounds, and the author states them first. N = 3, one point in time, coding-agent harnesses only. The three are not fully independent and the paper refuses to claim they are, decomposing the convergence into parallel discovery, diffusion, and one case of literal reuse. The five elements were induced during reading rather than fixed a priori, and are marked the primary construct risk. A single analyst read the source with AI assistance, one case less deeply than the other two. The checklist is scoped to attended long-horizon sessions; the author explicitly declines to press it on a scaffold that runs unattended to a benchmark score.
