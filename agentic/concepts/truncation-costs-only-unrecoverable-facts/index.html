The map's 2026-08-18 instrument dispute — how much of "decay" is plain absence — gets its first design that manipulates recoverability directly rather than inferring it, and the two readings it produces (pure cost, pure accuracy loss) are exactly what you'd expect if they're genuinely separable mechanisms rather than one phenomenon

Since 2026-08-18 this map has held an unresolved tension: the strongest T1 that controls fact availability directly reports a supplied fact is used as reliably at the far end of a 128,000-character context as beside the edit, while this map's own positional-decay and substitution findings were all measured where eviction was possible and coverage was not controlled — so the map cannot say how much of what it calls decay is plain absence. This source is the first to manipulate a third, related but distinct variable in one design: not whether a fact is present, but whether, once dropped, it can be re-observed or re-derived at all. The result is clean and bimodal rather than graded: when recoverable, truncation is purely a cost story — the model simply re-derives the fact, at a measured, bounded token/dollar/cache-rate penalty, with zero accuracy loss across two independent providers' models. When not recoverable, truncation is purely an accuracy story — success collapses to within a few points of chance (7.5%/0%), a lost fact behaving exactly like a fact that was never available to the map's own prior absence-controlled source. This does not resolve the 2026-08-18 dispute — PersistGym-lite is a controlled synthetic probe, one template family, not a naturalistic long-horizon trajectory, so it cannot yet tell the map how much of its existing positional-decay catalogue reflects unrecoverable loss versus recoverable loss nobody measured the recovery cost of. But it is the first evidence that the map's two readings of "decay" (value genuinely lost vs. value temporarily unavailable but reconstructable) produce qualitatively different signatures — one all-cost, one all-accuracy — which is itself new information: a future source that finds a graded accuracy loss under truncation, rather than this bimodal pattern, would be evidence the real-world case mixes recoverable and unrecoverable loss within the same window, something this synthetic design cannot speak to by construction. Flagged open-question rather than confirms/extends because the paper itself frames its contribution as separating two hypotheses the map had conflated, not as settling which one explains the map's prior naturalistic findings.

Interaction edge: agent-harness-architecture — the same source's ARC-AGI-3 harness-gap decomposition (see arc-agi3-harness-gap-decomposition) is the applied, real-benchmark face of this synthetic result: summarizing instead of dropping, paired with a note-taking instruction that makes dropped content recoverable-by-restatement, recovers the majority of a real harness's measured gap — the same mechanism (make the harness preserve recoverability, not just raw history) this map's own persist-and-deliver strand has been arguing for on different grounds.

Bounds: PersistGym-lite is a controlled synthetic probe with one template family and one task shape; whether real agent trajectories decompose as cleanly into "recoverable" and "unrecoverable" loss, rather than mixing both within one window, is untested here.
