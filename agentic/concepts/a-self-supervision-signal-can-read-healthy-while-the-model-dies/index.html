The vote got better while the model was dying

The finding is one arm of an audit whose subject is something else, reported as a diagnostic rather than a result, and it is the sharpest thing in the paper for anyone running an unattended loop.

The arm trains a model with its own majority vote as the reward — the loop grades itself. It collapsed on all three seeds into unbounded repetition. What the audit noticed while watching it collapse: on one seed, in the window between the update that damaged the model and the update that finished it, the majority vote's accuracy rose from 77.9% to 85.1% and its unanimity from 0.78 to 0.86, over 248 intact majorities — while the same checkpoint's evaluation went from 47% truncated to 96%.

The mechanism is not mysterious once stated. Agreement is a measure of consensus, and a model degenerating into repetition is maximally consistent with itself. Every quantity the loop was watching improved. The one quantity nobody was watching — how many completions ran off the end — is where the collapse lived.

This is the failure mode an-independent-checker-beats-the-same-model-checking-itself predicts, observed at the level of the training signal rather than the review verdict, and it converts a design preference into an operating rule:

  • A self-consistency signal is not admissible as a health check on the run that produces it. Vote share, self-agreement across samples, judge-agrees-with-generator — all of these move the wrong way under exactly the failure they would be deployed to catch.
  • The second signal has to be derived from something the model does not author. In this case the disqualifying evidence was mechanical and free: truncation rate per checkpoint, a byte count. See match-the-controls-design-to-the-arms, where logging generation length per checkpoint is one of the seven controls the audit recommends — it earns its place twice, once as a confound control and once as the only instrument that saw this.
  • A kill rule keyed to agreement will fire last. For a scheduled loop the practical form is a cheap non-model-derived tripwire (output length distribution, format-parse rate, wall-clock per step) evaluated before any quality signal, because the quality signal is the one that lies.

Carried as single-study: one arm, one backbone family, three seeds, and the collapse is a known pathology of that configuration rather than a general property of vote-supervised training. What generalises is not the collapse but the observation that the supervision signal read healthy throughout it.
