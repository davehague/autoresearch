The window was never the mechanism; the union of the two channels was

Model the task as a graph of coupled facts. At each edit a required fact arrives from recent context or from parametric memory, and whatever neither covers is coherence debt: D(e_i) = |C(i)_T \ (R_ti ∪ K_M)|. The union carries the whole prediction — a fact may arrive through either channel, so the outcome should turn on coverage and not on which channel supplied it, or on where in the window it sits.

It does, and the linearity is what makes the claim hard to argue with. Because the workload is authored, the required facts are known rather than inferred, so the intervention is exact: remove the value from m of eight motifs and leave everything else untouched. The median trial loses exactly four tests per withheld motif, at every level, under both removal modes. Maximum deviation over the nine cells is zero tests. Absence adds over the graph; it does not cascade through it. That is the arithmetic a debt count assumes when it sums uncovered facts, and it had not previously been measured.

This pushes back on the positional half of context-is-a-finite-decaying-resource, and only on that half. The finite-resource claim survives intact — the closed-book floor is exact at 0/154, and putting the same facts in the prompt takes 299 of 300 matched trials to near-ceiling, which is as clean a demonstration that context substitutes for missing knowledge as this map has. What does not survive is the inference the map has been drawing from lost-in-the-middle and long-context decay results: that where material sits in the window is a design variable of the same order as whether it is there. Vary distance alone and success is flat to 128k characters. State sixteen invariants once and they hold across 96 subsequent tasks and 140k tokens with no reread and no decay.

The authors decline the strong reading, and the reason is the useful part. These arms sit at ceiling, so they bound large effects rather than excluding small ones — and, more importantly, presence and residency coincide here because nothing evicts. A fact they supply stays supplied. The working-set account predicts the two separate only once a trajectory runs long enough to lose one, and the capacity result says they never reached that point. So the honest statement is: presence is measured, residency is bounded rather than confirmed, and every decay result this map carries was measured on trajectories where eviction was possible and coverage was not controlled. Nobody has run both manipulations in one design. Until someone does, an intervention that improves "where the fact sits" and an intervention that improves "whether the fact is there" have been priced against each other exactly once, and only one of them moved.

The practical form is narrow and immediately actionable: keep the facts an edit depends on available when the agent writes, and stop spending on placement. The corollary is a-stale-convention-file-costs-more-than-no-file — coverage is necessary and not sufficient, because two covered facts can disagree and the debt count has no room for that.
