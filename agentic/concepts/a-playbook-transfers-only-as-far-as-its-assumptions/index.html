Portability is a property of the assumptions, not of the artifact

This map has spent a month narrowing what is worth persisting from an agent's experience. Persist the contract, not the conversation (persist-the-contract-not-the-conversation). Raw trajectories are 22 points worse than a structured abstraction of the same trajectories (abstract-past-trajectories-raw-ones-do-not-transfer). Generic memory scaffolds degrade every model tested (memory-scaffolds-can-degrade-long-horizon-agents); LLM-written context files cost more than they help (generated-context-files-cost-more-than-they-help); a skill library trades working behaviour for new behaviour at task granularity (skills-regress-as-much-as-they-gain). All of that answers what to write down. This answers the next question, and it is the one teams actually face: the artifact exists and works here — does it work there?

The answer has a shape rather than a sign. Under matched conditions the transfer is real and large: greedy decoding on ALFWorld, positive on every target, up to +23.6 points, with shorter trajectories rather than cost inflation, and beating five canonical few-shot demonstrations by +10.8 points at near-matched prompt budget. Move to a heterogeneous service-agent setting and the same protocol yields a +1.72-point average matched-domain advantage — real, prespecified, and small — with almost no route-level effect surviving multiplicity correction (correct-a-transfer-grid-for-multiplicity-before-reading-it carries that half). Break the runtime assumption and the artifact turns actively expensive (an-imported-artifact-breaks-stopping-before-it-breaks-accuracy).

The temperature audit is the finding I would put in front of an engineer. The same four artifacts, the same targets, decoding as the only change: at t=0.3 the detailed artifact flips from helpful to clearly harmful on the 7B target while staying robust on the 32B, where the compact variant collapses instead. Robustness is decoding regime × target capability × artifact specificity — three-way, with no main effect you can rely on. Nobody validating a playbook is varying temperature as part of the check, and this says they should, because the sign of the effect changes there.

What the placebo buys. A same-length generic rewrite — matched length, sectioning and checklist style, with domain procedures, tool names, prerequisite logic and action-ordering rules removed — stays near baseline on all three routes checked while the real playbook reproduces its point-estimate direction. So the transferred content is doing the work, not the fact that a structured block of text was prepended. That is the control a-retry-study-needs-a-placebo-arm demands, run on a context artifact, and it is the reason this source can make a directional claim at all. It also raises the bar for every context-file result in this map that lacks one.

Where this sharpens the vault's existing rule. a-standing-policy-decays-into-one-more-retrieved-source and a-loaded-skill-is-not-a-retained-requirement establish that a persisted instruction loses force over a session. This establishes something adjacent and distinct: an instruction can retain full force and be wrong for where it landed. The failure is not decay, it is a false premise — the playbook confidently asserts a first-check, a tool priority, a recovery step, and the target's workflow, capability or budget no longer matches the source's. The mitigation follows directly and is the paper's own framing: treat playbook reuse as a validation problem, and validate four things on the target — success, termination, protocol compatibility, and cost. Termination and cost are the two nobody checks, and they are the two that fail first.

The honest scope note the authors insist on: frozen reuse is not their recommended default. It is a cold-start or amortized option for when target-side failure trajectories are unavailable, expensive, or constrained by privacy or latency. Once target traces exist, redistilling is a live alternative — and in their exploratory head-to-head across three domains, no strategy dominates (target-derived wins one domain, 7B-source transfer another, 32B-source the third) at similar interaction cost.

Bounds. Targets are Qwen2.5-Instruct / Qwen3 / Llama3.1 checkpoints with closed frontier models as distillers, so artifact quality is not held fixed against the target family. TAU2 route-level cells have wide intervals and are descriptive after correction. The placebo is three selected routes, not a format ablation across prose, JSON and Markdown styles. Human-authored playbooks and deployment-time retrieval are named as outside the comparison, so nothing here says a distilled artifact beats a well-written human one.
