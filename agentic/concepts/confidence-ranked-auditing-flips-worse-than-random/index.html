The queue can't be sorted by what the agent says about its own work

The setup is the one a fleet operator is already in: N persistent agents, a human who can audit B ≪ N of their outputs per round, and an obvious-looking way to spend the budget — audit the least confident outputs first. The signal is free, it is per-item, and it is supplied by the auditee.

Model the fleet as budgeted noisy inspection with two coupled imperfections — self-reported confidence whose informativeness degrades with an adversarial-miscalibration strength δ, and errors correlated through a two-level Gaussian copula — and confidence-ranked auditing has a flip. Past δ* it is dominated by picking at random. That much was expected. The direction was not: the authors state they expected δ* to fall as budgets tightened, and it rises — 0.75 at a 20% audit fraction, 0.93 at 2%. The mechanism is clean once said. Overconfident errors pile up at the high-confidence end, so the extreme low-confidence tail stays comparatively honest. A tiny budget only ever touches that tail and is protected by its own scarcity; a generous budget reaches down into the poisoned mid-ranks and flips first.

That inverts the intuitive remedy. Faced with a review queue sorted by an unreliable signal, the natural move is to buy more reviewer capacity. Doing that without fixing the signal spends the new capacity exactly where the signal is worst.

The empirical half is what makes this more than a phase diagram. Six models answering fixed 500-item manifests under one locked elicitation protocol: the five open-weight models emit near-constant verbalized confidence (Var(c) ≤ 0.016) piled at ≈1.0 regardless of correctness, so AUROC sits at 0.50–0.52 and the ranking is jitter — confidence-ranked auditing is not approaching random on these models, it is random, by construction. Only the hosted model carries usable signal and lands clearly below the flip.

This extends rather than contradicts review-effort-is-organizational-not-artifact-determined, and the pair is what matters. That result closed the artifact route to triage: submission-time features explain 12% of variance in time-to-merge, so there is nothing in the change itself to sort a queue on. This closes the self-report route. A capacity-constrained reviewer therefore cannot rank by what the change looks like or by what the agent claims about it, which leaves the levers this vault already names — reviewer supply, reviewer diversity, enforcement moved into gates (enforce-high-risk-constraints-below-the-prompt-layer), and shrinking the artifact (codeslop-is-the-search-surviving-into-the-patch).

It also lands on the unattended case with more force than the reviewed one. An operator sampling a fleet's work is choosing what to look at from signals the fleet produced, and this vault has already measured the narrative half of that: the closing summary asserts compliance essentially regardless of what happened (the-agents-final-report-is-its-least-reliable-artifact). The standing rule was that self-report cannot be a term in the admission decision (treat-agent-output-as-a-claim-not-lifecycle-state). It now has to cover the softer decision nobody thought to protect: which runs get looked at in the first place.

Bounds worth carrying. The phase diagram is simulated; the empirical work measures the model's premises, not the policy outcome in a deployment. The fleet is six question-answering models on GSM8K and HotpotQA, not coding agents on repositories, so transfer is by mechanism. δ̂ comes from one locked protocol — single-completion, direct-answer, no chain-of-thought — and the authors are explicit that separate-turn elicitation, logits or sampling variance might recover signal it suppresses. The five open-weight models have error rates high enough (0.43–0.94) that the inversion behind their placement is extrapolated, which is why their confidence intervals straddle the flip even though the point estimates clear it. The replay fleet is N = 15 and the authors call it preliminary. And agents are assumed honest-but-miscalibrated; deliberate under-reporting is scoped out.
