The confidence interval was for the tasks; nobody put one on the definitions

This map's standing demand is that a delta is not a result until its null is measured. This adds the axis the demand has been silently assuming away: which null, under whose definition of a scoreable row.

The two quantities are not comparable and the paper refuses to pool them. A finite specification interval summarizes variation over declared definitions — it is a range over a set of admissible meanings, and it does not shrink with more data. A paired or task-cluster bootstrap interval summarizes sampling variation for a fixed estimand under a fixed resampling design; under a superpopulation reading it treats the observed tasks as exchangeable draws while holding the semantic specification fixed. Reporting the second and calling the result robust is the error, because more tasks make the bootstrap narrower and do nothing at all to the other axis.

The numbers on this substrate make the point without argument. The sampling interval for the audited pair margin is [−12.10, −3.92] pp — clean, one-signed, the kind of interval a result gets published on. The simultaneous specification-plus-sampling envelope is [−13.36, +18.89] pp: it contains zero and both signs. Nothing about the data changed. The extra width is entirely the range of defensible definitions of what the evaluator was scoring.

Two supporting quantities are worth carrying because they are the ones a reader can look for elsewhere. The complete observed sign pattern across the eight specification cells appears in only 24.1% of bootstrap draws — so the pattern that made the original result look coherent is not itself stable under resampling. And the pooled effect is heterogeneous by corpus (−2.40 pp with no within-corpus reversal against −10.42 pp, interaction +8.02 [+1.47, +14.95]), meaning the headline reversal is concentrated in one of the two corpora. That is report-the-delta-inside-a-difficulty-band applied to composition rather than difficulty: a pooled delta whose components disagree is a delta whose subject is unstated.

The kinship to this map's existing catalogue is exact. a-null-measured-once-is-a-point-estimate says a measured null needs its own variability; this says the same for the definitional axis, where the variability is not estimated by repetition at all but enumerated by declaring the family. report-a-null-as-a-bounded-interval-not-a-failed-test asks what effect the design could have detected; the addition here is that the design's semantics also has a range, and it is not detectable by any amount of resampling. And match-the-controls-design-to-the-arms is the same instinct one layer down.

The reporting rule, stated plainly: name the declared family, report every alternative in it, report the specification interval and the sampling interval separately, and never let a narrow one stand in for a wide one. The paper's audit record makes this a checklist rather than an aspiration — fixed substrate, semantic map, endpoint plus declared family plus inference license, admissibility with a freeze receipt, every jointly frozen alternative, and the two uncertainty axes reported apart.
