Program structure is a controllable, independent lever on top of what a spec says — a skeleton narrows the decomposition space and cuts both the tests needed and the cost to a working implementation

This map's accumulated evidence varies the specification's content (behavioral completeness, scoping declarations, decision pins) and where it lives (a durable document, a working brief, a formally checked Lean certificate). This paper identifies a third, orthogonal axis: the internal program structure the agent chooses to satisfy the spec, which an end-to-end behavioral spec never constrains and a developer never sees. Different structures require different numbers of behavioral checks to pin down fully — some decompositions make correctness ambiguous in ways that require many more tests than a decomposition chosen to minimize that ambiguity.

Skeleton Coding operationalizes this: the developer supplies a lightweight, code-like structural scaffold — the shape of the decomposition, not an implementation — alongside the usual behavioral spec. The theoretical result (an exponential reduction in required tests under a formal control-problem model) is an idealization, but the empirical numbers track the same direction at a practical scale: cutting mean test error nearly in half relative to the strongest end-to-end spec, and cutting the dollar/token cost of reaching a working implementation by an order of magnitude or more. The small-model/large-model gap narrowing (52.4%) is the most actionable number for cost-sensitive deployments: a well-chosen skeleton substitutes, in part, for raw model capability.

Interaction edge: model-routing-token-economics — if structural scaffolding narrows the capability gap between small and large models by roughly half, that is a direct lever on model-routing decisions: a cheaper model plus a skeleton may match a frontier model's unscaffolded output on tasks where the structure is known in advance.

Bounds: the four models and three benchmarks are not named in the available abstract, so benchmark diversity and realism cannot be assessed independently; all headline figures are empirical means reported without variance or per-model/per-benchmark breakdown; the theoretical exponential-reduction claim holds under a formal idealization of AI-assisted coding as a control problem, which may not map cleanly onto messier real repositories.
