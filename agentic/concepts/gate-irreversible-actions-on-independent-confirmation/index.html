Make the stop available in the weights, admit it at the gate

This map's layering puts deterministic containment first, a contextual pre-execution classifier second, and human escalation last (contain-at-the-environment-layer-first, gate-actions-with-a-contextual-classifier-dial). Here is the classifier layer measured on the narrowest possible action class — the irreversible one — with a failure mode that shows why the gate cannot be replaced by better training.

The setup separates two problems that a single intervention normally conflates. Fine-tuning installs the terminal action: the base policy never emits done() (0 of 15 frames), and after the update it does, reliably. But at small data the same update over-generalizes it and stops on incomplete frames. Weights answer can it stop; they do not answer should it stop now. The deployment answer is an admission gate: the policy's proposal of done() — or any irreversible action — is accepted only if a separate model, judging completion from the same observation, confirms. Weights plus gate produce correct timing that neither weights alone nor the base policy reaches.

Two properties make this more than one system's engineering. First, the gate's judgment is not idiosyncratic: on a completion probe four different verifiers agree ≥14/16, and an open 72B model ties a closed frontier model at zero false positives — so "an independent confirmer" is a role you can staff, not a dependency on one vendor. Second, it is the vault's independence-of-observer property applied to the irreversibility boundary rather than to correctness. an-independent-checker-beats-the-same-model-checking-itself measured a cross-model re-derivation at 79% recovery against self-verification's 71%; structure-outweighs-the-verification-step watched rescues fall 6 → 2 when observe/compare moved back onto the generating model. The same asymmetry decides admission here, and for the same reason: the acting policy's internal state is consistent with its own mistake.

The governance reading is a small, cheap primitive worth generalizing: any action the environment cannot undo needs a second opinion produced from the environment's state, not from the actor's belief about it. That composes directly with persisted-state-without-revalidation-amplifies-errors (re-derive carried state before anything irreversible) and with external-effect-gate-closes-the-sibling-leak-with-formal-verification — an admission check at the effect boundary is the same fix, and the barrier this map keeps finding absent (sibling-leak-defeats-human-in-the-loop-approval-gates).

Caveats: T3, computer-use agents rather than coding, one policy, small n, and the gate's contribution is reported as a qualitative resolution of over-correction rather than with a measured rate — there is no false-accept/false-reject table for the gate itself. The verifier plays three roles in the frame-level metric (selecting frames, supplying labels, defining the counted action), which the authors disclose; the only verifier-independent numbers here are the DB-oracle end-to-end ones. Environments and backend are proprietary and unreleased. Carried as a design primitive with one study behind it.
