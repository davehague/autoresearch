Actionability beats insight, and it is not close

The measured hierarchy of what gets a review comment acted on: can I apply this with one click (OR 1.62), then is it short (longer is worse), then — barely — is it well written. Relevance, clarity and conciseness all clear significance on tens of thousands of comments and all land at effect sizes the authors call negligible.

The counterintuitive term is that having an explanation is negative (OR 0.593) while having a particular kind of explanation is positive (rule-based 1.14, benefit 1.09, example 1.08), and usefulness peaks at two explanation types before declining. The consistent reading across all of it is a cost-to-act story: every additional sentence a maintainer must read before deciding is a tax, and explanation only pays when it is the kind that licenses a decision quickly.

This lands on the map's capacity ceiling from a new direction. comprehension-debt-outpaces-review-capacity and automated-review-displaces-human-review-at-scale establish that reviewer attention is the scarce resource and that triage cannot relieve the shortage (review-effort-is-organizational-not-artifact-determined). This says the scarce resource is being spent on reading, and that the cheapest available lever on a reviewer agent is not making it smarter but making its output applicable — which is a harness change, not a model change. It sits beside codeslop-is-the-search-surviving-into-the-patch as the second automatable lever against the ceiling: shrink the artifact under review, and shrink the comment about it.

The uncomfortable corollary is that this is also a recipe for a reviewer that scores well without being right. A confident one-line comment with an attached patch is the most-resolved shape in the corpus, and nothing in the resolution signal distinguishes a correct one from a plausible one (resolution-rate-is-not-precision). Optimizing an agent against this metric optimizes for frictionless application. Pair any such optimization with a false-negative and false-positive measurement the metric cannot supply (verifier-catch-and-false-alarm-rates-are-measurable).

Caveats: resolution is the dependent variable, so this predicts adoption and not correctness; Python GitHub OSS only; LLM-assigned quality scores; effects stated to be largely driven by the single vendor supplying 83% of the corpus.
