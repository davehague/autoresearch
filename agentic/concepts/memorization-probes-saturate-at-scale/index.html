The contamination detector was validated on models nobody deploys

This map's central claim is that every cheap proxy for agent quality has been independently shown to misgrade. Its contamination limb has so far been built on counterfactuals — block the retrieval channel and re-measure (benchmark-scores-inflate-when-agents-can-retrieve-the-fix, search-time-leakage-inflates-web-agent-scores) — which this result leaves entirely intact. What it takes apart is the other method the field uses, the one that requires no environment control: perturb the prompt, read the pass@k drop, call the difference memorization.

Encoder-side probes go inert exactly where they are needed. Synonym fuzzing at 20% and 40% costs frontier models at most 2.1 to 5.4 points and, on two of three benchmarks, gains the strongest model points. The same protocol collapses sub-22B models, and the mid-tier is thresholded rather than graded — Llama-3.1-70B absorbs 20% fuzzing (−7.9) and folds at 40% (−27.3). A scaled encoder collapses semantically equivalent surface forms; that is what capacity buys.

The probe also fails its own prediction, which is the sharper indictment. If synonym fuzzing disrupted memorized retrieval, the largest drops should appear on MBPP — the one benchmark plausibly contaminated for every model tested — and be smaller on the two benchmarks that postdate every training cutoff. They are not. Relative losses on the post-cutoff benchmarks are often comparable or larger, confounded by baseline difficulty, so the measurement mixes encoder fragility with memorization and cannot separate them. That is the same defect this map catalogues everywhere else: an instrument responding to a nuisance variable correlated with the arm under test.

The decoder-side probe degrades on the same axis. CoDeC scores a corpus by how in-context examples move token log-likelihoods, then reports the AUC separating seen from unseen. At Pythia 410M, 1.4B and 12B it is perfect — 100.0. At Llama-3.1-70B it is 25.0, i.e. worse than a coin flip on four points; davinci-002, Nemotron-340B and Llama-405B sit at 75.0. The underlying scores converge as probability mass spreads over a larger set of plausible continuations: at 70B, seen scores 8.0% against unseen 9.0%.

The rule for reading the literature follows directly, and it is uncomfortable because most memorization results in circulation predate the models people run: a memorization or contamination figure obtained by prompt perturbation or likelihood probing carries information only for models inside the scale band where the probe was validated. Ported upward it does not report "less memorization" — it reports nothing, and it reports it as a small number. Both this map's remedies survive: build tasks from private artifacts (build-tasks-from-private-artifacts-not-public-issue-text), and license an inflation claim only with a counterfactual that blocks the channel and re-measures.

Bounds. Dense architectures only, by design — the authors say adaptation to mixture-of-experts routing is open. Python only, three benchmarks, five greedy completions per cell. The CoDeC arm rests on two seen and two unseen corpora per model, with the seen labels inherited from another group's designation, so each AUC is computed over four points. And "scale" is doing work the design does not isolate: the authors name training duration and composition as a largely uncontrolled variable, since two models of the same size can sit on opposite sides of a grokking-like transition.
