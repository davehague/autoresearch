Against a no-message baseline, the nudge is most of the effect

The setup is as favourable to the informational account as it gets. The feedback is true, specific, counterfactual and cheap: the harness takes the check the agent just watched pass, replays it on the original buggy code, and tells the agent the result. If the check passes there too, the agent is told its evidence does not distinguish the two states. That is real information the agent has no other way to obtain, delivered at the moment it matters.

Against a baseline that injects nothing, it moves evidence-inadequate closure 10.5 points. Against a generic reminder matched in structure, length, timing and directive strength — carrying no replay information at all, just a prompt to reconsider whether the evidence targets the reported defect — it moves it 7.8. So the reminder alone is worth roughly a third.

And then the second model reverses the split. On gpt-5.6-terra the generic reminder produces 7.5 points on its own, matching the full effect of bug-contrast feedback on the first model, while the replay content adds nothing detectable on top. Feedback reached 97.4% of intervention rollouts, so this is not a dosing artifact. Two models, one lever, and the component that survives on both is the one carrying no information.

This is the second arrival at that shape in four days. On 2026-07-30 a placebo-controlled retry study put the entire informational payload of real execution feedback — feedback minus a content-free failure notice — at +0.000 / +0.026 / +0.008, none significant at any scale (a-retry-study-needs-a-placebo-arm). Different task class, different intervention, different failure mode, same result: once you subtract the act of being interrupted and told to think again, the content is doing much less than it appears to. Two independent groups have now built the active control this vault has been asking for, and both found the control does most of the work.

For loop-engineering the practical rule follows directly and it is a measurement rule, not a design one. An agent-feedback intervention evaluated against "we sent nothing" is measuring attention. The comparison that licenses a claim about your feedback is a structure-matched message with the content removed — and it is nearly free to run, since the arm is a string. The map already holds the harder version of this for the verifier itself: measure its discrimination before granting the loop a budget (an-independent-checker-beats-the-same-model-checking-itself). The addition is that the channel now needs its own placebo, because a loop can improve for reasons that have nothing to do with what you put in it.

Two asymmetries make this less deflationary than it sounds. First, the intervention is genuinely cheap — 11 seconds of replay, no extra model call, token cost indistinguishable from the control arm — so deploying it on the model where it does help costs nothing much even if the mechanism is partly placebo. Second, and pointing the other way: the scaffold beats both arms. Under a plan-execute-verify structure that forces a verification phase before submission, baseline evidence-inadequate closure is already 8.3% against 18.9% for the same tasks in the unconstrained loop — a larger improvement than either message achieves, obtained by changing where the decision happens rather than what is said at it. That is the same lesson the map keeps recording from a different angle: what a loop iterates on is worth more attention than what it is told (re-derive-file-state-instead-of-storing-snapshots).

Bounds: two models from one provider and family, two Python benchmarks, and the scaffold and second-model results come from exploratory replications on 20 shared tasks with two repetitions, so their intervals are wide and the authors mark them descriptive. The confirmatory estimates themselves fall below the paper's own pre-registered 10-point smallest effect size of interest — the direction is established, the practical magnitude is not, and the authors say so rather than reframing it. The second-model worst-case missingness bound can reverse its sign; the confirmatory one cannot.
