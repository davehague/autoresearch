The matched score is a sum, and both terms are bigger than the score

Every harness result this map holds was produced by swapping something and watching the leaderboard move. That design proves a confound exists and cannot say which layer produced it, because a scaffold swap changes context handling, retry policy, verification and plumbing at once. This study fixes the model's output and changes exactly one parser. Everything that follows is attributable to the transport by construction, and the authors say so about the prior work explicitly: replacing the harness wholesale "measures variance but cannot attribute a reversal to any one mechanism."

What the fixed-reply replay shows. Take the reply the model already produced, run it directly, then run the same bytes interpolated into bash -c "…". Success falls 55.4 to 73.2 points in all eight configurations. This is not an adversarial-payload artifact — the fourteen benign control tasks alone lose 28.6 to 57.1 points, because models emit double-quote-active characters when writing ordinary commands. Only a quarter to a third of direct-path successes survive the added parser.

Why that matters for reading a published number. Tell the model in one sentence that its reply will be interpolated — no quoting advice, just the fact — and six of eight configurations recover 30.4 to 60.7 points. The two contrasts sum exactly to the matched gap an ordinary evaluation reports. GPT-5.6-sol's matched gap is −3.6 points: a number any reader would call "basically no effect," composed of −64.3 points of damage and +60.7 points of compensation. This map has argued that the harness is a term of the order of the effect being reported (the-harness-shifts-benchmark-rank-order, attribute-performance-to-the-stack-not-the-model); here the term is an order of magnitude larger than the effect and arithmetically invisible in it.

The reordering is the familiar shape. Kendall τ between the raw and deployment orderings is 0.57, and the top rank flips: selecting on raw success picks a model that reaches 50/56 on the deployed path, where the path-aware pick reaches 51/56. Consistent with no-harness-ordering-holds-across-models, there is no correction to subtract — the compensating ability is model-specific, spanning +60.7 to −5.4 across the same eight configurations.

One warning about the obvious mitigations. Effort ladders do not fix it. Across 26 crossover points the nested-replay pass rate of raw-conditioned replies moves at most 5.4 points within any ladder, while matched success climbs; Opus-4.8's matched gap improves from −48.2 to −3.6 as its damage grows from −58.9 to −67.9. A matched evaluation would call that repair. It is compensation on a declared path, with portability unchanged.

Bounds. One mechanism, one shell, one-shot generation, no multi-turn recovery. The 14 families are purposively built for mechanism attribution and carry no prevalence claim; the reported intervals quantify variation across those constructed families, not sampling of tasks or model calls. Effort-rung numbers rest on a single stored generation each. Hosted snapshots move under fixed identifiers, so the 2026-07-31 query date is part of the result.
