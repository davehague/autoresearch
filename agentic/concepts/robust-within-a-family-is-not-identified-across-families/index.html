Nobody changed a prediction; somebody changed what counts as scoreable

Every result in this map so far varies something the system does — the harness, the scaffold, the prompt, the task set. This varies something the evaluator does, with the artifacts and all seven prediction rules frozen, and gets a 43-point swing out of it.

The formal move is small and worth carrying verbatim, because it converts an argument into a check. An evaluation specification s controls a semantic map (which references are legal, which rows are eligible), a row-scoring rule, and an aggregation rule (including denominators and weights). Define the finite empirical identified set I_Q(F) = {Q(s) : s ∈ F} over a declared family. Point identification means |I_Q(F)| = 1. Two consequences do all the work:

  • One admissible disagreement witness establishes non-identification. You do not need a prevalence estimate.
  • Expansion cannot restore it: I_Q(F) ⊆ I_Q(F′) for any F′ ⊇ F. Adding more robustness checks can only grow the set.

And the corollary that indicts the standard robustness paragraph: if F_0 ⊊ F, then even zero variation over F_0 does not bound variation over F more tightly than the endpoint's a priori range without a coverage or structural-invariance assumption. Arbitrarily narrow sampling intervals conditional on the specifications you happened to try cannot supply that link. A "we varied seeds, thresholds and resampled rows and the result was robust" is a statement about F_0, and every analyzed cell can still share the same unexamined semantic operation.

Here that operation is one line: whether a structurally special terminal candidate stays comparable across the two sides of the contrast. The original convention retained it on one side and deleted it on the other. The row audit shows the mechanism at a single frozen trace — both methods legal on the inclusive side, scores (1,1), margin 0; the filter deletes one method's predicted candidate and keeps the other's, giving (0,1) and margin −1. Under either symmetric treatment (keep both sides, or exclude both sides) the row effect is exactly 0. At scale, a four-corner factorial attributes 100% of the effect to that deletion, with nonterminal contribution exactly zero in every background and policy.

The premise is what makes it bite. The restricted family looked genuinely robust — 20 of 21 method pairs stable, Kendall τ_b of 0.90–1.00 across cells, the same two methods first and second everywhere. This is not a sloppy analysis being caught; it is a careful one whose careful robustness check ranged over the wrong axis. And the axis was not exotic: merely changing how empty answers are dispositioned — credit, no-credit, or condition the metric on a nonempty legal family — already moves the pooled effect from −7.72 to −3.46 to −0.89 pp and takes one method from "loses in half the cells" to "leads in every cell."

This is a-raw-benchmark-score-needs-its-adjudication-band generalised past adjudication to the entire evaluator-controlled map, and it is the missing half of benchmarks-conflate-model-harness-and-penalize-alternatives: that result varies the harness below the score, this one varies the semantics above it. It is also the formal statement of what removing-defective-instances-reshuffles-the-leaderboard observed empirically — eligibility is a coordinate of the specification, and moving it moves the ranking.

The admissibility discipline is the reusable artifact, and it is deliberately strict enough that the check cannot be gamed after the fact: the degree of freedom must be describable without using the alternative's outcomes; data, methods, predictions and resampling stay fixed; alternatives are jointly frozen before either outcome is computed or inspected; every declared alternative is reported; and claims stay relative to the declared family. The paper classifies its own control honestly under that rubric — post-hoc motivated, prospectively frozen — rather than presenting it as pre-registered.

Scope. One localized witness in one diagnostic benchmark. It establishes a non-implication, not a prevalence: how often evaluator semantics are non-identified in the wild is unmeasured, and the behaviour of unenumerated ontologies, partial-credit rules, task weights and decision criteria is open. The original conditional estimate remains valid for its joint specification, and the audit does not crown a correct terminal ontology. A contemporaneous audit in an unrelated domain — recomputing video-anomaly AUC over global, per-category and within-video pair supports on fixed scores — reports rank reversals from the same class of move, which is weak independent evidence that the pattern is not peculiar to this substrate.
