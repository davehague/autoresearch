The sessions answered a different question correctly and were graded against this one

This map's negative-side ledger has two entries so far, and both concern a null that is an artifact of the apparatus rather than of the intervention. difficulty-bands-are-agent-specific found that on ~40% of a task set the agent that could reveal an effect is not the one under test. an-output-check-cannot-see-a-skipped-step found a gate structurally blind to an omission because the omission leaves no trace in the object being checked. This is a third and it sits earlier than both: the question was posed at a granularity the intervention does not operate on, so the treatment had nothing to contribute and the control had nothing to miss.

The specific instance is small and exactly reproducible in shape. Asking which behaviours are affected by "something they depend on" is true at file granularity whenever any symbol in an imported file changed — which was 81–91 of 96 behaviours. Grading used artifact granularity, where the true answer was 14–18. Sessions answered the question they were asked, nearly perfectly, and scored near zero against the answer being graded. Nothing was wrong with the store, the tools, the model, or the grader. With the criterion stated explicitly and identically across conditions, the treatment's median went from 0.67 to 1.000 — a change in the prompt, reported by the authors as a measurement defect rather than as a condition effect, which is the correct call and a rare one.

The cheap defence is the one the study adopted after the fact and is worth adopting before: report the score under both readings for every run. Treatment sessions score 0.087–0.333 at file granularity and 1.000 at artifact granularity; the two sessions that scored above 0.95 at file granularity scored 0.270 and 0.286 at artifact granularity. Printed side by side, "this session answered a coarser question well" is a visible fact rather than an inference, and no reader has to reconstruct it from prose.

Read against report-a-null-as-a-bounded-interval-not-a-failed-test the pair covers both halves of reporting a negative: that note says fix the resolution you can achieve before collecting, this says check that the question you asked is at the resolution your instrument works at. And read against one-run-scores-an-implementation-not-an-idea it is the same hazard one level out — that note finds a score crediting an implementation to a mechanism, this finds a score crediting a task framing to an instrument.

Bounds. One incident inside one study, reported by its own authors as a lesson rather than measured as a prevalence — there is no estimate of how often published nulls have this shape, and the generalization is a reading habit rather than a result. The magnitude (0.67 → 1.000) is specific to a task where the coarse and fine readings differ by roughly 5×, which is unusually stark. Carried as suggestive: the practice transfers, the number does not.
