Most attestation adopters cannot be checked where they declare, and the ones you can check are fine

The headline reads like an indictment and is not one, which is why it is useful. Among the adopters whose release surface can be measured at all, most realize their declared binding end to end — five of seven, checkable by any unauthenticated reader. The gap is not that pipelines lie about attestation. The gap is that the declaration lives in a workflow file and the checkable evidence lives on a package registry, and nothing in the published exhaust links the two surfaces.

The methodological rules are the transferable part, and they are the sort of thing a CI audit gets wrong by default:

  • Unavailability is data, not failure. The admin-only branch-protection endpoint returned not-readable on all 30 repositories, so intended depth was graded without it and every realized grade is stated as a lower bound. Empty ruleset listings are ambiguous — the API returns the same empty list for "no rulesets" and "none readable" — and support no determination.
  • The three-way attestation split must not be collapsed. A returned none is a result; a lookup never attempted because the assets carried no digests is an absence of measurement. Seven, six and seventeen repositories respectively. Merging the last two would have turned a surface limit into a fabricated compliance number.
  • The declaration and the realization must come from different records. Declared depth is read from configuration, realized depth from published exhaust — "so the gap cannot be an artifact of one record describing itself." This is the same discipline as audit-agent-claims-against-trajectory-evidence, applied to a pipeline instead of a run.
  • A correction is dated and disclosed, not absorbed. Seventeen repositories first read as "no digests on release assets" were re-inspected and found to publish source-only releases — a surface limit misread as a repository property — and nine cells moved from a measured rung to undetermined, with the reconciled file preserved unmodified.

The negative-gap repository is the finding nobody predicted. One pipeline's published evidence — complete per-asset digests, found attestations — exceeds what its workflows declare as a gate. Evidence flowing with no gate consuming it is the mirror image of a declaration with no evidence, and it is the reason the instrument is worth running on a pipeline you already trust: it returns zero as readily as it returns a gap, and it can run negative.

For a CI/CD pipeline shipping agent-authored code, the operational question this hands you is cheap to ask: does the surface where you declare your binding actually carry the artifacts the binding is over? If your release page holds only auto-generated source archives, the answer is no, whatever your workflow says — and the platform never digests those.

Bounds. One forge, one CI system, 30 repositories in two declared strata; the frame is frozen and published and no claim generalizes past it. Stratum A's intended column is the sampling frame restated, not a result — the stratum was selected for attestation adoption. Both measured shortfalls are n = 2, consistent with the prediction that the gap concentrates at the identity-binding rungs and far too small to establish it. The registry-side half of the seam was not examined, so whether the binding holds there is unknown. Single author adjudicating both passes' reconciliation.
