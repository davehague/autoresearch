Perf-optimization benchmark reference patches are unstable

The vault has a limb on agents gaming a performance benchmark — PERFOPT-Bench caught 107.9× raw speedup collapsing to 1.2× verified (speedup-is-an-environment-conditioned-claim). This is the complementary failure with no agent misbehavior at all: replay the official reference patches across Google Cloud machine types and they fail their own validity rules a large share of the time — valid across all replays on only 38% of GSO, 8% of SWE-Perf, and 82% of SWE-fficiency tasks. The substrate the leaderboard is measured against is itself noisy, and SWE-Perf is the extreme: many reference patches produce near-zero runtime change, so the "reference" fails to beat baseline on replay 92% of the time and noise dominates the signal.

Two further axes corrupt the number before capability enters. Scoring rules reorder rankings independent of measurement — 9 of 28 pairwise comparisons flip between GSO's and SWE-fficiency's schemes, and SWE-fficiency concentrates 58.5–82.8% of its weight on its worst tasks. And saturation: public submissions already match or exceed the reference on 85.3% of replay-valid tasks, so the reliable subset has little headroom left to measure.

This confirms the map's "report the harness or the number is unfalsifiable" thesis one level lower, in the continuous-metric domain: for speedup, the hardware, compiler, and workload are part of the number (speedup-is-an-environment-conditioned-claim), and a paired agent-vs-agent speedup delta on single- run cells is meaningless when the reference itself varies across machines (coordination-deltas-need-a-measured-noise-floor). Practical rule: for a continuous engineering metric, verify the reference reproduces across your target hardware before trusting any delta, and treat a leaderboard speedup as an environment-conditioned claim, not a constant.

Caveats: the audit is on three specific perf benchmarks (a continuous-metric claim, distinct from the resolve-rate contamination problem); v2 numbers; instability magnitudes are cloud-provider-conditioned, which is the point. Graded T1, strong on the 740-task replay scale.
