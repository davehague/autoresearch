Third arrival at the same shape: the better the agent, the more likely your curated context is a liability

This map governs what enters the window and how it got chosen. Its standing open question — whether any of this moves a repair outcome — got its first direct answer on 2026-08-17, and it was "no": a retrieval metric degraded by a factor of nine with downstream success flat to rising (skill-use-precision-collapses-while-success-does-not). This arrival answers a narrower and more uncomfortable version: when the retrieved material is authoritative and correct, does adding it help? Not reliably, and the sign depends on the agent.

The design is what makes it worth carrying. This is not "context versus no context." The required scientific context — the definitions and constraints without which the task is ill-posed — is held fixed in every condition, and every repository-intrinsic cue stays: code structure, interfaces, traces, tests, incomplete implementations. What is removed is the layer a well-meaning platform team would add — rationales, upstream repairs, audit findings, paper excerpts, expert guidance. So the estimate is the marginal value of curation on top of an already-adequate repository, which is precisely the intervention most context-engineering products sell.

The direction reverses with capability, and the overlap shows it is not noise in a mean. For the stronger configuration, 8 tasks pass only with the information and 12 pass only without it — the intervention is churning roughly 20 of 91 tasks and losing on net. For the weaker one it churns 12 and wins. A mean would have shown a small movement; the paired overlap shows a substantial reshuffle in both directions, which is the same lesson as an-aggregate-gain-and-a-reliable-regression-coexist-in-every-cell and one-draw-per-item-recovers-none-of-the-reliable-changes.

The named mechanism is anchoring, and this vault has the receipts. The authors report that supplied knowledge can furnish semantic constraints absent from local code symptoms — limiting cases, coordinate consistency, independent observables, authoritative interfaces — and can equally induce anchoring, scope spillover, or premature reliance on a supplied explanation. That is the same failure as showing-a-failed-attempt-anchors-the-retry on the retry side and an-oracle-anchored-on-the-code-it-judges-cannot-fail on the oracle side: an expectation delivered from outside displaces the independent derivation that would have caught it being wrong for this instance. Their prescription is the vault's — executable evidence and independent validation — which is gate-the-edit-on-evidence-observed-not-claimed restated for injected domain knowledge.

Where it sits in the capability-conditioned family. a-skill-set-helps-the-weaker-harness-and-hurts-the-stronger found the same crossing for skills; the-memory-layer-only-separates-on-a-capable-answerer found the mirror image for memory representations (only the strong model converts them); skills-work-by-anchoring-procedure-not-by-adding-knowledge proposed the mechanism — procedure transfers, content does not. Three independent measurements now say the value of injected context is conditional on the agent's own competence at the task, and at least two say the conditioning can flip the sign. The design consequence is uncomfortable and specific: a context pipeline validated on a cheaper model has not been shown to help the frontier one you will actually deploy, and may be subtracting from it.

The cost side does not resolve it either. Token use moves in opposite directions between the two configurations — the stronger agent spends less with the information (3.70M vs 3.86M input) while the weaker spends far more (7.40M vs 4.84M). So there is not even a consistent efficiency argument to fall back on when the accuracy argument fails.

Bounds. Explicitly descriptive — the authors state it establishes neither statistical significance nor a causal effect. Two model configurations at two reasoning depths, not the eight in the main table, so "stronger" and "weaker" here means one pair and the capability-conditioning reading is a hypothesis this result is consistent with rather than one it tests. 91 tasks across 20 domains with six domains contributing one task each. The knowledge layer is domain-scientific and may not generalise to the kinds of context a platform team supplies for ordinary application code. The authors call their own analysis of how domain knowledge is actually used preliminary.
