Separate "another round happened" from "the feedback was about anything" — and the first explains almost all of the movement

This map's rule is that a delta is not a result until its null is measured, and it has been accumulating the specific nulls: a-retry-study-needs-a-placebo-arm, coordination-deltas-need-a-measured-noise-floor, a-routing-claim-needs-a-no-router-ablation, a-coordination-arm-needs-an-always-serial-reference. This arrival supplies the sharpest instrument of the set, and it is worth stating precisely because a weaker placebo would have concluded the opposite.

What the placebo holds constant. Same iterative template. Same number of rounds. Same history size. Same detected-set cardinality. Same multiset of the two largest per-input kill counts — so aggregate progress is preserved. The only thing destroyed is which input detected which fault. So Real − Placebo isolates fine-grained behaviour-aligned attribution, and Placebo − Plain bundles prompt scaffolding with coarse progress. That decomposition is the contribution; it is a strictly stronger design than the placebo arm this map already carried, which broke the feedback content rather than only its alignment.

What it finds. Read alone, the loop looks model-dependent and interesting: real feedback hurts the weaker model by 6.11 points and helps the stronger by 2.73 against plain resampling. The placebo reproduces both directions at nearly the same magnitude (−6.24 and +3.23). On the held-out cohort the scaffolding effect is larger still — −11.39 and +4.47 — while Real − Placebo is +1.99 and +0.28. Under the frozen ±2-point rule the first fails superiority, the second misses equivalence by 0.03 points, and both are declared inconclusive. Then the sensitivity analysis removes even the sign: matching on observed reference-valid candidate yield turns +1.99 into −0.30.

The discipline to copy is the refusal. The authors do not report this as "execution feedback does not work." They report it as unresolved, not established absent — because the placebo retains coarse progress and removes only fine-grained alignment, because zero-crossing intervals detect no effect rather than establishing equivalence, because E15's placebo is post hoc and E16-B's cohort was qualification-amended after its predecessor's gate failed. That is report-a-null-as-a-bounded-interval-not-a-failed-test applied to their own headline, and it is why the finding is usable: the claim narrows to "scaffolding explains the dominant shift," which is a positive statement with evidence behind it.

They also report the way the design nearly broke. E16-A froze its gate before opening 200 held-out tasks and then failed it — only 36 tasks qualified — and the amendment to an 18-fault panel is signed separately and labelled outcome-blind-but-amended, with an explicit table of each phase's evidential status. Included tasks have more exact-compatible accepted programs than excluded ones, so selection bias cannot be eliminated, and the paper says its frozen analysis rules do not restore the evidential status of the failed design. That is seal-the-analysis-before-the-randomness-exists honoured under the condition where honouring it is expensive, and it is a better model for this vault than a study that never hit the problem.

One quiet lesson for anyone matching budgets. Equal slots did not deliver equal usable candidates — nominal slots fail parsing, duplicate an input, or fail reference execution — and the weaker model lost more of them. So a budget-matched arm needs its realised yield reported, not just its nominal allocation, or the comparison silently becomes a comparison of format compliance. Bounds. The placebo does not remove all coarse feedback and does not exactly match prompt tokens, so the contrast estimates fine-grained attribution only. E14 runs on an already-exposed cohort; E15's placebo was designed afterward and is exploratory; E16-B is outcome-blind but qualification-amended; the yield-matched replay is post-treatment truncation on later rounds that retain their original histories, so it shows non-robustness rather than a direct effect. Two models, three seeds, Python exact-output tasks. Cohorts are never pooled.
