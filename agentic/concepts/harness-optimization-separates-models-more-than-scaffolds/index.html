The field's harness-evolution results were never comparable, and now there is a protocol

Every harness-improvement result this vault carries was produced with its author's own target agent, seed, budget, disclosure policy and scoring rule — observability-driven evolution (evolve-the-harness-from-trajectory-observability), test-time evolution without labels (evolve-the-harness-at-test-time-without-labels), the matched-budget refutation (harness-evolution-underperforms-matched-budget-test-time-scaling), the raw-trace ablation (search-the-harness-with-raw-traces-not-summaries). Each is internally careful and none is on the same axis as the others, which is why the map has stayed contested on an argument nobody can settle with a comparison. This is the first source here that contributes no method at all and fixes the problem instead: pin the optimization problem, the invariants, the disclosure and the scoring, and let optimizers compete on common ground.

The first-order result reframes what the harness-evolution literature is measuring. Holding task and scaffold fixed, swapping the optimizer model moves normalized gain 0.142; holding task and model fixed, swapping the coding harness the optimizer works through moves it 0.079. Harness engineering is, to a first approximation, a model capability — and one that tracks progress: across five GPT releases with everything else pinned, gain rises monotonically +0.03 → +0.49, three of the four steps clearing the task's noise band. A result attributed to "our harness-evolution loop" is substantially a result about which model was proposing the edits.

The second result is a warning to anyone benchmarking optimizers. A model's native harness wins 9 of 20 model–task pairs against a shared one's 11 — no consistent advantage — but the aggregate near-tie is heterogeneity, not uniform smallness: 11 of the 20 pairs exceed the resolution band, in directions that vary by model and task. On GAIA, the only task with four harness levels, the per-model lines cross, and the native advantage is concentrated: both GPT models are four to five bands better under codex while the Claudes and Kimi sit within a band or two of zero. Reading only the shared harnesses would have suggested harness sensitivity scales with model capability; adding native harnesses erases that pattern. This is the pair thesis arriving one level up — a claim about optimizers is itself a harness–model pair property (the-harness-shifts-benchmark-rank-order, no-harness-ordering-holds-across-models, attribute-performance-to-the-stack-not-the-model) — and it is why the paper reports tiers rather than a ranking: intermediate configurations differ by less than their round-to-round variation.

What optimizers actually do, and it is not what the map assumed. Two trajectory-derived measures, both descriptive of process rather than independent measures of capability. Breadth — the fraction of eight pre-registered harness levers touched during search — is the only process measure with a consistent sign, positively associated with gain on all four tasks (ρ +0.34 to +0.88), though it is exploration rather than retained edits (one configuration touched three quarters of the levers, made seven edits and shipped the seed) and it correlates with total modification volume. And detailed trace inspection is rare and negatively associated with gain — a finding sharp enough to carry separately (optimizers-search-broadly-and-barely-read-traces).

Two protocol details worth stealing outright. The budget is denominated in evaluation calls and case passes rather than compute, so comparisons do not implicitly favour whoever brought more GPUs — the discipline charge-every-search-method-the-same-budget asked for, implemented. And the binding constraint turns out to be the one nobody caps: the median optimizer uses 8 of 100 evaluation calls (4%) but 82% of its case allowance, with 55 of 100 cells exhausting a partition's case budget. If you are budgeting your own harness search, count cases, not calls.

Bounds. Two replicates per configuration with ranges rather than intervals, and resolution bands that are descriptive thresholds rather than significance tests — the authors decline fine-grained rankings on exactly that basis. The optimizer's own inference is metered but uncapped, so this measures the ceiling when reasoning is not scarce, not a cost-matched contest. Candidates are Python only and each task pins one target model. The seed harness is itself a prior — improving a competent agent (three tasks) and building from a non-functional stub (GAIA) are different capabilities, and seed complexity is not varied systematically. Self-described as hack-resistant rather than hackproof: repeated development and validation feedback can still reward strategies specific to a fixed evaluation, with per-run jitter named as the fix not yet made.
