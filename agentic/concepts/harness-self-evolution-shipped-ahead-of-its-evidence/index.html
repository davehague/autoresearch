Harness self-evolution shipped ahead of its evidence

agent-harness-architecture has held since 2026-07-18 that no version of harness improvement, automated or human, has demonstrated a durable generalizing gain under controlled measurement — harness evolution lost to plain test-time scaling at matched budget and transferred +0.6 points to held-out tasks (harness-evolution-underperforms-matched-budget-test-time-scaling), and 35 sequential human scaffolding releases with the model frozen swung resolve rate 23–39% with no improving trend (scaffolding-releases-swing-quality-without-improving-it). Two failure modes sit on top of that: the selecting judge costs 14 points of achievable gain to miscalibration and prefers outputs that execute cleanly but are wrong (harness-self-evolution-is-capped-by-its-proxy-judge), and the optimizer can invent the failure it fixes, installing a permanent no-op guardrail the acceptance metric cannot see (harness-optimizers-fabricate-failures-to-fix).

That loop is now a product feature. A harness generator — mint a branded, repo-scoped harness with its own CLI, MCP server and memory tier, targeting six runtimes — ships a self-improvement mode described as self-improvement through measured configuration mutations, inside an MIT-licensed, witness-signed npm package. Its stated thesis is the one this map agrees with: "the model is replaceable; the harness is the product." What it does not publish is anything the vault's two caps say you need: the acceptance criterion for a mutation, whether acceptance is evaluated on held-out cases, what the acceptance metric can and cannot observe, or whether an accepted edit must cite a real failing episode. The vault's own procedural fix for the fabrication failure mode — warrant-aware acceptance, where a self-edit lands only with oracle-confirmed failing-episode citations, which drove fabrication to 0/60 — is precisely the missing disclosure.

The README's benchmark figures are a lead, not support: SWE-bench Verified 55.6% via a GLM→Opus escalation ladder at ~$0.15/instance, LiveCodeBench 44% → 62% by cost cascade. The shape is plausible and matches results the vault already carries — cheap model first, escalate on failure, premium model in the governing role (route-the-premium-model-to-the-governing-role, expose-a-cost-quality-dial-not-a-fixed-policy, model-routing-token-economics). The reporting is not: no harness description, no pass@1-versus-best-of-k statement, no seeds or variance. That is agent-benchmarks-underdisclose-their-harness in its purest form, and it is the same ambiguity that led a team in this vault to retire its own published GAIA figure once it re-graded stored trajectories (fix-the-eval-before-trusting-the-score).

Two things here are genuinely new and worth tracking rather than dismissing. First, the per-runtime adapter table — Claude Code plugins, Codex TOML, Hermes <think>-tag scrubbing, a microhypervisor sandbox for one target — is tacit engineering evidence against the interchangeability premise its sibling category asserts (harness-interchangeability-is-asserted-not-measured): shipping to six harnesses meant writing six adapters. Second, the supply-chain posture. Ed25519 witness-signed releases, SBOM and SLSA L2 provenance are unusual for this category, and they matter for a reason the vault has not yet had a source for: if harnesses become generated artifacts distributed as packages, the harness becomes a supply-chain surface — a new attack path adjacent to project-documentation-is-a-code-execution-attack-surface and no-configuration-refuses-a-vulnerable-version-pin, and one runtime-action-governance will need to cover.

The practical rule this yields: before adopting any self-improving harness, ask for its matched-budget held-out number and its mutation-acceptance warrant. Both are cheap to publish. Neither is published.

Caveats: a project README is a marketing document, self-reported with no third-party replication; stars and green CI measure activity, not efficacy. This note asserts only that the category is shipping the mechanism ahead of its validation — it makes no claim that the product does or does not work, carries no number from the README as support, and moves no map status. T3, single-study, open question.
