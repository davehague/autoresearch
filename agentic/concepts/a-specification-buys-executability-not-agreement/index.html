The specification made the code run; it did not make the implementers agree, because they already did

This map's argument is that the contract lives outside the agent — and every result so far has measured what happens when the contract is absent, decayed, or not re-presented. This is the first study whose experimental treatment is the specification, with the implementer panel frozen, the units frozen, a mandatory no-specification control arm, and execution against a live source engine as the judge. It is also the first to report a null there, and the null is more useful than a win would have been.

What the specification buys is executability, and it buys a lot of it: 72.0% → 97.3% clean-loading output on one panel, +28 points on the other, monotone across three arms, and — importantly for this vault — comfortably above the ≈13-point run-to-run spread the same study measured on that axis (measure-the-arm-delta-spread-not-the-arm-spread). Alongside it, two quieter wins: the four machine-checkable well-formedness conditions (acyclicity, single ownership, constraint domination, totality-or- ambiguity-stop) make the artifact mechanically checkable, and dependency closures cut mean per-task context by 71% — which is split-the-executable-spec-into-path-and-behavior and persist-the-contract-not-the-conversation getting a structural mechanism and a number.

What it does not buy is agreement. Aggregate cross-implementer determinacy moves 83.1% → 83.8% on one panel and 83.2% → 79.7% on the other — and the authors report both, noting that reporting only the favourable one would be the exact error the paper warns about elsewhere. The reason is not that the specification failed; it is that implementers with no specification already agree ~83% of the time on shared industry defaults, so there is almost no agreement left to buy. The estimator does not discriminate in this regime.

Nor does it buy correctness. Against a live Oracle, structured rows match on 19 of 42 probes in both arms. What changes is the kind of failure: four wrong-data divergences become outright missing objects. The authors flag this as the first measurement that could have shown the specification helping at the data level, and it did not. And the gap between the two things is enormous — on the behavioural corpus, executability is ~97% while exact behavioural match is 2–6 units of 20, event-level fidelity 0.15–0.26. "It loads" badly overstates "it works." Anyone reading a spec-driven result whose endpoint is compilation, load, or test-pass is reading the axis this study says is the only one that moved.

Two structural findings survive underneath the flat aggregate and both are directly actionable. Partial closure is worse than none — an incomplete specification disperses implementers (83.2 → 72.6) — which converts "finish the dependency closure" from tidiness into a measurable requirement and is the sharpest available evidence for this map's emergent-specs-corrupt-the-artifact claim from the completeness side rather than the delivery side. And where the specification offers options instead of deciding, it splits implementers (−17.8 pp on one axis): an under-decided contract is worse than a silent one on exactly the decisions it names.

The authors do not withdraw the formal notion. det(S) — agreement among all conforming implementations — remains coherent, and the ambiguity-stop condition gives explicit treatment to unresolved decisions. What is retired is the estimator's discriminating power in this regime, and the naive reading that high convergence evidences a good specification. The fix is the denominator: see agreement-needs-a-coverage-denominator.

Scope, and it is narrow. One specification, one migration direction, one demo estate of 18 documents, two implementers on a fixed panel, one model era. The determinacy null is explicitly a statement about implementers that already share strong defaults — on weaker or more heterogeneous implementers the same specification could show a large effect, and the study's own pilot with a weaker implementer already hints at it. Whether the results transfer to a production specification of hundreds of documents is named by the authors as the most important open question.
