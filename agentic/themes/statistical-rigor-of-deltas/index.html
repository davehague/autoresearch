Statistical rigor of agent deltas — map

Forked from evaluation-integrity on 2026-08-03 (bounded auto-fork; the parent was at 66 dated bullets with four declared sub-threads). The parent asks whether a number describes the agent — contamination, harness disclosure, judge reliability, proxy validity. This map asks the separable question of whether a difference between two numbers survives contact with its own null: what the score would have been if the mechanism being claimed did not exist, how much of the spread is the seed against the data draw against the implementation, which denominator the rate was taken over, and what shape the distribution had before anyone reported a mean. The split is a boundary between is the instrument valid and is the contrast powered, not a topical convenience — a benchmark can be uncontaminated, fully disclosed and honestly judged, and still yield a delta that is noise. Nine claims and eleven dated arrivals moved across intact; nothing was rewritten in the move.

Status: consolidating (inherited from the parent at fork time and independently supportable: the core demand — report the null beside the effect — arrives from five independent groups on five different subjects (coordination protocols, self-repair, classifiers, data draws, long horizons) and agrees every time. Held below consensus because almost nothing here has been replicated on a second benchmark, the noise floors are explicitly stated by their own authors to be local and non-transferring, and the field's published practice has not moved: the vault is still ingesting single-run, unpaired deltas weekly.)

Sub-threads: none — one argument, "a delta is not a result until its null is measured": the nulls differ by subject (paired same-model floor, content-free placebo, majority-class baseline, independent data draw, composed stage product) and the variance decompositions by layer (seed, draw, implementation, reference substrate), but each is an instance of the same demand. Pre-committing the plan is that demand enforced before the run rather than after. [reviewed 2026-08-13 @ 24 bullets]

What we believe now:

  • The analyst's degrees of freedom can be made physically unavailable rather than merely unused — seal the plan, then draw the assignment from randomness that does not yet exist. (Added 2026-08-13, T1, strong.) Everything else on this map detects a bad delta after the fact. This attacks the other end. Three mechanisms, each closing a different escape: (1) the full analysis-and-interpretation plan compiled and fixed with an RFC 3161 timestamp plus three OpenTimestamps commitments, with the sealed text reproduced byte for byte in an annex including the three statements execution later overtook — a preregistration quietly reconciled with what happened is not one, and printing the stale sentences is the proof; (2) treatment assigned by 16 fair bits from one exact future NIST Randomness Beacon 2.0 pulse, so no sealed choice can have been conditioned on the assignment and a third party can verify it afterwards; (3) inference by exact enumeration of all 65,536 assignments under the Fisher sharp null, with no resampling seed to argue about. The piece most worth stealing is the support gate: all 27 criteria had to pass in both a signed-linear and a magnitude-rank pipeline, so when the study's one interesting endpoint cleared Bonferroni under one transform and shrank by an order of magnitude under the other, the pre-committed rule already called it transform-dependent (seal-the-analysis-before-the-randomness-exists). Cost is near zero at benchmark scale — minutes of setup against days of compute, and exact enumeration is free below ~20 binary units. [T1 — and the trade is real: this buys credibility, not power. Effective N = 16, and no positive control was registered or run, so the study has no measured floor on what it could have detected. A sealed null without a sensitivity floor is a bounded statement about one instrument, not a fact about the world]
  • Two more entries in the null catalogue, and the second is the one this map had been missing: the null you get for free by looking at enough cells. (Added 2026-08-09, T1 both, single-study / strong.) (a) A detection-and-repair claim needs a withheld-hint arm. The catalogue's members each name what a number would be if the claimed mechanism did not exist; this one names what it would be if the harness had not already supplied the answer. A model router credited with containing latent cascades showed recovery 0.83, cascade radius cut ~5.5× at depth 7, and an effect growing with pipeline depth — the shape a real containment mechanism has. Then the authors re-ran it with the trusted upstream value withheld and the same component's licence to self-correct intact: recovery fell 0.67 → 0.08, paired drop 0.58 [0.33, 0.79], exact sign-flip permutation p = 5.19×10⁻⁴, n=24 pairs. The router was repairing a fault the experiment had localized for it (a-containment-claim-needs-a-withheld-hint-arm). The general form: any experiment measuring whether a component notices something must ask what else in the harness already knows — the verifier handed the reference, the monitor reading an annotated trace, the attribution agent scored on traces where the injected step is the only anomaly. It is a-verifier-sharing-the-interpretation-shares-the-error one layer out — not a shared error, but shared knowledge the deployment will not have. Two credits: the paper ran the ablation against its own best row and demoted the row, and it chose the permutation test because the percentile bootstrap collapses to 0 when every paired difference shares a sign (report-a-null-as-a-bounded-interval-not-a-failed-test). (b) A transfer grid is a multiple-comparison family, not a set of findings. A source × target × domain heatmap with cells from −9.5 to +11 points looks like evidence for whatever story you brought; under global Holm across its 135 route hypotheses, one route survives (correct-a-transfer-grid-for-multiplicity-before-reading-it). The transferable practice is what the study reports instead: a prespecified, route-agnostic aggregate contrast — the same artifact on its matched domain against two mismatched ones, controlling prompt length and format within source, over all 81 eligible configurations — at +1.72 pp [+0.25, +2.97], p = .035, with the grid demoted to descriptive evidence of heterogeneity, the correction level stated (family-specific Holm retains two negative routes that global Holm does not; BH reported as sensitivity, not swapped in), non-survival explicitly not read as zero, and the confirmatory family fenced so cost and secondary metrics cannot be promoted after the fact. Why it matters past one paper: nearly every artifact-reuse result an engineering team reads is a grid — this context file across these repos, this skill bank across these agents, this prompt across these models — and on a grid this size a handful of double-digit cells is what noise looks like. Ask how many cells were computed and what the prespecified contrast was; if the heatmap is the analysis, it is a hypothesis generator. [T1 — (a) is n=24 pairs on one arm of one synthetic single-agent construct, so it is a demand with a worked example, not a rate at which the leak occurs; (b) is one benchmark's grid, and the aggregate contrast is itself a single blocked permutation over configurations sharing sources. Both are protocol claims, which travel further than the effects they were used to report]
  • A third addition to the null catalogue — a routed system needs a no-router arm — plus a null that was the task framing rather than the intervention, and a variance component sitting below every one this map has decomposed. (Added 2026-08-06, T1 all three, strong / suggestive / strong.) (a) The catalogue's newest member is also its cheapest, and it arrives three days after the always-serial arm made the identical demand of coordination papers. "Routing helps" is silently two claims: the pipeline around the router helps, and choosing among models helps. Run the separating arm — send every task to the single cheapest model with the same pipeline and no routing decision — and on the one system that reported it the arm ties exactly: 159 of 266 solves at $0.227 per solve against the routed system's 159 at $0.230, with both sitting +3.60 points above the random-traffic-split line, so even the margin over chance belongs to the pipeline. The calibrated router had put 263 of 266 tasks on the cheapest fixer (a-routing-claim-needs-a-no-router-ablation). The paired calibration explains the shape and is the part to generalize: the intervention redistributes ability toward cheap models (+5.1 / +4.0 / +2.0 for the three cheaper fixers, −4.0 for the strongest, pooled +1.8 [−1.0, +4.5], nothing significant at n = 99) — which is precisely what makes a cost router look successful while having no decision left to make well. (b) On the negative side of the ledger, a third way a null is manufactured by the apparatus. The map already holds that a task set has no fixed informative band (difficulty-bands-are-agent-specific) and that an artifact check is blind to a skipped step (an-output-check-cannot-see-a-skipped-step). This one is earlier than both: the question was posed at a coarser granularity than the intervention operates on, so the treatment had nothing to contribute. Asking which behaviours are affected by "something they depend on" is true at file granularity for 81–91 of 96 items while grading used artifact granularity (14–18); the sessions answered the question asked, nearly perfectly, and scored near zero against the question graded, and the between-condition gap was near zero. Stating the criterion explicitly — same store, same tools, same model — took the treatment's median from 0.67 to 1.000. The cheap defence is to report both granularities per run so a mismatch is visible rather than inferred (the-task-must-encode-the-methodology-not-only-the-tool). (c) And a variance term downstream of everything this map has decomposed: hold weights, prompts, hardware, decoding and evaluation protocol fixed and change only the inference backend, and under greedy decoding the score still moves — structurally, model-dependently, and at roughly 39% of the variance seen out of the box, with per-item disagreement reaching 48% (the-inference-backend-is-a-term-in-the-score). control-the-data-draw-before-the-seed found the data draw dominating the training seed and one-run-scores-an-implementation-not-an-idea found implementation choice dominating the rerun; both concern what you built. This is how you served it, it is present even where σ_eval is zero by construction, and about 61% of it is removable for free by matching the generation configuration (match-the-generation-config-before-comparing-backends) — so a study that reports seeds while leaving the serving stack unstated has controlled the smaller axis. The no-router tie is a property of that pool and benchmark and its authors say so — in deployment nobody knows in advance that a cheap model reaches parity, which is what the router exists to predict. The task-framing lesson is one incident reported by its own authors, with no prevalence behind it.   • Two additions to the null catalogue, one on each side of the ledger: a coordination claim needs an always-serial arm, and a null needs a margin fixed before collection. (Added 2026-08-05, T1 both, strong.) The map's family of nulls each names what the number would be if the mechanism did not exist — a paired same-model floor, a content-free placebo, a majority-class baseline, a composed stage product. The cheapest member yet is also the most consistently omitted. "Coordination helps" is silently two claims: beats unconstrained parallel execution (the mechanism suppresses interference between concurrent writers) and beats one agent working in order (the architecture is worth building). The field reports the first and is read as the second. On the one study that instrumented both — 360 executions, four arms including always serial, planner declarations frozen once across every arm and seed so upstream variance cannot load onto the policy contrast, bootstrap over seven repository-task clusters rather than executions — deterministic pre-write admission beat unconstrained parallel by +26.7 pp [9.6, 60.0] and matched always serial at 50.0% vs 50.0%, identical inside every cluster, at a longer critical path (a-coordination-arm-needs-an-always-serial-reference). Note what the cluster bootstrap bought: resampling the 360 executions would have produced a tight interval around a number the design cannot support. On the negative side of the ledger, the map has held since 2026-07-31 that a reported null needs the same scrutiny as a reported effect; this adds the reporting form. Decide before collecting what precision the planned sample buys and make that the claim: with zero-event arms anticipated, fix the equivalence margin at ±2U where U is the exact one-sided Clopper–Pearson limit at the planned n (±7.01 pp at n = 84), derive simultaneous exact bounds in closed form, and conclude equivalence within a pre-declared margin rather than "no significant difference." Three cheap practices ride along — an adversarial missingness table that counts every excluded attempt as a failure and still bounds the worst arm (25.88%), deterministic program judges human-audited at 40/40 with synthetic positive controls proving the instrument can fire, and amendments frozen before the corresponding data are viewed. The same discipline is what surfaces the confound the design cannot remove: collection order was fixed, so the effort contrast is entangled with time, and the authors print it (report-a-null-as-a-bounded-interval-not-a-failed-test). [T1 both; each is one study. The serial-arm result rests on seven clusters, three Python project families, one planner and one coder model, oracle-localized context and physically sequential provider calls; the equivalence result is one model family, one 14-scenario suite, endpoints only, with the estimand defined within that fixed scenario set so the binomial bounds do not generalize. The protocols transfer; neither magnitude does]
  • A falling success rate on longer tasks is arithmetic before it is evidence — subtract the compounding before claiming a horizon effect. (Added 2026-08-01, T3, suggestive.) Four independent repairs at 80% each compose to 41% with no horizon mechanism whatsoever; a benchmark reporting 41% end-to-end has measured multiplication, and one reporting 10% has a 4× shortfall worth explaining. The proposed instrument is a pre-specified counterfactual: decompose into stages defined by acceptance conditions, run the same deployed agent (model + prompt + tools + memory + framework) from declared checkpoints to estimate each conditional stage rate, compose them, and report the log contrast against the natural rollout (a-long-horizon-claim-needs-a-composed-stage-baseline). This is the third member of a family the map already carries — coordination-deltas-need-a-measured-noise-floor for coordination deltas and a-retry-study-needs-a-placebo-arm for self-repair — all saying the same thing about different subjects: find out what the number would be if the mechanism you are claiming did not exist, and subtract it first. The distinction it turns on is portable and worth adopting as a reading rule: a deployment benchmark asks whether the whole system succeeds under realistic budgets; a diagnostic benchmark asks which controlled contrast explains behaviour a model of local competence does not. Read strictly, most long-horizon evidence in this vault — including long-horizon-failure-is-state-not-steps and chainswe-state-accumulation-degrades-maintenance — reports the observed rate without a declared composed prediction, which leaves it valid deployment evidence and not yet diagnostic. A second reporting demand follows: "long horizon" names at least five separable quantities, so declare work exposure and dependency depth rather than a turn count (declare-work-exposure-and-dependency-depth-separately). [T3 — a position paper with no experiment. The residual has never been computed on a real benchmark by its authors; the supporting numbers are re-readings of other groups' results not designed to be composed this way; the product baseline assumes constant stage accuracy, independent failures and no recovery, and once tasks branch or recover it concedes a checkpoint simulator it does not build; the estimator is unstable exactly where agents currently are, since a near-zero prediction makes an all-failure outcome diagnostically worthless; and it is protocol-dependent, so a residual quoted without its protocol means nothing]
  • The unit of evidence is wrong before the metric is: repeating a run controls the smallest term, and the choice among legitimate implementations of the same mechanism dominates it five- to tenfold. (Added 2026-07-31, T1, strong.) Every null this map has demanded controls a known noise source — a paired same-model floor (coordination-deltas-need-a-measured-noise-floor), a majority-class baseline (skewed-label-bases-inflate-classifier-headlines), a content-free placebo (a-retry-study-needs-a-placebo-arm), matched instances (proxy-metrics-rank-models-not-suites). This says the field's standard reliability move, repeat-the-run-and-fix-the-seed, is controlling the smallest one. Freeze a mechanism as an outcome-blind card, implement it three times in fresh sessions, replay each artifact byte-identically: same-artifact rerun variance is 4–6% of within-task variation (zero where the evaluator is deterministic) while implementation choice is 33–45% (71–82% in the deterministic arm). The decision consequence is the number to carry — leave-one-implementation-out winner reversal of 25.6% and 43.6%, surviving two outcome-blind degeneracy filters and the full penalty grid (one-run-scores-an-implementation-not-an-idea). One frozen card produced three valid, blind-faithful, exactly-reproducing realizations spanning +2.31% to −1.85%, reversing every one-shot choice against a competitor at +0.46%. So a score is honest evidence about the artifact it measured and a lottery ticket the moment it is credited to the mechanism — which is what happens whenever a result prunes a branch, justifies transferring an approach, or enters an agent's persistent memory. Directly downstream: loop-engineering's still-unrun three-armed sweep now needs several independent implementations per arm, because its contested effects are the same order as the reversal rate. [T1 — one task pool, four cards per task, three implementations, model-generated cards and model-assigned fidelity labels; the authors state the rates describe their deployed workflow rather than an error rate for arbitrary projects]
  • A reported null needs the same scrutiny as a reported effect, and an output check is blind to a skipped step. (Added 2026-07-31, T1, strong.) Two new failure modes on the negative side of the ledger, both cheap to defend against. First: a task set has no fixed informative band. Running the same 15 real tasks on two frontier agents, difficulty transfers (ρ = 0.75) but the band where an intervention could show an effect does not — 6 of 15 tasks are borderline for exactly one agent and 6 differ in floor/ceiling status, so on roughly 40% of the set the agent that would reveal an effect is not the one under test. That is enough to reconcile two published studies that disagreed about context files without either being wrong. The scaling arithmetic points opposite to instinct: at 17 tasks × 3 repeats even a true 30 pp effect is caught 57% of the time, 80% power on 10 pp needs 120–200 tasks, and raising repeats 3 → 10 at fixed n moves power only 13% → 58% — task-level variance dominates, so buy tasks, not repeats (difficulty-bands-are-agent-specific). Paired with harness-asymmetry-manufactures-model-differences this covers both signs: an apparatus asymmetry manufactures a difference, a task-set/agent mismatch manufactures its absence. Second: an artifact test is structurally blind to whether the procedure that establishes the artifact's validity ran. Across 30 skills a prose agent performs 56% of the steps its own skill mandates while producing artifacts that pass output checks throughout — including a skill whose stated Iron Law forbids any completion claim without fresh verification evidence, satisfied 30% of the time, with the agent writing "all tests pass, build succeeds" straight into the deliverable (an-output-check-cannot-see-a-skipped-step). Not a precision gap in the gate; the omission is outside the gate's field of view. The remedy needs no compiler: decide which mandated steps leave machine-checkable evidence and grade that, which is this map's own grade-the-trajectory principle (audit-agent-claims-against-trajectory-evidence, grade-process-defects-not-just-outcomes) applied to process rather than outcome. [T1 for both; the band result is two agents / three repositories / 15 shared tasks, the compliance result is judge-scored across 30 skills weighted toward document, process and compliance work]
  • The noise is not in your evaluator and not mainly in your seed — it is in which data you drew, and the cheap way of measuring it hides the worst runs. (Added 2026-07-25, T3 — method transfers, magnitudes do not; computer-use agents.) A crossed grid of three independent data draws × eight training seeds, every checkpoint re-scored, finds two invariants in every cell: σ_eval ≈ 0 (re-scoring a frozen checkpoint is deterministic) and σ_train ≤ 10%. What is left is the data draw and a run-to-run residual, and the data draw's share rises with difficulty — ~16–22% where the intervention works, 48% and dominant on the hardest cell — so it bites hardest exactly where effects are marginal. Same trainer, data and config run twice: 0.18 vs 0.68. The corollary is the one to act on: estimating variance from subsamples of one batch rather than independent draws halves it and erases the catastrophic-failure mode entirely (0/24 vs 3/10 runs below 0.10). Three reporting rules follow. Characterize the distribution's shape first — the hard cell is bimodal (modes ≈0.09 and ≈0.80; dip test p = 0.07 at k = 10 and underpowered at k = 5), so mean ± std is structurally wrong and a single run has ~30% chance of the failure mode. Set k from your own σ and the effect you mean to claim: existence is cheap at k ≤ 5, ordering two cells Δ ≈ 0.18 apart at σ ≈ 0.3 needs k ≈ 46. And expect the field's single-run deltas to be near coin-flips on sign — at the magnitudes papers publish (+7.7 points; +0.2), 33–44% wrong-sign in this variance regime. This is the third member of a family with skewed-label-bases-inflate-classifier-headlines (find the null first) and coordination-deltas-need-a-measured-noise-floor (measure a paired floor); the new member is about sampling (control-the-data-draw-before-the-seed, agent-improvement-distributions-can-be-bimodal). [T3, single-study]
  • Report the attempt count, not only the run count. (Added 2026-07-25.) In one controlled study only 50.9% of attempts produced a scoreable artifact — and 20 of 28 exclusions fell in a single condition, so the missingness was condition-correlated and probably not random. Model failure and operational scoreability are different denominators; pooling them inflates the apparent failure rate and hiding them puts a silent selection effect on the published comparison. Define a valid run, keep the exclusions in a separate operational denominator with a per-cause audit, and say so (scoreable-runs-are-a-separate-denominator). [T3, single-study]
  • Find the null before you read the headline metric — for classifiers that means the majority-class baseline. (Added 2026-07-20.) The newest proxy failure in this catalogue needs no contamination, no LLM judge, and no bad benchmark — only a skewed label and a metric that rewards it. At a ~91.8% positive base rate, predicting the majority scores F1 0.957; the best learned model scores 0.958 and the abstract reports "F1 above 0.95" while the discriminating metric (AUC) sits at 0.676, and the base rate itself is never printed. This is the classification analogue of the paired noise floor and of matched search budgets: a number is uninterpretable without the null it should be measured against, and where the null is absent, treat accuracy and F1 as unreported. [T1, single-study]
  • The reliability failure reaches the measurement substrate itself: for continuous metrics the reference is unstable before any agent runs. (Added 2026-07-21.) Replaying the official reference patches of the three popular performance-optimization benchmarks across cloud machine types, they satisfy their own validity rules on only 38% (GSO) / 8% (SWE-Perf) / 82% (SWE-fficiency) of tasks; scoring-rule choice flips 9 of 28 pairwise rankings; SWE-fficiency concentrates 58.5–82.8% of its weight on its worst tasks; and public submissions already match or exceed the reference on 85.3% of the replay-valid tasks (saturation). This is the complement to PERFOPT-Bench's agent-side gaming (107.9× → 1.2×, speedup-is-an-environment-conditioned-claim): even an honest agent against the gold patch is scored against a machine-dependent number. Two independent groups now converge from opposite ends — the agent games the metric, and the substrate is noisy — on one rule: a speedup is an environment-conditioned claim, and a paired agent-vs-agent delta on single-run cells is meaningless when the reference itself varies across hardware (coordination-deltas-need-a-measured-noise-floor). [T1]
  • The failure reaches the base unit: pass@1 itself misgrades, and the mechanism is variance. Capability and reliability rankings diverge as tasks lengthen (23,392 episodes, 396 tasks, 10 models) — and the strongest models melt down more, not less (up to 19% of long runs), because greater capability buys more ambitious multi-step strategies and ambition is a variance source. Software engineering is the steepest-degrading domain measured (graceful-degradation 0.90 → 0.44 vs 0.74 → 0.71 for document processing). Two agents at "70%" can be entirely different production assets. Report the distribution, or you have not reported the agent. [T1]
  • The failure also reaches the difference between two numbers, which is what architecture papers actually report. Take two coordination protocols, make them configuration-equivalent at the point where neither mechanism can yet have acted (verified by code inspection plus a byte-level payload audit), and they still disagree: paired gaps of +10 pp and 0 pp across two n=100 seeds, an observed envelope of [−3, +18] pp, and a pooled upper Wilson CI of ≲15 pp — on one model and one benchmark. The power arithmetic is brutal: detecting a 5 pp effect needs n≈1,000 paired, and the field routinely claims 2–10 pp wins on n≈100, once, unpaired. The corruption channel here is neither contamination nor optimization pressure — it is under-powered paired measurement, a sibling of the disclosure gap (contamination explains levels, under-specification explains spread, and noise explains a great deal of what is left). Report a same-model paired floor beside any architectural delta, or the delta is not a result. Scope discipline: the floor is local — its authors state it does not transfer, and their own cross-model probes confirm that — so the number is not portable; the protocol is. [T1, single-study]

  • 2026-08-13 — spec-prose-format-preregistered-null (Panossian, American University of Beirut, arXiv, "Does the way we write a theory change the program an LLM builds from it? A prospective randomized study of renderer format in LLM theory-to-program translation", 2026-08-10, T1, strong): the most tightly sealed experiment this vault holds, and its methodology is the contribution rather than its subject. The treatment-bearing unit is an anonymous renderer slot — 32 slots in 16 blocks — receiving either connected conditional prose or an intervention contract reading HOLD FIXED / INTERVENE / REQUIRED DIRECTION, over byte-identical proposition strings and a shared adapter. Two pinned snapshots (gpt-5.4-2026-03-05, gpt-4.1-2025-04-14) translate five anonymous account axes across 320 preauthorized single-shot calls per stage, each returning 11 equations in a frozen sparse quadratic language of 823 legal monomials, scored by a deterministic evaluator with no LLM in the loop that perturbs registered inputs and reads the response geometry. Two registered endpoints — matched-distance reduction and 40-comparison closed-set identifiability — each with unsigned, row-free and broken-alignment controls, and the design says in advance why both are needed: a renderer could improve relative identifiability merely by pushing all five accounts apart. Verdicts: H1 not_supported, H2 not_supported, conjunction not_supported; 19 of 108 criterion evaluations pass; identifiability 0.469–0.523 against a registered 0.80 in all four cells; smallest exact p in H1 across ten tests 0.0593. One endpoint moved — H2 matched-distance reduction 0.5174, exact p = 0.00125 (tail 82/65,536), the study's only test to clear the pre-stated Bonferroni 0.05/20 = 0.0025, surviving deletion of its largest block at 0.4729 — and is an order of magnitude smaller under the rank pipeline, which the registered rule classifies as transform-dependent and therefore not support (seal-the-analysis-before-the-randomness-exists). Thirteen validity gates per stage (13/13 and 12/13, the miss a single-execution-attempt counter after three local openssl ts -verify timeouts with no model in the loop); 320/320 samples complete in both stages; 1,280 tensor digests re-hashed with zero mismatches. Status held at consolidating — this adds the strongest available prophylactic to a map built mostly from post-hoc detectors, without changing what the map believes about deltas. The honest counterweight, volunteered by the author: effective N is 16, and no positive control was registered or run, so there is no measured floor on what this instrument could have found. Ripples: spec-driven-development (the substantive null it delivers — the-specs-format-is-not-what-pays), evaluation-integrity (a null published at the size it came out, with the disagreeing transform printed beside it).
  • 2026-08-13 — interaction edge from specpath-contract-equivalent-spec-histories (Wu, Wang, Yang, Ji & Lin, HKUST / USTC, arXiv, "SpecPath", 2026-08-10, T1, strong for this edge): a paired block design with the clusters counted honestly. The inferential unit is the task/PR family, and there are five — "histories, repeats, model deployments and scaffolds never increase cluster N" — so the 95% CI on the headline 36.4% comes from 10,000 nonparametric task-family resamples (seed 20260729) rather than from the 100 executions, and the paper says outright that 100 executions do not create 100 inferential clusters. This is the cluster-counting discipline this map has been asking for, executed without being the paper's subject (pair-the-runs-or-the-mean-hides-the-churn). Status held.   • 2026-08-10 — interaction edge from pmcoder-planning-episodic-memory-coupling (Zhang, Zhang & Huang, Vanderbilt University, arXiv, "PMCoder", 2026-08-07, T1, strong for this edge): a worked instance of nearly everything this map asks for, in a paper whose contribution is a method rather than a protocol — which is the useful part, since it demonstrates the discipline is affordable inside an ordinary systems paper. The measurement problem is stated rather than ignored: FP8 serving is not bit-reproducible even at temperature 0, so the headline and the ablation are each run three times per arm (one-run-scores-an-implementation-not-an-idea). The reported effect is an instance-level cluster bootstrap — resample 500 instance identifiers with replacement, average the per-instance resolved indicator across the three runs, take the mean paired difference — with a percentile 95% CI of [+14.3, +35.7], a two-sided bootstrap p-value, and complementary McNemar tests on the matched runs (all three significant, p = 0.002 to 0.033). The robustness statement this vault likes best is the raw one: the weakest treatment run (164) exceeds the strongest baseline run (144) by 20 instances, so the claim does not depend on the aggregation. The ablation is a balanced 2×2 run-level factorial with an explicit interaction contrast (+10.3 instances, F(1,8) = 10.92, p = 0.011) rather than a table of one-at-a-time deltas — which is what makes the superadditivity claim testable at all, and is a template for pricing any two scaffolds whose value may be relational (plan-and-memory-are-superadditive). And the baseline is harness-matched — the same loop with the components disabled — rather than a published leaderboard number (benchmarks-conflate-model-harness-and-penalize-alternatives, agent-benchmarks-underdisclose-their-harness). Hyperparameters were fixed from pilots before the final runs rather than tuned on resolved counts. The hardest cell's distribution is bimodal (modes ≈0.09 at 40% and ≈0.80 at 60%; Hartigan dip p = 0.07 at k = 10, ΔBIC = 13; underpowered at k = 5, p = 0.38 — it takes the tenth seed), so mean ± std is structurally wrong and a single run has ~30% chance of the failure mode. Seed budgeting splits claims in two: existence is cheap at k ≤ 5, resolving Δ ≈ 0.18 at σ ≈ 0.3 needs k ≈ 46, and a k-seed mean predicts a held-out half 1.3–1.7× better than a single run (which misestimates by up to 0.34–0.42 at the 90th percentile) — reporting k seeds changes the answer, not just the error bars. And the stress test aims at practice rather than a paper: at the improvement magnitudes the field publishes (+7.7 points; +0.2 task success), a single-run report has the wrong sign 33–44% (and 25–34%) of the time. Backed by two printed self-falsifications — a clean single-seed efficiency curve that went non-monotonic across data seeds, and an earlier draft's hard "cannot be bought" boundary (0/14) that was one draw from a distribution spanning 0.00–0.86, wider than the whole claimed effect. Caveats: this is computer-use agents, not coding — taken for a domain-general method, explicitly not for magnitudes; two authors, no venue; the environments and 35B backend are proprietary and unreleased; three cells, one policy; and the frame-level metric is verifier-circular by construction, which the authors disclose and route around via DB-oracle end-to-end numbers. Also ripples to loop-engineering, runtime-action-governance, agent-harness-architecture (identical configs scoring 5/5 then 0/5 on a per-rollout model reload) and observability (grade from database state and an audit log, never the transcript). See control-the-data-draw-before-the-seed, agent-improvement-distributions-can-be-bimodal.
