Statistical rigor of agent deltas — map

Forked from evaluation-integrity on 2026-08-03 (bounded auto-fork; the parent was at 66 dated bullets with four declared sub-threads). The parent asks whether a number describes the agent — contamination, harness disclosure, judge reliability, proxy validity. This map asks the separable question of whether a difference between two numbers survives contact with its own null: what the score would have been if the mechanism being claimed did not exist, how much of the spread is the seed against the data draw against the implementation, which denominator the rate was taken over, and what shape the distribution had before anyone reported a mean. The split is a boundary between is the instrument valid and is the contrast powered, not a topical convenience — a benchmark can be uncontaminated, fully disclosed and honestly judged, and still yield a delta that is noise. Nine claims and eleven dated arrivals moved across intact; nothing was rewritten in the move.

Status: consolidating (inherited from the parent at fork time and independently supportable: the core demand — report the null beside the effect — arrives from five independent groups on five different subjects (coordination protocols, self-repair, classifiers, data draws, long horizons) and agrees every time. Held below consensus because almost nothing here has been replicated on a second benchmark, the noise floors are explicitly stated by their own authors to be local and non-transferring, and the field's published practice has not moved: the vault is still ingesting single-run, unpaired deltas weekly. Held again 2026-08-22 on the strongest single arrival this map has taken — an audit that runs a frozen no-op through the identical pipeline as every treatment arm and catalogues seven measurement failures, each of which inverts a reported conclusion when uncontrolled, with the marginal cost of each control priced. It confirms the map's core demand from every side at once and still argues against promotion, because its central new finding is that the map's own remedy has a defect one level up: a null measured once is a point estimate, and the "corrected" statistic it certifies can have a non-zero null. A demand whose standard implementation is itself unsound is not a demand that has survived scrutiny. Held again 2026-08-23 on two arrivals that both widen the demand rather than satisfying it. One adds an uncertainty axis repetition cannot reach at all — variation over declared definitions, where the same frozen data gives a sampling interval of [-12.10, -3.92] pp and a specification-plus-sampling envelope of [-13.36, +18.89] pp. The other measures the run-to-run floor on the quantity claims are actually made of, the arm delta, finds it roughly double the within-arm figure and different by axis, and uses it to retire four of its own six candidate effects including an +18.7 pp one while a +17.4 pp one survives. A map whose core demand keeps acquiring new unmet sub-demands is not a map that has survived scrutiny. Held 2026-08-27 on an interaction edge that gives this map its cleanest unstratified-average failure: nothing about the estimator changed, one moderator was added, and a published average split into two populations two-fold apart. The moderator was a script-readable property of the repository, so the usual excuse — that the moderator was unmeasurable — does not apply here; nobody looked. It does not move the status: the stratified estimates themselves are observational and the authors present them as hypothesis-generating, and the primary contrast dichotomizes at a boundary chosen after inspecting outcomes (defended by showing three groupings locate the gap at the same place). What the map takes is the design rule, which is independent of whether the gradient survives.)

Sub-threads: none — one argument, "a delta is not a result until its null is measured": the nulls differ by subject (paired same-model floor, content-free placebo, density-matched placebo, majority-class baseline, independent data draw, composed stage product) and the variance decompositions by layer, but each is an instance of the same demand, and reporting inside difficulty bands is that demand applied to a delta's composition rather than its variance. What we believe now:

  • The unit of analysis is a fact about the artifact keys, computable before any data is read — and no multiplicity correction can see it, because a multiplicity correction and a design correction answer different questions. (Added 2026-08-28, T1 — strong.) Every comparison on a leaderboard is paired, because both systems attempt the same instances; instances nest in repositories, examples nest in tasks. All of it is legible in a (submission, repo, instance) key and absent from the results table where a reader could act on it. Getting it wrong is not a rounding error: on the SWE-bench Verified leaderboard, moving from the instance level to the declared 12-repository cluster level changes individual adjacent-rank p-values by up to 48× and takes supported claims from 5,518 to 0; on BIG-Bench Hard, the pooled claim goes from p = 1.76×10⁻¹⁹² treating 6,261 examples as independent trials to p = 8.37×10⁻⁶ respecting the 26 tasks — 187 orders of magnitude, with no data changed (the-unit-of-evidence-is-in-the-keys-not-the-p-values). A p-value of 10⁻¹⁹² is not something an experiment with 26 tasks can produce. And the row that should change how this vault reads a corrected result: Bonferroni, the most conservative of five fixed procedures, endorses 6,467 of 8,911 leaderboard comparisons at the instance level and 0 at the cluster level. So "we corrected for multiple comparisons" is silent on whether the units were right. This is robust-within-a-family-is-not-identified-across-families one level down — a correction is a claim about whichever unit the analyst happened to assume — and it is prior to every null this map collects, since you cannot measure the spread of a delta before deciding what an independent observation is. Machine-checked sound in Lean 4 given genuine declarations; a wrong cluster declaration is priced as though it were right. [T1, strong]
  • Whether a sample was selected by reading the keys or by reading the outcomes survives in the program that computed it, permanently — so pre-registration can be a typing rule instead of a promise, and nothing has to ask about the analyst's intent. (Added 2026-08-28, T1 — strong.) Two trials report the same subgroup: one on patients over 65 (read off enrollment records before any outcome existed), one on the patients who responded (available only after every patient was measured). Identical p-values, indistinguishable to any procedure handed the numbers. But selecting on a property of the key records a footprint of what it keeps, while selecting on a property of the outcome records a footprint of everything it read — so an outcome-selected sample can never match a pre-registration and is never granted a one-sided or confirmatory price (outcome-selection-is-caught-by-what-the-program-read). Translated to this vault's subject, the outcome-selected slice is "the instances this agent failed", "the trajectories where the judge disagreed", "the tasks where the harness change mattered" — exactly what agent papers report, read off outcomes by construction, currently charged nothing. Because the wealth transformer is antitone in the realized p-value, affordability is checkable before the analysis runs, and checker acceptance implies the guarantee without conditioning on the honesty of the declared bounds — a dishonest bound makes an analysis unaffordable rather than falsely licensed. The cheapest transferable finding is about ordering: on the 8,911-claim leaderboard family, reading order supports 0, a random draw a median of 28 (max 302), and an oracle best-first bound 6,545, so a study whose claim order is otherwise arbitrary has two orders of magnitude to gain by shuffling with a seed fixed before pricing. Carry the guarantee's weakness with it: mFDR_η is a ratio of expectations implying neither FDR nor FWER — in a 4,000-trial simulation where every discovery is false by construction, the raw false share reached 1.00 while mFDR_η read 0.0031 and passed.   • A published average effect of agent adoption can be a mixture of two populations with a 2× gap between them, and the pooled number describes neither. The moderator that splits them was sitting in the repository the whole time — computable by a script, with no survey and no cooperation from the teams. (Added 2026-08-27, T1 — strong.) The second half generalizes past this study: pooling across a different, correlated partition compresses the same gradient from 2.0× to 1.5× and nearly erases it on a second outcome, purely because one population sits almost entirely on one side of the second cut — so the effect size you report is set by which partition you happen to pool over, and neither choice is more natural.   • Your claims are differences, so the noise floor to measure is the spread of the arm delta across identical re-runs — roughly double the within-arm figure — and it is a property of the axis, not of the study. (Added 2026-08-23, T1 — strong.) Three repeats on a pinned 25-unit subsample, unit set held fixed across repeats. Within-arm median spread is 8.3 pp treated and 8.0 pp control; either number quoted alone makes a 12-point effect look comfortable. The arm-delta median is 14.4 pp with a maximum of 32.7, and one decision axis changes sign between identical re-runs (+23.3, then −9.3) (measure-the-arm-delta-spread-not-the-arm-spread). The part that generalises furthest is the per-axis finding: applying each axis's own measured spread retires four of the study's six candidate effects — including a +18.7 pp one — while a +17.4 pp one on a quieter axis survives. "Reporting a single global threshold would have gotten both of these backwards." Two methods worth stealing: hold the unit set fixed across repeats (an earlier pass inflated the estimate to 35 pp by comparing 25 units against the 12 that happened to finish, measuring the unit sets rather than the churn — caught and reported rather than quietly fixed), and look for corroboration in your own other arms (an unrelated experiment in the same study independently showed executability varying 12.9 pp across identical re-runs, a free replication of the floor). The justification this map should carry into every future arrival: "a benchmark that cannot bound its own noise cannot adjudicate anyone else's claims — including ours." This is a-null-measured-once-is-a-point-estimate arriving from the positive side, and it is the same instinct as coordination-deltas-need-a-measured-noise-floor with the per-axis correction attached. Scope: three repeats estimate the floor without characterizing its distribution, the subsample is 25 units, and the 1/√n projection to n=75 is flagged as a heuristic. [T1, strong]
  • A bootstrap interval and a specification interval answer different questions, and more data narrows only one of them. (Added 2026-08-23, T1 — strong.) A paired or task-cluster bootstrap summarizes sampling variation for a fixed estimand under a fixed resampling design — under a superpopulation reading it treats the observed tasks as exchangeable draws while holding the evaluator's semantics fixed. A finite specification interval summarizes variation over declared definitions and does not shrink with n at all. On one frozen substrate the pair margin's sampling interval is a clean one-signed [−12.10, −3.92] pp while the simultaneous specification-plus-sampling envelope is [−13.36, +18.89] pp — containing zero and both signs, with no data changed (report-specification-variation-separately-from-sampling-variation). Two supporting quantities a reader can look for elsewhere: the complete observed sign pattern across the eight specification cells appears in only 24.1% of bootstrap draws, and the pooled effect is heterogeneous by corpus (−2.40 pp with no within-corpus reversal against −10.42, interaction +8.02 [+1.47, +14.95]), so the headline reversal lives in one of the two corpora — report-the-delta-inside-a-difficulty-band applied to composition rather than difficulty. The reporting rule: name the declared family, report every alternative in it, report both intervals separately, and never let the narrow one stand in for the wide one. [T1, strong]
  • The demand this map exists to make has a defect one level up, and it is now measured: a null measured once is a point estimate, and the natural repair to a broken statistic can be fitted to the very noise it was fitted on. (Added 2026-08-22, T1 ×2 — strong.) This map has spent its life asking for a measured floor beside every delta. The floor is a measurement too. Worked example, and it is the cleanest thing in the vault on this: an expansion statistic gives a frozen, untrained model 0.280 — it "acquires" more than a quarter of the problems it had not reached. All seven of those are single lucky samples, so requiring two successes drives that comparison's null to exactly zero and appears to certify the fix. It does not. Estimated across the 110 frozen comparisons the same multi-arm design already contained, the repaired null is 0.058 [0.038, 0.078] — larger than the 0.048 it was invoked to dismiss — reaching 0.136 on the worst pair, with ER₃ at 0.023 and only ER₅ approaching zero (a-null-measured-once-is-a-point-estimate). The replacement is to stop thresholding: pool every independent evaluation of the untreated system into one baseline, give every arm that denominator, and run a per-problem exact test under false-discovery-rate control — which returns zero detections on all eleven held-out replicates and is unchanged under Bonferroni, under a joint procedure, at fixed thresholds and at every pool size. The replicates cost nothing (every arm's checkpoint 0 is an independent evaluation of the untreated system) and the count is attached: four is not enough — over subsets of size four the same null still ranges 0.022–0.098 — and nine is where it holds within ±0.02. Most studies this vault ingests run three or four arms. Two companions arrive with it. (a) The control must be run under the arm's design: a two-checkpoint no-op against four-checkpoint arms understates the floor (4/5 becomes a median 10/8, cutting the arms' apparent margin from 18–21× to 11–13×), and a shared token cap stops being shared the moment one arm's style drifts, scoring the most effective arm as the most destructive at 16.5% → 65.3% truncation (match-the-controls-design-to-the-arms). (b) A treatment that works by excluding work sets its own dose: credit rules that zero out turns drop them from the batch, so realised optimizer steps ranged 112 to 8 per round under identical budgets, and controlling for parameter displacement removes the entire between-arm instrument signature (partial Mantel ρ = +0.078, p = 0.774, against a credit-sparsity association of +0.912) — so a comparison of such rules that does not match effective sample size measures dose, not doctrine (match-the-training-dose-before-comparing-credit-rules).
  • A single greedy decode is not a state, and no amount of careful decoding makes it one. (Added 2026-08-22, T1 — strong.) An untrained model re-evaluated twice under one greedy sample per problem reports 6 learnings and 9 corruptions — a corruption-to-learning ratio of 1.5, the exact shape of the alarming finding the self-improvement literature publishes, manufactured from nothing; the solve-rate estimator on the same samples reports 1 and 0. The mechanism is tested rather than cited: 64 concurrent requests versus strict serialisation cuts verdict flips 16/200 → 4/200 (Fisher p = 0.005), and a fully serialised frozen model still flips 2% — server-side batching across tenants and nondeterminism below the API are not reachable from the client. On a 1,163-problem set, 18.9% of problems change state under greedy decoding against 0.8% under the estimator (a-single-greedy-decode-is-not-a-state). The repair is structural: estimate a solve rate at k samples and discretise with hysteresis, a dead band of ½ ± z·√(1/4k) inside which the previous state holds. This is the decode-level sibling of one-draw-per-item-recovers-none-of-the-reliable-changes — that note says one draw misses real changes, this one says it fabricates changes that are not there, and both are fatal to the same instrument.   • Two nulls arrive, and they cover the two things this map had no instrument for: whether an iterative loop's gain is about the feedback at all, and whether a headline delta is composed of hard tasks or easy ones. (Added 2026-08-21, T1 ×2 — strong on the placebo, suggestive on the stratification.) The first is the sharpest control this map carries. A density-matched feedback placebo keeps the iterative prompt, the round count, the history size, the detected-set cardinality and the progress density, and randomises only which input detected which fault — so Real − Placebo isolates behaviour-aligned attribution while Placebo − Plain bundles scaffolding with coarse progress. Run it and the entire model-dependent movement belongs to the scaffolding: Placebo − Plain is −6.24/+3.23 and −11.39/+4.47, while Real − Placebo is +0.13, −0.50, +1.99, +0.28, none meeting a pre-specified ±2-point equivalence-or-superiority rule, and yield-matched replay drives the two held-out contrasts to −0.30 and +0.78 with zero-crossing intervals (a-feedback-loop-needs-a-density-matched-placebo). The verdict the authors insist on is the one this map wants: unresolved, not established absent — a zero-crossing interval detects no effect and does not establish equivalence (report-a-null-as-a-bounded-interval-not-a-failed-test). Two procedural lessons ride along: their held-out gate failed on task supply and the amendment is signed, dated and reported with an explicit evidential-status table rather than laundered, which is seal-the-analysis-before-the-randomness-exists honoured where it is expensive; and equal slots did not deliver equal usable candidates, so a budget-matched arm must report realised yield or it silently becomes a comparison of format compliance (scoreable-runs-are-a-separate-denominator). The second arrival adds a composition axis rather than a variance one. Because task difficulty is now computable from deterministic static features with calibrated output — no model inference, so no judge-reliability problem imported — a delta can be reported inside difficulty quartiles, which is the only way to distinguish an agent that improved on tasks the reference already passed from one that improved on structurally hard ones (report-the-delta-inside-a-difficulty-band). The same source supplies an independent large-sample estimate of this map's irreducible floor: of 45,769 tasks, 49.8% pass on every trajectory, 32.5% on none, and 17.7% are mixed — about a sixth of a benchmark is a coin, and a single-draw comparison is scoring that sixth as signal.
  • The catalogue gains an entry about resolution rather than a missing arm, and a companion about a null that was correctly refused. (Added 2026-08-19, T1 ×2, strong.) Every prior member of this map's null catalogue names a comparison you forgot to run. (a) This one names a sample size that makes the question unanswerable. Nine model-migration cells contain 457 statistically reliable item-level changes under 50 trials per item with Fisher + Benjamini–Hochberg + a 20-point effect floor; draw one trial per item from the same archive and rerun the identical classification and zero of the 457 is recovered, because two single binary observations can never reach significance (one-draw-per-item-recovers-none-of-the-reliable-changes). The raw flips a single draw does produce are about a coin toss — of 221 incorrect→correct flips only 120 are reliable improvements — and 232 genuinely changed items produce no flip at all, which is the half an eyeball comparison never sees. The design detail worth copying past the result: the study permutes its own labels and reruns the complete classification procedure 1,000 times, reporting that the 95th percentile of the reliable-change share is 0.0% in every cell — so every positive sits above a measured floor rather than an assumed one, which is report-a-null-as-a-bounded-interval-not-a-failed-test applied to a pipeline instead of an estimate. (b) The companion is a study that had every incentive to claim a null and declined. Decomposing a verifier's shifting flag rate into signal-detection terms, the criterion moves in 15 of 15 combinations and survives correction in 13 while ∆d′ survives in 0 of 15 — and the paper refuses to call the second a null, because the two arms are resampled independently so SE(∆d′) is exactly twice SE(∆c) and 13 of 15 ∆d′ estimates lean the same way as the criterion anyway (a-criterion-shift-is-not-a-discrimination-gain). A test half as sensitive by construction returning nothing is not evidence of nothing, and the general rule is that an unpaired second arm silently halves your power on the term that arm exists to estimate — so report the ratio of standard errors, not two p-values side by side. Three further disciplines travel with it: cluster the bootstrap on whatever is reused (each frozen episode serves ~9.3 targets, widening the headline interval by a median 14%, with calibration verified by simulation at the study's own geometry); make every p the achieved significance level of the interval printed beside it, so the two cannot disagree; and declare multiplicity families in code before any p-value is visible, with the analysis printing a warning if a declared family is underpopulated, because a family missing members tests its survivors at a more lenient α/k than advertised.   • The noise floor this map demands for multi-agent work has now been measured, and it is not a constant — it is a property of the task. (Added 2026-08-18, T1, strong.) One condition was collected twice with byte-identical prompts under one pinned model, giving 54 matched same-configuration cell pairs. On chain-shaped work the instrument is excellent: no matched cell differs after Benjamini–Hochberg correction across twenty-seven comparisons, typical means move ~7%, and the scaling exponent reproduces to two decimal places (1.92 and 1.93). On the open-ended task where agents must reconstruct a shared specification, thirteen of twenty-seven cells differ after the same correction — one by a factor of fifteen in messaging (31.4 against 2.0 messages per run at the same configuration) — and the scaling exponent moves from 1.76 [1.57, 1.96] to 2.44 [2.32, 2.57], non-overlapping: two incompatible answers to "how does coordination scale" from the same prompts and overlapping calendar time (a-multi-agent-configuration-is-a-distribution-not-a-run). Drift between distant sessions is ruled out directly — the chained pairs span a longer wall-clock gap and still reproduce. Three consequences. A pinned model identifier is not an experimental control for multi-agent work; it fixes the weights label and nothing about the structure the team happens to build. The sample size that matters is sessions, not runs — ten runs within a session did not protect a topology result that dissolved once the second collection was pooled. And a headline owes its cross-session range where matched sessions exist, which is the honest form of report-a-null-as-a-bounded-interval-not-a-failed-test for a positive result. The bite for reading published work: the multi-agent evaluations the field favours are overwhelmingly single-collection, and the open-ended tasks they prefer are exactly the ones measured here as least reproducible.

  • The unit of evidence is wrong before the metric is: repeating a run controls the smallest term, and the choice among legitimate implementations of the same mechanism dominates it five- to tenfold. (Added 2026-07-31, T1, strong.) This says the field's standard reliability move, repeat-the-run-and-fix-the-seed, is controlling the smallest one.   • The noise is not in your evaluator and not mainly in your seed — it is in which data you drew, and the cheap way of measuring it hides the worst runs.
