Verifier independence — map

Coined 2026-08-12 during the nightly radar. Not a new subject — a subject that had been living in two maps at once and could not be stated in either. loop-engineering holds the within-agent repair results (who should check, and how many rounds); agentic-code-review holds the review-agent results (what a checker should say, and how much of it). Both had been converging for three weeks on the same variable from opposite ends, and on 2026-08-09/10 two independent T1s arrived that state it directly and belong to neither map: the operative property of a checker is what it is denied, not which model it is. Concordia's RETRACE verifies a patch by inferring the problem it implies with the issue withheld; Alibaba/Nanjing's OpenCodeReview filters review comments with a reflector that sees only the diff and never the agent's exploration. Both use the same backbone as the generator and both work, which is the finding. Opened consolidating rather than emerging: two independent T1s, different orgs, different tasks, different proxy layers, agreeing — plus three prior T1s from other angles already in the vault. Auto-coined by the unattended run and flagged for David's review; it is a git revert away if the strand reads better as a third limb of loop engineering.

Status: consolidating (status held 2026-08-14 on the map's first two arrivals since coining. Both extend it and neither is the second independent measurement of the same design that consensus would want: one is a peer-reviewed T1 that widens the mechanism from restrict the evidence to control what the checker compares against, in the tense rather than the content — a frozen prior referent — but it is a single self-reported case with no control arm; the other is an ASE '26 industrial case that finally reports the quantity this map's first open question demanded, what the gate costs when it declines, on judging natural-language responses rather than code. The core claim — that independence is engineered by controlling evidence flow rather than by swapping models — is now carried by six T1s from six groups. Three live tensions are recorded below rather than smoothed away: how much a restricted checker can adjudicate at all, whether anyone can certify independence rather than assert it, and whether the reliability knob is even monotone.)

What we believe now:

  • What a checker compares against is a second lever, independent of what it is shown: a referent frozen before the artifact existed cannot have been contaminated by it. (Added 2026-08-14, T1, single-study.) Every design on this map so far engineers independence by subtraction — withhold the issue text (reconstruct-the-problem-from-the-patch-to-verify-it), withhold the exploration trace (the-checker-should-see-less-than-the-generator) — or by swapping the model (an-independent-checker-beats-the-same-model-checking-itself). A fully instrumented case does none of these: the checker is a fresh session of the same agent, shown the entire codebase and the entire specification, and it audits code against a document written, refined against the source fourteen times, and frozen before a line of the implementation existed — seventeen passes, 116 corrections, terminating on two consecutive empty passes (the-referent-not-the-reviewer-carries-the-separation). The mechanism is the map's own, moved into the time dimension: the referent is immune to the artifact by construction, which is the same protection vacuous-oversight-has-a-measurable-threshold buys by withholding the goal. It is also, uncontrolled, the cell of this map's unrun 2×2 that looked least promising — same model, full evidence — and it worked. [T1 venue, n = 1 — self-reported by the author of the tool and the protocol, no control arm, no different-model or drifting-referent comparison, and the false-rejection side is entirely unmeasured; the specification was written by the same agent that wrote the code, which is a referent shared with the generator in exactly the way a-verifier-sharing-the-interpretation-shares-the-error warns about]
  • A gate's coverage cost can be measured and tuned, and the reliability knob is not monotone. (Added 2026-08-14, T1, strong — this map's first open question, partly answered.) Every design here reports what its checker catches and none reports what it wrongly blocks; this map opened with that complaint. An ASE '26 industrial case reports the whole curve: a cascade of judges from different model families, each tier's acceptance threshold calibrated by conformal risk control against expert labels, escalating on low confidence and abstaining to a human when no tier is confident, reaches 98.8% agreement on 86% of cases at α = 0.14 and 90.9% on 99% at α = 0.16 (price-the-checkers-abstention-as-lost-coverage). Two things to carry. The abstention is the product — the system's answer to is this check trustworthy is allowed to be no, with a finite-sample guarantee attached rather than a tuned threshold. And tightening past α = 0.13 makes things worse, dropping coverage 81% → 70% while discarding qualified verdicts, because judges hold high confidence in some of their wrong answers — so a confidence-gated checker has an interior optimum, the same shape repair-loops-have-an-interior-optimum found for round count. One tension left standing rather than smoothed: the cascade draws its judges from distinct model families on the assumption that this decorrelates their errors, never measures the correlation, and this vault holds a T1 that found the cross-family verifier the worst of three (verifier-diversity-is-not-verifier-independence). The calibration is load-bearing; the family diversity is unvalidated. [T1 peer-reviewed — one embedded case study, one domain, 100 evaluation verdicts, annotators drawn from the target software's own development team; the conformal guarantee assumes calibration and deployment are exchangeable, a condition partly stressed by synthetic persona generation; per-tier bounds do not compose into a system-level bound under adaptive routing; the task is adjudicating natural-language responses, not patches]
  • Independence is a property of the checker's information set, not of its model card. A checker running the same weights as the generator, denied one specific input, outperforms both self-critique and extra deliberation at matched budget. (Added 2026-08-12, T1, strong.) Two arrivals state it on different tasks in the same 48 hours. RETRACE withholds the issue text from a backward reconstructor that must infer, from the patch and trajectory alone, what problem the patch appears to solve — Pass@1 56.2% → 63.2% and 75.8% → 79.4% on SWE-bench Verified across two backbones, transferring to a second scaffold unmodified (reconstruct-the-problem-from-the-patch-to-verify-it). OpenCodeReview withholds the agent's tool-augmented exploration from a reflector that sees only the diff, applies falsification rather than verification, can delete but never generate, and fails open — precision 25–38% across six backends where a general-purpose agent's self-review runs 7–16% (the-checker-should-see-less-than-the-generator). The control that makes the first readable is the decisive one: Self-Refine at a comparable inference budget makes the same agents worse (−1.4 and −1.8 pp). Same model, same spend, opposite sign — so the mechanism is not more thinking, it is thinking that was starved of an input. This reframes rather than contradicts an-independent-checker-beats-the-same-model-checking-itself, which measured a different cheap model re-deriving from the original request reaching near-oracle error recovery: the model swap may have been carrying an information change all along, since a fresh model re-deriving from the original instruction is also a model denied the carried state.
  • Withholding the goal is what makes the check non-vacuous. (Added 2026-08-12, T1, strong.) A checker handed the objective can restate it; agreement between its output and the objective is then echo rather than evidence. Backward reconstruction cannot restate what it was never given, so the comparison against the real issue carries information by construction. That is the concrete, cheap answer to vacuous-oversight-has-a-measurable-threshold — and it generalizes past patches to any setting where the artifact implies its own specification.
  • Restricting the information set buys precision on the class the checker can see and buys nothing outside it. (Carried from 2026-08-05, T1, strong — the live tension.) a-verifier-sharing-the-interpretation-shares-the-error measures a narration-blind verifier with a real external anchor that wrongly passed 68 of 76 non-perfect tasks (~90% miss rate) while rejecting correctly 8 times in 9 — and only 14 of the 68 misses were errors a structural check could have caught. Independence of narration is not independence of interpretation. Read against tonight's arrivals, the reconciliation is that both new systems are careful to narrow the checker's remit to what its evidence can adjudicate — OpenCodeReview leaves untouched any comment referencing context outside the diff, and its filter is explicitly conservative — so they buy the high-precision half of that trade deliberately and concede the recall half. Nobody has yet built a checker that is both independent and broad.
  • Independence has to be measured as error correlation at the intended operating point, not asserted from a vendor diff. (Carried from 2026-08-02, T1, suggestive — the second live tension.) verifier-diversity-is-not-verifier-independence found the cross-family verifier the worst of three (risk 0.240 against 0.041 same-family at matched 70% coverage), same-family error correlation φ = 0.641 among hidden-incorrect revisions, and error dependence shifting with the acceptance threshold rather than being a fixed property of a model pair — at 75% coverage all three scored pairs had positive excess-joint false-acceptance intervals. So "use another vendor's model as the reviewer" is not a measurement, and neither is "use the same model with less context" until someone reports the correlation. This is the open methodological hole in tonight's two results: neither reports how often its restricted checker is itself wrong, and RETRACE never reports a false-rejection rate at all.
  • The generator and the evaluator must not share their reference. (Carried, T1.) separate-the-generator-from-the-evaluator and an-evaluator-sharing-its-reference-solver-cannot-be-self-checked are the same principle one layer up, applied to the evaluation apparatus rather than to the in-loop check — and the same failure recurs there: an evaluator whose reference solution comes from the system under test cannot detect its own blind spot.

  • 2026-08-14 — reag-calibrated-acceptance-testing (Wang, Arora, Xie, Liu, Tantithamthavorn, Aleti & Jiang, Monash / Jiangnan / ANU / Tianjin, ASE '26, "Requirements-Augmented Generation for Trustworthy Acceptance Testing of LLM-Based Software", 2026-08-13, T1, strong): the first checker in this vault allowed to say I don't know, with the cost of saying it printed. Setting: software whose core component is an LLM, where the same query has different correct answers per user persona, so no stored expected output can serve as an oracle. Three judges from distinct model families ordered by cost (Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite → GPT-4.1-mini → Gemini-2.5-Flash); a verdict is accepted when the tier's confidence clears its own threshold, calibrated by conformal risk control on 246 expert-annotated verdicts (fixed-sequence testing, exact binomial upper bound, δ = 0.1), escalated when it does not, and the oracle revoked as abstain when no tier is confident. Confidence is estimated from 5 simulated annotators × 3 in-context examples at temperature 0.7, an approximation forced by black-box APIs. Evaluation on 100 held-out verdicts, expert ground truth from three annotators at Fleiss' κ 0.76. Selective agreement / coverage by α: 0.16 → 90.9% / 99%; 0.15 → 96.7% / 92%; 0.14 → 98.8% / 86%; 0.13 → 96.3% / 81%; 0.12 → 97.1% / 70% — the last step discarding qualified verdicts as well as unqualified ones (price-the-checkers-abstention-as-lost-coverage). Status held at consolidating — it answers the map's first open question in a different task family (natural-language responses, not patches), which is a widening rather than a replication. Ripples: evaluation-integrity (the oracle itself is reconstructed from requirements, personas and domain constraints rather than from expected outputs, at 3.91/5 expert-rated quality with retrieval precision, not generation quality, named as the bottleneck — build-the-oracle-from-requirements-not-from-expected-outputs), model-routing-token-economics (CPP 3.49 against 3.61 and 5.11 for single judges, and a judge distribution of 0 / 72.1 / 27.9 in which the cheap tier handled nothing — escalate-judges-on-calibrated-confidence-not-on-a-fixed-tier).

Open questions:

  • Nobody prices the false rejection — for code. (Narrowed 2026-08-14.) The founding complaint stands for every patch- and diff-level result here: RETRACE reports no false-rejection rate at all, and OpenCodeReview reports precision without what its conservative filter drops. It is now answered in a neighbouring task family — price-the-checkers-abstention-as-lost-coverage reports the whole reliability/coverage curve for a calibrated judge cascade, including the α at which tightening starts discarding correct verdicts — which proves the measurement is cheap and nobody in this map's core task has made it. The one comparable code-side number in the vault (an-smt-gate-buys-interception-with-a-false-block-tax) still says that tax is where these designs usually die.
  • Is a frozen referent a substitute for a restricted information set, or a multiplier on it? (Opened 2026-08-14.) the-referent-not-the-reviewer-carries-the-separation achieves independence with no information restriction and no model boundary, purely by fixing the referent in time — one uncontrolled case. Nothing stops a checker from being restricted and comparing against a frozen prior document, and nobody has run the arms. The variant that would decide it is cheap: same task, same checker, referent frozen versus referent allowed to be rewritten alongside the code.
  • Is the model boundary worth anything once the information boundary is in place? RETRACE's reconstructor is the generator's own model. Nobody has run the 2×2 — same/different model × full/restricted evidence — and it is a cheap experiment that would settle whether an-independent-checker-beats-the-same-model-checking-itself was measuring what it thought it was.
  • What is the general rule for which input to withhold? "The issue" and "the exploration trace" were chosen by two groups on intuition and both worked. There is no principle yet for identifying, given a task, the input whose removal makes the check non-vacuous without making it blind — and a-verifier-sharing-the-interpretation-shares-the-error is the standing evidence that the wrong choice yields a checker that is confidently useless.
  • Does the boundary survive a stronger generator? Every result here is measured on generators that make the errors the checker is looking for. If an-evolved-harness-compensates-defects-it-does-not-raise-the-ceiling generalizes, a policy that stops committing a defect class makes the check that catches it worthless — and nobody has measured the independence payoff as a function of base-model capability.
