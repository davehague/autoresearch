Walking ranks among the top exercise modalities for sleep — against active controls, which is the comparison that counts

Across 200 RCTs (n = 23,523), the modalities showing the largest benefits versus active controls were qigong, walking and high-intensity interval training.

The active-control framing is what makes this worth recording at all. The vault's standing objection to the exercise-and-sleep corpus is that a no-treatment control cannot separate exercise from attention, expectation and the ritual of being in a trial — every included study is open-label with a self-reported endpoint. A contrast against an active comparator does not eliminate that problem, but it does subtract the largest part of it: both arms get attention, both arms get a protocol, and the difference that survives is closer to the exercise itself. This is the comparison the vault asked for on daily-steps and it is the first source to report one.

Two of the three winners are already Foundations, and the vault takes no credit for that. Walking is daily-steps; HIIT sits on zone-2-cardio, where Chu 2026 already found it beats moderate continuous training for VO₂max in healthy elderly. Qigong the vault has no map for and coins none — a single qualitative ranking mention is nowhere near the bar, and it would fail GOAL.md on burden and novelty long before benefit.

The ranking is recorded, and deliberately not read as a leaderboard. The vault has now caught rank inflation four times — Jiao's stretching crown (appraised unreliable), Drummond's collagen SUCRA 98.9%, Sun's collagen BMD SMD 2.67, and the ranking layer of Zhou's own insomnia network — and the discipline is to distrust the ordering and trust only the CI-backed contrast against control. That discount applies here even though walking placing well is a result the vault would like, which is exactly when the rule earns its keep. Compounding the caution: no effect sizes, intervals or ranking statistics are readable from the available abstract, so "largest benefits" is a qualitative claim the vault cannot inspect.

It changes nothing practical, for the reason the same paper supplies. Walking placing near the top of a sleep ranking is worth much less than it sounds once the same analysis establishes that the benefit concentrates in poor sleepers (exercise-sleep-benefit-concentrated-in-poor-sleepers) and that overall certainty is very low (exercise-sleep-certainty-very-low-at-200-trials). David already walks, and the walk is already recommended — on mortality, once.
