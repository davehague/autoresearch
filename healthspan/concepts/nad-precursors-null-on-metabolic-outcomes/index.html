NAD precursors do nothing to conventional metabolic health — the third independent outcome family to come back flat, and the trials agree that it is flat

Fifteen randomised trials of oral nicotinamide mononucleotide, pooled: no effect on body weight (−0.32 kg, 95% CI −1.19 to 0.54), BMI (+0.02 kg/m², −0.14 to 0.18), fasting glucose (+0.73 mg/dL, −1.36 to 2.82), HbA₁c (+0.03%, −0.04 to 0.10), HDL cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, total cholesterol, triglycerides, or systolic blood pressure.

Two features make this a more informative null than most. First, heterogeneity is essentially zero — I² = 0% for BMI, fasting glucose, HDL and systolic blood pressure, 14.7% for weight, 22.4% for HbA₁c. I² measures how much the trials disagree with each other; near-zero means they are not scattered around a hidden true effect, they are agreeing that nothing happened. Second, the review's own GRADE ratings are best on exactly these outcomes — moderate certainty for body weight, BMI, fasting glucose and triglycerides, which is the highest grade anything in the paper receives. The nulls are the best-evidenced results in the review and the one positive is among the worst.

HbA₁c deserves a sentence, because the interval is the finding. HbA₁c is the fraction of haemoglobin that has sugar attached, reflecting average blood glucose over roughly three months — the standard marker for whether someone's glucose control is improving. The pooled estimate is +0.03% with an interval from −0.04 to +0.10. That interval is narrower than the smallest change anyone would act on clinically. It does not merely fail to find a benefit; it rules out a clinically meaningful one in this population over these durations.

Insulin resistance is the one place a direction survives, and it does not reach significance. HOMA-IR — an index computed from fasting glucose and insulin as a proxy for how well insulin is working — came in at −0.22 (−0.55 to 0.11) on 5 comparisons and 160 people, with the same direction after a standardised-mean-difference sensitivity check, and very low GRADE certainty. Dose and duration subgroups showed nothing (p = 0.93, p = 0.50). The authors call it "hypothesis-generating" and the vault agrees; it is recorded here as a direction, not a finding. One high-precision glucose-clamp trial in overweight postmenopausal women with prediabetes did find improved peripheral insulin sensitivity — a real mechanistic clue in a narrow population, and the closest thing to a live thread the efficacy side of this map has.

⚑ Why this is the third strike, and why the pattern matters more than any single null. nad-precursors already holds two higher-tier failures in unrelated outcome domains: muscle — 10 RCTs, flat on muscle mass, grip strength, gait speed and chair-stand (nad-precursors-null-on-muscle-mass-function); and cognition — a phase-II randomised trial in adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment where blood NAD⁺ doubled, confirming the pill arrived and worked pharmacologically, and cognition, cerebral blood flow and blood pressure all stayed put (nicotinamide-riboside-raises-nad-without-cognitive-benefit). Now metabolic health, across 15 trials, with the trials agreeing.

Three outcome families. Three flat results. From a compound with a verified, measurable pharmacological effect. That is the sharpest available form of this vault's standing rule — target engagement is evidence the pill works; it is not evidence the pill helps — and it is why the map's position is not "the biology is nonsense" but the harder claim that the biology is coherent, the target is engageable, and the humans still do not get better.

What this note does not do: fade the map. Appraised mixed and therefore capped suggestive, this source cannot move a Status on its own (shared/evidence-model.md), and the same review carries one robustly-replicated diastolic-blood-pressure signal that keeps the map from being quiet (nmn-diastolic-blood-pressure-signal-is-exploratory-and-low-certainty). Whether three null outcome families plus one low-certainty surrogate should now read as fading rather than contested is a judgment beyond what this source licenses, and it is flagged for a human rather than taken.
