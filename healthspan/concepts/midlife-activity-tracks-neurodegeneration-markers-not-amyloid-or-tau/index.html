If activity protects the ageing brain, this says it is not doing it through amyloid

The vault holds a large observational literature associating physical activity with less dementia - physical-activity-domain-flips-the-sign-of-the-dementia-association pooled 4.2 million adults on it. What that literature does not say is by what mechanism. This cohort went at the question by measuring the molecules eleven years downstream.

Four plasma markers, two of which are Alzheimer's-specific and two of which are not.

  • p-tau217 and the amyloid-beta 42/40 ratio index the disease itself - tangles and plaques.
  • Neurofilament light chain (NfL) indexes neuronal damage from any cause; the authors tie it to cerebrovascular pathology, white-matter hyperintensities and small-vessel burden.
  • GFAP indexes astrocyte activation, a proxy for neuroinflammation.

Midlife MET-hours were inversely associated with NfL (B = -0.10, -0.17 to -0.03) and GFAP (B = -0.08, -0.15 to -0.01), and with neither Alzheimer's-specific marker.

⚑ The nulls are the informative half, because their intervals are tight and centred. p-tau217 sits at -0.00 with an interval of -0.08 to 0.07. That is a demonstrated absence across the range this study could detect, not a failure to look. Animal models show exercise reducing both amyloid and tau deposition; the authors note that human studies have largely failed to reproduce it, and this is one more failure with better markers than most.

The authors' reading, and it is the load-bearing sentence: the inverse association between activity and dementia "may be driven through amyloid- and tau-independent mechanisms that protect against vascular pathology (small vessel disease, white matter hyperintensities)." NfL's known association with exactly that pathology is what makes the hypothesis concrete rather than a shrug.

⚠ Three things bound it, and they are recorded in full in the-midlife-activity-biomarker-exposure-is-one-recalled-week-and-its-binary-version-is-null. In brief: the exposure is a single self-recalled week; the GFAP finding appears only after seven covariates are added; and the binary "met the WHO guideline" version of the same exposure was associated with nothing at all.

⚑ Two secondary results survive as directional signal. The association attaches to activity at 56 and not at 67 - the concurrent analysis is null on everything after full adjustment, which is the temporal ordering a causal reading would want. And splitting by intensity, moderate-to-vigorous but not light activity carried both associations, which agrees with vigorous-intensity-drives-the-mvpa-cancer-association and hiit-beats-mict-vo2max-elderly on other outcomes.

⚠ The outcome is a surrogate. Nobody here was followed to a diagnosis. NfL and GFAP predict decline; they are not decline, and the vault already records in most-consensus-aging-biomarkers-did-not-predict-mortality how often that gap turns out to matter.
