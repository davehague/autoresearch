The machine and the questionnaire finally agree about exercise and sleep — in 20 trials out of 200, and only a little

In the 20 trials (of 200) that reported objective sleep efficiency, exercise produced a small but significant improvement, described as consistent with the subjective outcomes pooled in the same analysis.

This is the first cross-instrument agreement in the vault's sleep literature, and every prior source disagreed with itself. The vault's sleep table records three meta-analyses in which "every paper's strongest result is the one its design is weakest at measuring":

  • Liu 2026 — device-measured total sleep time +34.23 min, subjective PSQI null (walking-improves-objective-sleep-duration-and-wakefulness, walking-subjective-sleep-quality-unresolved)
  • Kong 2026 — subjective g = −0.76, no objective outcome pooled at all (walking-subjective-sleep-quality-large-effect)
  • Zhou 2026 — subjective PSQI −2.80, total sleep time null across the whole network (exercise-does-not-increase-total-sleep-time)

Liang is the first to move both instruments in the same direction at once. That is real progress on the vault's central objection to this literature — that an unblindable intervention scored by self-report cannot distinguish sleeping better from believing you slept better. An objective endpoint moving in agreement is exactly the corroboration the questionnaire needed.

Bounded hard, because the numbers are small in two different senses. The effect is small, and it rests on 10% of the corpus — 20 trials, a subset that is almost certainly not random (trials that can afford actigraphy or polysomnography differ systematically from those that cannot). No point estimate or confidence interval is readable from the available abstract, so the vault holds the direction of the convergence and not its magnitude. It also does not rescue the paper's overall certainty, which the authors rate very low (exercise-sleep-certainty-very-low-at-200-trials).

Note what did and did not replicate. Sleep efficiency moved. This says nothing about Liu's +34 minutes of total sleep time, the vault's most-liked and most-distrusted sleep number, which Zhou's 31-RCT network already failed to corroborate. Different outcome, still unreplicated.

Nothing is promoted. A small improvement on a secondary instrument in a tenth of the trials does not turn a logged, ungraded sleep bonus into a recommendation. daily-steps stays counted once, on mortality.
