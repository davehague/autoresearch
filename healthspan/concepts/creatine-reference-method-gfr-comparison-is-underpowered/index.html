The comparison that exonerates creatine is a p = 0.06 null, and the vault refuses it as equivalence

Almeida 2026 concludes that creatine's apparent effect on kidney filtration reflects "altered creatinine metabolism rather than kidney injury, given the absence of changes when assessed using Cr-EDTA." That absence is the paper's decisive comparison, and it is MD +5.89 mL/min (95% CI −0.30 to 12.08), p = .06.

An interval that runs from −0.30 to +12.08 is compatible with three different worlds: filtration genuinely unchanged, filtration modestly improved, and — at the very edge — a trivial decline. It is not compatible with "proven equivalent," and no null ever is. A null result means this study did not detect a difference; demonstrating equivalence requires a study designed and powered to exclude a difference of a specified size, and the retrieved record does not even state how many trials or participants contributed to this comparison.

This vault has refused underpowered nulls eleven times, most of them in directions that would have been rhetorically satisfying: Shen 2026's fall-risk interval reaching 11.11 written up as "similar safety profiles" (rt-intensity-safety-comparison-underpowered); Liu's PSQI interval that turned out to contain a real effect within 24 hours; a Cochrane review's own attention-control comparison on 123 people (exercise-hip-osteoarthritis-pain-null-against-attention-control-underpowered); and tonight, a 10-trial HbA1c null (time-restricted-eating-null-on-hba1c-in-glucose-dysregulation) and a 7-trial lean-mass null (morning-protein-null-on-muscle-mass-and-strength-in-older-adults).

Refusing this one is harder, because the vault agrees with where it points, and that is exactly when the rule earns its keep. The discipline only means something if it runs against results the vault likes as readily as against results it doesn't.

What saves the artifact conclusion is not this null — it is the company it keeps. Four other kidney markers that do not run through creatinine are also flat (creatine-null-on-urea-albuminuria-and-proteinuria). Convergence across independent measures is a legitimate inference; a single p = .06 is not. So the honest statement of the vault's position is: four non-creatinine markers show nothing and the tracer method fails to reproduce the drop, which together make the measurement-artifact reading clearly the most parsimonious one — and not creatine has been shown not to affect kidney filtration.

The gap this leaves is specific and worth naming: a trial powered for equivalence on directly measured filtration, at a stated dose and duration, would close it. Nothing in this vault has that.
