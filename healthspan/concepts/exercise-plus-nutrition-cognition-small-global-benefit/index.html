Combined exercise + nutrition improves global cognition by SMD 0.15 — statistically real, practically near-invisible

A PRISMA-guided, prospectively registered (PROSPERO CRD42024528600) random-effects meta-analysis of 14 randomised controlled trials, 4,013 adults aged ≥65 (mean 71.4, cognitively healthy or with MCI, none with diagnosed dementia) finds that pairing an exercise intervention with a dietary or nutritional-supplementation intervention improves global cognitive function versus control: SMD 0.15 (95% CI 0.07–0.24), I² = 50%. Methodological quality was rated by PEDro, risk of bias by Cochrane RoB 2, and pooling used DerSimonian–Laird random effects.

The magnitude is the finding. The interval excludes zero, and its upper bound is still below 0.25 — so even the most favourable reading of this estimate is a small effect. The paper describes it as "modest." With n=4,013 pooled, statistical significance is cheap; the statistical vs. clinical significance lens (shared/study-appraisal.md) fires directly here, and the honest translation is that a combined program moves a global cognitive composite by an amount unlikely to be noticeable to the person doing it.

What this claim does not say — and the reason it is the most important caveat in this ingest. Every one of the 14 trials compares exercise+nutrition against a control, never against exercise alone. So the design is structurally incapable of showing that the nutritional component contributed anything. This claim therefore supports "a combined program beats doing neither" and not "adding nutrition to training helps." The vault records the former and explicitly declines the latter, per shared/safety.md — a plausible mechanism is not a finding. Given that the vault separately holds exercise as an established cognitive lever (zone-2-cardio, resistance-training) while omega-3 (omega-3) and multivitamins (multivitamins) are null on cognition, the most parsimonious reading of SMD 0.15 is exercise, diluted — a hypothesis this source cannot test and this vault does not grade.

Bounded honestly (why suggestive, capped). The source's reliability verdict is mixed, which caps strength (shared/evidence-model.md). Blinding is structurally impossible in a combined exercise-and-diet trial, cognitive testing is effort-sensitive, and expectancy is an unquantifiable contributor to an effect this small. The authors name protocol, duration, instrument, and adherence-measurement variability as limits on comparability.
