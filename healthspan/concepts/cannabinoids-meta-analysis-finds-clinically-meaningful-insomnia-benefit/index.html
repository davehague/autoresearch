A second meta-analysis finds cannabinoids clinically meaningfully improve insomnia — pushing back on this map's one other source

Pooling 10 RCTs (n=2,134), cannabinoids versus placebo or melatonin significantly reduced Insomnia Severity Index (MD −3.73) and Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (MD −3.94) — both effect sizes larger than the ~3-point minimal important difference this vault has used elsewhere to call a sleep intervention's effect clinically meaningful (see supplement-sleep-effect-small-and-below-clinical-threshold) — plus significant gains in total sleep time, sleep efficiency, and sleep onset latency. Adverse events were more frequent (RR 3.96, 1.75–9.00) but no serious harms were reported.

Why this matters for the map. medical-cannabis-insomnia was auto-coined on 2026-10-13 from a single source (Varela 2026, Cochrane review, 9 RCTs, n=537) whose central finding was the opposite practical conclusion: every efficacy interval too wide to call, with the one precise estimate being an elevenfold increase in adverse events. This source pulls from a similar, overlapping RCT literature over a similar search window and reaches a materially different conclusion with tighter, significant intervals. This is a genuine evidence-level tension, not a trivial disagreement, and stance: pushes-back records that honestly.

Why Status is held at emerging, not moved to contested. Per shared/evidence-model.md, a Status move to contested requires "a credible higher-tier result" pushing back on the incumbent claim. This source is the same nominal tier (meta-analysis-rct) as Varela 2026, not a higher one — and once the reliability axis is applied, neither clears reliable: Varela is capped suggestive by its own thin, heterogeneous, non-standardized-preparation evidence base; this source is capped suggestive by its unreported RoB2/GRADE/registration and its unstratified pooling of placebo and melatonin comparators (the unwritten "comparator strength" appraisal gap logged repeatedly in docs/backlog.md). Two suggestive-tier results in direct tension do not meet the letter of the contested bar as written — but the tension itself is real and load-bearing, which is why it is recorded prominently here rather than filed quietly as a third confirming source. Human review should weigh in on whether this vault's Status vocabulary needs a term for exactly this shape (two comparable-tier syntheses, opposite conclusions, neither individually higher-tier) — logged to docs/open-gaps-log.md.

Bounded honestly (why suggestive). See the source digest's full reliability appraisal: no RoB2/GRADE, no registration retrieved, and — most importantly — placebo and melatonin comparators pooled without a reported subgroup split, which could move the estimate in either direction depending on how much of the "insomnia or poor sleep quality" population and preparation mix differs from Varela's narrower, compounded-preparation-heavy sample. No dose or product is recommended; this remains prescription/ controlled-substance territory regardless of efficacy findings on either side of this tension (shared/safety.md).
