Moderate-intensity resistance training gave the clearest grip gain in sarcopenia — and the authors still refused to name a best modality

Across a 19-trial network in older adults with diagnosed sarcopenia, moderate-intensity resistance training (MRT) produced the clearest improvement in handgrip strength: MD 2.37 kg (0.76 to 3.98) against routine control. It also ranked highly for skeletal muscle mass index and gait speed, but the paper describes that evidence as uncertain — and on gait speed nothing beat control at all (no-exercise-intervention-beat-control-for-gait-speed-in-sarcopenia).

The effect is reported in kilograms, which is worth noticing. A standardised effect size would have hidden whether 2.37 means anything; a kilogram of grip is a quantity a clinician can hold against a cut-off. The lower bound (0.76 kg) is small but not trivially so.

⚑ The conclusion the authors drew is the finding. From a design built to produce rankings: "the current evidence does not support identifying a single best exercise approach across all outcomes. Exercise prescription for sarcopenia should therefore be guided by the target outcome and clinical relevance." A league-table paper that reads its own league table and declines to recommend the winner is reporting the width of its uncertainty honestly — the opposite of the behaviour in sucra-rankings-can-invert-the-effect-sizes-they-are-built-from.

⚠ This does not overturn the vault's load claim; it bounds it. high-intensity-rt-beats-moderate-for-lower-limb-strength (Shen 2026, ≥70% 1RM beats <70% on leg press, SMD 0.95) and this result are not in conflict: different outcome, different population, different comparators. Handgrip is not a trained lift — no resistance programme targets it directly, so a load–response gradient measured in leg press has no automatic claim on grip. What this adds is a scope condition: heavier builds more lower-limb strength has not been shown to govern grip, and has not been tested in a sarcopenic population at all.

⚠ Dose parameters remain unprescribed. Intensity, frequency and duration were examined in exploratory analyses and described only as possibly shaping responses jointly. No schedule is stated, and none is invented here (shared/safety.md).

⚠ Population caution. These are people with clinically diagnosed sarcopenia. Nothing here transfers by default to a healthy trained adult.
