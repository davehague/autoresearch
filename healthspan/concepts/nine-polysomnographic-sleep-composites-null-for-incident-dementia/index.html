Nine objectively-measured dimensions of sleep, 1,134 dementia cases, and not one association

Five US cohorts, harmonised home polysomnography — the full overnight recording, not a questionnaire and not a wrist tracker — reduced to nine latent sleep composites covering quantity and efficiency, fragmentation, light NREM predominance, N3 predominance, spindle number and duration, REM bouts, respiratory disturbances, slow oscillation–spindle coupling, and spindle amplitude. Run against incident dementia in 5,471 adults with 1,134 cases over a median 5–19 years: no composite reached significance.

Why this is unusual enough to record. Most nulls in this literature are small studies with few events, where "we could not detect it" and "it is not there" are indistinguishable. This one carries 1,134 hard endpoint events and the best exposure instrument the field has. Nine independent shots at a sleep–dementia association, all missing, is a substantive negative result rather than a shrug.

What it does NOT establish, and the vault refuses to over-read it in either direction. No power analysis is retrievable — the full text is paywalled — so this is not demonstrated absence of a sleep–dementia relationship, and it is graded as not demonstrated rather than disproved. Two further limits are structural. First, the nine composites explain only 49% of the variance in the 44 sleep metrics they were built from (sleep-composites-explain-only-half-the-variance-in-their-input-metrics) — so more than half the measured variation in these people's sleep never entered the outcome models. Second, sleep architecture is not randomisable; this is an observational design with an observational ceiling.

Graded confirms, not pushes-back, and the choice is deliberate. This vault has never carried a sleep→dementia claim. sleep-regularity and sleep-duration are built on mortality, and their cognition-adjacent notes already say the signals are small and cross-sectional (sleep-cognition-associations-small-and-cross-sectional) and that the one longitudinal signal on offer dissolved under its own multiplicity correction (sleep-variability-cognitive-decline-signal-does-not-survive-correction). This is an independent consortium reaching the same place at roughly 3.7× the sample size with a hard endpoint attached. It confirms a restraint the vault already had rather than overturning a belief it held.

The one claim in the vault this genuinely bears on is elsewhere. daily-steps carries incident dementia HR 0.62 from its founding dose-response meta-analysis (daily-steps-broad-healthspan-outcomes) — an observational association between a behaviour and a diagnosis. Nothing here contradicts it: this paper tested sleep architecture, not activity, and a null on one exposure says nothing about another. But it is a useful calibration on how easily large observational cohorts with excellent instruments produce nothing at all on this endpoint.
