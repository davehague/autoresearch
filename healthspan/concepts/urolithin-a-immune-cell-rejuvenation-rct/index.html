Urolithin A shifts aging immune cells toward a younger profile — surrogates only, sponsor-run (RCT, n=50)

In a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (NCT05735886) of 1,000 mg/day urolithin A for 28 days in 50 healthy adults aged 45–70, both pre-specified primary outcomes moved:

  • Naive-like, less terminally exhausted CD8⁺ T cells: +0.50 percentage points (95% CI 0.16–0.83, P=0.0437)
  • CD8⁺ fatty-acid oxidation capacity: +14.72 percentage points (6.46–22.99, P=0.0061)

Secondary outcomes moved coherently in the same direction: augmented CD8⁺ mitochondrial biogenesis, more CD56ᵈⁱᵐCD16ᵇʳⁱᵍʰᵗ NK cells and nonclassical CD14ˡᵒCD16ʰⁱ monocytes, improved activation-elicited TNF secretion in T cells, and improved ex vivo bacterial uptake by monocytes.

Why this matters for the vault. It is the methodologically cleanest urolithin-A evidence held here — randomized, blinded, pre-registered, essentially no dropout — and it lands in David's own age bracket (45–70). It reinforces what makes UA the interesting counter-case to nad-precursors: where NMN/NR raise NAD⁺ and nothing functional follows (nad-precursors-null-on-muscle-mass-function), UA engages mitophagy and drags something measurable downstream with it, here in the immune compartment as well as in muscle (urolithin-a-muscle-strength-and-mitophagy).

Why it changes nothing (why suggestive, why the map stays emerging). Three reasons, each sufficient on its own:

  1. It is not independent. Three authors are Amazentis employees, one is the company's president, the senior author consults for it, and Amazentis funded the trial and supplied Mitopure. urolithin-a was already coined on a manufacturer-generated base. This deepens one line of evidence; it does not add a second. The map's open gap asks for independent replication, and that gap is untouched.
  2. Every endpoint is a surrogate. Immunophenotype, fatty-acid-oxidation capacity, cytokines, ex vivo phagocytosis. No infection rate, no vaccine response, no illness, no physical function. "Counteracts immune aging" is a mechanistic claim, not a demonstrated clinical benefit.
  3. The lead result is thin. A 0.50-percentage-point shift in a CD8⁺ subset at P=0.0437, drawn from a large immune panel over 28 days in 50 people, has unknown clinical magnitude.

The vault's verdict is therefore unchanged and its gap sharper: what would move UA is an independent, non-Amazentis trial on a functional or clinical endpoint. Three sponsor-linked surrogate studies do not equal one independent functional one. Watch, don't buy.
