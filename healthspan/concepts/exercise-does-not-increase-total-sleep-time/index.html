The largest exercise-and-sleep network ever assembled moved nobody's total sleep time — which is a problem for a number the vault recorded yesterday

Thirty-one RCTs. 2,457 adults with insomnia — the population in which an effect should be easiest to detect. Ten intervention nodes, including exercise alone, exercise plus CBT, qigong, and four other combinations. Not one of them significantly increased total sleep time.

Set that against what this vault wrote twenty-four hours earlier. walking-improves-objective-sleep-duration-and-wakefulness recorded Liu 2026's finding that walking increased device-measured total sleep time by +34.23 minutes (24.72–43.74). The vault liked that result — it was the one sleep finding in the entire corpus where the machine moved rather than the questionnaire — and it graded the claim single-study anyway, because the objective outcomes rested on only two to three trials each, no publication-bias test was possible, and the pooled populations included Alzheimer's and cancer patients.

That caution was correct, and it is now correct twice over. Zhou's network is roughly four times the size of Liu's, in the population most likely to show a benefit, and it finds nothing on the same endpoint.

What this is, and what it isn't. It is not a refutation. Zhou's TST evidence is sparse (the authors say "limited studies reporting TST"), the instruments are largely subjective where Liu's were accelerometry-based, the comparators differ, and the populations differ (insomnia patients vs Liu's mixed clinical/community mix). Two syntheses that disagree on a thinly-sourced endpoint have not settled anything.

But it is a large, relevant, conspicuous failure to corroborate, and the vault records failures to corroborate with the same weight it records confirmations. A +34-minute effect built on three trials, which a 31-trial network cannot find, is a hypothesis under strain.

The pattern across three meta-analyses published within weeks of each other is genuinely strange, and worth stating plainly:

SourceInstrument that movedInstrument that didn't
Liu 2026 (8 RCTs, walking)device: TST +34 min, WASO −12 minsubjective: PSQI null
Kong 2026 (21 RCTs, walking)subjective: g = −0.76, huge(no objective outcomes pooled)
Zhou 2026 (31 RCTs, exercise combos)subjective: PSQI −2.80, SOL, WASOTST null

Every paper's strongest result is the one its design is weakest at measuring, and every instrument gives a different answer. The direction is consistently positive across all three. The magnitude is unknowable from this literature, and the specific claim that walking buys you half an hour of extra sleep is the least supported of the lot.

Consequence. daily-steps's sleep sub-thread stays thin and ungraded. No Foundation promotion, no number in the regimen, walking still counted once — on its mortality evidence (HR 0.53), which is what it was ever recommended for. The vault applied its own underpowered-evidence lens against a result it wanted to be true, and the next four weeks of literature have started proving it right.
