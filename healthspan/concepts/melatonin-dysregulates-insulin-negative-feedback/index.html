Melatonin dysregulates the β-cell brake, not just the accelerator — and it blocked insulin-induced hypoglycemia outright

Alongside the suppressed first-phase secretion, the same trial found that in MTNR1B carriers melatonin made the insulin-induced decline in second-phase insulin secretion 64.3% slower (95% CI 23.1–119.2, P<sub>adj</sub> = 0.001) — the β-cell's negative-feedback response to exogenous insulin, blunted. The countable consequence: melatonin prevented insulin-induced hypoglycemia entirely in carriers — zero events versus seven on placebo (P = 0.001). Neither effect appeared in noncarriers.

This is the trial's strongest internal-consistency evidence. Nearly everything else in the study is a modelled surrogate from minimal-model analysis; the hypoglycemia result is a hard, countable event tally pointing the same direction as the modelled parameters. Two independent kinds of measurement, one physiology: melatonin dysregulates insulin secretion in both directions in carriers — it blunts the rise when glucose arrives, and it blunts the fall when insulin does.

The co-administration edge worth carrying. A drug that abolishes the hypoglycemic response to exogenous insulin is a co-administration concern for anyone on insulin or an insulin secretagogue — the seven prevented events are, in a laboratory, a curiosity; in a person titrating insulin against a bedtime melatonin habit, they are a masked warning signal. safety_flags: [interaction] is set on that basis, per shared/safety.md's rule that a credible signal is enough to flag and that a missed interaction is worse than an over-cautious one. Nobody in this vault's regimen is on insulin, and this is not a prescription statement — it is recorded so the interaction is on file if the picture ever changes, and cross-carried to glp-1, the vault's only other glucose-acting entry.

Capped suggestive. Ten carriers, one acute 5 mg dose, an intravenous-glucose laboratory paradigm rather than ordinary eating and sleeping. The direction is coherent and the mechanism pre-committed; the magnitude from ten people is not.
