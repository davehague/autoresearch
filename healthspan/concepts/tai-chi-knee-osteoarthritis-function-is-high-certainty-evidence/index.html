Traditional Chinese exercise improving physical function in knee osteoarthritis is one of only twenty-three high-certainty findings in the entire literature

An umbrella review graded 547 outcome associations drawn from 92 meta-analyses of 1,744 randomised trials of traditional Chinese exercises for musculoskeletal disorders. Twenty-three reached high certainty on GRADE. Physical function in knee osteoarthritis is one of them.

What "high certainty" buys. GRADE rates confidence that an estimate is close to the truth, downgrading for risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias. High certainty means further research is unlikely to change the estimate. In a literature where 63% of associations landed at low or very low certainty, arriving at high is a strong signal about reliability.

⚠ And it says nothing whatsoever about size. The review names this association as high-certainty and publishes no effect estimate for it in any retrievable text — no point estimate, no interval, no standardised mean difference. A finding can be certain and trivial. This vault has caught precisely that twice: exercise-hip-osteoarthritis-benefit-real-but-below-clinical-threshold records a Cochrane pain reduction the authors themselves called "unlikely to be clinically meaningful," and celecoxib-knee-oa-pain-advantage-is-below-clinical-perceptibility records a drug advantage below the threshold a patient can feel. Whether this belongs in that company is unanswerable from what can be read.

⚠ And nothing isolates the Tai Chi from the exercise. Comparators in this literature are typically usual care, education or waitlist. No retrievable contrast pits traditional Chinese exercise against an equivalent dose of ordinary exercise, so some unknown share of this effect is the benefit of moving at all — the recurring comparator problem this vault has now logged four times over (exergaming-fall-benefit-tracks-the-weakness-of-the-comparator).

⚠ Physical function in knee OA is measured on self-reported instruments (WOMAC-type scales predominate in this literature), and nobody can be blinded to whether they are practising Tai Chi. Expectation effects are structural, not incidental.

⚑ Where it lands against this vault's existing arthritis evidence. exercise-for-osteoarthritis was coined on a Cochrane review of hip OA in which the benefit against no treatment was real but sub-threshold, and the comparison against other care was null. This is a different joint, from a different design, and it is the first high-certainty signal the vault holds on exercising a painful joint. It does not resolve the hip tension and no Status moves on it — but it points the same practice at the joint that matters most in GOAL.md.

Dose, direction only. The review reports that longer, more frequent, higher-weekly-volume programmes produced greater gains in knee OA. The thresholds are behind the paywall and this vault sets none (shared/safety.md).
