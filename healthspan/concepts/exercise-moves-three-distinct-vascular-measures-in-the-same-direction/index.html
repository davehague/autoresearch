Three physiologically distinct vascular measurements, one direction

Across 51 randomised trials and 2,638 overweight or obese adults, exercise improved endothelial function (FMD, SMD 0.99), reduced arterial stiffness (PWV, SMD −0.31) and reduced arterial wall thickness (CIMT, SMD −0.20). The three outcomes measure different things by different instruments on different timescales, and they moved together.

⚑ The coherence is the finding; the magnitudes are not. Agreement across three physiologically separate measurements is harder to manufacture from bias than any single estimate — which is why this note grades the pattern at suggestive while treating the FMD number itself as unusable. An SMD of 0.99 approaches a doubling of a measurement that typically runs 3-8%, against +1.41 percentage points for anthocyanins across 65 trials (anthocyanins-improve-flow-mediated-dilation-at-dietary-doses) and nothing at all for passive heat (passive-heat-null-on-most-cardiometabolic-markers). This vault's standing instruction on FMD magnitudes, written for a different network, applies unchanged: read the ordering; discount the size (combined-aerobic-resistance-tops-endothelial-dose-response).

⚠ Four diagnostics constrain everything above. CINeMA confidence is LOW for all five control-versus-modality comparisons and no included trial is at low risk of bias; FMD heterogeneity is I² = 87.1%, unexplained by subgroup analysis or meta-regression; Egger's test fires on all three outcomes (every-vascular-outcome-in-this-network-fails-its-own-publication-bias-test); and the descriptive rankings crown a different modality for each outcome, with the FMD winner being a residual category (the-winning-vascular-node-is-the-category-of-everything-that-did-not-fit).

⚠ These are surrogates, all three. A wider, softer, thinner-walled artery is not an averted cardiovascular event. exercise-for-blood-pressure already holds the outcome this mechanism is meant to produce, at much better evidence — two independent networks, 189 randomised trials, combined training at −9.52 mmHg at high certainty. A low-certainty surrogate network cannot add to that, and this note is deliberately not counted as independent support for it.
