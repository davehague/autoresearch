More sprints per session buy no more aerobic fitness — and this time the interval is tight enough to say it

Across 93 trials (78 studies, n = 1,188 untrained adults), sprint interval training raised VO₂max by 8.3% (95% CrI 7.0–9.5%) — and the number of sprints per session did not moderate that gain: −0.04% per additional sprint (95% CrI −0.32 to 0.24%), or −0.09% (−0.64 to 0.48) over a practically meaningful +2-sprint contrast. Excluding the four atypical protocols using more than ten sprints left the conclusion intact (−0.01%, −0.43 to 0.41).

This is the vault's first genuinely well-powered equivalence-shaped claim, and it is recorded as such deliberately. shared/study-appraisal.md's fatal flag #3 fires on an underpowered null sold as "no effect" — the flag behind five refusals on resistance-training-programming (rt-intensity-safety-comparison-underpowered, rt-non-failure-nulls-hypertrophy-endurance-power-underpowered, unstable-vs-stable-rt-strength-mobility-null-underpowered) and behind the hold on stretching-age-generalisation-rests-on-three-middle-aged-trials. The objection was always power, never nulls. Here the interval runs from −0.64% to +0.48% on a Bayesian hierarchical model over 93 trials, with published code, no detected publication bias, and small heterogeneity (τ = 0.018). There is no room in that interval for a benefit anyone would notice. Accepting it is the same rule as the refusals, not an exception to them.

What it does NOT establish, in the authors' own words. The model estimates change per additional sprint around the corpus mean of about six, so it cannot license a minimum effective dose. The authors state this explicitly and write that the minimum "remains to be determined," noting only one study has tested a single sprint. So this concept says extra sprints add nothing; it does not say two sprints suffice. No dose is invented here (shared/safety.md), and a future run must not convert this into a protocol.

Held suggestive, and the cap is about reach rather than trust. The synthesis is registered (PROSPERO CRD42022349104), conflict-free and reproducible; the corpus is of moderate risk of bias with randomisation absent in some included trials, the endpoint is a surrogate (VO₂max, not mortality or function), the population is untrained adults averaging 27.5 years, and the modality is all-out supramaximal cycling — which GOAL.md's patellar-contusion constraint disfavours. Status held emerging on zone-2-cardio: a surrogate cannot lift a map gated by observational mortality data.
