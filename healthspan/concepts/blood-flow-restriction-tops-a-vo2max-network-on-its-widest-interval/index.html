BFR took first place in a VO2max network, on the least certain estimate in it

Across 11 supplementary training modalities in trained athletes, blood flow restriction training posted the largest VO₂max gain — 3.93 mL/kg/min (95% credible interval 0.60 to 7.27) — and the highest SUCRA rank (0.857). It survived both pre-specified sensitivity analyses (dropping high-risk-of-bias studies, and dropping intermittent hypoxic training), alongside HIIT and sprint intervals.

⚑ This is the first source in this vault to place BFR at the top of an aerobic comparison. blood-flow-restriction's benefit ledger is a strength ledger — bfr-adds-lower-limb-strength-in-athletes, bfr-matches-heavy-load-strength-in-knee-rehab, bfr-is-adjunct-not-substitute-for-heavy-load. A claim about maximal oxygen uptake is a different physiological object from a claim about strength under occlusion, and the map did not previously hold one.

⚠ And the rank is the part of this that survives scrutiny least. The authors say so in their own results text: "SUCRA reflects ranking probability, not certainty: BFR's top rank coexists with its widest credible interval." Its lower bound of 0.60 sits below the point estimates of all four modalities beneath it. On these numbers the top five are not distinguishable from one another, and the one ranked first is the one the network knows least about.

⚑ The vault has caught this exact shape three times now, and naming it is the point. balance-modality-rankings-rest-on-overlapping-credible-intervals — a 423-trial balance network whose top four overlapped. bone-density-network-ranks-sites-where-no-intervention-differed — a bone network that ranked sites where nothing differed. Now a VO₂max network. Network meta-analyses reliably produce a headline ranking that their own intervals do not support, and the ranking is the part that gets quoted.

⚠ There is a deeper problem specific to BFR, and this map already documents it: "BFR" is not one dose. bfr-cuff-pressure-prescription-remains-unstandardised and bfr-pressure-band-recommended-twice-and-adopted-neither-time record that cuff pressure is prescribed under at least nine different conventions across this literature, reaching 350 mmHg under one of them (bfr-cuff-pressure-reaches-350-mmhg-under-a-seventh-prescription-convention). A network node labelled "BFR" is pooling interventions that differ in the variable that defines them. Ranking that node first ranks a label.

⚠ The cost ledger is not in this table at all. This network measured VO₂max and nothing else. bfr-occlusion-reduces-walking-volume-and-comfort, bfr-at-80-percent-occlusion-lowered-mental-quality-of-life-against-sham and bfr-safety-not-established-in-trials record burdens and uncounted harms that a ranking on a single benefit outcome cannot see. A first-place finish on one surrogate is not a recommendation.
