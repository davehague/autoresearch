The better the method, the smaller the answer — a base rate worth more than the estimate it qualifies

Żurawski 2026's discussion contains a finding about its own field rather than about its own trials, drawn from a published systematic review of reviews:

"Meta-analyses rated as critically low quality more often reported larger advantages of low-carbohydrate diets, whereas high-quality syntheses generally found small differences or no difference. Larger participant samples were also associated with smaller estimated effects."

Two independent quality axes — methodological rating and sample size — both predict a smaller answer. The authors show the gradient concretely with three syntheses of the same broad question:

SynthesisDefinition / designEstimate
Broad low-carb definition, comparators often energy-restricted while intervention arms ate ad libitumloosest−2.17 kg (−3.36 to −0.99)
This synthesis — strict ≤50 g/day or ≤10% energy, both arms energy-matchedstrictest on exposure−1.49 kg (−2.41 to −0.58)
Restricted to ≥12 months of follow-upstrictest on horizon−0.91 kg (−1.65 to −0.17)

Tighten the definition and the advantage falls. Extend the horizon and it falls further. The authors' own conclusion: "These findings caution against interpreting the magnitude of any single pooled estimate independently of the methods used to generate it."

⚑ The vault takes this as a transferable base rate, not a fact about ketogenic diets. It is the dietary-intervention analogue of a pattern already recorded across several maps: an effect that shrinks as the design that measures it improves is behaving like an artefact of the design. The vault has logged the same directional structure in loneliness-health-association-halves-on-objective-measures (r = −.23 subjective → −.12 objective), in cognitive-training-gait-signal-vanishes-in-high-quality-trials, and in self-reported-sleep-duration-preserves-shape-loses-magnitude. Four literatures, one shape: the further the measurement gets from the loosest version of itself, the less there is.

Where this applies inside the vault, immediately. The next time any dietary approach arrives here with a large effect from a small or low-quality synthesis, the prior is that the effect will shrink under better methods — and the specific reading rule is that a synthesis's eligibility criteria deserve more attention than its pooled number. intermittent-fasting, mediterranean-diet and fermented-foods all rest on syntheses whose inclusion criteria the vault has graded with varying care; this is a reason to read those criteria again rather than a reason to change any of their Statuses.
