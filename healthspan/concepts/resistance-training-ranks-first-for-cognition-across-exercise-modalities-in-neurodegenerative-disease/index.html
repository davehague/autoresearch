A second network meta-analysis independently crowns resistance training for cognition in degenerative brain disease

Comparing six exercise modalities for global cognitive function across Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, and mild cognitive impairment, this network meta-analysis ranked resistance training first (SMD 1.50, 95% CI 0.77–2.23) — ahead of multi-component exercise, functional training, mind-body exercise, and aerobic exercise (all also significant), with flexibility training showing no clear effect. The ranking held after excluding any one of the three diagnosis groups individually — a real robustness check against the result being driven by just one disease population.

Why this matters for the map. resistance-training already carries a near-identical finding from Liu 2026 — a 74-RCT, MCI-only network meta-analysis that handed resistance training the largest cognitive effect in its own paper (ADAS-Cog −5.02, GRADE HIGH) and which the map explicitly declined to turn into a cognitive claim, for three stated reasons: wait-list-only comparators, two-thirds of estimates rated low/very-low certainty, and the headline GRADE-high estimate resting on a network of eight studies (grade-high-certainty-can-sit-on-a-network-of-eight-studies). This is a second, independent team, a broader population (AD + PD + MCI rather than MCI alone), and the same result — resistance training ranking first for cognition among exercise modalities. Independent replication of a ranking is worth more than a single paper's ranking, even when each individually falls short of reliable.

Bounded honestly (why suggestive, and why Status does not move). Certainty is low to very low throughout this network too, heterogeneity is substantial (I²=80%), and several modalities (flexibility training especially) rest on thin per-node evidence. Control-arm composition (active vs wait-list) is not confirmed from the retrieved abstract — the same unresolved confound this map's Liu 2026 note already carries. Population is diagnosed neurodegenerative disease, not healthy aging, and not David's situation (GOAL.md). confirms the existing thread; the map adds no cognitive claim, same as it did for Liu 2026 — the mortality/strength claim this map is actually built on is untouched.
