Somebody finally checked whether the protein was the protein, and published the answer

The problem this addresses is endemic to supplement literature and this vault keeps hitting it. Trials routinely test a tub, not a molecule — whey plus creatine, protein plus leucine, a multi-ingredient "recovery" formula — and then the abstract reports the result as if it were about the named ingredient. The vault has recorded that shape repeatedly: hmb-trial-cannot-separate-hmb-from-the-24-grams-of-protein-beside-it, probiotic-adds-nothing-to-protein-for-performance, synbiotic-added-nothing-to-protein-and-resistance-training-in-older-men, protein-probiotic-headline-confounded-by-comparator-pooling.

⚑ What this paper did. It ran a pre-planned sensitivity analysis excluding every trial arm containing a multi-ingredient formulation, creatine co-supplementation, added amino acids/BCAA/leucine, or a whole high-protein dietary intervention — 5 arms from 4 studies — and re-fitted the whole network on the remaining 33 RCTs, 81 arms, 1,457 participants. Its stated purpose, verbatim: "to assess whether the observed rankings reflect the protein source itself rather than the influence of co-interventions."

The answer. The rankings held and slightly strengthened: whey 0.91 → 0.93, milk 0.78 → 0.80, casein 0.72 → 0.76, with the whey-versus-placebo mean difference moving by less than 0.05 kg. One node moved materially: blends, SUCRA 0.48 → 0.30, after removal of two co-supplemented arms — a node the paper already graded very low certainty on three trials. The authors' conclusion: "the primary findings for the top-ranked protein sources are not driven by co-interventions."

⚑ Why the vault records the method as a finding. This vault holds a consolidating creatine map, nine creatine sources, and a standing complaint that supplement trials bundle ingredients. This is the first synthesis in the corpus to unbundle them on purpose and publish the re-fitted network either way. It also settles a specific local question: whey's ranking is not creatine's ranking in a different tub. And it demonstrates the shape of the check the vault has been asking for — remove the co-intervention, re-run, report the delta — which costs one table.

⚠ What it does not establish. Only 4 trials carried a co-intervention arm, so the analysis had little to remove; a network in which half the arms were bundled would be a sterner test. The excluded arms were identified from published descriptions, not from assay. And the analysis says nothing about the reverse question this vault also cares about — whether protein added to creatine adds anything — which is protein-creatine-omega3-athletes-nma-wang-2026's territory, not this one's.

Capped suggestive. stance: extends — it does not change what the map believes; it raises how much of the belief is attributable to the named ingredient.
