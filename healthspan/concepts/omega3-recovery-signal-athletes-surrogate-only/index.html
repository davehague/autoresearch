Omega-3's biggest number in this vault is for athletic "recovery" — a soft composite, in athletes, from a sparse network

Omega-3 ranked first for recovery: SMD 0.40 (95% CI 0.18–0.62), SUCRA 88.7%, in a network of 35 RCTs in trained athletes.

This is the largest effect omega-3 carries anywhere in the vault, and it is the least consequential one. Set it against what the map already holds:

  • null on global cognition — high-tier, the outcome that would have mattered most (omega3-no-global-cognition-benefit)
  • CVD hard-outcome benefit confined to high-risk / secondary prevention, arriving paired with an atrial-fibrillation harm (Mattumpuram 2025, 42 RCTs) (omega3-reduces-cvd-events-high-risk, omega3-atrial-fibrillation-gi-harm)
  • slows epigenetic-aging clocks — post-hoc, surrogate (omega3-slows-epigenetic-aging)
  • no telomere effect (omega3-no-telomere-effect)

A soft win on a soft outcome does not offset a hard null on a hard one. The vault records the recovery signal and refuses to let it migrate into the map's summary line, because the pattern of "this supplement's best-looking result is always on its softest endpoint" is exactly what the map's honest read is built to resist.

Three specific discounts apply.

"Recovery" is a composite of three different constructs. From the archived full text, recovery was measured as serum creatine kinase (n = 12 trials), delayed-onset muscle soreness on visual analog scales (n = 14), or functional recovery via repeated performance testing (n = 8). A blood enzyme, a self-reported pain rating and a performance test are not one outcome — and the largest single contributor is the self-reported one, in trials where blinding a fish-oil capsule is feasible but a fishy aftertaste famously undermines it. Pooling three constructs produces a number with more apparent precision than the underlying evidence supports.

The ranking is not trustworthy, and the paper's own GRADE table says so. Certainty is moderate for omega-3 versus placebo on recovery — but LOW for the indirect active-vs-active comparisons that generate the SUCRA ordering, downgraded for imprecision (supplement-outcome-specific-ranking-too-tidy-for-a-sparse-network). So the vault keeps the interval against placebo — 0.18 to 0.62, at moderate certainty — and discards the crown, which is exactly the split the authors documented and then declined to apply to their own conclusion.

The population is trained athletes. Post-exercise recovery in people training hard six-plus months is not a healthspan outcome and is not David's situation. GOAL.md optimises for least work and least discomfort for maximum benefit in a middle-aged adult with a recovering knee — a soreness endpoint in athletes does not enter that calculation.

Net: a new, thin thread on the map; no Status move, nothing bought. Omega-3's position is unchanged — emerging, valuable mainly as a cardiovascular consideration for high-risk people, with an AF harm that has to be stated in the same breath.
