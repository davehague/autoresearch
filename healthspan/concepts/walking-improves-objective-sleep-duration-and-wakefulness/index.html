Walking lengthened device-measured sleep (+34 min) and cut wakefulness (−12 min) — on a base of two-to-three trials

A meta-analysis of 8 RCTs (n=683) found walking interventions associated with +34.23 minutes of total sleep time (95% CI 24.72 to 43.74) and −11.58 minutes of wake after sleep onset (−18.50 to −4.66). These are device-based outcomes. Sleep efficiency and sleep latency showed no significant effect.

Why this is graded single-study despite sitting on a meta-analysis of RCTs. The evidence_tier records what the design is; strength records what this evidence earns. The authors state plainly that objective sleep outcomes were available from only two to three studies per outcome. A 34-minute pooled effect drawn from three small trials is a direction with a number attached, not an established magnitude — and the source's mixed reliability verdict caps it at suggestive before that consideration even applies. The vault grades it one step lower still. No publication-bias test was possible (<10 studies), which is exactly the condition under which a handful of small positive trials produces a large pooled effect.

Why it matters anyway — the signature is the point. Every other sleep lever in the vault moves the questionnaire and not the machine: l-theanine-subjective-sleep-quality (subjective gain, while the highest-tier synthesis pools no sleep endpoint at all — l-theanine-stress-benefit-driven-by-biased-trials), chamomile-sleep-quality-awakenings (I²=88%), magnesium-sleep-quality-weak-signal, and melatonin-modest-sleep-cognition-gain-in-impairment (+12 min, in cognitively impaired elders). Here the accelerometer moved and the PSQI did not (walking-subjective-sleep-quality-unresolved). Expectancy inflates a questionnaire far more easily than it inflates a device, so this is the harder signature to fake — which is why a thin result in this direction is still worth carrying.

What it does not license. No Status change, no new map, and no regimen promotion. Walking already earns its place on mortality grounds (daily-steps-mortality-dose-response — ~7,000 steps/day, HR 0.53; daily-steps-broad-healthspan-outcomes). If the sleep dividend is real, it is a second return on a lever David is already pulling, not a reason to add anything. The source's own trials ran 30–75 min, 3–5×/week, with a subgroup hint that ≥30-minute sessions did better; no dose is invented beyond that range (shared/safety.md).
