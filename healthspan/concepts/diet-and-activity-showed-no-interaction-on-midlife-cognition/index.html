Diet and activity did not combine — the midlife synergy the multidomain premise assumes was absent

Formal interaction terms between Mediterranean-diet adherence and device-measured moderate-to-vigorous activity on cognition were tested and none was observed. In combined tertile analyses, better diet quality tracked with better cognition at age 51 irrespective of activity level — the high-diet/low-activity group beat the low-diet/low-activity group by β = 0.185 (0.046 to 0.325), and being active on top of a good diet did not add a detectable increment.

Why this matters for the vault. multidomain-lifestyle exists because of a premise: that diet, exercise, cognitive training and vascular management combine into something larger than any one of them, which is what "multidomain" means and what the FINGER-family trials are built to deliver. This cohort found no combination effect at all between the two largest components, in the age group multidomain programmes are increasingly being aimed at.

It also lands on a live tension in that map. exercise-nutrition-cognition-effect-survives-excluding-multidomain records that an exercise-plus-nutrition benefit persisted when full multidomain programmes were removed — additivity of a sort. This finds no interaction between the same two levers when both are measured in one population. Additive-but-not-synergistic is a coherent reconciliation of the two, and it is a smaller claim than the multidomain literature usually makes.

Bounded honestly (why suggestive, and why this is not a refutation). A null interaction is the weakest kind of null: interaction terms are notoriously underpowered, typically requiring several times the sample size needed for a main effect, and n = 3,028 across a tertile grid is not much for the job. No power analysis for the interaction is retrievable, the full text is behind an inaccessible host, and one observational cohort does not overturn randomised multidomain trials. This is an observational data point against a premise, not evidence that combining levers fails. The vault's standing rule applies unchanged: an underpowered null is an open question, not a demonstration of absence.

Rebutting note. Recorded as pushes-back on the synergy premise rather than on any single claim; the back-edge sits on exercise-nutrition-cognition-effect-survives-excluding-multidomain.
