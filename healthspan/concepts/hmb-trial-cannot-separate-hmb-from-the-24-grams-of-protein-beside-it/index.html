A trial named after one ingredient tested seven of them at once

Jiang 2026 preserved 1.3 kg of skeletal muscle mass through a 12-week calorie deficit (hmb-enriched-supplement-preserved-muscle-through-a-calorie-deficit-without-exercise). It cannot say what did it.

Energy was matched. Nothing else was. The comparator was sugar. The trial's title names one of at least seven differences between the arms, and the authors concede it in their own conclusion: "Because the intervention also contained protein, longer factorial trials are needed to isolate CaHMB-specific effects." The concession is in the discussion; the attribution is in the title, and the title is what gets cited.

⚑ The vault already grades the most likely candidate, and it is not HMB. protein-target sits at consolidating on Morton 2018's meta-regression across 49 RCTs: the lever is total daily protein, plateauing around ~1.6 g/kg/day (protein-supplementation-augments-resistance-training). Adding 24 g/day to sedentary adults on a prescribed deficit — people who were, on the trial's own inclusion profile, unlikely to be at that target — is precisely the condition in which protein's muscle-sparing effect is best established. The parsimonious reading is that this is a protein trial with HMB in it, and nothing in the design can refuse that reading. Per shared/safety.md the vault records the hypothesis and declines to grade it — a plausible mechanism is not a finding — but it belongs at the front of the map rather than in a footnote.

Why this is the tenth attribution failure the vault has logged, and the cleanest. multidomain-lifestyle holds nine, all of the same shape: diet plus exercise plus counselling plus monitoring delivered as one arm (multidomain-nutrition-exercise-bundle-not-separable), and a 14-RCT literature in which not one trial used exercise-alone as its comparator (exercise-plus-nutrition-cognition-small-global-benefit). Those are bundles of behaviours that genuinely resist separation. This one is different and worse: a single powder whose ingredient list could have been separated by a second arm, and was not. A protein-matched control — protein alone against protein plus HMB — is a trial anyone could run. Nobody in this literature has run it, and this trial's design makes it look as though somebody had.

Why no hmb intervention map is coined. Naming a map after a compound whose effect this evidence cannot isolate would assert in the filename exactly what the experiment does not support. That is laundering by title, and it is the failure mode shared/evidence-model.md's bias-watch exists to prevent. The finding lands on fat-free-mass, where the question is what preserves muscle during weight loss, and the candidate refusal is logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md.

What would close it. A factorial trial with a protein-matched control: isonitrogenous arms differing only in HMB. Until one exists, HMB's independent contribution in humans losing weight is unmeasured, not zero — and the vault says the first, not the second.
