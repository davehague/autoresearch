A GLP-1 you can swallow with food produced class-typical weight loss over sixteen weeks

VCT220, a nonpeptide small-molecule GLP-1 receptor agonist taken once daily by mouth, reduced body weight by 5.75% (80 mg) to 9.73% (160 mg with fast titration) against 1.61% on placebo over 16 weeks, in a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase II trial across 13 Chinese sites. Between 55.4% and 90.3% of participants reached the conventional ≥5% threshold, against 13.1% on placebo. HbA1c, fasting insulin and blood pressure improved; liver enzymes fell.

⚑ The pharmacological point is the delivery route, not the effect size. Most GLP-1 agonists are peptides requiring subcutaneous injection. Oral semaglutide exists but needs an absorption enhancer and strict fasted dosing with tight timing rules. A nonpeptide small molecule needs neither — it can be taken with a meal — which is also why the authors could use a short 4–6 week titration.

⚠ This is a surrogate endpoint at phase II, and it cannot move anything. glp-1 is consolidating on randomised hard-outcome evidence — SELECT's MACE reduction, HR 0.80 (0.72–0.90), NNT 67 (glp1-mace-benefit-rct-select). Under shared/evidence-model.md's highest-tier-wins rule, a 16-week body-weight result cannot move a Status resting on a 17,604-person cardiovascular outcome trial, in either direction. What the map gains is a column, not a tier.

⚠ Sponsor-controlled. The trial was funded by Chengdu Vincentage Pharma, the drug's manufacturer; two co-authors are company employees, one a stockholder. Per shared/study-appraisal.md that is a RED flag and not fatal on its own — but here it compounds a 16-week horizon, a surrogate endpoint and no active comparator, which is what lands the appraisal at mixed.

⚠ What sixteen weeks and 250 people cannot tell you. Nothing about durability or weight regain. Nothing about lean-mass loss, which glp1-lean-mass-loss-magnitude records as a real cost of this class and which this trial did not break out in the retrievable text. Nothing about uncommon harms — the population is far too small, and bfr-adverse-event-absence-replicates-without-becoming-safety records the general form of that reasoning error. A new molecule inherits the class's open questions until someone measures them.

⚠ Population. Chinese adults, mean age 32.4, recruited under a China-specific overweight threshold (BMI 24–28) lower than the WHO cut-off, so "overweight" here does not denote the same population it denotes elsewhere.
