Creatine improves strength in trained athletes, SMD 0.46 — the third independent confirmation, and usefully boring

In a network meta-analysis of 35 RCTs (n = 1,211) in athletes training for at least six months, creatine produced the largest strength effect of the three supplements compared: SMD 0.46 (95% CI 0.29–0.63).

This is a moderate, well-bounded effect that agrees with everything the vault already holds, which is precisely its value. The vault's creatine strength claim now rests on three independent syntheses pointing the same way:

  • Gu 2026 (37 RCTs, healthy men 18–30, GRADE moderate–high) — creatine's lean-mass and strength benefit exists only alongside resistance training: FFM +3.39 kg with training, −0.89 kg, p = 0.566 without (creatine-lean-mass-requires-resistance-training)
  • Bai 2026 (52-RCT athlete network) — the same directional benefit across sprint, jump and upper-body measures
  • this — SMD 0.46 in trained athletes

Confirmation that changes nothing is what a healthy map looks like. creatine-monohydrate is consolidating and stays there; the strength thread was already the map's best-supported one, and adding a third concordant estimate neither promotes it nor complicates it.

What this does not extend. The population is trained athletes, the outcome is a strength surrogate, and the effect is measured against training that is already happening — so it says nothing about healthspan, nothing about untrained or middle-aged adults, and nothing about dose. The vault's dose position is unchanged at 5 g/day: the 10 g-over-5 g case has collapsed across muscle, brain and bone, and this source states no dose that would revisit it. Per shared/safety.md, no dose is inferred from a paper that does not report one for this population.

One caution carried from the parent source. This estimate sits inside a sparse network (~4 trials per intervention-outcome cell) whose overall conclusion — that each supplement has its own outcome speciality — the vault declines (supplement-outcome-specific-ranking-too-tidy-for-a-sparse-network). The creatine-strength cell is the best-populated part of that network and the interval is tight, so this particular estimate is the one the vault trusts most in the paper — but it is graded suggestive, not strong, because the source is appraised mixed.
