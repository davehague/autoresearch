The reference case for an honest subgroup null

Wang 2026 ran eight moderator analyses — green-space type, intervention duration, frequency, participant age, proportion female, geographic region, outcome instrument, publication year — and found none significant. It then declined to claim what every incentive pushed it to claim. Its Limitations section states that the absence of subgroup differences "should not be interpreted as evidence that intervention characteristics or participant factors are unimportant," and instead concludes that "existing evidence is not yet sufficient to determine which specific features of green exercise are most strongly associated with mental health benefits." The authors also restricted subgroup analysis to levels populated by at least three studies, and named thin cells plus methodological heterogeneity as the reason their nulls are uninformative rather than reassuring.

This is the discipline the vault has demanded repeatedly and rarely received. resistance-training-programming carries five consecutive underpowered nulls written as equivalence: Shen's fall-risk RR 2.68 (0.65–11.11) called "similar safety profiles" (rt-intensity-safety-comparison-underpowered); Wu's four I²=0% nulls (rt-non-failure-nulls-hypertrophy-endurance-power-underpowered); Doan's unstable-surface nulls (unstable-vs-stable-rt-strength-mobility-null-underpowered); Chen's 4-trial BFR substitution (bfr-matches-heavy-load-strength-in-knee-rehab). Eight days ago Oba 2026 sold five moderator nulls on 3 middle-aged and 4 older-adult trials against 59 in young adults as evidence the effect "works across diverse healthy individuals" — a claim that would have closed stretching's own population gap had the vault accepted it, and was refused for that reason (stretching-age-generalisation-rests-on-three-middle-aged-trials).

Recorded as a concept rather than admired in an appraisal block, so the standard is citable. shared/study-appraisal.md's third fatal red flag — an underpowered null sold as "no effect" — describes the failure. This is what passing looks like, and the vault now holds two named reference cases: potassium citrate for an honest primary null whose own trial-sequential analysis declared the evidence inconclusive, not negative (potassium-citrate-bone-nulls-honestly-underpowered), and this one for an honest subgroup null.

Symmetry note, since the point is a rule and not a compliment. The same run that records this also accepts two nulls at face value — ASPREE's HR 0.98 (0.92–1.05) across 19,114 people (aspirin-no-effect-on-healthspan-loss-older-adults) and Hutchinson's −0.09% (−0.64 to 0.48) across 93 trials (sit-vo2max-gain-independent-of-sprint-number) — because those have the power the refused ones lacked. The rule is about power, not about whether the vault likes the answer. Wang's eight nulls are held as uninformative; Wang's authors said so first.
