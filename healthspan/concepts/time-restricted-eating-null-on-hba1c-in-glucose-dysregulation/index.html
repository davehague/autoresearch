Time-restricted eating did not lower long-term blood sugar, in the people it should work best for

Ten randomised trials in adults who already have a glucose problem, and HbA1c did not move: −0.30 percentage points (95% CI −0.63 to +0.02), p = 0.06, at moderate GRADE certainty.

What HbA1c is, and why it is the endpoint that counts. Red blood cells live about three months, and sugar sticks to the haemoglobin inside them in proportion to how much sugar is in the blood. Measuring what fraction is sugar-coated therefore gives an integrated average of blood glucose over roughly a quarter. It is the number that defines diabetes, sets treatment targets, and predicts complications — unlike a fasting glucose reading, which is one morning's snapshot and moves with last night's meal, sleep and recent weight change.

Why this matters to intermittent-fasting specifically. The map concluded on 2026-07-21, from the BMJ's 99-trial network meta-analysis, that intermittent fasting is a delivery format for eating less — that no fasting strategy beat plain calorie restriction and that HbA1c was flat everywhere (intermittent-fasting-matches-caloric-restriction-for-weight, intermittent-fasting-cardiometabolic-markers-match-caloric-restriction). The strongest objection to that conclusion was that it came from the wrong population — mostly healthy overweight adults, in whom there was little glucose dysregulation to fix. This is that objection tested, in the specialist journal, and it fails. A confirmation obtained in the population most likely to break a rule is worth more than another confirmation in the population that generated it.

⚠ Refused as equivalence, and the arithmetic is why. The interval runs to −0.63 percentage points, which would be a genuinely useful clinical effect, comparable to what some glucose-lowering drugs deliver. That value is inside the interval. So this is an underpowered null — the twelfth this vault has refused to read as a demonstration of no effect — and it is refused here even though the conclusion it would support is one the vault already holds. It is graded suggestive on the strength of confirming a direction already established by a much larger independent synthesis, not on its own power.

Two structural reasons the null was hard to avoid, stated so the result is not over-read as decisive either. The corpus is 10 trials and 599 people — small for a top-tier question. And mean trial duration was 4 months against an endpoint that integrates 3 months, so HbA1c had barely one clean cycle in which to move. A better-powered, longer trial could still find something.

⚠ Conflict noted, and its direction is unusual. An author is currently employed by Novo Nordisk A/S, and the paper's conclusion — that a free behavioural approach does not move the endpoint that glucose-lowering drugs are sold on — points in the sponsor-favourable direction.
