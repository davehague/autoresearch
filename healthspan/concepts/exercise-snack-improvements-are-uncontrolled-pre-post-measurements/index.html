Everything that improved in this trial improved in people who were exercising, compared with nobody

Jin 2026's headline numbers are large and they are not findings. Body weight, BMI, body fat percentage, estimated VO₂max, systolic blood pressure, standing long jump, total cholesterol and LDL-C all moved with large partial eta-squared values (0.26–0.60) — as time effects across both arms, with no non-exercising control group of any kind.

A pre-post change in people who exercised for eight weeks is not randomised evidence that the exercise caused it. The randomised contrast in this trial is exercise snacks versus HIIT, and that contrast is null on everything except diastolic blood pressure (favouring HIIT) and perceived exertion (favouring snacks). Everything else is a before-and-after in a young student cohort across an eight-week window that also contained semester schedules, seasonal change, and the ordinary behavioural effect of being enrolled in a study about body weight.

Two of the instruments make it worse rather than better.

  • VO₂max was estimated from a 12-minute Cooper run, administered twice. People get faster at a self-paced field test by learning to pace it, independent of any change in aerobic capacity. A reported rise from 29.85 to 35.85 mL/kg/min — about 20% in eight weeks — is larger than most supervised laboratory-measured training studies produce, which is itself the tell.
  • Body composition was measured by bioelectrical impedance, which infers lean mass from body water and is the weakest instrument on the gradient fat-free-mass documents in fat-free-mass-measurement-method-gradient.

Why the vault records this as its own concept rather than a caveat inside another one. The pattern — an active-versus-active comparison whose within-group changes get read as evidence for the intervention — is one this vault meets repeatedly and now has a standing rule about. multidomain-lifestyle states it as "the less the control arm got, the bigger the effect looked" (latam-fingers-cognitive-benefit-is-differential-improvement-not-prevented-decline), and daily-steps holds the mirror case where a null turned out to be partly an artefact of active comparators (walking-cognition-null-is-partly-against-active-comparators). This trial is the limiting case of the same axis: the control arm got the full intervention in a different shape, so there is no zero anywhere in the design.

What the trial can and cannot support, stated cleanly.

  • Can: exercise snacks and HIIT were not distinguishable on these outcomes at this sample size (exercise-snacks-versus-hiit-null-is-fifteen-people-per-arm), and snacks felt easier (exercise-snacks-feel-easier-than-matched-duration-hiit).
  • Cannot: that either format improves body composition, fitness, blood pressure or lipids relative to doing nothing. That claim requires an arm that did nothing, and there wasn't one.
