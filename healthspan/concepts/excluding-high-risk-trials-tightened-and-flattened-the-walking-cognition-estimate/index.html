When the biased trials came out, the effect got smaller and the noise went with it

The pooled walking-versus-control estimate for attention and executive function was SMD 0.12 (95% CI −0.16 to 0.41) across five trials, with I² = 83% — a small positive point estimate buried in enormous disagreement between trials. Removing the two trials that RoB 2 rated at high risk of bias produced SMD 0.04 (−0.09 to 0.17) with I² = 29%. The estimate moved toward zero and became roughly twice as precise at the same time.

Why that combination is the informative part. There are two competing stories about a small null. The first is there is a real effect and this literature is too weak to see it — in which case removing the weakest trials should leave the effect intact or larger, on fewer but better studies. The second is the apparent effect is being generated by the weakest trials — in which case removing them should shrink it. This paper shows the second pattern. The signal and the heterogeneity were being supplied by the same two studies, and when they left, both left together.

I² in plain terms. It is the share of the variation between trial results that is not explained by chance. At 83%, five trials of the same intervention are producing answers that genuinely disagree, and any single summary number is misleading. At 29%, they are broadly telling the same story — and the story they tell is 0.04, which is nothing.

What this does and does not license. It does not turn the null into a demonstration of equivalence: the remaining three trials are still few, there is no power analysis and no minimal important difference in the paper, and 0.04 with an upper bound of 0.17 excludes only effects larger than small. What it does is remove the standard escape hatch — the trials were just too messy to detect it — because the mess and the effect were the same thing.

The vault has repeatedly seen the mirror image of this, in literatures where an effect survives only in the open-label, self-reported, high-risk-of-bias cells and evaporates wherever measurement improves (bayesian-certainty-cannot-repair-biased-inputs, exercise-sleep-certainty-very-low-at-200-trials). This is the same diagnostic run forward instead of backward, and it is worth keeping as a reading habit: when a sensitivity analysis is reported, look at whether the effect shrank or held, not just at whether the conclusion changed.
