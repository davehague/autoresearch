A meta-analysis that named the smallest gain worth having, then reported the probability of having reached it

Sixteen randomised trials, 35 effect sizes, HIIT against non-exercise control: VO₂max +2.61 mL·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹ (95% credible interval 1.68–3.51), probability of direction = 1.00, GRADE moderate. ⚑ And the number this note exists for: a 99.96% posterior probability that the true effect exceeds 1.0 mL·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹, a threshold the authors specified in advance as the bar for clinical relevance.

Why the method is the finding. shared/study-appraisal.md carries "statistical vs clinical significance" as a Universal lens, with the failure mode "significant only because N is huge; effect trivial." This vault has fired that lens repeatedly — a bone-density network that ranked sites where nothing differed, an abstract that upgraded its own GRADE rating, a leaderboard published with the effect sizes removed. Zhang 2026 answers the lens by construction: it decides what would count as meaningful before looking, and then reports how confident it is of having got there. ⚑ Recorded as a reporting standard worth crediting and looking for, not as a threshold adopted from this source.

⚠ The population is why nothing moves on the map. zone-2-cardio's live question is HIIT versus moderate continuous training in healthy people; this study's comparator is no exercise at all, in cancer patients whose cardiorespiratory fitness has usually been driven down by treatment. Gains are largest where baseline is lowest. The map's existing position — hiit-beats-mict-vo2max-elderly, on surrogates — is untouched.

⚠ And the biggest limitation is one this vault rarely gets to state. The full text is paywalled, including the priors. A Bayesian posterior is a function of its priors, and a 99.96% probability is a number whose provenance cannot be inspected. No prospective registration is retrievable either. Every figure here comes from a structured abstract.

What the threshold is worth outside this paper. zone-2-cardio rests on cardiorespiratory-fitness-all-cause-mortality — a 122,007-person cohort where mortality falls with fitness and shows no ceiling. The reason a 1 mL·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹ bar is defensible at all is that literature, not this one; and that literature is about fitness people had, not fitness a programme produced in a deconditioned patient.
