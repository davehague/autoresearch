A 13-person cell reports the lean-mass gain that 37 randomised trials could not find without training

In a 12-week trial of 10 g/day creatine monohydrate in adults aged 45–65, participants who took creatine while doing no exercise are reported to have gained fat-free mass relative to placebo. The supporting statistic is a univariate group × time interaction on fat-free mass at p = 0.016, ηp² = 0.123, followed by a pairwise claim generated under Fisher's least significant difference with no across-outcome multiplicity adjustment. The non-exercise creatine group contained 13 completers. No magnitude for the gain appears in the article text.

This contradicts the vault's best-evidenced clause about creatine, and it loses. creatine-lean-mass-requires-resistance-training pools 37 RCTs at GRADE moderate-to-high and finds that without resistance training creatine moves fat-free mass by −0.89 kg (p = 0.566) and lean body mass by −0.54 kg (p = 0.39) — nulls whose point estimates run the opposite way to this finding. Per shared/evidence-model.md, Status is set by the strongest evidence rather than by vote-counting, and an unreliable source is capped at single-study, which cannot move a consolidating map. The map's clause — creatine is a resistance-training amplifier, not a substitute for the training — stands unchanged.

Why it is recorded at all. A reader arriving at the Gu note directly should not see a settled finding when something has pushed against it, however weakly. That is the whole purpose of the back-edge rule, and the honest version of the rule includes rebuttals that fail. The correct summary is: one small, uncorrected, sponsor-supplied trial in the right age bracket reports the opposite of what the pooled randomised evidence reports, and the pooled evidence wins on every axis that matters — tier, sample, correction and independence.

What would actually change the answer: a randomised, adequately powered, multiplicity-corrected trial of creatine versus placebo in untrained middle-aged adults, with the lean-mass change reported as a quantity. This is not that trial, and it is the closest one the vault holds.
