One bone marker moved, at one timepoint, at p = 0.0474 — this is what chance looks like

Potassium citrate reduced urinary NTX (a bone-resorption marker) at 1 month: MD −4.05, 95% CI −8.05 to −0.05, p = 0.0474, I² = 0%. That is the entire positive finding of the vault's potassium-citrate source.

Count the comparisons before believing the p-value. The same synthesis tested NTX, CTX at 3 and 6 months, P1NP at 3, 6 and 12 months, urinary potassium at 3 and 6 months, urinary calcium, serum calcium, parathyroid hormone, and urinary pH — roughly a dozen marker × timepoint tests, with no correction for multiplicity. Exactly one came in under 0.05, and it came in at 0.0474, with a confidence interval whose upper bound (−0.05) is within a rounding error of zero. Under the null hypothesis, a dozen uncorrected tests producing one marginal hit is not a surprise — it is the expected yield. Per shared/study-appraisal.md, this is the multiple-comparisons lens firing at full strength.

The one thing the paper unambiguously demonstrates is adherence: urinary potassium excretion rose at 3 and 6 months. The participants took the supplement. Whether it did anything to their skeleton is not established here.

And it is a surrogate of a surrogate. Bone turnover markers are not bone mineral density, and BMD is not fracture. The chain from "one resorption marker fell for a month" to "this person will not break a hip" has three unvalidated links in it, and this paper tests none of them.

Graded single-study / open-question, and the vault gives it no more. The source's mixed verdict caps strength at suggestive; this note is graded lower because the claim itself is a borderline result in a multiplicity thicket. Notably, the paper's own authors decline to overclaim it: "The clinical relevance of this finding remains uncertain." Where the vault's recent sources have inflated their weak results (rt-intensity-safety-comparison-underpowered, rt-non-failure-nulls-hypertrophy-endurance-power-underpowered, exercise-type-cognition-ranking-not-supported), this one deflates its own (potassium-citrate-bone-nulls-honestly-underpowered).

Population: postmenopausal women. A neutral fact, and a distant one from the vault's owner (GOAL.md: middle-aged male). Population match is computed at regimen time, and it will not survive the computation. No dose is transcribed (shared/safety.md).
