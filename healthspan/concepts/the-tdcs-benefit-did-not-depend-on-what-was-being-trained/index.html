A stimulation effect that does not care what you were practising is harder to explain than one that does

Transcranial direct current stimulation is sold on a specific story: run a current through the prefrontal cortex while it is working on a memory task, and the concurrent activity is what gets strengthened. That story makes two testable predictions — the benefit should be bigger when the training is working-memory training, and bigger when the current runs during training rather than before it.

Neither held. Training type: p = 0.990, with point estimates of 0.182 for working-memory training and 0.184 for everything else — effectively identical. Stimulation timing: p = 0.559. Age group: p = 0.705. The authors state the implication plainly: "the additional effect of active tDCS did not appear to depend specifically on working memory training."

⚑ Why this matters more than the headline effect size. A small benefit that appears regardless of what is being trained is compatible with several explanations that are not the advertised one — a general arousal or alertness effect, a residual sensation the sham did not fully mask, or a non-specific practice enhancement. It is not the mechanism that justifies pairing the device with a particular training programme, which is what the category is marketed on.

⚠ And here is the discipline this vault applies to itself: these moderator tests could not have succeeded. Only 2 studies used non-working-memory training and only 5 used non-online stimulation. A comparison with two studies in a cell is not a test that failed to find a difference; it is a test with almost no chance of finding one — exactly the failure mode recorded elsewhere in the vault where an interaction test was found to carry 5% power (the-interaction-test-licensing-glp1-extrapolation-had-five-percent-power). So this is a failure to find the expected pattern, not a demonstration of its absence, and the vault records it at that strength.

⚑ What survives either way. Substantial residual heterogeneity remained after every moderator was fitted (Q(134) = 572.37, p < 0.001; effect-size-level τ² barely moved from 0.318 by any of them). Something real is driving variation between these studies, and none of the five variables tested — outcome type, time point, training type, stimulation timing, age — is it. The honest position is that nobody currently knows who this works for or why.

⚠ What it does not establish. Nothing here shows the advertised mechanism is wrong; it shows this evidence base cannot check it. A properly powered dose- and montage-varying trial would settle it and does not exist in this vault. Capped suggestive.
