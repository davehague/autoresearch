Sixth logging of the pattern, fourth on this map: the therapy did not change, the zero did

On 2026-08-05 this map recorded Barroso 2026's demonstration of comparator strength inside a single paper: acceptance and commitment therapy scored SMD −0.67 against a waitlist and 0.21 against CBT-I (act-improves-insomnia-severity-against-inactive-controls, act-versus-cbt-i-difference-is-unresolved-not-equivalent). The vault's note at the time: "The therapy did not change; the zero did."

Reesen 2026's control condition is keeping a sleep diary. Participants monitor their own sleep and receive no therapeutic content, no sleep-restriction protocol, no stimulus control, no cognitive work. It is close to the inactive end of the range Barroso mapped — and against it, guided digital CBT-I scores d = −1.04.

Both facts about CBT-I are true and they belong together:

ComparatorEstimateSource
Sleep-diary controld = −1.04 post-treatment, −0.98 at 8 monthsReesen 2026
Waitlist / standard control (for ACT, the rival)SMD −0.67Barroso 2026
Active rival therapy (ACT)remission RR 0.81 (0.67–0.98); mean-difference contrast nullBarroso 2026

Against nothing, CBT-I is a large effect. Against the best-studied alternative psychotherapy, its advantage is a risk ratio whose upper bound is 0.98 and a mean-difference contrast that does not separate at all.

Why this is not a criticism of the trial. A sleep-diary control is the standard and defensible comparator for a first efficacy question in an under-studied population, and diary-keeping is not nothing — self-monitoring has a known small effect. The point is about how the number transfers, not about whether it was earned.

Four of the six are on sleep or cognition maps, where the outcome is self-reported and blinding is impossible — which is where the pattern should be expected to concentrate.

The operating rule this map now carries. Its headline magnitudes — the exercise-plus-CBT PSQI −2.80, Öst's within-group remission rates, and now Reesen's d ≈ −1.0 — were each measured against controls of unstated or minimal strength. The direction and durability stand. The magnitudes are comparator-relative, and the only number in the map measured against a live rival is much smaller than any of them.
