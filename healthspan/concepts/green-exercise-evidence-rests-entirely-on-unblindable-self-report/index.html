Twenty-six randomised trials of green exercise, and not one pooled objective outcome

The green-exercise literature synthesised by Wang 2026 measures itself exclusively by questionnaire: POMS in 8 of 26 trials, plus PANAS, WEMWBS, WHO-5, WHOQOL, SRI and adjective checklists. No physiological or behavioural endpoint is pooled — no cortisol, no heart-rate variability, no actigraphy, no adherence count — although the paper's Discussion invokes cortisol, sympathetic activation and parasympathetic tone as mechanisms. The exposure is unmeasured too: green settings were classified from what each trial reported, with no NDVI or vegetation-coverage index anywhere in the corpus (the authors list this as a limitation).

The design cannot be blinded, and the comparison is the one participants can see most easily. Nobody is unaware of whether they are in a forest or a gym. The largest effect in the paper is the green-versus-indoor contrast (SMD 0.79) — the comparison with the greatest perceptual contrast and therefore the greatest expectancy load. In a corpus where arms run as small as 5 vs 5, that combination is where inflated effects are known to come from.

Why this is recorded as a finding rather than left in an appraisal block. The vault has now measured the size of this discount from its own sources, in two independent literatures: loneliness's health association halved on objective versus subjective measurement (r = −.23 → −.12, p < .0001, loneliness-health-association-halves-on-objective-measures); and cold-water immersion was null on every objective outcome while its subjective benefits failed the authors' own bias tests (cold-water-immersion-does-not-speed-strength-recovery, cold-water-immersion-damage-marker-benefit-fails-bias-adjustment). Add the three exercise-and-sleep meta-analyses that contradicted each other, where each paper's strongest result was the one its design was weakest at measuring (walking-subjective-sleep-quality-unresolved). This is now a standing expectation, not a one-off caution — and it is the reason the green-exercise magnitude is held at suggestive and no map is coined.

What would resolve it. A trial of the same exercise in green versus built settings with a pre-registered objective co-primary — salivary cortisol, HRV, actigraphic sleep, or best of all measured adherence over months, since adherence is the mechanism through which a mood benefit would actually reach a health outcome. Until then the honest statement is: green exercise reliably makes people report feeling better, and nothing in this corpus establishes that anything else changes.
