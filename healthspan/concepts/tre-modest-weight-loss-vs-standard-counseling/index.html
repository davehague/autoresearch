Time-restricted eating produces a real, modest, randomized weight loss — the map's first dedicated TRE trial

Until tonight intermittent-fasting rested entirely on one network meta-analysis of surrogate markers. TREAD is its first dedicated randomized trial of time-restricted eating with a hard body-composition endpoint, and the endpoint moved.

Twelve weeks, adults with obesity and a habitually long (≥14 h) eating window, randomized to a 10-hour eating window or to standard-of-care dietary counseling:

  • TRE: −4.59 kg (−4.6%)
  • Standard care: −1.68 kg (−1.7%)
  • Between-group: −2.91 kg (−4.21 to −1.60), p < 0.001, primarily body fat
  • No adverse events

Three things make this better than most TRE trials. The primary endpoint was prespecified and powered (target n=62, 80% power). The outcome is objective — you cannot talk yourself into weighing less, which is the failure mode that wrecks the vault's wind-down supplement literature. And adherence was measured rather than assumed: the TRE arm genuinely compressed its window (14.5 h → 10.2 h) while controls did not (14.8 h). A great many fasting trials never show you that.

And it lands well against GOAL.md. The philosophy is least work, least discomfort, adherence-first — and TRE is one rule, no counting, no forbidden foods, which is about as low-friction as a dietary intervention gets. The map already called TRE the lowest-burden fasting variant on theoretical grounds; this is the first randomized evidence that the low-burden variant does something on its own.

Two hard limits, recorded rather than smoothed over.

(1) No intention-to-treat analysis. 61 randomized → 52 completed, with n drifting by timepoint and no imputation strategy described. In a behavioral weight-loss trial the dropouts are, by construction, the people who could not sustain the behavior — the people with the worst results. So this estimate answers "what happens to people who stick to a 10-hour window", not "what happens if you try one." It is therefore graded single-study, below the source's suggestive cap.

(2) The population is not David's. Every participant had obesity (BMI 30–50) and a habitually ≥14-hour eating window — a lot of fat to lose and an unusually long window to compress. Whatever mechanism produced −2.91 kg ran through those two facts, and there is nothing in this trial about a lean-ish adult who already eats within a normal span. Recorded as a neutral fact per shared/evidence-model.md; population-match is computed at regimen time.
