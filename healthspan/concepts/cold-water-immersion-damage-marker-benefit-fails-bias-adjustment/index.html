The two findings that would sell a plunge tub are the two that do not survive the authors' own bias tests

Cold-water immersion appeared to reduce creatine kinase — the muscle-damage marker — at g = −0.43 (95% CI −0.67 to −0.19, p<0.01). Egger's test flagged publication bias at p<0.001, and after Duval–Tweedie trim-and-fill the effect collapsed to g = −0.160 (−0.443 to 0.124), non-significant. Subjective soreness fell at g = −0.58 (−0.99 to −0.16), also with Egger's p<0.001; trim-and-fill imputed no studies and left the estimate intact, but the asymmetry traced to a single extreme outlier (removing it gave Egger's p=0.471), heterogeneity was substantial, and GRADE certainty was rated VERY LOW — specifically because soreness is participant-reported in an intervention nobody can be blinded to. A dependency-adjusted cluster-robust analysis preserved the direction of both effects and attenuated both to non-significance.

The distribution of trustworthiness across this dataset is the finding. The objective outcomes are null or negative — strength recovery flat with no detected bias (cold-water-immersion-does-not-speed-strength-recovery), explosive power transiently impaired (cold-water-immersion-transiently-impairs-explosive-power). The biochemical outcome vanishes under bias correction. The only survivor is the subjective one, in an unblindable paradigm, rated very low certainty. That ordering — objective null, subjective positive — is what an expectancy effect looks like, and the authors say as much.

Credit where it is due, because it is rare. This meta-analysis ran the bias tests, applied trim-and-fill, added a cluster-robust sensitivity analysis, and reported that its own positive results did not hold. Most recovery-literature syntheses do not. The finding is unflattering to cold plunging and flattering to the paper.

What it changes. It does not retract cold-water-immersion-stress-sleep-wellbeing — different outcomes, different timescale, a different source. It does show, inside a single dataset, that the subjective endpoint was the least reliable thing measured in exactly the expectancy-prone design the map's founding source also used. Hold the wellbeing claims more loosely. Capped suggestive; Status held emerging on cold-water-immersion.
