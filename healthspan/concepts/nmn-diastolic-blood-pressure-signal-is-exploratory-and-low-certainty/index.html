The one NMN outcome that reached significance is a surrogate the vault has specific grounds to decline

Among a dozen-plus pooled outcomes in the first meta-analysis of oral nicotinamide mononucleotide safety and metabolic effects, exactly one was statistically significant: diastolic blood pressure fell 2.43 mmHg (95% CI −4.21 to −0.66), p = 0.007, with I² = 0% across 11 comparisons and 339 people.

It is not a fragile p-value, and that is worth conceding before declining it. The estimate held in every leave-one-out re-analysis — dropping each study or comparison in turn produced pooled effects from −2.75 to −2.02 mmHg with every interval still below zero, including the most conservative exclusion. Heterogeneity was zero. The direction was consistent in the subgroups where a vascular effect would be most plausible: people aged ≥60 (−3.18 mmHg, −6.19 to −0.16) and people with metabolic risk (−3.61 mmHg, −6.74 to −0.47). This is not a one-study artefact.

Four things nonetheless hold it at single-study and open-question.

One — its own authors grade it low certainty and call it hypothesis-generating, in both the Results and the Discussion. GRADE downgrades for risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias; the best-graded outcomes in this review were the nulls (moderate certainty for body weight, BMI, fasting glucose, triglycerides), and this positive is graded below them.

Two — it was not a primary endpoint in most of the trials that supplied it. The review tested more than a dozen outcomes with no family-wise correction across them, and this is the one that came out. That is the textbook setup for a chance finding, and its robustness to leave-one-out does not address it: leave-one-out tests whether one study drives a result, not whether one of many outcomes was going to win.

Three — the systolic result in the same people is null (−0.89 mmHg, −3.83 to 2.05). A real vascular effect on blood pressure would not usually be confined to the diastolic number. And the subgroup pattern the authors lean on comes with their own concession that "most formal tests for subgroup differences were not statistically significant" — which is precisely the definition of a subgroup pattern that has not been demonstrated.

Four — and this is the specific reason rather than the generic one — this map's entire history is surrogates that did not translate. nad-precursors holds a trial in which blood NAD⁺ doubled, confirming the compound arrived and did its pharmacological job, and cognition, total cerebral blood flow and blood pressure all stayed put (nicotinamide-riboside-raises-nad-without-cognitive-benefit). It holds a methylation module that looked modifiable in a computer model while the human trials were already null (nad-methylation-module-modifiable-in-silico-only). A vault that declined a doubling of the actual target molecule cannot then bank a 2.4 mmHg move in a secondary endpoint. That is not caution; it is consistency.

What would settle it: the trial the authors themselves ask for — adequately powered, in older or metabolically-at-risk adults, with blood pressure pre-specified as the primary endpoint. Until then this is a signal, not a finding.

⚑ And the comparison that puts 2.43 mmHg in perspective. If lower blood pressure is the reason someone would buy this, the vault already holds randomised evidence for cheaper, better-graded routes to the same endpoint — resistance-training-lowers-blood-pressure-older-adults and longitudinal-stretching-lowers-arterial-stiffness — neither of which requires a supplement slot, and one of which the vault has separately noted produces a larger blood-pressure effect than its evidence base deserves (stretching-blood-pressure-effect-is-larger-than-the-better-evidenced-levers). The same argument this map already makes for muscle, arriving in a second outcome family.

Why it is recorded at all rather than dismissed. It is the reason nad-precursors holds contested rather than dropping to fading after a third outcome-family null (nad-precursors-null-on-metabolic-outcomes). The vault's fading bar is superseded, debunked, or gone quiet, and a compound with a leave-one-out-stable blood-pressure signal — however poorly graded — is not quiet. This note is the thread the map is hanging on, stated honestly, at the strength it deserves.
