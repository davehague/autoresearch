Fasted cardio's surrogate moved as advertised; the outcome moved twenty-four times further the other way

Höchsmann 2026 measured, in the same people and the same sessions, both the marker fasted training is prescribed on and the behaviour it is supposed to serve. Fat oxidation was greater in fasted and evening conditions — 12h evening exercise exceeded 12h morning exercise by 0.77 kcal/min (0.37–1.17, p < 0.001). Across the 30-minute bout that is approximately 23 kcal of additional fat oxidised (vault-derived arithmetic from the paper's reported rate and bout duration, not a figure the paper states). The accompanying 24-hour energy intake difference was 547 kcal (335–759).

The ratio is about 1 to 24, and it points the wrong way.

The second half of the dissociation is what makes it a mechanism finding rather than a coincidence. Pre-exercise feeding suppressed fat oxidation — the marker responded exactly as the physiology predicts — and did not attenuate energy intake or appetite at all. So the marker can be moved up or down at will, in either direction, without the behaviour following. The authors call this a dissociation between metabolic and behavioural regulation; the vault calls it a surrogate that does not govern its outcome.

This is the vault's fourth verified surrogate-versus-outcome dissociation this month, and the first from a behaviour rather than a molecule. Creatine loads the brain and buys no cognition (creatine-20g-raises-brain-creatine). Nicotinamide riboside doubles blood NAD⁺ and buys nothing (nicotinamide-riboside-raises-nad-without-cognitive-benefit). DHA reaches the cerebrospinal fluid, independent of APOE ε4, and two years later cognition and brain volumes are unchanged (dha-brain-delivery-without-cognitive-or-structural-benefit). Fasted cardio raises fat oxidation, and more food arrives. The standing expectation the vault takes from the series: a verified mechanism is evidence that the intervention does something — never that the something is what you wanted.

Read at its true strength. mixed, capped suggestive. The 23-kcal figure is the vault's own multiplication of a reported rate by a reported duration and is labelled as such. No non-exercise control arm is described, so the 547 kcal is not cleanly attributable to exercise (evening-fasted-exercise-followed-by-higher-24h-energy-intake). One acute bout, one modality, a 24-hour horizon, no weight or body-composition outcome, 35 healthy young adults (17 for the fed conditions). Substrate oxidation itself was measured well — indirect calorimetry — which is precisely why the comparison is informative.

What it does not establish. Not that fat oxidation during exercise is meaningless; it is a real physiological quantity and this trial measured it properly. Only that it is far too small to be the thing you optimise, and that it can be manipulated in both directions without the intake following.

For David: the practical version is one sentence — fasted cardio is not a fat-loss strategy on the strength of its fat-oxidation number. Nothing to buy, nothing to stop, and one fewer reason to schedule training around a fasting window. GOAL.md's knee constraint rules out this trial's modality anyway.
