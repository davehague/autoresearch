Two-thirds of this network is low or very low certainty, and the abstract carries the other third

The authors graded every estimate in their network with GRADE — the standard framework for rating how much confidence a body of evidence deserves, running high / moderate / low / very low, and downgraded for things like risk of bias, imprecision, inconsistency and publication bias. Their own distribution across 359 estimates:

CertaintyEstimates
High29
Moderate90
Low135
Very low105

Two hundred and forty of 359 — sixty-seven percent — are low or very low. The abstract reports the high- and moderate-certainty results and describes them as favourable estimates. Nothing is concealed: the full table is in the paper, and the discussion says findings "based on sparse networks, indirect evidence, or moderate-to-low certainty should be interpreted cautiously."

Why the vault records this as a finding rather than a complaint. This is the fourth time in three weeks it has met the same shape — a scrupulous paper whose travelling sentence is stronger than its own certainty table:

  • Kong 2026 reported a 99.97% posterior probability for walking improving sleep, and rated that finding GRADE very low (bayesian-certainty-cannot-repair-biased-inputs).
  • Liang 2026 pooled 200 trials and 23,523 people on exercise and sleep and rated the certainty very low (exercise-sleep-certainty-very-low-at-200-trials).
  • Crivelli 2026 produced a headline of "55% greater improvement" for a between-group difference of 0.11 standard deviations per year with no clinical anchor (latam-fingers-cognitive-benefit-is-differential-improvement-not-prevented-decline).
  • And here, 67% of a network is graded low or very low while its abstract carries the 33%.

The habit this generates: read the certainty table before the abstract. In none of these four cases did an author hide anything. The inflation happens downstream, in the reading — a percentage travels and a GRADE rating does not. Scale does not launder design, and neither does registration, and neither does a formal certainty framework if nobody reads its output.

What it means for this specific network. The estimates the vault carries from it (exercise-improves-cognition-in-mci-against-wait-list-controls) are held at suggestive partly on this basis. It does not make the positive results false; it makes them provisional in exactly the way the authors say they are.
