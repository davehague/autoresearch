The trial's other two endpoints were flat — including in the arm containing an hour of cycling

In the same n = 16 randomized crossover, postprandial triglycerides (p = 0.112) and postprandial glucose (p = 0.150) did not differ across any of the three conditions — prolonged sitting, sitting + a 60-min moderate cycle bout, or standing breaks + that bout.

This is not a demonstrated absence, and the trial's own design says why. The nulls span the SIT vs FIT comparison too — meaning a full hour of moderate cycling also failed to shift postprandial triglycerides, an effect this literature generally reports. When a trial's positive control fails to register, its null arms are reporting sample size rather than biology. No power analysis for these endpoints is published. Per shared/study-appraisal.md, reading such a null as "no effect" is the fatal flag the vault refuses in both directions — the same discipline applied to rt-non-failure-nulls-hypertrophy-endurance-power-underpowered, rt-intensity-safety-comparison-underpowered and potassium-citrate-bone-nulls-honestly-underpowered.

What it does do is discipline the positive result. The trial's primary outcome family has three members. Two are null; one survives at p = 0.041 / p = 0.010, uncorrected (sitting-breaks-add-insulin-benefit-beyond-exercise). The honest summary of this trial is one marker of three moved, in sixteen people — not breaking up sitting improves cardiometabolic health, which is closer to how the conclusion reads.

To the authors' credit, the nulls are reported prominently in the abstract rather than buried, so this is not the selective-reporting pathology (shared/study-appraisal.md fatal flag #2). It is an underpowered trial being honest about being underpowered, and then drawing a conclusion slightly ahead of its evidence.

What would resolve it: an adequately powered trial of sitting interruption with a pre-specified single primary endpoint — ideally in adults without dyslipidaemia — and a horizon longer than a 6-hour postprandial window.
