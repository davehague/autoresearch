The entire supplement shelf moves the sleep questionnaire by two-thirds of a point — significant, and below the threshold that would mean anything

Pooling 28 RCTs of dietary supplement interventions, subjective sleep quality improved by PSQI MD −0.70 (95% CI −1.37 to −0.03). Objective outcomes moved in the same direction and by similarly small amounts: total sleep time SMD +0.23, sleep latency SMD −0.24, wake after sleep onset SMD −0.30; number of awakenings was not significant (MD −1.57, −3.16 to 0.02).

The PSQI number is the one that decides this. The scale runs 0–21, and the minimal important difference commonly cited for it is around 3 points. A pooled estimate of 0.70 whose upper bound is −0.03 describes an effect whose entire plausible range sits below the threshold at which a person would notice. This is the exact split shared/study-appraisal.md flags as statistical versus clinical significance — the result is real in the arithmetic sense and absent in the sense that matters.

The comparison that makes it concrete, on the same instrument. The vault holds PSQI effects for three other things:

The free behaviour beats the shelf by roughly four to one, at higher certainty, on the shelf's own instrument. On 2026-07-13 the vault estimated this gap at "roughly twice" from triangulation (exercise-plus-cbt-improves-sleep-in-insomnia); measured directly it is four times, and the vault had been under-stating it. The comparison is across different trials, populations and comparators, so it is an orientation, not a head-to-head — but the ordering is not close enough for that caveat to rescue the supplements.

What this does not do is single out any product. The pooled category spans a dozen unrelated substances, so this note describes the shelf, not magnesium, L-theanine, melatonin or chamomile individually (supplement-sleep-benefit-not-attributable-to-any-supplement). It is also built on trials of 7–50 people with I² = 67% (supplement-sleep-trials-are-small-and-heterogeneous), a configuration in which small-study inflation is the leading risk — meaning 0.70 is more likely an over-estimate than an under-estimate.

For David: an effect whose whole interval sits under its own clinical threshold does not justify a purchase, a nightly habit, or a third-party-testing investigation under GOAL.md's least-work standard. The wind-down cluster stays on the Watch list, unbought — now with a measured ceiling on the entire cluster rather than an inferred one.
