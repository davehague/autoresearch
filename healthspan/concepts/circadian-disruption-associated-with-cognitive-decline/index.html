Irregular body clocks track with cognitive decline — direction only, no magnitude, and the disease breaks the clock too

A PRISMA-guided systematic review (10 studies, n=5,731, age ≥ 60) found circadian dysregulation consistently associated with development or progression of MCI and dementia, across three exposure families: objective actigraphic rest-activity metrics, sleep quality and architecture, and rotating night-shift work. No pooled effect size is reported anywhere — this is a direction, not a number.

What it adds to sleep-regularity is instrument continuity, and that is the real value. The actigraphic metrics used here — interdaily stability, intradaily variability, amplitude, acrophase — are the same machine-measured family as Windred 2024's Sleep Regularity Index, which gives this map its 20–48% mortality spread and its lead over sleep-duration (sleep-regularity-predicts-mortality). The map's central exposure now carries an objectively-measured association with a second outcome domain. Kalkanis 2025 named the cognitive domain (sleep-regularity-multi-outcome-review); this is the first source built around it.

Three instruments converging is worth something. The vault credits sedentary-behavior precisely because its signal replicated across accelerometry and self-report. Here the convergence spans wrist actigraphy, sleep architecture, and occupational history — three quite different ways of being wrong, all pointing the same way.

The discount is large and it is structural. Neurodegeneration destroys the suprachiasmatic nucleus; circadian fragmentation is a recognised prodrome of the disease being predicted, and the review deliberately retains MCI-to-dementia progression studies. See circadian-cognition-association-confounded-by-reverse-causation. Add no pooled magnitude, no risk-of-bias tool, no GRADE, no registration, ten studies, and a population of elderly adults substantially with MCI — and this cannot move a map resting on objective accelerometry in ~61,000 adults.

Recorded at single-study because there is no effect size to record. Direction consistent, causation unestablished, magnitude absent. Status held emerging; nothing added to the regimen, nothing double-counted.
