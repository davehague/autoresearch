The greenspace–epigenetic-age effect lives in three small studies and vanishes in the one properly pooled analysis of 5,975 people

A systematic review of 14 studies concludes in its abstract that greenness is "consistently associated with a deceleration of GrimAge acceleration" worth 1.0–1.6 years per step up in vegetation. The same review's body reports that the largest and only meta-analysed evidence in the field found nothing.

The positive side, with its arithmetic made visible. Three studies: 116 people in North Carolina (−1.2 years per interquartile-range increase in tree cover within 500 m), 157 in Detroit (−0.59 years, 0.18–1.01), and 482 in the CARDIA cohort (−3.03 years per IQR — but only in White participants; −0.80, interval spanning zero, in Black participants). Roughly 755 people in total. The abstract's headline "1.0 to 1.6 years" is not a pooled estimate at all: it is the range of point estimates from the 116-person study.

⚑ The null side. The EXPANSE project meta-analysed 5,975 adults across seven European cohorts and found β = −0.01 (95% CI −0.08 to 0.06), p = 0.78 for vegetation index against GrimAge acceleration. That interval is narrow and centred on zero. It is not an inconclusive null waiting for more data — it rules out anything but a trivial effect, in eight times as many people as the positive studies combined, with proper cross-cohort pooling and air-pollution adjustment. Alongside it: null in placental tissue across 550 mother–child pairs; null on two gestational clocks; null on cord-blood Horvath age in ~300 pairs; and null on the first-generation Horvath and Hannum clocks in the very cohort where GrimAge moved.

This is the small-study-effects fingerprint, and this vault has learned to read it. The pattern — several small studies finding an effect, the large pooled analysis finding none — is the same magnitude-tell the ledger applied to a collagen bone-density result at an implausible effect size with I² = 80% (collagen-bone-signal-heterogeneous-postmenopausal) and to network rankings crowning the mechanistically least likely option (collagen-ranking-rests-on-indirect-evidence). When the biggest, best-pooled estimate is the flattest, the small positives are the thing that needs explaining.

⚑ And the clock that matters most was never looked at. biological-age-clocks holds evidence that of 14 expert-consensus ageing biomarkers raced against 316 real deaths in one cohort, nine predicted nothing, the whole panel reached a C-index of 0.65 — 65% correct ordering of a random pair against 50% for a coin flip — and DunedinPACE was the strongest and most consistent predictor (dunedinpace-strongest-mortality-predictor-among-consensus-biomarkers, most-consensus-aging-biomarkers-did-not-predict-mortality, aging-biomarker-panels-discriminate-mortality-weakly). The entire greenspace signal sits on GrimAge. Not one study has examined residential greenness against DunedinPACE. The single DunedinPACE study in the review looked at 99 firefighters exposed to vegetation fires — a different exposure entirely — and was null.

So this literature multiplies two soft measurements together: an exposure defined by satellite reflectance around a home address, and a surrogate the vault has independently graded as weakly predictive of the outcome anyone cares about. Neither is strong; the product is weaker than either.

⚑ The generalisation this note earns for biological-age-clocks, stated as a pattern across three unrelated fields. This is the third literature in which a rich instrument panel produced scattered small associations and nothing that survived scale — after 14 consensus ageing biomarkers reaching C-index 0.65, and 39 sleep variables reduced to 13 composites whose longitudinal signals did not survive multiple-comparison adjustment (sleep-variability-cognitive-decline-signal-does-not-survive-correction, sleep-cognition-associations-small-and-cross-sectional). The map's rule is predictor ≠ responder. The stronger version this supports: in every literature where this vault has watched a clock or a panel be scaled up, the effect shrank. Recorded as a reading discipline for the vault's own surrogate-outcome sources — including omega3-slows-epigenetic-aging, physical-activity-lowers-epigenetic-age-cross-sectional and epigenetic-clock-signal-rests-on-one-of-five-clocks — and not as evidence about greenspace, exercise, omega-3 or sleep. No Status moves anywhere.

What the vault does not claim. It does not claim greenness is irrelevant to health, or that the molecular findings are noise — differentially methylated regions converged independently on genes for stress response (NR3C1), immune regulation (IL6) and neurodevelopment (BDNF, HTR2A), which is a coherent biology. A coherent mechanism co-existing with a null in the largest cohort is exactly the position nad-precursors is in, and the vault's answer is the same in both places: the biology can be right and the humans can still show nothing.
