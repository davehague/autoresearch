Breaking up sitting lowered postprandial insulin beyond what an hour of cycling did — n=16, one marker of three

In a randomized crossover trial (n = 16, mean age 47.8, BMI 24.6, dyslipidaemia) run in free-living desk-based work environments, interrupting prolonged sitting with standing breaks 30 min/h on top of a 60-min moderate cycle bout (MIX) produced lower 6-hour postprandial insulin than prolonged sitting (p = 0.041) and — the comparison that carries the finding — than the same exercise bout without the breaks (FIT, p = 0.010).

Why the second contrast is the one that matters. FIT already contains the workout. If MIX beats FIT, the standing breaks did something the cycling did not. That is the first randomised support in this vault for the position sedentary-behavior has held on observational grounds since it was coined — that sitting and physical activity are two exposures, not one behaviour counted twice (sedentary-time-independent-of-exercise-mortality-risk) — and it is the reason the map is deliberately not folded into daily-steps.

It closes a gap the vault wrote down against itself. When sedentary-behavior was coined from an umbrella review (2026-07-10), the logged residual gap read: no source yet tests whether breaking up sitting — the actionable version — changes outcomes, as opposed to total sitting predicting them. This trial tests exactly that, prospectively registered (NCT06277713), with objectively measured exposure rather than the self-report that caps most of this map's evidence.

Read at its true strength — which is thin. Sixteen people. The primary outcome family has three members and two of them are null (sitting-breaks-null-on-glucose-and-triglycerides); the survivor is reported without correction for multiple comparisons and without a published effect magnitude. The endpoint is postprandial insulin over 6 hours — a surrogate several steps removed from the mortality association the map is actually built on — and the cohort has dyslipidaemia. Graded single-study on sample size despite the randomised design; appraised mixed.

For David: the cohort here is unusually close to him — mid-to-late forties, normal BMI, desk-based — and the practical content is that standing through the workday is not redundant with the workout. Free, knee-safe, zero-equipment. It changes no dose, no number, and no Status (emerging holds; a 16-person surrogate cannot promote a 2.8-million-person mortality map).
