The most quotable numbers in the domain paper are its least supported ones

The domain-specific dementia estimates are not equally evidenced, and the imbalance runs exactly opposite to how surprising each finding is.

DomainStudies (k)Effect sizesWhat it is used to claim
Leisure-time2033the familiar protective association
Occupational513activity at work raises dementia risk
Commuting24active commuting raises dementia risk
Household12housework lowers dementia risk

A pooled relative risk from one cohort is not a meta-analytic finding. The household estimate — RR 0.85 (0.74–0.98) — comes from a single study contributing two effect sizes. Commuting comes from two. The authors say so directly ("findings for household and commuting physical activity were limited to a total of three studies") and decline to run dose-response analyses for either on the grounds that there is nothing to fit a curve to.

⚑ The authors' own GRADE rating is very low to low certainty across every domain, driven by heterogeneity and study count. In GRADE's vocabulary that means the true effect may be substantially different from the estimate — not "probably right, with error bars." Anyone quoting the 1.20 or the 1.10 without that rating attached is quoting a number the paper does not stand behind at that strength.

Other structural limits on the base. 47% of the 74 studies scored low quality on the paper's own instrument; only 40 reached moderate or high. 93% were conducted in high-income countries, where leisure-time activity is roughly 28% of total movement — against 4% in low-income countries. So the domain with the most studies is also the domain that is largest in the settings that were studied, and the harmful domains are those that dominate movement where nobody looked. The paper makes this its secondary finding, mapping the mismatch between where the research exists and where dementia burden is projected to grow fastest.

⚑ What this note is for. The sign-flip finding (physical-activity-domain-flips-the-sign-of-the-dementia-association) is structurally important and survived every sensitivity analysis its authors ran. It is also, numerically, a claim about five studies. Both things are true, and the vault's failure mode is to keep the first sentence and lose the second. Recorded so that a later run reads the domain result as a research agenda with one well-evidenced cell, not as four estimates of equal standing.
