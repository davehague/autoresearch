The altitude benefit shrinks against the comparator that isolates the altitude

A three-level meta-analysis of 14 RCTs (n = 534) found hypoxic HIIT improved cardiorespiratory fitness (g = 0.369, 0.186–0.552) and relative oxygen uptake (g = 0.553, 0.308–0.797) — with the finding that decides how to read it: effects were larger versus non-exercise controls than versus exercise controls.

That subgroup is the whole question. A trial of hypoxic intervals against sitting still measures HIIT. Only the contrast against normoxic exercise measures hypoxia. When the effect is bigger in the first and smaller in the second, most of the headline belongs to the intervals — which the vault already holds from hiit-beats-mict-vo2max-elderly — and the thin-air part is doing much less than the name implies.

Fifth instance of the bundled-arm attribution failure. The vault has now caught this shape five times: creatine + beta-alanine (creatine-beta-alanine-no-synergy), protein + probiotic (protein-probiotic-headline-confounded-by-comparator-pooling), exercise + CBT (cbt-sleep-effect-not-isolable-no-cbt-alone-node), unstable-surface + resistance training (unstable-vs-stable-rt-strength-mobility-null-underpowered), and now hypoxia + HIIT. Trialists bundle, and bundling destroys attribution. To Gao's credit — unlike Chang 2026's probiotic headline — the authors built the subgroup that undercuts their own conclusion and reported it.

Read at its true strength. 14 trials, ~38 participants each, hypoxic dose not standardised across altitude/duration/simulated-vs-real, substantial heterogeneity acknowledged by the authors, certainty low-to-moderate, endpoints all surrogate, population young adults by the authors' own evidence- distribution assessment. Graded suggestive, appraised mixed.

⚑ Candidate intervention flagged, map NOT coined (spec §6.5). No independent hypoxic-training literature exists in this vault; the isolating contrast is where the effect shrinks; the mechanism it is sold on came back null (hypoxic-hiit-null-on-body-composition-cardiometabolic); and it fails GOAL.md on burden — altitude tents and chambers are expensive, equipment-dependent, and HIIT-based, on the wrong side of the knee constraint. Fifth candidate refusal in nine days, after beta-alanine, probiotics, collagen-muscle and collagen-bone.

For David: nothing to do. The transferable content is that the intervals are the intervention and the environment is decoration — a pattern now visible across three maps (sauna-heat null on most cardiometabolic markers, cold-water-immersion actively blunting the adaptation it is layered on).
