Subcutaneous semaglutide is the only weight-loss drug linked to lower mortality — on events largely supplied by SELECT

In a network meta-analysis of 19 drugs, subcutaneous semaglutide was the only agent associated with reduced all-cause mortality — RR 0.81 (95% CI 0.72–0.93) — and with reduced myocardial infarction, RR 0.72 (0.61–0.85). It and tirzepatide both reduced heart failure (RR 0.43 and 0.49). The authors immediately qualify the headline: these estimates are "largely informed by cardiovascular outcome trials in high risk populations."

Why this matters for the vault. Read carelessly, this looks like the independent confirmation that would lift glp-1 from consolidating toward consensus. It is not, and the reason is worth stating precisely. The cardiovascular outcome trials supplying those events are SELECT and its peers — the trial the vault already holds as select-semaglutide-cardiovascular-lincoff-2023. SELECT's pre-specified hierarchical testing chain broke at CV death (p=0.07), leaving all-cause mortality nominal only (glp1-mace-benefit-rct-select). A network meta-analysis that re-pools the same deaths, in the same patients, cannot repair that broken hierarchy — it re-presents those events inside a different statistical frame, one with no multiplicity penalty carried over.

So the correct update is precision, not independence. shared/evidence-model.md requires ≥2 independent groups for consolidating and a higher bar again for consensus; this synthesis adds neither a new group nor a new population. The map's Status is unchanged. This is the same discipline the vault applied to urolithin-a, where a beautifully designed RCT changed nothing because it shared a sponsor with every prior trial: design quality and pooled precision cannot substitute for independence.

Bounded honestly (why suggestive, despite a reliable source). The source's reliability verdict permits strong, and the paper's methods deserve it. This concept does not, for two evidentiary reasons. (1) Indirectness: the events come from established-cardiovascular-disease populations, not from metabolically healthy adults — the vault's standing primary-prevention gap, and the population closest to David. (2) Non-independence: the estimate is not a second look at the question, it is a second presentation of the first look.

The practical shape of the claim. safety_flags: [prescription] — never ranked, no dose set (shared/safety.md). What it adds to the clinician conversation is a comparative fact the vault could not state before: among nineteen drugs that all reduce weight, exactly one has an association with living longer, and it is not the one that removes the most weight (tirzepatide-largest-fat-and-lean-mass-loss).
