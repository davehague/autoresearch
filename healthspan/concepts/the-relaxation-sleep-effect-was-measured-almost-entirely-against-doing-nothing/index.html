Twelve of fourteen trials asked "is doing this better than doing nothing?" — and then asked the participants

The setup, laid out. The intervention is thirty minutes of guided muscle tensing and releasing, sometimes twice daily. Nobody can be blinded to receiving it. The comparator was routine care in six trials and literally no intervention in six more — twelve of fourteen. The outcome is the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, a self-report questionnaire. ROB2 flagged participant blinding in 5 trials and assessor blinding in 7. And the meta-analysis pooled post-intervention scores, not change from baseline, so any group imbalance at randomisation feeds straight into the effect.

⚑ Every ingredient of a non-specific effect is present at once. Attention from a researcher; a structured ritual; an obvious group assignment; a subjective outcome; and no active comparator to absorb any of it. The two trials that did use something active are instructive: one compared against "light activity" and one against standard pulmonary rehabilitation — and neither is reported separately.

⚑ This is the seventh time this vault has met this problem, and it is the cleanest case. The prior six each involve one weak comparator among several: cbt-i-large-effect-was-measured-against-a-sleep-diary-control, walking-cognition-null-is-partly-against-active-comparators, traditional-chinese-sports-ranking-is-unseparable-from-its-comparator, exergaming-fall-benefit-tracks-the-weakness-of-the-comparator, cogtrail-executive-benefit-rests-on-a-waiting-list-comparator, and Barroso 2026. ⚑ Here it is nearly the whole evidence base, and it concentrates exactly where the vault predicted it would — on a sleep or cognition outcome that is self-reported and where blinding is impossible.

⚠ A lens asking "how strong was the zero?" would have fired on all seven. shared/study-appraisal.md has no such lens. It has a Blinding lens (RED for open-label where blinding was feasible) — but blinding was NOT feasible here, so that lens gives the trials a pass, and the question of what the control group actually did goes unasked. ⚑ The gap is precisely that: when blinding is impossible, comparator strength is the only remaining defence against expectancy, and nothing in the appraisal contract asks about it. ⚠ shared/study-appraisal.md is copied across five ICM vaults, so per CLAUDE.md's propagation rule this is logged for David rather than applied.

What would change the picture. A trial of progressive muscle relaxation against an active comparator matched for contact time and ritual — sham relaxation, an audiobook, a stretching routine — with an objective sleep measure alongside the questionnaire. Nothing in this synthesis's 14 trials is that trial.

Capped single-study on an unreliable appraisal. stance: pushes-back — against the interpretation of its own source's pooled estimate.
