The paper's biggest number is a questionnaire correlating with a subset of itself

Zhu 2026's largest reported effect is not about cues at all: "PA habit strength was found to be significantly positively correlated with PA behaviour, with a medium-to-large effect size." It functions in the paper as the load-bearing link — cue consistency predicts habit strength (r = 0.20), habit strength predicts behaviour strongly, therefore cues matter.

⚠ The last step is much weaker than it looks, because the two constructs are measured with overlapping instruments. Habit-strength scales in this literature — the Self-Report Habit Index and its descendants — ask respondents how far exercising is something I do frequently, something I do automatically, something characteristic of me. Physical-activity self-report asks how often and how long someone exercises. A frequency item inside the predictor is being correlated with a frequency outcome. A medium-to-large correlation is close to what the instruments guarantee.

⚑ The vault has already measured what this kind of shared-method inflation is worth, on a different literature and with an unusually clean natural comparison. social-connection ingested a pre-registered meta-analysis where the loneliness–health association was r = −.23 when the outcome was subjective and r = −.12 when it was objective (p < .0001) — a near-halving — with the authors' own candid diagnosis being common-method variance, "one disposition measured twice" (loneliness-health-association-halves-on-objective-measures). That map records the resulting operating rule: the further a literature gets from self-report, the weaker its claim.

Applied here, the discount runs in the direction that matters. If the habit-strength → behaviour link is substantially method variance, then the chain the paper builds to justify cue-based interventions has an inflated middle, and what remains is the direct, un-inflated, and very small association: r = 0.11.

⚠ Stated fairly, this is not a claim that habit strength is meaningless. Automaticity is a real construct with a plausible mechanism, and self-report is the only practical way to measure a felt sense. The claim is narrower: this paper's largest number cannot be used to rescue its smallest one, because the large one is inflated by exactly the shared measurement the small one lacks.

What the vault records as the closing gap for the new map, and it is the same gap in different words for all three of its founding concepts: the entire literature is self-report on both sides of every association. exercise-adherence needs a study in which cue consistency is manipulated or objectively logged and physical activity is measured by device. Until then the map holds a direction, a caution, and no magnitude it trusts.
