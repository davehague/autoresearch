The validated marker of small-vessel disease did not move at all

US POINTER's prespecified imaging analysis tracked six cerebrovascular MRI markers across two years of a structured multidomain lifestyle programme against a self-guided one, in 959 people aged 60–79.

Five were null:

MarkerResult
White-matter hyperintensity volume — the validated standard measure of small-vessel diseaseP = .90
Fractional anisotropyP = .30
Peak width of skeletonized mean diffusivity (PSMD)P = .60
ALPS index (perivascular clearance)P = .79
Incident cerebral microbleedsOR 0.59, P = .22

The sixth — white-matter free water — moved, and only in participants under 70 (multidomain-lifestyle-attenuates-white-matter-free-water-only-under-seventy).

⚑ Which marker moved is the whole issue. WMH volume is the established, clinically anchored, decades-old measure of exactly the pathology this intervention is hypothesised to slow, and it was flat to three decimal places of a p-value. Free water is the newest metric in the set, a diffusion-derived quantity whose relationship to clinical outcomes is still being established — partly by this paper. A novel marker moving while the validated marker stays flat is a pattern that has historically resolved in both directions, and the vault does not assume the flattering one.

And there is no multiplicity correction. Six markers × two prespecified age strata is at least twelve tests before any exploratory analysis. The paper does not adjust. shared/study-appraisal.md's p-hacking lens does not fire outright — the strata were prespecified and a continuous-age sensitivity check held — but one significant result in a set of twelve is not the same object as one significant result out of one test, and the vault grades accordingly.

⚠ One honest limit on reading these as negatives. The authors state they had limited statistical power to detect small intervention effects on WMH and microbleeds within age strata. So within the under-70 group specifically, those two nulls are uninformative rather than negative — the failure-to-detect/demonstrated- absence distinction shared/study-appraisal.md insists on, applied in the direction that is inconvenient for this concept. The pooled nulls at P = .90, .30, .60 and .79 across 959 people are a different matter, and those are what this concept mainly rests on.

Why pushes-back and suggestive. multidomain-lifestyle holds three prior surrogate wins on cognitive test scores. This is the first source on the map to ask what is happening in the tissue, and the answer across five of six measures is: nothing detectable. That is not a refutation of the cognitive findings — different outcome, different mechanism — but it is a live counterweight to the assumption that those score improvements reflect a structural change in the brain. It is graded suggestive rather than strong because the source's mixed verdict caps it, and because the effect sizes throughout are unstandardised betas on diffusion metrics with no clinical anchor of any kind.
