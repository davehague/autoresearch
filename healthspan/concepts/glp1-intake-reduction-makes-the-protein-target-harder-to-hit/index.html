The vault's oldest un-numbered GLP-1 warning finally has a number behind it

Since 2026-07-09, glp-1 and protein-target have both carried a sentence with nothing behind it: "appetite suppression can crowd out the ~1.6 g/kg/day protein target." It was a plausibility argument. It is now a measurement: the drug removes about 271 kcal from a single unlimited meal, placebo-controlled, with the food weighed (glp1-cuts-standardised-meal-intake-by-about-270-kcal).

Why the two facts are worse together than apart. The drug's documented cost is lean mass — −1.74 kg overall, −5.44 kg for semaglutide (glp1-lean-mass-loss-magnitude) — with a gradient in which the agents that remove the most weight remove the most muscle (tirzepatide-largest-fat-and-lean-mass-loss). The vault's evidence-based countermeasure is resistance training plus adequate protein plus creatine (protein-supplementation-augments-resistance-training, creatine-lean-mass-requires-resistance-training). The drug's mechanism is that you eat less, and eating less is precisely what makes the countermeasure harder to execute — at the exact moment the muscle needs it most.

This is not the vault inferring past the source. Quan 2026's own conclusion, from a group with no funding and no manufacturer ties, is that "structured nutritional counseling emphasizing adequate protein should complement pharmacotherapy." Laverde 2026's authors reached the same recommendation independently. Two unrelated syntheses arrive at the same countermeasure.

⚠ Flagged, and here is exactly what the flag does and does not do. Per shared/safety.md interaction evidence is deliberately asymmetric — a missed interaction is worse than an over-cautious flag, and a credible signal is enough. This one is measured rather than mechanistic, which is stronger than the bar requires. The flag gates naive co-recommendation and enters no benefit score, so it changes no ranking. It also changes nothing about GLP-1's regimen status: prescription/injectable means it stays in the clinician subsection, never ranked, with no dose set.

⚠ Two honest limits. The intake figure is one laboratory meal on 209 people across 3 trials, not a daily deficit (glp1-intake-reduction-measured-only-at-a-laboratory-lunch) — so the vault does not claim that the drug removes 271 kcal of protein-carrying food per day. And no trial has measured protein intake, muscle strength or physical function during GLP-1 treatment; every pooled muscle endpoint is lean mass, an imaging compartment. The functional cost of losing 1.7 to 5.4 kg of lean tissue remains unmeasured, and this note does not invent it.

The practical shape, for the clinician conversation GOAL.md points at: the question is what the protein plan is before starting, not after — and it is a question, not an instruction.
