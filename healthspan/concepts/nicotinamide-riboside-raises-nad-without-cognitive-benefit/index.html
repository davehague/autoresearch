Nicotinamide riboside doubles blood NAD⁺ and does not improve cognition — the same failure mode, a new outcome domain

A 12-week double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled phase-II pilot (n=42 completers) gave nicotinamide riboside to older adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment. Blood NAD⁺ increased roughly twofold — target engagement confirmed — and the primary cognitive outcome did not improve. Neither did total cerebral blood flow nor blood pressure. Adherence was similar across arms and no serious adverse events occurred.

The verified target engagement is what makes this null informative. The trial did not fail to deliver the compound, and NR is not poorly absorbed: the biomarker it is marketed on moved decisively, and nothing downstream followed. A null with confirmed target engagement is a statement about the hypothesis, not about the execution.

It lands on the map's own escape hatch. nad-precursors has held contested rather than fading partly because a plausible responder population was thought to exist — clinical deficiency or disease, not healthy aging (nad-precursors-benefit-confined-to-deficiency-populations). Amnestic MCI is that kind of population, with declining NAD⁺ as the specific proposed mechanism. It was tested and it did not respond. The escape hatch narrows; it does not close. Forty-two people over twelve weeks is a pilot, not a verdict — suggestive-capped, so Status holds contested per shared/evidence-model.md.

Second outcome domain, same shape. The map's founding source found NMN/NR null on muscle mass, grip, gait speed and chair-stand across 10 RCTs (nad-precursors-null-on-muscle-mass-function). This adds cognition and cerebral perfusion. Biomarker up, outcome flat — twice, in unrelated tissues, by independent groups.
