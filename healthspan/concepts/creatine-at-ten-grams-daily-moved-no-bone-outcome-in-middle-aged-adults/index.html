Ten grams a day for twelve weeks moved nothing in the skeleton

On the same calibrated DXA scanner that produced this trial's headline body-composition claims, bone mineral content, bone area and bone mineral density showed no statistically significant multivariate or univariate effect of group, time, or group × time, and no significant pairwise comparison — in adults aged 45–65 taking 10 g/day creatine monohydrate for 12 weeks, with and without a supervised exercise and weight-loss programme.

This lands on the one open question in the map's bone thread. creatine-bone-bmd-null establishes from five RCTs (Forbes 2018, n = 193) that creatine plus resistance training produces no greater bone mineral density than training alone at any site. The live counter-argument is creatine-high-dose-bone-indices — a narrative Letter to the Editor tallying 0 of 4 positive trials at 5 g/day against 5 of 6 at roughly 7–9 g/day, on heterogeneous bone geometry and content outcomes, written by authors who co-wrote the trials they were grading. The letter's claim is that the dose is the reason the nulls are nulls. Here is a trial at 10 g/day — above that band — measuring bone content, area and density, in a middle-aged cohort, run by a lab with a sponsor that manufactures creatine, and it is flat on all three.

Read at its true strength, which is low. This source is appraised unreliable on multiplicity grounds and is capped single-study; twelve weeks is short for bone, which remodels on a timescale of months to years and is conventionally measured over at least a year; and a null in a trial that never computed the power to detect a bone effect is an absence of demonstrated benefit, not a demonstrated absence — the same discipline this vault applies to underpowered nulls that flatter their authors. It does not close the geometry question and it is not a debunk.

What it does do is remove the easiest remaining excuse. The high-dose bone hypothesis said the trials failed because the dose was too low. A trial at a higher dose has now failed too. The map's practical position is unchanged and slightly better founded: no supplement in this vault has earned a purchase for bone, at any dose.
