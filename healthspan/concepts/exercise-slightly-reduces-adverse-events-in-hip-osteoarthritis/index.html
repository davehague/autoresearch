Loading a painful arthritic joint alongside other care slightly reduced adverse events

In the best-powered safety comparison Cochrane's hip osteoarthritis review could make, adding land-based exercise to an existing treatment probably slightly reduced adverse events: RR 0.75 (95% CI 0.58–0.97) across 6 trials and 731 participants, at moderate GRADE certainty.

Why this is a new kind of object in this vault. For three weeks the ledger has been unable to find a usable adverse-event number for loading a compromised lower limb. Six separate blood-flow-restriction syntheses across more than fifty trials report no adverse-event tallies at all (bfr-adverse-event-absence-replicates-without-becoming-safety, bfr-safety-not-established-in-trials). Shen 2026's fall-risk comparison had an interval reaching 11.11 and was written up as "similar safety profiles," which the vault refused (rt-intensity-safety-comparison-underpowered). LatAm-FINGERS' supervised multidomain programme generated six times the adverse events of its control arm, a cost absent from every summary of it (supervised-multidomain-programme-carries-musculoskeletal-adverse-event-burden). This is the first moderate-certainty adverse-event estimate on exercising a painful weight-bearing joint that the vault holds.

Read the direction carefully, because "reduced" invites over-reading. This is not evidence that exercise protects against harm. The most likely reading is mundane: people whose pain and function improve slightly report fewer of the symptomatic events that get counted as adverse. The interval's upper bound is 0.97, so the effect is barely distinguishable from no difference. The defensible claim is the absence of a harm signal, not the presence of a protective one — and for a decision about whether to load a joint that hurts, absence of harm is the thing that was actually in question.

Two of the three comparisons cannot support any claim, and the review says so. Versus usual care, adverse events were RR 2.95 (0.62–13.96) on 257 participants; versus attention control, RR 8.00 (1.13–56.79) on 18 participants. Cochrane rated both very low certainty and worded its conclusions as "the evidence is very uncertain." An interval reaching 56.79 carries no information in either direction, and the vault records it as such rather than as either a reassurance or an alarm. Graded suggestive rather than strong for that reason: the one usable cell is moderate certainty, but the safety picture across the review as a whole is thin.

Population, and what does not transfer. Hip osteoarthritis patients, in trials measuring events to end of follow-up. Not knees, not acute injury, not healthy people. shared/safety.md keeps knee-specific decisions where they belong — proposed, review with your doctor or physio.
