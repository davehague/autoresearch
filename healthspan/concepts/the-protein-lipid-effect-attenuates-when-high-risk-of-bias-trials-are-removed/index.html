The authors filtered their own corpus by quality, the headline shrank, and they did not print the shrunken number

Khosravi 2026's Conclusions read, verbatim:

"The findings of this study indicate that supplementation with HQPs was associated with reductions in LDL-C and TC; however, these effects were attenuated when high-risk-of-bias studies were excluded."

No attenuated estimate appears anywhere in the abstract. Not the point estimate, not the interval, not how many of the 32 trials were rated high-risk, not what share of the pooled weight they carried, not whether the attenuated LDL-C estimate still excluded zero. The sensitivity analysis is behind the paywall.

⚑ This is the fourth instance in three weeks of one shape, in a fourth unrelated literature. The pattern docs/open-gaps-log.md has been requesting a lens for is: the trials rated lowest risk of bias — or largest, or longest — are systematically the ones that agree least with the summary, and the synthesis does not reconcile it. Prior instances:

  • the-only-low-risk-student-sleep-trial-studied-good-sleepers — sleep, 2026-08-06.
  • integrated-aerobic-resistance-training-survives-the-quality-filter-that-sinks-circuit-training — blood pressure, 2026-08-21: the case where somebody ran the filter and the leaderboard collapsed.
  • Ren 2026's frailty grid, 2026-08-24: two of 34 trials rated low risk across all six domains, and both were nulls, under an abstract saying exercise "consistently improved" frailty.

What is new here, and it sharpens the proposed lens. In the three prior instances the vault had to run the comparison itself, or found it buried in a supplementary table. Here the authors ran it, disclosed it in their Conclusions, and then wrote the sentence so that the unfiltered estimate comes first and the filtered one arrives as a subordinate clause with no number attached. Disclosure without quantification is a third state between transparency and concealment, and the current lens wording does not distinguish it.

Proposed refinement to the standing lens request: "Does the summary survive restriction to the low-risk subset, and is the restricted estimate printed? RED — the synthesis reports that the effect attenuates under quality filtering without publishing the attenuated estimate. GREEN — the restricted estimate is given alongside the unrestricted one and the conclusion names which it rests on."

What follows for the finding itself. protein-supplementation-lowers-ldl-and-total-cholesterol-by-a-few-milligrams is a 3.45 mg/dL LDL reduction that becomes something smaller — possibly something crossing zero — when the weaker trials come out. Since the vault cannot see how much smaller, the honest position is that the effect is directionally supported and quantitatively unresolved, which is why the parent note is capped suggestive and the map's Status does not move.
