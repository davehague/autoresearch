A twentieth of a standard deviation, translated into two years of life

0.05 SD. That is the reported difference in leukocyte telomere length between cohorts whose first 1,000 days were sugar-rationed and those whose were not. The authors convert it to "approximately 2.2 fewer years of biological aging."

⚑ This is the exact move biological-age-clocks was coined to catch. That map's operating rule, from Vetter 2026's fourteen-biomarker head-to-head against 316 real deaths, is predictor ≠ responder: a marker that forecasts mortality in an untouched cohort is not thereby a validated outcome you can move (most-consensus-aging-biomarkers-did-not-predict-mortality). The whole expert-consensus panel discriminates weakly — C-index 0.65, with eleven of fourteen markers collectively worth 0.02 (aging-biomarker-panels-discriminate-mortality-weakly).

Telomere length is among the noisiest measures in that family, and this vault already holds three results on it that do not agree: exercise maintains it (exercise-maintains-telomere-length), omega-3 does not affect it (omega3-no-telomere-effect), vitamin D preserves it (vitamin-d-preserves-telomere-length).

The conversion is where the trouble is, not the measurement. A fifth of a fifth of a standard deviation is a small, plausibly-real difference. Expressed as "2.2 years," it acquires the grammar of a life outcome — and it becomes the most quotable number in a paper whose actual hard endpoint, cancer incidence, is reported separately and stands or falls on its own (early-life-sugar-restriction-lowers-adult-cancer-incidence-without-reported-intervals).

⚑ The structural observation worth keeping: this paper contains both a hard endpoint and an aging clock in one population, and treats the clock as a second confirmation rather than a second question. Nothing in it tests whether the telomere difference mediates the cancer difference. Two results in the same cohort pointing the same way is not a mechanism; it is two results.

Graded single-study — the effect is tiny, the instrument is weak, no interval is reported, and the source is abstract-only against a confirmed paywall.
