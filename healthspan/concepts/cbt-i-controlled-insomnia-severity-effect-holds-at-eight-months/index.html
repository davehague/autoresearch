The map's first controlled, de-bundled CBT-I magnitude — after four sources that each missed one criterion

cbt-insomnia has spent a month collecting CBT-I effect sizes that each failed a different test. Fiori 2026 measured depression, not sleep. Zhou 2026 had no CBT-alone node — every CBT arm was bundled with exercise (cbt-sleep-effect-not-isolable-no-cbt-alone-node). Yan 2026 affirmed isolated efficacy from guideline consensus but reported no pooled effect size (cbt-i-sleep-effect-size-not-pooled-here). Öst 2025 supplied a de-bundled magnitude that was within-group and uncontrolled (cbt-i-routine-care-effect-sizes-within-group).

Reesen 2026 is de-bundled, randomised, controlled, on insomnia severity, with a number at two horizons: d = −1.04 at two months and d = −0.98 at eight.

The durability is the part that matters, and it is the map's founding claim made measurable. This map was coined on the observation that CBT-I is "the only intervention anywhere in the vault whose benefit is measured after you stop doing it" — a skill rather than a dose. Six months after the last session, roughly 94% of the treatment effect is still present. Every substance on the vault's sleep shelf — melatonin, l-theanine, chamomile, magnesium-glycinate — stops working the day it is stopped, and none of them reaches a fraction of this effect size while it is being taken (supplement-sleep-effect-small-and-below-clinical-threshold puts the entire supplement shelf at PSQI −0.70, below the clinical threshold).

⚠ Three discounts, and the first is the one this map taught itself eight days ago.

(1) The comparator is a sleep diary. Participants in the control condition tracked their sleep and received no therapeutic content (cbt-i-large-effect-was-measured-against-a-sleep-diary-control). Barroso 2026 demonstrated inside a single paper that the same therapy scores −0.67 against a waitlist and 0.21 against CBT-I (act-improves-insomnia-severity-against-inactive-controls). A d of −1.04 against a near-inactive control and a risk ratio of 0.81 against an active rival are both true of CBT-I, and they are different currencies.

(2) The outcome is an unblindable self-report. ISI is a questionnaire, the therapy cannot be blinded, and the control carries no therapeutic expectation.

(3) One trial, n = 157, not a pooled estimate — so the map's gap narrows rather than closes. What it now wants is a controlled meta-analytic sleep-continuity estimate, in adults without an insomnia diagnosis.

⚠ The population hold is untouched, for the fifth consecutive source, and this is its strongest instance. These participants had insomnia plus active anxiety, PTSD or borderline personality-disorder symptoms — further from a healthy sleeper than any of the four sources before it (cbt-i-population-is-insomnia-patients). Status held emerging.

What genuinely improves is the trigger, not the ranking. The map already says: if sleep stops being a foundation to protect and becomes a problem to treat, this is the first thing to reach for. That instruction now carries a controlled magnitude, an eight-month durability horizon, and a delivery format — guided and digital — that costs less than a clinic course.
