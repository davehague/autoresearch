A second independent randomised multidomain trial improves a cognitive composite

SUPERBRAIN-MEET randomised 300 South Korean adults aged 60–85 with mild cognitive impairment to a 24-week multidomain lifestyle programme — dietary counselling, physical exercise, cognitive training and vascular/metabolic risk monitoring, delivered face-to-face and through a tablet application — or to usual care. In the 277 analysed, the RBANS total index score improved by 4.17 points more in the intervention arm (95% CI 1.92–6.43, p < 0.001).

What RBANS is. The Repeatable Battery for the Assessment of Neuropsychological Status: a short neuropsychological test battery built to be re-administered over time, whose index scores are age-adjusted standard scores with a mean of 100 and a standard deviation of 15. On that scale 4.17 points is about 0.28 of a standard deviation — a small-to-moderate difference. The paper states no clinical anchor, so what it means for a person's day is not established here.

Why this matters to multidomain-lifestyle. It is the map's second independent randomised trial with a positive main effect, after LatAm-FINGERS (multidomain-lifestyle-improves-cognitive-composite-vs-health-advice) — different investigators, different continent, different delivery model, both assessor-blinded, both positive on a cognitive composite. Status was nonetheless held at emerging, because shared/evidence-model.md bars consolidating while a live rebuttal stands, and Look AHEAD's ten-year null does — the two positives measure test scores over 24 weeks and 2 years, while the null measures incident cognitive impairment over 12–14 years.

⚠ The comparator is the qualification that must travel with the number. The control arm received usual care. The intervention arm received sessions, an app, counselling, supervised exercise, cognitive training and risk monitoring. Attention, contact time and expectation are entirely unmatched, and the outcome is a learnable cognitive battery administered twice. The contrast is instructive: LatAm-FINGERS used an active health-advice control and produced 0.11 standard deviations per year; this trial used usual care and produced 0.28 standard deviations over 24 weeks. The weaker comparator, the larger separation (walking-cognition-null-is-partly-against-active-comparators).

And the attribution problem carries an extra edge here. Cognitive training is one of the intervention components, and cognitive testing is the outcome. Part of the intervention is practice at the kind of task being measured. This map has recorded the unseparable-bundle defect eight times (multidomain-nutrition-exercise-bundle-not-separable); this is the version where one component and the endpoint overlap directly.

Graded suggestive — the source is appraised mixed, capping strength. Held there rather than higher by the usual-care comparator, the unaddressed practice effect, the 24-week surrogate endpoint with no incident-dementia outcome, and an unrecoverable funding and competing-interests statement in a paper whose author list includes a digital-therapeutics company.

Rebutted by: multidomain-lifestyle-null-on-five-of-six-cerebrovascular-mri-markers — US POINTER's prespecified imaging analysis (n=959) found five of six cerebrovascular MRI markers unmoved by two years of a structured multidomain programme, including white-matter hyperintensity volume at P = .90. That does not refute a cognitive-score gain — different outcome, different mechanism — but it is a live counterweight to reading such gains as evidence of a structural change in the brain.
