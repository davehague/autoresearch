The 10% "VO2max improvement" is two heart-rate numbers printed three rows above it

VO2max — maximal oxygen uptake — is the amount of oxygen a body can consume per kilogram per minute at peak effort, and it is the standard measure of cardiorespiratory fitness. Measuring it requires analysing expired gas. This trial did not do that. Its Methods state the computation:

VO2max = 15 × HRmax / HRrest

That is a published estimating equation, and it is entirely determined by two heart rates. There is no gas analysis, no oxygen uptake, no respiratory exchange ratio anywhere in the protocol.

The paper reports both inputs in the same table as the output. For the treatment group: maximal heart rate 163.33 → 176.50, resting heart rate 83.83 → 82.38.

⚑ The arithmetic closes exactly. (176.50 / 82.38) ÷ (163.33 / 83.83) = 2.1425 / 1.9484 = 1.0996. The reported VO2max change is 32.20 / 29.29 = 1.0994. They agree to rounding, because they are the same number.

The primary endpoint therefore contains no information that the heart-rate rows do not already contain. It is not a second, corroborating observation — it is the first two observations restated in different units, under the name of a measurement the trial did not perform. The Discussion nonetheless describes VO2max as "the gold standard metric for assessing cardiorespiratory fitness" and reports the trial as having improved it.

⚑ Restating the finding in its own terms is what exposes the problem. "Eight weeks of a 30 mg botanical extract raised maximal heart rate by 13 beats per minute" is what the data say. "Improved VO2max by 10%" is what the abstract says. They are the same claim, and only one of them invites the obvious question — see a-thirteen-beat-rise-in-maximal-heart-rate-is-reported-as-improved-fitness.

⚑ This is a shape shared/study-appraisal.md does not currently name. The lens set distinguishes surrogate endpoints from hard ones — a surrogate being a real measurement standing in for an outcome. This is a level worse: a derived endpoint, arithmetically reconstructible from other variables the same paper reports. A concrete check would be: is the primary endpoint an independent observation, or a function of other reported variables — and if the latter, does the causal story survive being restated in those variables? Here it does not. Logged as an appraisal-lens request in docs/open-gaps-log.md rather than applied, per CLAUDE.md's propagation rule.

⚠ None of this is an accusation of misconduct. The formula is legitimate and widely used in field settings; the trial was prospectively registered, properly blinded and adequately powered. The criticism is about what the number can support, not about how it was produced.
