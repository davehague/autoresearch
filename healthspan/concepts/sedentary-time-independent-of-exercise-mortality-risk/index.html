Sedentary time predicts mortality even after accounting for physical activity (umbrella review, 2.8M adults)

An umbrella review of 30 systematic reviews — 83 unique studies, 2,778,595 unique participants — found that 29 of 30 reviews observed a positive relationship between sedentary time and all-cause mortality. The association was attenuated but remained significant after accounting for physical activity, and it was consistent across device-based and self-reported measures of sedentary time. Mean review quality was 9.6 ± 1.6 out of 11 on the Joanna Briggs Institute checklist.

No effect size is given here because the source does not report one. An umbrella review of reviews reports direction, consistency, and quality; it does not produce a pooled hazard ratio. Per shared/safety.md, the vault reports the claim it can source and invents no number to fill the gap.

Why this matters for the vault. Every exercise lever the vault holds measures what you do: daily-steps-mortality-dose-response (~7,000 steps/day, HR 0.53), cardiorespiratory-fitness-all-cause-mortality, resistance-training-lowers-mortality. This measures what you don't, and finds it is a separate exposure. Adjusting for physical activity attenuated the association without erasing it — meaning a person can hit the step target and still carry risk from eleven seated waking hours. You cannot out-train the chair. That non-redundancy is the entire reason sedentary-behavior earns its own map rather than a bullet on daily-steps.

Bounded honestly (why suggestive). The source's reliability verdict is mixed, which caps strength (shared/evidence-model.md). Every study inside the umbrella is observational — sedentary time cannot be randomized across decades — so residual confounding by occupation, disability, and socioeconomic status remains. More seriously, reverse causation is not excluded: illness, frailty, and pain all cause sitting, and no lag analyses or early-death exclusions are reported. That is the same "cart before the horse" lens that capped the ≥9-hour tail of long-sleep-mortality-likely-reverse-causal.

Two things distinguish this from that case, and they are why it clears emerging rather than sitting as a caution. The association survives adjustment for the confounder a skeptic reaches for first (physical activity), and it replicates across accelerometers and questionnaires alike — so unlike the sleep-duration literature, measurement error is not a live explanation here.

The practical shape of the claim. Directional, and enough to act on: break up sitting, and start with the most discretionary slice of it (tv-viewing-time-mortality-association). It is free, requires no equipment, is entirely knee-safe (GOAL.md), and composes with the step target rather than duplicating it. It supports no numeric sitting target, because none was published.
