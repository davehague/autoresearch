Two arms that differ by one prescription — the rarest structure in this vault's exercise literature

Yang 2026 randomised postmenopausal women to three arms: Nordic walking + a 1.4 g/kg/day protein target + a 10-hour eating window, Nordic walking + the same 10-hour window, or usual routine. Both active arms did the same 36 supervised walking sessions under the same eating schedule, at 95.45% attendance and ~94% window compliance. The only prescribed difference between them is the protein target, hit on 94.05% of days and verified against dietary records reviewed by the study team rather than taken on self-report alone.

That structure is why this note exists. This vault has logged the opposite pattern until it became a running theme: a CBT+exercise arm cannot price CBT (cbt-sleep-effect-not-isolable-no-cbt-alone-node); a creatine+beta-alanine arm cannot price beta-alanine; a protein+probiotic arm cannot price the probiotic (probiotic-adds-nothing-to-protein-for-performance); an HMB trial cannot separate HMB from the protein beside it (hmb-trial-cannot-separate-hmb-from-the-24-grams-of-protein-beside-it). Yang bundled too — but symmetrically, so the bundle cancels and one component is left standing.

What the contrast showed. Both walking arms beat control on grip strength, gait speed and chair-stand. The arm with the protein target beat the arm without it on all three, and the separation on lean mass is categorical rather than graded: +1.19 kg of appendicular lean mass against +0.04 kg from identical walking inside an identical eating window.

Direction taken; magnitude refused, and the refusal is not a formality. Three things sit between this contrast and a clean protein effect:

(1) Energy intake was never compared across the arms — by the authors' explicit choice, stated in the methods and repeated in the limitations (nordic-walking-trial-never-compared-energy-intake-across-its-protein-contrast). "Coached to eat more protein" and "ate more" are not separated.

(2) Lean mass came from bioelectrical impedance in a trial whose other arm fasts fourteen hours a day (lean-mass-gain-measured-by-impedance-in-a-fasting-trial).

(3) The number fails a cross-vault sanity check. creatine-monohydrate holds creatine plus resistance training at +1.37 kg of lean tissue (Chilibeck 2017, 22 RCTs), and creatine's fat-free-mass gain is null without training (creatine-lean-mass-requires-resistance-training). A +1.19 kg limb-only gain from pole-walking plus a protein target, in women averaging 22 kg/m² BMI, is in the same range as the vault's best-evidenced anabolic stack — from a modality the authors themselves concede "is not a conventional strength-training modality."

What protein-target actually gains. That map's position is that daily total protein around 1.6 g/kg/day drives the training-adaptation benefit, sourced almost entirely on resistance training in younger and mixed cohorts. This is a lower target (1.4), a non-resistance modality, an under-represented population, and it points the same way: protein availability gates what an exercise stimulus converts into. It does not move the target, does not extend the map to walking as a strength stimulus, and is capped suggestive.
