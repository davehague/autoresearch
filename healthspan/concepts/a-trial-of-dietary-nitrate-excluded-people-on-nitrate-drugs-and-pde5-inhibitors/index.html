The interaction this vault flagged three times and could not evidence is written into a trial's exclusion criteria

beetroot-juice has carried a standing open gap since 2026-08-02, re-logged on 08-03 and again on 08-14. Its terms: dietary nitrate is a nitric-oxide donor and therefore vasoactive; this vault holds several maps making blood-pressure claims; shared/safety.md permits raising an interaction flag on a credible mechanistic signal. But no source in the vault had ever measured nitrate alongside blood pressure or an antihypertensive, and the vault does not fabricate interactions. The map records that the trigger "has now failed to fire three times, which is itself worth recording."

⚑ It fires here, and not from the results — from the Methods. Verbatim: "individuals on the following medications at the time of screening were excluded: hormone replacement, nitrates, phosphodiesterase inhibitors, and long-term anti-inflammatory drug usage."

Why those two drug classes and not others. Nitrate medications — nitroglycerin, isosorbide mononitrate and dinitrate — are prescribed for angina and work by donating nitric oxide to relax blood vessels. Phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors — sildenafil, tadalafil — work on the same pathway from the other end, by blocking the enzyme that breaks down the downstream signalling molecule nitric oxide produces. ⚑ Their combination with each other is a recognised contraindication in clinical practice, because stacking two nitric-oxide-pathway drugs can cause a large and dangerous drop in blood pressure.

Dietary nitrate is a third entry point to the same pathway. ⚑ A team of cardiovascular physiologists designing a trial to give people 6.4 mmol of it daily for four weeks treated it as belonging to that class and excluded the combination from their study. That is an expert judgement recorded in a peer-reviewed protocol, not an inference this vault constructed.

Under shared/safety.md, that is enough. The contract states that interaction evidence is asymmetric — a missed interaction is worse than an over-cautious flag — and that a mechanistic or case-report-level signal is sufficient to raise a flag, provided its tier is recorded honestly in the note. ⚑ This note is graded mechanistic tier and single-study strength precisely so the flag gates co-recommendation without inflating anyone's confidence that a clinically important interaction has been demonstrated.

⚠ What this does NOT claim, stated as plainly as possible. No interaction was measured. No trial in this vault has co-administered dietary nitrate with a nitrate drug or a PDE5 inhibitor and observed anything. The magnitude is unknown. This is not a claim that beetroot juice is dangerous, and it says nothing whatever about eating beetroot, spinach, rocket or any other nitrate-rich vegetable — which supply the same compound in ordinary amounts and are part of the Mediterranean pattern the vault holds on mediterranean-diet. It concerns concentrated supplemental doses taken alongside a prescription drug acting on the same pathway.

What the flag does operationally. Under shared/safety.md, a safety_flags: [interaction] entry caps the corresponding regimen item at "proposed — review with your doctor," regardless of its Benefit score, and requires a note in both affected maps. Beetroot juice is not currently in David's regimen at any tier, so nothing in regimen/current-protocol.md changes — but the flag is now on the record for the moment it might.

⚑ The general lesson worth keeping: a trial's exclusion criteria are an under-read source of safety information. They record what the investigators believed was risky enough to design around, which is often knowledge that never makes it into a results section because the combination was never allowed to happen. This vault should read exclusion lists on every ingest, not just methods and results.

In one sentence: the interaction this map flagged three times without evidence is now supported by a specialist team's decision to exclude exactly that combination from a trial of exactly this compound.
