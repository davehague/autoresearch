The nine composites threw away half of the sleep before the outcome models ran

The Sleep and Dementia Consortium measured 44 sleep metrics by home polysomnography and compressed them into 9 latent composites. Those nine composites explain 49% of the total variance in the 44 inputs.

That single number is the correct frame for reading everything else in the paper. More than half of the measured variation in these people's sleep did not survive into the analysis. So when all nine composites came back null for incident dementia (nine-polysomnographic-sleep-composites-null-for-incident-dementia), what was tested was not "sleep" — it was nine constructs jointly representing about half of what the overnight recordings captured.

This cuts in both directions, and the vault records both.

Against over-reading the null: a sleep–dementia association living in the discarded 51% would be invisible to this design. The null is real and it is large, but it is bounded by its own dimension reduction.

Against over-reading the positives: the same compression means the two significant cognition associations (polysomnographic-sleep-quantity-and-coupling-track-cognition-at-0-03-sd) are associations with latent constructs whose units exist only inside this paper. "β = 0.03 per one-unit change in composite" cannot be translated into hours, into a sleep-efficiency percentage, or into anything a person or a device could act on.

The comparison with the vault's other multidimensional-sleep source is instructive. Paquet 2026 reduced 39 variables to 13 composites explaining ~71% of variance (sleep-is-a-multidimensional-construct-not-a-single-metric). More composites, more variance retained, and it reached the same substantive conclusions — tiny cross-sectional cognition signals, nothing surviving longitudinally. Two teams chose different points on the compression trade-off and got the same answer, which is mild evidence that the answer is not an artefact of where either one cut.

The standing implication for this vault. Every sleep claim it holds rests on a narrower instrument than 44 polysomnography metrics — self-reported duration in sleep-duration, wrist accelerometry in sleep-regularity, questionnaire severity scores in cbt-insomnia. If nine research-grade composites off a full polysomnogram retain only half the variance, a single number off a wrist device retains dramatically less. That is a reason to distrust precision, not a reason to distrust the direction of the vault's sleep advice — which is why the practical guidance is behavioural (regular timing) rather than metric-targeted.
