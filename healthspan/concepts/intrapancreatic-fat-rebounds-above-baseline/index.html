One fat depot came back worse than it started — and the vault carries it rather than burying it

The good news in Klein/Shai 2026 is that abdominal fat depots partially keep their reductions 5–10 years after an 18-month lifestyle intervention ends, even though the weight comes back completely (abdominal-fat-loss-partially-persists-despite-weight-regain).

The bad news, in the same table: two depots did the opposite. Intrahepatic fat was fully regained — back to where it started. And intrapancreatic fat was excessively regained: it "increased beyond baseline." It is the only depot in the study that ended up worse than before the participants ever did the intervention.

The vault records this for the same reason it recorded Look AHEAD's cognitive-harm signal at BMI ≥40 and omega-3's atrial-fibrillation harm: shared/evidence-model.md says Status moves both ways, and that applies inside a paper as well as across a literature. A source that supports an intervention on four measures and undercuts it on one gets reported on all five.

What it does not license, stated so a future run does not overreach. This is not evidence that lifestyle intervention causes pancreatic harm. There is no control group of people who never intervened in this analysis — everyone in the cohort did the program — so the counterfactual is unknown. Ectopic pancreatic fat rises with age and with weight cycling, and these participants did both. The honest statement is: after an intervention-and-relapse cycle, this depot ended up above where it started, and nobody knows whether it would have ended up higher still without the intervention. No safety_flags are set on this basis; no magnitude is invented, because the paper publishes none.

Why it is worth keeping anyway. It is a concrete, MRI-measured caution against the most natural misreading of this paper — "do the program, and the benefit sticks around even if you regain" — which is true for three depots and false for two. And it puts a real question in front of anyone who diets and relapses repeatedly: what does the cycle itself do? Nothing in the vault answers that. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md.
