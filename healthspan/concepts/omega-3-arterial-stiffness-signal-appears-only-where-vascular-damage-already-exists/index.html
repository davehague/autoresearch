Where omega-3 moved arterial stiffness, the arteries were already damaged

Across the 20 randomised trials in Ibarra-Peso 2026, the trials reporting significant reductions in pulse wave velocity cluster in people who already have vascular disease or elevated risk: metabolic syndrome, dyslipidaemia, diabetes with neuropathy, abdominal aortic aneurysm, and older adults. The trials reporting nothing cluster at low doses, in acute or short interventions, and with plant-derived ALA rather than EPA/DHA.

Two details sharpen it into something more than a subgroup list:

  • Within a single trial, 12 weeks of EPA and DHA reduced carotid–femoral PWV in the older participants and not in the younger ones — same protocol, same investigators, split by age.
  • Of the three trials enrolling people under 30, the only one showing an effect was in smokers — the one under-30 group with accelerated arterial ageing, i.e. a vascular age older than their chronological age.

The review's reading, and it is a reasonable one: benefit appears to require pre-existing vascular damage to reverse. A healthy artery has little room to improve on this measure, so a null in a healthy cohort may be a ceiling effect rather than an absence of drug action.

The atrial-fibrillation trade-off only exists for people with enough baseline risk to trade (the-omega-3-arrhythmia-benefit-trade-only-exists-for-people-with-enough-baseline-risk-to-buy-it). And now arterial stiffness. Three separate endpoints, three separate literatures, one consistent boundary: the healthier the person, the less omega-3 appears to do.

Two limits. The pattern is post-hoc and it does not hold inside the review's own largest, longest, lowest-risk-of-bias trials — see the-omega-3-arterial-stiffness-evidence-is-a-vote-count-that-fails-inside-its-own-best-studies. And PWV is a surrogate: it is arterial mechanics, not an event. This sits on the surrogate-markers strand of omega-3 and does not extend the hard-outcome ledger.

For a healthy adult without diagnosed metabolic disease, this is the null cluster, not the signal cluster. Not medical advice.
