One of the two positive findings was an outcome added after the protocol was registered

Ten pooled outcomes, two significant. Handgrip strength: SMD 0.40 (0.05–0.74), p = 0.027, five studies — and handgrip strength was one of two outcomes added after registration. The authors state it in their methods, verbatim: "Countermovement jump and handgrip strength were added after registration when comparable data became available and are therefore reported as post-registration outcomes." Of the two late additions, one came back null and one came back positive — which is what chance produces as readily as biology.

The other positive is narrow rather than late. Time to peak power SMD −0.92 (−1.29 to −0.54) — participants reached their peak power sooner — from four Wingate-based studies only, while peak power itself was null. The authors say the result "limit[s] its generalizability to other sprint modalities and sport-specific settings."
