The vault went looking for CBT-I's sleep evidence and came back with CBT-plus-exercise — the gap narrows, it does not close

cbt-insomnia was coined on 2026-07-12 with an unusually blunt admission: this vault had ingested a paper about the wrong outcome. Fiori 2026 measured CBT-I's effect on depressive symptoms (durable at 3, 6 and 12 months post-treatment — cbt-i-durable-depression-benefit), not on sleep. The map was held at emerging for exactly that reason, and docs/open-gaps-log.md tasked the next sweep with going to fetch the sleep evidence that makes CBT-I first-line in every clinical guideline.

The next sweep came back with Zhou 2026 — and it does not contain a CBT-alone arm.

The network's ten nodes are: EX, QG (qigong), TCM, CON (conventional control), EX+CBT, QG+CBT, EX+TCM, QG+TCM, QG+ArTMS, QG+SrTMS. Every single CBT arm is CBT bundled with exercise or qigong. There is no node against which CBT's own contribution can be estimated.

So what the vault now knows is precise, and narrower than it wanted: exercise combined with CBT beats conventional control on subjective sleep quality, sleep latency and wake-after-sleep-onset, at moderate certainty on the first of those (exercise-plus-cbt-improves-sleep-in-insomnia). What it still does not know is what CBT-I does by itself — and it will not infer that number from a bundled arm, any more than it inferred creatine's effect from a creatine-plus-beta-alanine arm (creatine-beta-alanine-no-synergy) or a probiotic's effect from a protein-plus-probiotic arm (probiotic-adds-nothing-to-protein-for-performance).

That is three times in three days that the vault has hit the same structural problem: the combination arm is where the evidence is, and the combination arm cannot answer the question about the component. It is worth naming as a recurring failure mode of this literature rather than a coincidence — trialists bundle, and bundling destroys attribution.

The honest position, restated. CBT-I's sleep efficacy is real in the wider literature and still not sourced in this vault. The map stays at emerging. The gap in docs/open-gaps-log.md is narrowed, not closed, and is rewritten to be more specific: a meta-analysis of CBT-I as a standalone intervention, with sleep as the primary outcome — ideally in adults without a formal insomnia diagnosis, which would close the population hold at the same time.

And the vault does not treat this as a technicality. It matters because the trigger written into cbt-insomnia — if sleep ever becomes a problem to treat rather than a foundation to protect, this is the first thing to reach for — is a real clinical recommendation in waiting, and the vault should know what it is recommending before it recommends it. Right now it would be recommending, on its own evidence, "CBT plus exercise." Which, given the run's other findings, may be the right answer anyway.
