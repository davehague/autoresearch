Five objectively-measured sleep dimensions track with cognition — at about 0.05 SD each, cross-sectionally

In MESA (n=1,628, mean age 68.0), 13 multidimensional sleep composites derived from 39 objective and subjective sleep variables were regressed on a global cognition composite. After adjustment for demographic and lifestyle factors, five reached significance: EEG power spectral density (β = 0.065, 0.03–0.10), spindle density (0.052), spindle–slow oscillation coupling (0.050), actigraphy-based sleep continuity (0.049) and REM sleep (0.047) — all per standard deviation of exposure.

The instrument is the best thing about this result and the design is the worst. Polysomnographic EEG and wrist actigraphy are far stronger exposure measurement than the questionnaires most of the vault's sleep literature runs on. But the significant associations are cross-sectional, measured at the same visit, in a cohort with a mean age of 68 — so direction is undetermined.

Reverse causation is the more parsimonious explanation, item for item. Reduced spindle density, degraded spindle–slow-oscillation coupling, fragmented sleep continuity and shortened REM are established features of preclinical neurodegeneration. A brain that scores lower on Digit Symbol Coding is expected to have exactly this sleep signature because of the same underlying pathology, with no causal contribution from sleep required. shared/study-appraisal.md's reverse-causation lens fires directly.

The magnitudes are inside confounding range. β ≈ 0.05 per SD is the effect-size band where, in the appraisal file's words, "anything unaccounted-for can distort reality." And five hits out of thirteen composites at that magnitude is the arithmetic shape of a noisy screen — the same shape the vault named in epigenetic-clock-signal-rests-on-one-of-five-clocks and in Zhu 2026's one-significant-cell-in-36 hemodynamics.

Three of the five are not levers. EEG power spectral density, spindle density and spindle–slow-oscillation coupling are measurements of sleep microstructure, not behaviours. Nothing in this vault targets them, no intervention is known to move them, and no consumer device that scores them is thereby actionable. Only sleep continuity connects to anything a person can change.

Read at its true strength. mixed, capped suggestive. Cross-sectional; tiny; selected analytic subsample (1,628 of a much larger cohort — participants with full PSG and actigraphy and cognition are not random); no hard endpoint. Covariate list and composite loadings UNVERIFIED (paywalled).

For David: nothing to act on and nothing to buy — least of all a wearable that reports spindles or REM percentage. The actionable sleep lever remains consistent sleep and wake timing, earned on sleep-regularity-predicts-mortality's accelerometry-measured mortality evidence, not on a cross-sectional cognitive correlation in 68-year-olds.
