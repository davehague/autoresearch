The one "modifiable" module points at NAD+ — and the vault already has two human trials saying it doesn't help

Against a background in which most aging co-methylation networks proved fragile and resistant to beneficial modulation (aging-methylation-networks-resist-beneficial-modulation), the authors found one notable exception: a resilient module enriched for NAD⁺ biosynthesis via the nicotinamide-riboside salvage pathway. They read this as "supporting NAD⁺ as a potential therapeutic target in aging."

The vault records the finding and declines the conclusion, by rule rather than by preference. shared/evidence-model.md sets the tier hierarchy meta-analysis-rct > rct > cohort > mechanistic/animal and says to apply it deterministically. This claim is mechanistic, and computational within that — an in silico perturbation on cross-sectional bulk-tissue data, whose supporting citations are tissue-level NR studies rather than human outcomes, and whose own authors state that "experimental validation is essential to confirm causal roles for identified genes and modules."

nad-precursors already holds two higher-tier human results, both null. A meta-analysis of 10 RCTs found NMN and NR null on muscle mass, strength, gait speed and chair-stand in adults over 60 (nad-precursors-null-on-muscle-mass-function). A phase-II double-blind RCT in amnestic MCI — precisely the "responder population" escape hatch the map had been holding open — doubled blood NAD⁺ and moved neither the primary cognitive outcome, nor total cerebral blood flow, nor blood pressure (nicotinamide-riboside-raises-nad-without-cognitive-benefit). A module score does not outrank two human outcome studies. Status held contested; capped single-study, so it cannot move a map alone in any case.

Why this is worth writing down rather than ignoring. The vault's position on NAD⁺ precursors is often misread as "the biology is nonsense." It is not. The mechanism is coherent: NAD⁺ declines with age, the salvage pathway is real, NR demonstrably raises NAD⁺, and now the associated methylation module appears unusually responsive. The vault's actual claim is the sharper and more awkward one — the biology is coherent, the target is engageable, and the humans still do not get better. That is the same shape as dha-brain-delivery-without-cognitive-or-structural-benefit and creatine-20g-raises-brain-creatine: verified engagement, absent outcome. A reader who finds the mechanism persuasive should treat that as a reason for more scepticism of mechanism-led supplement marketing, not less.
