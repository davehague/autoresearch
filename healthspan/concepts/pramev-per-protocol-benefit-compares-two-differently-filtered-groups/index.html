The programme's one positive result is a comparison between 61% of one arm and 79% of the other

PRAMEV's intention-to-treat analysis of its prespecified weight endpoint was null (OR 1.34, 0.61–2.92, P = .46). Its per-protocol analysis, restricted to "participants who completed the 20-week intervention," was positive: 25.4% (15 of 59) versus 12.3% (10 of 81), OR 2.52, 95% CI 1.03–6.13, P = .04. That is the result the abstract's closing sentence carries — "secondary and per-protocol analyses suggested potential cardiometabolic benefits among adherent participants."

The two analyses are not two views of the same comparison, because the two arms were filtered differently. 38 of 97 intervention participants (39.2%) dropped out against 22 of 103 controls (21.4%), P = .008 — and among the intervention arm's stated reasons, 28.9% cited difficulty with protocol adherence. So the per-protocol comparison sets the 61% of programme participants who could sustain a demanding 20-week regimen against the 79% of control participants who managed to keep attending usual care. Surviving the harder arm selects harder for the traits that also produce weight loss: motivation, schedule control, absence of competing life crises. Randomisation equalises those traits at baseline; conditioning on completion un-equalises them. This is shared/study-appraisal.md's healthy-adherer bias, arriving through the back door of a completer analysis rather than through the front door of a non-randomised comparison.

Note also how thin the survivor is. The per-protocol interval's lower bound is 1.03. A handful of reclassified participants erases it.

Why the appraisal did not fire fatal flag #4. That flag is "per-protocol/completer analysis concealing non-random dropout." Nothing here is concealed: the dropout is counted by arm, broken down by reason, formally tested at P = .008, and named in the authors' own limitations. The trial's analysis is honest and its concluding sentence is not — the ITT null is in the Results and the per-protocol result is what a reader carries away. The failure mode is in the writing, which is the same diagnosis within-subject-unilateral-design-strengthens-a-training-null-without-rescuing-it reached about a registered, powered, conflict-free trial that still wrote its null up as equivalence.

The reusable rule. Before reading a lifestyle trial's headline, read its dropout by arm. In an unblinded behavioural intervention, differential attrition is not noise around the effect; it is often the largest single determinant of the reported effect.
