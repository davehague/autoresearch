Inside the trial that found no drug effect, the predictors of healthspan loss were smoking, blood pressure and frailty

ASPREE reported that participants who were older, male, smokers, hypertensive, or frail were more likely to lose healthspan over the trial's median 4.7 years. Age and sex are fixed. Smoking, hypertension and frailty are not.

Graded down deliberately, despite sitting inside a reliable randomised trial. The drug comparison was randomised; this is not. It is an observational analysis of baseline characteristics against subsequent events, and it carries every observational weakness the appraisal file names: residual confounding, and reverse causation in the specific and likely form that preclinical disease causes frailty. Only direction is reported at abstract level — no effect sizes, no confidence intervals, no adjustment set are recoverable. So this is suggestive, not strong. The source's reliable verdict is a ceiling, not a floor: it does not launder a within-trial observational analysis into randomised evidence.

What makes it worth a note anyway is the shape of the answer. A trial designed to test a pill found nothing from the pill, and found that the people who lost healthy years were distinguished by hypertension, smoking status and frailty — three things the rest of this vault is already about. Resistance training lowers systolic and diastolic blood pressure by −6.11 / −2.53 mmHg in adults ≥60 (resistance-training-lowers-blood-pressure-older-adults). Supervised multidomain programmes improve frailty scores in an RCT (multidomain-intervention-improves-frailty-and-function-frail-elders). Low fat-free mass tracks mortality mostly as a frailty/illness marker rather than a tissue effect (fat-free-mass-measurement-method-gradient).

The honest limit on that reading. Predicting healthspan loss is not the same as modifying it — the same distinction biological-age-clocks draws between a predictor and a responder. This concept identifies where risk concentrates; it does not establish that intervening on those markers moves the endpoint. Nothing here is a recommendation and no dose or target is set (shared/safety.md).
