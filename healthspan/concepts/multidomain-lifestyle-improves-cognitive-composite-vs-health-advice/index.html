A structured supervised multidomain programme beats health advice on a cognitive composite — by 0.11 SD/year

LatAm-FINGERS randomised 1,065 at-risk adults aged 60–77 across 11 Latin American countries to a 2-year structured multidomain lifestyle intervention (SLI) with supervised support and monitoring, or a flexible lifestyle intervention (FLI) — health advice. Assessors were masked; participants and staff were not. Global cognitive composite trajectories improved in both arms — +0.31 SD/year (0.28–0.34) in SLI versus +0.20 SD/year (0.17–0.23) in FLI — a between-group difference of 0.11 SD per year (0.06–0.15, p<0.0001).

This is the first positive main effect on multidomain-lifestyle. Every prior multidomain result in the vault was either a subgroup interaction with no published main effect (multidomain-lifestyle-cognitive-impairment-benefit-overweight, from a trial stopped for futility) or a small bundled trial in frail 80-year-olds (multidomain-intervention-improves-frailty-and-function-frail-elders, n=47). LatAm-FINGERS is large, randomised, two years long, and answers the question it was asked.

Read the effect at its true size. The number in circulation is "55% greater improvement"; the number measured is 0.11 SD per year, against no stated anchor for clinical meaningfulness. The absolute-versus- relative lens in shared/study-appraisal.md fires here exactly as it fired on cocoa-flavanol-absolute-cvd-benefit-under-one-percent — and the vault applies it the same way when the conclusion is one it would like to believe.

Read the comparator precisely. The control arm was not untreated: it received health advice. So the estimate is structured-and-supervised versus advised, not lifestyle change versus nothing. That framing makes the result more interesting rather than less — the unsupervised version of the same advice is what most people actually get, and it improved less.

Read at its true strength. mixed, capped suggestive: retrospectively registered (NCT06492967 first submitted 2023-05-30 for a trial that started 2021-10-27); feasibility is a co-primary outcome; the endpoint is a surrogate composite, not incident MCI or dementia; both arms improved, so practice and engagement effects are unexcluded (latam-fingers-cognitive-benefit-is-differential-improvement-not-prevented-decline); dropout was differential (20.2% FLI vs 15.2% SLI, p=0.042) in the arm that did worse. Full text paywalled, so the intervention protocol and any power calculation are UNVERIFIED.

Status did not move. shared/evidence-model.md bars consolidating while a live rebuttal stands, and Look AHEAD's 10-year, 3,655-person cardiovascular null with a sign-flipping cognitive effect (multidomain-lifestyle-cognitive-impairment-harm-severe-obesity) is exactly that. Two randomised trials disagreeing about what a multidomain programme does over a decade is an unresolved literature, not a consolidating one.

For David: wrong population (60–77, elevated dementia risk) and wrong endpoint (a test score) to act on, and every component already sits in his regimen individually and better-sourced there. The transferable point is structural, not additive: the arm with supervision and monitoring outperformed the arm with the same advice and less support, which argues for a small stack sustained rather than a large one attempted.
