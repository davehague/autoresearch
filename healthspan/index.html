Morgan 2026 (epigenetic-clocks-adverse-childhood-experiences-meta-morgan-2026, *Molecular Psychiatry*, 17 studies/n=10,940, 8 clocks compared) found GrimAge and DunedinPACE track adverse childhood experiences most consistently — the same clock (DunedinPACE) this map already ranks first for mortality prediction, now also leading on a completely different validity test. Colhoun 2026 (semaglutide-select-mediation-analysis-colhoun-2026, *European Heart Journal*, SELECT mediation sub-study, n=17,604) found known biomarkers (weight, waist, hsCRP, HbA1c, lipids, BP, kidney markers) jointly explain only 31.4% (CI crossing both zero and 100%) of semaglutide's cardiovascular benefit — the mechanism remains largely unexplained. Cesaro 2026 (semaglutide-limb-events-meta-cesaro-2026, *European Journal of Preventive Cardiology*, 19 RCTs, n=51,557, appraised `reliable`) found semaglutide cuts major limb/PAD events by ~30% (OR 0.70, I²=0%) — a new outcome family and the map's cleanest safety-outcome signal yet. (5) ⚑ ischemic-preconditioning AUTO-COINED, flagged for human review.
