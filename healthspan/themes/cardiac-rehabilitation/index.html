Cardiac rehabilitation (exercise-based, post-CHD) — exercise

Status: emerging (2026-09-21) — auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. Coined from the 2026 update of Cochrane's standing cardiac-rehabilitation review (107 RCTs, 26,886 people with coronary heart disease). ⚠ Scope, stated once and binding on every future entry: this map is about exercise-based rehabilitation after a coronary heart disease diagnosis — mostly post-MI or post-revascularisation patients — a secondary-prevention lever, structurally different from every other exercise map in this vault, which study healthy or general-population adults. Nothing here should be read as evidence about exercise in people without diagnosed CHD; that evidence lives on zone-2-cardio, resistance-training, and siblings.

What we believe now

In people who already have coronary heart disease, a structured exercise programme reduces MI and hospital admissions at high/moderate certainty, and likely reduces mortality — but does not change how often clinicians revascularise. Against a no-exercise control at 6–12 months: MI RR 0.72 (high certainty), all-cause hospital admissions RR 0.65 (moderate certainty), all-cause mortality RR 0.86 (moderate certainty, interval just touching 1.00). CABG and PCI rates were unchanged (CABG null at high certainty). Quality-of-life gains were clinically meaningful across multiple domains, and every economic evaluation found it cost-effective.

Why emerging despite a Cochrane, GRADE-assessed, non-industry-funded, 26,886-person source. A single source — however strong — cannot carry a map past emerging under this vault's status-aggregation rule; emerging names "early, mechanistic, or single-group evidence" loosely, but the more precise read here (as with several other maps opened by one very strong meta-analysis, e.g. exercise-for-osteoarthritis) is a map with one large, high-quality source and no second independent line yet, not a map built on thin evidence. Flagged for human review on that basis, per docs/open-gaps-log.md convention for maps whose founding source outruns the mechanical Status rule.
