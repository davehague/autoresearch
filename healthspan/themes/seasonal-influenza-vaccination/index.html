Seasonal influenza vaccination (effectiveness vs. not vaccinated) — preventive / pharmacologic

Status: emerging (2026-09-19) — auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. Sibling to influenza-vaccine-dose, and deliberately distinct from it. That map holds one question — does a higher antigen dose beat a standard one, in adults 65+, among people who are already vaccinated — and its scope box explicitly excludes any vaccinated-versus-unvaccinated comparison. This map holds the question the dose map puts out of bounds: does the standard seasonal vaccine work, compared with not being vaccinated, and has that changed over time.

⚠ Scope, stated once and binding on every future entry: this map is about whether/how well the vaccine works, via test-negative-design (TND) studies that compare vaccinated to unvaccinated status. It says nothing about dose (see influenza-vaccine-dose for that), nothing about adjuvanted or recombinant formulations specifically, and nothing about any age group's decision to vaccinate — shared/safety.md applies in full, this summarises published evidence and recommends no vaccination decision to anyone.

What we believe now

The one founding source says the vaccine works, its performance shifted after the COVID-19 pandemic, and the shift does not point to one clean cause.

(1) The baseline claim, unsurprising but foundational. Pooling 85 TND studies, seasonal influenza vaccination shows real, positive effectiveness against medically-attended, lab-confirmed influenza across every strain category and age group examined — the numbers move (see below), but never toward "no effect."

(2) The pattern that makes this worth a map. Comparing pre-pandemic (2010/11–2019/20) to post-pandemic (2022/23–2024/25) seasons, effectiveness against A(H3N2) and influenza B rose in children/adolescents and working-age adults, while effectiveness against A(H1N1)pdm09 fell in working-age adults, and nothing moved significantly in adults 65+ — the age group influenza-vaccine-dose actually covers. A single "the pandemic changed population immunity, so the vaccine works better now" story does not fit a pattern this uneven, and the source's own authors decline to claim more than a hypothesis.

(3) Why emerging, not higher. One source, appraised mixed (the pre/post comparison is ecological — surveillance practices, antigenic match by season, and behavior changes could all contribute alongside or instead of the immunity hypothesis), capping every concept suggestive. shared/evidence-model.md does not let a suggestive-capped single source carry a map past emerging.

What would move it. A second independent synthesis; direct evidence on antigenic match by season (this source, like Zhao 2026 on the dose map, could not examine it); or a mechanistic study of population immunity directly (serology) rather than inferring it from the VE pattern.

Regimen implication: none. This map, like its sibling, summarises published evidence and makes no vaccination recommendation. shared/safety.md applies in full; no dose, schedule, or formulation choice is stated or implied anywhere on this map.
