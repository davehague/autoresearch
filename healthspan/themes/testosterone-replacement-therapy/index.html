Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) — metabolic

Status: emerging (2026-10-09) — ⚑ auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. The vault's third prescription/pharmacologic map, after glp-1 and orexin-antagonists. Coined from a single PROSPERO-registered meta-analysis of 31 RCTs, n=9,437, in middle-aged and older men with low or low-normal testosterone — a genuinely two-sided founding source, in the same shape glp-1 opened with: real, machine-measured metabolic/vascular benefit on one side, a real-but-unresolved cardiac-rhythm safety signal on the other. What we believe now

TRT measurably improves lipids and two vascular surrogates in hypogonadal men, and carries an arrhythmia signal its own authors call unresolved rather than safe.

What the founding meta-analysis measured, all of it machine-read rather than patient-reported. Across 31 RCTs (n=9,437, treatment duration 40 days–36 months) in men with low/low-normal testosterone, TRT significantly reduced total cholesterol (−10.20 mg/dL), triglycerides (−7.03 mg/dL) and LDL (−6.64 mg/dL), with no change in HDL. It prolonged exercise time to 1mm ST-segment depression on treadmill testing by 67.4 seconds, and reduced carotid intima-media thickness by 0.03mm over 12–36 months (trt-improves-lipid-panel-and-vascular-surrogates-in-hypogonadal-men). These are the kind of objectively-measured endpoints this vault's sleep and cognition literature almost never clears.

⚑ And the same review's safety side is the reason this map opens two-sided rather than clean. Among nonfatal cardiovascular events, an arrhythmia signal appeared: OR 1.58 (95% CI 1.21–2.05) across 6,598 participants — drawn from randomized, not merely observational, data, which is more credible than most pharmacologic safety signals this vault holds. The authors' own conclusion states the clinical significance of that signal "remains uncertain" — not resolved, not dismissed (trt-carries-an-unresolved-arrhythmia-safety-signal). ⚠ It is one of several secondary endpoints tested without a stated multiplicity correction, and the paper's blinding/ITT detail could not be verified from the retrieved abstract — real limits on how much weight to put on either side yet.

⚠ Why a map rather than silence. TRT is one of the most commonly considered hormone-optimization interventions for middle-aged men, alongside the compounded GLP-1 David is already weighing with his clinician (GOAL.md). A 31-RCT, PROSPERO-registered, machine-outcome meta-analysis clears a real evidentiary bar even at mixed reliability — this is not a single small trial or an industry-funded surrogate chase.

⚠ For David (GOAL.md: least work and least discomfort for maximum benefit, nothing that disrupts sleep, adherence-first): nothing here changes anything, and that is the honest answer. He carries no diagnosed hypogonadism, and this literature is in men with low or low-normal testosterone specifically. This is a prescription-only intervention; by rule it is never ranked into the daily protocol (shared/safety.md) — the same rule that confines the compounded GLP-1 to a clinician-only section. No dose, ester, delivery route, or duration is set here.
