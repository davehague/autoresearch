NAD precursors — NMN & nicotinamide riboside (NR) — supplements

Status: contested (2026-07-07) — coined this run from a human-greenlit sweep of the longevity trio. Opened contested rather than emerging because the anchor is a meta-analysis of RCTs (the top tier) landing null on the headline "restore aging muscle/function" claim: the NAD⁺-boosting mechanism is real, but the human functional payoff isn't showing up.

What we believe now

NMN and nicotinamide riboside reliably raise blood NAD⁺, but that biomarker movement does not translate into better muscle, strength, or physical function in older adults. A meta-analysis of 10 RCTs (6 NMN, 4 NR; ages ~61–83; 250–2000 mg/day, 3–24 weeks) found no effect on skeletal muscle index (MD −0.42 kg/m², ns), grip strength (ns), gait speed (ns), or chair-stand (ns), with the authors concluding current evidence "does not support" NMN/NR for muscle in adults over 60.

Two honest bounds keep this contested, not a flat fading:

  • (1) The mechanism and biomarker are genuine. These compounds do elevate NAD⁺; this is a biomarker-moves-but-outcome-doesn't story (the vault's recurring surrogate-vs-outcome discipline, cf. omega-3), not a debunk of the biochemistry.
  • (2) A plausible responder population exists — clinical deficiency, not healthy aging. The only positive signals were disease-specific (NR improved 6-minute walk +22.4 m in peripheral artery disease); benefit is most plausible in mitochondrial myopathy or severe NAD⁺ deficiency, i.e. not the healthy adult the supplement is marketed to.

Regimen implication. NAD precursors are a supplement (so, unlike rapamycin/metformin, they can be ranked) — but on this evidence they rank low: short trials, functional null, benefit confined to deficiency states. The vault's proven muscle levers are the boring ones — resistance-training, protein-target, and the one supplement with real strength/lean-mass RCT support, creatine-monohydrate.

⚠ The responder hypothesis has now been tested once, in a plausible responder population, and failed (new 2026-07-22). Bound (2) above held the map at contested rather than fading on the argument that a deficiency/disease population might respond where healthy adults do not. A phase-II RCT gave NR to adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment — a population with a real pathological process in which declining NAD⁺ is the specific proposed mechanism. Blood NAD⁺ doubled and cognition did not move (nicotinamide-riboside-raises-nad-without-cognitive-benefit). Forty-two completers over twelve weeks is a pilot, not a verdict, so Status holds contested — but the hope is now two hedges deep: maybe in a sicker population, over a longer horizon, on regional rather than global perfusion (nicotinamide-riboside-regional-cerebral-blood-flow-exploratory).

Through-line

  • 2026-07-07 — Ingested Prokopidis 2025 (J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle, meta-analysis of 10 RCTs): NMN and NR null on muscle mass, grip, gait speed, and chair-stand in adults >60; only narrow disease-specific benefit (PAD 6MWD). Coins the map at contested — mechanism real, human functional outcomes absent.

  • 2026-07-22 — Ingested Martens 2026 (Alzheimer's & Dementia, phase-II double-blind randomized placebo-controlled pilot, 12 weeks, n=42 completers — NR=22 / placebo=20 — adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment; NIA + NIGMS funded; PMID 42478598) as the second independent outcome domain in which NAD⁺ elevation buys nothing. Blood NAD⁺ increased ~2-fold — target engagement verified — and the primary cognitive outcome did not improve, nor did total cerebral blood flow or blood pressure (secondaries). Adherence comparable, no serious adverse events. An exploratory analysis hinted at raised regional CBF, particularly hippocampal. Appraised mixed (randomized, double-blind, independently funded, primary reported as null, and a null made informative by confirmed target engagement — against a 42-person pilot, 12 weeks, completer-based reporting with ITT status unverifiable, and an uncorrected exploratory region search after the planned CBF secondary failed). Capped suggestive; the exploratory CBF concept single-study. confirms. Status held contested — a pilot cannot fade a map, but it narrows the map's own responder escape hatch. Full text confirmed non-OA (Wiley, Europe PMC isOpenAccess=N, no PMCID); pdf_unavailable logged with rungs tried — NIH-funded, so retry for a PMC author manuscript.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • A methodological sibling to the vault's "hyped supplement, thin human outcomes" cluster. NAD precursors join taurine (animal-real, human-premise-null) and magnesium-l-threonate (single conflicted trial) — a coherence pattern the vault holds to surrogate-vs-outcome honesty, not a safety edge.
  • Contrast with creatine-monohydrate. Both are supplements marketed for muscle/aging; creatine has real strength/lean-mass RCT support, NAD precursors do not — a useful side-by-side when the regimen weighs supplement slots.
  • The muscle goal routes to the proven levers. The failure here reinforces resistance-training + protein-target as the evidence-based route to preserving aging muscle.
  • Global-ripple: no safety interaction found — NMN/NR are well tolerated; the finding is absence of benefit, not harm. No interaction with the sleep, exercise, or metabolic maps.
  • Open gap: no ingested RCT tests NAD precursors combined with exercise + adequate protein (the authors' own suggested next question), and no hard-outcome (mortality/healthspan-event) trial exists — logged, not fabricated. Narrowed and re-pointed 2026-07-22: the cognition axis is now covered at pilot scale (Martens 2026, null); what remains open there is an adequately powered, longer trial with regional cerebral blood flow pre-specified as a primary endpoint — the study the authors themselves ask for.
  • ⚑ Global-ripple re-run 2026-07-22 across all 41 maps — no safety interaction, and one sharpened pattern. NR was well tolerated with no serious adverse events; the finding remains absence of benefit, not harm, and no safety_flags are set. The vault's "biomarker moves, outcome doesn't" cluster gains its clearest member: NAD⁺ doubled and two outcome domains stayed flat. Compare omega-3 (an epigenetic-clock nudge with a cognition null), urolithin-a (mitophagy induction, entirely surrogate, entirely manufacturer-generated), taurine (animal-real, human-premise-null), and — from this same run — melatonin, where the biomarker story now moves in the harmful direction for one genotype. Target engagement is evidence the pill works. It is not evidence the pill helps. That sentence is the reusable lesson; this source is its cleanest demonstration, because nobody can argue the compound failed to arrive.
  • The urolithin-A contrast is now sharper (added 2026-07-09). urolithin-a has a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial showing mitophagy induction and downstream immune remodeling (urolithin-a-immune-cell-rejuvenation-rct) — where NMN/NR raise NAD⁺ and nothing functional follows. The comparison cuts both ways, and neither supplement earns a slot: UA's evidence is entirely manufacturer-generated and entirely surrogate, while NAD precursors are functionally null on a clean independent meta-analysis. Mechanism-with-surrogates and no-mechanism-payoff are different failures, not a ranking.
