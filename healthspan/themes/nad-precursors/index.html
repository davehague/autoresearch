NAD precursors — NMN & nicotinamide riboside (NR) — supplements

Status: contested (2026-07-07) — coined this run from a human-greenlit sweep of the longevity trio. Opened contested rather than emerging because the anchor is a meta-analysis of RCTs (the top tier) landing null on the headline "restore aging muscle/function" claim: the NAD⁺-boosting mechanism is real, but the human functional payoff isn't showing up.

What we believe now

NMN and nicotinamide riboside reliably raise blood NAD⁺, but that biomarker movement does not translate into better muscle, strength, or physical function in older adults. A meta-analysis of 10 RCTs (6 NMN, 4 NR; ages ~61–83; 250–2000 mg/day, 3–24 weeks) found no effect on skeletal muscle index (MD −0.42 kg/m², ns), grip strength (ns), gait speed (ns), or chair-stand (ns), with the authors concluding current evidence "does not support" NMN/NR for muscle in adults over 60.

Two honest bounds keep this contested, not a flat fading:

  • (1) The mechanism and biomarker are genuine. These compounds do elevate NAD⁺; this is a biomarker-moves-but-outcome-doesn't story (the vault's recurring surrogate-vs-outcome discipline, cf. omega-3), not a debunk of the biochemistry.
  • (2) A plausible responder population exists — clinical deficiency, not healthy aging. The only positive signals were disease-specific (NR improved 6-minute walk +22.4 m in peripheral artery disease); benefit is most plausible in mitochondrial myopathy or severe NAD⁺ deficiency, i.e. not the healthy adult the supplement is marketed to.

Regimen implication. NAD precursors are a supplement (so, unlike rapamycin/metformin, they can be ranked) — but on this evidence they rank low: short trials, functional null, benefit confined to deficiency states. The vault's proven muscle levers are the boring ones — resistance-training, protein-target, and the one supplement with real strength/lean-mass RCT support, creatine-monohydrate.

⚑ Updated 2026-07-30 — the count is now three outcome families, and the map's position is a sentence rather than a hedge. Conventional metabolic health joins muscle function and cognition on the null list: across 15 RCTs, no effect on weight, BMI, fasting glucose, HbA₁c or any lipid, with heterogeneity near zero and moderate GRADE certainty on the nulls themselves (nad-precursors-null-on-metabolic-outcomes). Three independent outcome domains, three flat results, from a compound whose target engagement is verified. What the same review adds that the map previously only asserted is safety: pooled across 10 trials and 383 people there is no excess of adverse events, serious adverse events, withdrawals, or liver-enzyme disturbance (nmn-short-term-safety-shows-no-excess-adverse-events) — bounded to short trials at studied doses, and explicitly not a claim about rare or long-term risk. The map's live thread is now a single low-certainty surrogate: a 2.43 mmHg diastolic blood-pressure reduction that survives every leave-one-out check and that this vault declines (nmn-diastolic-blood-pressure-signal-is-exploratory-and-low-certainty).

⚠ The responder hypothesis has now been tested once, in a plausible responder population, and failed (new 2026-07-22). Bound (2) above held the map at contested rather than fading on the argument that a deficiency/disease population might respond where healthy adults do not. A phase-II RCT gave NR to adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment — a population with a real pathological process in which declining NAD⁺ is the specific proposed mechanism. Blood NAD⁺ doubled and cognition did not move (nicotinamide-riboside-raises-nad-without-cognitive-benefit). Forty-two completers over twelve weeks is a pilot, not a verdict, so Status holds contested — but the hope is now two hedges deep: maybe in a sicker population, over a longer horizon, on regional rather than global perfusion (nicotinamide-riboside-regional-cerebral-blood-flow-exploratory).

Through-line

  • 2026-07-07 — Ingested Prokopidis 2025 (J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle, meta-analysis of 10 RCTs): NMN and NR null on muscle mass, grip, gait speed, and chair-stand in adults >60; only narrow disease-specific benefit (PAD 6MWD). Coins the map at contested — mechanism real, human functional outcomes absent.

  • 2026-07-22 — Ingested Martens 2026 (Alzheimer's & Dementia, phase-II double-blind randomized placebo-controlled pilot, 12 weeks, n=42 completers — NR=22 / placebo=20 — adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment; NIA + NIGMS funded; PMID 42478598) as the second independent outcome domain in which NAD⁺ elevation buys nothing. Blood NAD⁺ increased ~2-fold — target engagement verified — and the primary cognitive outcome did not improve, nor did total cerebral blood flow or blood pressure (secondaries). Adherence comparable, no serious adverse events. An exploratory analysis hinted at raised regional CBF, particularly hippocampal. Appraised mixed (randomized, double-blind, independently funded, primary reported as null, and a null made informative by confirmed target engagement — against a 42-person pilot, 12 weeks, completer-based reporting with ITT status unverifiable, and an uncorrected exploratory region search after the planned CBF secondary failed). Capped suggestive; the exploratory CBF concept single-study. confirms. Status held contested — a pilot cannot fade a map, but it narrows the map's own responder escape hatch. Full text confirmed non-OA (Wiley, Europe PMC isOpenAccess=N, no PMCID); pdf_unavailable logged with rungs tried — NIH-funded, so retry for a PMC author manuscript.

  • 2026-07-23 — The mechanism gets its best endorsement yet, and it changes nothing — Status held contested. Jacques 2026 (dna-methylation-aging-signatures-17-tissues-jacques-2026, Nature Aging, >15,000 methylomes across 17 tissues) found that most aging co-methylation networks are "fragile and resistant to beneficial modulation" — with one notable exception: a resilient module enriched for NAD⁺ biosynthesis via the nicotinamide-riboside salvage pathway, which the authors read as "supporting NAD⁺ as a potential therapeutic target in aging" (nad-methylation-module-modifiable-in-silico-only). The vault records it and declines it, by rule. The claim is mechanistic and computational — an in silico perturbation on cross-sectional bulk-tissue data whose own authors say "experimental validation is essential" — and shared/evidence-model.md's hierarchy puts it below the two human outcome results this map already holds, both null: Prokopidis 2025 (10 RCTs, muscle mass/strength/gait/chair-stand) and Martens 2026 (phase-II RCT, amnestic MCI, NAD⁺ doubled and cognition/CBF/BP unmoved). Appraised mixed, capped single-study, so it cannot move a map alone regardless. The map's position is not "the biology is nonsense" — it is the sharper claim that the biology is coherent, the target is engageable, and the humans still do not get better.

  • 2026-07-30 — A THIRD independent outcome family comes back flat, and the map finally gets a graded answer to "is it at least harmless?" — Status held contested (Yang 2026, Nutrients 18(14):2251, systematic review + meta-analysis of 15 parallel RCTs of oral NMN, PROSPERO CRD420261382497 registered before extraction, PRISMA 2020, six databases including CNKI and Wanfang with no language restriction — real coverage of an East-Asian trial base most English-only reviews miss; two independent screeners, Cochrane RoB 2, GRADE on 13 outcomes, leave-one-out on every key outcome, funnel plots/Egger/trim-and-fill where ≥10 comparisons; 250–2000 mg/day for 14 days to 24 weeks; no external funding, no conflicts, no vendor affiliation; CC BY OA PDF archived via MDPI's attachment host after the publisher edge returned 403). Every conventional metabolic outcome null, and the trials AGREE they are null: body weight −0.32 kg (95% CI −1.19 to 0.54), BMI +0.02 kg/m² (−0.14 to 0.18), fasting glucose +0.73 mg/dL (−1.36 to 2.82), HbA₁c +0.03% (−0.04 to 0.10), and HDL, LDL, total cholesterol and triglycerides all flat — with I² = 0% on most of them, and moderate GRADE certainty, the highest anything in the paper receives (nad-precursors-null-on-metabolic-outcomes). The HbA₁c interval is narrower than any change anyone would act on. So: muscle function null (10 RCTs), cognition null with NAD⁺ verifiably doubled, and now metabolic health null across 15 RCTs. ⚑ Three outcome families, three flat results, from a compound with a confirmed pharmacological effect — the sharpest available statement of this map's own thesis. ⚑ NEW AXIS — the map's tolerability assertion becomes a citation. This map has stated since 2026-07-22 that "NMN/NR are well tolerated" with nothing behind it, which shared/safety.md forbids. Now pooled across 10 trials and 383 people: any adverse event risk difference −0.008 (−0.061 to 0.045), I² = 0%; serious adverse events −0.003 (−0.036 to 0.031); withdrawals for adverse events −0.001 (−0.036 to 0.034); ALT −1.03 U/L (−2.51 to 0.44) and AST −0.24 U/L (−1.43 to 0.94), both I² = 0% (nmn-short-term-safety-shows-no-excess-adverse-events). ⚠ And the bound matters as much as the finding: the authors state these data "should not be interpreted as excluding potential rare, delayed, long-term, high-dose, or population-specific risks," serious events are graded very low certainty, and the Peto odds ratio interval (0.61, 0.18 to 2.14) spans a doubling. "No short-term signal in a small pool" is established. "Safe" is not. safety_flags: [] remains a finding, not an oversight. ⚑ One thread survives, and it is why the map does not fade tonight. Diastolic blood pressure −2.43 mmHg (−4.21 to −0.66), p = 0.007, I² = 0%, stable in every leave-one-out re-analysis (−2.75 to −2.02, all intervals below zero), directionally larger in over-60s and metabolic-risk groups. ⚠ The vault records it and declines it (nmn-diastolic-blood-pressure-signal-is-exploratory-and-low-certainty): the authors grade it low certainty and call it hypothesis-generating, systolic in the same people is null (−0.89, −3.83 to 2.05), it was not a primary endpoint in most contributing trials, it is one win among a dozen-plus uncorrected outcomes, and "most formal tests for subgroup differences were not statistically significant." A map that declined a DOUBLING of the target molecule cannot bank 2.4 mmHg on a secondary endpoint — that is consistency, not caution. Graded single-study. ⚠ Appraised mixed and the reasons are specific: the PROSPERO protocol was amended after registration to make safety the primary interpretive focus (disclosed and justified, and it conveniently makes the best-supported claim the headline); a 383-person pool for a safety question; conceded indirectness spanning healthy runners to hospitalised patients with acute kidney injury; three trials used MIB-626, a preparation the authors say may not be pharmacokinetically equivalent, pooled with free NMN anyway; and the abstract oversells HOMA-IR, which did not reach significance (−0.22, −0.55 to 0.11, very low certainty). Fatal flag #2 does not fire — every null is reported and GRADE is applied against the paper's own positive. Capped suggestive. confirms; Status contested HELD, by rule: a suggestive-capped source cannot move a Status (shared/evidence-model.md), and fading means superseded, debunked, or gone quiet — a compound with a leave-one-out-stable blood-pressure signal is not quiet. ⚠ Whether three null outcome families plus one low-certainty surrogate should now read fading is beyond what this source licenses; flagged for a human in docs/backlog.md.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • A methodological sibling to the vault's "hyped supplement, thin human outcomes" cluster. NAD precursors join taurine (animal-real, human-premise-null) and magnesium-l-threonate (single conflicted trial) — a coherence pattern the vault holds to surrogate-vs-outcome honesty, not a safety edge.
  • Contrast with creatine-monohydrate. Both are supplements marketed for muscle/aging; creatine has real strength/lean-mass RCT support, NAD precursors do not — a useful side-by-side when the regimen weighs supplement slots.
  • The muscle goal routes to the proven levers. The failure here reinforces resistance-training + protein-target as the evidence-based route to preserving aging muscle.
  • ⚑ Now the vault's clearest case of "engagement ≠ outcome" — a rule this map shares with two others (new 2026-07-23). NR demonstrably doubles blood NAD⁺ and moves nothing clinical. On the same run, omega-3 recorded DHA demonstrably entering cerebrospinal fluid with no cognitive or structural benefit over 24 months (dha-brain-delivery-without-cognitive-or-structural-benefit), joining creatine-cognition's ≥20 g brain-loading result (creatine-20g-raises-brain-creatine). Three molecules, three verified deliveries, three absent outcomes. It is the supplement-side twin of biological-age-clocks's predictor ≠ responder. A heuristic across literatures, not evidence about any one — no Status moved anywhere.
  • ⚠ And the same Nature Aging paper is the reason to be MORE careful here, not less. Jacques finds most aging methylation networks resist beneficial modulation (aging-methylation-networks-resist-beneficial-modulation); NAD⁺ is the exception that looks pushable. A pathway being movable is precisely what makes "target engagement verified, outcome absent" the informative result it is — this map's two nulls are not failures of delivery, and now there is a mechanistic reason to believe delivery was never the problem.
  • Global-ripple: no safety interaction found — NMN/NR are well tolerated; the finding is absence of benefit, not harm. No interaction with the sleep, exercise, or metabolic maps.
  • Open gap: no ingested RCT tests NAD precursors combined with exercise + adequate protein (the authors' own suggested next question), and no hard-outcome (mortality/healthspan-event) trial exists — logged, not fabricated. Narrowed and re-pointed 2026-07-22: the cognition axis is now covered at pilot scale (Martens 2026, null); what remains open there is an adequately powered, longer trial with regional cerebral blood flow pre-specified as a primary endpoint — the study the authors themselves ask for.
  • ⚑ Global-ripple re-run 2026-07-22 across all 41 maps — no safety interaction, and one sharpened pattern. NR was well tolerated with no serious adverse events; the finding remains absence of benefit, not harm, and no safety_flags are set. The vault's "biomarker moves, outcome doesn't" cluster gains its clearest member: NAD⁺ doubled and two outcome domains stayed flat. Compare omega-3 (an epigenetic-clock nudge with a cognition null), urolithin-a (mitophagy induction, entirely surrogate, entirely manufacturer-generated), taurine (animal-real, human-premise-null), and — from this same run — melatonin, where the biomarker story now moves in the harmful direction for one genotype. Target engagement is evidence the pill works. It is not evidence the pill helps. That sentence is the reusable lesson; this source is its cleanest demonstration, because nobody can argue the compound failed to arrive.
  • The urolithin-A contrast is now sharper (added 2026-07-09). urolithin-a has a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial showing mitophagy induction and downstream immune remodeling (urolithin-a-immune-cell-rejuvenation-rct) — where NMN/NR raise NAD⁺ and nothing functional follows. The comparison cuts both ways, and neither supplement earns a slot: UA's evidence is entirely manufacturer-generated and entirely surrogate, while NAD precursors are functionally null on a clean independent meta-analysis. Mechanism-with-surrogates and no-mechanism-payoff are different failures, not a ranking.
  • ⚑ 2026-07-30 — global ripple re-run across all 43 maps: no safety interaction, one framing edge, and one contrast the vault should keep. NMN's pooled safety profile shows no excess adverse events and no liver-enzyme signal (nmn-short-term-safety-shows-no-excess-adverse-events), so no safety_flags anywhere and no contraindication against any other map — the review reports no drug-interaction data at all, which is itself a logged absence rather than a clean bill. The one edge worth recording runs to magnesium-glycinate, which holds magnesium-null-on-insulin-resistance — a second supplement whose insulin-resistance signal failed to survive pooling, alongside this review's non-significant HOMA-IR (−0.22, −0.55 to 0.11, very low certainty) and its post-hoc-baseline-moderator sibling (magnesium-insulin-baseline-moderator-post-hoc). ⚠ A framing note, not graded: the two literatures share no data, no mechanism and no population, and neither Status moves. And the anti-purchase contrast the map should carry into any blood-pressure conversation: if lower blood pressure is the reason to buy NMN, resistance-training and stretching hold randomised evidence for the same endpoint at no cost (resistance-training-lowers-blood-pressure-older-adults, longitudinal-stretching-lowers-arterial-stiffness) — the same argument this map already makes for muscle, now available in a second outcome family.
  • ⚑ 2026-07-30 — the vault's engagement ≠ outcome cluster gains a member from a different map on the same night, and that is the point. vitamin-d ingested a 45-RCT meta-analysis finding small but significant improvements in LDL, systolic blood pressure, fasting glucose and HbA₁c on a map whose five hard-endpoint outcome families are all null (vitamin-d-small-improvements-in-cardiometabolic-risk-markers, vitamin-d-risk-factor-gains-should-have-shown-up-in-vital-and-did-not). Same shape as this map's: the measurement moves, the person does not. Read alongside biological-age-clocks's predictor ≠ responder rule, which gained its own third independent instance tonight (greenspace-epigenetic-age-signal-fails-in-the-largest-pooled-cohort). Three maps, one night, one lesson arriving from three unrelated literatures. A heuristic across fields, not evidence about any of them — no Status moved anywhere.
