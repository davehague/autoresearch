Sleep regularity — sleep

Status: emerging (2026-07-05) — a large, objective mortality signal, now corroborated and broadened by a systematic review across multiple outcome domains. Tier gating holds it below consolidating, but the honest reason is the observational ceiling (regularity can't be randomized), not weak or thin evidence. Behaviorally, this is a foundation lever that plausibly out-benefits several supplements in the vault.

What we believe now

How consistently you sleep (regular sleep-wake timing) predicts mortality more strongly than how long you sleep. In ~61,000 UK Biobank adults with wrist accelerometry, the most-regular sleepers had 20–48% lower all-cause mortality than the least-regular. A 2025 systematic review (Kalkanis) broadens the picture beyond that single dataset and beyond mortality: more-regular sleep tracks with better outcomes across mental, cardiometabolic, inflammatory, and cognitive domains too, and it frames regularity as a distinct sleep-hygiene target in its own right. The actionable core is low-cost and adherence-friendly — anchor consistent sleep and wake times — and it reframes sleep advice away from chasing a nightly duration number.

⚑ NEW 2026-07-24 — the cognitive limb of that broadening gets its first primary test with an objective exposure, and it does not hold. Paquet 2026 (MESA, four exams 2010–2024) reduced 39 objective and subjective sleep variables to 13 composites covering ~71% of variance and raced them against a global cognition composite. Cross-sectionally (n=1,628, mean age 68.0), five of thirteen were significant — and every effect is ~0.05 SD per SD: EEG power spectral density 0.065, spindle density 0.052, spindle–slow oscillation coupling 0.050, actigraphy sleep continuity 0.049, REM 0.047 (sleep-cognition-associations-small-and-cross-sectional). Longitudinally — the analysis that could speak to direction — sleep continuity AND variability tracked cognitive decline, and "significance decreased after multiple comparison adjustment." Sleep variability is this map's own exposure, and it did not survive (sleep-variability-cognitive-decline-signal-does-not-survive-correction). The mortality claim is untouched; the cognitive claim is now explicitly weaker than Kalkanis's summary implies. Two further honest notes. (a) Reverse causation is the more parsimonious reading of the cross-sectional hits: reduced spindle density, degraded spindle coupling, fragmented continuity and shortened REM are established features of preclinical neurodegeneration, so the brain that scores worse is expected to sleep worse. (b) Read symmetrically — the longitudinal result is an unsupported claim, not a refuted one; no surviving estimate or interval is reported, so the vault cannot say what effect size was excluded. And the measurement finding is the durable one, cutting both ways. If sleep needs thirteen dimensions, then every single-metric sleep result in this vault is a slice — including this map's own Sleep Regularity Index (sleep-is-a-multidimensional-construct-not-a-single-metric). The "count sleep once" rule survives, better justified; the claim that any one index is sleep does not.

Why still emerging, not consolidating: the base is now replicated across many independent cohorts (a systematic review, not one study), but it remains observational — sleep regularity is an exposure you essentially cannot randomize. Per the evidence model's deterministic tier gating, aggregating cohort evidence adds replication, not tier. So this is "about as strong as observational evidence gets here," held below consolidating by the ceiling of the design, not by any weakness in the signal.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-04 — Ingested Windred 2024 (cohort, n=60,977, objective accelerometry): Sleep Regularity Index top quintiles → 20–48% lower all-cause mortality; SRI out-predicted sleep duration. Status → emerging (strong but observational; single study in vault).

  • 2026-07-05 — Ingested Kalkanis 2025 (systematic review, 147 full texts, searched to Jul 2025): regular sleep associates with better outcomes across mortality, cardiometabolic, cognitive, and mental domains; positions regularity as a component of sleep hygiene. Confirms + broadens Windred; adds replication, not tier. Status stays emerging (observational ceiling).

  • 2026-07-24 — The map's cognitive limb gets a primary test and comes back fragile; the mortality claim is untouched; Status held emerging. Ingested Paquet 2026 (SLEEP, zsag187, Multi-Ethnic Study of Atherosclerosis, four examinations 2010–2024; Brigham and Women's / Harvard; NHLBI-funded cohort, no commercial sponsor identified; OUP subscription-gated, pdf_unavailable after the full ladder incl. two 403s on the advance-article endpoints). 39 objective and subjective sleep variables → PCA → 13 composites, ~71% of variance; outcome a global cognition composite (Digit Symbol Coding, Digit Span, CASI). Cross-sectional (n=1,628, mean age 68.0, 54.9% female), five composites significant, all at β ≈ 0.05 per SD: EEG power spectral density 0.065 (0.03–0.10), spindle density 0.052, spindle–slow oscillation coupling 0.050, actigraphy sleep continuity 0.049, REM 0.047 (sleep-cognition-associations-small-and-cross-sectional). Longitudinal: sleep continuity and variability tracked cognitive decline, and significance decreased after multiple-comparison adjustment (sleep-variability-cognitive-decline-signal-does-not-survive-correction). The vault's rule applied: a result that does not survive its own paper's correction is not a finding — the abstract's conclusion restates it as an association, and the correction governs. Read symmetrically: no surviving estimate is published, so this is an unsupported claim, not a refuted one; the longitudinal n and multiplicity method are UNVERIFIED. Reverse causation is the parsimonious read of the cross-sectional hits — every one of the five predictive composites is a sleep feature that preclinical neurodegeneration is known to damage. Three of the five are not levers at all (EEG microstructure); only continuity connects to behaviour. And the measurement finding is the durable one: sleep needs 13 dimensions, so no single index — including this map's SRI — is the construct (sleep-is-a-multidimensional-construct-not-a-single-metric). Appraised mixed (green: polysomnography + actigraphy rather than questionnaires, prospective multi-ethnic cohort, four exams, multiplicity correction applied and its unflattering consequence reported; red: significant results cross-sectional, β ≈ 0.05 inside confounding range, 5-of-13 screen shape, selected analytic subsample, no hard endpoint). Capped suggestive. Nothing for David: he sleeps adequately, the actionable lever is unchanged, and three of the five predictive dimensions are a reason NOT to buy a sleep tracker — nobody can practise spindle density.

  • 2026-07-25 — Ingested Duan 2026 (Obesity Reviews, systematic review, 30 studies — 13 RCTs + 17 quasi-experimental, n=10,944 adults with BMI ≥25, OSF-preregistered, RU-SATED-structured, duplicate screening and risk-of-bias, no meta-analysis possible; appraised mixed) — and the second day running that this map's primacy claim narrows without being refuted. A review that deliberately organised itself by all six dimensions of sleep health, on a highly measurable and randomisable outcome, found sleep regularity and sleep timing to be its thinnest cells: "limited evidence," directions only, firm conclusions explicitly refused, and both named by the authors as the priority for future research (sleep-regularity-and-timing-barely-studied-in-weight-loss). The reported directions favour this map — lower regularity associated with reduced weight or attenuated fat loss; later or more irregular timing with less favourable outcomes in some studies; earlier wake times with greater weight loss in three studies — but the authors state the study count is "insufficient to draw firm conclusions." Daytime alertness was flatly null. ⚠ Meanwhile the dimension this map ranks BELOW itself is the one with randomised evidence here: two RCTs manipulated sleep duration and both found reduced fat-mass loss (sleep-restriction-reduces-fat-loss-during-calorie-restriction, carried on sleep-duration). An absence of studies is not a refutation and no Status moves — regularity's mortality evidence is untouched — but read alongside yesterday's Paquet 2026, where this map's own exposure lost significance under the paper's own multiple-comparison correction (sleep-variability-cognitive-decline-signal-does-not-survive-correction), the honest position is that the mortality claim stands and the extrapolations from it are thinner than they looked a week ago. ⚠ Non-independence recorded: senior author Chaput also authored sleep-duration's 2026-07-23 source and one of this review's included studies. open-question; Status held emerging; sleep counted once. OA PDF archived.

  • 2026-08-03 — A fourteenth way to measure sleep, on 43,611 nights, and the mortality signal it finds appears only where people were referred to a sleep clinic. Status held emerging. Ingested Ganglberger 2026 (SLEEP zsag212, accepted manuscript published 2026-07-31; 43,611 overnight polysomnograms across two clinical cohorts — Massachusetts General Hospital and Beth Israel Deaconess — and one community cohort, MrOS; MGH/BIDMC group; appraised mixed, capped suggestive; ⚠ confirmed NON-OPEN-ACCESS — OUP subscription, landing page offers 24-hour access at USD $43, browser rung returned HTTP 500 on the advance-article PDF endpoint, pdf_unavailable recorded with the route named). The object. A Breathing Stability Index (BSI) derived from abdominal and thoracic effort envelopes — a continuous measure of respiratory-effort instability rather than an event count. It rose with apnea severity, varied sensibly with sleep stage (lowest in deep NREM, higher in light sleep and REM), and stayed associated with periodic breathing, AHI, arousal index and desaturation index in mutually adjusted models, so its incremental information was tested rather than asserted (breathing-stability-adds-signal-beyond-apnea-event-counts). ⚑ THE FINDING THIS MAP CARES ABOUT, AND IT IS ABOUT SELECTION. All-cause mortality, Cox-adjusted for age, sex and the comparator sleep metrics: MGH HR 1.38 per SD (1.23–1.54); BIDMC HR 1.10 (1.02–1.19) — a threefold difference in effect size between two academic sleep laboratories in the same city — and MrOS not significant (breathing-stability-mortality-signal-appears-only-in-sleep-clinic-cohorts). The only cohort not selected by referral is the one where the association disappears. Unstable nocturnal breathing is a known downstream feature of heart failure, obesity, opioid use, stroke and neurodegeneration — each of which causes both the instability and the death — and no lag analysis or early-death exclusion is described in anything retrievable. This is the same argument the vault applies to the long-sleep tail on sleep-duration (long-sleep-mortality-likely-reverse-causal), and it lands the same way: a severity marker inside a clinical population, not a demonstrated risk factor in the population at large. ⚑ The rich-panel pattern takes its third instance and holds at scale. biological-age-clocks raced 14 consensus biomarkers against mortality and 5 predicted. Paquet 2026 reduced 39 sleep variables to 13 composites and 5 predicted cross-sectionally at β ≈ 0.05, with effectively nothing surviving correction longitudinally. This source measures a new dimension on a corpus twenty-six times larger and returns a cohort-dependent association, cross-sectional disease discrimination of AUROC 0.509–0.642 — the bottom of that range is a coin flip — and a cognition result the authors themselves call "cohort dependent." The discipline stated on 2026-07-24 survives its biggest test: the number of things you can measure about a system is not evidence about how much of it you can change. ⚠ What is UNVERIFIED, which is most of it. Follow-up durations, event counts, covariate sets, sensitivity analyses, pre-registration, funding and conflicts — all behind the paywall. That last one is flagged deliberately rather than left implicit: the product of the paper is a named index computed from signals that consumer and clinical devices increasingly record, and the disclosure that would let a reader rule out a commercial interest is not readable. Recorded as unknown, never as absent. extends. Status held emerging — the map's mortality claim still rests where it did, on wrist accelerometry in an unselected community cohort, and BSI is not a lever: nobody can practise breathing stability.

A device that scores more of them measures more of a construct whose actionable part is still consistent sleep and wake timing. On GOAL.md's least-work, cost-as-tiebreaker terms: nothing to buy.   • ⚑ 2026-07-24 — global ripple: no interaction added. Walked every other map. Sleep regularity's only graded interaction remains caffeine-timing (below).   • ⚠ Interaction — caffeine-timing. Caffeine consumed too late in the day erodes this lever (reduces total sleep time, efficiency, and deep sleep — Gardiner 2023 meta-analysis). The interaction is flagged on caffeine-evening-cutoff-for-sleep (safety_flags: [interaction]) and noted in both maps. Fixable by timing (coffee ≥~8.8 h before bed); it gates naive co-recommendation of an evening stimulant against sleep regularity, but does not change either intervention's benefit score.
  • Outranks sleep-duration — same domain, and this map is the actionable one (added 2026-07-09). A 79-cohort meta-analysis now quantifies duration's mortality association: HR 1.14 for <7 h, HR 1.34 for ≥9 h (short-sleep-under-7h-mortality-risk, long-sleep-mortality-likely-reverse-causal) — from self-reported exposure, with reverse causation unexcluded on the long-sleep tail. Compare this map's 20–48% mortality spread, measured with objective wrist accelerometry, where the Sleep Regularity Index out-predicted duration. Bigger effect, better instrument, and an exposure you can actually act on. At regimen time these are one sleep-foundation lever, not two — anchor consistent sleep and wake times, and let ~7 hours be the floor that falls out of doing so. Do not double-count them.
  • Tentative synergy with magnesium-glycinate — evening magnesium may support sleep quality.
  • ⚠ Possible synergy with daily-steps — and it is the first sleep signal in the vault that moved a machine rather than a questionnaire (new 2026-07-12). A meta-analysis of 8 RCTs (n=683) found walking associated with +34.23 min total sleep time and −11.58 min wake after sleep onset, both device-measured, while subjective sleep quality did not significantly move (walking-improves-objective-sleep-duration-and-wakefulness, walking-subjective-sleep-quality-unresolved). Every wind-down item this cluster contains (l-theanine, chamomile, magnesium-glycinate, melatonin) has the opposite signature — self-report moves, instruments don't. Held as a watch item, not a graded synergy: the objective outcomes rest on 2–3 trials each, publication bias was untestable, and the trial populations included Alzheimer's and cancer patients. Not double-counted at regimen time; logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md.
  • Global-ripple: no contraindication found against the strength/nutrition seed maps. One thin watch item added 2026-07-09: high-dose (20 g/day) creatine listed sleep disturbance among mild adverse events in an uncontrolled pilot (creatine-20g-raises-brain-creatine); flagged over-cautiously on creatine-cognition and sleep-duration, and one more reason the vault's creatine answer stays at ~5 g/day.
  • ⚠ The exercise→sleep literature is a TREATMENT literature, confirmed at scale (new 2026-07-19). Liang 2026 (200 RCTs, n=23,523, Bayesian multilevel + MetaForest + SEM) found baseline sleep quality and population type are the main determinants of whether exercise improves sleep, with poor sleepers far more likely to reach a clinically important improvement — the authors' "benefit-on-demand" pattern (exercise-sleep-benefit-concentrated-in-poor-sleepers). This matters for this map because regularity is a foundation to PROTECT, not a deficit to repair — so exercise's sleep bonus, for someone already sleeping adequately, is expected to be small. The composition remains as recorded: the step target and structured aerobic work plausibly support regular timing, still logged, still not graded, and certainty on the whole exercise→sleep literature is VERY LOW even at n=23,523 (exercise-sleep-certainty-very-low-at-200-trials). Nothing is double-counted; this map keeps its lead on accelerometry-measured mortality evidence.
