Shingles vaccine (recombinant / Shingrix) — medical

Status: emerging (2026-08-28) — ⚑ auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. The vault's first map on an adult vaccine as a healthspan lever rather than as infection control. Coined from a single Nature Medicine natural experiment in 72,920 US adults aged 60+, and coined on design class rather than effect size: this is the observational design built specifically to defeat the bias that ruins vaccine-benefit studies, landing a 9% reduction in cardiovascular burden whose own robustness statistic says a modest unmeasured confounder could erase it. ⚑ Two maps were coined on the same night, the highest rate this vault has run at. git is the undo.

Sub-threads: none — one claim (a recombinant adult vaccine may have a cardiovascular benefit) resting on one source, with the mechanism question and the confounding question both being ways of interrogating that same claim rather than separable strands. [reviewed 2026-08-28 @ 1 bullets]

What we believe now

People aged 60+ who received the recombinant shingles vaccine rather than the older live one spent about 9% less of the following seven years living with cardiovascular disease. The design is the best this vault has seen on an observational question. The effect is small enough that the design is the only thing holding it up.

(1) The result. 36,460 propensity-matched pairs, 83 matched covariates, 7 years of follow-up. Composite of ischaemic heart disease, heart failure and ischaemic stroke: restricted mean time lost ratio 0.91 (95% CI 0.88–0.95), P<0.001 — 9% less of the window spent living with the diagnosis, or 0.8% absolute difference in cumulative incidence (the-recombinant-shingles-vaccine-tracks-with-fewer-cardiovascular-events, suggestive). Ischaemic heart disease 0.90 (0.87–0.94), heart failure 0.88 (0.83–0.93), atrial fibrillation 0.93 (0.88–0.98). Ischaemic stroke reached significance in males only (0.88, 0.78–0.98); null for myocarditis, peripheral arterial disease, haemorrhagic stroke, transient ischaemic attack and non-cardiovascular death. The effect appeared in the first half of follow-up and faded in the second.

(2) Why the design matters more than the number. In October 2017 the US switched rapidly from the live attenuated shingles vaccine to the recombinant one. The study compares people vaccinated April–September 2017 (98.6% live) with people vaccinated April–September 2018 (93.5% recombinant) — same months, one year apart. ⚑ Both groups chose to be vaccinated, which removes healthy-vaccinee bias by construction rather than by adjustment (comparing-vaccinated-people-to-other-vaccinated-people-removes-the-healthy-vaccinee-bias, suggestive). The paper demonstrates the point against itself: analysed the conventional way — shingles-vaccine recipients against influenza or tetanus recipients — the same data produce larger effects, which the authors attribute to the bias their main design avoids.

Four separate attempts to break the result, all reported, all null: a pre-specified negative control outcome (painful conditions requiring care but unrelated to vaccination); a tetanus-vaccine secular-trend control in cohorts ten times larger (RMTL 1.01, 0.99–1.03); the year before vaccination (0.99, 0.92–1.06); and truncation to the pre-COVID period. Plus competing-risk analysis, follow-up-time matching, and covariate windows re-run at 1, 2, 5 and 10 years (all giving 0.94).

⚑ (3) And the counterweight the map carries in the same breath: the E-value is 1.42. An unmeasured factor 1.42 times more common in one cohort and 1.42-fold linked to heart disease erases the finding (an-e-value-of-1-4-is-a-low-bar-for-an-unmeasured-confounder, suggestive). Socioeconomic status — education, income, occupation, neighbourhood — is not in electronic health records at all, and the transition followed an updated ACIP recommendation that the authors concede "might have influenced who got vaccinated." The design excludes several whole classes of confounder; it does not exclude an ordinary one of the right size.

(4) The mechanism is a hypothesis, and the obvious one is argued down by the authors themselves. Fewer shingles cases cannot explain it: the absolute shingles difference was under 0.5% against a 1.5% reduction in ischaemic heart disease; infection-prevention benefit would accumulate rather than diverge early and reconverge; and a separate natural experiment found the live vaccine had no cardiovascular effect versus no vaccine. Their preferred alternative is the AS01 adjuvant — unique to the recombinant product — reprogramming monocytes and lowering interleukin-6 responses, with IL-6 inhibition causally linked to lower cardiovascular risk (the-shingles-cardiovascular-signal-is-probably-not-about-preventing-shingles, single-study). ⚠ No immune marker was measured in this study. If the adjuvant story is right, the finding is not about shingles at all.

Why this map exists, and the argument against it

⚑ Scored against the four-part coining bar (beetroot-juice, 2026-08-02): isolating contrast PASS (natural experiment, vaccinated versus vaccinated, four null controls); machine-measured outcome PASS (exposure is a vaccination record, outcomes are ICD-10-coded hard events — nothing self-reported on either side, which is the failure that sank ultra-processed-food to ~1 of 4); low risk of bias PARTIAL (one observational study, no pre-registration, small effect, E-value 1.42, socioeconomic status unmeasured); pre-specified dose PARTIAL (no protocol registration, but a pre-specified primary composite and a negative control inherited unchanged from the group's prior paper, and a licensed product rather than a post-hoc threshold). Roughly 2.5 to 3 of 4.

⚑ The design-class precedent is exact. alcohol was coined on 2026-08-26 from a Mendelian randomisation review — an observational design whose whole value is being built to get at causation. A natural experiment is the same category of object. The 2026-08-27 gaps entry drew this distinction itself when it argued alcohol was not a precedent for ultra-processed-food, "because that map was founded on a Mendelian randomisation review, a design built to get at causation, and nothing here has any equivalent." This one has the equivalent. And like hearing-aids coined the same night, it does not invoke the disputed structural-homelessness override.

⚠ The case against, stated so a human can act on it. (a) It is one study — every other single-source coining in this vault rested on a synthesis; this is one analysis of one health-record database. (b) The effect is in confounding range. 9% relative, 0.8% absolute, E-value 1.42 — the Universal appraisal lens's effect-size flag fires cleanly, and this vault has refused candidates on that flag before. (c) influenza-vaccine-dose already exists, so adult vaccination is not literally homeless — but that map asks whether a higher-dose flu vaccine beats a standard one, a within-product dose question, and filing a shingles cardiovascular finding there would repeat the drawer-drift problem docs/open-gaps-log.md has already flagged twice on anthocyanins. (d) The corresponding author consults for GSK, which makes the exposure, and a co-author co-directs an Oxford-GSK institute; the statement that GSK was unaware until acceptance is the authors' own and unverifiable. One git revert removes the map.

What would move this

A randomised trial, which the authors explicitly call for and which is the only thing that settles it. Failing that: replication of the same natural experiment in a different health system, ideally one that captures socioeconomic status; the same design applied to the group's prior dementia finding, so the two outcomes rise or fall together; or a mechanistic study measuring interleukin-6 and monocyte phenotype after AS01, which would convert the hypothesis in (4) into evidence. ⚠ A larger E-value is what this claim most needs and is the one thing more data alone will not supply — it needs better-measured data, not more of it.
