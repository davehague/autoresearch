Magnesium L-threonate — supplements

Status: emerging (2026-07-05) — auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. A single, industry-adjacent RCT of the branded Magtein® ingredient shows a cognition (+ sleep) benefit in young adults. Opened at the bottom of the ladder: one trial, young cohort, strong commercial conflict. A lever distinct from magnesium glycinate.

What we believe now

Magnesium L-threonate is a distinct magnesium compound from the glycinate form already in the vault — formulated and marketed for brain penetration and cognition, not primarily for sleep. The only vault evidence is one 6-week RCT (Lopresti 2026, n=100, adults 18–45 with poor sleep, 2 g/day Magtein®): improved overall cognitive performance (NIH Total Cognition Composite, p=0.043; working & episodic memory), a ~7.5-year lower estimated "cognitive brain age," faster reaction time (p=0.031), plus secondary gains in sleep-related impairment and stress physiology (HR down, HRV up).

Read it honestly and low: a single trial in a young cohort (says little about older-adult healthspan), with a strong industry-conflict flag — a branded ingredient, a sponsor-funded research group (Lopresti & Smith, who also co-authored the vault's Schuster 2025 glycinate trial), and several borderline p-values across many endpoints (multiple-comparisons risk). This is a lever to watch, not adopt. No invented dose — 2 g/day Magtein® is the studied amount, not a recommendation (shared/safety.md).

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Sibling of magnesium-glycinate — same element, different form and target. Glycinate carries a small-effect sleep RCT; L-threonate carries a single cognition RCT. At regimen time these are candidate forms of one magnesium lever, not two independent wins — do not double-count magnesium.
  • ⚠ Interaction watch — inherits the oral-magnesium ↔ oral-drug absorption OPEN GAP. Oral magnesium can reduce absorption of some oral medications (e.g. certain antibiotics, thyroid medication, bisphosphonates) by chelation; the standard fix is to separate dosing by hours. This is a property of magnesium, so it applies to the L-threonate form too. Still logged as an open gap (no vault-quality source cited yet), not fabricated into a graded safety_flags concept — mirrored from magnesium-glycinate.
  • Cognition-lever landscape (not an interaction). This single-RCT cognition signal sits alongside a high-tier cognition null for omega-3 and a suggestive cognition thread for creatine-monohydrate — three compounds, one outcome, three verdicts. A framing note, not a safety edge.
  • Global-ripple: no safety interaction with other vault interventions found beyond the shared magnesium absorption gap above.
