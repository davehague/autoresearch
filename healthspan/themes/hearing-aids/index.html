Hearing aids — sensory

Status: emerging (2026-08-28) — ⚑ auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. Coined from the 2026 Cochrane update on hearing aids for mild-to-moderate hearing loss (16 randomised trials, 2,261 adults), and coined on a precondition this vault wrote for itself six days earlier. ⚑ The map opens at emerging despite holding a moderate-certainty, very large effect — because the large effect is on the outcome closest to the device, and the outcome the healthspan argument actually needs is null or very uncertain. git is the undo.

Sub-threads: none — one argument with two halves that cannot be separated (a device improves the sense it restores; whether restoring the sense protects the brain is unresolved), and both halves come from the same 16 trials and the same Cochrane synthesis. Splitting them would put the evidence for a claim and the evidence against its extension into different files. [reviewed 2026-08-28 @ 1 bullets]

What we believe now

Hearing aids reliably and substantially reduce self-reported hearing difficulty. Whether they do anything for cognition, loneliness or mood is, after sixteen randomised trials, still unanswered — and the trial that would answer it may not be ethical to run.

(1) The claim that holds. Pooling 8 trials and 1,683 people, hearing aids improved hearing-specific health-related quality of life — how much hearing loss stops you participating in your own life — by a standardised mean difference of −1.25 (95% CI −1.63 to −0.87) at GRADE moderate certainty, which Cochrane back-translates as roughly a 27-point improvement on the 0–100 Hearing Handicap Inventory for the Elderly (hearing-aids-produce-a-large-improvement-in-hearing-specific-quality-of-life, strong). Listening ability moved comparably (−1.28, −2.41 to −0.15). ⚑ This is the largest effect size in this vault. General quality of life — not hearing-specific, just life — moved about a fifth as much (−0.27, −0.46 to −0.09, low certainty). The narrower the question, the bigger the answer.

(2) The claim that does not hold, and it is the reason hearing is in the healthspan conversation at all. Hearing loss is widely named the largest modifiable dementia risk factor, so the inference is irresistible: treat it, protect the brain. The prioritised cognitive outcome — working memory — comes out at SMD +0.51 with a 95% confidence interval of −0.21 to +1.23, from 3 trials and 910 people, at GRADE very low certainty after a three-level downgrade (the-hearing-aid-cognition-evidence-is-uncertain-rather-than-positive, strong). ⚑ That interval is compatible with a large benefit and with a moderate harm at the same time. And the six cognitive subdomains that are precise are flat — memory +0.09 (−0.04 to 0.21), set shifting −0.02, language +0.01, processing speed +0.05, global screening +0.07, each on 850 to 1,050 people. Where this literature is precise it finds nothing; where it finds something it is imprecise.

(3) The social pathway is not there either. Loneliness: −0.12 (−0.25 to 0.01) across 907 people — about one point on a twenty-item loneliness scale (hearing-aids-did-not-move-loneliness, strong). Depression −0.09 (−0.56 to 0.38). Anxiety comes from one trial of 61 people; combined anxiety-and-depression from one trial of 22. ⚑ The device changes self-rated hearing by an enormous margin and self-rated loneliness by about a point, in the same people. Whatever hearing aids do, it does not appear to travel through the social-connection route the healthspan argument assumes.

(4) Harms are real but small and actively sought. Six trials (1,186 people) monitored adverse effects; one trial reported two events — one person stopped because the device hurt, one because of ear dryness needing treatment. No trial reported noise-induced hearing loss. Certainty on pain: very low, downgraded for extreme imprecision, which here means "almost nothing happened and we cannot tell you how reliably."

⚑ (5) The methodological finding that outlives this map. Five trials fitted placebo hearing aids — visually identical devices providing no amplification. In the two Brewster trials, more than 80% of participants correctly guessed their allocation anyway, and Cochrane rated both high risk of performance bias as a result. Exactly one trial of sixteen (Adrait 2017) was low risk for both performance and detection bias (placebo-hearing-aids-failed-to-blind-more-than-eighty-percent-of-participants, strong). The pattern this produces is the map in miniature: the outcomes that could not be blinded moved enormously, and the objectively measured ones did not move at all.

Why this map exists, and the argument against it

⚑ Scored against the four-part coining bar (beetroot-juice, 2026-08-02): isolating contrast PASS (16 randomised trials, allocation concealment adequate in 7, five with placebo devices); machine-measured outcome FAIL on the outcome that carries the effect (the −1.25 is a questionnaire answered by unblinded people; the machine-measured cognitive tests are the ones that found nothing); low risk of bias PARTIAL (one of 16 low risk for blinding — but allocation concealment, attrition and selective reporting are mostly low risk, and 10 of the 13 post-2017 trials registered protocols, which is a different picture from a corpus at high risk in every domain); pre-specified dose PASS in the form this question admits (PROSPERO CRD42016043834, registered 2015 — eleven years before publication — with prioritised outcomes and subdomains declared in advance; the "dose" is a fitted regulated device). Roughly 2.5 of 4.

⚑ It clears the written bar without invoking the disputed structural-homelessness override, and that matters: docs/backlog.md has carried since 2026-08-07 the unresolved question of whether that override should exist, and the 2026-08-27 entry escalated it as "an unwritten rule the bar cannot audit" after it permitted a coining at ~1 of 4. Two supporting grounds. ⚑ The vault named this precondition itself. On 2026-08-22, ingesting a secondary analysis of the ACHIEVE trial, it wrote: "The vault has no hearing map and this source does not coin one — one secondary analysis of one trial's measurement artefact is not an intervention map, and the hearing question would need the parent trial ingested on its own terms" (cognitive-retest-practice-effects-achieve-secondary-wang-2026). Tonight the parent trial arrives inside the whole randomised literature, graded by Cochrane. And before tonight a 712-concept vault holding maps for chamomile, molecular hydrogen and potassium citrate had no hearing content at all.

⚠ The case against, stated so a human can act on it. (a) The moderate-certainty effect is close to definitional — a device that amplifies sound reduces self-reported difficulty hearing — so the map's strongest number may not be telling anyone anything they did not know. (b) Every author's institutional funding runs one way: four of six authors' institutions have taken money from Sonova, WSAudiology, Cochlear or Specsavers, and one author is separately salaried by Sonova. All declare no personal payments, and the review's central finding is unflattering to that industry — which is why it was still appraised reliable — but the direction is recorded. (c) David has no hearing-loss finding in GOAL.md, so this map currently describes a lever he cannot pull. One git revert removes it.

⚑ The ceiling on this literature may be permanent

Cochrane's own closing recommendation: "Longer-term, placebo-controlled studies may be more sensitive to potential effects on mental health and cognition, but it may not be ethically justifiable to withhold hearing aids long term." The trial that would settle the dementia question is one nobody may be allowed to run. That is a different object from an ordinary research gap, and this map records it as a structural limit: the cognitive question here may never move from emerging on randomised evidence alone. What would move it anyway: a long trial using an active comparator rather than withholding (different device settings, delayed fitting with a declared crossover), or an observational design with an instrument strong enough to carry causal weight — the shape shingles-vaccine was coined on tonight.
