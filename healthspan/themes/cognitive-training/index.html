Cognitive training — stress & mental

Status: emerging (2026-08-08) — ⚑ auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. Coined by an OSF-preregistered network meta-analysis of 20 RCTs (n = 1,358, adults ≥65) on digital cognitive training and verbal fluency — and, more to the point, by the six graded concept notes this vault had already accumulated about cognitive training with nowhere to put them. Before tonight those notes were scattered across resistance-training-programming (where they had landed because the outcome happened to be gait) and multidomain-lifestyle (where they had landed because cognitive training happened to be one arm of a bundle). Neither map is about cognitive training, and a reader looking for what the vault thinks of brain training would have found nothing. Opened emerging for the ordinary reason: one mixed-appraised source caps every concept at suggestive, and a suggestive-capped source cannot carry a map further.

⚠ THIS MAP REVERSES A REFUSAL, AND THE REVERSAL IS THE FIRST THING A REVIEWER SHOULD CHECK. On 2026-08-03 the vault explicitly declined to coin cognitive-training — its fifteenth declined candidate — and re-affirmed the refusal on 2026-08-05. Both refusals are recorded in resistance-training-programming. The stated grounds were: "one weakly-bounded task-specific positive, three nulls, no falls outcome anywhere in the corpus, and balance resting on 5 trials and 3 instruments."

Why the reversal is not simply the vault changing its mind under pressure. Both refusals judged cognitive training as a balance-and-falls intervention — which is the outcome family resistance-training-programming owns, and where cognitive training was correctly found null. This map is coined on its native outcome instead: cognition. Tonight's source is 20 RCTs and 1,358 participants, preregistered, on a cognitive endpoint — a different corpus asking a different question, and the third cognitive-training source the vault holds. Every ground given for the refusal is still true of the falls literature, and none of it was ever a claim about cognition.

⚠ What has NOT changed, and what would justify reverting this: the evidence here is one mixed source whose interval nearly contains zero and whose winning arm is confounded with social contact. If a reviewer judges that a map should be coined on the strength of its evidence rather than on the size of its orphaned concept cluster, this map should be reverted — six homeless notes are an argument about vault hygiene, not about whether brain training works. Git is the undo.

Sub-threads: none — one argument (cognitive training reliably moves the thing it trains and has not been shown to move anything else, and its best-performing arms are the ones that added something other than software); the transfer question and the comparator question are two faces of that single claim rather than separable strands. What we believe now

⚑ NEW 2026-08-21 — THE MAP'S FOUNDING CLAIM SURVIVES THE ONE DESIGN THAT COULD HAVE KILLED IT, AND THIS IS THE STRONGEST NIGHT THIS MAP HAS HAD. Every prior source on this map had the same escape hatch available: the transfer failure might be an artefact of a weak comparator — a wait-list, a passive control, an unblinded assessor, an active-versus-nothing design. Tonight's source removes all of them. Wang 2026 pooled 23 randomised trials and 135 effect sizes in HEALTHY adults, in which both arms did the identical cognitive training and the only variable was whether the weak scalp current — transcranial direct current stimulation — was real or a sham. Participants were genuinely blinded. The comparator problem does not exist in this design.

And the map's shape appears anyway. On the task that was drilled: Hedges' g = 0.424 (95% CI 0.180 to 0.667), significant. On untrained working-memory tests — actual transfer — g = 0.130 (−0.040 to 0.301), not significant. Overall g = 0.191 (0.062 to 0.320) (brain-stimulation-added-to-cognitive-training-moves-the-trained-task-not-transfer).

⚠ The honest limit, supplied by the authors before anyone else could: the direct contrast between trained and untrained outcomes was NOT significant (p = 0.076), and they write that "the current evidence does not establish a reliable difference between outcome types." So this is the same pattern arriving from a third literature, not a demonstrated dissociation — and the map records it at that strength. The overall effect is also genuinely positive and survived every robustness check: three correlation assumptions, a high-risk-entry exclusion, and two Egger-type tests, both null.

⚑ The moderator result is the quietly damaging one. tDCS is sold on a mechanism — stimulate the prefrontal cortex while it works a memory task and the concurrent activity consolidates. That predicts a bigger effect for working-memory training and for stimulation delivered during training. Neither held: training type p = 0.990, stimulation timing p = 0.559, age group p = 0.705 (the-tdcs-benefit-did-not-depend-on-what-was-being-trained). ⚠ And this map applies its own rule to that result: 2 studies used non-working-memory training and 5 used non-online stimulation, so these are tests that could not have succeeded — a failure to find the expected pattern, not a demonstration of its absence.

⚠ The base is uncertain rather than bad: 34 of 40 study-outcome entries were rated "some concerns" and exactly ONE was low risk of bias; the review was not prospectively registered, no protocol was archived, and screening was not fully duplicated. The "intervention" spans 1–20 sessions at 1–2 mA across several montages under one label, with no dose-response fitted. Status HELD emerging; concepts capped suggestive.

⚑ A candidate map — brain-stimulation / tdcs — was scored against the four-part coining bar and REFUSED at ~2 of 4 (see the through-line and docs/open-gaps-log.md). Filed here instead, because this map owns exactly this question.

⚑ NEW 2026-08-20 — THE PHYSICAL-EXERCISE ARM OF THE SAME TRANSFER QUESTION ARRIVES, AND IT IS BOUNDED THE SAME WAY. Hu Q 2026 pooled 51 RCTs and 3,673 adults ≥60 on digitally delivered physical exercise, and — decisively for this map — restricted eligibility to UNTRAINED cognitive outcomes. A trial could not qualify by measuring the task it drilled. That is the map's founding worry, designed out of the review. The answer that survives a fair comparator is small: SMD 0.32 for exergames over conventional exercise on global cognition, at LOW certainty — the single best certainty rating in that network, where the other fourteen comparisons are VERY LOW. ⚠ This neither rescues nor refutes the map's claim about cognitive training, because the intervention is different. It sits beside it as the physical-exercise arm of the same question, bounded identically: real-looking point estimates, unblindable outcomes, prediction intervals that cross zero, and certainty at the floor. ⚑ What it adds that is genuinely new is a direction of travel: the arms that stack technology on technology lose, and the arms with a human in them win. VR-plus-cognitive-training finished last and was significantly inferior to a videoconference exercise class for memory; a console game on a television and a person on Zoom are the only two categories that beat conventional exercise at all. That is the same shape as the-top-ranked-cognitive-training-arm-cannot-separate-software-from-social-contact, arriving from a different literature.

The trained task moves. Transfer is where every one of these studies gets thin, and nobody has shown it cleanly. That is the through-line the vault had already assembled without noticing, and tonight's source fits it exactly rather than contradicting it.

⚑ The headline result and the thing it actually shows are different results. Hu 2026 ranks digital cognitive-training regimens on verbal fluency — a good outcome choice, because fluency is standardised, well-normed, and hard to train directly with a puzzle game, so moving it is closer to a test of transfer than of practice. The winner: computerised cognitive training combined with social/language activities, SMD 0.47 (95% CI 0.02 to 0.93), SUCRA 0.84, against passive control (digital-cognitive-training-verbal-fluency-benefit-clears-zero-by-a-hair).

So the best digital brain-training regimen is the one with a person bolted onto it. No arm isolates the computerised component from the structured human contact (the-top-ranked-cognitive-training-arm-cannot-separate-software-from-social-contact) — and the vault holds independent, higher-graded evidence that the confounder is itself a real lever: low social contact carries the top credibility grade against incident dementia in low-social-contact-dementia-risk-graded-convincing. There is a second problem stacked on the first: the outcome is verbal fluency and the winning arm added language activities. A language-enriched intervention outperforming on a language outcome is what a practice effect looks like from outside.

⚠ And the interval clears zero by 0.02. Against a passive comparator, in an intervention nobody can be blinded to, on a timed effortful task that responds to motivation and to having been attended to. The vault has already refused to read this comparator as informative twice in this literature (cogtrail-executive-benefit-rests-on-a-waiting-list-comparator, exercise-improves-cognition-in-mci-against-wait-list-controls).

The benefit concentrates where the deficit is — larger in participants with cognitive impairment and with lower education (digital-cognitive-training-benefit-concentrates-in-impairment-and-low-education). That is the vault's most-repeated shape, and here it points the unhelpful way: a result that concentrates in impairment transfers worse, not better, to a healthy adult.

What the vault already held, and why it converges. Ophey 2026 (CogTrail, prodromal Lewy body disease) found cognitive training moved only the domain it trained in, across twelve pre-specified outcomes (cognitive-training-moves-only-the-domain-it-trains-in-prodromal-lewy-body-disease) — and its one positive rested on a waiting-list comparator. Li 2026 found cognitive training improved only dual-task walking speed, with the single-task gait signal vanishing in the high-quality trials (cognitive-training-improves-only-dual-task-walking-speed, cognitive-training-gait-signal-vanishes-in-high-quality-trials). Three independent syntheses, three versions of the same sentence: the effect is real where it is trained and evaporates at the edges.

Why emerging, and what would move it. One mixed source of its own, capped at suggestive, whose headline interval nearly contains zero and whose winning arm is confounded with a different intervention. What would move it: a trial against an active, time-and-attention-and-social-contact-matched sham, reporting a clinically anchored change (not a standardised one) on an outcome the training did not directly drill, in cognitively healthy older adults.

What this map does not contain and will not invent. ⚠ No protocol and no dose. Hu 2026's ">20 hours" is a meta-regression moderator across heterogeneous trials, not a prescription, and shared/safety.md forbids synthesising one. ⚠ No product recommendation. The commercial brain-training market is large and this vault has graded none of it.
