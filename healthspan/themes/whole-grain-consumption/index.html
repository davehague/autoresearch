Whole-grain consumption — nutrition

Status: emerging (2026-11-14) — ⚑ auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. The vault's largest dedicated randomized-trial base for any single food category: 87 trials, 101 publications, pre-registered and independently funded. Opened at emerging despite the unusually strong design quality, because every outcome measured is a cardiometabolic risk-factor surrogate — no trial reports a cardiovascular event, cancer incidence, or death.

What we believe now

Each 48 g/day increment of whole-grain intake (dry weight; ≈90 g / 3 servings fresh weight) tracks with small, dose-responsive reductions in body weight, waist circumference, total cholesterol and IL-6 (GRADE high certainty), and in LDL-C, triglycerides, fasting glucose and systolic blood pressure (GRADE moderate). Effects concentrate at 60–100 g/day, with data too sparse above that to support a higher-dose claim. Why emerging, not higher, despite the design quality: shared/evidence-model.md caps Status by outcome reach as well as by tier — eight risk-factor markers moving together is a strong mechanistic case, not a demonstrated reduction in cardiovascular events or mortality, and this paper does not supply that evidence.
