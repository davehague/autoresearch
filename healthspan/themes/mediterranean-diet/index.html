Mediterranean diet — nutrition

Status: emerging (2026-07-08) — auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. The vault's second whole-food dietary-pattern lever (after fermented-foods) — and a far larger, more consistent base. Coined by the systematic review + meta-analysis underpinning Italy's national Mediterranean-diet guideline: 54 cohorts, 1.8M adults, a dose-responsive mortality association. Opened emerging by the observational / self-report ceiling (diet can't be randomized in these cohorts), not by weak evidence.

What we believe now

Higher adherence to a Mediterranean dietary pattern tracks with lower all-cause mortality — the largest, most consistent "what to eat" signal in the vault, but a pattern, not a pill. Pooling 54 cohort studies (1,833,267 adults, 346,034 deaths, 2–60 y follow-up), each 1-point rise in a validated adherence score carried all-cause mortality RR 0.96 (95% CI 0.95–0.97) — small per step but dose-responsive, compounding across the ~9-point index range to a materially lower mortality at high vs low adherence (consistent with prior high-vs-low estimates of ~20–25%). GRADE certainty moderate, and — unusually for nutrition — the pattern has RCT support in the wider literature (PREDIMED; not this meta), which is why it reads as foundation-quality rather than just another food-frequency correlation.

The tension worth keeping visible (why emerging, capped suggestive): this specific evidence is all observational — self-reported diet, heterogeneous scoring, plausible healthy-user/adherer confounding, and a small per-step effect (RR 0.96) squarely in the range confounding can move. It's the strongest dietary-pattern case the vault has, but held at the same honest ceiling as daily-steps and fermented-foods: real, large, consistent — and observational.

⚑ NEW 2026-07-24 — the map gets a cognition thread, and the honest version of it is a null. A MIND-style diet (Mediterranean × DASH, tuned for the brain) has actually been randomized: the MIND trial (Barnes 2023, NEJM, n=604, aged 65–84, 3 years, MIND diet vs an active control diet, mild caloric restriction and matched counselling in both arms, 93.4% completion) found global cognition rose in both arms — 0.205 vs 0.170 standardized units — for a null difference of 0.035 (−0.022 to 0.092), p=0.23. That is the randomized answer for diet-and-cognition and it belongs on this map. The 2026 paper the vault ingested is the post-hoc rescue: re-partition those same 590 people by how much their diet actually changed and the substantial-improvers gain β = 0.035 SD per year (0.006–0.064) (mind-diet-adherence-associates-with-cognition-post-randomization) — the same digits, three times the magnitude, one missing randomization. Appraised unreliable (fatal flag #1: a practice-relevant claim from a small-effect observational comparison whose central confounder — the disposition to adhere — is unadjustable by design), capped single-study, so it moves nothing. Two rules leave this ingest and apply beyond it. (a) When a source's headline disagrees with the pre-specified primary analysis of the trial it is drawn from, the pre-specified analysis is the finding (null-diet-trial-recovered-by-adherence-subsetting) — the vault's second null-trial recovery in five days, after COSMOS's win-ratio. (b) The paper's genuinely good move — validating adherence against plasma lutein-zeaxanthin and β-carotene — proves the food was eaten and says nothing about who eats it (diet-adherence-biomarkers-validate-exposure-not-causal-inference); a nutrient biomarker upgrades one of seven observational lenses and leaves healthy-adherer bias untouched. This map's load-bearing claim is still mortality, not cognition, and Status holds emerging.

How it sits with the other nutrition items (not a competitor). The Mediterranean pattern is the frame the vault's other nutrition levers live inside: hit the protein-target number within it; fermented-foods (yogurt/fermented dairy) is a Mediterranean-consistent food that doubles as protein; the food-first reading of omega-3 (fish over capsules) is a Mediterranean staple. For David (GOAL.md) it's the lowest-burden, most durable "what to eat" default — a pattern to lean toward, not a supplement to buy.

⚑ NEW 2026-08-23 — THE PATTERN GETS ITS FIRST 24-MONTH RANDOMISED TEST AGAINST A REAL COMPETITOR, AND IT DRAWS. Status held emerging. DELISH randomised 360 US primary-care patients with obesity to a three-phase, dietitian-delivered Mediterranean-style programme or to two years of free WeightWatchers, and followed them to 24 months. Percentage change in body weight: −3.40% versus −3.51%, difference −0.11 percentage points (95% CI −1.79 to +1.57), P = .90 (mediterranean-style-weight-loss-matched-a-commercial-programme-at-24-months). ⚑ The interval is the finding. A window 3.4 points wide and centred on zero rules out any difference bigger than about 1.8% of body weight in either direction — for a 165 lb adult, under three pounds at the extreme edge. Sixteen analysis cells across four variants all agree. This is a bounded null, not a failure to detect.

⚑ And the reason the null is worth having is the comparator, which is the exact inverse of the problem this vault keeps logging. The trial's sample size assumed the control would lose about 1% — the pooled figure from 15 primary-care behavioural weight-loss trials. It lost 3.5% (the-weight-loss-control-arm-outperformed-its-own-design-assumption-threefold). So the claim is "no better than a good commercial programme," not "does not work" — a distinction that seven of this vault's comparator-strength lens requests exist because most trials cannot make.

⚠ The result the paper leads with is a self-reported secondary, and the trial's own machine-read check did not confirm it. Diet quality improved substantially more in the Mediterranean arm on all three questionnaire indices (AHEI +3.62, 1.11 to 6.12; DASH +1.91; alternate-Mediterranean +1.15). But skin carotenoid concentration — reflection spectroscopy reading pigment actually deposited in the skin — favoured the CONTROL at 4 months (difference 23.32, 9.87 to 36.77, P < .001) and was flat at 12 and 24 months (the-diet-quality-win-did-not-appear-in-the-objective-carotenoid-biomarker). Read fairly, this is a failed corroboration rather than a refutation — carotenoids cannot see olive oil, nuts, fish or whole grains, which is where the self-reported change was largest. What it removes is the right to say the diet-quality finding was objectively confirmed, in an open-label trial whose intervention arm spent four months being taught what a good diet looks like. The vault's standing rule, from this map's own MIND ingest, is diet-adherence-biomarkers-validate-exposure-not-causal-inference; DELISH supplies its mirror image.

⚠ Two more rows that do not flatter the intervention, recorded rather than tidied away. The only physiological secondary to clear zero at 24 months was HbA1c, favouring the control (0.00 vs −0.22 percentage points, difference −0.22, −0.41 to −0.03) — in a trial with no multiplicity adjustment across roughly fifteen secondary outcomes at three time points. And at 4 months, when the control arm had lost 3.44 percentage points more body weight, blood pressure fell identically in both arms (systolic difference −0.41, −3.23 to +2.41) (blood-pressure-fell-equally-while-weight-loss-differed-threefold).

⚑ What this does NOT touch, and the map should be explicit about it. This map's load-bearing claim is all-cause mortality across 54 cohorts and 1,833,267 adults, with PREDIMED behind it in the wider literature. DELISH did not test that. It tested a proposition the map never made — that a Mediterranean-style programme is a superior weight-loss tool — and the answer is no. A suggestive-capped null on an endpoint the map does not rest on cannot move its Status. ⚑ The same night, from the other direction: biological-age-clocks ingested a 51-study methylation database in which two different Mediterranean diets were one of only TWO interventions to satisfy both of that paper's consistency rules — the pattern moves a methylation panel consistently while not moving a scale differently. Both are true; a consistent clock response is not a health outcome (clock-responsiveness-does-not-establish-surrogacy-and-there-is-no-defined-minimal-important-difference).

⚑ NEW 2026-08-21 — A COMPONENT OF THE PATTERN IS TESTED ON ITS OWN AND DOES NOT LOWER BLOOD PRESSURE, AND THE NULL IS PRECISE ENOUGH TO BE USEFUL. Walnuts are the main dietary source of the plant omega-3 alpha-linolenic acid and a defined constituent of this pattern — PREDIMED's landmark randomised evidence has a nut arm. Pooling 36 randomised controlled trials of walnut consumption: systolic blood pressure −0.65 mmHg (95% CI −1.61 to +0.30, p = 0.183), diastolic −0.68 mmHg (−1.56 to +0.19, p = 0.125). Both null (walnuts-are-a-precise-blood-pressure-null-not-an-uncertain-one).

⚑ The interval width is what makes this worth recording. Most nulls in this vault are shrugs. This systolic interval is under two millimetres of mercury wide, and its most favourable edge is 1.61 mmHg — while the same 24 hours produced a 159-trial exercise network in which the weakest active modality managed −4.18 mmHg and combined aerobic-plus-resistance training −7.72 (integrated-aerobic-resistance-training-survives-the-quality-filter-that-sinks-circuit-training). The honest sentence is not "we could not tell." It is "not by an amount worth acting on."

⚠ The one significant outcome is a surrogate and does not rescue the food. Flow-mediated dilation — how much an artery widens on ultrasound after blood flow is briefly cut off and restored — improved by 0.93 percentage points (0.27 to 1.59). It is the most operator-technique-sensitive outcome in the vascular literature, and it sits alongside nulls on ICAM-1 and VCAM-1, the two inflammatory markers that share its mechanism. One measurement moving while its mechanistic companions do not is not a vascular effect.

⚠ ⚑ THE LOAD-BEARING CAVEAT IS ABOUT RETRIEVAL, NOT ABOUT THE STUDY. The full text is behind a Wiley subscription and a Cloudflare bot-wall that defeated the entire fetch ladder, so risk of bias, GRADE, heterogeneity (I²) for every outcome, the walnut dose range, what the control arms ate, and the FUNDING SOURCE are all UNVERIFIED. In a literature where nut-industry bodies fund a substantial share of the trials, an unknown funder is a material gap rather than a formality. Status HELD emerging; capped suggestive.

⚑ A candidate map, walnuts, was scored and REFUSED at ~1.5 of 4 — and the filing decision deliberately breaks a precedent. See the through-line below.
