Multidomain lifestyle intervention (diet + exercise + behavioral counseling, bundled) — nutrition/exercise

Status: emerging (2026-07-11) — auto-coined this run, flagged for human review, and it is a close call (see the connect section — the case against coining it is real). This is the map for the whole stack tested as one thing: a supervised, counseled, calorie-plus-activity program run for a decade. Its first source is a post-trial analysis of Look AHEAD, whose effect on incident cognitive impairment varied by baseline BMI — benefit in the overweight, nothing in the moderately obese, and a harm signal in the severely obese.

What we believe now

The one thing this map can say is that "lifestyle intervention" is not a single dose that everyone gets more of — its sign appears to flip depending on who takes it. In 3,655 adults with type 2 diabetes followed 12–14 years after a 10-year intensive lifestyle intervention ended, the effect on incident clinically significant cognitive impairment (MCI or dementia) split cleanly by baseline BMI (interaction p=0.004):

Baseline BMIHazard ratio for cognitive impairmentRead
25–29 (overweight)0.64 (0.46–0.89)benefit
30–39 (obese)0.98 (0.85–1.16)nothing
≥40 (severely obese)1.40 (1.01–1.93)harm signal

The harm arm is carried, not buried (multidomain-lifestyle-cognitive-impairment-harm-severe-obesity). shared/evidence-model.md says Status moves both ways, and that applies inside a map as well as across one. But the honesty runs in both directions: that interval's lower bound is 1.01. It barely excludes no-effect. This is a signal to state and watch, not a result to act on.

Three things hold this map down hard, and they should be read before the table above.

(1) There is no overall effect reported — the entire finding is a subgroup interaction. The paper does not publish a main effect of the intervention on cognitive impairment. It goes straight from the sample size to the BMI-stratified result. That is a structural tell: when a paper leads with a stratified result and never shows you the pooled one, the pooled one is usually null. The same investigators' 2017 predecessor on the same cohort was null overall (MCI 6.4% vs 6.6%, p=0.93), and of five subgroups it tested, only BMI showed heterogeneity.

(2) But it is not a fresh dredge, and that matters. The BMI interaction was already reported in 2017 (p=0.03); this 2026 paper re-tests it on an independent, longer follow-up window with a harder endpoint, and the interaction tightened to p=0.004 rather than fading. A subgroup that replicates and strengthens on new data is doing something a p-hacked subgroup usually does not. That is why the appraisal landed mixed and not unreliable — fatal flag #2 was weighed and deliberately not fired.

(3) The parent trial failed. Look AHEAD's actual primary endpoint — a cardiovascular composite — was null (HR 0.95, 0.83–1.09, P=0.51) and the trial was stopped early for futility in 2012. Everything on this map is an ancillary analysis of a trial whose central hypothesis did not pan out.

The unaddressed confound the vault names because the source doesn't. Cognitive status was assessed across 12–14 years after the trial. People with type 2 diabetes and a BMI ≥40 die earlier. Differential survival is a live competing risk that could manufacture either the benefit or the harm arm, and no accessible text addresses it. Do not treat the BMI≥40 hazard ratio as a demonstrated hazard of the intervention.

Population. Every participant had type 2 diabetes and was overweight or obese at baseline. Recorded as a neutral fact per shared/evidence-model.md; population-match to David is computed at regimen time (GOAL.md), not here.

⚑ NEW 2026-07-24 — the independent multidomain trial this map logged as its top gap has arrived, and it is the map's best evidence by a distance. LatAm-FINGERS (Crivelli 2026, The Lancet): a single-blind, multicentre RCT in 11 Latin American countries, n=1,065, aged 60–77, at elevated dementia risk, randomised 1:1 to a 2-year structured supervised multidomain intervention or a flexible (health-advice) intervention, assessors masked. Global cognition improved in both arms — +0.31 SD/year vs +0.20 SD/year — for a between-group difference of 0.11 SD/year (0.06–0.15, p<0.0001) (multidomain-lifestyle-improves-cognitive-composite-vs-health-advice). The first positive main effect on this map, after a subgroup interaction (Look AHEAD) and a 47-person bundle (Olaso-Gonzalez). Three things must be read with it, and they are why Status held: (a) the headline in circulation is a relative number — "55% greater improvement" is 0.31 over 0.20; the absolute quantity is 0.11 SD/year against no stated clinical anchor; (b) cognition rose in BOTH arms, so this is a difference in improvement, not a prevented decline, in an unblinded behavioural trial with a battery re-administered four times and no incident MCI/dementia endpoint (latam-fingers-cognitive-benefit-is-differential-improvement-not-prevented-decline); (c) the intervention arm generated six times the adverse events — 412 vs 66, with musculoskeletal symptoms in 21% vs 2% (supervised-multidomain-programme-carries-musculoskeletal-adverse-event-burden, safety_flags: [high-dose-risk]), a cost absent from every summary of this trial. And the rule that decides it: shared/evidence-model.md bars consolidating while a live rebuttal stands. Look AHEAD — 10 years, n=3,655, cardiovascular primary endpoint null and stopped for futility, cognitive effect flipping sign by BMI — is that rebuttal. Two randomised trials disagreeing about what a multidomain programme does over a decade is an unresolved literature. emerging holds, deterministically.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-11 — Ingested Espeland 2026 (Alzheimer's & Dementia, post-trial ancillary analysis of the Look AHEAD RCT, N=3,655, 12–14 years of post-trial follow-up, adults with type 2 diabetes, overweight/obese at baseline): the effect of a 10-year multidomain Intensive Lifestyle Intervention (calorie restriction + physical activity + behavioral counseling) on incident clinically significant cognitive impairment (MCI or dementia) varied by baseline BMI, interaction p=0.004 — BMI 25–29: HR 0.64 (0.46–0.89) (multidomain-lifestyle-cognitive-impairment-benefit-overweight); BMI 30–39: HR 0.98 (0.85–1.16) (multidomain-lifestyle-cognitive-impairment-null-moderate-obesity); BMI ≥40: HR 1.40 (1.01–1.93), a harm signal whose CI barely excludes null (multidomain-lifestyle-cognitive-impairment-harm-severe-obesity). Auto-coins the map at emerging: no overall main effect is reported (the whole finding is a subgroup interaction), the interaction descends from a five-subgroup screen in the same cohort in 2017 — though it tightened (p 0.03→0.004) on this independent longer window rather than fading — the parent trial's own primary cardiovascular endpoint was null and stopped for futility, and 12–14 years of post-trial follow-up in a T2D/severe-obesity cohort carries an unaddressed competing-risk-of-death confound. Capped suggestive.

  • 2026-07-19 — The map's second RCT: small, positive on function, and its advertised novelty is one clock in twenty-four people; Status held emerging (Olaso-Gonzalez 2026, Aging Cell 25(2):e70376, randomized controlled trial, n = 47 frail community-dwelling Spanish adults, mean age ~80, 3:2 allocation, 6 months; retrospectively registered NCT06975540; publicly funded (ISCIII/CIBERFES, EU H2020) with partial support from Nestlé Health Science; OA PDF archived). Intervention: supervised multicomponent exercise 3×/week for 24 weeks plus a daily oral nutritional supplement (Meritene, 199 kcal, 16 g protein), vs habitual care. Adherence 64.3% / 71.2%. The functional half is solid and unsurprising. Frailty SHARE-FI −2.5 [−2.9, −2.0], p < 0.0001, plus grip (p=0.0053), gait speed (p=0.0125), Tinetti (p=0.0031), Barthel (p=0.0484) and fewer primary-care visits (multidomain-intervention-improves-frailty-and-function-frail-elders). Objective instruments moving together is what a real functional effect looks like; that supervised exercise reverses aspects of frailty is well-established behavioural medicine, and this replicates it cleanly. ⚑ The epigenetic half — the title claim and the reason this is in Aging Cell — does not survive arithmetic. Complete methylome data existed for 24 participants, EIGHT of them controls. Across five clocks, ONE moved: DNAm PhenoAge (p = 0.03), with matching REA (p = 0.03) and preserved DNAm telomere length (p = 0.0246). Horvath, Hannum, GrimAge2 and FitAge were all null — including the two with the best mortality validation (epigenetic-clock-signal-rests-on-one-of-five-clocks). One hit among five correlated tests, uncorrected, in n = 24, is the same noise signature the vault named in Zhu 2026's BFR hemodynamics (one significant cell in ~36). And the clock that fired is the one built from clinical chemistry — a nutritional supplement that raises albumin, total protein and folate (which this trial reports it did) moves PhenoAge through its own inputs, no change in biological aging required. Note also that Shan 2026 (n = 145,465) found PhenoAge among its NULL clocks while GrimAge/Horvath moved — the opposite pattern, in a study six thousand times larger. Seventh attribution failure: exercise and a branded supplement made by a funder of the trial were delivered as one arm, and the authors concede the components cannot be separated (multidomain-nutrition-exercise-bundle-not-separable). This does NOT soften Look AHEAD. A 47-person, 6-month, surrogate-and-function trial in frail 80-year-olds does not offset a 10-year, n=3,655 RCT whose cardiovascular primary endpoint was null and stopped for futility. Different population, horizon, and endpoints — and decisively, a different starting point: the multidomain literature keeps working best on people already losing something, which is the same benefit-on-demand structure Liang 2026 established for exercise-and-sleep in this very run, arriving from an independent direction. Appraised mixed; capped suggestive. Nothing for David — he is forty-something and not frail; the components are already in his regimen individually and better-sourced there, and no supplement is warranted.

  • 2026-07-20 — The map's first meta-analysis, and it turns the bundle question inside out. Ingested Castillo-Bernad 2026 (Mechanisms of Ageing and Development 231:112183, PRISMA systematic review + random-effects meta-analysis of RCTs, PROSPERO CRD42024528600, 14 RCTs, n=4,013, adults ≥65 (mean 71.4), cognitively healthy or MCI, no diagnosed dementia; PEDro + Cochrane RoB 2; public/academic funding, no commercial sponsor). Combined exercise + nutritional/dietary supplementation improved global cognition vs control: SMD 0.15 (95% CI 0.07–0.24), I² = 50% (exercise-plus-nutrition-cognition-small-global-benefit). A pre-planned sensitivity analysis excluding multi-domain interventions held the effect and collapsed heterogeneity to zero — d = 0.12 (0.02–0.22), I² = 0% (exercise-nutrition-cognition-effect-survives-excluding-multidomain) — which is the good news, and it is real: a small effect measured consistently is worth more than a large one measured raggedly. But no specific cognitive domain moved — executive function and visual-perceptual ability both null (exercise-nutrition-cognition-null-in-specific-domains). The load-bearing limitation is structural: all 14 trials compare exercise+nutrition against control, never against exercise alone, so this literature is incapable of saying whether the nutrition contributed anything. Given that the vault holds exercise as an established cognitive lever while omega-3 and multivitamins are null on cognition, the parsimonious reading is exercise, diluted — a hypothesis the vault names and refuses to grade. Appraised mixed; capped suggestive. Status held emerging — an SMD of 0.15 with a 0.02 lower bound and no domain-level effect does not consolidate anything.

  • 2026-07-24 — The map's top open gap closes on its existence half: an independent, adequately-sized, randomised multidomain trial now exists — and Status still holds emerging by rule. Ingested Crivelli 2026 (The Lancet, single-blind multicentre RCT, 11 Latin American countries, n=1,065 randomised of 1,719 assessed, aged 60–77 with a dementia risk score ≥6 plus suboptimal cognition; permuted blocks of eight stratified by centre; assessors masked, participants and staff not; enrolled 2021-10-27 → 2023-07-07, last follow-up 2025-11-07; funded by the Alzheimer's Association, no commercial product tested; part of the WW-FINGERS network; retrospectively registered NCT06492967 — first submitted 2023-05-30, first posted 2024-07-09; Lancet paywalled, pdf_unavailable after the full ladder). Structured supervised multidomain intervention (SLI, n=539) vs flexible health-advice intervention (FLI, n=526), 2 years. Global cognitive composite: SLI +0.31 SD/y (0.28–0.34), FLI +0.20 SD/y (0.17–0.23), between-group 0.11 SD/y (0.06–0.15), p<0.0001 (multidomain-lifestyle-improves-cognitive-composite-vs-health-advice). Feasibility was a co-primary outcome (RE-AIM: Reach 62.0%, adherence 71.6%); 82.3% completed 24 months, with dropout higher in the FLI arm (20.2% vs 15.2%, p=0.042) — i.e. differential, in the arm that did worse. Three refusals recorded. (1) The public framing — "55% greater improvement" — is shared/study-appraisal.md's first universal lens firing on the field's flagship result; the vault carries 0.11 SD/year and notes that no clinical anchor for it is given. (2) Both arms improved, so the finding is a difference between two rising trajectories in an unblinded trial with four repeated administrations of the battery — practice and engagement are unexcluded, and no incident MCI or dementia endpoint is reported; the authors' own claim is feasibility, scalability, and "greater cognitive improvements than a flexible health-advice intervention," which is the claim the vault adopts (latam-fingers-cognitive-benefit-is-differential-improvement-not-prevented-decline). (3) The cost side is in the paper and in no summary of it: 478 adverse events, 412 SLI vs 66 FLI; musculoskeletal symptoms 113 (21%) vs 13 (2%); serious AEs 9% vs 5%; 8 deaths (3 vs 5), none related (supervised-multidomain-programme-carries-musculoskeletal-adverse-event-burden, safety_flags: [high-dose-risk]). The vault refused Shen 2026's "similar safety profiles" on a weaker signal than this one; the discipline has to survive contact with an intervention it likes. Eighth attribution failure — diet, exercise, cognitive training and vascular monitoring delivered as one arm; and the control arm was the unsupervised version of the same advice, so supervision itself is a live candidate for the active ingredient. Appraised mixed (green: true randomisation, assessor masking, scale, duration, full AE reporting, non-commercial funder; red: retrospective registration, co-primary feasibility design, surrogate composite endpoint, both-arms-improved, differential dropout, relative-only headline, active control). Capped suggestive. Status emerging HELD — consolidating requires no live rebuttal, and Look AHEAD's 10-year null is live. Nothing for David: wrong decade, wrong risk stratum, surrogate endpoint, and every component already better-sourced on its own map.

  • 2026-07-29 — The map gets its SECOND independent randomised trial with a positive main effect, and the distance between emerging and consolidating is now the narrowest it has been. Status HELD emerging, deterministically — and the call is flagged for human review (Kim 2026, Neurology 107(3):e218297, pre-specified subgroup analysis of the SUPERBRAIN-MEET randomised controlled trial; 24 weeks, 17 South Korean hospitals, outcome-assessor-blinded, parallel-group, 1:1; adults aged 60–85 with mild cognitive impairment; 300 randomised, 277 in the modified intention-to-treat set (142 vs 135), mean age 72.9, 69.3% women; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05023057, registered before enrolment; Neurology paywalled, pdf_unavailable logged after the full ladder — Unpaywall confirms closed, the publisher PDF returns 403). RBANS total index score improved 4.17 points more than control (95% CI 1.92–6.43, p < 0.001) — the Repeatable Battery for the Assessment of Neuropsychological Status, whose index scores are standardised to mean 100, SD 15, so ≈0.28 standard deviations (arithmetic performed here; the paper states no clinical anchor) (multidomain-lifestyle-improves-rbans-over-24-weeks-vs-usual-care). Intervention components — dietary counselling, physical exercise, cognitive training, vascular/metabolic risk monitoring, face-to-face plus a tablet app — are cited from the parent trial report (Moon 2025, Alzheimer's & Dementia, doi:10.1002/alz.14517), not inferred. ⚑ Why Status holds, and it is the rule rather than a judgement. shared/evidence-model.md bars consolidating while a live rebuttal stands. Four clauses are now satisfied for the first time — LatAm-FINGERS (Lancet, n=1,065, 11 countries) and SUPERBRAIN-MEET (Neurology, n=300, South Korea) are different investigators, different continents, different delivery models, both randomised, both assessor-blinded, both positive. The fifth is not. Look AHEAD remains live, and the asymmetry decides it: the two positives measure cognitive test scores over 24 weeks and 2 years; the null measures incident cognitive impairment over 12–14 years. Two surrogate wins do not retire a hard-endpoint null — the same rule that governs creatine-cognition, nad-precursors and omega-3, where three molecules have now been verified to reach their targets with no outcome attached (dha-brain-delivery-without-cognitive-or-structural-benefit). ⚠ The comparator gap is the sharpest new fact, and it points the wrong way for the effect size. SUPERBRAIN-MEET's control arm received usual care; LatAm-FINGERS's received an active health-advice programme. The separations: 0.28 SD over 24 weeks here, 0.11 SD per year there. The trial with the weaker comparator produced the larger effect, which is what unmatched attention, contact time and expectation buy in an unblindable behavioural trial with a learnable battery administered twice. ⚑ Ninth attribution failure, with an extra edge: cognitive training is one of the intervention components and cognitive testing is the outcome — part of the intervention is practice at the task being measured. ⚠ The subgroup half is refused, and the refusal is the eleventh of its kind. Roughly seventeen characteristics were tested — sex, age, education, living status, internet access, device use, baseline MMSE, BMI, obesity, smoking, both blood pressures, three lipid fractions, fasting glucose, cardiovascular comorbidity — and none remained significant after false-discovery-rate correction. The authors read that as "generalizability and scalability" one sentence after naming their own "limited statistical power." In 277 people, roughly 138 per cell, that is a statement about detection, not biology (superbrain-meet-subgroup-nulls-are-underpowered-not-generalisability) — the same move the vault refused in Oba 2026 five days ago when accepting it would have closed a gap the vault wanted closed. ⚑ One subgroup deserves singling out: BMI is the single moderator whose sign FLIPPED in Look AHEAD (interaction p = 0.004; harm above BMI 40). SUPERBRAIN-MEET tested BMI and obesity status and found nothing. That is the first time anything has looked again — and it is not a refutation: different population, different endpoint, different horizon, and 277 people cannot overturn 3,655. Recorded as a first non-replication attempt, nothing more. ✅ Credit, because this vault is quick to catch and slow to praise. Registered before enrolment (both prior trials on this map were registered retrospectively), assessor-blinded, 92.3% retained, subgroups pre-specified, multiplicity corrected, every subgroup reported null. Almost every methodological failure this vault has catalogued in a month is the absence of one of those five things. ⚠ Held down by an unrecoverable funding and competing-interests statement in a paper whose author list includes a digital-therapeutics company whose product category is the delivery mechanism under test. Appraised mixed (fatal flag #3 weighed and not fired — it targets the secondary subgroup conclusions while the primary is a positive, not a null sold as absence); capped suggestive; extends. Nothing for David — wrong decade, wrong population, surrogate endpoint, and this map's standing rule holds: a combination result is never evidence for its components.

  • 2026-07-31 — A third randomised programme moves function in a hard population — and this one comes with a placebo-controlled proof that the pill in it was doing nothing. Status held emerging (Nava 2026, DEMFOS, Lancet Healthy Longevity 100883, randomised assessor-masked, ITT, n=114, NCT04221750, VA-funded with no pharmaceutical sponsor; ingested primarily for metformin). Adults aged 65–85 (mean 72.8) with obesity, mild-to-moderate frailty and a mean of 2.7 chronic conditions did three supervised 90-minute sessions a week — 15 min flexibility, 30 min aerobic at 70–85% peak heart rate, 30 min progressive resistance training to ~85% 1RM, 15 min balance — inside a 500–750 kcal/day deficit with weekly dietitian contact. Against an arm given monthly one-hour diet education: physical performance test +2.5 points on a 0–36 scale (1.2 to 3.8), p = 0.0002; VO₂peak +3.4 mL/kg/min (2.0 to 4.9), about +19%; 6-minute walk +44.5 m (14.2 to 74.7); knee extensor torque +12.2 N·m (5.8 to 18.5) and power +13.5 W (3.9 to 23.1); gait speed +0.11 m/s (0.03 to 0.19); weight −7.8 kg; SF-36 physical +3.6 (0.5 to 6.7) (intensive-lifestyle-intervention-improves-physical-function-in-older-adults-with-obesity). Adherence 92% diet / 85% exercise sessions over six months. ⚠ The map's standing rule still binds: a combination result is never evidence for its components. DEMFOS bundles diet, aerobic, resistance, balance and flexibility, and cannot say which did the work. ⚠ And the comparator is monthly education, not an active alternative — the weak-comparator discount this vault has now applied four times in a week applies here in full. ⚑ What is genuinely new is the internal control. Both compared arms took metformin, and a third arm ran the identical programme on placebo; the drug changed nothing on any of seventeen outcomes (metformin-adds-nothing-to-lifestyle-intervention-for-physical-function). The delta is attributable to the programme, with a blinded demonstration that it is not the pill. ⚠ It also carried a real cost: lean mass, thigh muscle volume and hip bone density all fell (weight-loss-with-supervised-training-still-costs-lean-mass-and-hip-bone). Appraised reliable; concept single-study (one trial, 89% men, one Veterans Affairs centre, six months).
