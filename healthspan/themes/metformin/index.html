Metformin — metabolic / pharmacologic

Status: contested (2026-07-07) — coined this run from a human-greenlit sweep of the longevity trio. Prescription drug → by rule it is never ranked in the daily protocol and no dose is set here (shared/safety.md). A clinician-only pharmacologic lever, alongside glp-1 and rapamycin.

What we believe now

For the healthspan/anti-aging use case in non-diabetics, metformin's clean human evidence is null — and in frail elders, net-negative. The MET-PREVENT RCT (n=72, mean age 80, sarcopenic + pre-frail/frail; 500 mg TID × 4 months) found no effect on 4-metre walk speed (adjusted difference 0.001 m/s, p=0.96) and a harm signal: hospital admissions 34% (metformin) vs 8% (placebo).

The tension worth keeping visible (why contested, not fading):

  • The pro-longevity case is observational and preclinical. Enthusiasm rests on diabetic-cohort mortality associations, mechanism (AMPK, autophagy), and animal data — not on outcome RCTs in non-diabetics.
  • The population-appropriate landmark hasn't reported. TAME (Barzilai, n≈3,000, aging as the primary endpoint, healthier non-diabetics 65–79) is expected ~2026–27; the long DPPOS follow-up found no mortality difference (HR 0.99). So MET-PREVENT's null is in a specific frail population and doesn't by itself settle TAME's question — but the human outcome data that do exist are null-to-harmful.

Regimen implication. Clinician-only; lives in the regimen's "discuss with your clinician" section with the evidence summarized, never scored against Benefit/Burden. Honest one-liner: strong observational/mechanistic story, but the human healthspan RCTs are null (and, in the frail, harmful) — wait for TAME.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚠ Interaction with the exercise levers — AMPK antagonism of training adaptation. Metformin activates AMPK, which can blunt the muscle-hypertrophy and cardiorespiratory adaptations that resistance-training and zone-2-cardio are built on (and can crowd the protein-target anabolic response). For someone whose foundation is exercise, this is a real trade-off. Flagged in both directions (safety_flags: [interaction]); magnitude is an open gap — flagged, not fabricated (no vault-quality source ingested yet).
  • Coherence with glp-1 and rapamycin. Three clinician-only pharmacologic levers held to the same "never ranked, evidence summarized for the clinician" discipline; metformin is sometimes co-used with GLP-1 clinically (a clinician-conversation item, not a co-recommendation here).
