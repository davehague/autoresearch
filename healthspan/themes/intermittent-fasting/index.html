Intermittent fasting — nutrition

Status: emerging (2026-07-06) — auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. A network meta-analysis of RCTs shows intermittent fasting improves cardiometabolic risk factors (weight, blood pressure, glucose, lipids), modality-dependent — but every endpoint is a surrogate, the trials are mostly short, and a third are at high risk of bias. Opened at the bottom of the ladder: randomized signal on markers, no hard-outcome evidence, real adherence cost.

What we believe now

Intermittent fasting moves cardiometabolic risk markers, and which marker depends on the variant. In a network meta-analysis of 56 RCTs (3,965 participants): modified alternate-day fasting (mADF) ranked best for weight (−5.18 kg), blood pressure (−7.24 / −4.70 mmHg), and waist; time-restricted eating (TRE) ranked best for fasting glucose (−3.74 mg/dL); 5:2 periodic fasting best for LDL (−6.80 mg/dL).

Read honestly and low. These are surrogate endpoints — risk factors, not events or mortality — in mostly overweight/obese cohorts, from short trials, with 37.5% at high risk of bias. So the claim is "intermittent fasting improves risk markers," not "prevents cardiovascular disease" or "extends healthspan." The most decision-relevant nuance for GOAL.md's least-discomfort / adherence-first philosophy: TRE is the lowest-friction variant (an eating window, no calorie counting), while the more effective mADF/ADF protocols carry a larger hunger and social cost. There is no invented protocol here — the studied windows/schedules are the evidence, not a recommendation (shared/safety.md).

⚠ Updated 2026-07-21 — the field's largest synthesis lands, and it says the fasting is just the calorie restriction. Semnani-Azad 2025 (BMJ, network meta-analysis, 99 RCTs, n=6,582, >80% low risk of bias, GRADE-assessed) compared ADF, TRE, WDF, continuous energy restriction (CER), and ad libitum. Against ad libitum, weight loss sorted by restrictiveness (ADF −3.40 kg > WDF −2.36 > CER −2.11 > TRE −1.72). Against CER — the contrast that decides whether fasting adds anything — only ADF beat calorie restriction (−1.29 kg), and that edge vanished in trials ≥24 weeks; TRE and WDF were no different from CER (intermittent-fasting-matches-caloric-restriction-for-weight). The cardiometabolic markers refused to distinguish the strategies — HbA1c flat everywhere, glucose/lipids/BP/CRP largely indistinguishable from CER (intermittent-fasting-cardiometabolic-markers-match-caloric-restriction). The honest read: intermittent fasting is a delivery format for eating less; the eating-less is the effect. This lowers the stakes of the whole question — the format is a personal adherence choice, not a physiological one — and it reinforces the map's standing point that TRE gives up nothing meaningful to the harsher schedules. Kibret 2025's between-variant marker rankings (mADF best for weight, TRE for glucose) are mostly noise at this larger scale. Appraised reliable; the weight-equivalence concept graded strong, the markers suggestive. Still surrogate, still short, still emerging — a bigger, better synthesis of markers is still markers.

⚑ Updated 2026-07-24 — if the format is an adherence choice, then WHEN YOU TRAIN INSIDE IT is part of the format, and it has now been randomized. Höchsmann 2026 (registered, conflict-free crossover, n=35 healthy young adults, 30 min treadmill running at 65% VO₂peak) found the same session in the evening at the end of a 12-hour fast was followed by +400 kcal at the next meal (286–515) and +547 kcal over 24 hours (335–759) versus the morning (evening-fasted-exercise-followed-by-higher-24h-energy-intake, safety_flags: [interaction]). A 547-kcal day is larger than a compressed eating window plausibly removes — so a fasting schedule and aerobic training, two levers this vault scores separately, can cancel each other depending only on their order in the day. The obvious fix fails: shortening the fast to 6 h fixed the meal and not the day, and eating beforehand suppressed fat oxidation while leaving intake and appetite untouched (shortening-the-pre-exercise-fast-does-not-restore-24h-intake). And the arithmetic retires fasted cardio's rationale: the extra fat oxidised is 0.77 kcal/min ≈ 23 kcal across the bout, against 547 kcal of intake — about 1 to 24, the wrong way (fat-oxidation-gain-is-dwarfed-by-compensatory-intake). The load-bearing caveat, stated up front: the trial describes no non-exercise control arm, and people eat more in the evening regardless of exercise — so the vault carries the number and declines the word "compensatory." Acute, one day, one modality, 35 young adults. Appraised mixed, capped suggestive. Status held emerging.

  • 2026-07-21 — The field's largest synthesis reframes the map: fasting works by energy restriction, not timing; Status held emerging. Ingested Semnani-Azad 2025 (BMJ 389:e082007, systematic review + network meta-analysis, 99 RCTs, n=6,582, median age 45 / BMI 31.3, median 12-week trials; searched to Nov 2024; >80% low risk of bias; GRADE-assessed; commissioned by the EASD Diabetes and Nutrition Study Group; OA). Against continuous energy restriction, only ADF beat CER (−1.29 kg) and only in trials <24 weeks; TRE and WDF matched CER, and at ≥24 weeks no IF strategy differed from CER (intermittent-fasting-matches-caloric-restriction-for-weight). Cardiometabolic markers (HbA1c/glucose/lipids/BP/CRP) did not distinguish the strategies (intermittent-fasting-cardiometabolic-markers-match-caloric-restriction). Intermittent fasting is a delivery format for eating less — which makes the choice among formats an adherence question and reinforces TRE as the lowest-friction option. Appraised reliable; weight-equivalence strong, markers suggestive. Surrogate endpoints, short trials → Status held emerging.

  • 2026-07-24 — The map's first graded INTERACTION, and it is with exercise timing; Status held emerging. Ingested Höchsmann 2026 (Am J Clin Nutr, randomized crossover, n=35 healthy young adults, fed subsample n=17; prospectively registered NCT06947824; Technical University of Munich; authors declare no commercial or financial relationships; Elsevier paywalled, pdf_unavailable after the full ladder). Three fasted conditions — morning after a 12 h fast, evening after a 12 h fast, evening after a 6 h fast — each 30 min treadmill running at 65% VO₂peak; intake by ad libitum test meal + weighed 24 h food records; substrate use by indirect calorimetry. Evening fasted vs morning fasted: acute intake +400 kcal (286–515), 24-hour intake +547 kcal (335–759), both p<0.001, with hunger, desire to eat and prospective consumption up and fullness down (evening-fasted-exercise-followed-by-higher-24h-energy-intake, safety_flags: [interaction], noted in both this map and zone-2-cardio per §6.5). The mitigation half-works and then fails: 12 h → 6 h attenuated acute intake but not 24-hour intake, and full pre-exercise feeding suppressed fat oxidation without touching intake or appetite (shortening-the-pre-exercise-fast-does-not-restore-24h-intake) — a study that stopped at the test meal would have reported a working fix. The surrogate arithmetic is the finding's sharpest edge: fat oxidation rose 0.77 kcal/min (0.37–1.17) — about 23 kcal across a 30-minute bout (vault-derived from the paper's rate × duration, not a stated figure) — against 547 kcal of intake (fat-oxidation-gain-is-dwarfed-by-compensatory-intake). The vault's fourth verified surrogate-versus-outcome dissociation this month and the first from a behaviour rather than a molecule, after creatine's brain loading, NR's doubled NAD⁺ and DHA's CSF entry. Appraised mixed — green: randomized crossover (each participant their own control), prospectively registered, conflict-free, objective calorimetry and weighed records rather than recall, large precise intervals, and the authors' own candidate mechanism tested and reported null; red: no non-exercise control condition described, so a circadian appetite gradient cannot be separated from exercise-induced compensation and the word "compensatory" is declined; one acute bout, a 24-hour horizon, no weight or body-composition outcome, 35 healthy young adults, one modality (running — the modality GOAL.md's knee constraint most disfavours), self-reported appetite in an unblindable protocol. Capped suggestive. Status emerging HELD. For David: a free scheduling observation, not a rule — if aerobic work and a compressed window are both in play, the earlier session is the one less likely to be followed by a large evening intake. No window, schedule or dose is invented here (shared/safety.md).

  • 2026-07-28 — The map's central claim survives its hardest test: in the population where meal timing should matter most, the long-term glucose endpoint still does not move. Status held emerging (Viple 2026, Diabetologia, systematic review + meta-analysis of 10 RCTs, n = 599, PROSPERO CRD42024523591, MEDLINE/Embase/CENTRAL to 5 Aug 2025 plus forward and backward citation searching, RoB 2, GRADE; mean trial length 4 months, eating windows 4–10 h/day versus a non-time-restricted control diet; paywalled — pdf_unavailable after the full ladder including the browser rung). On 2026-07-21 this map concluded, from the BMJ's 99-trial network meta-analysis, that intermittent fasting is a delivery format for eating less. The strongest objection to that was the population — mostly healthy overweight adults, with little glucose dysregulation to fix. This is that objection tested, in the specialist journal, and it fails. HbA1c — the three-month average that defines diabetes control — did not move: −3.33 mmol/mol (−6.87 to 0.20), i.e. −0.30 percentage points (−0.63 to 0.02), p = 0.06, moderate certainty (time-restricted-eating-null-on-hba1c-in-glucose-dysregulation, suggestive). Fasting glucose fell 0.30 mmol/l (−0.53 to −0.07) — about 5.4 mg/dL, a fifth of a diagnostic band (time-restricted-eating-lowers-fasting-glucose-slightly, suggestive). Insulin sensitivity, beta-cell function and continuous-glucose-monitoring outcomes: inconclusive. ⚑ Two catches the vault records and the abstract does not. (1) The subgroup split reads backwards. HbA1c was "significant" in prediabetes at −0.09 percentage points and "not significant" in type 2 diabetes at −0.43 — the non-significant subgroup's effect is nearly five times larger, and the significant one is smaller than the assay's own variability. Significance is tracking precision, not effect size (tre-hba1c-subgroup-significance-tracks-precision-not-effect-size, single-study). (2) The same trials lost 1.6 kg (−2.2 to −1.0), and 1.6 kg reliably lowers fasting glucose by about the observed amount — so the glucose change is not separable from the weight change, which is precisely what this map's "delivery format for eating less" claim predicts (tre-glucose-effect-not-separable-from-concurrent-weight-loss, single-study). ⚠ The HbA1c null is refused as equivalence — its interval reaches −0.63 percentage points, a clinically useful effect, on 10 trials over a median 4 months against an endpoint that integrates 3 months. Twelfth refusal, and again in a direction the vault would have liked. ⚠ Conflict recorded with its direction named: an author is currently employed by Novo Nordisk A/S, and the conclusion — a free behaviour failing to move the endpoint glucose-lowering drugs are sold on — points the sponsor-favourable way. Appraised mixed; capped suggestive. confirms; Status emerging HELD — surrogate endpoints, short trials, and a confirmation is not a promotion. For David: nothing changes, and he is not in this population (GOAL.md records no diabetes or prediabetes). What transfers is calibration: if a compressed window appeals, the honest case for it is adherence, not metabolism. No window or schedule is invented here (shared/safety.md).

  • 2026-08-13 — A lean-mass observation the map records and refuses to act on; Status held emerging. Ingested Yang 2026 (nordic-walking-protein-tre-postmenopausal-muscle-rct-yang-2026, ingested on protein-target; Nutrients 18(15):2470, 12-week three-arm RCT, 100 postmenopausal women randomised / 96 analysed, ChiCTR2500105425, ~94% eating-window compliance). The arm doing Nordic walking inside a 10-hour window with no protein target improved grip strength, gait speed and chair-stand against control — and was the only arm whose lean mass went nowhere: appendicular lean mass +0.04 ± 0.32 kg against +1.19 ± 0.30 kg when a 1.4 g/kg/day protein target was added, with "a slight decline" in some lower-limb indicators and the largest weight and BMI reductions of the three groups (time-restricted-eating-arm-without-a-protein-target-showed-the-least-favourable-lean-mass). ⚠ The obvious reading — fasting plus exercise without deliberate protein costs lean tissue — is DECLINED, on three grounds. (1) There is no TRE-alone arm and no exercise-alone arm, so nothing in this trial isolates time-restricted eating from anything; the authors say so and conclude "TRE should therefore be understood not as an independently validated anabolic strategy." (2) Lean mass is bioelectrical impedance in a trial prescribing a 14-hour daily fast — an arm eating in a shortened window, losing the most weight, and reading lower on a body-water-derived estimate is what a hydration artefact produces and what real tissue loss produces, and this design separates neither (lean-mass-gain-measured-by-impedance-in-a-fasting-trial). (3) Total energy intake was never compared across arms, so the size of any deficit is unknown (nordic-walking-trial-never-compared-energy-intake-across-its-protein-contrast). Why it is recorded at all: the vault already holds the same shape from better evidence — weight-loss-with-supervised-training-still-costs-lean-mass-and-hip-bone and exercise-preserves-fat-free-mass-during-calorie-restriction — so this is a weak instance of a standing expectation, not new evidence for it. ⚠ NOT read as evidence that TRE harms muscle; no safety_flags, no Status move, and intermittent-fasting-matches-caloric-restriction-for-weight stands untouched. The transferable note: in every trial this vault holds that combined an eating-window restriction with exercise, protein adequacy was the variable that separated the lean-mass outcomes — and no trial has yet isolated the window itself. Appraised mixed; capped suggestive.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚑ 2026-08-16 — a third outcome joins the convergence, and this map is one of the three patterns it tested. Ripple from an ingest filed on ketogenic-diet: La Vignera 2026 (diet-patterns-testosterone-obese-men-sr-lavignera-2026) compared the Mediterranean diet, VLCKD and intermittent fasting on testosterone in men with obesity. IF was rated "promising but more heterogeneous" — it did not win, and the reason the ranking exists at all dissolves in the same paper: weight loss is the mediator, at ~3 nmol/L per 10 kg, common to all three patterns (weight-loss-not-diet-composition-carries-the-testosterone-gain). ⚑ This map already holds the same finding on two other outcomes — intermittent-fasting-matches-caloric-restriction-for-weight and intermittent-fasting-cardiometabolic-markers-match-caloric-restriction. Weight, cardiometabolic markers, and now testosterone: three outcomes, and each time fasting is a way of eating less rather than a lever of its own. ⚠ Status HELD — a non-registered narrative review on a hormone surrogate moves nothing, and the finding it contributes is one this map had already reached twice by better routes.   • ⚠ 2026-07-28 — a third route to under-eating lands on this map, and this one is a food rather than a schedule. Ikeda 2026 (protein-target) found that protein added at breakfast displaced other food — total daily energy intake SMD −0.35 (−0.70 to 0.00) overall, and −0.47 (−0.82 to −0.11) in trials without exercise (protein-at-breakfast-displaces-total-daily-energy-intake, safety_flags: [interaction], noted here, on protein-target and on glp-1). This map's own conclusion is that a compressed window works by making people eat less. A satiating protein strategy stacked on top of it therefore pushes the same lever twice — and the map already carries a timing tension with protein-target whose agreed fix was hit the daily total inside the window. That fix assumes the protein adds to intake rather than substituting for it, and this is the first evidence that it may substitute. Efficacy-relevant rather than hazardous; enters no benefit score; raised because shared/safety.md makes interaction evidence asymmetric. This is the map's third graded interaction in five days, after evening fasted exercise (+547 kcal/24 h) and sleep restriction during energy restriction — and none of the three is yet carried in the regimen, because stage 05 triggers on a Status move and none has occurred.   • ⚑ 2026-07-28 — the pharmacological route to this map's own mechanism now has a measured magnitude. glp-1 gained a placebo-controlled, food-weighed figure: about 271 kcal removed from a single unlimited meal (glp1-cuts-standardised-meal-intake-by-about-270-kcal). It is the SECOND such interaction on this map in five days, after evening-fasted-exercise-followed-by-higher-24h-energy-intake (+547 kcal/24 h from evening fasted exercise) — and neither is yet carried in the regimen, because stage 05 triggers on a Status move and none has occurred.
