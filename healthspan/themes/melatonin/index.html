Melatonin — sleep

Status: contested (2026-07-22) — ⚠ downgraded this run from emerging, flagged for human review. Coined 2026-07-07 on a meta-analysis giving melatonin a small, real sleep-and-cognition signal in cognitively impaired elders, not healthy adults. It moves to contested tonight not because that benefit weakened but because the tolerability half of the map was never sourced, and now a randomized trial contradicts it: melatonin blunts β-cell function in carriers of a common genetic variant, and does nothing in noncarriers. The tension is stated below, not buried.

What we believe now

Melatonin is the best-characterized chronobiotic, but its meta-analytic benefit is small, lands in the wrong population for a healthy adult, and its metabolic tolerability now depends on a genotype most people have never tested. In 10 RCTs (516 adults ≥65 with MCI or dementia), it increased total sleep time by +12.4 min and MMSE by +1.8 points, with a modest neuropsychiatric-symptom reduction — but no improvement in sleep efficiency, circadian markers, depression, or daily function, and high heterogeneity.

Two framing points the vault should carry:

(1) Right mechanism, wrong population for David. The randomized evidence is in impaired elders, and even there the total-sleep gain is ~12 minutes. For a healthy adult, melatonin's evidence-based role is a short-term circadian tool (jet lag, delayed sleep phase), where its chronobiotic action is best-established — not a nightly sedative. OTC melatonin content is also notoriously inconsistent, so third-party testing matters.

(2) It complements — and would stack redundantly with — the vault's existing sleep levers. Melatonin targets circadian timing, which makes it a mechanistic complement to the behavioral sleep-regularity lever and the caffeine-timing rule; but it overlaps in use-case with the wind-down stack (l-theanine, chamomile, magnesium-glycinate, ashwagandha) — piling several sleep aids together is redundancy, not additive benefit.

(3) ⚠ And the "it's harmless, so why not" argument no longer holds unexamined (new 2026-07-22). A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial with pre-specified genotype strata (Qian 2026, Diabetes Care, n=21 — 10 carriers, 11 noncarriers) found that in carriers of the common MTNR1B G risk allele, a 5 mg dose worsened glucose tolerance by 11.7%, cut early C-peptide response by 19.2%, and suppressed glucose-stimulated first-phase β-cell responsivity by 40% — all with adjusted p-values (melatonin-impairs-glucose-tolerance-in-mtnr1b-carriers). It also abolished insulin-induced hypoglycemia in carriers (0 events vs 7), the same defect read from the other direction (melatonin-dysregulates-insulin-negative-feedback). In the 11 noncarriers, nothing happened (melatonin-metabolic-effect-is-genotype-dependent) — which is what makes it a credible pharmacogenetic interaction rather than a fishing expedition. This does not say melatonin causes diabetes; the endpoints are IVGTT surrogates from a single acute dose in twenty-one people. It says the map's unexamined tolerability premise now has a randomized counterexample in a common genotype.

Why contested (2026-07-22) — the judgement call, stated openly. By strict highest-tier-wins the sleep-benefit claim is untouched: it rests on a meta-analysis-rct and Qian is rct tier. What moved is a different axis. This map's tolerability claim — "generally well tolerated," "no safety_flags set" — had no source and no tier at all; it was an assertion. Against an assertion, a multiplicity-adjusted, pre-stratified randomized crossover is the highest-tier evidence in the vault on that axis, and it points the other way. shared/evidence-model.md defines contested as "a credible result pushes back; state the tension, never bury it" — which is exactly this. The counter-argument a human should weigh: twenty-one people on a surrogate endpoint is thin ground for relabelling a map, and holding emerging while merely adding the safety flag would be defensible. The vault takes the cautious branch because shared/safety.md is explicit that a missed interaction is worse than an over-cautious flag. git is the undo.

Why not lower still: the sleep evidence is unchanged, the harm is genotype-confined and absent in noncarriers, no clinical endpoint was measured, and melatonin's narrow evidence-based role — a short-term circadian tool for jet lag or a delayed phase — survives intact. This is contested, not fading.

  • 2026-07-22 — ⚠ STATUS DOWNGRADE emerging → contested. Ingested Qian 2026 (Diabetes Care, randomized double-blind placebo-controlled crossover, n=21 healthy adults of European ancestry — 10 MTNR1B G-allele carriers pre-stratified vs 11 noncarriers, 5-day controlled laboratory protocol, 5 mg oral melatonin vs placebo, insulin-modified IVGTT with minimal-model analysis; PMID 42346809). In carriers: glucose tolerance worse by 11.7% (95% CI 1.0–22.3), early C-peptide −19.2% (−33.9 to −1.2), glucose-stimulated first-phase β-cell responsivity −40% (−52.4 to −24.3, P<sub>adj</sub>=0.0003), insulin-induced decline in second-phase secretion 64.3% slower (23.1–119.2, P<sub>adj</sub>=0.001), and insulin-induced hypoglycemia abolished — 0 events vs 7 (P=0.001). In noncarriers: none of it. Appraised mixed (randomized crossover, multiplicity-adjusted, pre-specified genotype strata, independent NIH/ADA/Danish funding, large coherent effects — against n=21, IVGTT surrogates, one acute 5 mg dose that is not habitual bedtime use, European ancestry only, and a paywalled full text). All three concepts capped suggestive; safety_flags: [interaction] set on two of them. pushes-back. The map's unsourced "well tolerated / no safety_flags" claim is retired. Full text confirmed non-OA (ADA, Europe PMC isOpenAccess=N, no PMCID); pdf_unavailable logged with the rungs tried — NIH-funded, so retry for a PMC author manuscript.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Complements the behavioral circadian levers. Melatonin (a chronobiotic) targets the same sleep-timing system as sleep-regularity (consistent sleep/wake) and the caffeine-timing cutoff — mechanistic complements, not conflicts. Behavioral regularity is the foundation; melatonin is a short-term timing tool on top of it.
  • ⚠ Redundant with the wind-down stack — flag, don't co-recommend silently. Melatonin overlaps in use with l-theanine, chamomile, magnesium-glycinate, and ashwagandha. Treat these as one sleep-aid lever; stacking multiple hypnotic/sedative agents is redundancy plus compounded next-day grogginess, not four independent wins.
  • ⚠ The cluster now has a guideline-grade alternative, and it is not a substance (new 2026-07-12). cbt-insomnia arrives with 53 RCTs (n=13,608) and an effect still measurable 12 months after treatment ends (cbt-i-durable-depression-benefit). Melatonin's evidence — +12 min, in cognitively impaired elders (melatonin-population-caveat-and-limits) — is not in the same weight class. If sleep ever becomes a problem to treat rather than a foundation to protect, the correct move is not another item in this cluster; it is the one intervention with randomised evidence that outlasts stopping. Recorded here; no Status change (CBT-I's population is insomnia patients, and its own vault evidence is on depression, not sleep).
  • Global-ripple / safety: no hard safety interaction with vault interventions; melatonin is well tolerated. No safety_flags set. RETIRED 2026-07-22 — this was an unsourced assertion and a randomized trial now contradicts it. See the global-ripple re-run below. General cautions (next-morning grogginess, interaction with sedatives/anticoagulants outside the vault) remain standard and un-sourced — noted, not graded.
  • ⚠ Global-ripple re-run 2026-07-22 across all 41 maps — one graded interaction edge, two logged leads.
    • glp-1 — graded cross-link. The vault's only other glucose-acting entry. Melatonin's blunting of β-cell negative feedback abolished insulin-induced hypoglycemia in carriers (0 vs 7 events), which is a genuine co-administration consideration for anyone on insulin or an insulin secretagogue. Nobody in this vault's regimen is, and this is not a prescription statement — it is recorded so the edge is on file if the picture changes. safety_flags: [interaction] set on melatonin-dysregulates-insulin-negative-feedback; carried on both maps.
    • magnesium-glycinate / magnesium-l-threonate — noted, not graded. These sit in the same bedtime stack and the vault has already tested magnesium on the same physiology and found it inert (magnesium-null-on-insulin-resistance). So within the wind-down cluster, melatonin is now the one agent with a randomized metabolic signal, and it points the wrong way. That is a ranking-relevant fact about the cluster, not a drug–drug interaction; no flag set on the magnesium maps.
    • intermittent-fasting — logged lead, NOT graded. Endogenous melatonin peaks at night, and the MTNR1B literature's central story is that eating during high-melatonin windows is worse for carriers — which would bear directly on late-eating and time-restricted-eating windows. This source does not test that; it studies exogenous melatonin. A plausible mechanism is not a graded interaction (shared/safety.md); logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md awaiting a vault-quality source.
    • sleep-regularity and cbt-insomnia — sharpened, not changed. Both are non-substance levers with no metabolic question attached. The vault already preferred them; the preference is now better argued rather than newly formed.
    • No confirm/refute or safety edge found on any other map. The exercise, nutrition and recovery levers do not interact with exogenous melatonin on any evidence this vault holds.
  • ⚠ The whole wind-down cluster now has a measured ceiling (new 2026-07-19). Mei 2025 pooled 28 RCTs across the entire supplement shelf — including melatonin's neighbours tryptophan, GABA, magnesium, zinc, vitamin D, omega-3 and ashwagandha — and returned PSQI MD −0.70 (−1.37 to −0.03), an effect whose entire interval sits below the instrument's ~3-point MID (supplement-sleep-effect-small-and-below-clinical-threshold), on trials of 7–50 people with detected funnel asymmetry (supplement-sleep-trials-are-small-and-heterogeneous). The pooled node cannot speak about melatonin specifically — a dozen unrelated substances in one estimate (supplement-sleep-benefit-not-attributable-to-any-supplement) — so this map's own reading is untouched: +12 min, in cognitively impaired elders, best used as a short-term circadian tool. What changes is the category-level conclusion: the shelf as a whole underperforms exercise+CBT (−2.80) roughly four to one. Still redundant within the cluster; still nothing to buy.
