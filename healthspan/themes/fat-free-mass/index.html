Fat-free mass as a mortality marker — exercise

Status: emerging (2026-07-18) — auto-forked this run from resistance-training, flagged for human review. Forked because it is not an intervention at all: it is an observational marker literature about the tissue resistance training builds, and it kept getting read as if it were evidence for the lever. Separating it makes the boundary explicit.

Nothing on this map WAS something David can do — that was true from the fork until 2026-07-22, when a 34-RCT synthesis arrived showing fat-free mass during calorie restriction is modifiable by roughly half with training. The map's founding claim is amended, not abandoned: the marker→mortality literature is still observational and still licenses nothing, but "there is no interventional evidence on this map" is no longer accurate. See the 2026-07-22 through-line entry. It remains context for why the parent map matters, priced honestly — and a standing warning against a specific invalid inference.

What we believe now

Low fat-free mass tracks with dying sooner — and that is an association about a marker, not a case for building muscle. A 49-cohort meta-analysis (n=1,149,807, 83,798 deaths) finds low vs high fat-free mass associated with all-cause mortality RR 1.42 (1.30–1.55), falling to RR 1.31 (1.20–1.44) after the authors' own trim-and-fill correction for publication bias they detected themselves (fat-free-mass-mortality-association-observational). The vault quotes both numbers, never the flattering one alone. Men 1.56 (1.32–1.85) vs women 1.25 (1.11–1.39), p_diff<0.0001 (fat-free-mass-sex-difference-mortality).

The tell the vault leads with: the effect shrinks as the instrument improves. Calf circumference RR 2.19 > DXA 1.52 > BIA 1.23 (fat-free-mass-measurement-method-gradient). A genuine tissue effect should not get weaker as the measurement gets better. That gradient is what a frailty/illness marker looks like — and reverse causation (cancer cachexia, occult disease destroying lean mass before it kills) is unaddressed anywhere in the accessible text.

What this map does NOT license. Nothing here shows that building fat-free mass lowers mortality. That is an interventional claim and this is an observational marker design. The parent map's own mortality evidence (resistance-training-lowers-mortality) is what supports lifting; this map raises the stakes of that evidence without adding to it.

Read down the third column and the pattern is uncomfortable for the map's framing. Exercise preserves roughly 0.87 kg; a protein-containing powder with no exercise preserved 1.3 kg; and the arm that got both exercise and protein — at ~1 g/kg/day, below the plateau protein-target carries — still lost 2.1–2.3 kg. The variable that best orders those three results is not exercise. It is how much protein people were eating.

⚠ The vault names that as a hypothesis and refuses to grade it, because none of the three trials randomised protein intake and Jiang's supplement differed from its control in at least seven ingredients (hmb-trial-cannot-separate-hmb-from-the-24-grams-of-protein-beside-it). Three trials pointing the same way across different designs is a direction, not a finding (shared/safety.md). What would settle it is the trial nobody has run: a protein-matched, isonitrogenous comparison during an energy deficit.

⚠ And Jiang carries a second uncontrolled difference that matters more than the first. Its arms lost 6.6 kg vs 1.5 kg of fat on an identical prescribed deficit with energy-matched powders — so achieved intake almost certainly diverged, most likely through the satiety of 24 g protein and 9.5 g fibre against 65 g of maltodextrin (energy-matched-supplement-arms-diverged-fourfold-on-fat-loss). ⚑ That confound runs AGAINST the muscle finding rather than producing it — a steeper deficit normally costs more lean mass, and this arm on the steeper deficit lost none — but it voids the fat, visceral-fat and basal-metabolic-rate comparisons entirely.

Why emerging: large and consistent, but observational throughout, with self-detected publication bias, an instrument gradient pointing at confounding, and unaddressed reverse causation. E-value 2.19; leave-one-out 1.39–1.43. The interventional half is now three trials deep and still cannot attribute its effect to a named ingredient.

  • 2026-07-20 — ⚠ Two independent sources, one night, both pointing away from muscle mass as the thing that matters — and the map does NOT move. (a) Yu 2026 (Frontiers in Public Health, 24 RCTs, n=1,298, sarcopenic obesity): across nine strategies, no intervention moved skeletal muscle index — reported by only 5 of 24 trials, 80% low certainty — while resistance training clearly raised grip strength (sarcopenic-obesity-muscle-index-unmoved-by-every-intervention). (b) Vetter 2026 (Biomarker Research, BASE-II, n=1,083, 316 deaths): racing 14 expert-consensus aging biomarkers against mortality, muscle mass did not predict all-cause mortality while handgrip strength did (most-consensus-aging-biomarkers-did-not-predict-mortality). Muscle mass is thus, across these two sources, both the outcome interventions failed to move and the marker that failed to predict — while strength is what moved and what predicted. Status held emerging, deliberately, and the restraint is the point. Neither source is close to sufficient to fade this map: Vetter is one convenience-recruited single-city cohort of 1,083 with restricted range (which attenuates exactly this kind of association by construction), no replication, and fourteen uncorrected comparisons; Yu's SMI null is the weakest-evidenced result in its own paper. Both are appraised mixed and capped suggestive, and per shared/evidence-model.md a suggestive result cannot move a map. Against them stands this map's founding source: 49 cohorts, n=1,149,807, 83,798 deaths, RR 1.42. A million-person association is not overturned by a thousand-person null. Recorded as a genuine tension a human should look at — logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md — not as a downgrade.

  • 2026-07-22 — ⚑ The map's first actionable source, and it partly redraws the map's own boundary. Ingested Deller 2026 (Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism, systematic review + pairwise meta-analysis + network meta-analysis, 34 RCTs, n=1,455, overweight/obese adults; PROSPERO CRD420251058428; no conflicts; PMID 42144246): adding exercise to calorie restriction preserved +0.87 kg fat-free mass (95% CI +0.59, +1.16; p ≤ 0.001; I² = 62.1%) versus dieting alone — ~45.7% of FFM loss prevented (abstract; the discussion says 54%, an internal inconsistency recorded rather than adjudicated) — without costing weight loss (body mass −0.88 kg, i.e. slightly more). Crucially, meta-regression rejected the obvious confound: the FFM benefit was not explained by weight-loss magnitude in any of three models (R² = 0% each) nor by training volume (exercise-preserves-fat-free-mass-during-calorie-restriction). The NMA ranked mixed training +1.20 kg (P-score 0.93) > strength +0.83 kg (p=0.013) > endurance +0.51 kg (p=0.067, short of significance), with inconsistency non-significant for FFM but substantial for body mass (p<0.001), so only the muscle ranking is quoted (mixed-and-strength-training-outrank-endurance-for-preserving-lean-mass). Appraised mixed — registered, conflict-free, head-to-head-only, and it tests and rejects its own best alternative explanation; against self-detected publication bias on the primary outcome that the authors leave uncorrected while stating the effect "may be overestimated," PEDro mean 5 ("fair"), RoB 2 41% high risk, FFM measured by BIA in 9 of 34 trials (the instrument this map's own gradient concept distrusts, during the weight loss that most disturbs it), and skeletal muscle mass reported by only 2 of 34 trials. Capped suggestive; the mode ranking single-study. extends; Status held emerging. Wiley open-access via Projekt DEAL but the Europe PMC deposit has not propagated; full text retrieved via web-to-md, no PDF binary — pdf_unavailable logged with rungs tried.

  • 2026-07-31 — The Deller countermeasure gets its first look at what it leaves behind, and the residual is about two kilograms of lean mass plus hip bone. Status held emerging (Nava 2026, DEMFOS, Lancet Healthy Longevity, randomised assessor-masked trial, ITT, n=114, adults 65–85 with obesity and mild-to-moderate frailty; VA-funded, no pharmaceutical sponsor — ingested primarily for metformin). Its two intensive- lifestyle arms ran a 500–750 kcal/day deficit at ~1 g/kg/day protein for six months with 30 minutes of progressive resistance training three times a week, and every participant received calcium ~1500 mg/day and vitamin D 1000 IU/day. DXA lean mass still fell 2.1–2.3 kg, MRI thigh muscle volume fell 79–92 cm³ (~4%), and total hip bone mineral density fell 0.011–0.012 g/cm² — the bone loss significantly greater than the education arm (−0.013, −0.021 to −0.005), while lumbar spine BMD did not differ. Total weight loss was 8.9–10.7 kg, so roughly a fifth to a quarter of it was lean tissue (weight-loss-with-supervised-training-still-costs-lean-mass-and-hip-bone). ⚠ This does NOT contradict exercise-preserves-fat-free-mass-during-calorie-restriction and cannot test it — DEMFOS has no diet-without-exercise arm, so it never runs Deller's comparison. It supplies the other half of the sentence: the countermeasure works and is not sufficient. extends. ⚠ The protein prescription is worth naming and refusing to build on: ~1 g/kg/day sits well below the ~1.6 g/kg/day plateau protein-target carries, in exactly the population and deficit where that gap would be expected to show — but DEMFOS randomised no protein intake, so the vault records the coincidence and states no dose (shared/safety.md). Appraised reliable; the concept graded single-study because it is one trial, 89% men, one centre, six months, with osteoporosis excluded at entry.

  • 2026-08-01 — ⚑ THE FIRST PURELY NUTRITIONAL COUNTERMEASURE ON THIS MAP, AND IT WORKS — but the trial is named after the wrong ingredient. Status held emerging. Ingested Jiang 2026 (Journal of Cachexia, Sarcopenia and Muscle, randomised, double-blind, energy-matched RCT, n=102 Chinese adults with obesity (BMI ≥ 28), mean age 36.8 ± 5.2, 37.3% female, 12 weeks; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04953936; funded by the Sichuan Science and Technology Department with a stated no-role clause and no supplement manufacturer anywhere in the funding or author list; appraised mixed; CC BY PDF archived 2.64 MB via the browser rung). A 65 g/day powder containing 3.0 g calcium-HMB, 24 g protein, 9.5 g fibre, 9.3 g fat, L-glutamine, wheat oligopeptides and vitamins D and E (237 kcal) versus 65 g/day of energy-matched maltodextrin, alongside a prescribed deficit of (height cm − 105) × 25 kcal/kg/day and ~8 video counselling sessions. Skeletal muscle mass (BIA, InBody 770): CaHMB median +0.7 kg (IQR −0.2 to 2.0) vs control −0.6 kg (−1.9 to −0.1); between-group 1.3 kg (0.5–1.9), p < 0.001; 52.9% vs 11.8% gained ≥0.5 kg, OR 8.44 (3.23–25.22); basal metabolic rate +62.0 kcal/day (35.0–89.0); visceral fat −14.9 cm² (−25.1 to −4.5); body fat −4.9 kg (−6.8 to −3.1); fasting glucose −0.4 mmol/L; HOMA-IR, lipids and blood pressure null; no adverse events; attrition 5.9%, ITT with multiple imputation (hmb-enriched-supplement-preserved-muscle-through-a-calorie-deficit-without-exercise, suggestive). ⚑ The design does one thing no other source on this map does: it isolates nutrition from training completely — sedentary participants by inclusion criterion, no exercise prescribed, and IPAQ-SF measured at five timepoints with no between-group difference at any of them. That is the comparison multidomain-lifestyle's standing unseparable-bundle complaint has been asking for. ⚠ And it is undone by its own comparator. The intervention differed from maltodextrin in at least seven components; only energy was matched. The authors concede it — "Because the intervention also contained protein, longer factorial trials are needed to isolate CaHMB-specific effects" — but the title names only HMB, and this is the vault's tenth attribution failure and its cleanest: not a bundle of behaviours that resist separation, but a single powder whose ingredient list a second arm could have separated (hmb-trial-cannot-separate-hmb-from-the-24-grams-of-protein-beside-it, single-study). ⚠ Second uncontrolled difference, unaddressed by the paper: the arms did not lose comparable weight. Fat mass fell 6.6 vs 1.5 kg on an identical prescribed deficit with energy-matched powders; the measured BMR advantage (+62 kcal/day) accounts for at most ~1.5 kg of a 4.9 kg gap, so achieved intake diverged — 24 g protein plus 9.5 g fibre satiate, 65 g maltodextrin does not (energy-matched-supplement-arms-diverged-fourfold-on-fat-loss, single-study). The confound runs against the muscle finding and voids the fat/VFA/BMR ones. ⚠ Instrument caution, from this map's own gradient: the primary outcome is BIA, weakest on the calf > DXA > BIA ordering fat-free-mass-measurement-method-gradient documents — and BIA infers lean mass from body water in two arms that differed in protein, fibre and fat. A DXA replication is the single thing that would most change how this reads. ✅ Conduct credit, because it is unusually good: stratified blocked randomisation, sequentially numbered opaque sealed envelopes, true double-blinding through analysis, a prospective power calculation (assumed d = 0.93, target 72, expanded to 102), ITT, 5.9% attrition, registration before enrolment. ⚑ NO hmb MAP COINED — the twelfth candidate-intervention refusal, on the narrow ground that a map named after a compound this evidence cannot isolate would assert in its filename what the experiment does not support. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md. extends; Status unchanged (emerging).

  • ⚑ 2026-07-31 — the lean-mass tax is a property of the DEFICIT, not of the method used to create it. glp-1 holds that pharmacologic weight loss costs lean mass, with training-plus-protein as the proposed countermeasure (glp1-lean-mass-loss-magnitude, glp1-intake-reduction-makes-the-protein-target-harder-to-hit).   • ⚑ THE DELLER RULE NOW HAS A SECOND, INDEPENDENT INSTANCE — and it is not exercise (new 2026-07-25). This map's first actionable finding was that adding exercise to calorie restriction preserves +0.87 kg of fat-free mass — about half the loss — without costing weight loss, with meta-regression rejecting the obvious confound (exercise-preserves-fat-free-mass-during-calorie-restriction). Duan 2026 (Obesity Reviews, 30 studies, n=10,944) supplies the mirror term from a completely separate literature: in two RCTs that randomised sleep restriction alongside calorie restriction, fat-mass loss was consistently reduced (sleep-restriction-reduces-fat-loss-during-calorie-restriction, carried on sleep-duration with safety_flags: [interaction]). Stated as a rule the vault can now carry across two sources: during an energy deficit, the co-factors decide which tissue you give up — exercise protects lean mass, and short sleep costs you fat. Neither finding is about how much weight comes off. ⚠ Three limits, applied consistently with how this map treats Deller. The sleep evidence is two trials; it reports no pooled magnitude (the review could not meta-analyse); and it is in adults with BMI ≥25 undergoing behavioural weight loss, so it does NOT transfer to a glp-1 population — the same non-transfer refusal this map has stood on since 2026-07-18 applies unchanged. And this map's own instrument scepticism applies here too: the sleep corpus measured sleep mostly by questionnaire, and body composition by whatever each trial used. Status held.
  • ⚠ THE glp-1 NON-TRANSFER — the leap this vault pre-refuses. It is tempting to chain two facts the vault holds — GLP-1 drugs strip 1.7–5.4 kg of lean mass (glp1-lean-mass-loss-magnitude; tirzepatide −8.3%, tirzepatide-largest-fat-and-lean-mass-loss) and low fat-free mass tracks with 42% higher mortality — into "GLP-1 lean-mass loss will kill you." That chain does not hold, and it is refused in advance. Haas measured untreated community-dwelling adults, where low lean mass is substantially an illness/frailty marker (its own instrument gradient says so). GLP-1 lean-mass loss is pharmacologic, deliberate, and accompanied by large fat loss, in a population where the randomised evidence (SELECT) shows lower mortality despite the muscle cost (glp1-mace-benefit-rct-select). No safety_flags are set on this basis. What would actually resolve it: a trial measuring muscle FUNCTION — strength, power, gait speed, falls — during GLP-1 treatment. After four ingests bearing on this, not one exists. Do not fabricate it, and do not substitute the observational marker for it. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md; carried identically on glp-1 and resistance-training.
