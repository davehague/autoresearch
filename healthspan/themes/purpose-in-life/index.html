Purpose in life (meaning, direction, "ikigai") — stress & mental

Status: emerging (2026-07-11) — auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. An individual-participant-data meta-analysis pooling 25 samples across the US, Europe, and Asia (488,765 participants, 48,928 deaths, up to 32 years of follow-up) finds a stronger sense of purpose in life associated with ~30% lower risk of earlier mortality (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.70–0.83). The association survives adjustment for behavioral and clinical risk factors (HR 0.85) and, separately, for depression (HR 0.91). Opened at emerging by the observational ceiling — not by weak evidence.

What we believe now

Having a reason to get up in the morning tracks with living longer, and the association is unusually hard to explain away. The headline number is large (HR 0.76) and it is drawn from the biggest, most geographically diverse base of any single source in this vault. But the number that actually matters is the third one.

(1) The depression-adjusted HR is the load-bearing result. The obvious rival explanation for "purposeful people live longer" is that purposelessness is a symptom, not a cause — that depression, illness, frailty, and cognitive decline all destroy a sense of purpose, and it is the underlying disease doing the killing. Sutin 2026 tests exactly that, and the association shrinks a great deal but does not vanish: HR 0.76 → 0.91 once depression is controlled (purpose-in-life-mortality-persists-depression-adjusted). Read that honestly in both directions. It means most of the raw 30% is not purpose per se — a large share is depression and its correlates. It also means something survives that is not depression. The vault records both halves, because the flattering half alone would be a lie.

(2) The behavioral adjustment tells you the mechanism is partly just… the other levers. Controlling for behavioral and clinical risk factors pulls the HR to 0.85. Some of purpose's apparent benefit plausibly runs through things this vault already tracks — purposeful people move more, sleep better, smoke less. The source does not decompose the adjustment by named covariate, so the vault does not assert a specific mediation chain (daily-steps, sleep-regularity, resistance-training are the plausible candidates, and that plausibility is not a graded finding).

(3) This is the vault's first intervention that may never be randomizable — and that is a structural ceiling, not a temporary one. You cannot allocate 500 people to a life of meaning and 500 to a control arm. Every other map here has at least a hypothetical RCT path — you can randomize steps, protein, creatine, a sleep schedule, even a Mediterranean diet (PREDIMED did). Purpose in life has no interventional evidence at all in this source (purpose-in-life-mortality-no-interventional-evidence). So the usual promise — "this will firm up when the trial lands" — may simply never come due here. That is worth stating plainly rather than leaving as an implied someday.

Why emerging, not higher. The vault's standing rule is that observational syntheses open at emerging regardless of size — daily-steps (HR 0.53, 160k), mediterranean-diet (1.8M), sedentary-behavior (2.8M) all sit there. Size does not buy a tier. 488,765 people is the largest base in the vault and it changes nothing about that rule. Reverse causation is attenuated by the depression adjustment but not excluded (the source does not report lag analyses or early-death exclusions), the exposure is self-reported across heterogeneous purpose instruments, and healthy-user / socioeconomic confounding remains live (the effect varied somewhat by education).

What it costs: nothing. No dose, no product, no equipment, no daily maintenance, no sleep cost, and no conceivable interaction with anything else in the vault. On GOAL.md's terms — least work, least discomfort, healthspan not lifespan, living a happy life — this is the only map in the vault whose intervention is the goal itself rather than a means to it. That is a genuinely odd and interesting position, and the regimen layer should be careful not to turn it into a task.
