Sedentary behaviour (sitting time, television viewing) — exercise

Status: emerging (2026-07-10) — auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. An umbrella review of 30 systematic reviews (83 unique studies, 2,778,595 participants) finds sedentary time and television viewing time track with higher all-cause mortality even after accounting for physical activity, and consistently across device-measured and self-reported exposure. Opened at emerging by the observational ceiling and by an unexcluded reverse-causation explanation — not by weak evidence.

This map reports no effect size, because its source does not publish one. An umbrella review of reviews reports direction, consistency, and quality, not a pooled hazard ratio. Per shared/safety.md, no number is invented to fill the gap.

What we believe now

Sitting is a distinct exposure from exercise, and you cannot out-train it. Across 30 high-quality reviews (mean Joanna Briggs quality 9.6 ± 1.6 / 11), 29 of 30 found more sedentary time associated with higher all-cause mortality. Adjusting for physical activity attenuated the association without erasing it — which is the entire reason this earns its own map rather than a bullet on daily-steps. A person can walk 7,000 steps and still spend eleven waking hours seated; the evidence says those hours carry their own risk.

Two framing points the vault should carry:

(1) The measurement objection does not apply here — which is unusual. Results held across device-based accelerometry and self-report alike. That is a real methodological distinction from sleep-duration, whose entire evidence base is self-reported and which the vault therefore holds beneath sleep-regularity. Here, recall bias is not a live explanation. Reverse causation still is.

(2) The lever is the discretionary slice, not the whole exposure. Occupational sitting is frequently not a person's to change; television and evening screen time is (tv-viewing-time-mortality-association — 7 of 7 reviews examining it were meta-analyses, all positive). GOAL.md's least-work philosophy points squarely at the part that competes with nothing: break up the seated evening block. It is free, needs no equipment, and is entirely knee-safe during patellar-contusion recovery.

Why emerging, not higher: every study inside the umbrella is observational — sedentary time cannot be randomized across decades — and reverse causation is unexcluded. Illness, frailty, and pain all cause sitting; no lag analyses or early-death exclusions are reported. This is the same "cart before the horse" lens that capped the long-sleep arm of sleep-duration. The association surviving physical-activity adjustment, and replicating across instruments, is what lifts it above that tail — but the design ceiling is the same one that holds daily-steps, zone-2-cardio, and resistance-training at emerging. And with no pooled effect size published, the magnitude is genuinely unknown.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-10 — Ingested Shivgulam 2026 (Preventive Medicine, umbrella review / systematic review of systematic reviews, 30 reviews, 83 unique studies, n=2,778,595; searched inception–March 2025; JBI + ROBIS quality appraisal, PRISMA): 29 of 30 reviews found sedentary time or television viewing time associated with higher all-cause mortality; the association remained significant, though attenuated, after accounting for physical activity, and was consistent across device-based and self-report measures. Auto-coins the map at emerging (hard endpoint, enormous base, observational ceiling, reverse causation unexcluded, no pooled effect estimate published). Capped suggestive.

  • 2026-07-18 — Ingested Vanherle 2026 (Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports, randomized crossover trial, n=16 sedentary adults with dyslipidaemia, mean age 47.8 ± 9.3, BMI 24.6; registered NCT06277713 before enrolment; free-living desk-based setting, objectively measured exposure) as the map's first randomised source and the first test of the actionable version of its own claim. Three two-day conditions — prolonged sitting (SIT), sitting + a 60-min moderate cycle bout (FIT), and standing breaks 30 min/h + that bout (MIX). Postprandial insulin was lower in MIX than SIT (p=0.041) and lower than FIT (p=0.010) — the second contrast is load-bearing, because FIT already contains the workout, so the breaks did something the exercise did not (sitting-breaks-add-insulin-benefit-beyond-exercise). Postprandial triglycerides (p=0.112) and glucose (p=0.150) were flat across all three conditions — including SIT vs FIT, meaning an hour of moderate cycling also failed to register, which is a trial reporting its own power rather than biology (sitting-breaks-null-on-glucose-and-triglycerides). This closes — partially — the gap this map logged against itself on 2026-07-10: no source yet tests whether breaking up sitting changes outcomes, as opposed to total sitting predicting them. One now does. Appraised mixed (right design, registered, objective exposure, free-living — against n=16, an uncorrected three-endpoint family with one survivor, and a 6-hour surrogate horizon); insulin concept graded single-study, nulls suggestive. extends; Status unchanged (emerging — a 16-person postprandial surrogate cannot promote a 2.8-million-person mortality map, and the reasons for emerging are untouched). Full text confirmed non-OA (Wiley; Unpaywall oa_status: closed), pdf_unavailable logged.

  • 2026-07-22 — Ingested Zhuang 2026 (International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity, systematic review + three-level meta-analysis, 21 randomized crossover trials, n=433; PROSPERO CRD420251273873 registered 26 Dec 2025; RoB 2 + GRADE; no external funding, no competing interests; PMID 42443952) as the map's third evidence layer and its first on cognition. Interrupting prolonged sitting with brief standing or activity breaks improved executive function (k=16, g = 0.18, 95% CI 0.03–0.32, p=0.016) and memory (k=10, g = 0.37, 0.13–0.61, p=0.003), both GRADE low certainty (sitting-breaks-small-acute-cognitive-benefit). Attention was flat — g = −0.08 (−0.38 to 0.22, k=5) — the tightest estimate in the paper, while global cognition (k=2) and information processing (k=4) were wide, underpowered non-findings the vault refuses to report as the same kind of null (sitting-breaks-null-on-attention). Appraised mixed (registered, unfunded, conflict-free, randomized-crossover-only, a three-level model with cluster-robust variance that genuinely solves the effect-size dependency problem, and authors who refuse their own prescriptive overclaim — against 433 participants, five outcome families with two significant and no cross-family correction, acute single-session exposure only, and exploratory moderators that contradict each other, higher break frequency favouring executive function while lower frequency favours memory). Capped suggestive. extends; Status held emerging — an acute cognitive meta-analysis does not touch the observational ceiling or the reverse-causation problem that put this map where it is. IJBNPA is fully open access but the deposit has not propagated; pdf_unavailable logged with rungs tried — retry.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Not redundant with daily-steps — this is the map's reason for existing. The umbrella's central finding is that the mortality association survives adjustment for physical activity. Steps measure what you do; sedentary time measures what you don't. They are separate exposures on the same clock, and at regimen time they must be counted as two levers, not one — the opposite of the sleep-duration ↔ sleep-regularity ruling, and for the opposite reason (there, the same behaviour measured twice; here, two behaviours competing for the same hours). Cross-linked on both maps.
  • Not cancelled by zone-2-cardio or resistance-training either. The same adjustment applies. A structured training session does not buy back the seated hours around it. Noted on this map; the exercise Foundations remain what they were, and gain a companion rather than a competitor.
  • Adjacent to the sleep levers, but ungraded (logged, not fabricated). Prolonged evening television sits temporally next to sleep-regularity — the vault's strongest Foundation — and next to the caffeine-timing cutoff window. Whether TV time erodes sleep regularity or merely co-occurs with it is not tested by this source. A plausible mechanism is not a graded interaction (shared/safety.md); it is logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md and awaits a vault-quality source. No safety_flags are set on that basis.
  • Methodological sibling to sleep-duration, with one crucial difference. Both are large observational syntheses of a behaviour that cannot be randomized, and both face reverse causation. Sleep-duration's exposure was self-reported throughout; this one replicates across accelerometry and questionnaire. That is why sleep-duration was folded beneath sleep-regularity as "the weaker lever, measured worse," while sedentary behaviour opens as a lever in its own right.
  • ⚑ The "two exposures, not one" claim now has a randomised echo (new 2026-07-18). This map's reason for existing has always been an adjustment argument: the mortality association survives controlling for physical activity (sedentary-time-independent-of-exercise-mortality-risk). Vanherle 2026 supplies the experimental version — standing breaks beat the trial's own exercise arm on postprandial insulin (p=0.010), meaning the breaks contributed something a 60-minute ride did not (sitting-breaks-add-insulin-benefit-beyond-exercise). One 16-person trial on a surrogate, so it corroborates the framing rather than proving it — but the framing is no longer only statistical adjustment. Carried on daily-steps and zone-2-cardio too.
  • Global-ripple: re-run 2026-07-18 across all 36 maps — no safety interaction found with any vault intervention. No supplement, drug, or recovery modality in the vault interacts with sitting time; the new randomised source adds no interaction either (its only co-intervention is moderate cycling, which is zone-2-cardio and is complementary by construction). There is no dose, no product, and nothing to buy — which is, on GOAL.md's terms, the highest recommendation an intervention can carry. One adjacent note: the run's other ingest flagged occlusion as reducing walking volume (bfr-occlusion-reduces-walking-volume-and-comfort) — that interaction lands on daily-steps and blood-flow-restriction, not here, since sitting time is a separate exposure.
  • ⚑ Three designs, three outcome types, one behaviour (new 2026-07-22). This map now holds an observational mortality association across 2.8 million people, a randomised metabolic benefit that beat its own trial's exercise arm, and a randomised same-day cognitive readout. They are independent literatures agreeing about the same free, equipment-free, knee-safe action. The convergence is the map's strongest argument — and it is an argument about consistency, not about magnitude: the mortality estimate has no published effect size, the insulin trial had 16 people, and the cognitive effect is g = 0.18 at low certainty. Three modest, well-bounded findings pointing the same way is exactly what this vault treats as more persuasive than one impressive number — and the reasoning is recorded here so a future run does not quietly upgrade "consistent" into "large."
  • The attention null is a reusable methodological note, not just a result. If breaking up sitting worked through simple arousal, attention is the domain that should have moved most; it is the one that moved least, at g = −0.08 with a tight interval, while executive function and memory did move (sitting-breaks-null-on-attention). Domain-specificity constrains the mechanism and deflates the standing-desk marketing in one stroke. Compare fat-free-mass's instrument gradient: when the pattern inside a result contradicts the simplest story about it, believe the pattern.
  • Open gap (new 2026-07-22): durability. All 21 cognitive trials are single-session laboratory paradigms with immediate post-testing. A healthspan ledger cares whether the habit sustained over years changes anything, and this literature cannot say. The same limit applies to the map's metabolic evidence (a 6-hour postprandial horizon). The map's mortality claim is chronic and observational; its experimental claims are all acute. Nothing bridges them yet — logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md, not fabricated.
  • Global-ripple re-run 2026-07-22 across all 41 maps — no safety interaction, unchanged. Breaking up sitting has no dose, no product, and nothing to buy; it cannot interact with anything in the vault. One exploratory note recorded and deliberately not carried into any recommendation: the cognitive effects were larger in older adults and in people with overweight or obesity — the authors label these exploratory and so does the vault.
