NOVOS Core (12-ingredient longevity-supplement blend) — supplements

Status: emerging (2026-11-12) — ⚑ auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. A commercial, company-partnered proprietary blend tested as a whole in a small (n=61 randomized, 43 completers), 6-month RCT with real randomization/blinding but a substantial funding/authorship conflict. The vault's first branded multi-ingredient "longevity stack" product with its own dedicated human RCT.

What we believe now

A 15 g/day proprietary powder (vitamin C, magnesium malate, calcium alpha-ketoglutarate, glycine, glucosamine sulphate, Rhodiola rosea extract, L-theanine, hyaluronic acid, fisetin, ginger root extract, pterostilbene, lithium aspartate) improved vascular surrogates over 6 months in healthy adults ≥40: flow-mediated dilation rose 2.9 percentage points more than placebo (p<0.0001, measurable within an hour of the first dose), arterial stiffness (pulse-wave velocity) fell 1.18 m/s (p=0.006), peripheral systolic blood pressure fell 6.1 mmHg (p=0.014), and projected 10-year cardiovascular risk fell 0.45 points (p=0.036). Lipids were unchanged. All endpoints are vascular surrogates — no hard cardiovascular outcome (event or mortality) was measured or statistically possible at this n and duration. The 12-ingredient blend was tested only as a whole: no component (fisetin, pterostilbene, Rhodiola rosea, etc.) can be individually credited.

Why emerging, not higher, despite a clean primary-endpoint p-value: small completer sample (n=43), surrogate-only outcomes, and a funding/authorship structure (product manufacturer supplied materials and an unrestricted grant; a company employee co-authored; the senior author discloses company research funding) that compounds rather than merely accompanies the design's other limits.
