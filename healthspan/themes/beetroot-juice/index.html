Beetroot juice (dietary nitrate) — supplements

Status: emerging (2026-08-02) — auto-coined this run, flagged for human review. Coined from a single systematic review + meta-analysis of 38 placebo-controlled trials (n = 703), on a surrogate endpoint (VO₂max) with detected publication bias. Opened at emerging and it will take a hard endpoint to move it. git is the undo.

What we believe now

Beetroot juice produces a small increase in maximal oxygen uptake in recreationally active adults, and there is no evidence that it changes anything a person would notice off an exercise-testing ergometer.

The pooled effect is SMD 0.24 (95% CI 0.11–0.37), falling to 0.19 (0.07–0.31) once trim-and-fill models the five studies the funnel plot says are missing — Egger's test detects small-study effects at p = 0.014 (beetroot-juice-raises-vo2max-by-a-small-amount). The effect is entirely a function of how untrained you are: recreationally active 0.38 (0.21–0.55) at high GRADE certainty, moderately trained 0.17, highly trained/elite 0.07 (−0.09 to 0.23), not significant at low certainty (beetroot-juice-vo2max-effect-disappears-in-trained-athletes). Dose shows a threshold then a plateau — null below 6.4 mmol nitrate, 0.27 in the standard 6.4–12.8 mmol band, no larger above it — and three or more consecutive days beats a single acute dose (beetroot-nitrate-dose-plateaus-in-the-standard-band).

⚑ Why this map exists when fourteen other candidates were refused, stated plainly because it is the whole justification. This vault has declined beta-alanine, probiotics, collagen (twice), hypoxic training, cocoa flavanols, green exercise, whole-body vibration, traditional Chinese sports, exercise snacks and exergaming. Those refusals turned overwhelmingly on one thing: the design could not separate the thing being sold from the things beside it. This corpus answers that question with its comparator. The standard placebo here is nitrate-depleted beetroot juice — the identical beverage with the nitrate stripped out — used in 34 of the 38 trials, and restricting the analysis to those trials returns SMD 0.25 against the full pool's 0.24 (nitrate-depleted-placebo-isolates-the-beetroot-effect). The isolating contrast exists and the effect survives it. Add 22 of 38 trials at low risk of bias, zero at high risk, a machine-measured outcome, publication bias detected and corrected for, and no funding and no conflicts in a commercially saturated literature — and the bar the refusals failed is cleared.

⚑ On the name. The obvious alternative is dietary-nitrate, and the depleted-juice placebo arguably licenses it: the contrast really does isolate the nitrate rather than the beetroot. The map is named for the beverage anyway — four of the thirty-eight trials did not use a depleted placebo, the dose is delivered as juice, and what a person buys is a bottle of concentrate. The nitrate attribution lives in the map, with its evidence attached, rather than being asserted in a filename. Same rule that refused to coin a map called hmb, applied in the direction the evidence supports.

⚠ Why this is nowhere near a healthspan claim, and why emerging is generous rather than harsh. Every outcome in this corpus is VO₂max measured during a graded exercise test, usually on a supplement day, usually in a crossover. No trial measured anything after a washout, anything sustained, or any health outcome. zone-2-cardio holds the reason to care about aerobic fitness at all — cardiorespiratory-fitness-all-cause-mortality, a 122,007-person cohort where mortality falls with fitness and shows no ceiling — and that is fitness people had, not fitness a drink produced for an afternoon. An acute change in a fitness measurement and a change in fitness are different things, and this literature has only tested the first.

The practical bottom line. Beetroot juice is a food, it is cheap, it carries no interaction anywhere in this vault, and its effect is best evidenced in exactly the population GOAL.md describes. Nothing here argues against drinking it. Nothing here makes it a healthspan lever, and against this map's own neighbours it is badly outclassed — training-duration-outperforms-session-volume-for-vo2max says another four weeks of consistent training raises VO₂max by +2.69% (1.25–4.19) on well-powered randomised evidence, which is a better-evidenced route to the same number and free. Not medical advice; no dose is prescribed here — the mmol bands above are the source's, in the source's units.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚑ 2026-08-02 — this map's outcome is zone-2-cardio's anchor, and the two must not be double-banked. VO₂max is the measurement; cardiorespiratory-fitness-all-cause-mortality is why the vault cares about it. But that anchor is a cohort-tier association about fitness people possess, and this map holds an acute, surrogate, supplement-day reading of the same variable. The mortality evidence belongs to zone-2-cardio and is not inherited here — the same rule that stopped a telomere finding from rehabilitating vitamin-d and stopped an epigenetic-clock signal from lifting zone-2-cardio itself. Count the aerobic base once, on the training; this map enters no mortality claim.
  • ⚑ 2026-08-02 — the comparison that actually decides whether this is worth anything, and it is unflattering. zone-2-cardio holds training-duration-outperforms-session-volume-for-vo2max: across 93 trials and 1,188 untrained adults, the one moderator that moved VO₂max was how many weeks you keep going — +0.7%/week (0.3–1.0), +2.69% (1.25–4.19) over four extra weeks — on Bayesian hierarchical meta-regression with published analysis code and no conflicts. That is a better-evidenced, free route to this map's own outcome. Beetroot juice and consistent training are not in competition and are not additive evidence either; the vault records that the behaviour has the stronger case and the supplement is at best an increment on top of it.
  • ⚠ 2026-08-02 — the relationship to training status is the mirror image of creatine-monohydrate's, and the pair is instructive. Creatine's lean-mass benefit exists only alongside resistance training and vanishes without it (creatine-lean-mass-requires-resistance-training), and its strength effect replicates in trained athletes (creatine-strength-benefit-replicates-in-trained-athletes). Beetroot juice runs the other way: the effect is largest in the least trained and undetectable in the most. Two supplements, opposite relationships to the same behavioural lever — and in both cases the behaviour is what is doing the work. Recorded as a coherence observation, not a graded finding.
  • ⚑ 2026-08-02 — the dose curve joins a five-literature pattern and makes it six. Threshold then plateau, the same shape as protein (~1.6 g/kg/day), creatine (flat above ~5 g/day, creatine-dose-response-strength), aerobic volume (aerobic-volume-mortality-dose-response-nonlinear), steps (daily-steps-mortality-dose-response) and exercise for glycaemic control (exercise-glycemic-benefit-starts-near-500-met-minutes-and-plateaus). Six independent literatures, one structural instruction: find the threshold, then stop paying for more. A framing note the vault should carry into future sweeps, not a claim from any one of them.
  • 2026-08-02 — global ripple across all 45 maps: no interaction, no safety flag, and one adjacency worth logging. Walked every other map. Dietary nitrate is a food constituent — beetroot, leafy greens — at doses obtainable from ordinary vegetables, with no compound in this vault it is known to potentiate or blunt. No safety_flags are raised and the concepts carry []. The one adjacency is blood pressure: nitrate is vasoactive and several maps hold blood-pressure surrogates (sauna-heat's passive-heat-modest-bp-drop-in-at-risk, resistance-training's resistance-training-lowers-blood-pressure-older-adults, stretching's contested estimate). This source measured no blood pressure at all, so the vault logs the adjacency as an open gap rather than fabricating an interaction — shared/safety.md permits flagging on a credible mechanistic signal, and a vasodilator meeting antihypertensive levers would qualify, but no source in this vault has tested it. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md; revisit if a sweep surfaces nitrate and blood pressure together.
  • Complementary to zone-2-cardio and orthogonal to resistance-training — an aerobic-surrogate supplement against two behavioural foundation levers. No conflict, no stacking claim, and no independent win to bank.
