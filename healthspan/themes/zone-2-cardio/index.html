Zone-2 cardio — exercise

Status: emerging (2026-07-06) — the aerobic-fitness→longevity signal is large but the vault's evidence is cohort (observational) and measures CRF as the outcome, not Zone-2 as the tested intervention. Honest tier gating keeps this below consolidating. The intensity tension is now sharper: a meta-analysis of RCTs shows HIIT beats moderate-intensity continuous training for VO2max even in healthy elderly — Zone-2 stays justified here by knee-safety and adherence, not by being the optimal intensity.

What we believe now

Higher cardiorespiratory fitness (CRF) tracks with dramatically lower mortality, with no observed ceiling of benefit. Zone-2 (low-intensity, conversational-pace steady-state) is one practical, low-impact route to building that aerobic base — and its low-impact nature makes it knee-friendly, which matters given David's patellar-contusion recovery (GOAL.md): it raises CRF without the joint load of running or high-intensity work.

The intensity tension (Storoschuk 2025 → now RCT-corroborated by Chu 2026): Zone-2 is a practical route, not the uniquely optimal one. Storoschuk (narrative review) argued the "Zone-2 is optimal for mitochondrial/fat-oxidation capacity" claim comes from elite-athlete observation, and that higher intensities drive those adaptations more time-efficiently. Chu 2026 now upgrades that from opinion to meta-analytic RCT evidence in the exact older population: across 16 RCTs in healthy adults 60–80, HIIT raised VO2max more than moderate-intensity continuous training (head-to-head +1.17 mL·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹), in less session time (~21–39 min vs ≥60 min). This refines rather than refutes the map: it targets fitness-adaptation efficiency on a surrogate (VO2max), not mortality, so the CRF→mortality foundation and the map's tier are untouched. The trade-off it sharpens — higher intensity is more efficient but higher joint load — is precisely why the low-impact route was chosen here; an efficiency cost knowingly paid for knee safety, revisitable as the knee recovers.

Randomized evidence that aerobic work moves cellular aging (added 2026-07-09). A PROSPERO-registered meta-analysis of 16 RCTs (n=1,908) finds exercise raises telomerase activity (SMD 0.35, 95% CI 0.20–0.51, I²=39%), carried by the aerobic subgroup (11 of 16 trials), and maintains telomere length on a fragile estimate (SMD 0.59, 0.14–1.06, I²=92%) — with ≥16 weeks required before anything moves (Sun 2025). This is the only randomized, independently-funded evidence in the vault that any intervention touches these markers: omega-3 was null on telomeres in VITAL, and vitamin-d moved them only in a post-hoc sub-study of a trial null on every hard endpoint. The free intervention beats the pills on the pills' favorite biomarker. These remain surrogates, so the Status does not move — the same rule that kept vitamin-d from being rehabilitated by a telomere finding.

The surrogate row now has a second biomarker family (added 2026-07-15). A Lancet Healthy Longevity systematic review + meta-analysis (Shan 2026, 44 studies, 7 cross-sectional pooled, n=145,465) finds physical activity associated with lower epigenetic age on the two most mortality-relevant DNA-methylation clocks — GrimAge EAA −0.09 SD (−0.12 to −0.05) and Horvath EAA −0.03 SD per 1 SD higher MET-min/week — null on Hannum and PhenoAge (physical-activity-lowers-epigenetic-age-cross-sectional). It coheres with the telomere finding but adds no causal weight: it is cross-sectional (Sun's telomere evidence was randomised), the effects are small, and reverse causation is especially live here — biological youth and health enable activity. Same rule, applied harder: a cross-sectional surrogate moves the Status even less than a randomised one.

Minimum effective dose — now answered at the volume level (added 2026-07-15). For the first time the map carries a dose, not just a fitness→mortality association. A 94-cohort dose-response meta-analysis (Garcia 2023, >30M adults, 811,616 deaths) shows aerobic activity lowers mortality on a steeply non-linear curve whose benefit is front-loaded: ~150 min/week of moderate-to-vigorous activity (8.75 mMET-h/wk) → all-cause RR 0.69, with half that (~75 min/wk) already ~23%, and little added benefit beyond 17.5 mMET-h/wk (aerobic-volume-mortality-dose-response-nonlinear). An independent Chinese cohort (Wang 2025, n=109,407) lands the floor at the same ~75 min/week from a different population and a different unit (moderate-activity-75min-week-mortality-minimum). The practical read: the first ~75–150 min/week is where the mortality benefit lives — the same "less is enough" shape as resistance-training (~90–120 min/wk) and daily-steps (~7,000 not 10,000). Both are observational with self-reported exposure and measure aerobic volume, not Zone-2 specifically, so this fills the dose gap without lifting Status off emerging.

Honest gap: the vault now holds an aerobic-volume→mortality dose-response (Garcia/Wang, minimum ~75 min/wk) but still only at the observational tier and for total volume, not a randomized Zone-2 protocol; the intensity comparison is now RCT-pooled but on a surrogate endpoint (VO2max), not mortality; and the telomere/telomerase evidence is likewise surrogate, from mostly clinical populations (cancer, PCOS, post-MI, menopause) rather than healthy middle-aged adults. Knee-friendliness is a property of the modality (low-impact steady-state), not a study finding here.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-09 — Ingested Sun 2025 (Frontiers in Physiology, meta-analysis, 16 RCTs, n=1,908, PROSPERO CRD420251006569): exercise enhanced telomerase activity SMD 0.35 (0.20–0.51), I²=39% — the aerobic subgroup carrying it (SMD 0.33, P=0.0001) — and maintained telomere length SMD 0.59 (0.14–1.06) on a fragile, I²=92% estimate; ≥16 weeks needed; no publication bias (Egger's P=0.66 / 0.24); independently funded. extends on a cellular-aging surrogate; Status unchanged (emerging) — the map's tier is gated by its observational mortality evidence, and a biomarker cannot lift it (same rule applied to vitamin-d). Note resistance exercise was non-significant here (see resistance-training).

  • 2026-07-04 — Ingested Mandsager 2018 (cohort, n=122,007): all-cause mortality inversely proportional to CRF, HR 5.04 low-vs-elite, no upper limit; benefit held in ≥70 and hypertensive patients. Grounds why an aerobic base matters. Status → emerging (observational; Zone-2-specific RCT evidence still an open gap).

  • 2026-07-04 — Ingested Storoschuk 2025 (narrative review): Zone-2 is not the optimal intensity for mitochondrial/fat-oxidation capacity; higher intensities are more time-efficient, especially at low volume. pushes-back on the "optimal route" framing (efficiency, not mortality) — Status stays emerging, now carrying the intensity trade-off explicitly.

  • 2026-07-06 — Ingested Chu 2026 (meta-analysis, 16 RCTs, n=1,434, healthy elderly 60–80): HIIT raised VO2max more than moderate-intensity continuous training (head-to-head +1.17 mL·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹), in less time. RCT-corroborates the Storoschuk intensity argument in-population; pushes-back on route- optimality but on a surrogate (VO2max), so Status stays emerging — Zone-2 held for knee-safety/ adherence, not intensity-optimality.

  • 2026-07-12 — Ingested Jiao 2026 (Frontiers in Public Health, network meta-analysis, 18 RCTs, n=1,316, older adults) — appraised unreliable; adds a caution, not a claim; Status unchanged. Exercise beat non-exercise controls on cognitive scales at low-to-moderate GRADE certainty (MMSE SMD 0.55, LOW; MoCA 0.93 with a CI crossing zero, LOW; WAIS 1.26, MODERATE but n=211; WCST 2 studies, LOW) — exercise-improves-cognition-low-certainty-modality-agnostic. The ranking it exists to provide is noise, and the paper says so in its own Results ("no statistically significant differences in efficacy among the various intervention types… the SUCRA-based ranking should be interpreted with extreme caution") before ranking them in the abstract anyway — and the modality that "wins" MoCA (94.7) and WAIS (98.6) is stretching, the near-sham control (exercise-type-cognition-ranking-not-supported). No cognitive claim is added to this map. Aerobic training's cognitive value in the vault continues to rest where it already did — on the hard-endpoint dementia signal in daily-steps-broad-healthspan-outcomes (HR 0.62), not on a cognitive scale in an 18-trial network with I² up to 97%.

  • 2026-07-15 — Ingested Shan 2026 (The Lancet Healthy Longevity, systematic review of 44 studies + meta-analysis of 7 cross-sectional studies, n=145,465; NUS/NMRC-funded, one author with longevity-clinic conflict disclosed): physical activity → lower GrimAge (−0.09 SD) and Horvath (−0.03 SD) epigenetic age acceleration per 1 SD higher MET-min/week; Hannum and PhenoAge null; no RCTs eligible, no GRADE. extends the cellular-aging surrogate row onto a second biomarker family (physical-activity-lowers-epigenetic-age-cross-sectional); Status unchanged (emerging) — a cross-sectional surrogate with reverse causation unexcluded cannot lift a map (same rule as vitamin-d). Capped suggestive.

  • 2026-07-15 — Ingested Garcia 2023 (British Journal of Sports Medicine, dose-response meta-analysis of 94 prospective cohorts, >30M adults, 811,616 all-cause deaths; MRC/Wellcome-funded, no conflicts): leisure-time aerobic activity → mortality on a steeply non-linear curve — 8.75 mMET-h/wk (~150 min/wk MVPA) → all-cause RR 0.69 (0.65–0.73), CVD 0.71, cancer 0.85; most benefit banked 0–8.75, ~75 min/wk already ~23%, plateau beyond 17.5 mMET-h/wk (aerobic-volume-mortality-dose-response-nonlinear). Fills the map's standing minimum-effective-dose gap — with an aerobic-volume number. extends; Status unchanged (emerging) — a meta-analysis of cohorts is still cohort tier, exposure is self-reported, and it measures volume, not a Zone-2 protocol. Reliability mixed → capped suggestive.

  • 2026-07-15 — Ingested Wang 2025 (Scientific Reports, prospective cohort, n=109,407 Chinese adults, 4,571 deaths, OA, no conflicts): self-reported weekly minutes → all-cause mortality on an L-shaped curve with the minimum protective dose ~75 min/week (75–360 min HR 0.89; >720 min 0.77; 0–75 not protective), plateau ~2,400 min/wk (moderate-activity-75min-week-mortality-minimum). Independent, cross-population corroboration of Garcia's ~75 min/wk floor — different country, different unit, same threshold. confirms; Status unchanged (emerging) (observational, self-report, 78.9% male). Reliability mixed → suggestive.

  • 2026-07-18 — Ingested Gao 2026 (BMC Sports Science, Medicine & Rehabilitation, PRISMA-2020 three-level meta-analysis, 14 RCTs, n=534, non-clinical populations, CR2 small-sample correction, no competing interests, OA) — ⚑ hypoxic training flagged as a CANDIDATE intervention and deliberately NOT coined as a map; Status unchanged. Hypoxic HIIT improved cardiorespiratory fitness (g=0.369, 0.186–0.552) and relative oxygen uptake (g=0.553, 0.308–0.797) — but effects were larger against non-exercise controls than against exercise controls, and that subgroup is the modality's whole question: a trial against sitting measures HIIT, only the normoxic-exercise contrast measures altitude (hypoxic-hiit-benefit-shrinks-against-exercise-control). The fifth bundled-arm attribution failure the vault has caught (creatine+beta-alanine, protein+probiotic, exercise+CBT, unstable-surface RT, hypoxia+HIIT) — though to Gao's credit the authors built and reported the subgroup that undercuts their own headline. Everything non-fitness was null: body composition + cardiometabolic g=−0.018 (−0.133 to 0.097) — tight enough to be genuinely informative — and hematological g=0.122 (−0.292 to 0.535), which is the pointed one, because more red cells is the mechanism altitude training is sold on (hypoxic-hiit-null-on-body-composition-cardiometabolic). ⚑ No map coined (§6.5): no independent hypoxic literature in the vault, the isolating contrast is where the effect shrinks, the proposed mechanism is null, and altitude tents/chambers fail GOAL.md on burden before reaching benefit — the fifth candidate refusal in nine days after beta-alanine, probiotics, collagen-muscle and collagen-bone. Consistent with, and adding nothing to, hiit-beats-mict-vo2max-elderly. Appraised mixed (conservative three-level statistics, conflict-free, self-critical — against 14 small trials, non-standardised hypoxic dose, low-to-moderate certainty, young-adult population, all surrogates); capped suggestive. open-question; Status unchanged (emerging). OA PDF archived.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚑ THE ENVIRONMENTAL-ADD-ON PATTERN — three maps, three underdeliveries, one of them subtractive (new 2026-07-18). The vault now holds three separate literatures in which an environmental stressor is bolted onto exercise, and none of them earns its keep: (1) Heat — sauna-heat: passive heating null on FMD/PWV/glucose/lipids, only a modest systolic-BP drop (passive-heat-null-on-most-cardiometabolic-markers). (2) Cold — cold-water-immersion: post-workout cold actively blunts the resistance-training adaptation it is layered on (cwi-post-workout-blunts-hypertrophy, safety_flags: [interaction]). (3) Altitude — this run: hypoxic HIIT null on body composition, cardiometabolic and hematological outcomes, with its fitness benefit shrinking against normoxic-exercise controls (hypoxic-hiit-benefit-shrinks-against-exercise-control). The exercise is the intervention; the environment is decoration — and in one of the three cases the decoration takes something away. This is a framing note the vault should carry into future sweeps, not a graded finding: it is an observation about three literatures, not a claim from any one of them. It points where the whole month has pointed — the free, boring lever is the one with the evidence.
  • ⚠ Aerobic training does not cancel sitting (new 2026-07-10). An umbrella review of 30 systematic reviews (n=2,778,595) finds sedentary time predicts all-cause mortality after adjusting for physical activity (sedentary-time-independent-of-exercise-mortality-risk). A structured Zone-2 session does not buy back the seated hours around it — sedentary-behavior is a separate exposure, counted separately at regimen time. No safety edge; a non-redundancy finding.
  • Ergogenic overlap with ashwagandha — noted, not adopted (new 2026-07-10). A conflict-free 13-RCT meta-analysis finds ashwagandha's most consistent effect is on aerobic endurance (g = 0.54, ashwagandha-aerobic-endurance-performance) — nominally this map's axis. But the population is young athletes, the endpoint is a performance surrogate, and the pooled prediction interval crosses zero (ashwagandha-performance-effect-does-not-generalize). Recorded as a redundancy/coherence edge, not a co-recommendation: the aerobic base is a behaviour, not a supplement.
  • Convergent "less is enough" dose with daily-steps (new 2026-07-15). Garcia's aerobic-volume curve (minimum ~75 min/wk, value zone to ~150 min/wk, plateau ~17.5 mMET-h/wk) and daily-steps' step curve (~7,000 not 10,000) are two instruments measuring overlapping activity — they corroborate the same front-loaded shape, not two independent wins. Count the aerobic base once at regimen time; don't double-bank steps and Zone-2 minutes as separate mortality levers.
  • Complementary to creatine-monohydrate and protein-target — aerobic axis vs strength axis; different levers, no conflict.
  • Pairs with resistance-training — the strength axis, now with its own mortality data. Combining aerobic + resistance training gave the lowest mortality in a 147,374-adult cohort (HR ~0.55, aerobic-plus-resistance-lowest-mortality). Zone-2 and resistance-training are two parallel foundation levers, not an either/or — run both.
  • ⚠ AMPK interaction with metformin (added 2026-07-07). Metformin activates AMPK, which can blunt cardiorespiratory-fitness adaptations to aerobic training — directly relevant to a Zone-2 base. Metformin is prescription/clinician-only; flagged in both directions, magnitude a logged open gap.
  • Beats the supplements on the supplements' own biomarker (added 2026-07-09). On cellular-aging surrogates, the vault now holds a clean row: omega-3 null on telomere length in VITAL (omega3-no-telomere-effect); vitamin-d positive but post-hoc, in a trial null on its hard endpoints (vitamin-d-preserves-telomere-length); and aerobic exercise positive in a randomized, independently-funded meta-analysis (aerobic-exercise-raises-telomerase-activity). A comparison worth carrying at regimen time — and still not a reason to upgrade anything, because all three are biomarkers.
  • Run both axes (reinforced 2026-07-09). The same meta-analysis found resistance exercise non-significant on telomerase (3 trials — absence of evidence, not evidence of absence), while resistance-training holds the vault's direct mortality cohort evidence and Zone-2 holds only a fitness→mortality association. The two levers are strong in different places, which is a reason to run both, as aerobic-plus-resistance-lowest-mortality already concludes (HR ~0.55 combined).
  • Global-ripple: no interaction or contraindication found with the remaining maps. The Zone-2-specific / higher-tier mortality evidence to move this off emerging is still an open gap; the intensity comparison is now RCT-pooled (Chu 2026) but surrogate-only; and the telomere/telomerase evidence is likewise surrogate, in mostly clinical populations.
  • ⚠ HIIT places among the top sleep modalities against ACTIVE controls (new 2026-07-19). Liang 2026 (Sleep Medicine Reviews, 200 RCTs, n=23,523) found qigong, walking and high-intensity interval training produced the largest subjective-sleep benefits versus active comparators (walking-among-top-sleep-modalities-against-active-controls) — the second time HIIT has topped a ranking in this vault, after Chu 2026 on VO₂max (hiit-beats-mict-vo2max-elderly). Recorded, not banked, and for three reasons: the ranking is qualitative with no readable effect sizes, the paper's overall certainty is VERY LOW at n=23,523 (exercise-sleep-certainty-very-low-at-200-trials), and the benefit concentrates in poor sleepers (exercise-sleep-benefit-concentrated-in-poor-sleepers) — which David is not. No sleep claim enters this map, and aerobic activity is still counted once, on CRF/mortality. The vault's five-case SUCRA discipline applies to a ranking it would like as much as to one it would not.
