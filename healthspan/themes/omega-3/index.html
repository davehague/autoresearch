Omega-3 (fish oil) — supplements

Status: emerging (2026-07-06) — three honestly-bounded threads: a single post-hoc RCT shows omega-3 slowing epigenetic-aging clocks (surrogate, small effect); a high-tier meta-analysis of RCTs returns a null on global cognition; and a new meta-analysis of 42 RCTs shows a cardiovascular hard-outcome benefit — but only in high-risk / established-CVD populations, and paired with an atrial-fibrillation harm. A lever opened low on the ladder, with the healthy-adult primary-prevention case still unestablished.

What we believe now

Two threads, both honestly bounded. (1) Biological-aging surrogate: omega-3 (1 g/day) may modestly slow DNA-methylation aging clocks — the only vault evidence is a post hoc analysis of the DO-HEALTH RCT (n=777 with methylation data), where omega-3 alone slowed three next-gen clocks (PhenoAge, GrimAge2, DunedinPACE) by ~2.9–3.8 months over 3 years, additive with vitamin D + exercise on PhenoAge. A small effect on a surrogate endpoint from a single trial's post hoc analysis. (2) Cognition — now a high-tier null: a 2025 meta-analysis of 11 placebo-controlled RCTs in cognitively-unimpaired older adults found no effect on global cognition (SMD −0.02, 95% CI −0.07 to 0.04). So on the functional cognitive outcome, pooled RCT evidence says no benefit. (3) Cardiovascular hard outcomes — benefit, but population-restricted and with a harm: a 2025 meta-analysis of 42 RCTs (176,253 participants) found omega-3 significantly reduced CV mortality, CVD, CHD, MI, fatal MI, and revascularization — in people with established CVD or high CV risk (secondary prevention) — while significantly increasing atrial fibrillation and GI adverse events. A subgroup signal favored EPA monotherapy over EPA+DHA.

The three threads bound each other: a surrogate biomarker moved, a functional cognitive endpoint did not, and a cardiovascular benefit appears only where baseline risk is high — the large primary-prevention trials (VITAL/ASCEND) were null, and the pooled benefit is paired with a real AF/GI harm. So this does not establish a healthspan outcome for a healthy, low-risk adult, it pushes back on "fish oil for the brain," and it reframes the cardiovascular case as secondary-prevention, not everyone-should-supplement — the same baseline-dependence pattern as vitamin-d.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-04 — Ingested Bischoff-Ferrari 2025 / DO-HEALTH (RCT post hoc, n=777): omega-3 1 g/day slowed PhenoAge, GrimAge2, DunedinPACE (0.16–0.32 SD ≈ 2.9–3.8 months); additive with vitamin D + exercise on PhenoAge. Opens the map at emerging (surrogate endpoints, post hoc, small effect).
  • 2026-07-05 — Ingested Mulargia 2025 (meta-analysis, 11 placebo-controlled RCTs pooled of 19 reviewed): no effect on global cognition in cognitively-unimpaired elders, SMD −0.02 (95% CI −0.07 to 0.04). High-tier null pushes back on the cognition claim; partly closes the "omega-3 hard-outcome" open gap on the cognition axis. Status stays emerging (surrogate-aging thread unchanged).
  • 2026-07-06 — Ingested Mattumpuram 2025 (meta-analysis, 42 RCTs, n=176,253, established-CVD/high-risk): significant reductions in CV mortality, CVD, CHD, MI, fatal MI, revascularization; significant increase in atrial fibrillation + GI events; EPA monotherapy > EPA+DHA. Adds a third thread (cardiovascular hard outcomes) — closes the CVD side of the hard-outcome gap for secondary prevention, leaves the healthy-primary-prevention case open. Status stays emerging (benefit is population-restricted and paired with a harm).
  • 2026-07-08 — Ingested Zhu 2025 / VITAL telomere sub-study (RCT, n=1,031, 4 y): marine omega-3 1 g/day had no significant effect on leukocyte telomere length (while vitamin D3 in the same trial did). Adds one more flat surrogate to the aging-biomarker thread — omega-3's surrogate signals are inconsistent (epigenetic clock nudged post-hoc, telomere null, cognition null). Status stays emerging.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Surrogate signals track with vitamin-d, but diverge. On the PhenoAge clock in DO-HEALTH the two were additive; on telomere length in the VITAL sub-study (Zhu 2025) they diverged — vitamin D3 preserved telomeres, omega-3 was null (omega3-no-telomere-effect vs vitamin-d-preserves-telomere-length). Both are surrogate-endpoint signals co-existing with null/contested hard outcomes; a nuance, not a rehabilitation of the "supplement everyone" case (see vitamin-d).
  • Cognition-lever landscape (not an interaction). The new high-tier cognition null (omega3-no-global-cognition-benefit) sits alongside creatine's suggestive cognition thread (creatine-monohydrate) and magnesium L-threonate's single-RCT cognition signal (magnesium-l-threonate): same outcome, three different compounds, three different verdicts. A framing note for regimen-time honesty, not a safety edge.
  • Global-ripple: no safety interaction with other vault interventions found.
  • ⚠ Safety — atrial-fibrillation / GI harm (now graded). Mattumpuram 2025 makes the omega-3 harm signal a graded concept: omega3-atrial-fibrillation-gi-harm carries safety_flags: [high-dose-risk] for the significant increase in incident AF and GI adverse events. This gates naive high-dose recommendation at regimen time without changing the benefit score (shared/safety.md).
  • ⚠ Clinician note — still-OPEN GAP (bleeding interaction, not yet a graded concept). Distinct from the AF harm above: higher-dose omega-3 can potentiate anticoagulant/antiplatelet therapy (bleeding) — well-established pharmacology, but this run still found no vault-quality source to cite. Logged as an open gap (flag it, don't fabricate a graded interaction concept) → resolve by ingesting a proper omega-3 drug-interaction / bleeding-risk review.
  • CVD hard-outcome gap — closed for secondary prevention, open for primary. Mattumpuram 2025 supplies the high-tier cardiovascular evidence the gap wanted — but only in established-CVD / high-risk populations. The healthy-adult primary-prevention question (David's situation) remains unestablished (VITAL/ASCEND null), so the gap narrows to primary prevention rather than closing.
  • Context on the telomere null (added 2026-07-09). Omega-3 was flat on telomere length in VITAL (omega3-no-telomere-effect). A 16-RCT meta-analysis now shows aerobic exercise does move that class of marker — telomerase activity SMD 0.35, telomere length SMD 0.59 (aerobic-exercise-raises-telomerase-activity, on zone-2-cardio) — randomized and independently funded. On the cellular-aging surrogates omega-3 is marketed against, the free intervention outperforms the capsule. All of these are biomarkers, so this changes no Status; it is a framing note for regimen time, where omega-3's real case remains the epigenetic-clock post-hoc signal and secondary- prevention CVD benefit, not telomeres.
