Vitamin D — supplements

Status: contested (2026-07-04) — downgraded. Two high-tier RCT nulls (VITAL) push back on years of observational "vitamin D prevents everything" enthusiasm. This is the moves-both-ways case in the seed set: strong RCTs nullifying a weaker observational signal drag the map down, and we state the tension rather than bury it under a confident summary.

What we believe now

For generally replete adults, routine vitamin D supplementation (2000 IU/day) does not prevent cancer, cardiovascular disease, or fractures — the large VITAL RCT found flat nulls on all of these, including no benefit in lower-baseline-25(OH)D subgroups for fractures.

The tension we keep visible: the observational literature associates low vitamin D with worse outcomes, but the RCTs testing supplementation in non-deficient people came back null. The unresolved, legitimate question is treatment of genuine deficiency — VITAL did not select for deficient participants, so it does not speak to correcting a true deficiency. That is where the remaining case, if any, lives; the "supplement everyone" case is what got downgraded.

A 2026 re-analysis sharpens (does not resolve) that question. A target-trial emulation (Wang 2026, UK Biobank) shows the VITAL/D-Health nulls are exactly what you'd expect in replete populations, while emulated trials restricted to vitamin-D-insufficient/deficient people suggest the potential for a major all-cause-mortality reduction. This is observational emulation, not a new RCT — so it gives the deficiency-correction case a concrete estimate without lifting vitamin D off contested, and without rehabilitating routine supplementation for replete adults.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-04 — Ingested Manson 2019 / VITAL (RCT, n=25,871): vitamin D3 2000 IU/day → no reduction in cancer (HR 0.96) or major CVD (HR 0.97). High-tier null.

  • 2026-07-04 — Ingested LeBoff 2022 / VITAL fractures (RCT): no reduction in total/nonvertebral/hip fractures (HR ~0.98–1.01), no effect modification by baseline 25(OH)D. Both nulls move Status → contested (down from observational enthusiasm).

  • 2026-07-05 — Ingested Wang 2026 (target-trial emulation, UK Biobank): population-weighted emulation reproduces the RCT nulls; deficiency-restricted emulation predicts a major all-cause-mortality reduction. Observational tier → sharpens the deficiency open gap; Status stays contested.

  • 2026-07-08 — Ingested Zhu 2025 / VITAL telomere sub-study (RCT, n=1,031, 4 y): vitamin D3 2,000 IU/day slowed leukocyte telomere attrition (~0.14 kb / ≈140 bp less than placebo, ≈3–4 y slower cellular aging); omega-3 null. A surrogate/biomarker benefit from the same trial whose hard endpoints were null — open-question, joins the DO-HEALTH clock signal as surrogate-moves-while-outcomes-don't. Status stays contested (a surrogate does not overturn VITAL's high-tier hard-outcome nulls).

  • 2026-07-18 — Ingested Paez-Allendes 2026 (Nutrients, PROSPERO CRD420261365005, systematic review + meta-analysis of RCTs in adult men, 27 studies → 21 comparison-level records, 11 comparisons in the primary model; REML + Hartung-Knapp; no conflicts; OA) as the map's fourth outcome family, and the fourth randomised null. Vitamin D supplementation did not raise total testosterone — MD 0.47 nmol/L (−0.50 to 1.44), I²=24.1% — nor SHBG (0.27, −2.14 to 2.68), free androgen index (−0.37, −4.28 to 3.55), calculated free testosterone (−0.0096, −0.0525 to 0.0332) or bioactive testosterone (−0.47, −1.77 to 0.83) (vitamin-d-no-testosterone-androgen-effect). Unusually informative for a null in this vault: low heterogeneity (the trials agree), Hartung-Knapp widening applied, and an upper bound of +1.44 nmol/L against a normal range of roughly 10–35 — a ceiling too small to matter clinically even if real. What holds it short of "excluded" is a disclosed retrieval hole: Embase was not searched (institutional access unavailable, amendment made before screening) and 27 of 162 assessable reports could not be obtained; GRADE low (TT/SHBG/FAI) to very low (FT/BAT). It does NOT touch the map's live question — no baseline-vitamin-D-status stratification is readable, so it says nothing about deficiency correction (vitamin-d-deficiency-mortality-signal); recorded so a future run does not close that gap on this citation. Appraised mixed; capped suggestive. confirms; Status unchanged (contested — a fourth null on an already-downgraded map confirms rather than moves it, and fading would require the deficiency thread to fail too). OA PDF archived via the browser rung.

  • 2026-07-29 — The map's live thread finally gets a randomised test in the right population — and the pre-specified endpoint is null. Status held contested (Lee 2026, European Journal of Nutrition 65(5):221, partially randomised patient preference trial, 108 outpatients aged 10–24 with major depression AND vitamin D insufficiency/deficiency, MacKay Memorial Hospital, Taipei; 4,800 IU vitamin D3 daily for 8 weeks; randomised vitamin D n = 41, preference vitamin D n = 28, placebo n = 39; computer-generated blocks of four, allocation concealed from researchers, participants and treating physicians; funded by MacKay Memorial Hospital and Taiwan's Ministry of Science and Technology, no supplement manufacturer, no declared conflicts; IRB 19MMHIS391e; Springer paywalled, pdf_unavailable logged after the full ladder — Unpaywall confirms closed). Primary outcome, the assessor-administered 17-item Hamilton Depression Rating Scale: B = −0.30 (95% CI −1.81 to 1.21), p = 0.698 — null (vitamin-d-null-on-clinician-rated-depression-in-deficient-young-people). Self-reported anxiety (GAD-7) B = −1.78 (−3.35 to −0.32), p = 0.026. No excess hypercalcaemia or hyperphosphataemia. ⚑ Why this matters more than its size suggests: every prior vitamin D source in this vault tested people who were already REPLETE. The map has sat at contested rather than fading on exactly one surviving thread — deficiency correction (vitamin-d-deficiency-mortality-signal) — and recorded explicitly on 2026-07-18 that Paez-Allendes "does NOT touch the map's live question." This trial selected for deficiency, and its pre-specified primary is flat. That makes five randomised outcome families, five null primaries: cancer, cardiovascular disease, fractures, androgens, and now mood. ⚠ And here is exactly how far it goes, because the gap must not be closed on the wrong citation twice. The vault's deficiency thread is about all-cause mortality in adults, from an observational target-trial emulation. This is depressive symptoms in 10-to-24-year-olds over eight weeks. The exposure matches; the outcome family, the age band and the horizon do not. A null on adolescent mood is not a null on adult mortality. ⚠ No baseline or achieved 25-hydroxyvitamin D concentrations are recoverable, so the trial cannot even confirm the deficiency was corrected. The gap narrows by a fraction and stays open. ⚠ The paper's conclusion is refused. It is written from an uncorrected severity subgroup of roughly 18 versus 16 people — which does not survive its own subdivision, showing nothing when restricted to adolescents aged 10–19 — while the properly randomised, clinician-rated primary is the null one and the significant result is on self-report (vitamin-d-depression-conclusion-rests-on-an-uncorrected-subgroup-of-a-null-trial). This is the COSMOS shape — null pre-specified primary, a secondary that wins, the winner travelling (cocoa-flavanol-cvd-benefit-is-estimator-dependent) — at roughly two hundredth of the scale. ✅ It differs from COSMOS in the way that decides the appraisal: the authors say so themselves, calling the reading "exploratory" and naming the sample-size constraint, and reporting the null primary prominently. Fatal flag #2 targets concealment and there is none, so mixed, not unreliable; capped suggestive. ⚑ The honest counterweight, recorded because it is not a silly place to look. A benefit confined to the more severely affected is the benefit-on-demand shape the vault has now seen in four literatures — exercise and sleep in poor sleepers, multidomain programmes in people already declining, and twice tonight in exercise-and-cognition (exercise-improves-cognition-in-mci-against-wait-list-controls versus walking-training-null-on-cognition-in-older-adults). That is an argument for a properly powered, severity-stratified deficiency trial. It is not an argument for banking this subgroup. confirms; Status contested HELD — a fifth null confirms an already-downgraded map rather than moving it, and fading still requires the mortality deficiency thread to fail, which this does not test.

  • 2026-07-30 — The map's FIRST positive pooled randomised result — and it is entirely on surrogates, at magnitudes a laboratory would call noise. Status held contested (Abumweis 2026, Asia Pacific Journal of Clinical Nutrition 35(4):631-643, systematic review + meta-analysis of RCTs, 45 trials screened from 14,051 abstracts; PubMed, Cochrane Library and ClinicalTrials.gov; Cochrane risk-of-bias tool; random-effects; pre-planned exploration of age and baseline-status modification; publicly funded by Hashemite University, Jordan, no industry money; ⚠ ABSTRACT-ONLY — the August 2026 issue is unposted and the DOI is UNREGISTERED, returning 404 at doi.org, Crossref and Unpaywall, so risk-of-bias results, heterogeneity, per-outcome trial counts, GRADE and the full list of non-significant outcomes are UNVERIFIED; pdf_unavailable logged with the full ladder and a retry note). LDL cholesterol −0.136 mmol/L (95% CI −0.215 to −0.056) — about 5 mg/dL; systolic blood pressure −2.79 mmHg (−4.65 to −0.94); fasting glucose −0.11 mmol/L (−0.19 to −0.04) — about 2 mg/dL; HbA₁c −0.164% (−0.32 to −0.01), an interval that only just clears zero (vitamin-d-small-improvements-in-cardiometabolic-risk-markers). This is the sixth randomised outcome family on this map and the first that is not null. ⚑ And the arithmetic is the finding, not the p-values. Effects of that size, sustained, are the kind that register in event counts — and VITAL randomised 25,871 people and found major cardiovascular events FLAT (HR 0.97) (vitamin-d-no-cancer-cvd-benefit). Both results cannot describe the same biology over the same horizon. Three explanations survive and the vault records all three rather than choosing: the marker changes do not persist beyond short trials; they do not translate; or they are artefacts of a heterogeneous literature whose multiplicity handling cannot be inspected (vitamin-d-risk-factor-gains-should-have-shown-up-in-vital-and-did-not). The hierarchy decides it: both sides are randomised, so the tiebreaker is the ENDPOINT, and a hard outcome outranks a surrogate (shared/evidence-model.md). ⚠ The subgroup pattern argues against a coherent effect rather than for one. Systolic blood pressure and LDL improved in participants aged ≥55 while fasting glucose improved in those aged <55 — opposite age-dependence for outcomes on a shared pathway. That is what a scatter of chance wins across a heterogeneous literature looks like, and it is the same tell this vault used to decline five network-analysis leaderboards (supplement-outcome-specific-ranking-too-tidy-for-a-sparse-network). Four outcomes are reported significant out of a minimum of eight to ten tested, with no recoverable correction. ⚑ The map's live thread is touched for the SECOND time in two days, and refused for the THIRD time overall. Favourable glycaemic effects concentrated where baseline vitamin D was below 50 nmol/L — insufficiency, the right population — making this the first pooled randomised evidence to bear on the deficiency-correction thread at all (vitamin-d-glycaemic-signal-concentrates-in-low-baseline-status). ⚠ And it is declined on exactly the grounds the map used for Paez-Allendes (2026-07-18) and Lee (2026-07-29): the thread is about ALL-CAUSE MORTALITY IN ADULTS, and this is fasting glucose and HbA₁c in an uncorrected subgroup of a surrogate analysis whose interaction test cannot be inspected. Same exposure, different outcome family. The gap narrows a second time by a fraction and stays open — and this is now the third distinct citation the vault has refused to close it on. ⚑ The consistency test this vault had to pass. vitamin-d-preserves-telomere-length is a flattering surrogate from VITAL itself, and the map declined to let it rehabilitate anything. Declining a flattering surrogate from an unrelated meta-analysis is that same decision applied twice — a ledger that discounted the telomere result and banked this one would be picking, not reasoning. ⚠ Appraised mixed: abstract-only retrieval bars reliable on its own, plus thin search coverage (no Embase, Scopus or Web of Science), no stated PROSPERO registration, incoherent subgroup directions, trivial magnitudes, and all-surrogate outcomes. Fatal flag #2 (p-hacking) is the live risk and does not fire, because concealment cannot be established from an abstract — the vault does not fire a fatal flag on an inference it cannot check. Capped suggestive; the two subgroup-derived concepts graded single-study. extends; Status contested HELD — a positive result on surrogates does not upgrade a map that was downgraded on hard endpoints. Six outcome families now say one thing six ways: vitamin D moves measurements and does not move events.

  • 2026-08-31 — The largest, most carefully pre-specified synthesis this map holds — 69 trials, 153,902 people — confirms the map's whole pattern on a seventh outcome family, at GRADE HIGH: vitamin D moves nothing tested at adequate scale. Status held contested. Ingested Massé 2026 (calcium-vitamin-d-combined-supplementation-fractures-falls-meta-masse-2026, BMJ, PROSPERO CRD42023483915, systematic review + meta-analysis of RCTs testing calcium alone, vitamin D alone, and combined supplementation against fracture and fall outcomes; no industry funding anywhere in the review). Vitamin D alone: a precise null at GRADE HIGH on every outcome — any fracture RR 1.00 (95% CI 0.95–1.06, 92,045 people), non-vertebral 1.01, vertebral 1.04, risk of falling 1.01, total falls 1.00; hip fracture 1.13 (1.00–1.27), trending toward harm (vitamin-d-alone-null-on-fracture-risk-at-92000-person-scale). This is nearly ten times the sample size of this map's founding VITAL fracture null and the seventh outcome family the map has now recorded this pattern for. Combined calcium + vitamin D is nominally significant on any fracture (RR 0.91, 0.84–0.99) and misses the review's own pre-specified clinical-significance bar — an absolute reduction of 1.0% (NNT≈100) against a 2% threshold set before pooling (combined-calcium-vitamin-d-fracture-benefit-misses-its-own-clinical-significance-bar) — and a leave-one-out sensitivity analysis found the combined-therapy signal is substantially carried by one 1992 trial in institutionalised elderly Frenchwomen; removing it eliminates significance (one-1992-trial-in-institutionalised-elders-drives-the-combined-supplementation-signal). Appraised reliable (PROSPERO-registered with a-priori clinical-significance thresholds, 88% of headline findings moderate-to-high GRADE, no industry funding, the authors' own unflattering sensitivity result published rather than buried); concepts not capped — strength: strong where GRADE and sample size support it. confirms; Status contested HELD — this is the seventh confirmation of an already-downgraded map's pattern, not a new signal, and does not touch the deficiency-correction thread (only ~3% of pooled trials recruited people below the 25 nmol/L deficiency threshold, so the map's one surviving open question remains untouched).
