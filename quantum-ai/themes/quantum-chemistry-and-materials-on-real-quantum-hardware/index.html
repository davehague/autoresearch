Quantum chemistry and materials on real quantum hardware

Status: contested

Sub-threads: none — opened 2026-11-04 with 4 bullets; too early to split [reviewed 2026-11-04 @ 4 bullets]

Scope

What have quantum processors actually computed for chemistry and materials, and does any of it beat or even match the best classical method? Covers hybrid quantum-classical workflows such as sample-based quantum diagonalization (SQD), error-corrected phase estimation, and chemistry-flavoured demonstrations such as the Quantum Echoes NMR experiment. The standard is a like-for-like comparison against classical heuristics, not against exact diagonalization. The classical side lives in classical-and-ai-methods-as-the-rival-to-quantum-for-chemistry; the fault-tolerant cost side in fault-tolerance-timelines-roadmaps-and-resource-estimates.

Through-line

  • 2026-11-04 — the first AI-discovered chemistry circuit on hardware is a single point inside its error bar. An LLM-evolved VQE-style ansatz for 12-qubit LiH (STO-3G basis) run on Quantinuum H2-1 deviated from the exact energy by 3.1 mHa with a 3.4 mHa uncertainty, consistent with chemical precision (1.6 mHa) only within that uncertainty and at one geometry; default-noise emulator runs gave about 6 to 12 mHa (finger-automated-near-term-quantum-algorithm-discovery-for-molecular-ground-states). A molecule this small is solved exactly by classical methods, so it joins the hardware runs above as a demonstration rather than an advantage; the status stays contested. An LLM search also cut the Toffoli-qubit cost of a Fe2S2 alias-select chemistry subroutine by 4.1x on paper (gu-discovering-state-of-the-art-quantum-circuits-with-large-language-models).
  • 2026-11-04 — opened at contested. The IBM, RIKEN and Fugaku SQD runs are real, peer-reviewed hardware computations (circuits up to 77 qubits and 10,570 gates) on N2 and iron-sulfur clusters (robledo-moreno-sqd-chemistry-beyond-exact-diagonalization-on-a-quantum-centric-supercomputer), and the full-scale follow-up used 152,064 Fugaku nodes with accuracy the authors call comparable to some all-classical approximation methods (shirakawa-closed-loop-electronic-structure-on-heron-and-fugaku-at-full-scale). The push-back is a peer-reviewed numerical study: on N2 and 2Fe-2S the same method keeps resampling configurations it already has and builds less compact expansions than classical selected-CI, so it ends up more expensive (reinholdt-critical-limitations-in-quantum-selected-configuration-interaction). The authors' claim atom carries a rebutted-by link to that study. Status is contested because credible pushback has landed on the only substantial hardware chemistry workflow; in our read, every hardware chemistry result so far is at or below what classical heuristics do.
  • 2026-11-04 — error-corrected chemistry works as a pipeline and not yet as chemistry. Quantinuum ran phase estimation for H2 on [7,1,3] color-code logical qubits with real-time error correction, but the energy came out at 0.001(13) hartree, an uncertainty about eight times the 0.0016 hartree chemical-accuracy target, on a molecule classical computers solve trivially (yamamoto-quantum-error-corrected-computation-of-molecular-energies). The scout's gloss that the uncertainty was 'about as large as' the target was corrected here. Evidence that logical qubits help a real workload, but a tiny one.
  • 2026-11-04 — Google's chemistry application is a proposal. The Quantum Echoes blog says the NMR molecular-geometry demonstration is 'not yet beyond classical'; the beyond-classical result is on an abstract observable (google-s-blog-says-its-nmr-molecular-geometry-demonstration-built-on-otocs-is-not-yet-beyond-classical-so-the-chemistry-application-of-quantum-echoes-is-a-proposed-route-through-hamiltonian-learning; see quantum-advantage-vs-classical-simulation).

Open questions

  • A like-for-like comparison of SQD against classical selected-CI or DMRG on the same hardware-run systems; the SQD papers compare mainly against exact diagonalization.
  • Whether hardware noise changes the Reinholdt resampling result; the critique is numerical and does not use a quantum device.
  • Further error-corrected chemistry runs: Kosugi et al. (arXiv 2605.29674, QPE with Steane-code logical qubits for CO on Fe5C2) was seen by the scout but has no quantitative results in its abstract and is not ingested.
  • Neural-network wavefunctions and neural quantum states as the competing classical approach to SQD and QPE targets; see Open gaps in the index.
