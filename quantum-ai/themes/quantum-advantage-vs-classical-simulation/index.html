Quantum advantage claims versus classical simulation

Status: contested

Sub-threads: none — 3 bullets; too early to split [reviewed 2026-11-04 @ 3 bullets]

Scope

Which "beyond classical" experimental claims survive attempts to simulate them classically, and which have been beaten by clever classical algorithms such as tensor networks with belief propagation? Dequantization and classical-simulation results that undercut an advantage claim belong here as push-back. Dequantization of quantum machine learning has its own theme, quantum-machine-learning-advantage-versus-dequantization.

Through-line

  • 2026-11-04 — status stays contested; the IBM rebuttal is now two-fold and the Google claim carries a data-hygiene caveat. The 2023 IBM 127-qubit claim (kim-ibm-evidence-for-the-utility-of-quantum-computing-before-fault-tolerance) was rebutted by a second, independent classical group in Science Advances: sparse Pauli dynamics and tensor networks converge the observables to better than 0.01 absolute accuracy without extrapolation and flag inaccuracies in the experimental extrapolations (begusic-fast-and-converged-classical-simulations-of-ibm-utility-experiment), alongside the earlier Flatiron result. With two groups and partly different methods, the earlier rebuttal atom is raised from suggestive to strong. On the Google side, re-reading the blog shows the abstract's 103 qubits and the figure's 65 of 105 qubits do not obviously match (google-s-quantum-echoes-materials-give-inconsistent-qubit-counts-the-abstract-and-blog-text-cite-103-qubits-while-the-blog-s-figure-caption-uses-65-of-105-available-qubits-and-its-exact-simulation), and the NMR chemistry application is 'not yet beyond classical' (google-s-blog-says-its-nmr-molecular-geometry-demonstration-built-on-otocs-is-not-yet-beyond-classical-so-the-chemistry-application-of-quantum-echoes-is-a-proposed-route-through-hamiltonian-learning). Separately, a peer-reviewed end-to-end timing analysis shows a restricted Simon's problem experiment running about two orders of magnitude slower than a tuned classical baseline (tuziemski-recent-quantum-runtime-disadvantages-limits-of-quantum-run-time-advantage).
  • 2026-11-04 — opened at contested. Credible pushback has already landed on two claims: a belief-propagation tensor-network simulation was more accurate than IBM's error-mitigated 127-qubit result (tindall-tensor-network-simulation-ibm-eagle-kicked-ising), and the same family of methods reaches state-of-the-art accuracy on D-Wave's spin-glass dynamics in a Science paper, with D-Wave reportedly disputing the scope (tindall-tensor-networks-dwave-spin-glass-dynamics). Disputed claim atoms carry rebutted-by links. The recurring pattern, in our read: a restricted-regime advantage claim meets a lattice-matched classical method; treat "beyond classical" as provisional until the classical side has had a year or so to respond.
  • 2026-11-04 — the counter-case: Google's Quantum Echoes OTOC(2) result (google-quantum-echoes-otoc-constructive-interference) is claimed to exceed known classical algorithms by about 13,000x on Google's own estimate, and a 2026 preprint says the tensor-network belief-propagation method fails on it (bermejo-tnbp-cannot-simulate-quantum-echoes). That preprint has Google-affiliated authors, so the claim has survived the obvious attack only in the sense that no one outside Google has been recorded breaking it.

Open questions

  • An independent (non-Google) attempt to simulate or reproduce the OTOC(2) data.
  • D-Wave's own response preprint ("Evaluating Classical Simulations with a Quantum Processor") and the remaining hard regimes; not retrieved.
  • The original claim papers (King et al. 2025; IBM Nature 2023) are not ingested.
  • Whether any advantage claim is on a task with a practical use; the Quantum Echoes NMR application matches rather than beats classical analysis.
  • Which qubit count (103 or 65) applies to the OTOC(2) data behind the 13,000x claim; read the Nature methods.
  • Neural quantum states were reportedly flagged by a Nature reviewer as an untested classical threat to Quantum Echoes; this is aggregator commentary only and unverified.
