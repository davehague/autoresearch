Topological qubits: the disputed route

Status: contested

Sub-threads: none — opened 2026-11-04 with 1 bullets; too early to split [reviewed 2026-11-04 @ 1 bullets]

Scope

Microsoft's hardware path via Majorana-based topological qubits, and the challenge to the physics underneath it. Separate from other error-correction routes because its core evidence is under direct dispute.

Through-line

  • 2026-11-04 — opened at contested. Henry Legg's re-analysis of Microsoft's public data says the topological gap protocol is unreliable and the parity-readout regions are gapless (legg-comment-on-microsoft-interferometric-parity-readout); the dispute reached a formal Nature exchange on 2026-06-24 with a Microsoft reply, and the claim atom carries rebutted-by to the critique. Microsoft's June 2026 Majorana 2 blog reports 20-second parity lifetimes and a 2029 target (microsoft-majorana-2-announcement-blog), but the technical paper is not peer reviewed and critics say it does not show a qubit. We have read neither side of the Nature exchange in full.
