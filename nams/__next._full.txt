Two of three cross-model checkers also produced nothing, because they *"assigned passing judgments too liberally."* Only the checker willing to fail things recovered anything — parameter accuracy ~0.4 → 0.7–0.8 for mid-sized models, and **0.069 → ~0.13** for the smallest, a large relative gain and a useless absolute one. **This is `correlated_blind_spot` with n reduced to 1**, and the authors name the mechanism in the rail's vocabulary. → [[concepts/a-model-checking-its-own-work-is-not-a-check]] **(b) The same conclusion, from a physics simulation, by a different route.** [[sources/eulig-2026-correct-answer-wrong-mechanism]] (arXiv 2606.23175, **preprint**, ICML workshop spotlight): **7 of 28** agent episodes produced a right-looking answer defended by physics their own printed tables refute.
