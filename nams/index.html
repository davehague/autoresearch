  • 2026-07-18 — endocrine-thyroid-disruption-nams → emerging → CONSOLIDATING · validates (Deisenroth et al. 2026, *Toxicological Sciences*, DOI 10.1093/toxsci/kfaf166) · the vault's purest documented blank takes its first real hit. The human thyroid microtissue assay — 3D tissue from primary human thyroid cells, measuring hormone synthesis output rather than one fragment of the pathway — transferred reliably across three laboratories, on four blinded reference chemicals (3 true positive, 1 true negative), across three independent human donors, meeting pre-set acceptance criteria for dynamic range, precision and screening quality. `evidence_tier` `peer-reviewed-method` → `formally-validated`; `regulatory_status` `none` → `under-validation`; `loop_readiness` HELD at `automatable-open-loop`. The honest size of it: this asked "does the assay give the same answer in different hands?", not "is the answer right?" — no sensitivity, no specificity, no apical concordance, four chemicals, no decision rule, no Test Guideline. What changed is the blocker's *composition*: thyroid's was doubled (no validated method AND no rule, so no rule could attach to anything) and is now single. `funding_flag: [industry]` — Bayer Crop Science and Corteva Agrisciences, agrochemical companies whose products this assay screens, were collaborators; US EPA/NICEATM coordinated with an independent validation management team. Opens a second validation jurisdiction on the endpoint, on methods that do not overlap the eight EU-NETVAL candidates.   • 2026-07-18 — ml-model-reporting-reproducibility → emerging · 🚩 AUTO-COINED model-layer quality map (convention departure) · limits (Kappenberg et al. 2026, PMID 42059907, *Archives of Toxicology*) · the vault audits its own optimism about the model half of the loop. A systematic review of toxicology machine-learning papers 2022–2024: internal validation generally done well, but interpretation methods reported in only ~half of cases, and missing-value handling, data availability and code availability substantially underreported. The reframe: the model layer's blocker is accountability, not accuracy — nothing here says the models are wrong; it says you frequently cannot check, which is exactly what FDA's credibility assessment is built to refuse.   • 2026-07-18 — genotoxicity-buccal-micronucleus-cytome → emerging · 🚩 AUTO-COINED map (convention departure — the vault's first human-biomonitoring NAM and a new endpoint, `genotoxicity-biomonitoring`) · loop-move (Smith et al. 2026, PMID 41236179, *Mutagenesis* 41(1-2):37-45) · a fourth eye-scored map — and the first to open already carrying its escape hatch. The buccal micronucleus cytome assay scores cheek-swab cells for chromosome damage and cell death; it measures harm already done to an exposed person rather than predicting hazard for a chemical, which makes it a category apart from everything else in the vault and explicitly not a fourth vote in the genotox battery. `loop_readiness` set at `human-in-the-loop` and HELD. The weakest of the four escape hatches, for four stated reasons: the source is a review of others' models with no primary result; cited performance is 82–85% on micronucleated cells versus 95–98% on the abundant easy classes (class imbalance — weakest exactly where the signal is); a shared co-author with the vault's existing micronucleus scorer means it is not an independent second opinion; and the authors state there is no universal standard for AI-based buccal scoring. `funding_flag: none`
