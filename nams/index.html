  • 2026-07-22 — genotoxicity-tgx-ddi-transcriptomic-biomarker → consolidating (auto-coined) · the genotoxicity column's first `closed-loop-ready` map, and it qualifies the tier on arrival (Wang & Crute et al. 2025, *Toxicological Sciences* 208(2):233–243, PMID 41031489, DOI 10.1093/toxsci/kfaf138). A 64-gene expression signature in human TK6 cells, read by digital transcript counting, classifying chemicals as DNA damage-inducing or not. Four independent laboratories, 14 blinded compounds, 47 valid tests: 100% sensitivity (95% CI 82–100%), 86% specificity (95% CI 67–96%), 91% accuracy, inter-lab Kappa 0.61–1.0 → `evidence_tier: formally-validated`, a bar few sources here clear. The specificity is the contribution, not the sensitivity: it exists to fix the in-vitro micronucleus assay's 30–65% specificity — an adopted, required test that wrongly flags a third to two-thirds of harmless chemicals, each false alarm historically buying a follow-up animal study. (1) Loop: `closed-loop-ready` — machine-readable readout and a published rule (three classifiers, positive if any fires). ⚠️ But the rule is *fixed* without being *sample-independent*: two of the three legs (PCA, hierarchical clustering) are unsupervised and batch-relative, so a compound's call can depend on what else was analysed with it. Invisible in a lab running a standard reference set; not invisible in a loop that chooses its own next compounds, which changes that context on purpose, adaptively, in a direction correlated with the last result. New distinction for the axis: *fixed* ≠ *sample-independent*. Practical fix is cheap and exactly the kind of thing an autonomous system would optimise away as redundant — pin the reference set, run it every batch. (2) Its `domain_gate` is biological, not chemical — the signature is p53-enriched, so it needs functional p53 in the cells, misses aneugens by design, and false-positives on non-genotoxic p53 modulators (kinase inhibitors, protein-synthesis inhibitors). Better than an empirical domain (you can check it in advance) but it has a compound-side half and a cell-side half that live in different places. (3) ⚠️ Rail: paired with the micronucleus assay it is complementary on aneugens and correlated on metabolism — both TK6, both rat liver S9, so a pro-genotoxicant needing a route rat S9 doesn't perform reads negative in both for the same reason. Both maps flagged. Honest brakes: 14 compounds and wide CIs ("100%" is zero misses out of six); adopted nowhere (`regulatory_status: proposed`, package submitted to FDA's Biomarker Qualification Program); and the developers are on the author list and are the party seeking qualification — a strong ring trial, not an independent ECVAM/ICCVAM one. `funding_flag: [industry]`. → tgx-ddi-ring-trial-performance, tgx-ddi-decision-rule-is-fixed-but-batch-relative, tgx-ddi-p53-dependency-defines-the-domain, tgx-ddi-micronucleus-share-the-s9-metabolism-gap

  • 2026-07-22 — automation-self-driving-labs → emerging (held) · loop-move · the part of the loop this vault never measured, measured — and it is the weakest (Liu et al. 2026, "Pipette", arXiv 2606.12936, preprint, simulation only). A 12-task benchmark for general wet-lab robot manipulation — sample handling, culture-ware, device operation, precision placement. With 30 demonstrations per task, general robot-learning policies score 37–72% task success (ACT 60.3%; SmolVLA 40.4%→71.8% with augmentation; pi0 37.3%→44.1%). The map's inventory of the loop has been mind (Robin) / hands-at-scale (Tox21) / wire (LAP) / executor (ProtoPilot) / ledger (ToxMCP) — every one of them software or fixed-purpose automation, and the vault has been quietly assuming general dexterity because Tox21 proved plate handling works. Tox21 is exactly why that assumption fails: it achieves near-perfect reliability by giving up choice — fixed workflow, one plate format, purpose-built hardware, years and a national budget. The moment an AI wants a different vessel or an unplanned step it needs *general* manipulation, and general manipulation is at 44–72% in a simulator with no surface tension, no condensation and no transparent labware (which the same group's companion paper names as an open problem). And reliability compounds multiplicatively: at a generous 95% per step a 20-step protocol completes 36% of the time; at 90%, 12% — and every sophisticated part of the loop sits *downstream* of that chain, so it does not degrade gracefully, it produces confident analysis of runs that silently went wrong. Which is why the companion architecture matters more than the raw number: detecting the failure beats avoiding it — a loop that knows it dropped the tube retries; one that doesn't, analyses garbage. Same argument the vault made for ToxMCP's provenance ledger, arriving from the physical end. Status held at `emerging`; a non-peer-reviewed simulation benchmark authored by the group whose companion system it evaluates, on a commercial 3D-asset pipeline (`funding_flag: [industry]`), establishes nothing about real lab robots — it establishes that nobody has demonstrated they work well, and that `loop_readiness` has been grading the *decision* half of the loop while taking the *execution* half on trust. No toxicology anywhere in it; the relevance is by inference and the vault says so. → wet-lab-robot-manipulation-is-the-weakest-link
