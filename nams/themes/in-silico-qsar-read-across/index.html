In-silico (Q)SAR & read-across — computational NAM — method

Status: consolidating (2026-07-07) — the vault's first computational method, filling the standing In-silico (standalone) — none yet coverage blank. Sharpest split-axes case in the vault, and it lands on an endpoint we already map. evidence_tier: in-silico-mechanistic for the broad class, rising to regulatory-adopted in one narrow domain; regulatory_status: proposed/within-IATA broadly, but a genuine adopted stand-alone use (ICH M7) exists. consolidating, not consensus, because it is a stand-alone adopted method only in that one domain — broadly it is a within-IATA supporting tool.

What we believe now

(Q)SAR predicts an endpoint from molecular structure; read-across infers a data-poor chemical's toxicity from data-rich analogues (category formation). Both are purely computational and governed by the OECD (Q)SAR validation principles (defined endpoint, unambiguous algorithm, defined applicability domain, appropriate predictivity, mechanistic interpretation where possible), operationalised in the free OECD QSAR Toolbox and commercial platforms. Two standings coexist:

  • Broadly (most endpoints): accepted as supporting evidence within a weight-of-evidence / IATA (e.g. REACH Annex XI permits read-across/(Q)SAR under conditions) — not a stand-alone test replacement. Applicability-domain-bounded and endpoint-dependent: a prediction outside its training domain is not trustworthy.
  • Narrowly (ICH M7): for bacterial mutagenicity of pharmaceutical impurities, two complementary (Q)SAR methodologies (expert rule-based + statistical) + expert review are accepted as a stand-alone substitute for the Ames test — computation replacing an adopted wet assay in a bounded domain.

The honest summary: strongest where it's narrow and adopted (ICH M7), weaker but broadly useful as a within-IATA supporting NAM.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: computational-connective — Not an assay, so it is scored off the ladder. This is the model layer of a closed loop. And it is further along than anything else in the vault: under ICH M7 a (Q)SAR prediction can replace the Ames test outright — the existence proof that a computational method can be allowed to make the regulatory call unaided. Everything in AI-for-toxicology is trying to reach where this already is.

  • Is a scored component of an adopted wet-assay DA (composes-with, added 2026-07-10). skin-sensitization-potency-its-da takes an in-silico prediction — from Derek Nexus or the OECD QSAR Toolbox — as one of its three information sources, scoring it 1 (positive) / 0 (negative) into a 0–7 potency total.
