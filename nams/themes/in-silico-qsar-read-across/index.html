In-silico (Q)SAR & read-across — computational NAM — method

Status: consolidating (2026-07-07) — the vault's first computational method, filling the standing In-silico (standalone) — none yet coverage blank. Sharpest split-axes case in the vault, and it lands on an endpoint we already map. evidence_tier: in-silico-mechanistic for the broad class, rising to regulatory-adopted in one narrow domain; regulatory_status: proposed/within-IATA broadly, but a genuine adopted stand-alone use (ICH M7) exists. consolidating, not consensus, because it is a stand-alone adopted method only in that one domain — broadly it is a within-IATA supporting tool.

What we believe now

(Q)SAR predicts an endpoint from molecular structure; read-across infers a data-poor chemical's toxicity from data-rich analogues (category formation). Both are purely computational and governed by the OECD (Q)SAR validation principles (defined endpoint, unambiguous algorithm, defined applicability domain, appropriate predictivity, mechanistic interpretation where possible), operationalised in the free OECD QSAR Toolbox and commercial platforms. Two standings coexist:

  • Broadly (most endpoints): accepted as supporting evidence within a weight-of-evidence / IATA (e.g. REACH Annex XI permits read-across/(Q)SAR under conditions) — not a stand-alone test replacement. Applicability-domain-bounded and endpoint-dependent: a prediction outside its training domain is not trustworthy.
  • Narrowly (ICH M7): for bacterial mutagenicity of pharmaceutical impurities, two complementary (Q)SAR methodologies (expert rule-based + statistical) + expert review are accepted as a stand-alone substitute for the Ames test — computation replacing an adopted wet assay in a bounded domain.

The honest summary: strongest where it's narrow and adopted (ICH M7), weaker but broadly useful as a within-IATA supporting NAM.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: computational-connective — Not an assay, so it is scored off the ladder. This is the model layer of a closed loop. And it is further along than anything else in the vault: under ICH M7 a (Q)SAR prediction can replace the Ames test outright — the existence proof that a computational method can be allowed to make the regulatory call unaided. Everything in AI-for-toxicology is trying to reach where this already is.

  • 2026-07-19 — A deep-learning attempt on the vault's hardest endpoint, and it does not land. Status holds at consolidating; tier holds at computational-connective. Ahn et al. 2026 (Front. Toxicol., DOI 10.3389/ftox.2026.1751644) train graph neural networks on 23 US EPA ToxCast bioassays pre-mapped to DART mechanisms and aggregate them by majority vote to predict developmental and reproductive toxicity, validated against the ECVAM ReProTect in-vivo reference set: F1 0.68, recall 0.57, precision 0.93, specificity 0.81, accuracy 0.61 on 90 chemicals. Recall 0.57 means it misses 43% of chemicals with established reproductive toxicity — the wrong direction for a screening tier, which the vault grades on the reliability of its negative call (cf. TG 495 at 100% sensitivity, TG 492 at 95–99%, and TG 496's OI® flagged as weak at 54%). ⚠️ And the set is 75 positive / 16 negative — ~83% positive — so an always-positive baseline scores F1 ≈ 0.91, above the model's 0.68. The paper computes no baseline and its abstract calls the result "balanced predictive performance … confirming the reliability and generalizability" of the model. That arithmetic is a vault inference on the authors' own reported numbers, flagged as one. Multi-task learning — the paper's title feature — gave no meaningful gain, which the authors report honestly. evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, regulatory_status: none, strength: single-study, funding_flag: none (publicly funded, academic, no stake in the assays or any platform). Adds no standing to this map's broad class; kept as evidence about the model layer's reporting rather than its capability. → deep-learning-dart-prediction-recall-gap

  • 2026-07-27 — A named failure mode for structure-based models, and a regulatory listing, on the same night. Status and loop_readiness both HELD (consolidating, computational-connective). Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Substitutes-for the Ames test (narrow domain): via ICH M7, in-silico (Q)SAR replaces the bacterial reverse mutation test genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation for pharmaceutical-impurity mutagenicity — same endpoint (bacterial gene mutation), computational route, accepted for a defined purpose. The vault's cleanest computation-replaces-a-wet-assay edge.
  • Is a scored component of an adopted wet-assay DA (composes-with, added 2026-07-10). skin-sensitization-potency-its-da takes an in-silico prediction — from Derek Nexus or the OECD QSAR Toolbox — as one of its three information sources, scoring it 1 (positive) / 0 (negative) into a 0–7 potency total. This is not computation prioritising wet testing (the tier-1 edge below); it is computation sitting inside the adopted method as a graded input. A distinct and stronger relationship than "supporting evidence," and the first of its kind in the vault.
  • Accessibility split inside one DA (a regulatory axis the vault does not currently track): the classic ITSv1 uses Derek Nexus, a commercial license-gated Lhasa tool; ITSv2 uses the free OECD QSAR Toolbox. Two otherwise identical adopted defined approaches, differing only in whether a laboratory can afford the in-silico leg. Same family as the patented HepaRG™ line under metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg and the proprietary Corrositex® kit under skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435.
  • Tier-1 screen for the genotox battery (composes-with): structural-alert (Q)SAR is the customary first tier feeding the mammalian gene-mutation genotoxicity-mammalian-gene-mutation and chromosome-damage genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus assays — computation prioritises what gets tested wet.
  • Carcinogenicity structural alerts (composes-with): (Q)SAR alerts for genotoxic carcinogens complement the cell-transformation assays carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay that catch non-genotoxic carcinogens — the computational and wet halves of the carcinogenicity picture.
  • Cross-cutting computational tier (global-ripple): unlike every other map (each tied to a wet assay/endpoint), this is the computational layer that in principle composes-with all of them (read-across for repeat-dose, skin-sens, etc.). It parallels the PBK/QIVIVE hub pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics as the vault's second cross-cutting, non-endpoint-specific method — PBK is the toxicokinetic connective tissue, (Q)SAR/read-across the structure-based connective tissue. Added 2026-07-19 (Ahn et al., deep learning for DART)

  • ⚠️ Does NOT narrow the fertility blank, and this map must not read as though it did. reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams stays bench-bound / no-nam-exists. The authors themselves scope it: "the model does not predict DART outcomes directly" — it predicts mechanism-linked bioactivity, aggregated by majority vote. That is the vault's most-repeated category error, restated in machine learning: mechanism screens are not apical endpoints. Kumbhar 2026 named exactly this about TG 455/458/493/456 — "receptor activation and hormone synthesis, not fertility." A model trained on those screens inherits the same gap; training on 23 mechanism assays does not synthesise an apical prediction, and 57% recall against in-vivo labels is what that gap looks like numerically.

  • ⚠️ The sharpest thing here is about reporting, not capability — and it belongs to ml-model-reporting-reproducibility. That map found interpretation methods reported in about half of toxicology ML papers, with data and code availability substantially underreported, and reframed the model layer's blocker from accuracy to accountability. Ahn et al. is the good case by every one of those measures: publicly funded, funding_flag: none, data shared, imbalance-appropriate primary metric chosen and justified, applicability domain computed and applied, limitations listed, and the failure of its own headline method (multi-task learning) reported plainly. And the abstract still claims "reliability and generalizability" for an external result that sits below its own dataset's majority-class baseline. If that happens with clean funding, open data and honest limitations, the model half's problem is not candour — it is that no reporting convention requires the one comparison that would catch it. The audit measured the gap; this is the gap with a face on it.

  • Composes-with automation-tox21-qhts-platform — the model layer reaching back into the screening layer. The 23 assays are ToxCast bioassays: the same US EPA high-throughput screening infrastructure whose robotic half the vault mapped on 2026-07-13. This is the first source in the vault where the computational-connective layer is built directly on top of the automatable-open-loop screening layer — robots generate the data, a model tries to supply the interpretation the robots never had. Structurally, that is the closed loop the vault is looking for. And on the endpoint it was tried on, the interpretation layer came out worse than answering "toxic" every time. Not a refutation of the architecture; a measurement of how far the hard endpoints are from it.

  • Composes-with eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494 — a shared design pattern, found on both sides the same night. This model carries a computed applicability domain (nearest-neighbour distance on MACCS fingerprints, threshold mean + 0.5σ; out-of-domain predictions excluded). TG 494 enforces its domain with a machine-runnable pre-test (pH, phase separation, absorbance delta). A wet assay and a machine-learning model, in different literatures, independently building the ability to decline to answer — see machine-checkable-applicability-domain-gate. (Vault inference; neither source frames it this way.)

  • Composes-with automation-self-driving-labs — this map is now a kernel a proposed orchestrator drives (added 2026-07-21). ToxMCP (Djidrovski 2026) wraps the OECD QSAR Toolbox as a typed tool an LLM agent can call under provenance and applicability-domain guardrails. The read-across method this map grades is one of the five kernels that orchestration layer orchestrates — the model half of the loop, with a ledger bolted on. Changes nothing about this map's science or standing; a note that the graded model layer is starting to be wrapped for agentic use. A one-author preprint, no executed evaluation — no status moves.
