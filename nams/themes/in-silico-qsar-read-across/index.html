In-silico (Q)SAR & read-across — computational NAM — method

Status: consolidating (2026-07-07) — the vault's first computational method, filling the standing In-silico (standalone) — none yet coverage blank. Sharpest split-axes case in the vault, and it lands on an endpoint we already map. evidence_tier: in-silico-mechanistic for the broad class, rising to regulatory-adopted in one narrow domain; regulatory_status: proposed/within-IATA broadly, but a genuine adopted stand-alone use (ICH M7) exists. consolidating, not consensus, because it is a stand-alone adopted method only in that one domain — broadly it is a within-IATA supporting tool.

Sub-threads: none — one argument at 10 dated bullets, and it is a single split-axes claim: computation is a supporting tool almost everywhere and a licensed replacement in exactly one bounded domain (ICH M7, bacterial mutagenicity of pharmaceutical impurities). Every bullet either widens or narrows that one bounded domain, or prices how far the broad case is from it. The apparent strands — regulatory adoptions, benchmark accuracy, tooling — are not separable arguments; they are the evidence for and against a single question about scope. [reviewed 2026-08-09 @ 10 bullets]

What we believe now

(Q)SAR predicts an endpoint from molecular structure; read-across infers a data-poor chemical's toxicity from data-rich analogues (category formation). Both are purely computational and governed by the OECD (Q)SAR validation principles (defined endpoint, unambiguous algorithm, defined applicability domain, appropriate predictivity, mechanistic interpretation where possible), operationalised in the free OECD QSAR Toolbox and commercial platforms. Two standings coexist:

  • Broadly (most endpoints): accepted as supporting evidence within a weight-of-evidence / IATA (e.g. REACH Annex XI permits read-across/(Q)SAR under conditions) — not a stand-alone test replacement. Applicability-domain-bounded and endpoint-dependent: a prediction outside its training domain is not trustworthy.
  • Narrowly (ICH M7): for bacterial mutagenicity of pharmaceutical impurities, two complementary (Q)SAR methodologies (expert rule-based + statistical) + expert review are accepted as a stand-alone substitute for the Ames test — computation replacing an adopted wet assay in a bounded domain.

The honest summary: strongest where it's narrow and adopted (ICH M7), weaker but broadly useful as a within-IATA supporting NAM.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: computational-connective — Not an assay, so it is scored off the ladder. This is the model layer of a closed loop. And it is further along than anything else in the vault: under ICH M7 a (Q)SAR prediction can replace the Ames test outright — the existence proof that a computational method can be allowed to make the regulatory call unaided. Everything in AI-for-toxicology is trying to reach where this already is.

  • 2026-08-05 — THE METRIC THAT IMPROVED IS NOT THE ONE A DECISION USES, AND THE KNOWLEDGE BASE IS THINNEST WHERE THE HAZARDS ARE. Status HELD at consolidating, loop_readiness HELD at computational-connective. Ingested albi-2026-semantic-knowledge-qsar-gnn (iScience 29:116937, PMID 42542678, DOI 10.1016/j.isci.2026.116937, open access; US NIH R00LM013646 + P30-ES013508; declared no competing interests, but funding_flag: [method-developer] — the senior author built ComptoxAI, the knowledge graph being evaluated). Graph neural networks predicting activity on 37 Tox21 assays were given a knowledge graph wiring each chemical to associated genes and pathways. (1) Ranking improved significantly and consistently: AUROC 0.894 ± 0.053 / 0.890 ± 0.054 for the two semantic architectures against 0.845 ± 0.065 (random forest), 0.855 ± 0.061 (gradient boosting) and 0.849 ± 0.061 (structure-only neural encoder), p < 0.001 in every pairwise comparison, top score in 33 of 37 assays. (2) ⚠ And on the metrics a screening decision runs on, it did not beat a plain random forest: the semantic GNN's AUPRC 0.443 / 0.393 and MCC 0.382 / 0.351 were significantly worse than random forest's 0.479 / 0.431; the joint SemMol variant only reached parity. The authors state it themselves — "semantic models improve AUROC but do not always improve AUPRC or MCC." AUROC is a ranking statistic; AUPRC and MCC describe what happens when you draw a line and act. (3) The mechanism was measured, and it generalises past this paper: active compounds have lower median knowledge-graph connectivity than inactives in 36 of 37 assays, and in 11 of the 12 assays with under 5% actives. A literature-derived graph records attention, not biology — so context enriches the well-studied inactive majority and thins out exactly at the rare actives a screen exists to find. (4) Expanding the graph to pathway entities gave no gain: relevance and proximity govern whether added knowledge helps, not volume. (5) No operating threshold is proposed anywhere, no chemical is called active, and nothing states what score licenses what action. A better predictor is not a decision procedure — this map's standing position, now with a clean measurement behind it.   • 2026-08-14 — A structure-only model predicts three cardiac ion channels at once and does worst on the one the field screens for — and the rule that does it is behind a login. Status HELD at consolidating; loop_readiness HELD at computational-connective. Ingested nursyafi-2026-cardiosim-tox-multitask-qsar (Nursyafi et al. 2026, Archives of Toxicology, online ahead of print 11 August 2026, PMID 42581112, DOI 10.1007/s00204-026-04519-4; Kumoh National Institute of Technology + Institute of Bioinformatics Indonesia + Meta Heart Co., Ltd; full text GATED at Springer, everything below from the verbatim abstract). (1) The science. For twenty years the standard early cardiac screen has asked one question — does the molecule block hERG, a potassium channel whose blockade risks a dangerous rhythm disturbance. Multitask consistently beat single-task — the channels share the structural features that make a molecule bind, so learning them together learns the chemistry rather than three shadows of it. Potency regression R² = 0.94 under multitask learning (validation set not stated in the abstract, so not attributed to the hardest one).   • ⚠️ Does NOT narrow the fertility blank, and this map must not read as though it did. reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams stays bench-bound / no-nam-exists.
