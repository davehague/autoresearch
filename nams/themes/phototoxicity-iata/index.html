Phototoxicity IATA (OECD GD 397) — phototoxicity — battery hub

Status: consolidating (2026-07-11) — the two axes disagree, and that is the finding. evidence_tier: formally-validated (every method it composes is an adopted, validated OECD TG, and the AOP grounding is sound) but regulatory_status: proposed — GD 397 is a Guidance Document (OECD Series on Testing and Assessment No. 397, 2024), applied case-by-case, not a Test Guideline. Guidance-level composition over adopted parts: the same shape as OECD GD 331 (pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics). The vault's third guidance-level hub and the composition layer of the phototoxicity column.

What we believe now

GD 397 — proposed by Japan to the OECD Working Party on Hazard Assessment — maps the adopted phototoxicity methods onto the Adverse Outcome Pathway for photo-irritation and orders them into a tiered strategy, cheapest gate first:

TierMethodReadsIn the vault
1TG 101 — UV-VIS absorption spectraMEC < 1,000 L mol⁻¹ cm⁻¹ → very unlikely to be photoreactivenot yet ingested
2TG 495 — ROS assay (in chemico)photo-generated reactive oxygen species; ICH S10 names it an optional initial screen for pharmaceuticalsnot yet ingested
3TG 432 — 3T3 NRUlight/dark cytotoxicity ratio, no barrier → sensitive screenphototoxicity-3t3-nru
4TG 498 — RhE PTtopical confirmation in human epidermis, with a barrierphototoxicity-rhe-pt

The top gate is a filter, not a verdict: the guidance is explicit that a low MEC does not close the question, because photolabile chemicals can still be caught downstream.

Two structural facts about this hub matter more than the flowchart.

(1) This IATA is not a replacement strategy, because there is nothing to replace. Every other battery in this vault exists to displace an animal incumbent — the 2-out-of-3 DA displaces the LLNA, the eye DA the Draize, the DNT battery the in-vivo DNT study, FET and RTgill the acute fish test. Phototoxicity has no validated in-vivo method at all: TG 498 records that its reference classifications are "mostly based on clinical human data as no validated in vivo method is available for this endpoint." So this IATA is the primary regulatory strategy for the endpoint, benchmarked directly against humans. It is the vault's first sight of what an integrated testing approach looks like when regulators design one NAM-native, rather than retro-fitting one around an animal test — and the answer turns out to be: a physico-chemical gate, an in-chemico reactivity screen, a sensitive cell screen, a human-tissue confirmation.

(2) Two of the three light-activated hazards have no validated method whatsoever. GD 397 splits the domain into phototoxicity (photoirritation), photoallergy (immune-mediated; light turns the chemical into a hapten) and photogenotoxicity (direct DNA photoexcitation, or indirect via an excited chemical) — and states that "there are no validated test methods (i.e., OECD Test Guideline) to evaluate photosensitive or photogenotoxic potential of chemicals to this date." It then scopes itself around the hole, explaining that an IATA over non-validated methods "would be difficult to use in regulatory contexts." A documented absence, of the same species as the fertility blank (reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams) — see photoallergy-photogenotoxicity-no-validated-method. This is the column's honest headline, and it is not "animal testing displaced."

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — As an architecture this is what a loopable battery looks like: a tiered cascade (TG 101 -> TG 495 -> TG 432 -> TG 498) with a decision gate at each rung. Its two lower tiers share a biotransformation blind spot, which an automated cascade would propagate rather than catch.

  • Correlated blind spot: shares a failure mode with [phototoxicity-3t3-nru, phototoxicity-ros-assay]. In a battery these are one vote, not two — and an automated loop trusting the decision rule will compound the error rather than catch it.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — tier 2 is now a map. phototoxicity-ros-assay (TG 495) is this IATA's second rung: 100% sensitivity, 42–82% specificity, 100% negative predictive value, no false negatives on 42 coded chemicals. Its negative terminates the assessment; its positive routes onward to phototoxicity-3t3-nru (TG 432) then phototoxicity-rhe-pt (TG 498). That NPV is what makes the tiering work — and it is the arithmetic proof of the vault's 2026-07-11 correction that specificity must be read against battery position, not as standalone method quality. The IATA's architecture is now quantitatively justified, not merely asserted.
  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — the IATA's shared blind spot: no tier has metabolic competence. TG 495 "has not been designed to address indirect mechanisms of phototoxicity, such as effects of metabolites." Neither TG 101 (a spectrum), nor TG 432 (3T3 fibroblasts), nor TG 498 (RhE) carries meaningful biotransformation either. So a photo-inert parent with a photo-reactive metabolite can pass every tier of this IATA. This is the same structural failure the connect layer found in the aquatic column, where fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet and ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line both under-predict bioactivation-dependent chemicals. Absent biotransformation is now a named, cross-column, correlated blind spot — and inside a tiered IATA it is worse than in a battery, because the tiers are sequential: one shared blind spot is enough to let a chemical through the whole cascade. Flagged for a human.
  • Battery composition (this map IS the composition): tiers TG 101 → TG 495 → TG 432 → TG 498. Two of the four are mapped (phototoxicity-3t3-nru, phototoxicity-rhe-pt); TG 101 (UV-VIS/MEC) and TG 495 (ROS assay) are not yet ingested — the column's own open gap, recorded in plain text, not as wikilinks.
  • Battery-archetype comparison (structural, cross-endpoint): the vault now holds five composition archetypes and this one is a new kind. The fixed-rule 2-out-of-3 DA (skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach) and the eye DA (eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da) are data-interpretation procedures returning a classification from a fixed assay set. The DNT battery (developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb) is judgement-integrated. The repeat-dose framework (repeat-dose-systemic-toxicity-nam) is tiered concern-based. This one is a sequential screening cascade with an early physico-chemical exit — the only battery in the vault whose first gate is not biological at all, and the only one that is not shadowing an animal test.
  • Guidance-level-hub sibling (structural): pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics (GD 331) is the vault's other WNT-approved-guidance hub composing adopted parts without itself being a TG — same two-axis signature (formally-validated / not-adopted), same case-by-case application. Worth reading together as "what a regulator does when the composition, not the assay, is the innovation."
  • In-silico edge (composes-with, weak but real): the TG 101 tier is a physico-chemical property gate (molar extinction coefficient), which is the closest thing in the vault to a non-biological first filter. It is adjacent to — but is not — the (Q)SAR/read-across hub (in-silico-qsar-read-across): a measured absorption spectrum is not a computational prediction. Named to keep the distinction sharp.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. Two near-edges named and refused (the important output of this ripple): photoallergy is mechanistically the skin-sensitisation AOP with a photochemical step at the molecular initiating event — yet the adopted sensitisation battery (DPRA/KeratinoSens/h-CLAT) has never been validated with an irradiation arm, so it does not cover it; and the genotox battery (genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation, genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus, genotoxicity-mammalian-gene-mutation, genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa) has no irradiation arm either and does not cover photogenotoxicity. Both adjacencies are real; neither is coverage. This vault does not invent applicability domains — the blanks stay blank, and are logged as gaps.
