ToxTracker mechanistic reporter assay (OECD TG 446A) — genotoxicity — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-07) — regulatorily adopted on both axes, freshly and narrowly. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 446A, adopted 2026). Adoption is what earns consensus (deterministic per the evidence model), but two honest qualifiers travel with it: it is ~1 day old at ingest, and it is a mechanistic follow-up tool, not a primary hazard call — its job is to interpret genotox positives, not detect them.

What we believe now

ToxTracker (Toxys) is a panel of GFP-reporter mouse embryonic stem cell (mESC) lines — published design: six reporters — that activate along distinct cellular-stress pathways, so a single run discriminates the mode of action behind a genotoxic signal: direct DNA reactivity / DNA damage (ATR–Chk1), oxidative stress (Nrf2), and protein damage / general cellular stress (unfolded-protein response, p53). OECD adopted it as Test Guideline 446A (announced 6 Jul 2026), positioned "as a follow-up tool for equivocal findings from established in-vitro genotoxicity assays and … to support the interpretation of positive genotoxicity findings." It complements — does not replace — the gene-mutation (Ames, TG 471) and chromosome-damage (micronucleus, TG 487) members of the primary battery. We record no sensitivity/specificity or inter-lab numbers: the adoption announcement carries none, and the TG document itself is not yet archived (pdf_unavailable).

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Battery composition (composes-with, a distinct layer): ToxTracker is the mechanistic follow-up to the primary genotox battery — genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation (gene mutation) and genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus (chromosome damage). Those two detect; TG 446A explains (why a positive scored — DNA-reactive vs indirect oxidative/protein stress) and helps resolve equivocal results. Not redundant: a different job on the same endpoint.
  • Carcinogenicity edge (composes-with): discriminating DNA-reactive from indirect (oxidative/protein-stress) genotoxicity is the same genotoxic-vs-non-genotoxic distinction the cell-transformation-assay carcinogenicity map turns on — carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay catches non-genotoxic carcinogens the gene/chromosome assays miss; ToxTracker helps classify the mechanism of the genotoxic ones.
  • Global-ripple: distinct from the skin/eye local maps and the systemic maps (DNT, hepatotox, repeat-dose, PBK, developmental); its only edges are within genotoxicity and to carcinogenicity. Ames' known false-positive tendency is exactly the kind of equivocal result a mechanistic follow-up is meant to adjudicate.
