Buccal micronucleus cytome (BMCyt) assay — the vault's first human-biomonitoring NAM — and its fourth eye-scored map

🚩 CONVENTION DEPARTURE — flagged for human review. This is a biomonitoring method, not a predictive hazard assay, and its endpoint (genotoxicity-biomonitoring) is new to the vault. Every other method here answers "would this chemical harm someone?" This one answers "was this person harmed?" It is non-animal — it is human — so it is a NAM by the vault's scope, but it is an exposure/effect biomarker, and reading it as a hazard test would be a category error. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md.

Status: emerging (2026-07-18). One source, and that source is a review — a synthesis of other groups' deep-learning results, with no primary data of its own. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method, regulatory_status: none.

What we believe now

The assay. Scrape a few cells painlessly from the inside of a person's cheek, stain them, and look at them. Exfoliated buccal cells turn over fast and sit in constant contact with whatever the person breathes, eats, smokes or drinks. The buccal micronucleus cytome (BMCyt) assay scores several cell fates on those cells at once:

What is scoredWhat it means, in plain terms
MicronucleiA fragment of chromosome left outside the main nucleus — a chromosome got broken or lost
Nuclear budsThe cell extruding amplified or damaged DNA
KaryorrhexisThe nucleus fragmenting — a cell dying
KaryolysisThe nucleus dissolving — a cell dying, later stage
PyknosisThe nucleus condensing into a dense blob — a cell dying, another route

Together: genotoxic damage plus cytotoxic damage, measured directly in a living person, with a cheek swab. Because sampling is minimally invasive it is usable in children, patients and other populations where a blood draw is a barrier — which is the assay's real argument for existing.

Why it is human-in-the-loop. The whole readout is morphology judged by eye. The source is blunt that manual scoring "suffers from user subjectivity and scorer variability in addition to being extremely time and labour-intensive." That is the same blocker, for the same reason, as genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus, carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay and developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity.

⚠️ The escape hatch arrives WITH the map — a first, and a small one

The vault's three other eye-scored maps each sat blocked for weeks and then acquired a demonstrated machine-vision escape hatch (zebrafish 2026-07-15, micronucleus 2026-07-16, cell transformation 2026-07-17). This map is the first to open already carrying one. That is a genuine change in the pattern — and it is also the weakest of the four demonstrations, for reasons that must not be glossed:

1. It is secondary evidence. Smith et al. is a review. It reports no dataset and no result of its own. Every number below belongs to somebody else's study and is cited as such:

Cited fromApproachReported
Rodrigues et al.Classification CNN, imaging flow cytometry98% mononucleated, 95% binucleated — but only 82–85% micronucleated
Panchbhai et al.Faster R-CNN object detection>90% average precision for micronuclei quantification
YOLOv7 variantsObject detection, small-object tuned"higher accuracy and faster processing times" — no figure given
Modified AlexNet + attentionClassification"elevated levels of accuracy" — no figure given
U-Net / U-Net+Segmentation"often >85–90% on held-out test sets"

2. The performance is worst exactly where the signal is. Read the Rodrigues row again: 98% and 95% on the abundant, easy classes; 82–85% on the micronucleated cells — the rare class the entire assay exists to count. That is textbook class imbalance: the model is strongest on the common case and weakest on the event of interest. A headline "98% accurate" would be true and would mean almost nothing here.

3. It is not an independent second opinion. Co-author Paul Rees also co-authored Wills et al. 2021, the imaging-flow-cytometry micronucleus scorer the vault already holds on genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus. The same group is a substantial part of the evidence for both of the vault's micronucleus-scoring escape hatches. Recorded, not laundered.

4. The authors' own stated limits. No positive controls exist for buccal cells; samples vary with hydration, oral hygiene and inflammation; models may not generalise across imaging conditions; the black-box problem complicates clinical acceptance; there is no universal standard for AI-based buccal micronucleus detection; and compute cost is non-trivial.

funding_flag: none — no funding and no conflicts declared. Clean on commercial bias; the independence problem above is a different problem and is flagged separately.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: human-in-the-loop — the deterministic ladder:

  • Readout? Images of cells, scored by a trained human on morphology. → rung 3, human judgment load-bearing in the call.
  • Decision rule? Not reached; the ladder stops at rung 3.
  • Inputs? No in-vivo animal study consumed. Not bench-bound.

The tier does NOT move on this source, and the reason is the point. A demonstrated scorer is not a validated scorer. None of the cited models is ring-trialled, standardised, or written into any protocol, and the review says outright that no universal standard exists. A human stays load-bearing in the call.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚠️ Does NOT compose into the in-vitro genotoxicity battery — an anti-edge, stated to prevent the obvious error. genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus (TG 487), genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation (TG 471) and genotoxicity-mammalian-gene-mutation (TG 476/490) form a battery that asks "is this chemical genotoxic?" BMCyt does not answer that question at all. It measures accumulated damage in a person whose exposure history is mixed, uncontrolled and mostly unknown. It shares the word "micronucleus" with TG 487 and shares neither its input nor its output. Adjacency is not applicability; this is not a fourth vote in the genotox battery.
  • Shares the scoring problem, and therefore the escape hatch, with three maps. The blocker on genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus, carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay, developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity and this map is one blocker wearing four hats: a trained human judging cell or organism morphology. All four now carry a demonstrated machine-vision path and none carries a validated one. Whichever gets a ring trial or a guideline-adopted automated readout first is the vault's next likely human-in-the-loop → automatable-open-loop promotion — and on current evidence that is still micronucleus (inter-laboratory, benchmark-dose-matching), not this map.
  • ⚠️ A correlated blind spot between the two micronucleus scorers — flagged as a VAULT INFERENCE. The automated scorer here and the Wills 2021 scorer on genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus share a co-author, an imaging-flow-cytometry lineage, and the same underlying task (find a small bright object next to a big bright object). They are not two independent demonstrations that micronucleus scoring is automatable; they are closer to one demonstration reported twice. The rail applies to evidence here, not to a testing battery — a use the rail was not written for, and flagged as a departure. An AI loop treating "two published scorers agree it works" as replication would be making exactly the error the rail exists to catch.
  • Limited by ml-model-reporting-reproducibility (tonight's other new map). Every model cited here sits inside the literature Kappenberg et al. audited — a literature where interpretation methods are reported in only about half of papers and code and data availability are substantially underreported. The base rate is the context for the claim.
  • Ripples to automation-self-driving-labs — agenda drift, with a twist. The agenda-drift finding says effort flows toward what is already automatable. BMCyt is cheap, minimally invasive and image-based: highly automatable, and pointed at human populations rather than at replacing an animal test. Automating it would produce a great deal of human exposure data and would not retire a single animal study. Worth holding beside the vault's premise rather than counting as progress against it.
  • Global-ripple: walked every map. Refutes nothing, duplicates nothing, moves no other map's Status or loop tier. It opens a new endpoint (biomonitoring) and a fourth instance of the vault's eye-scoring blocker.
