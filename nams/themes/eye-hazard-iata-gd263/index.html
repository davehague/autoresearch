Eye-hazard IATA (OECD GD 263, 3rd ed.) — eye irritation — battery hub

Status: consolidating (2026-07-25) — 🚩 auto-coined 2026-07-25 · the two axes disagree, and so do the two IATAs in this vault. evidence_tier: formally-validated (every one of the nine methods it composes is an adopted, validated OECD Test Guideline) but regulatory_status: proposed — GD 263 is a Guidance Document (OECD Series on Testing and Assessment No. 263, third edition 2024), applied case-by-case, not a Test Guideline. The vault's fourth guidance-level hub, alongside GD 397 (phototoxicity IATA) and GD 331 (PBK/QIVIVE). It closes a coverage gap the vault named against itself on 2026-07-22: "the vault has a phototoxicity IATA map and no eye IATA map, which means it tracks the eye components and not the thing that makes them a battery."

What we believe now

GD 263 replaced the old "sequential testing strategy" that used to sit in a supplement to TG 405 (the rabbit Draize eye test). It sorts every kind of eye-hazard evidence into nine Modules in three Parts:

  • Part 1 — existing information, physico-chemical properties, non-testing methods (Modules 1–8): human data; rabbit data (TG 405); in-vitro data from nine adopted TGs; non-OECD animal data; non-OECD alternative-method data; skin-corrosion data; physico-chemical properties (pH and acid/alkaline reserve above all); and non-testing predictions — (Q)SAR, expert systems, grouping and read-across for substances, bridging and additivity for mixtures.
  • Part 2 — Module 9: a weight-of-evidence analysis over all of it.
  • Part 3 — new testing, entered only "if the WoE analysis is inconclusive," starting with in-vitro methods. The rabbit test is "to be used only as a last resort."

Four of the nine are new to the vault, and two of those four are not replacement methods at all. TG 437 runs on eyes from slaughtered cattle and TG 438 on eyes from slaughtered chickens. They reduce animal testing and do not remove animals from the process — the vault's replacement-versus-reduction line (immunotoxicity-myelotoxicity-cfu-gm) running through the middle of an adopted battery, unremarked by the guidance.

The strategy

  • Top-Down — begin with a method that identifies Cat 1 with few false positives; use when the prior says the chemical is probably severe.
  • Bottom-Up — begin with a method that identifies No Cat with few false negatives; "should be followed only when all available collected information and the WoE assessment result in a high a priori probability that the test chemical does not require classification."
  • Suspected Cat 2 — neither ladder resolves the middle class, so GD 263 routes to TG 467 or TG 492B, "stand-alone methods that can predict three UN GHS categories."

TG 437, 438, 491 and 496 can start both ladders at once (they call Cat 1 and No Cat), covering two tiers with one assay. TG 492 and TG 494 can only rule out; TG 460 can only rule in.

The three findings bigger than the flowchart

(1) ⚠️ OECD names conditional independence — the vault's rail is not the vault's invention. Verbatim: "when using two or more test methods, the conditional independence of these test methods should be considered (Adriaens et al. 2017a; Hoffman et al., 2008)." The property the vault has been calling the correlated-blind-spot rail and describing in CLAUDE.md as "the vault's sharpest contribution" is a named statistical concept with a 2008 literature, written into adopted guidance as an instruction. What survives is sharper than the original claim: GD 263 gives the instruction and no procedure, records it for no pair, and contains a maximally dependent pair whose relationship it states as a cross-reference. And every regulatory proposal the vault has caught building a correlated battery — the UK's FET+RTgill fish stack, the EU's REACH revision, the EU-NETVAL thyroid battery — was written by a body with this sentence available. The gap was never conceptual; nobody runs the check. → oecd-names-conditional-independence-as-a-battery-design-rule

(2) The guidance names a shared failure mode across its own Cat-1 tier, and the remedy. "Currently accepted methods for identifying UN GHS Cat. 1, like BCOP and ICE, underpredict 14-47% of the in vivo Cat. 1 chemicals … mostly those inducing persistent effects without occurrence of initial high level injuries." Both measure acute injury; GHS Category 1 is defined on irreversibility. A missing time dimension rather than a missing biological subsystem — a new species of correlated blind spot for this vault, and one that mechanistic diversity cannot fix. GD 263's remedy is nevertheless the Tox21 counter-design: "additional in vitro test methods … based, if possible, on different mechanism of actions." → bcop-and-ice-share-a-persistence-blind-spot

(3) A specificity ceiling set by the regulator, stated as arithmetic. "due to the very high sensitivity required by regulatory authorities … their specificity will never go beyond 60-80% (the highest the specificity, the more limited the applicability)." ICE and STE, the best rule-out performers, manage only 76–81% specificity at ~97% sensitivity. This re-frames four numbers the vault has recorded with mild alarm (TG 492's 63–72%, TG 494's 67%, TG 495's 42–82%, h-CLAT's 66%) as in-family design consequences, not four independent disappointments — and the parenthesis warns that a better specificity may just mean a smaller domain. → regulatory-sensitivity-demand-caps-specificity-at-60-80-percent

Also recorded: TG 467's real numbers, which the vault did not hold — "The rate of true positives varies between 56.3 and 69.2 %. Between 18.3% and 30.4 % are overpredicted as Cat1, whereas between 0.9 and 13.6 are false negatives." And the human-relevance principle: "it is important that the test methods employed reflect mechanisms of eye irritation that are relevant to humans, and do not necessarily need to reflect or cover the mechanisms that take place in the rabbit test."

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: human-in-the-loop — and this is the vault's two IATAs landing in different tiers, which is the axis doing its job.

GD 397 (phototoxicity) is closed-loop-ready because it is a sequential cascade — TG 101 → TG 495 → TG 432 → TG 498, a gate at each rung, the flowchart is the decision procedure.

GD 263 is not, because Part 2 is a weight-of-evidence judgement. Module 9's subsections are headings for a thought process (coverage of sources, data quality, adequacy, consistency, conclusions), Annex I is "an example of matrix," Annex II is "examples of WoE evaluations." Examples, not an algorithm. And the branch choice is itself a judgement: the assessor must "formulate a hypothesis of the most likely eye hazard potential" before choosing top-down or bottom-up. A human-set prior selects the experiment.

⚠️ This is not the usual human-in-the-loop shape, and the distinction decides where to invest. The vault's four other human-in-the-loop maps are blocked at the scoring step — a trained eye down a microscope — and for those, machine vision is a demonstrated escape hatch. This hub is blocked after every scoring step: all nine components produce clean instrument numbers and several are closed-loop-ready alone. No image classifier reaches this blocker. What moves it is a defined approach — a published data-interpretation procedure over the module outputs — which is what TG 467 is for one fixed pair, and what the DNT battery has been waiting for with no date. Same distinction the vault drew on 2026-07-20. → eye-iata-integration-step-is-weight-of-evidence

The partial escape: the Cat-2 sub-path (TG 467 or TG 492B) is fully specified and closed-loop-ready. A loop can run the machine-runnable middle of this guidance; it needs a human to decide whether to enter it, and for every inconclusive outcome.

  • Correlated blind spot: shares a failure mode with [eye-irritation-rhce-no-category, eye-irritation-rhce-time-to-toxicity-tg492b, eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494] — three of its nine components are living human corneal epithelium without metabolism, and two of those three read the same property by the same readout. In a battery these are one vote, not three — and an automated loop trusting the decision rule will compound the error rather than catch it.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Battery composition (this map IS the composition). Nine adopted TGs across Module 3, plus six other evidence modules. Five components are mapped: eye-irritation-rhce-no-category (TG 492), eye-irritation-rhce-time-to-toxicity-tg492b (TG 492B), eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494 (TG 494), eye-macromolecular-ordered-matrix-tg496 (TG 496), eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da (TG 467). Four are not: TG 437 (BCOP), TG 438 (ICE), TG 491 (STE), TG 460 (FL) — recorded here as plain text and in the index's open gaps, not as wikilinks.
  • ⚠️ Independence audit of this battery, which the guidance asks for and does not perform. Of the five mapped components, four are living epithelium with no metabolism and three of those are RhCE-family: TG 492 and TG 492B are the same tissue and the same MTT readout (one measurement, two timetables — tg492-and-tg492b-are-one-measurement-read-twice); TG 494 is the same tissue read as electrical resistance, which the guideline's own text ties to the viability TG 492 measures. Only eye-macromolecular-ordered-matrix-tg496 is structurally independent — acellular protein denaturation, no cells at all. Adding the four unmapped methods would improve this: TG 460 reads tight-junction permeability in a kidney cell line, TG 437/438 read whole-cornea opacity and swelling in two other species. So the full nine-method set is more mechanistically diverse than the vault's five — but its two Cat-1 anchors share the persistence gap, so diversity of mechanism does not buy independence on the question that matters most.
  • Battery-archetype comparison (structural, cross-endpoint). The vault now holds six composition archetypes and this is the second judgement-integrated one. The fixed-rule DAs — skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach (GL 497 Part I) and eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da (TG 467) — return a class from a fixed assay set by a published rule. phototoxicity-iata (GD 397) is a sequential cascade with gates. repeat-dose-systemic-toxicity-nam is tiered concern-based. developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb is judgement-integrated — and so is this one. Two of six batteries in this vault are blocked on the same missing object: a defined approach over module outputs. That is the field's dominant decision-rule gap stated at the battery level rather than the assay level.
  • Composes-with, outside the endpoint. Module 6 pulls in skin-corrosion evidence (a skin corrosive is presumed a Cat 1 eye hazard), which makes skin-corrosion-rhe (TG 431) and skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435 (TG 435) inputs to an eye assessment. Module 8 pulls in (Q)SAR, read-across and mixture bridging, making in-silico-qsar-read-across a first-class evidence source here rather than a supporting one. This is the vault's clearest instance of the model layer sitting inside an adopted battery's evidence base, and it is a compose-with the phototoxicity IATA does not have.
  • Guidance-level-hub siblings (structural). phototoxicity-iata (GD 397) and pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics (GD 331) share the signature: formally-validated science, proposed acceptance, case-by-case application, composition-as-the-innovation. Read the three together as "what a regulator does when the assembly, not the assay, is the contribution" — and note that only the cascade one is loopable.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. One near-edge named and refused: the "very high sensitivity required by regulatory authorities" argument generalises beyond the eye — it is the same logic that makes phototoxicity-ros-assay's 42–82% specificity acceptable at 100% negative predictive value, and the same logic behind skin-sensitization-hclat's 93%/66% split. But GD 263's 60–80% number is specific to eye No-Category methods and the vault does not transplant it to other endpoints. The principle ripples; the number does not.
