h-CLAT — dendritic-cell activation (skin sensitisation KE3) — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-05) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 442E). A component of the 2-out-of-3 defined approach.

What we believe now

h-CLAT reads the dendritic-cell-activation key event (KE3) via flow-cytometric CD86/CD54 up-regulation in THP-1 cells. It calls sensitiser vs non-sensitiser at 85% accuracy (N=142), 93% sensitivity, 66% specificity vs LLNA, ~80% within/between-lab reproducibility. Highest sensitivity of the three 2o3 components (rarely misses a sensitiser) but lowest specificity (over-calls) — which is precisely why the 2o3 pairs it with the more specific DPRA and KeratinoSens. One input to the defined approaches, not a stand-alone test.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — CD86/CD54 dendritic-cell activation read by flow cytometry against MIT thresholds.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-05 — Ingested OECD TG 442E. Coins this map as the KE3 component of the 2o3 battery. 85/93/66 vs LLNA; adopted on both axes → consensus. TG 442E now houses four KE3 methods (h-CLAT, U-SENS, IL-8 Luc, GARDskin).

  • 2026-07-10 — Now a scored leg of the potency DA. Status holds at consensus. skin-sensitization-potency-its-da (GL 497 Part II) bands h-CLAT's MIT (minimum induction threshold, µg/mL) into a 0–3 score — ≤10 scores 3, >10 to ≤150 scores 2, >150 to ≤5000 scores 1, not calculated scores 0 — summed with a KE1 score and an in-silico score into a 0–7 potency total. The 2026 edition admits GARD®skin and U-SENS™ as alternative KE3 sources inside the DAs, confirming from primary text that the "KE3 new entrants" arrived as substitutable information sources, not as standalone TGs to watch. Both are proprietary platforms.

  • 2026-07-26 — The adoption history is less smooth than the adopted status suggests. Status and tier unchanged. jacobs-2024-ring-trials-reproducibility-crisis records that this method's between-laboratory reproducibility was initially below the target, and that approval followed multiple rounds in which "protocols [were] adapted based on ring trial experiences." The assay did not arrive transferable; it was made transferable by the ring trial finding it was not. Recorded because the vault's flagship closed-loop-ready stack rests on this method as one of its three components, and the vault should hold the fact that the component's protocol is partly a product of the interlaboratory process that several actors now propose to make optional (see validation-paradigm-nam-confidence). Nothing here downgrades h-CLAT — it passed, and it is adopted. → eight-methods-that-broke-when-a-second-lab-ran-them

  • 2026-08-08 — The interchangeable alternate got measured, and it is the same vote. Status, tier and loop-readiness all hold; correlated_blind_spot is now SET. This map has recorded since 2026-07-10 that Guideline 497 admits U-SENS™ as a substitutable key-event-3 source beside the h-CLAT, and filed it under "intra-slot redundancy — redundancy by design." nardelli-2025-u-sens-borderline-range-2o3 measures the substitution and forces a sharper reading. U-SENS™ (skin-sensitization-u-sens, coined this run) reads the same CD86 marker on another immortalised human myeloid line (U937 against this map's THP-1) for the same key event. Substituted into the 2-out-of-3 battery it scores 71% balanced accuracy against the LLNA (n=168) and 77% against human data (n=66) on its plain cut-off — at or below the bottom of the incumbent battery's published 72–97% range. "Redundancy by design" is right about the design and wrong about the redundancy: these are not two chances to catch a sensitiser, they are one measurement available from two suppliers, and an automated system running both permutations and reading agreement as corroboration is double-counting. → u-sens-substituted-for-hclat-in-the-2o3-defined-approach The same source publishes a borderline range around the alternate's adopted cut-off — a measured band in which a fixed, deterministic rule returns a call the data does not support. Nothing in it was computed for the h-CLAT, so this map's own domain_gate is deliberately left unset; but the h-CLAT's rule is the same kind of object (a single threshold on a noisy ratio), and whether it carries an equivalent band is now an explicit open question rather than an unexamined assumption. → the-adopted-cut-off-carries-an-uncertainty-band-the-decision-rule-does-not

Ingested krzykwa-2026-botanicals-dermal-nams-mixtures (Pharmaceutical Biology 64(1):922–948, DOI 10.1080/13880209.2026.2717803; Botanical Safety Consortium Dermal Working Group under a US FDA / NIEHS Memorandum of Understanding; funding_flag: [industry]). (a) The bound is in this assay's own guideline. Per Test Guideline 442E, test materials with log Kow above 3.5 may produce false negatives — log Kow measures how strongly a substance prefers oil to water, and essential oils are exactly that class. (b) The published record on botanicals runs both ways. Kolle et al. 2023: botanical extracts induced CD54, and five of them were non-sensitisers in vivo — over-calling. Shiraishi et al. 2021: Brazilian green propolis was negative here but a moderate sensitiser in the mouse local lymph node assay — under-calling. Same assay, same substance class, opposite errors. (c) ⚠ The shared masking route. For this assay and skin-sensitization-keratinosens, the consortium names cytotoxic constituents masking sensitising ones; both read against viability (this one stains viability alongside CD86/CD54 and runs a cytotoxicity dose-finder first), so a mixture that kills cells at the informative concentration returns two quiet negatives out of three, which the rule scores as a pass. The existing correlated_blind_spot row on this map is about a different thing — the U-SENS alternate reading the same marker on a second myeloid line — and is left unchanged; the new route is flagged for a human, not adopted, because no experiment has been run. (d) The paper names GARDskin as a more recently adopted key-event-3 alternative with "greater compatibility with difficult-to-test substances", to be considered after the first phase.
