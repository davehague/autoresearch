ALI airway epithelial models — acute inhalation toxicity — method

Status: emerging (2026-07-07) — a split-axes, cautious case. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method, regulatory_status: none. Coins the vault's inhalation / respiratory endpoint — the last of the fully-empty headline coverage gaps to get a first map.

What we believe now

Organotypic air–liquid-interface (ALI) airway epithelial models — a human model (HAEM, EpiAirway) and a characterised rat model (RAEM) — are exposed apically to test chemicals; toxicity is read by TEER (barrier integrity) and MTT (viability). Wallace et al. 2023 (Toxicol Sci 194(2):178–190, PMID 37280087) reported high rat↔human concordance across 14 reference chemicals (TEER R² = 0.78 and 0.88; MTT R² = 0.92), positioning ALI airway models as an inhalation screen, explicitly not a replacement. The honest read is the cautious one: (1) the numbers are in-vitro rat-vs-human concordance, not predictivity against in-vivo acute-inhalation lethality (LC50); (2) no in-vitro acute-inhalation method is regulatorily adopted (no OECD in-vitro TG as of 2025; the adopted tests remain in-vivo TG 403/436; NICEATM/OECD run case studies); (3) funding_flag: method-developer — EpiAirway's developer (MatTek) co-authored, so the concordance claim is developer-involved and not laundered into certainty.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — FDA's draft framework describes this method almost by name. regulatory-acceptance-fda-nam-framework (CDER, March 2026) gives as its worked example of "human biological relevance" for respiratory toxicity: "the model may be a tissue reconstructed from primary human lung cells that capture key mechanisms of toxicity (e.g., loss of membrane integrity) and cellular properties (e.g., beating cilia) of the in vivo human situation." That is a description of an ALI airway epithelial model, and "loss of membrane integrity" is this map's TEER readout. The Status does not move — emerging / regulatory_status: none remain correct: there is no adopted in-vitro TG (TG 403/436 remain the standard), there is still no in-vivo predictivity number, and the work is developer-involved. But the axis reading changes: none means "has no guideline," not "cannot be used regulatorily." Under CDER's fit-for-purpose route this method could support a specific context of use in a specific submission today, without a TG and without formal validation. That is a real change in what this map implies, and the vault should not keep narrating none as a closed door.
  • Technology family (not redundancy): the human-tissue ALI airway model is a technology cousin of the RhE skin/eye models and the hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps MPS — human-tissue in-vitro systems across different endpoints. A composition of approach, not overlapping assays.
