ALI airway epithelial models — acute inhalation toxicity — method

Status: emerging (2026-07-07) — a split-axes, cautious case. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method, regulatory_status: none. Coins the vault's inhalation / respiratory endpoint — the last of the fully-empty headline coverage gaps to get a first map.

What we believe now

Organotypic air–liquid-interface (ALI) airway epithelial models — a human model (HAEM, EpiAirway) and a characterised rat model (RAEM) — are exposed apically to test chemicals; toxicity is read by TEER (barrier integrity) and MTT (viability). Wallace et al. 2023 (Toxicol Sci 194(2):178–190, PMID 37280087) reported high rat↔human concordance across 14 reference chemicals (TEER R² = 0.78 and 0.88; MTT R² = 0.92), positioning ALI airway models as an inhalation screen, explicitly not a replacement. The honest read is the cautious one: (1) the numbers are in-vitro rat-vs-human concordance, not predictivity against in-vivo acute-inhalation lethality (LC50); (2) no in-vitro acute-inhalation method is regulatorily adopted (no OECD in-vitro TG as of 2025; the adopted tests remain in-vivo TG 403/436; NICEATM/OECD run case studies); (3) funding_flag: method-developer — EpiAirway's developer (MatTek) co-authored, so the concordance claim is developer-involved and not laundered into certainty.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — TEER and MTT are machine-read; no agreed cutoff exists to turn them into a classification.

  • Blocker: no classification threshold published — and, per 2026-08-22, no standardised measurement for a threshold to be written about. Dose characterisation for a volatile chemical is the named unresolved item, on two independent reviews a fortnight apart.

Ingested byeon-2026-mps-contexts-of-use-readiness (International Journal of Stem Cells, DOI 10.15283/ijsc26088, PMID 42618331, 20 August 2026; open access, Korean national research funding, funding_flag: [none]). A review grading seven organ-chip categories for regulatory readiness places lung at "early-stage" — the weakest of the seven alongside tumour and multi-organ. It credits the science (air–liquid interface culture, cyclic mechanical strain, vascularised epithelial barriers reproducing respiratory features static culture cannot) and notes rising pharmaceutical and consumer-product interest specifically for inhalation safety. What it says is missing is not biology: "public evidence remains dominated by application-specific and mechanistic studies rather than multicenter performance evaluations or formal qualification programs," and wider regulatory use "will require standardized exposure methods, dosimetry, reference substances and cross-laboratory reproducibility." Dosimetry is the one to keep — for an inhalation model the delivered dose at the tissue surface is not the applied concentration, and an unstandardised delivery method makes results incomparable between laboratories before any biology is disputed. This is a review, no experiment, nothing on this map's axes moves. → organ-chip-readiness-tracks-the-evidence-not-the-biology

  • 2026-08-22 — The blocker on this map is one layer deeper than "no threshold published." Status HELD; loop_readiness HELD at automatable-open-loop. Ingested lampe-2026-voc-respiratory-in-vitro-scoping-review (Toxicological Sciences, DOI 10.1093/toxsci/kfag110, PMID 42623132, 10 August 2026; academic, Texas A&M, funding_flag: [none]; confirmed paywalled — digest written from the complete structured abstract). A scoping review of 3,052 records screened, 144 studies included, 105 volatile organic compounds — the evaporating chemicals people actually breathe, solvents and paints and formaldehyde — tested in human lung cell models. (1) The finding is methodological, not biological: the field is "expansive but methodologically fragmented." Most studies submerge the cells in medium with the chemical dissolved in it rather than exposing them at the air-liquid interface — that is, most of the literature this map sits in does not use the arrangement this map is built on. Endpoints cluster on cytotoxicity, oxidative stress, inflammation and apoptosis; metabolism and barrier-function studies are "relatively uncommon"; "chronic or repeated exposures were rarely conducted." (2) Why that changes the blocker's description: a classification threshold is a rule applied to a number, and 144 studies producing incommensurable numbers give a rule nothing to be written about. For a volatile chemical the specific unknown is dose characterisation — what actually reaches the tissue when the compound's defining property is that it evaporates. (3) ⚠ Second independent review naming the same item within a fortnight. byeon-2026-mps-contexts-of-use-readiness (2026-08-20) graded lung the weakest of seven organ-chip categories and listed "standardized exposure methods, dosimetry, reference substances and cross-laboratory reproducibility." Different field, different country, no cross-citation, delivered dose first on both lists. (4) This is a review — no experiment, no performance number, nothing that can move a Status, and the adopted acute inhalation tests remain the in-vivo ones. → no-decision-rule-without-a-standardised-measurement

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — FDA's draft framework describes this method almost by name. regulatory-acceptance-fda-nam-framework (CDER, March 2026) gives as its worked example of "human biological relevance" for respiratory toxicity: "the model may be a tissue reconstructed from primary human lung cells that capture key mechanisms of toxicity (e.g., loss of membrane integrity) and cellular properties (e.g., beating cilia) of the in vivo human situation." That is a description of an ALI airway epithelial model, and "loss of membrane integrity" is this map's TEER readout. The Status does not move — emerging / regulatory_status: none remain correct: there is no adopted in-vitro TG (TG 403/436 remain the standard), there is still no in-vivo predictivity number, and the work is developer-involved. But the axis reading changes: none means "has no guideline," not "cannot be used regulatorily." Under CDER's fit-for-purpose route this method could support a specific context of use in a specific submission today, without a TG and without formal validation. That is a real change in what this map implies, and the vault should not keep narrating none as a closed door.

  • Technology family (not redundancy): the human-tissue ALI airway model is a technology cousin of the RhE skin/eye models and the hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps MPS — human-tissue in-vitro systems across different endpoints. A composition of approach, not overlapping assays.

  • Two-axis pattern: like hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps, this is a developer-involved, not-yet- adopted systemic-style case — validated-science-not-yet-accepted is now the vault's dominant shape, and the bias flag is load-bearing here.

  • 2026-08-24 — THE UNSTANDARDISED DELIVERY METHOD WAS NOT JUST INCOMPARABLE — IT WAS PRODUCING THE TOXICITY. Status HELD at emerging; loop_readiness HELD at automatable-open-loop; loop_blocker EXTENDED. Ingested hopkins-2026-bronchial-ali-nebulised-protease (Frontiers in Immunology 2026;17:1875147, DOI 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1875147, PMID 42630249; open access, peer reviewed, funded by Unilever with four of seven authors Unilever employees → funding_flag: [industry, method-developer]). A commercial 3D human bronchial air–liquid-interface tissue (MucilAir, Epithelix — single supplier, an access_gate) was dosed with a detergent-type protease by nebulisation rather than by pipette. (a) The headline, and it is a control result. Repeated apical application of the vehicle alone — plain saline, no test substance — reduced barrier integrity and raised the neutrophil-recruiting signal IL-8/CXCL8. Nebulising the same vehicle "produced no detectable changes in barrier integrity or inflammatory signalling." The authors name the regulatory consequence themselves: "false-positive responses arising from experimental artefacts can confound hazard identification and undermine confidence in NAM-derived data." → the-delivery-method-was-generating-the-response This is the 2026-08-20 dosimetry warning promoted from "results are incomparable between laboratories" to "the act of dosing is itself an exposure." And it is dose-shaped: more applications, more damage, so the artefact yields a clean-looking concentration–response that a curve-fitter fits and an optimiser chases. It joins the-dose-the-loop-controls-is-not-the-dose-the-cell-receives (perfused liver chip) and the-scheduler-is-an-uncontrolled-variable-the-loop-cannot-see as the third instance of the same structure: the loop's model has one variable called "dose" and the world has several. (b) The sharper finding, and it is about a readout three maps share. At the top dose the tissue was visibly degrading — inserts too damaged to image — and the standard LDH release cytotoxicity assay reported no significant cell death, because the protease had digested the LDH over the 24-hour incubation. "An important limitation of conventional cytotoxicity assays when assessing proteolytically active substances." A machine reading that plate returns a well-formed, in-range number meaning the exact opposite of the truth. → a-readout-the-test-substance-can-digest (c) The dose–response, honestly sized. Three donors, three inserts each. No barrier effect to 0.08 µg/cm²; a non-significant fall at 2 µg/cm²; a significant fall at 24 µg/cm² with reduced GRO-α, IL-8/CXCL8, IL-9 and VEGF and increased MIF. Logistic modelling puts extracellular-vesicle release as the most sensitive endpoint (median effective 0.21 µg/cm², >90% probability at 8.43), then barrier loss (≥12.97), then IL-8/CXCL8 (≥239 — roughly sixty-fold higher). The entire microRNA arm is one donor. (d) What did not move, and why. No decision rule, no guideline, no classification — this map's blocker is untouched, and the source makes it harder: the authors call their threshold "model-specific rather than a definitive human exposure limit" because aerosol deposition varies with droplet size, viscosity and chamber geometry, and each new substance needs its own deposition characterisation. The number a rule would apply to now varies with the method of application as well as with the laboratory — no-decision-rule-without-a-standardised-measurement, one layer deeper. (e) Brakes. Industry-funded and industry-authored on a chemical class the funder sells; the practical conclusion (disruption only well above occupational exposure levels) is that funder's preferred conclusion and is recorded as a claim. The liquid-vs-aerosol contrast is two protocols differing in several ways at once, and the authors decline to call it a controlled comparison. And the model has no immune cells — for an endpoint that is about immune sensitisation, only the epithelial half is present.
