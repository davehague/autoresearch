What we believe now

What it is. Human foetal neural progenitor cells are grown as free-floating spheres — "neurospheres" — and allowed to do, in a dish, the things a developing brain does: divide, migrate outward, turn into neurons and glia, and grow neurites. A chemical is added and each of those processes is measured. It is the human-cell core of the DNT in-vitro battery, and it covers more of the battery's eight key neurodevelopmental processes than any other single assay in it.

The applicability domain has been mapped from the bench, and it has named holes. Kuchovska et al. 2026 probed the assay with activator/inhibitor pairs for 18 signalling pathways implicated in neurodevelopmental disorders:

Pathways that moved at least one readout16 of 18
Pathways that moved nothing2 — STAT3 and TrkB, "outside the applicability domain"
Why STAT3 is invisibleits process, astrogliogenesis, "is not assessed in the DNT IVB"
Neuronal-migration readoutresponded to 0 of 18 probe pathways, and to 1 of 120 chemicals in the same group's prior EFSA screen

16 out of 18 is a good result and the vault records it as one. The two misses matter because of what they are: a chemical acting through STAT3 or TrkB returns a clean negative, and nothing in the negative says "this pathway is out of scope." That is the vault's first biological domain_gate — defined over mechanism rather than chemistry, so a loop cannot measure it in advance the way eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494 measures pH and phase separation before deciding to answer.

The neuronal-migration readout is the sharper finding, and it belongs to this map rather than the battery. A readout that fires for 1 chemical in 120 and 0 probe pathways in 18 is contributing very little information per plate. Whether it should be retained, retuned, or dropped is an assay-design question the source does not answer — and it is exactly the kind of decision an active-learning loop would make quickly and possibly wrongly, since a readout that almost never fires looks like overhead right up until the chemical it was built for arrives.

Four of fifteen authors — including the senior author and two of three corresponding authors — hold DNTOX GmbH equity, a company selling DNT-IVB services. The findings are limits on their own product. The flag stays; the candour is recorded.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — and this assay is the vault's clearest demonstration that automating the scoring is not the same as automating the call.

  • The scoring is already a neural network. Cellomics ArrayScan high-content imaging; two Keras convolutional neural networks identifying neurons and oligodendrocytes, "trained with historical manually analyzed data"; Omnisphero skeletonisation for neurites; CRStats for concentration-response fitting. Six of seven readouts fully automated. Radial glia migration is still traced manually in Fiji.
  • And the tier does not move, because the blocker is downstream of all of that. This is the distinction the vault drew on 2026-07-20 and re-confirmed on 2026-07-25: the human-in-the-loop maps are blocked at the scoring step, where machine vision genuinely is the escape hatch; this map is blocked after it, where no image classifier reaches. Same-looking blocker, entirely different investment.
  • ⚠ New as of 2026-07-26: the CNNs carry their own applicability domain. The European Commission's JRC names domain shift — "discrepancies in statistics between training and test data" — as the operative failure mode for a deployed deep-learning scorer, and these CNNs were "trained with historical manually analyzed data" from one laboratory's imaging. A second site with a different ArrayScan configuration is outside a domain nobody has characterised, and the assay's own acceptance criteria cannot detect it, because they test the biology. This is now a second reason the transferability work the JRC says is "ongoing" is harder than it looks. (a-trained-scorer-has-its-own-applicability-domain.)
  • ⚠ And the layers below the CNNs are choices too. nam-data-processing-pipeline (coined tonight) takes its central worked example from this battery: "variations in curve fitting models can yield different results and impact subsequent level V data", citing five biostatistical procedures that change the final call. CRStats is a level III→V pipeline, and it is a version-pinning obligation, not a settled fact.
