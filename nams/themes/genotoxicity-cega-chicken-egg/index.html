What we believe now

CEGA is a whole-embryo genotoxicity follow-up assay, like HET-MN, but reading a different tissue and a different endpoint. Chemicals are injected into the air sac of a fertilised chicken egg (not legally an animal experiment under EU law, the same regulatory carve-out HET-MN uses) and the readout is run directly in the embryo-fetal liver — genotoxicity assessed there (Comet assay / DNA-adduct formation, per the broader CEGA literature this vault has not yet ingested a primary source for) rather than in circulating blood cells.

This paper tests the assay's premise, not its performance. Thakkar et al. 2025 (thakkar-2025-cega-chicken-egg-liver-metabolism) asks a logically prior question to any validation study: does an air-sac-injected chemical actually reach the embryo-fetal liver, and does that liver actually metabolise it the way a mammalian liver would? Three independent readouts converge on yes, for benzo(a)pyrene specifically — a pro-carcinogen that is classically negative in standard in-vitro genotoxicity assays without added S9 metabolic activation, chosen because its bioactivation requirement is the textbook case:

  • Imaging: acridine orange dye reaches the fetal liver after air-sac injection (non-uniform across lobes).
  • Metabolite chemistry: LC-HRMS detects 9 liver metabolites after B(a)P dosing, 0 in controls, including a tentative assignment to BPDE, the DNA-reactive ultimate carcinogen B(a)P must be bioactivated into.
  • Gene expression: RNA-seq shows CYP1A1 (log2FC 4.85) and CYP1A2 (log2FC 3.84) induction — the AhR-pathway cytochromes that perform exactly the bioactivation step the metabolite data implies — with CYP1A1 enzyme activity itself rising at 125 and 250 μg/egg.

Why this is still a long way from a graded method. One chemical, chosen for its textbook bioactivation profile rather than to stress the assay's chemical-diversity range. No reference chemical set, no classification accuracy, no inter-laboratory data. The authors' own stated gaps: a small number of chemicals, unexplained liver-lobe exposure variability, and no inter/intra-lab validation yet. funding_flag: [industry] — RIFM-funded; the same fragrance-industry interest (via RIFM here, Cosmetics Europe/Henkel for HET-MN) runs through the whole chicken-egg-assay literature this vault has now ingested three sources from.
