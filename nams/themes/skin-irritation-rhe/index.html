Reconstructed human epidermis — skin irritation (GHS Cat 2) — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-05) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 439). A mature in-vitro replacement for the irritation hazard call; the vault's first entry for a new endpoint (skin & eye irritation/corrosion).

What we believe now

The RhE skin-irritation test applies the chemical to a 3-D reconstructed human epidermis and reads viability by MTT; viability ≤50% flags a UN GHS Category 2 irritant. Adopted as OECD TG 439, replacing the Draize rabbit test for this call. Performance-standard reference: 80% sensitivity, 70% specificity, 75% accuracy; validated me-too models (EpiDerm, SkinEthic, KeraSkin, EPiTRI) meet the same standards. Scope-bounded — resolves Cat 2 and runs inside a bottom-up/top-down IATA with corrosion tests (TG 431/430/435).

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — MTT absorbance against a single explicit cutoff: <=50% viability -> GHS Cat 2.

⚠ Qualified 2026-08-14 by a domain_gate, not a demotion. The rule is machine-readable and published in an adopted guideline, which is what rung 5 grades — so the tier holds. But the cutoff's stability was measured for the first time and it is not what the tier's name implies: across models the guideline treats as interchangeable, agreement with the reference answer runs from Cohen's kappa 0.339 to 1.000, and the source attributes the spread to the single-threshold structure itself. A loop reading 51% and a loop reading 49% on the same chemical in two licensed tissue models will emit opposite classifications with identical confidence, and neither will signal that it was close. See the domain_gate field and one-fixed-threshold-and-agreement-ranging-from-poor-to-perfect.

The vault's first case of an adopted method being built by construction from two of its own maps, and a demonstration that the RhE platform is a substrate on which new hazard endpoints get built (irritation → corrosion → phototoxicity) by varying only the exposure protocol.

  • 2026-07-22 — regulatory-move (national, hedged). The UK Replacing animals in science strategy (regulatory-acceptance-uk-animal-replacement-strategy, published 11 Nov 2025) places skin irritation in basket 1 — "mature replacement technologies already exist" — naming OECD TG 439 (this map) and noting three validated methods adhere to it, with the target: "By the end of 2026 we will aim to apply only validated alternative methods to satisfy UK regulatory requirements for skin irritation testing." Status and both other axes hold. Two things recorded rather than celebrated: it is an aim in a non-binding policy paper, not a mandate — trade coverage reporting it as "must" is wrong on the decisive word (uk-2026-targets-are-aims-not-mandates) — and basket 1 is the easy bin, containing items already effectively replaced elsewhere. Status HOLDS at consensus; loop_readiness HOLDS at closed-loop-ready; a domain_gate is SET. (1) The number. A 234-substance reference database, scored across the commercially validated RhE models this guideline treats as interchangeable: Cohen's kappa 0.339 to 1.000, accuracy 66.4–100.0%, sensitivity 80.6–100.0%, specificity 60.2–100.0%. Kappa measures agreement beyond chance — below 0.4 is poor, above 0.8 near-perfect. The band spans nearly the whole scale, between models that all passed the same validation and are all admissible under the same guideline. (2) The authors blame the rule, not the models, and that is why this belongs here rather than in a methods footnote: "variability in specificity linked to the single-threshold structure of irritation classification," with the instability located in borderline Category 2 and Category 3 substances near the line, because the method "relies on a single 50% cell viability cutoff for binary classification." It is chemical-class dependent, worst for surfactants and reactive organics, with a mechanism attached — lipid solubilization gives "progressive rather than abrupt cytotoxic responses", and a gradual dose-response crossing a hard threshold is exactly the geometry that makes a single cutoff unstable. Surfactants are also most of the household and personal-care chemistry this test exists to serve. → one-fixed-threshold-and-agreement-ranging-from-poor-to-perfect (3) Recorded as a domain_gate, on the U-SENS precedent. skin-sensitization-u-sens took a 128–176% grey zone around its adopted 150% cutoff as a domain_gate on 2026-08-08, not a demotion. Same object, different endpoint, found by a different route. The tier grades whether a fixed rule exists and is machine-readable; it does not grade whether the rule is stable, and the vault has now found four separate ways it can fail to be — fixed ≠ deterministic, fixed ≠ sample-independent, fixed ≠ transferable, and tonight fixed ≠ stable. The fourth is the simplest and the most general: it needs no Bayesian model, no batch effect and no local history, and it applies in principle to every threshold rule in the closed-loop-ready tier. (4) The controlled comparison, which is the run's sharpest single result and lives on the companion map: skin-corrosion-rhe (TG 431), same tissue technology, same reference set, same authors, same analysis, differing only in that its thresholds vary with exposure time, came out at kappa 0.622–0.720 — substantial and narrow. The guideline with the more structured rule is the more reproducible one. → the-guideline-with-the-richer-rule-is-the-more-reproducible-one (5) Brakes. Retrospective assembly of published results, not a fresh ring trial, so some of the spread is inter-study rather than inter-model variation and the paper cannot separate the two. Coverage is unequal — a kappa of 1.000 on a small subset is not a perfect model. And the reference answers are animal-derived (the precautionary hierarchy's second tier is in-vivo data), so every accuracy figure here is agreement with a rabbit test of unstated reproducibility — the standing measurement problem on validation-paradigm-nam-confidence. Ingested krzykwa-2026-botanicals-dermal-nams-mixtures (Pharmaceutical Biology 64(1):922–948, DOI 10.1080/13880209.2026.2717803; Botanical Safety Consortium Dermal Working Group under a US FDA / NIEHS Memorandum of Understanding; funding_flag: [industry]). This map already carries a domain gate about where a chemical sits relative to the 50% viability cutoff. This adds one about whether the readout is measuring viability at all: on plant-derived materials, constituents can reduce the MTT dye directly — producing the colour with no living cell involved — and pigments in the extract absorb at the measurement wavelength. Either fakes viability, and both push the same way. The guideline already handles it, which is the point worth recording: a killed-tissue control and a colorant control with corrections computed from them are written into the procedure, and solubility is not a limitation here because the material is applied neat — unlike every dilution-based assay in the dermal column. For a loop this is a mandatory control, not an optional one: an automated run that skipped the colorant control on a coloured botanical would return a wrong viability number with no signal that anything was off, and the same interference sits under skin-sensitization-keratinosens, so one artefact would corrupt two endpoints' readouts at once. No experiment has been run; nothing about performance on defined chemicals moves. → cytotoxic-masking-is-a-second-shared-failure-route-through-the-battery

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Methodological kinship (flagged): the reconstructed-human-tissue viability approach here is the same class of model used by EpiOcular in the eye defined approaches — eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da. Same technology (RhE/RhO barrier-integrity + MTT viability), adjacent endpoints (skin vs ocular local irritation).
  • Not a defined approach itself: TG 439 is a single assay that plugs into a broader IATA; contrast with the defined-approach pattern in skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach and eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da.
  • IATA composition (now mapped): composes-with skin-corrosion-rhe — TG 439 identifies GHS Cat 2 irritants among the non-corrosives; TG 431 identifies GHS Cat 1 corrosives above it. Same RhE tissue, one topical-hazard ladder. The in-chemico membrane-barrier rung is now mapped too: skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435 (TG 435 — ingested 2026-07-10; this line previously said "still to ingest" and was stale).
  • Parent-of (composes-with, structural — new 2026-07-11): this guideline's tissue + MTT readout is one of the two halves of TG 498 (phototoxicity-rhe-pt); TG 432 (phototoxicity-3t3-nru) supplies the other (irradiation). Recorded on all three maps.
  • RhE/RhCE tissue-technology family (composes-with, not redundancy — and a concentration risk): the reconstructed-human-tissue platform now underpins four adopted OECD methods in this vault — skin irritation (this map), skin corrosion (skin-corrosion-rhe), phototoxicity (phototoxicity-rhe-pt) and the cornea-like epithelium of eye-irritation-rhce-no-category. Same tetrazolium viability logic, four different hazards, distinguished by tissue and exposure protocol. A systematic flaw in reconstructed-tissue models would ripple across four adopted TGs at once — named, not modelled.
  • Global-ripple: distinct endpoint/mechanism from the skin-sensitisation maps — no confirm/refute edge, a coverage-breadth addition.
