Automated planarian behavioral high-throughput screening — neurotoxicity / developmental neurotoxicity — method

Status: emerging (2026-11-07) — evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, regulatory_status: none. Coins the vault's first whole-invertebrate, automated behavioral screening method — a different technology class from the cell-based or zebrafish-embryo NAMs already tracked on developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb and developmental-neurotoxicity-zebrafish-behavior-ldtt.

What we believe now

Adult and regenerating (decapitated, re-growing) Dugesia japonica planarians — whole organisms with a true nervous system and intact xenobiotic-metabolism machinery — are exposed to test chemicals in an automated screening platform that scores 22-26 readouts per well: lethality, a stress behavior called "stickiness," and locomotor/behavioral metrics, on exposure days 7 and 12. The platform is commercially offered (Inveritek, LLC, founded by the lab's senior author — funding_flag: [method-developer]).

Two founding sources, same lab, converge on one picture: (1) the platform can detect non-additive interactions among organophosphorus pesticide mixtures that a simple shared-mechanism additivity model does not predict, with the developing/regenerating nervous system more sensitive to non-additivity than the mature one; (2) upgrading the statistical pipeline (benchmark-concentration modeling plus 8 added behavioral readouts, replacing a lowest-observed-effect-level-only analysis) increases sensitivity to known developmental neurotoxicants on the NTP 87-compound library, and the resulting planarian benchmark-concentration values show similar sensitivity to an independent developing-zebrafish screen on the same library — a cross-species concordance result, not a shared failure.

Honest ceiling. Single academic lab (Swarthmore College) across both founding papers — one research group, not independent replication. No OECD or regulatory recognition of this species for any toxicological endpoint. The platform's commercial availability is the founder's own business, a reason to watch future throughput/cost claims skeptically, though the specific findings graded here are mechanistic and comparative rather than performance claims about the platform itself.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — every readout across both founding sources (22-26 total) is machine-scored by the automated platform; no trained human reads behavior by eye here. What is missing is a fixed, agreed decision rule: the additivity call rests on comparing data to a Loewe model by research judgment, and the developmental-neurotoxicant call rests on benchmark-concentration modeling used for sensitivity comparison, not for publishing a pass/fail threshold. No guideline exists to supply one — this is Tox21's own stated-common state (axis-3 rule 4), reached here by an invertebrate whole-organism platform rather than a cell-based one.

  • Blocker: no agreed regulatory decision rule; research-grade statistical modeling stands in its place; species has no OECD standing on any endpoint.
  • Domain gate: see frontmatter — richer biology (nervous system, metabolic competence) than the vault's acellular ecotox NAMs, but transfer beyond one 87-compound cross-species comparison is untested.
