3D reconstructed human skin Comet assay (RS Comet) — genotoxicity — method

🚩 AUTO-COINED 2026-08-02, flagged for human review. The vault's 72nd map. Coined together with its companion genotoxicity-3d-skin-micronucleus-rsmn — two methods validated by the same consortium, in the same journal issue, for the same job, which the vault holds as separate maps because they are separate methods with different readouts, different decision rules and different loop blockers. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md. Git is the undo.

Status: consolidating (2026-08-02). Multi-laboratory validation with coded chemicals (5 laboratories, 32 chemicals analysed), between-laboratory reproducibility 88%, and an independent peer-review opinion from the EU validation laboratory's scientific advisory committee (ESAC Opinion 2025-01, 24 January 2025) supporting its scientific validity. Not consensus: there is no Test Guideline and no regulatory adoption. The validation study was funded and run by Cosmetics Europe — the industry trade association — with the tissue manufacturer's employee as a co-author, so this is developer-adjacent validation independently reviewed, not an independently conducted ring trial. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method, regulatory_status: under-validation.

What we believe now

The problem this exists to solve. The standard in-vitro genotoxicity battery — the Ames bacterial test (genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation), the micronucleus test (genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus), the mammalian gene-mutation assays (genotoxicity-mammalian-gene-mutation) — has a well-documented false-positive tendency. It flags a substantial fraction of harmless chemicals. Historically, resolving one of those alarms meant a rodent study. This method is a non-animal way to do the follow-up for chemicals people put on their skin.

How it works. The comet assay measures broken DNA: cells are embedded in gel, an electric field is applied, and fragments of damaged DNA drift away from the nucleus into a comet-shaped tail. The measured quantity is % tail DNA. The innovation here is not the electrophoresis but the tissue — the assay runs inside reconstructed three-dimensional human skin grown from primary human keratinocytes and fibroblasts, with the chemical applied topically, three times over 48 hours, so it must cross a barrier the way it would on a person. A refinement adds aphidicolin, which blocks DNA repair and so amplifies the breaks produced during repair of damage caused by chemicals needing metabolic activation.

Within-laboratory reproducibility 93% (40 experiments, 5 laboratories); between-laboratory 88%.

Plainly: applying the written criteria, the assay caught about four in five real genotoxicants and wrongly flagged about three harmless chemicals in a hundred. That the criteria beat the experts on the same data is the map's most interesting single fact — see the-mechanical-rule-beat-the-experts-on-the-same-data.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — and it lands here by a route the vault has seen only once before.

  • Readout: instrumented. SYBR Gold stain, fluorescence microscopy, scored by commercial software (Comet Assay IV or CometImager), 50 cells per slide, 300 cells per compartment. Nobody counts anything. This clears the rung that blocks its companion map and three other genotoxicity maps in this vault.
  • Decision rule: exists, works, and is not portable. Three criteria — statistical significance under two prediction models, a dose–response relationship, and a result outside this laboratory's historical negative-control range (mean ± 2 SD). The third criterion is the blocker. The rule is a function of local accumulated history, so it is not a constant across sites, and a running loop changes its own reference distribution. → a-decision-rule-that-reads-this-laboratorys-own-history
  • The precedent. This is the same defect the vault recorded for TGx-DDI (genotoxicity-tgx-ddi-transcriptomic-biomarker), whose published rule is fixed but whose two unsupervised classifier legs are batch-relative. Two unrelated genotoxicity methods, twenty years apart in design, arriving at the rule is written down and still depends on what else was measured.
  • Promotion path, and it is specific. A Test Guideline publishing a portable interpretation procedure — fixed thresholds, or a defined reference-control construction that does not depend on one site's history — moves this to closed-loop-ready without any change to the wet method. That guideline project has been in the OECD work plan since January 2019 and on hold ever since.

⚠ Loop-ready is not endorsement, and here it is barely even progress. Being one criterion away from the top rung does not make this assay a good idea to automate. It carries a domain_gate (dermal exposure only, bioactivation-dependent chemicals under-called), an access_gate (single-supplier tissue), and a correlated_blind_spot with the one other method it is meant to be run alongside.

⚠ Correlated blind spot — with its own companion

correlated_blind_spot: [genotoxicity-3d-skin-micronucleus-rsmn]. The two methods read genuinely different biology — strand breaks versus chromosome-scale damage — which is what makes them look complementary. They share the tissue, and the tissue is where both fail. Both name false negatives caused by skin's metabolic enzyme complement differing from liver's: this assay on IQ (inadequate NAT activity) and 4-chloroaniline, its companion on 2-AAF and DMBA. A chemical that is harmless as drawn and dangerous only once an enzyme modifies it can pass both and be recorded as cleared twice. An automated system reads that concordance as confirmation. → two-skin-genotox-assays-that-fail-on-the-same-chemistry

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Battery position — this is a follow-up test, not a battery member. It composes downstream of genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation and genotoxicity-mammalian-gene-mutation: its job is to adjudicate a positive those tests produced. That matters for the blind-spot reading, because the metabolically competent vote in the chain happens upstream and earlier, not alongside. Whether an upstream positive protects this pair's negatives is not addressed by either validation paper and the vault does not claim it does.
  • The genotoxicity column's automation ledger is now clearer, and worse. The 2026-08-01 monthly connect named automated micronucleus scoring as the single highest-leverage fix in this column, because one eye-scored assay blocks the whole battery. Tonight adds a third eye-scored micronucleus assay (genotoxicity-3d-skin-micronucleus-rsmn, after TG 487 and genotoxicity-buccal-micronucleus-cytome) — and one assay that is not eye-scored at all and is blocked by rule portability instead. The column has two distinct repairs to fund, not one, and neither repair touches the other's maps.
  • Rule-portability is now a named blocker shape with two instances in one endpoint. This map and genotoxicity-tgx-ddi-transcriptomic-biomarker both have a published rule that is not sample-independent. That is different from the vault's 23 no-rule maps and should not be counted with them: the repair is a specification change, not a research programme.
