What we believe now

South Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) established the Korean Center for the Validation of Alternative Methods (KoCVAM) in 2009, which contributed two human-tissue models to international OECD recognition: MCTT HCE™ under Test Guideline 492 and KeraSkin™, validated under TG 439 performance standards. Korea passed the Promotion of Alternative Animal Methods (PAAM) Act in 2020. A national "Regulatory Science Innovation Roadmap" designates organoids and organ-on-chip platforms "innovative strategic technologies." Most concretely: the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment (MCEE) states a roadmap to replace more than 60% of the toxicity data required for chemical registration with alternative methods by 2030, backed by an approximately KRW 33.4 billion (~USD 24M) dedicated animal-free testing facility under construction in Incheon (completion slated 2026), intended as an evaluation/certification hub for 3D tissue models and computational prediction systems. Korea banned the sale of animal-tested cosmetics in 2016.

What makes this entry different from the vault's other four jurisdictions. None of the EU, US, UK, or Brazil maps carries a single dated, numeric replacement target stated by a named ministry. 2030 is a concrete check-back date this vault should revisit as it approaches.
