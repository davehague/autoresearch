Status: emerging (2026-08-26). evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, regulatory_status: none, no inter-laboratory work, no validation body, full text unread.

What we believe now

The gap it addresses is the interesting part and it is independent of the method. Ultraviolet light splits into UVA (315–400 nm), the bulk of what reaches the ground, and UVB (280–315 nm), a small fraction with far more energy per photon. The adopted non-animal phototoxicity tests deliver a UVA-dominated spectrum: Test Guideline 432 (the mouse-fibroblast light/dark viability test) uses 5 J/cm² UVA; Test Guideline 495 (the cell-free reactive-oxygen-species assay) and Test Guideline 498 (the human-skin confirmation tier) use a solar simulator. A molecule absorbs where it absorbs — shine UVA at a UVB-only absorber and nothing happens. The European Food Safety Authority nonetheless requires UVB assessment for such substances, and this paper's premise sentence is that "currently, no suitable method is available" for it.

The method. Retinal pigment epithelial cells — the pigmented layer behind the retina — grown from human embryonic or induced pluripotent stem cells. They were chosen for an assay-engineering reason the authors state plainly: "UVB irradiation did not significantly affect the viability of RPEs, suggesting their resistance to UVB-induced damage." A cell type that does not die of the light on its own gives a clean window in which chemical-plus-light damage is visible.

The evidence. Nine reference substances — four phototoxic UVB absorbers, five non-phototoxic — with "results consistent with in vivo tests … for all nine", and a comparative claim that the method "assessed UVB absorbers more accurately than current methods (OECD TG 432 and TG 495)." Then five agrochemicals (etoxazole, tolclofos-methyl, esfenvalerate, mandestrobin, flumioxazin) that were TG 432-negative under UVA and are UVB absorbers: all five negative again under UVB.

⚠ Read the ownership with the result. All three authors work for Sumitomo Chemical, a major agrochemical manufacturer. The company developed the method and then used it to return a clean answer on its own industry's product class. Two of the vault's scepticism rails fire at once — a developer's claim about its own method, and an interested party's claim about its own products. Neither makes the result wrong; both mean an independent laboratory has to run it before it means anything.

And the comparator is a hole the vault can name precisely. This vault already records, from OECD Guidance Document 397, that phototoxicity has no validated in-vivo method at all — the endpoint's reference standard is human clinical data. So "consistent with in vivo tests" is agreement with something whose identity and standing are unread, and that is the single most load-bearing unread fact in the source.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — the readout is cell viability under two light conditions, which is a plate-reader measurement and machine-readable. Rule 4 of the deterministic ladder fires: machine-readable readout, no fixed data-interpretation procedure in what was read. There is no published threshold, no photo-irritation-factor equivalent, no stated prediction model.

  • domain_gate: UVB absorbers. The method's entire rationale is chemicals invisible to a UVA-lit test. Nothing read establishes value for a UVA absorber, and a loop must therefore decide which lamp to use from the chemical's absorption spectrum before it can pick the assay. That is a real pre-assay decision an automated pipeline would have to make, and Test Guideline 101 (the UV-VIS absorption gate at the top of the IATA) is where the information to make it already lives.
  • ⚠ Correlated blind spot (candidate, INFERRED, explicitly not stated by the source): [phototoxicity-3t3-nru, phototoxicity-ros-assay]. This is a monolayer of cultured cells with no exogenous metabolic activation system described, which is the same configuration that makes those two assays blind to a photo-inert parent chemical with a photo-reactive metabolite. The source does not address metabolic competence and the inference is from assay format alone — it is recorded so the rail does not silently skip a new battery member, and it is marked unverified rather than asserted. If it holds, the new tier closes the spectral gap in the cascade while inheriting the metabolic one.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Battery composition (composes-with, and the reason this map exists): phototoxicity-iata is OECD Guidance Document 397, the tiered scheme running TG 101 (UV-VIS absorption gate) → TG 495 (reactive-oxygen-species screen) → TG 432 (3T3 screen) → TG 498 (human-tissue confirmation). This method proposes a parallel branch off the TG 101 gate: the absorption spectrum measured at tier 1 already tells you whether a chemical is a UVB absorber, and on this evidence that is the point at which the cascade should route to a differently-lit assay rather than continuing down a UVA-lit one. Flagged as a candidate battery revision, not adopted — the vault flags, it does not compose.
  • ⚠ Spectral blind spot across the adopted tiers (the rail, new axis): phototoxicity-3t3-nru (UVA), phototoxicity-ros-assay (solar simulator) and phototoxicity-rhe-pt (simulated sunlight) deliver UVA-dominated spectra. A UVB-only absorber clears them for the same reason, and because the IATA is a tiered cascade rather than a parallel battery, a shared gap does not weaken the answer — it ends the assessment at gate one. This is structurally identical to the metabolism gap the vault already records across the same tiers, and independent of it: the two are disjoint escape routes through the same set of gates.
  • The comparator problem (cross-cutting, and it is the endpoint's not the method's): photoallergy-photogenotoxicity-no-validated-method and this map's own source both run into the same wall — GD 397 records that phototoxicity has no validated in-vivo method, so every "agrees with in vivo" claim in this column is agreement with an unspecified standard.
