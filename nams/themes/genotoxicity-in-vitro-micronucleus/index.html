In-vitro micronucleus test (OECD TG 487) — genotoxicity — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-06) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 487). The vault's first entry for the genotoxicity / mutagenicity endpoint, previously a headline coverage gap.

What we believe now

The in-vitro mammalian cell micronucleus test applies a chemical (± S9) to cultured mammalian cells and scores micronuclei in divided interphase cells, detecting both clastogenic (acentric-fragment) and aneugenic (whole-chromosome) events in one assay. Adopted as OECD TG 487 (2010; revised July 2023), ECVAM/ESAC-endorsed. It is a core member of the standard in-vitro genotoxicity battery (with the Ames test TG 471 and gene-mutation assays) and reduces reliance on the in-vivo micronucleus test (TG 474). Scope caveats are the field's: no single genotox assay is sufficient alone, and in-vitro genotox tests carry a known false-positive tendency that follow-up resolves.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: human-in-the-loop — The vault's sharpest axis-disagreement case: an adopted OECD Test Guideline (consensus, OECD-TG-adopted) that is nonetheless un-loopable, because the readout is morphological scoring by a human. Regulatory acceptance says nothing about automatability. The escape hatch is machine vision, and the sweep watches for it — and on 2026-07-16 it landed here, harder than anywhere else in the vault.

  • Blocker: micronuclei are counted by a trained eye down a microscope.
  • Escape hatch — demonstrated, not yet adopted (2026-07-16). Wills et al. 2021 automate the scoring with imaging flow cytometry (cells photographed one at a time in flowing suspension) + a deep-learning classifier (DeepFlow). Trained on two labs, it scored an unseen third lab without reconfiguration (85% on the micronucleus-positive class) and reproduced the human benchmark dose — the number that drives the safety call. This is a rung stronger than the zebrafish demonstration (developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity): inter-laboratory and regulatory-answer-matching, not single-lab and slower-than-human. The map still does not move to loop-ready, and the reasons are recorded: the scorer is (1) not part of the guideline (regulatory_status: none for the automation itself — never inferred from the adopted assay), (2) a single peer-reviewed method with no ECVAM/ICCVAM ring trial, (3) access_gated to the Amnis ImageStream instrument and co-authored by that instrument's maker (funding_flag: [industry]), and (4) still ~25% wrong on the key micronucleus-positive class, needing ~19,000 human-labelled cells to train. A trained human remains load-bearing in the regulatory call.
  • Next promotion path. A validated automated scorer — an independent ring trial, or adoption of the automated readout into the guideline — would move this map human-in-the-loop → automatable-open-loop. As of 2026-07-17 all three of the vault's eye-scored human-in-the-loop maps carry a demonstrated escape hatch (developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity since 2026-07-15, carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay since 2026-07-17). This one remains the strongest — inter-lab and benchmark-dose-matching — but none is validated, so the promotion is still open.

  • 2026-07-06 — Maturation move: the July 2023 revision refined data interpretation/controls in the OECD genotoxicity-guideline review — a housekeeping update to an adopted method, not a new validation.

  • 2026-07-16 — loop-move (blocker reading changes; tier held): ingested Wills et al. 2021 (Arch Tox) — imaging flow cytometry + a deep-learning classifier automate the micronucleus scoring step across three labs, reproducing the human benchmark dose. Only the blocker's reading moves: "counted by eye" is now a demonstrated-solvable problem, not an architectural fact. → imaging-flow-cytometry-micronucleus-deep-learning-scoring

  • 2026-07-22 — ⚠ Rail hit, and a specificity number that stings. Ingested the TGx-DDI four-lab blinded ring trial (genotoxicity-tgx-ddi-transcriptomic-biomarker), which exists specifically to fix this assay's weakest property. It reports 86% specificity against this assay's 30–54% (or 65%) — i.e. the adopted, required chromosome-damage test wrongly flags between a third and two-thirds of harmless chemicals, and a transcriptomic alternative roughly halves that. Status holds at consensus (adoption is what sets it, and nothing was refuted), but the map now carries the number honestly rather than only in the generic "known false-positive tendency" phrasing. The pairing is half independent and half not: TGx-DDI is blind to aneugens by design, which is exactly this assay's strength — genuinely complementary — but both run in TK6 cells with rat liver S9 as their only metabolic competence, so a pro-genotoxicant requiring a route rat S9 doesn't perform reads negative in both, for the same reason. correlated_blind_spot set on both maps. → tgx-ddi-micronucleus-share-the-s9-metabolism-gap, tgx-ddi-ring-trial-performance

  • 2026-07-24 — The lymphoblastoid metabolism blind spot gains a third member, and a worse failure shape. Status and loop_readiness both HELD. population-variability-human-lcl-panel (auto-coined tonight) derives chemical-specific human variability factors in lymphoblastoid donor cell lines — the same lineage as this assay's TK6 workhorse — with no metabolic activation whatsoever. Because its output is a multiplier applied to another assay's point of departure rather than a second vote alongside it, a shared blindness to bioactivation-dependent chemistry compounds rather than merely repeating: the wrong dose gets divided by the wrong factor, in the same direction, for the same reason. correlated_blind_spot extended. Vault inference, flagged — the new source never mentions TG 487. → lcl-metabolic-gap-multiplies-rather-than-repeats

  • 2026-07-26 — The escape hatch gets a ceiling, and it is not a technical one. Tier HELD at human-in-the-loop; Status unchanged. piergiovanni-2025-imaging-based-methods-regulatory-tox — the European Commission's Joint Research Centre, the unit that hosts EURL ECVAM (the EU's method-validation reference laboratory), writing with EFSA and the German BfR — states: "AI algorithms excel at automated tasks, human expertise remains irreplaceable for confirming and interpreting the outcome and toxicologists will maintain responsibility for final interpretation and decision-making." Read it precisely: it does not say the scoring cannot be automated — the same paper documents CNN-scored assays. It says responsibility for the interpretation stays with a person. In vault terms: the readout can move, the call may not. That is an accountability constraint, not a capability one, and it does not expire when the technology improves. This map's scorer (Wills 2021) remains the vault's strongest machine-vision demonstration — inter-laboratory, benchmark-dose-matching — and this map remains the front-runner for the next promotion. But the vault has been modelling the path as build → validate → guideline adopts → tier promotes, and step three may terminate short of the tier. It also rhymes with FDA's model-risk framing (regulatory-acceptance-ai-model-credibility), where a human cross-check "would reduce the AI model influence" and therefore the evidentiary burden: two regulators, two continents, both structurally rewarding a human in the loop. Second point, same source: the scorer carries its own applicability domain, set by training-image statistics, with domain shift the named failure mode — a second gate stacked on the assay's chemical one, invisible to the assay's acceptance criteria. strength: suggestive; one position statement in one review, binding on nobody. → machine-vision-does-not-remove-the-human-from-the-regulatory-call, a-trained-scorer-has-its-own-applicability-domain

  • 2026-07-27 — The documented blind spot gets a candidate REPAIR, and the scoring blocker gets a second reading. Status HELD at consensus; loop_readiness HELD at human-in-the-loop. Ingested Allemang & Pfuhler 2026 (Environ Mol Mutagen 67(5-7):e70062, Procter & Gamble, funding_flag: [industry]), 28 chemicals run in both cell lines. (1) Swapping the standard TK6 lymphoblast line for metabolically competent human hepatic HepaRG raises overall accuracy 75% → 86%, entirely by removing false positives: 9/9 vs 3/9 correct on the known in-vitro-positive/in-vivo-negative set, with the gain attributed to "a more in vivo-like balance of phase I and II metabolism" detoxifying indirect oxidative-stress compounds that produced "66% false-positive calls in TK6." Independently corroborated inside the paper by genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa (TG 446A), which confirmed the oxidative mechanism. This is the first source in the vault that proposes a FIX for a documented correlated_blind_spot rather than adding one — the shared TK6/rat-S9 metabolic gap set on 2026-07-22 is a property of the cell, not of the endpoint. (2) ⚠ And the fix was evaluated with the failure mode excluded: "Compounds requiring metabolic activation were not selected for this study." correlated_blind_spot HELD on all three maps — credibly addressed, not yet demonstrably fixed. (3) The trade runs under-protective: 12/14 vs 14/14 sensitivity, missing ENU and o-toluidine; and cadmium chloride flipped positive-to-negative between exposure protocols. (4) ⚠ Loop — the scoring step was never done by eye here: "MN induction was measured flow cytometrically … using the Litron In Vitro MicroFlow method", >5000 nuclei per sample, no images and no classifier. The machine-vision escape hatch the vault has tracked across four maps may be the harder route to a problem a different instrument already handles — a sampling bias in the vault's own sweep, which reads the automation literature and therefore over-finds machine learning and under-finds instrumentation. Tier does not move: the guideline's call is a statistical and biological-relevance judgement, and this source establishes nothing about the flow-cytometric readout's standing inside TG 487. Logged as an open gap. (5) access_gate extended — TK6 is widely distributed, HepaRG is patented and single-licensor (Lonza), so the accuracy upgrade makes the assay harder to replicate elsewhere. (6) regulatory_status: none for the HepaRG variant: TG 487 requires non-validated cell types to be "justified based on their demonstrated performance", and this is the justification being offered, from one industrial lab with no ring trial — not an acceptance of it. → heparg-metabolism-fixes-the-micronucleus-false-positives, the-metabolic-fix-was-tested-without-the-metabolism-case, micronuclei-are-already-counted-by-machine

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Battery composition (now fully mapped on detection): TG 487 (chromosome damage) composes-with genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation (TG 471, bacterial gene mutation) and, added 2026-07-07, the mammalian gene-mutation assays genotoxicity-mammalian-gene-mutation (TG 476 Hprt/xprt + TG 490 Tk) — the complementary mutational endpoints of the core in-vitro genotoxicity battery. TG 490's Tk locus overlaps into chromosome damage, so it and this micronucleus test share a deliberate overlap, not redundancy. Contrast with the fully-mapped skin-sensitisation 2o3 hub skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach.
  • Mechanistic follow-up (composes-with, added 2026-07-07): genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa (OECD TG 446A) sits above the primary battery as an adopted MoA discriminator — it explains why a micronucleus/Ames positive scored (DNA-reactive vs indirect oxidative/protein stress) and resolves equivocal calls. Detection vs interpretation: complementary layers, not redundant.
  • Composition with carcinogenicity: the genotox battery covers genotoxic carcinogens; the non-genotoxic ones are the domain of carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay — complementary, not redundant.
  • Loop-move edge (added 2026-07-16, completed 2026-07-17): the machine-vision escape hatch that Wills et al. demonstrate here is the same pattern the vault first saw in developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity (Sivaprasad 2026, zebrafish malformation scoring) and now, as of 2026-07-17, in carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay (Masumoto 2021, a CNN scoring transformed foci). All three eye-scored human-in-the-loop maps now carry a demonstrated escape hatch — they share one blocker (a human scoring morphology) and therefore one class of fix (a validated automated scorer), an infrastructure link, not a battery-composition link. The micronucleus case is the strongest of the three: inter-lab and benchmark-dose-matching, vs zebrafish (single-lab, direct malformation scoring) and cell transformation (single-lab, foci classification only). None is validated.
  • Global-ripple: distinct endpoint/mechanism from the skin/eye local-toxicity maps and the systemic maps (DNT, hepatotox, repeat-dose) — no confirm/refute edge; a coverage-breadth addition within the genotoxicity endpoint group.
