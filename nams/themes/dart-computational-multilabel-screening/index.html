What we believe now

  • 2026-08-30 — MAP OPENED. dart-predictor-multilabel-attention-performance. Gao et al. 2025 (Environ. Sci. Technol. 59(46):24853-24862, PMID 41232073) train DART Predictor, a multi-label deep learning model using a label-aware attention mechanism, on 25,175 chemically diverse records (molecular descriptors + bioassay activity features, calibrated with exposure parameters) to predict six distinct DART outcomes at once — growth disorders, malformation, fetal viability loss, maternal systemic toxicity, maternal pathology, fertility impairment — rather than the field's usual single binary call. Average AUC 0.964 / recall 0.923 in training, falling to a still-strong AUC 0.889 / recall 0.959 on two external validation sets the model never trained on — the number that actually tests generalization. Folding in exposure parameters (dose/route/timing context, not just structure) improved AUC by up to 8.6 percentage points on some outcomes, a specific ablation-style claim rather than generic motivation. Deployed as a public web tool (ai4environ.cn/dartpredictor). No regulatory body has looked at this model; regulatory_status: none, evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method, funding_flag: [none] visible (three Chinese academic institutions; no vendor on the author list).
