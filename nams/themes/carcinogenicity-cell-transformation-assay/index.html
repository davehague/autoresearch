Cell transformation assays (SHE / Bhas 42) — carcinogenicity — method

Status: consolidating (2026-07-07) — formally validated science, not regulatorily adopted — the two-axis gap is the whole story. evidence_tier: formally-validated, regulatory_status: proposed. Coins the vault's carcinogenicity endpoint — the hardest to replace.

What we believe now

In-vitro cell transformation assays (CTA) score the morphological transformation of cultured cells into a cancer-like phenotype — a functional readout capturing both genotoxic and non-genotoxic carcinogens, the latter of which the genotox battery cannot detect. The Syrian Hamster Embryo (SHE) assay uses primary cells (~7 days); the Bhas 42 assay uses a v-Ha-ras-transfected BALB/c 3T3 line already "initiated," running as a promotion/initiation assay sensitive to tumour promoters / non-genotoxic carcinogens. The Bhas 42 validation (Hadano Research Institute, JaCVAM-supervised) was peer-reviewed by EURL-ECVAM's ESAC (Dec 2012) and fully endorsed — "standardised, transferable and reproducible between laboratories." OECD Guidance Documents exist (SHE = GD 214, Bhas 42 = GD 231), but there is no OECD Test Guideline, EURL-ECVAM's recommendation to develop one is not yet acted on, and the assays cannot be used stand-alone (weight-of-evidence / integrated testing strategy only). A residual subjectivity concern attaches to morphological-focus scoring.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: human-in-the-loop — Plate-based and otherwise automatable, but the call rests on scoring transformed foci by eye — and the guidance names the residual subjectivity as a known weakness. On 2026-07-17 this map got its first demonstrated machine-vision escape hatch — the last of the vault's three eye-scored maps to do so, and the thinnest of the three.

  • Blocker: transformed foci are scored subjectively by eye; the guidance flags the subjectivity itself.
  • Escape hatch — demonstrated, weakest of three, not adopted (2026-07-17). Masumoto et al. 2021 train an 18-layer ResNet (a convolutional neural network) to classify a colony image as a transformed focus or not — the morphological judgment the human otherwise makes. AUC 0.95 on the training chemical (the tumour promoter TPA), dropping to AUC 0.87 on two chemicals it had never seen; its real value is removing the beginner-to-expert scoring spread (novices scored 20–80% of identical images positive). The map does not move to loop-ready, and the reasons are recorded: (1) it classifies individual foci only, not the transformation call and not the carcinogenicity call — one step short of the regulatory answer that Wills 2021 reproduced for micronucleus; (2) single-lab, trained largely on one chemical, generalisation already visibly weaker → single-lab-exploratory, strength: single-study; (3) trained on the subjective expert labels it aims to standardise, so it makes the human call more consistent rather than objective; and it is unvalidated and not in the guidance. Clean on one axis the micronucleus case is not: funding_flag: none (academic/public-sector, no competing interest), against Wills' industry co-author.
  • Next promotion path. A validated automated focus scorer — an ECVAM/ICCVAM ring trial, or adoption of an automated readout into the guidance — plus a scorer that reproduces the actual carcinogenicity call, not just per-focus classification, would move this map human-in-the-loop → automatable-open-loop. As of 2026-07-17 all three of the vault's eye-scored human-in-the-loop maps (genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus, developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity, and this one) carry a demonstrated escape hatch; none is validated. This is the weakest of the three, on the hardest endpoint to replace.

  • 2026-07-26 — This map's escape hatch turns out to be the vault's only MEASURED case of the failure mode the JRC names. Tier HELD at human-in-the-loop; Status unchanged. piergiovanni-2025-imaging-based-methods-regulatory-tox identifies domain shift — "discrepancies in statistics between training and test data" — as the operative risk for a deployed deep-learning image scorer, and says a trained model needs its own applicability domain defined. Masumoto et al. 2021, this map's escape hatch, is exactly that, quantified: AUC 0.95 on the chemical it was trained on, 0.87 on unseen chemicals. The vault filed that eight-point drop as "single-lab, weaker evidence." It is more specific and more useful than that — it is the model's applicability domain being crossed, and the cost being visible only because somebody happened to test for it. None of the vault's other three machine-vision scorers has been characterised this way. Second point from the same source: the JRC states that automated scoring does not move responsibility for the call — "toxicologists will maintain responsibility for final interpretation and decision-making" — so the path from a validated scorer to a tier promotion has a non-technical ceiling on it. → a-trained-scorer-has-its-own-applicability-domain, machine-vision-does-not-remove-the-human-from-the-regulatory-call

  • 2026-07-31 — A sibling arrives on this endpoint, two loop tiers higher, with none of this map's regulatory standing. Status and tier both HELD. tumorigenicity-anchorage-independent-growth was coined tonight from a four-laboratory study of the soft agar colony formation and growth in low attachment assays for gene-edited cell therapies. The contrast is the finding and it runs both ways. This map has two OECD Guidance Documents and an EURL ECVAM/ESAC-endorsed validation; that one has no regulatory standing at all. This map is human-in-the-loop because a trained human judges the morphology of a transformed focus; that one is automatable-open-loop because a colony is counted by an imager. Same endpoint. The regulatory axis and the loop axis point in opposite directions, cleanly, between two assays measuring the same biological property. This is the sharpest instance of axis disagreement the vault holds on a single endpoint. ⚠ And a correlated_blind_spot row is added. Both measure a transformation phenotype in a cultured cell line — morphological focus formation here, anchorage-independent growth there. Neither has an immune system, a stroma, a blood supply or time. A phenotype a body would clear, and a carcinogenic mechanism needing tissue architecture, are invisible to both; in a weight-of-evidence package citing both, their agreement is close to one vote. For a loop: an automated system reading concordance between two transformation assays as corroboration compounds a shared architectural gap. Note this row is a vault inference from assay design, not a claim either paper makes.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Composes-with the genotox battery: CTAs cover the non-genotoxic carcinogens that genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation (gene mutation) and genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus (chromosome damage) cannot — complementary, not redundant. Carcinogenicity assessment is the weight-of-evidence integration of genotox + transformation + repeat-dose bioactivity.
  • Mechanistic bridge (composes-with, added 2026-07-07): genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa (OECD TG 446A) discriminates DNA-reactive vs indirect (oxidative/protein-stress) genotoxicity — the same genotoxic-vs-non-genotoxic axis this map turns on. ToxTracker helps classify the mechanism of a genotoxic carcinogen; the CTA catches the non-genotoxic ones the whole genotox battery misses.
  • Composes-with the systemic route: repeat-dose-systemic-toxicity-nam bioactivity is the indirect read on carcinogenic potential; CTA is the direct in-vitro transformation read — both sit inside the same ITS/weight-of-evidence for the hardest-to-replace endpoint.
  • Two-axis exemplar: like developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb and pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics, this is validated-but-not-TG-adopted — the vault's dominant shape. Here funding_flag: none because validation was independent-body (ESAC/JaCVAM), which weighs more than a developer self-validation.
  • Loop-move edge (added 2026-07-17) — the escape-hatch infrastructure link, now complete. This map's eye-scoring blocker is the same blocker (a human scoring morphology) shared by genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus (micronuclei counted by eye; Wills 2021, inter-lab, benchmark-dose-matching) and developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity (malformations scored by eye; Sivaprasad 2026, single-lab). As of 2026-07-17 all three carry a demonstrated machine-vision escape hatch — one blocker, one class of fix (a validated automated scorer). This is an infrastructure link, not a battery-composition link: the three assays measure different endpoints and never compose into one battery. Ranked by strength of the demonstration: micronucleus (strongest — inter-lab, reproduces the regulatory number) > zebrafish (single-lab, scores the malformation directly) > this one (weakest — single-lab, foci classification only). None is validated; whichever gets a ring trial or guideline adoption first is the vault's next likely human-in-the-loop → automatable-open-loop promotion.
