Quantitative point-of-departure DAs (OECD GL 497 Part III) — skin sensitisation — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-09) — adopted on both axes on arrival, and one week old. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD Guideline No. 497, 2 July 2026 edition, Part III / Section 4). Adoption earns consensus deterministically per the evidence model. The qualifier that travels with it is claim type, not standing: these DAs return a dose, not a hazard or potency class, and the guideline expressly forbids using them for the Part I / Part II calls.

What we believe now

Part III adds two defined approaches that convert existing key-event measurements into a quantitative point of departure (PoD) for skin-sensitisation risk assessment. This is a new claim type for the vault. Every other method here answers a classification question — is it a sensitiser, is it GHS 1A, is it a corrosive. These answer at what dose.

  • SARA-ICE (§4.1) — a Bayesian model returning an ED01 distribution (dermal dose at which 1% of an HPPT-eligible population is sensitised). Inputs: DPRA, kDPRA, KeratinoSens, h-CLAT, U-SENS, plus historical LLNA/human patch-test data. SARA database expanded from 81 to 434 chemicals (the evaluation database is static, finalised Dec 2022). Free NICEATM-hosted app.
  • Regression-based DA (§4.2) — multiple linear regressions on kDPRA + KeratinoSens + h-CLAT, trained separately on LLNA EC3 and on human weight-of-evidence potency values, taking the more conservative of the two. Includes a vapour-pressure volatility correction. Runs in a transparent spreadsheet.

Both require ≥2 information sources across ≥2 key events. Neither generates new wet biology: they are a statistical layer that extracts a dose from assays the vault already maps. Existing in-vivo data may be used as input; new in-vivo data must not be generated for them.

Read the performance carefully. Both land ~85% of estimates within 10-fold of their reference value — which is a wide band, not a classification accuracy. Only about one third land within 2-fold. What is good is the near-absence of directional bias against human reference data (SARA-ICE mean/median fold-difference 1.1; regression geometric-mean fold-difference 0.99). What is worrying, and reported openly by the guideline, is the regression DA's tail: 5.5–8.9% of chemicals have potency under-predicted by >10-fold, i.e. a PoD that is too high — the under-protective direction. A symmetric "within 10-fold" summary hides that asymmetry.

Industry origin, regulator evaluation — both recorded. funding_flag: [industry, method-developer]. SARA-ICE descends from Unilever SEAC (Reynolds/MacKay/Gilmour/Maxwell 2019), later built with NICEATM/NIEHS; the regression DA from Givaudan (Natsch & Gerberick 2022). Both were independently evaluated by the OECD EG DASS against a curated 196-chemical reference set with LLNA and human data. Developer origin plus independent regulatory evaluation is stronger than self-validation and weaker than independent origin. Neither half is dropped. The companies that originated these models are the companies that use PoDs to set ingredient concentrations.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — The most sophisticated loop-ready case in the vault: the DIP is not a threshold but a statistical model — a Bayesian ED01 distribution (SARA-ICE) and a regression — over machine-readable inputs. This is already the model updates leg of a closed loop, running in a regulatory guideline.

  • Access gate: SARA-ICE (Unilever) and the regression DA (Givaudan) are developer-origin tools — bears on whether the loop can be stood up outside the lab that built it.

  • 2026-07-10 — The sibling that completes the triad arrived. skin-sensitization-potency-its-da (Part II, potency) ingested; GL 497's three DIPs — hazard, potency, dose — are now all mapped. Status holds at consensus; nothing in Part II bears on the Part III PoD claim, and the guideline's non-composition rule ("cannot be used in Part I or Part II") is confirmed from the same archived PDF. One structural detail sharpened: kDPRA is load-bearing here and nowhere else. The regression DA needs a kinetic rate constant that DPRA's depletion percentage cannot supply, while the ITS scores plain DPRA or ADRA. The three DIPs therefore do not consume one fixed battery — they draw different, overlapping subsets, and only the dose question requires the kinetic assay.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Consumes the battery it sits above (composes-with, strongly): its inputs are skin-sensitization-dpra (KE1), skin-sensitization-keratinosens (KE2) and skin-sensitization-hclat (KE3). This is the third distinct thing GL 497 does with the same three assays — hazard (skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach), potency (skin-sensitization-potency-its-da, ingested 2026-07-10), and now dose. One battery, three regulatory questions, three data-interpretation procedures — the clearest illustration in the vault of why a defined approach is a distinct object from the assays it reads. Each DIP draws a different subset: the ITS takes KE1 + KE3 + in-silico and skips KE2; these PoD DAs take kDPRA, KeratinoSens and h-CLAT.
  • Sibling under the same guideline (does NOT compose): skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach is Part I. The guideline explicitly states Part III DAs "cannot be used in Part I or Part II unless mentioned specifically." So this is a sibling that must not be chained into a hazard call — an unusual, load-bearing non-edge, recorded because a reader would otherwise assume composability.
  • Computation-extracts-more-from-existing-assays family (composes-with): structurally these DAs are kin to in-silico-qsar-read-across and pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics — layers that add no new wet biology and instead squeeze a further regulatory answer out of measurements already made. With this map, that family now has three members and is arguably the vault's most interesting cross-cutting shape.
  • Dose-anchoring contrast (composes-with, distinct): pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics converts an in-vitro bioactive concentration to a human-equivalent systemic dose. SARA-ICE / the regression DA derive an applied dermal dose directly from key-event data, with no PBK step. Same currency (a dose), different bridge — worth reading together, not merged.
  • Risk-assessment kinship (composes-with): repeat-dose-systemic-toxicity-nam also aims at a risk-relevant call rather than a hazard class, but lands at emerging with regulatory_status: none. The contrast is instructive: a local endpoint with a mature assay battery reaches an adopted quantitative PoD; the systemic endpoint does not. Maturity of the underlying battery, not ambition, is what gates the quantitative step.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No confirm/refute edge to the genotoxicity battery, carcinogenicity CTA, immunotoxicity, hepatotoxicity, inhalation ALI, eye/skin irritation & corrosion, FET ecotox, or the DART maps. Nothing in the vault is refuted or duplicated. The new information is a claim type, not a new endpoint — the first time this vault has recorded that distinction.
