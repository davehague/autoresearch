Genomic / transcriptomic biomarker screening for surface-functionalised nanomaterials — method

Status: emerging (2026-10-25). Coined from a single academic consortium study; no independent replication, no external reference panel, single exposure scenario.

Sub-threads: none — one founding study, one argument. [reviewed 2026-10-25 @ 1 bullets]

Why this map exists

nanomaterial-nam-landscape's founding review names "single-cell multi-omics" and a generic computational layer as enabling technologies for non-animal nanomaterial risk assessment, but — like most of that review's named technology classes — without a dedicated study measuring one of them against a specific hazard question. This map is coined, the same way nanomaterial-pulmonary-3d-floating-ecm was on 2026-09-15, because a source now supplies a specific technology with a specific measured result: genome-wide transcriptomic screening of surface-functionalised silver nanoparticles at the air-liquid interface, with real-time PCR validation. Distinguishing it from the parent landscape map keeps a domain-wide survey from absorbing a technique that now has its own evidence base to track independently.

What we believe now

Genome-wide microarray profiling of an A549 (human alveolar epithelial) + THP-1-derived-macrophage co-culture, exposed at the air-liquid interface, detects a coherent type I interferon / antiviral innate immune transcriptional signature induced by bare silver nanoparticles that conventional viability and inflammation endpoints miss entirely. Surface functionalisation changes this signature's shape, not merely its size: a curcumin coating suppresses a separate stress-response/AP-1/NR4A transcription-factor axis while leaving the antiviral signature intact; a hydroxyethyl-cellulose coating does the reverse. A four-gene sentinel panel (ATF3, PLGLB1, PARP9, PARP8) recurs across all comparisons and is proposed as the basis for a future low-cost qPCR screening assay. See surface-coating-silver-nanoparticles-distinct-transcriptional-signatures for the full findings and the statistical caveats (pathway-level FDR significance, individual-gene FDR largely not reached, no coating-only control).

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — rule 4 of the deterministic ladder: the readout (microarray fold-change, qPCR ΔΔCt) is fully machine-readable, but no fixed data-interpretation procedure exists. The founding study is explicit that it is proposing a candidate biomarker panel for future validation, not a decision rule ready to apply. The authors' own stated next step — reduce the genome-wide screen to a targeted qPCR panel for routine screening — is exactly the shape of a promotion path toward closed-loop-ready this vault watches for elsewhere (a guideline or validation study publishing a fixed cutoff on the proposed sentinel genes would be the trigger), but that step has not happened yet.

⚠ Correlated blind spot check

No correlated_blind_spot recorded. This map holds one technology with no sibling assay in the same battery yet; nothing to compose against. Revisit once a second nanomaterial-specific transcriptomic or genomic method is graded.

Open gaps

  • The founding study has no coating-only (silver-free) control, so whether the signature shift is driven by coating chemistry or by altered silver-ion release kinetics cannot be distinguished from this study alone.
  • No dose-response curve — a single occupationally-relevant concentration only. Whether the signature (or the coating-dependent divergence) holds at other doses is untested.
  • Whether the proposed four-gene sentinel panel (ATF3, PLGLB1, PARP9, PARP8) generalises beyond silver nanoparticles to other engineered nanomaterial classes is not addressed by this study and is an open question for a future ingest.
