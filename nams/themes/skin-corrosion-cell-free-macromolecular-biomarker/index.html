What we believe now

DermaSafe™ is a cell-free in chemico skin-corrosion test: a test chemical is incubated 4 hours at room temperature with a proprietary panel of "specific epithelial macromolecules" (composition undisclosed) in two solvent systems, then read spectrophotometrically as optical-density reduction against untreated control. A fixed cutoff (IC > 40% → non-corrosive; ≤ 40% → corrosive) makes the hazard call, with a separate scheme assigning GHS 1A/1B/1C sub-categories from the same readout.

Compared head-to-head against Corrositex® (the sole OECD TG 435-validated reference kit, skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435) on a balanced 60-chemical panel (30 non-corrosive, 30 corrosive, spanning acids, amines, phenols, alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, esters, halides and mixtures), DermaSafe reported 98.3% accuracy / 100% sensitivity / 96.7% specificity, against Corrositex's own 78.5% / 85.4% / 70.3% on the identical panel — the first sensitivity/specificity numbers this vault holds for Corrositex at all, and they come from a competitor's paper, not an independent audit. One false positive occurred (a skin irritant scored corrosive). All repeats were within one lab; within-lab repeatability is not the inter-laboratory reproducibility this vault's ladder requires to move past single-lab-exploratory.

Every author is a Lebrun Labs LLC employee with a patent-pending interest in the kit; the study was funded by a US NIEHS SBIR grant awarded to the same company. The comparison design (a real head-to-head panel against a named adopted comparator) is more rigorous than a bare proof-of-concept, and that is recorded alongside the bias flag rather than instead of it.
