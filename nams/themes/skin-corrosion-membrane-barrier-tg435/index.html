In-vitro membrane barrier test (OECD TG 435) — skin corrosion — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-10) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 435, adopted 19 July 2006). Unusual in this vault for being a chemistry method rather than a biological one, and for being — on a strict reading — the most complete animal replacement in the skin-corrosion IATA.

What we believe now

An artificial membrane "designed to respond to corrosive substances in a manner similar to animal skin in situ" sits above a chemical detection fluid (e.g. a cresol-red pH-indicator system). The measured quantity is the breakthrough time — how long the chemical takes to penetrate the barrier and trip the indicator. Because the GHS corrosion sub-categories are themselves defined by exposure duration, a time readout maps onto them directly:

GHS sub-categoryBreakthrough timeIn-vivo exposure / observation
1A< 3 minutes≤ 1 hour
1B> 3 minutes, < 1 hour≤ 14 days
1C> 1 hour, < 4 hours≤ 14 days

TG 435 is the only adopted in-vitro method that separates 1B from 1C. This corrects a claim the vault has been carrying. Read against the archived TG 431 PDF, TG 431's own text states that "a limitation of this Test Guideline is that it does not allow discriminating between skin corrosive Sub-category 1B and Sub-category 1C" — it discriminates 1A versus 1B-and-1C versus NC. TG 430 (TER) gives a bare Cat-1 call with no sub-categorisation at all. vault/index.md said TG 431 "sub-categorises 1A/1B/1C"; it does not, and both have been corrected.

The applicability wall is the trade. A chemical must first pass a compatibility/qualification step and, for strong acids and bases, a timescale categorisation test. The guideline states plainly that "some corrosive chemicals and chemical mixtures may not qualify for testing" and that "aqueous substances with a pH in the range of 4.5 to 8.5 often do not qualify for testing" — then gives the statistic that makes this workable: 85% of chemicals tested in that pH range were non-corrosive in animal tests. So the excluded domain is mostly one where the answer would have been "not corrosive." Mostly. A corrosive near-neutral chemical is invisible to this method, and the reassurance is a population statistic, not a property of the chemical in front of you. No sensitivity/specificity figures appear in the 2006 text.

A 3Rs distinction worth holding. TG 435's membrane is entirely synthetic — no cells, no tissue, no animal-derived material; hence no donor variability, no culture drift, no species question. TG 431 uses reconstructed human epidermis. TG 430 still uses excised rat skin — a reduction, not a full replacement. Ordered by animal dependence, TG 435 sits furthest from the animal.

funding_flag: method-developer — the one validated reference test method under TG 435 is Corrositex®, a single commercial, proprietary kit. The guideline's performance-standards framing is what keeps it from being a vendor specification; in practice Corrositex® is TG 435.

Edition caveat. The archived bytes are the 2006 adoption text (mirrored by the US NTP/ICCVAM document server). The current OECD edition is 2015. Nothing above has been re-read against the 2015 revision. Recorded as drift, not assumed away.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — A synthetic membrane and a chemical detection system: breakthrough time in minutes maps onto the duration-defined GHS sub-categories. No biological variability at all.

  • Domain gate: a compatibility step screens out much of pH 4.5-8.5 — a loop must check the applicability domain before trusting the decision rule.
  • Access gate: Corrositex(R) is the sole validated reference kit — bears on whether the loop can be stood up outside the lab that built it.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • IATA composition — parallel options, NOT a cascade (composes-with): skin-corrosion-rhe (TG 431, RhE + MTT viability) and this map (TG 435, synthetic membrane + breakthrough time) are parallel stand-alone options in the skin corrosion/irritation IATA, alongside TG 430 (TER, excised rat skin — not yet mapped; logged as an open gap, deliberately as plain text). Each resolves a different slice of the same GHS call: TG 430 → Cat 1 or not; TG 431 → 1A vs 1B-and-1C vs NC; TG 435 → full 1A / 1B / 1C, over a narrower domain. They are complementary in resolution, not tiered in sequence. This is a different battery shape from the skin-sensitisation DAs, where a fixed DIP combines assays into one call.
  • The irritation tier below (composes-with): skin-irritation-rhe (TG 439) sits under the corrosion methods on the same GHS ladder — a negative corrosion call routes a chemical into the irritation assay. TG 435 strengthens the top of that ladder without touching TG 439's own performance.
  • Adopted-but-commercially-gated (structural pattern, third instance): Corrositex® is one vendor's kit, as Derek Nexus is a license-gated leg of skin-sensitization-potency-its-da and HepaRG™ is a patented line under metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg. Three adopted OECD methods with unimpeachable scientific standing and bounded practical accessibility. The vault's two axes do not capture this; naming it.
  • The replacement line, made precise (a distinction, not a link): TG 435 uses no animal-derived material at all; TG 430 uses excised rat tissue; and immunotoxicity-myelotoxicity-cfu-gm's prediction model requires a prior mouse in-vivo study. All three are scientifically sound. Only the last one was thereby barred from ever becoming a replacement Test Guideline — the dependence that matters is on an experiment, not a material.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No edge to the eye DA (a different tissue and a different GHS ladder, though eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da shares the "each method resolves a different slice of the call" shape), skin sensitisation, the genotox battery, carcinogenicity, hepatotox, metabolism, endocrine, DART, inhalation, or FET ecotox. No dose bridge: corrosion is a physico-chemical hazard call, so unlike every in-vitro potency method here it needs nothing from pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics. Nothing in the vault is duplicated; one claim in the vault is refuted and corrected (TG 431's sub-categorisation reach).
