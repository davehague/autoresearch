NAM framework — repeat-dose systemic toxicity — method

Status: emerging (2026-07-06) — early frontier, honest limits. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method but strength: single-study (12-chemical proof-of-concept, no formal concordance), and regulatory_status: none (no adoption pipeline). The vault's first entry for the repeat-dose / systemic toxicity endpoint — the hardest for NAMs and a headline coverage gap.

What we believe now

No single NAM reproduces a whole-organism repeat-dose study, so this framework composes three streams into a tiered concern call: (Q)SAR in-silico flags, ToxCast in-vitro bioactivity (potency–severity matrix from AC50 tiers), and PBK plasma-Cmax bioavailability tiers (httk / PK-Sim / GastroPlus). Chemicals sort into Low / Medium / High concern. Demonstrated on 12 chemicals with no formal concordance; the NAM calls trended toward lower concern than conventional in-vivo categories (cutoffs had to be tuned). Acknowledged gaps: missing assays (methemoglobinemia, cardiotoxicity), parent-vs-metabolite uncertainty, expert-judgement dependence. Direction of travel, not a decision tool. The framework is industry-originated (an ECETOC Task Force) — funding_flag: [industry], and load-bearing, because the lower-concern trend and the cutoff-tuning that would resolve it are proposed by the regulated party.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — A composite of QSAR + ToxCast HTS + PBK — all machine-readable inputs — but the Low/Med/High concern cutoffs were tuned by hand and the framework admits its expert-judgement dependence.

  • Blocker: concern cutoffs were hand-tuned; the framework names expert-judgement dependence as an open gap
  • Added 2026-07-31 — the endpoint now has a published regulatory waiver procedure, and it has the same shape. EFSA's tiered strategy for skipping the required 90-day dog study names its inputs (in-vitro comparative metabolism, in-vitro human Caco-2 permeability, in-vivo rat ADME, a PBK model of internal exposure) and its branches — but EFSA calls it a "tiered, weight-of-evidence (WoE)-based approach", and no branch carries a number. How dissimilar a metabolite profile must be, and how large the compensating extra uncertainty factor should be, are left "depending on the available data and context of the assessment." The most specific published waiver procedure in the vault, still with an unquantified judgement at every branch point.

  • 2026-07-31 — regulatory-move + limits. Status HELD at emerging; loop_readiness HELD at automatable-open-loop; the blocker's wording is amended. Ingested efsa-2026-waiving-dog-studies-agrochemicals — an adopted Scientific Opinion of the EFSA Panel on Plant Protection Products and their Residues, on whether the 90-day dog study required by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 283/2013 can be waived. This is the endpoint's regulatory story, and it moves without moving this framework. (1) The animal reference standard was measured, on the record, at scale. Of 461 approved active substances, the dog drove a health-based guidance value in 288 cases (107 = 23% for the consumer limit, 183 = 40% for the operator limit). Re-examined, 97% (consumer) and 95% (operator) of those apparent dog-specific sensitivities were experimental and biological variability, dose spacing, study-design differences or body-size scaling — most of them inside a threefold NOAEL ratio, i.e. ordinary scatter. EFSA's own conclusion: "selecting two species for toxicity testing does not necessarily reduce uncertainty regarding potential human health risks." This is a measured noise floor for the reference standard every method in this vault is scored against. (2) A regulator published the shape of a procedure for deleting an animal study — two preliminary phases plus a flowchart, consuming in-vitro comparative metabolism, in-vitro human Caco-2 permeability, in-vivo rat ADME and PBK modelling of internal exposure. Tested on four substances; three reached medium-to-high confidence. regulatory_status for the strategy: proposed — it is a proposal inside an adopted opinion, not in the Regulation. (3) ⚠ The model layer is given a job and denied another one, in writing. PBK modelling may decide whether a species is informative; it may not set the number — "and not for the derivation of HBGVs. The application of standard regulatory uncertainty factors remains unchanged." (4) Reduction, not replacement. The substitute for the dog is the rat plus modelling. Same line the vault has drawn on CFU-GM myelotoxicity, the eye IATA's two slaughterhouse-tissue methods and the developmental-neurotoxicity battery's first worked case. → a-regulator-published-the-procedure-for-deleting-an-animal-study, the-second-species-was-mostly-measuring-experimental-noise

  • 2026-08-06 — ⚠️ A THIRD LITERATURE ARRIVES AT THE SAME NEGATIVE, AND IT IS A CLINICAL ONE. Status HELD at emerging; loop_readiness HELD at automatable-open-loop; nothing moves. Ingested starr-2026-nams-cannot-replace-animals-critical-care (Journal of Leukocyte Biology, advance article qiag108, PMID 42547063 — seven critical-care and immunology investigators). (1) The claim, verbatim: non-animal methods "excel at interrogating isolated subsystems" but "cannot currently replicate integrated physiological responses" — naming "dynamic organ–organ interactions, immune–microbiome crosstalk, and adaptive systemic feedback loops" as the properties that defeat them — so animal models "remain essential for questions requiring assessment of multi-organ dysfunction, therapeutic safety evaluation, and clinically relevant disease trajectories." (2) It is a direct rebuttal of this map's own framework, and the back-edge is written: repeat-dose-nam-tiered-classification composes three subsystem-level streams — structure-based prediction, robotic in-vitro screening, and a kinetic model — into a whole-body concern class, which is precisely the move Starr et al. say does not work. The tension is partly a scope difference and must not be over-read: their subject is sepsis and trauma pathophysiology, this framework's is chemical hazard screening, and a screening-tier concern call is a much weaker claim than reproducing a disease trajectory. Neither side supplies a number, so the disagreement is unresolvable from the evidence in this vault. (3) ⚠ The conflict of interest runs the opposite way from this vault's usual bias watch and is graded the same. These are investigators whose research programmes run on animal models of sepsis, haemorrhage and burn injury, arguing in an immunology journal against US FDA/NIH policy that would defund them. Recorded, not laundered. The counterweight: their claim is about what non-animal methods currently cannot do, which is checkable against this vault's coverage map, and it checks out. (4) Three literatures now, one conclusion about this endpoint class. The framework's own authors name expert-judgement dependence as an open gap (2026-07-06); a cosmetics NGRA review found "no apparent validated NGRA frameworks for systemic toxicity endpoints" (2026-08-05); a clinical field says the integrated response cannot currently be replicated at all. Corroboration of a negative from three directions — and none of the three measures anything. (5) Honest ceiling. evidence_tier: in-silico-mechanistic, strength: suggestive — no concordance figure, no failed-substitution case series, no benchmark in the accessible record. Full text GATED (OUP served HTML, not a PDF, at the browser rung), so whether the paper engages with multi-organ microphysiological systems is unread, and that is the crux of its own argument. → a-clinical-field-says-the-whole-organism-question-has-no-non-animal-answer

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — weight-of-evidence is not this map's weakness; FDA says it is the mode. regulatory-acceptance-fda-nam-framework (CDER draft, March 2026) states that when a NAM is used "it is always in the context of the weight of evidence (WOE)" — and that a fit-for-purpose NAM "even if not validated, may adequately address specific toxicological concerns." This map's method is a weight-of-evidence framework composing (Q)SAR + ToxCast + PBK into a Low/Med/High concern call, and the vault has been filing that composite, unvalidated character as the reason it sits at emerging / none. The Status does not move — a 12-chemical proof-of-concept with no formal concordance is genuinely emerging, and the funding_flag: [industry] (an ECETOC Task Force output) is genuinely a bias-watch item. But the framing was wrong in one respect: a tiered WoE concern call is not a lesser kind of NAM awaiting promotion to a real one. On CDER's account it is the native form in which NAMs enter regulatory decisions. What this map still lacks is not the right shape — it is a performance number.
  • Toxicokinetic anchor (composes-with): its PBK/bioavailability layer is exactly the pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics method — a concrete instance of PBK as the connective tissue for a systemic-tox call.
  • Organ-specific building block (composes-with): organ-specific injury models like the hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps Liver-Chip feed the bioactivity/severity side of a repeat-dose picture — related but not redundant (DILI-specific vs whole-organism concern).
  • IATA archetype: a tiered integrate-multiple-streams approach, kin to the defined-approach pattern of skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach — but far less mature (exploratory vs adopted).
  • Carcinogenicity route (composes-with): repeat-dose bioactivity is the indirect read on carcinogenic potential — it feeds the weight-of-evidence alongside the direct in-vitro transformation read of carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay (added 2026-07-07).
  • Global-ripple: no confirm/refute edge with the local-endpoint or genotoxicity maps — a coverage-breadth addition opening the systemic endpoint.
  • Reduction and replacement arrived on the same endpoint the same night (added 2026-07-31). transcriptomic-point-of-departure proposes a potency number from human cells that could in principle stand in for a chronic animal study; EFSA proposes deleting the second animal species and keeping the rat. Neither is the other, and a reader who conflates them will overstate how much of this endpoint is being replaced. Recorded together deliberately.
  • The reference-noise edge (added 2026-07-31) — this is a global ripple, not a local one. EFSA's finding that a threefold NOAEL disagreement between two whole-animal studies is ordinary variability applies to every map in this vault whose performance number is scored against a single animal study. It confirms from the regulatory record what validation-paradigm-nam-confidence holds as a framework principle (nam-variance-judged-against-animal-test-variance) and what eye-irritation-rhce-no-category showed with rabbit-data variability in 2026-07-15.
