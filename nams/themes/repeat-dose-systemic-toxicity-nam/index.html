NAM framework — repeat-dose systemic toxicity — method

Status: emerging (2026-07-06) — early frontier, honest limits. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method but strength: single-study (12-chemical proof-of-concept, no formal concordance), and regulatory_status: none (no adoption pipeline). The vault's first entry for the repeat-dose / systemic toxicity endpoint — the hardest for NAMs and a headline coverage gap.

What we believe now

No single NAM reproduces a whole-organism repeat-dose study, so this framework composes three streams into a tiered concern call: (Q)SAR in-silico flags, ToxCast in-vitro bioactivity (potency–severity matrix from AC50 tiers), and PBK plasma-Cmax bioavailability tiers (httk / PK-Sim / GastroPlus). Chemicals sort into Low / Medium / High concern. Demonstrated on 12 chemicals with no formal concordance; the NAM calls trended toward lower concern than conventional in-vivo categories (cutoffs had to be tuned). Acknowledged gaps: missing assays (methemoglobinemia, cardiotoxicity), parent-vs-metabolite uncertainty, expert-judgement dependence. Direction of travel, not a decision tool. The framework is industry-originated (an ECETOC Task Force) — funding_flag: [industry], and load-bearing, because the lower-concern trend and the cutoff-tuning that would resolve it are proposed by the regulated party.

This map's method is a weight-of-evidence framework composing (Q)SAR + ToxCast + PBK into a Low/Med/High concern call, and the vault has been filing that composite, unvalidated character as the reason it sits at emerging / none. The Status does not move — a 12-chemical proof-of-concept with no formal concordance is genuinely emerging, and the funding_flag: [industry] (an ECETOC Task Force output) is genuinely a bias-watch item. But the framing was wrong in one respect: a tiered WoE concern call is not a lesser kind of NAM awaiting promotion to a real one. On CDER's account it is the native form in which NAMs enter regulatory decisions. What this map still lacks is not the right shape — it is a performance number.
