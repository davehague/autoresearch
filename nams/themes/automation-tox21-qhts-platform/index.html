Status: consolidating (2026-07-13). evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method, regulatory_status: none. A production system, publicly funded, running continuously since 2013, whose ~50 million public data points are used across EPA and FDA programmes — but the platform itself has no Test Guideline and is not a regulatory method. It generates data; it does not make calls. Not consensus, because nothing has adopted it as a method; not emerging, because it is thirteen years old and the largest thing of its kind on earth.

What we believe now

The robot half of the closed loop was finished in 2013, and the humans never left.

A Stäubli robotic arm surrounded by compound carousels, climate-controlled incubators, acoustic dispensers and plate readers runs the Tox21 10K library — ~10,000 environmental chemicals — through a 15-point concentration–response curve, in triplicate (three separate days, varied well positions), in 1536-well plates, every week, with "hands-free assay completion."

That is quantitative high-throughput screening (qHTS): not a single-dose hit/no-hit screen, but a full dose–response for every chemical in every assay, fitted to a four-parameter Hill equation → AC50 (the concentration producing half the maximal effect) and efficacy.

And then a person opens the ambiguous plots.

"Class 5 curves and other cases … are manually inspected to correct the curve class, if necessary, normally by masking or unmasking data points."

Masking a point changes the fit; the fit sets the AC50; the AC50 is the answer. The human is not supervising the decision layer — the human is in it. And beyond the curve, there is no published procedure that turns a Tox21 AC50 into "this chemical is a hazard." That conversion is a research judgement, made case by case.

This map exists because the vault has been asserting all of this without a source. Since the third axis was invented on 2026-07-12, shared/evidence-model.md has defined automatable-open-loop as "runs robotically at scale, but interpretation still needs a human, or no agreed decision rule exists — this is Tox21's state." Twelve maps were graded against a programme the vault had never ingested. The primary document now backs it, and says something sharper than the assertion did.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — by the deterministic ladder in shared/evidence-model.md:

  • Readout? Fully instrument-readable — luminescence and fluorescence off a plate reader, no human eye.
  • Decision rule? No. No fixed data-interpretation procedure maps the output to a hazard call, and the automated curve-classification itself is manually corrected. → rung 4, automatable-open-loop.
  • Inputs? No in-vivo study is consumed. Not bench-bound.

The blocker is the decision rule, and Tox21 is the proof at the largest scale the field has. Thirteen years of the most automated toxicology on earth did not close the loop, because automating the wet work does not automate the epistemology. Every one of the vault's 12 automatable-open-loop maps is a small version of this.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Grounds the third axis for the whole vault. Every map carrying loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop inherits its definition from a claim about Tox21 that had no citation until tonight. The twelve: endocrine-er-binding-hrer, developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb, endocrine-ar-transactivation, endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation, genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation, genotoxicity-mammalian-gene-mutation, genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa, immunotoxicity-il2-luc, hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps, inhalation-airway-epithelium-ali, fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet, repeat-dose-systemic-toxicity-nam. No status moves — this is a provenance repair, not new evidence about any assay.
  • ⚠️ The counter-design for the blind-spot rail, found in production. The rail says two assays failing the same way cast one vote. Tox21's answer to its own panel-wide failure mode (cytotoxicity mimicking activity) is not a concordance vote across correlated assays — it is an orthogonal measurement in the same well. Read that against the vault's three recorded blind spots: FET ↔ RTgill-W1 (fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet / ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line — adding a third fish assay adds nothing; adding metabolic activation adds everything), ROS ↔ 3T3 NRU (phototoxicity-ros-assay / phototoxicity-3t3-nru), and the MMTV promoter trio (endocrine-er-transactivation / endocrine-ar-transactivation / endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation). The remedy for a correlated blind spot is a measurement that fails differently, not a bigger battery. That is the sharpest actionable design rule the connect layer has produced, and Tox21 has been running it for thirteen years.
  • Composes-with the DNT battery — the highest-leverage automation gap in the vault. developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb is 17 plate-based assays integrated by expert IATA judgement rather than a fixed rule. It is Tox21's exact pathology (loop-shaped wet work, missing interpretation layer) at endpoint scale. The two together make the case that the field's dominant blocker is a decision-rule gap, and that it is cheap to fix relative to any wet-lab science.
  • Composes-with in-silico-qsar-read-across and repeat-dose-systemic-toxicity-nam. Tox21 and ToxCast data are the training corpus those computational methods consume — which is why the paper's insistence that "the accuracy of activity assignment is of paramount importance" (because the output is a training set, not a hit list) matters far beyond this map. A false positive here is inherited by every model trained downstream.
  • ⚠️ Converges with tonight's other two loop sources. automation-self-driving-labs (Hartung 2025) says the bottleneck to closing the loop is data and interpretation, not robotics — arrived at from the AI literature. This map says the same thing from a 2013 robotics paper. And regulatory-acceptance-ai-model-credibility says the regulator prices the interpretation layer: the more the machine decides alone, the more evidence it owes. Three independent sources, one conclusion: the arm was never the problem.
  • ⚠️ This platform is one HALF of the loop; Robin is the other (added 2026-07-15). Tox21 automated the hands — a robot arm runs the assay at scale — and left the mind manual (a human still edits the ambiguous curves). Ghareeb 2026's Robin (robin-semi-autonomous-mind-not-hands, under automation-self-driving-labs) is the mirror image: it automated the mind (an agent system generates every hypothesis, plan and analysis) and left the hands manual (humans run the experiments). The two demonstrated half-loops sit at opposite ends, and neither is joined to a regulatory tox endpoint with the decision rule inside the loop — which is exactly the missing wiring the vault's central question names. Tox21's specific gap (no decision rule over its AC50s) is the precise thing a Robin-style reasoning layer would supply; nobody has bolted them together on a safety assay.
  • Global-ripple: walked every map. This platform confirms and grounds the third axis; it refutes nothing and duplicates nothing. It is infrastructure, not a method, so its edges run to the axis and to the training-data layer rather than to any endpoint — the same shape of ripple as regulatory-acceptance-fda-nam-framework, and part of why both are flagged convention departures.

Added 2026-07-19 — the first source that builds the missing half on top of this platform

No status moves, no tier moves — this platform is exactly what it was. But deep-learning-dart-prediction-recall-gap (Ahn et al. 2026) is the first source in the vault that takes ToxCast data — the EPA's sibling high-throughput screening programme to this one, the same "automated hands, manual mind" architecture — and attempts to supply, computationally, the interpretation layer this map records as never having been built.

Structurally that is the closed loop the vault exists to look for: robotic screening generates the data (automatable-open-loop), a model turns it into a call (computational-connective), and the missing decision rule gets learned instead of legislated.

On the endpoint it was tried on, the interpretation layer came out worse than answering "toxic" every time. Against ECVAM ReProTect reference chemicals it scored F1 0.68 with recall 0.57, on a set 83% positive where an always-positive baseline scores ≈0.91 (vault-computed; the paper reports no baseline).

Read as a measurement, not a refutation. It says nothing about whether a decision layer can be learned in general — it says that the endpoint chosen was among the hardest in the vault (reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams, no-nam-exists), and that pointing a model at mechanism-screen bioactivity does not manufacture an apical answer. This map's thirteen-year-old finding survives intact: automating the wet work did not automate the epistemology, and automating the epistemology is not free either.
