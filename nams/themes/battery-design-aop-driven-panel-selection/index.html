Why this map exists

The vault holds nine batteries — the skin-sensitisation 2-out-of-3 defined approach, the eye-hazard integrated approach, the developmental-neurotoxicity in-vitro battery, the cholestasis and steatosis liver batteries, the kidney adverse-outcome-pathway battery, the seizure-liability battery, the phototoxicity integrated approach, the safety-pharmacology core battery — and for every one of them the vault can say what is in it.

For none of them can the vault say how the membership was decided. The composition arrived as a fact: a committee, a validation programme, a historical accident, a guideline. stages/07-connect has asked the question from the outside every month — which methods stack into a defined approach, which are redundant, which battery clears end to end — and has had nowhere to put the answer to "why these assays and not others?"

The vault has bumped into the question three times from three different maps and never had a home for it: an-aop-network-used-as-a-battery-design-tool (two liver/kidney batteries both chose their components from a published pathway network), an-aop-network-is-the-surface-a-decision-rule-gets-written-on (a thyroid pathway network proposed as the framework a decision rule would be written on), and oecd-names-conditional-independence-as-a-battery-design-rule (adopted OECD guidance instructing assessors to consider whether two combined methods are conditionally independent — the vault's own rail, already written down by a regulator).

This map is that home. And it opens with a finding that makes the question urgent rather than academic: the most obvious systematic answer selects for the assays that agree with each other.

What we believe now

There is now a stated, repeatable, machine-executable procedure for composing a screening panel. Curate the public adverse-outcome-pathway collection, map the survivors onto clusters of chemically-triggerable human disease, count which key events recur, and build assays for the top of the list. Stevens, Muncke and Escher ran it: 487 pathways curated to 266, mapped onto six disease clusters of 25–84 pathways each, producing a proposed panel of 15 multiplexed high-throughput cell-based assays.

And the procedure's own output is the vault's correlated-blind-spot rail, arrived at forwards. The three key events that recur most across clusters are:

Key eventIn plain termsClusters
Cytotoxicitythe cell dies6 of 6
Oxidative stressdamaging reactive chemistry builds up inside the cell5 of 6
Mitochondrial dysfunctionthe cell's power supply fails4 of 6

These are not mechanisms so much as final common paths. They recur everywhere because they are what a cell does when almost anything serious goes wrong — which is exactly why assays reading them will tend to fire together, on the same compounds, for the same reason. Frequency and independence are opposite selection criteria, and the adopted guidance already names independence as the one that matters.

Nothing has been tested. No panel was built, no chemical run, no sensitivity or specificity exists. This is a proposal with a counting exercise behind it.

The authors know their corpus is incomplete and say so inside their own method — they bolt on the separate "key characteristics of toxicants" framework specifically "to ensure coverage beyond current AOP limitations." A frequency count returns zero for an endpoint nobody has written a pathway for, and zero reads as "no such mechanism," not as "nobody looked."

Loop readiness

not-applicable on the ladder — there is no assay to grade. In prose, and this is the point of the map:

  • The procedure itself is loopable. Curate a database, count, rank, select. A machine could execute it and re-execute it nightly as the database grows. It is the first machine-executable answer the vault has seen to the front-of-loop question what goes on the plate?
  • Which is precisely the risk. A human reading three concordant generic-stress assays may ask why they agree. A loop reads concordance as confirmation, trusts the decision rule, and iterates — fast, and with growing confidence — in the direction the shared gap points. Automation converts a known bias into a compounding one, and here the bias is installed by the selection rule before any chemical is touched.
  • The completeness check on the procedure is currently a human. The authors' own fix for their corpus's holes is a second, expert-assembled framework. That is a judgement step at the front of the loop, matching the judgement step risk-assessment-ngra records at the back of it.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚠ The rail, and it is why this map was coined rather than filed as a bullet elsewhere. automation-tox21-qhts-platform already carries correlated_blind_spot: every cell-based reporter assay on the panel — cytotoxicity mimics activity. Tox21 answers it not with cross-assay concordance but with a viability control multiplexed into the same well. A panel selected by key-event frequency walks into that confound by construction, and the vault should carry the Tox21 answer as the known mitigation rather than re-deriving it. See tox21-cytotoxicity-multiplexing-shared-failure-mode and cytotoxic-masking-is-a-second-shared-failure-route-through-the-battery.
  • The adopted counter-criterion already exists. eye-hazard-iata-gd263 holds OECD guidance instructing that "the conditional independence of these test methods should be considered." A frequency ranking does not compute conditional independence, and nothing in the abstract says the authors did. Recorded as a tension to carry.
  • Composes-with the batteries whose membership this could explain retrospectively: hepatotoxicity-cholestasis-battery and nephrotoxicity-ciptec-aop-battery both chose components from published pathway networks and are the vault's existing instances of the same method (an-aop-network-used-as-a-battery-design-tool); endocrine-thyroid-disruption-nams proposes a pathway network as the surface an integration rule would be written on. No source has yet applied this map's frequency procedure to any of them.
  • Composes-with mixture-toxicity-in-vitro-nams — the proposed panel is explicitly for "single chemicals or chemical mixtures", and that map records that no framework for mixture assessment exists anywhere. A panel is not a framework; the composition question and the interpretation question are different, and only the first is addressed here.
  • ⚠ Composes-with risk-assessment-ngra, and the pairing is tonight's headline. This map holds the front of the pipeline (which assays go on the plate) and that map holds the back (how their results become a decision). Tonight both were shown to terminate in expert judgement: the panel's completeness check is a hand-assembled framework, and the integration step is "resolved through expert judgement." The assays in the middle are the only part anyone has automated.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No edge to the skin, eye, sensitisation, genotoxicity, phototoxicity, developmental-neurotoxicity, cardiotoxicity, inhalation or ecotoxicity columns — those hold batteries whose membership is fixed by guideline, and this map holds a method for choosing membership that no guideline uses. No access_gate, no domain_gate: there is no assay and no applicability domain.
