In-vitro cytokine release assay (CRS hazard, plate-bound mAb) — immunotoxicity — method

🚩 AUTO-COINED 2026-08-28 — flagged for human review. A conventional method map on an existing endpoint, coined on one source, one company laboratory, one positive-control antibody, and it is the vault's second auto-coined method map in two runs on the immunotoxicity endpoint (after immunotoxicity-human-pbmc-model on 2026-08-26, itself flagged in an open backlog item about coining rate). The case for coining rather than folding it into that map: they share a cell preparation and nothing else. That map is a research screen for chemical immunosuppression with no regulatory standing; this is a de facto regulatory requirement for antibody drugs with no Test Guideline — a different question, a different regulator, a different failure mode, and the vault has no other example of a NAM that a regulator expects and nobody has specified. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md. Git is the undo.

Status: emerging (2026-08-28). One source, evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method (a competent methodological comparison, published, from an industrial laboratory), regulatory_status: proposed recorded as schema strain — see below. No prediction model, no validation exercise, no ring trial, no reference antibody set, full text unread.

What we believe now

Why the assay exists, and it is one clinical event. In 2006 six healthy volunteers in a first-in-human trial received TGN1412, an antibody engineered to switch T cells on. It had looked safe in animals. Within hours they were in intensive care with cytokine release syndrome — the surge of immune signalling proteins that causes fever, collapsing blood pressure and organ failure. The source's opening sentence: "Following the incidence of life-threatening cytokine release syndrome (CRS) in healthy volunteers in the 2006 first-time-in-human (FTIH) trial of a CD28 mAb (TGN1412), there is now a regulatory expectation to investigate immuno-stimulatory mAbs for CRS hazard using in vitro cytokine release assays (CRAs) prior to FTIH trials."

The method. Human peripheral blood mononuclear cells — the immune cells from a healthy donor's blood — are exposed to the candidate antibody and the cytokines they release are measured. The dominant format immobilises the antibody on the bottom of the well first, because that mimics an antibody held in place by tissue rather than floating free.

⚠ And there is no specification. No Test Guideline, no ICH guideline naming a method, no validated reference protocol. The source: "methodology is not standardized, and differences in methods used throughout the pharmaceutical / biotechnology industry could influence assay outcomes." Every company runs its own version.

⚠ The measured consequence, which is why this map is worth having. GSK held cells, antibody and readout fixed and varied only the microtitre plate:

PlateWhat happenedDirection of the error
Costar polystyrene"superior assay performance" — the recommendation—
Falcon polystyrene"reduced cytokine induction, and lower apparent potency, reflecting slower association of mAbs"the drug looks safer than it is
Polypropylene"higher background stimulation of PBMCs"signal compressed against noise
Flat vs round bottom (Costar)"essentially no difference"— (rules out geometry)

Costar plates saturate at OKT3 ≥ 10 µg/mL (2 µg/well); above that, added antibody presents no additional surface, so any dose-response through that region measures nothing.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — by the deterministic ladder. Not rule 1 (a physical assay), not rule 2 (no in-vivo input), not rule 3 (nothing is scored by eye — cytokines are read on plate-based immunoassays). Rule 4: machine-readable readout, no fixed data-interpretation procedure.

  • The blocker is one step earlier than usual, and the vault should not flatten it. The ordinary case is administrative: everyone agrees on the measurement, nobody wrote the cut-off (OECD Test Guideline 493). Here the measurement procedure itself is unwritten, so two laboratories' numbers are not comparable before a threshold can even be argued about. This is the third distinct shape the phrase "no agreed decision rule" has taken in this vault, after the administrative case and immunotoxicity-human-pbmc-model's no agreed sign — and an open backlog item (2026-08-26) already says the schema cannot tell them apart. This is a third instance of that item, not a new one.
  • ⚠ The plate finding is the vault's first DIRECTLY MEASURED shared-pre-analytical-parameter effect. The standing generalisation — a closed loop inherits every fixed pre-analytical parameter of the protocol it automates, and fixed parameters never appear in a result table — has been carried since 2026-08-26 on three inferred instances (the phototoxicity column's shared lamp, ambient oxygen across the in-vitro column, SENS-IS tissue thickness). Somebody has now varied the parameter and measured the swing.
  • The failure has all three properties that defeat an automated system at once. It is silent (the curve looks fine), perfectly reproducible (same plate, same attenuated answer, every run — and a second laboratory buying the same plate would reproduce it, which in a ring trial reads as agreement), and directional toward under-protection. A loop cannot detect a bias constant across all of its own experiments; it has no contrast to measure against.
  • access_gate set (frontmatter): the fix offered for under-specification is a catalogue number.

Note on regulatory_status: proposed. All four values on that axis assert the existence of a document, and here the requirement is real while the document is missing. none would read as "no regulator cares," which is false; proposed is the least-wrong value and is recorded as strain, not as a resolution. See a-regulatory-expectation-with-no-standardised-protocol.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚠ Correlated blind spot, set at coining — and it is a cell-preparation blind spot, not a chemistry one. This assay and immunotoxicity-human-pbmc-model both read primary human peripheral blood mononuclear cells in short-term culture. Neither contains a lymph node, a germinal centre, or the days-long T-cell-licenses-B-cell handoff that produces an antibody. immunotoxicity-il2-luc (OECD Test Guideline 444A) is worse off in the same direction — an immortalised T-cell line reading a cytokine promoter. Any battery assembled from these three casts one vote about "can immune cells in a tube be provoked," not three votes about immunotoxicity, and an automated system taking their concordance as confirmation would compound that rather than catch it. Recorded on all three maps.
  • Distinguished from its nearest sibling, deliberately. immunotoxicity-human-pbmc-model asks whether a chemical suppresses immune function (its blocker: the readouts have no agreed sign, since proliferation rose while cytokine secretion fell). This map asks whether a drug over-activates it (its blocker: no specified protocol). Same cells, opposite hazard direction, different blockers. Both are automatable-open-loop for different reasons and the vault should not let the shared tier imply a shared problem.
  • Composes-with (evidence class, not battery): immunotoxicity-myelotoxicity-cfu-gm is the vault's other immunotoxicity method with strong evidence and no adoption — but it is bench-bound for a different reason entirely (animal-input: its scaling model consumes a prior mouse maximum tolerated dose). Three immunotoxicity methods, three distinct reasons a loop cannot close: no protocol, no sign, an animal in the maths. That is a useful column-level summary and it is not redundancy.
  • Cross-cutting to the pre-analytical rail: this is the same object as ambient-oxygen-is-an-unexamined-shared-condition-across-the-in-vitro-column and the phototoxicity column's shared lamp (the-tiers-agreed-because-they-used-the-same-lamp) — with the difference that those were inferred from protocol text and this was measured by varying it.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. Genuine edges are the three immunotoxicity maps above and the pre-analytical rail. One near-edge named and refused: nam-reagent-provenance-animal-derived-components tracks animal-derived reagents in nominally non-animal assays; a microtitre plate is not a reagent and the finding here is about surface chemistry, not provenance. A second refused: regulatory-acceptance-fda-nam-framework concerns how a regulator evaluates a submitted method; this is a case of a regulator expecting one without evaluating any, which is adjacent and not the same.
