3T3 NRU phototoxicity test (OECD TG 432) — phototoxicity — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-11) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 432; originally adopted 2004, current edition 18 June 2019). Adoption earns consensus deterministically. Coins the phototoxicity endpoint in this vault, together with phototoxicity-rhe-pt and the hub phototoxicity-iata.

What we believe now

The method is a single comparison. BALB/c 3T3 mouse fibroblast monolayers get eight concentrations of the chemical on two plates; one is irradiated with 5 J/cm² UVA, the other held dark; viability is read by Neutral Red Uptake. The endpoint is the shift between the two curves — how much more cytotoxic the chemical becomes in light. Nothing else.

The prediction model is three-way, on either of two metrics:

PredictionPIF (= IC50 dark / IC50 irradiated)MPE (weighted average photo effect)
No phototoxicity< 2or < 0.1
Equivocal> 2 and < 5or > 0.1 and < 0.15
Phototoxicity> 5or > 0.15

We record no sensitivity/specificity for this method — the guideline text carries none, asserting only that the test "was shown to be predictive of acute phototoxic effects." That is a hole in what the vault can say, and it is left as a hole rather than filled from a secondary source.

Its role, per the OECD's own IATA (phototoxicity-iata), is the sensitive screening tier: a bare monolayer has no stratum corneum, so nothing limits how much chemical reaches the cell, and the assay over-flags by construction. That is a feature at the tier it occupies — you run it to rule out — and it is why a positive is adjudicated by the barrier-bearing human-tissue method above it.

Scope, per the guideline against itself: acute photo-irritation only. It "does not [address] other adverse effects that may arise from combined action of a chemical and light" — photogenotoxicity, photoallergy, photocarcinogenicity are all out. No metabolic activation. Unlike TG 498, its positive speaks to topical and systemic exposure (a chemical distributed to skin/eyes).

One thing to say plainly in a vault about non-animal methods: the test system is BALB/c 3T3 clone A31, an immortalised mouse fibroblast line. No animal is dosed, so it is a non-animal method in the sense that counts regulatorily — but it sits on the animal-material side of the line this vault drew on 2026-07-10 (TG 430's excised rat skin is adopted; the CFU-GM assay, which consumes a live mouse's MTD, never was). funding_flag: none — no proprietary kit, no sponsor, cells from a recognised depository. Along with ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line, one of the vault's very few adopted methods with neither a commercial test system nor an industry funder.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — NRU absorbance against PIF/MPE thresholds. But see the blind spot — it shares the ROS assay's lack of biotransformation, so the two are not independent votes.

  • Correlated blind spot: shares a failure mode with [phototoxicity-ros-assay]. In a battery these are one vote, not two — and an automated loop trusting the decision rule will compound the error rather than catch it.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-11 — Ingested OECD TG 432 (current edition 18 June 2019). Opens the phototoxicity endpoint as the screening tier. An adopted light/dark cytotoxicity-ratio assay (PIF/MPE), honest about its narrow scope, with no predictivity figure in the guideline → adopted on both axes → consensus, with the missing performance number recorded as missing. → 3t3-nru-phototoxicity-screen

  • 2026-07-26 — An adopted Test Guideline was being run wrong by an experienced laboratory, and only a multi-lab study caught it. Status and tier unchanged. jacobs-2024-ring-trials-reproducibility-crisis records that during validation work on this assay, an experienced laboratory "had adapted the SOP and was adding a culture-enhancing treatment to their culture plates" — an undeclared local modification to an OECD-adopted method. It was detected, in the authors' words, only because "transferability and reliability activities … were conducted in several laboratories, rather than just one." Nothing about being adopted protects a method from silent local drift, and nothing in a single laboratory's own data reveals it — the modification improved that lab's cultures, so its internal quality metrics would have looked fine or better. The loop consequence is the uncomfortable one: a closed loop is one laboratory running very fast, with one protocol and one set of local habits, and it has no second site against which such a drift could ever show up. → eight-methods-that-broke-when-a-second-lab-ran-them

  • 2026-08-20 — Solubility named as the binding constraint for a whole substance class. Status HELD at consensus; loop_readiness HELD at closed-loop-ready; the existing correlated_blind_spot row with the reactive-oxygen-species assay is unchanged. Ingested krzykwa-2026-botanicals-dermal-nams-mixtures (Pharmaceutical Biology 64(1):922–948, DOI 10.1080/13880209.2026.2717803; Botanical Safety Consortium Dermal Working Group under a US FDA / NIEHS Memorandum of Understanding; funding_flag: [industry]). This assay is dilution-based, so for poorly soluble plant-derived materials solubility is the limiting factor rather than any biological property. The consortium's honest read: useful comparisons between the irradiated and non-irradiated arms "can still be made for test compounds with limited solubility up to the maximum tested concentration" — i.e. a positive still means something and a negative does not, because you cannot rule out an effect at concentrations you could not reach. Solvent options named beyond the usual buffered salt solutions, dimethyl sulfoxide and ethanol. The consortium's stated escape route is phototoxicity-rhe-pt (Test Guideline 498), which "may overcome solubility limitations." For a loop the consequence is asymmetric and should be encoded as such: a negative from this assay on a poorly soluble mixture is a weaker result than a positive, and the decision rule does not distinguish them. No experiment has been run. → the-two-out-of-three-rule-was-built-for-one-chemical-at-a-time

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — the tier immediately above this one is now mapped. phototoxicity-ros-assay (OECD TG 495, adopted 2019) is the IATA's tier 2 in-chemico ROS photoreactivity screen; this map (TG 432) is tier 3, the first cellular tier. A chemical reaches TG 432 only if it clears the TG 101 MEC gate and reads photoreactive in TG 495. Two consequences worth stating: (1) this map's over-flagging is partly upstream-corrected — TG 495's 100% NPV means the chemicals arriving here are already enriched for genuine photoreactivity, so 3T3 NRU is not screening the whole chemical universe; (2) TG 495 supplies the predictivity number this map lacks. This map records, as a gap, that TG 432's guideline contains no sensitivity/specificity figure. TG 495's guideline does (100% / 42–82%, NPV 100%), and it is the tier whose job is most similar. That does not fill this map's gap — a figure for TG 495 is not a figure for TG 432 — but it does mean the IATA as a whole is no longer unquantified at the screening tiers.
  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — shared blind spot with its neighbouring tier: neither has metabolic competence. TG 495 "has not been designed to address indirect mechanisms of phototoxicity, such as effects of metabolites." BALB/c 3T3 fibroblasts have little biotransformation capacity either. So tiers 2 and 3 of the phototoxicity IATA fail on the same chemical class for the same reason — a photo-inert parent with a photo-reactive metabolite. In a tiered cascade this is more serious than in a parallel battery, because the tiers are sequential: one shared blind spot lets the chemical through the entire IATA. Same structural failure the connect layer found between fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet and ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line in the aquatic column. Flagged for a human.
  • Battery composition (composes-with, by the OECD's own guidance): TG 432 is tier 3 of the phototoxicity IATA, phototoxicity-iata (OECD GD 397) — beneath the physico-chemical MEC gate (TG 101) and the in-chemico ROS assay (TG 495), both still to ingest (open gaps, plain text), and above the topical confirmation tier phototoxicity-rhe-pt. This is a genuine tiered IATA, not a fixed-rule DA like the skin-sens 2-out-of-3.
  • Parent method (composes-with, structural): TG 498 is built by combining this guideline's irradiation procedures with the tissue and MTT readout of TG 439 (skin-irritation-rhe). TG 432 is therefore literally one of TG 498's two parents — the first time the vault holds a method that is the ancestor of another map by the guideline's own construction.
  • Screen-versus-confirm pair (redundancy? no — complementary by design): TG 432 and phototoxicity-rhe-pt read the same biology (light-dependent cytotoxicity) with the same logic (irradiated vs dark viability). They are not redundant, because the discriminator is the barrier: the 3T3 monolayer measures intrinsic photo-reactivity of the chemical against a cell, the RhE tissue measures whether enough of it gets through skin to matter. The pairing is the vault's cleanest example of two assays that overlap almost completely in mechanism yet are not substitutes.
  • Non-animal-status boundary (cross-cutting, structural): the mouse-fibroblast test system links this map to the vault's live question about what "non-animal" means — the animal-material vs animal-experiment line separating adopted TG 430 (excised rat skin) from the never-adopted immunotoxicity-myelotoxicity-cfu-gm (consumes an in-vivo mouse MTD). TG 432 is squarely on the adopted side and shows the line is about dependence on a live animal experiment, not about cellular provenance.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No edge to the sensitisation battery, the corrosion tier, the genotox battery, DART, immunotox, hepatotox, metabolism, inhalation, ecotox, repeat-dose or the in-silico hub. Phototoxicity is a genuinely new column; its only real edges are the three above. Nothing in the vault is refuted or duplicated. One near-edge worth naming and refusing: the genotox battery has no irradiation arm, so it does not cover photogenotoxicity — see photoallergy-photogenotoxicity-no-validated-method. Adjacency is not applicability.
