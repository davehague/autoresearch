What this is, and why it is not a duplicate of an existing map

Every immunotoxicity map this vault already holds — immunotoxicity-myelotoxicity-cfu-gm (CFU-GM), immunotoxicity-cytokine-release-assay-crs, immunotoxicity-il2-luc (OECD TG 444A) — grades whether a chemical or drug harms or dysregulates immune function. This endpoint asks the mirror question, specific to gene therapies, cell-based treatments and gene-editing products: does the patient's immune system mount an antigen-specific adaptive response against the therapy itself — anti-drug antibodies, T-cell responses against a transgene product or viral delivery vehicle, pre-existing immunity from prior natural exposure to a common vector backbone, or sensitization that blocks a second dose. developmental-immunotoxicity-nam-landscape is the vault's other endpoint-level immune-system coverage gap, but at a different life stage (prenatal immune development) and a different question entirely — the two must not be merged.
