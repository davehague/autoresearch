No single assay resolves developmental neurotoxicity, so the field standardised a battery of 17 in-vitro assays (7 human-cell-based, the remainder rat-based) spanning 8 key neurodevelopmental processes: proliferation, apoptosis, migration, neuronal differentiation, neurite outgrowth, neurite maturation and synaptogenesis, glial differentiation, and neural network formation. Assays were built at the US EPA, the Leibniz Research Institute for Environmental Medicine (IUF) and the University of Konstanz (UKN). Results are integrated within an IATA to flag DNT potential, intended to reduce or replace the in-vivo DNT study (OECD TG 426). OECD's WNT approved document 377 describing the battery (April 2023) and issued Initial Recommendations (June 2023).

⚠️ Coverage figure corrected 2026-07-20. This map said 9 key neurodevelopmental processes from 2026-07-06, taken from the 2023 Initial Recommendations. The OECD Secretariat's own 2026 account tabulates 8. Corrected; the discrepancy is recorded rather than reconciled, because the vault cannot tell from the outside whether a process was merged, dropped, or counted differently.

It is used constantly, at scale, and can conclude nothing on its own. Roughly 650 chemicals have gone through it — >200 at EFSA, ~200 at NIEHS/DTT, ~250 at the US EPA including ~160 PFAS — with harmonised concentration–response modelling and centralised databases. The disposition of every one: "These applications did not treat DNT-IVB outcomes as stand-alone replacements for guideline studies, but rather as complementary evidence streams informing regulatory judgement."

The limitations, from the OECD's own list: incomplete process coverage ("astrocyte maturation, myelination, and the peripheral nervous system"), "limited metabolic competence," no placenta or blood–brain barrier, no behavioural outcomes, few chemicals with robust human DNT reference data, and "reliance on expert judgement." The QIVIVE proof-of-principle gap confirmed by EFSA in 2025 remains open — no published comparison of DNT-IVB-derived oral-equivalent doses against in-vivo DNT reference values.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — held since 2026-07-06 and, as of 2026-07-20, held for a much better-evidenced reason than it was assigned for.

The vault graded this tier on the assumption that plate-based assays are machine-readable and only interpretation is missing. Both halves are now sourced, and the first half is further along than assumed: on the Neurosphere Assay, imaging runs on a Cellomics ArrayScan, cells are identified by "two convolutional neural networks based on the Keras architecture," neurites by a skeletonisation algorithm in Omnisphero, and curves by a scripted R/Shiny tool (CRStats). Six of seven readouts run end-to-end without a human; the exception is radial glia migration at 72 h, "analyzed manually with the Fiji ImageJ software."

And the blocker is untouched by all of it, because it sits downstream of every automated step. This is the vault's sharpest instance of the Tox21 lesson: automating the measurement, and even the scoring, does not automate the judgement.

  • Blocker: no fixed data-interpretation procedure. OECD names the remedy — "The aspiration is to increase standardization and pave the way towards the development of Defined Approaches for DNT" — and attaches no date. Calibrate against metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg: ECVAM-validated 2014, adopted 2026.
  • domain_gate, and it is a new kind. Every other domain gate in the vault is chemical and therefore measurable — TG 435's pH window, TG 249's log KOW bounds, TG 494's runnable pre-test. This one is biological: the assay is blind to STAT3- and TrkB-mediated mechanisms, the battery omits astrogliogenesis entirely, and neuronal migration fires for almost nothing. A loop cannot measure which pathway a chemical acts through before deciding whether to trust the answer — that is what the assay was run to find out. The vault celebrated TG 494 on 2026-07-19 for a gate a machine can execute; this is the opposite pole.
  • Throughput reality check: ~2 months and ~€20,000 per chemical (developers' internal estimate). automatable-open-loop describes architecture, not speed. Tox21 does 10,000 chemicals a week.

(1) What the battery was actually used for. Since 2026-07-20 this map has carried the OECD Secretariat's summary of every one of the ~650 chemicals run through the battery — "not … stand-alone replacements for guideline studies, but rather … complementary evidence streams informing regulatory judgement" — with no worked example behind it. This is the example. An older rat study of acibenzolar-S-methyl produced equivocal brain morphometry findings; the battery was run to decide whether to believe them. Same line the vault drew on the CFU-GM myelotoxicity assay and on the eye IATA's two slaughterhouse-tissue methods. (2) ⚠ THE LOOP FINDING, AND IT LOCATES THE BLOCKER PRECISELY. Trace the decision path: seventeen mostly-automated assays returned numbers; one was non-zero; a physiologically based pharmacokinetic model was consulted and said that concentration is unreachable; a person decided the gap was big enough. No assay's decision rule appears anywhere in that chain. This map's blocker has read "integrated by expert IATA judgement" since 2026-07-20 — true and abstract. The concrete shape is that the final step consumes a second model and then an unquantified sufficiency judgement, and no published procedure states how large the in-vitro-to-in-vivo margin must be. Automating the wet work does not reach it (Tox21, 2013); automating the scoring does not reach it either (six of seven readouts already CNN-scored here, tier unmoved); automating the interpretation would require the loop to carry pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics in its inner loop and then apply a rule nobody has written. First case in the vault where that model layer is load-bearing in an individual chemical's call rather than a bridge in principle. ⚠ And the uncomfortable direction: were the margin rule written down, this becomes automatable — and the automated version would dismiss positive results on the strength of a model whose own uncertainty is not in the rule, confidently, fast, with every error pointing the same way. A fully computational integration pipeline — benchmark-concentration curve fitting, Pareto multi-objective optimisation, ToxPi weighted-slice scoring — turns the 17 assay outputs into a ranked priority list (fungicides, drugs and insecticides highest). Combined with the six-of-seven CNN-scored readouts recorded on 2026-07-20, the chain from plate to number is now automated along its entire length. And the output is a ranking, not a hazard classification. No published threshold converts a ToxPi score or Pareto rank into a call, and no reference-chemical performance figure exists that would let anyone choose one. The blocker is unchanged and is now specifiable in one sentence — see developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-data-integration. (3) pushes-back: the ranking is not chemical-set-invariant. "Selectivity varies significantly by assay and chemical class with neurite outgrowth assays and fungicides exhibiting the highest percentage of selective hits." A selective hit fires below the concentration that simply kills cells; a non-selective one just says the chemical is poisonous. If the selective-hit fraction moves with chemical class, a chemical's rank depends on what it was ranked against — the arithmetic cousin of the correlated-blind-spot rail, and the pipeline carries no correction. ⚠ For a loop: an automated system iterating on this ranking would concentrate its next experiments on the classes the battery happens to see best, and read the resulting high hit rate as confirmation it was looking in the right place. Added 2026-07-20

  • ⚠️ The contrast with GL 497 is now the cleanest teaching case in the vault, and it is not about assay quality. skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach is closed-loop-ready on three assays. This battery is automatable-open-loop on seventeen, with more automation, more chemicals run, and better imaging. The entire difference is that somebody wrote GL 497's rule down — the guideline calls its procedure "rule-based… requiring no expert judgement," and the OECD's own 2026 account uses that same rule-based/expert-judgement distinction to place DNT on the far side of it. More assays, more data and more automation do not add up to a decision rule; a committee writing one paragraph does.

  • ⚠️ A THIRD, different reading of the correlated-blind-spot rail — and this time the source calls it a virtue. Kuchovska reports that PI3K-AKT inhibition and mTOR activation both reduce oligodendrocyte differentiation, and reads the convergence as "support[ing] the robustness and physiological relevance of this endpoint as a convergent readout of pathway disruption." Under the rail, two mechanistically linked perturbations landing on one readout is not two votes. Both readings are defensible and they are about different questions: convergence is reassuring for detection (the readout catches disruption arriving by more than one route) and misleading for confirmation (agreement between them is not independent corroboration). The rail's previous applications were to batteries (FET ↔ RTgill-W1) and once to a body of evidence (the two micronucleus scorers, 2026-07-18); this is its first application to two pathways converging on one readout inside a single assay. Flagged as a vault inference — the paper contains no discussion of convergence as a false-confidence risk, and its framing is the opposite of the vault's.

  • ⚠️ The domain gate here is the anti-TG-494, and the pair is worth holding together. On 2026-07-19 the vault celebrated eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494 for the first domain_gate composed entirely of measurements — pH, phase separation, absorbance shift — so a loop can execute it rather than be told it. This map sets the opposite extreme: a gate defined over mechanism (STAT3, TrkB, astrogliogenesis), which a loop cannot evaluate without already knowing the answer. Domain gates are not one thing, and the vault should stop treating them as a single modifier. Between a gate you can measure and a gate you can only be told lies most of the difference between an assay a machine can be trusted to run and one it cannot — and neither evidence_tier, regulatory_status nor loop_readiness records which kind you have. Schema question logged for a human in docs/open-gaps-log.md.
