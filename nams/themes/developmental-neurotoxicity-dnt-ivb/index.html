DNT in-vitro battery (DNT IVB) — developmental neurotoxicity — method

Status: consolidating (2026-07-07) — split axes, held after a depth check. evidence_tier: formally-validated (multi-lab, multi-party standardised battery with a defined process-coverage domain) but regulatory_status: under-validation (OECD-recognised guidance document, 2023 — not yet a Test Guideline). A second, higher-authority source (EFSA PPR Panel, 2025) arrived and we asked whether the Status should move up; it does not — EFSA confirms the proof-of-principle gap is still open. The vault's first entry for the developmental & reproductive toxicity (DART) endpoint.

What we believe now

No single assay resolves developmental neurotoxicity, so the field standardised a battery of 17 in-vitro assays (13 human-cell-based, 4 rat-based) spanning 9 key neurodevelopmental processes: neuroprogenitor proliferation and apoptosis, migration (four cell types), differentiation to neurons and glia, neurite outgrowth and maturation, synaptogenesis, and neural network formation. Assay results are integrated within an IATA to flag DNT potential, intended to replace/reduce the in-vivo DNT study (OECD TG 426). OECD's WNT approved the battery (April 2023) and issued Initial Recommendations (June 2023, updated Nov 2023). The honest limitation carried forward — and now confirmed by an EFSA panel (2025): no published proof-of-principle comparing DNT-IVB-derived oral-equivalent doses to in-vivo DNT reference values, and it is guidance, not an adopted TG. The QIVIVE bridge that would close it is defined but not yet demonstrated for DNT.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — Seventeen plate-based assays — the most automatable battery in the vault by volume — and the guidance explicitly integrates them by expert judgement rather than a fixed rule. This is the single highest-leverage decision-rule gap in the vault: the wet work is already loop-shaped, and only the interpretation layer is missing.

This is a Scientific Opinion, not a TG. Net: consolidating held — the exact gap keeping DNT-IVB out of a stand-alone regulatory call is now EFSA-sourced, not merely inferred. → qivive-dnt-ivb-proof-of-principle-gap

  • Battery / IATA archetype (composes-with, methodological kinship): the DNT IVB is a defined battery read within an IATA, the same integrate-multiple-assays pattern as the skin-sensitisation skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach and the eye eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da defined approaches — different endpoint, same architecture.   • Toxicokinetic anchor (composes-with): requires pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics for QIVIVE to make in-vitro potency human-relevant — the OECD QIVIVE principles (2025) use this battery as the example.
