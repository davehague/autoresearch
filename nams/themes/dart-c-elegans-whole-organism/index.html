What we believe now

DART — developmental and reproductive toxicity — asks two questions: does a chemical harm an embryo, and does it harm the ability to produce one. The vault has methods for the first (zebrafish teratogenicity, the developmental-neurotoxicity battery) and nothing validated for the second: fertility is recorded on reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams as bench-bound with bench_bound_reason: no-nam-exists.

vivoDART immobilises Caenorhabditis elegans — a 1 mm soil nematode, the workhorse organism of developmental genetics — inside a microfluidic chip and photographs it at high resolution. Up to 960 worms per device (24 wells × 40 trapping channels); one run produces ~11,000 images and ~325 GB in under 30 minutes.

Six endpoints. Until 2026-08-22 the split between how they were read was the whole story of this map; that split has now closed, and the table is kept in both states because the closing is the finding:

The numbers. Across five independent experiments run on different days by different scientists, the coefficient of variation — how far repeat measurements scatter around their own average, as a percentage, lower being steadier — came out at 1.2–7.8% for the developmental endpoints and 5.2–17.0% for the embryo endpoints. The nearest regulatory yardstick, OECD Test Guideline 222 (the adopted earthworm reproduction test), accepts up to 30%. Three replicate experiments detect a >23% change; five replicates tighten that to 11% on the noisiest endpoint.

Two reference chemicals. Half-maximal effect concentrations (EC₅₀ — lower means more potent):

ChemicalMost sensitive developmental endpointMost sensitive reproductive endpoint
Methylmercurybody volume, 4.61 µMlate-stage embryos, 1.49 µM
Propiconazole (fungicide)body volume, 336 µMlate-stage embryos, 78 µM

The reproductive readout fires first, consistently. For propiconazole, late-stage embryo loss appeared at less than half the concentration that moved total embryo number, and far below anything lethal. That is the result that matters scientifically: the assay is detecting a specific reproductive effect rather than general poisoning — the standard failure of whole-organism screens that only score death.

What this is not. It is not evidence that fertility can be assessed without animals. It is a proof-of-concept in a hermaphroditic worm, from the company that sells the chip, on two well-known positives.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — promoted 2026-08-22 from human-in-the-loop. By the deterministic ladder in shared/evidence-model.md:

  • Readout? Machine-read, all six endpoints. Body dimensions were already automated (a convolutional encoder with a Vision Transformer bottleneck, <5 minutes). As of tonight the three reproductive endpoints — total, early-stage and late-stage in-utero embryo counts — are read by EmbryoMAE-Det, a masked-autoencoder vision model that finds each embryo and classifies its stage. Mean average precision 88.74% (92.79% early-stage, 84.70% late-stage) on held-out data; trained on the 47,852 hand-annotated embryos that were the July map's blocker.
  • Decision rule? Reached, and refused. The downstream computation is mechanical — four-parameter logistic curve fits producing EC₁₀/EC₂₅/EC₅₀ with confidence intervals — but nothing converts an EC₅₀ into a hazard classification. There is no OECD Test Guideline for C. elegans at all, so there is no agreed data-interpretation procedure to automate. Rung 4, not rung 5. This is exactly what the July map predicted would happen if the readout were automated, written down before it was.
  • Inputs? No in-vivo mammalian study consumed. Not bench-bound — and that distinction still matters, because the fertility map next door is bench-bound for a different reason entirely.

Did it agree with the human? That is the question that decides whether this is an automation or a substitution. Six chemical-endpoint pairs across propiconazole and methylmercury, EC₅₀ compared by F-test against the hand-scored curves: not significantly different in any of them (p = 0.77–0.99). And it is steadier than the person — coefficient of variation 4.5% / 10.4% / 2.9% against manual scoring roughly 5.5 percentage points higher on the noisiest endpoint, which converts into detecting an 18.3% change where manual scoring needed 23%. → automated-scoring-beat-the-human-on-variance-not-just-speed

The escape hatch closed two-thirds of the way. In July this map recorded a working machine step the reader could not obtain, the second such instance in four days — cardiotoxicity-hipsc-cm-proarrhythmia carries a neural-network classifier available only "from the corresponding author upon reasonable request." The vault's ruling was that an unpublished classifier does not move a tier. Tonight the classifier is published, so the tier moves. It is still not obtainable: no weights, no code, no data-availability statement, on top of a single-vendor chip and imager. Demonstrated and distributable are different properties and only the first one changed.

Loop-readiness is not endorsement, and here the caution runs in both directions. It is still a hermaphroditic nematode with no placenta and no vertebrate endocrine axis, tested against two known positives and no negatives — and it is now a fast, steady, automated measurement of that. Automating a measurement does nothing about whether the model organism answers the question.

⚠️ Correlated blind spot — no placenta anywhere in the DART column

All three of the vault's whole-organism developmental/reproductive screens lack a placenta, a maternal-fetal circulation, and mammalian maternal metabolism. A chemical toxic only through placental transfer, placental damage, or a maternally generated metabolite returns clean in all three, in agreement. Three concordant negatives that are one negative repeated. Unlike the vault's other rail rows these assays are complementary — reproduction, malformation, neurodevelopment — so the apparent breadth of a battery built from them is wider than its actual coverage.

Mechanistic reasoning, not a measured false-negative rate; no source here has run any of the three against a chemical set chosen for that mechanism. That absence is the recordable part. → two-whole-organism-dart-screens-that-both-lack-a-placenta
