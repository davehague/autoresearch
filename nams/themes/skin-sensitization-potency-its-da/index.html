The ITS defined approach (OECD GL 497 Part II) — skin sensitisation potency — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-10) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD Guideline No. 497, Part II, 2 July 2026 edition). Adoption earns consensus deterministically. The qualifier that travels with it is performance, not standing: against human potency data the ITS is roughly LLNA-equivalent, not clearly better, and two of its ten permutations are worse than the animal test in the under-protective direction.

What we believe now

The Integrated Testing Strategy (ITS) is the second of GL 497's three defined approaches. It covers KE1 (protein binding, TG 442C) and KE3 (dendritic-cell activation, TG 442E) of the skin-sensitisation AOP — deliberately skipping KE2 — and adds an in-silico prediction. Each information source is scored; the scores sum to a total battery score of 0–7:

Information sourceAssays permitted (2026 edition)Score
KE1 (TG 442C)ADRA, DPRA0–3 (banded on % depletion)
KE3 (TG 442E)GARD®skin, h-CLAT, U-SENS™0–3 (banded on MIT or EC150, µg/mL)
In silicoDerek Nexus, OECD QSAR Toolbox1 positive / 0 negative

The DIP reads the total: 6–7 → UN GHS 1A (strong sensitiser); 2–5 → UN GHS 1B (other sensitiser); 0–1 → Not Classified. Hazard falls out as a by-product — ≥2 identifies a Cat. 1 sensitiser — so the ITS can also do the 2o3's job, less transparently.

Performance, from the guideline's own text. Against human potency reference data: 54–75% accuracy, where the LLNA scores 60% on the same data; 8 of 10 ITS permutations equal or beat the LLNA. Against LLNA potency reference data: 66–80%. For hazard (Cat. 1 vs NC): balanced accuracy 78–88% (LLNA reference) and 67–76% (human reference). Evaluated by the OECD EG DASS on up to 168 chemicals with LLNA reference data and up to 66 with human reference data (permutations: 72–159 and 37–64 respectively).

Three honesty notes the guideline supplies itself, and this map keeps. (1) Two ITS permutations scored below the LLNA on human data "due to several underpredictions of 1A sensitisers as 1B sensitisers" — the under-protective direction — with the guideline instructing that "selection of these specific DAs needs to be justified." (2) The reference data are imbalanced (more Cat. 1 than NC), so balanced-accuracy measures are "more uncertain, particularly in the case of the human data comparison." (3) The DIP is not the originally published scheme: the OECD EG DASS moved the 1A cut-off from a score of 7 to 6 and re-based it from ECETOC categories onto UN GHS sub-categories.

A defined approach is an object separable from its assays — this map is where the vault learned that. The 2021 edition fixed two variants: ITSv1 (DPRA + h-CLAT + Derek Nexus) and ITSv2 (DPRA + h-CLAT + OECD QSAR Toolbox), differing only in the in-silico leg. The 2026 edition keeps those as the "classic ITS DA" and admits alternative information sources at every position, generating a space of ITS permutations, each separately evaluated. The battery changed underneath the DA. What is adopted is a DIP plus an admissible set of information sources — not a fixed triple of assays.

Accessibility is unequal between two otherwise identical adopted DAs. ITSv1's Derek Nexus is a commercial, license-gated tool; ITSv2's OECD QSAR Toolbox is free. GARD®skin and U-SENS™ are likewise proprietary platforms. funding_flag: [industry, method-developer] — the scoring scheme originates in Takenouchi et al. (2015), J Appl Toxicol 35:1318–32, an industry-authored test-battery paper, extended to ADRA by Imamura et al. (2022), J Appl Toxicol 42(7):1159–1167; the OECD EG DASS independently re-parameterised and evaluated it before adoption. Developer origin plus independent regulator re-parameterisation: stronger than self-validation, weaker than independent origin. Recorded as both.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • The triad completed — one battery, three DIPs, three regulatory questions (composes-with, and an explicit NON-composition): GL 497 now holds three defined approaches over substantially the same in-chemico / in-vitro chemistry. skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach (Part I) answers hazard — sensitiser or not. This map (Part II) answers potency — GHS 1A / 1B / NC. And skin-sensitization-quantitative-pod-da (Part III) answers dose — a quantitative point of departure. The guideline forbids chaining them: Part III's DAs "cannot be used in Part I or Part II." Three sibling DAs, one battery, no cascade. This is the sharpest evidence in the vault that a defined approach is an object separable from its assays — the DIP, not the chemistry, is what is adopted.
  • Battery components (composes-with, and their composition just changed): skin-sensitization-dpra (TG 442C, KE1) and skin-sensitization-hclat (TG 442E, KE3) are the ITS's classic legs. skin-sensitization-keratinosens (TG 442D, KE2) is not used by the ITS at all — the ITS skips KE2, which the 2o3 requires. The 2026 edition admits ADRA beside DPRA and GARD®skin / U-SENS™ beside h-CLAT; none of those entrants has its own map yet (logged as an open gap, deliberately as plain text, not a wikilink).
  • The in-silico leg is a direct compose edge to the computational hub (composes-with): in-silico-qsar-read-across is not merely adjacent to this DA — it is one of its three information sources. Derek Nexus and the OECD QSAR Toolbox each contribute a 0/1 score to the ITS battery total. This is the first place in the vault where a computational method is a scored, adopted component of a wet-assay defined approach, rather than a supporting tool used alongside one. It also sharpens that map's split-axes story: (Q)SAR is proposed as a general within-IATA NAM, an adopted stand-alone Ames substitute under ICH M7, and — here — an adopted scored leg of a skin-sensitisation DA. Three different regulatory standings for one technology.
  • Adopted-but-commercially-gated (structural pattern, three instances now): Derek Nexus (ITSv1) is license-gated where the QSAR Toolbox (ITSv2) is free — two adopted DAs, identical but for who can afford to run them. Same family as the patented HepaRG™ line under metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg and the proprietary Corrositex® kit under skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435. The vault's two axes do not capture this; it is now visible enough to name.
  • Dose bridge (composes-with): a GHS potency sub-category is a hazard class, not a dose. Turning ITS output into a human-relevant exposure still requires pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics — or, within GL 497 itself, the Part III PoD DAs, which the guideline forbids chaining to this one.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No edge to the genotox battery, carcinogenicity, hepatotox, metabolism (TG 445A), endocrine, immunotoxicity, DART, inhalation, or FET ecotox — skin sensitisation remains a self-contained endpoint whose only outward edges are the in-silico hub and the PBK dose bridge. Nothing is refuted; the 2o3 map is not downgraded by this ingest (the ITS answers a different question), but its performance figures are now known to be 2021-sourced against a 2026 text.
