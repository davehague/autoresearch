What we believe now

U-SENS™ exposes U937 cells — an immortalised line of human white blood cells standing in for the dendritic cells that present foreign material to the immune system in real skin — to a test chemical, and measures by flow cytometry how much CD86 the cells put on their surface. CD86 is the "I have been switched on" flag an immune cell displays. The readout is a stimulation index: treated over untreated, as a percentage. Above 150% → sensitiser. This addresses key event 3 of the adverse outcome pathway for skin allergy, the same step the h-CLAT covers.

Two things are known about it in this vault, both from a single 2025 study by its owners:

  1. Substituted for the h-CLAT in the 2-out-of-3 defined approach, it holds performance roughly level and narrows what gets answered. 71% balanced accuracy against the animal LLNA and 77% against human data on the plain cut-off; 77% and 88% once the borderline band withdraws the chemicals it cannot resolve. The incumbent battery's published range against human data is 72–97%. This is a lateral move, not an upgrade.
  2. Its adopted cut-off has a measured grey zone the adopted rule does not encode. 128–176%, holding 15–17% of chemicals, and reclassifying 18% of OECD's own reference database when applied.

Both claims come from one paper, written by the assay's owner, arguing for the assay's admission to a guideline. Nothing here has been independently reproduced.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — flow cytometry is an instrument readout, and the data-interpretation procedure is a published numeric threshold. By the vault's deterministic ladder that is rung 5, and no human judgement is load-bearing in the call.

And the tier is not an endorsement, in two specific ways.

  • domain_gate — the band. The rule is fixed; it is not determinate everywhere. Between 128% and 176% the assay's own scatter is wider than the distance to the threshold, and the published rule still returns a hard positive or negative. An automated system reads 151% with the same confidence as 900%. A loop that should exist would carry the three-band model (positive / negative / declines to answer) — which is itself perfectly automatable, and arguably a better loop primitive, because "no call" is a machine-legible state a scheduler can act on. The three-band model is not in the adopted guideline. → the-adopted-cut-off-carries-an-uncertainty-band-the-decision-rule-does-not
  • access_gate — the owner. Proprietary L'Oréal method.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚠️ CORRELATED BLIND SPOT — set on both maps, 2026-08-08. skin-sensitization-hclat and this map are alternates for the same slot, not independent tests. Both read CD86 up-regulation — the same protein, the same direction — on an immortalised human myeloid leukaemia line (THP-1 for the h-CLAT, U937 here), for the same key event in the same adverse outcome pathway. The source states the substitution is offered as "NAM for AOP KE 3", which is the sourced half of this flag.

    What it means for a loop. Guideline 497's 2026 edition turns the defined approach into a space of permutations — an admissible set of assays at each step, freely combined. A permutation using U-SENS™ and a permutation using the h-CLAT look like two different batteries and produce one measurement of one thing. An automated system that ran both and treated their agreement as corroboration would be counting the same vote twice, quickly and repeatably. That is the rail's exact failure shape: speed converting a structural redundancy into a compounding one.

    The metabolic half is a vault inference and is flagged as such. Neither U937 nor THP-1 has meaningful skin xenobiotic metabolism, so both should under-detect pro-haptens — chemicals that are inert until an enzyme in the skin activates them. That claim is not from this source; it is the same unresolved pro-hapten flag raised across the whole 2o3 battery by the 2026-08-01 monthly connect pass, which is still awaiting a citation and a human verdict (docs/backlog.md). What this source adds is that the substitution does nothing to fix it — you cannot escape a shared blind spot by swapping one member of the pair for the other.

  • Composes-with (substitutable member). skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach — this is a candidate key-event-3 leg of that battery, and this map's numbers are the first measurement in the vault of what one of Guideline 497's permitted substitutions actually costs.

  • Composes-with (potency). skin-sensitization-potency-its-da (Guideline 497 Part II) bands the key-event-3 assay's minimum induction threshold into a 0–3 potency score. U-SENS™ is admitted as an alternative source at that position. The borderline band is a binary-call model and this source says nothing about how it would propagate into a banded potency score — an open question, not a claim.

  • Global-ripple. No refutation edges against the other skin-sensitisation maps (skin-sensitization-dpra, skin-sensitization-keratinosens, skin-sensitization-quantitative-pod-da) — different key events, different measurements. Outward, the nearest kin is not in this endpoint at all: it is eye-irritation-rhce-time-to-toxicity-tg492b, the vault's other adopted method whose value comes partly from knowing when to decline, and nam-data-processing-pipeline, where a threshold's sensitivity to processing choices is the standing theme.
