What we believe now

Drug-induced nephrotoxicity is a major cause of FDA-approved-drug withdrawal, and animal models plus standard 2D cell culture are stated not to faithfully reproduce human kidney structure or drug response. This platform grows kidney organoids from human induced pluripotent stem cells (hiPSCs) through a staged protocol — mesodermal induction → organoid formation → kidney differentiation — and confirms by immunofluorescence imaging that the resulting multicellular organoids contain endothelial cells and renal tubular cells.

Organoids were exposed to four drugs across 25–800 µM: cisplatin and gentamicin (established high-toxicity nephrotoxicants) versus dexamethasone and probenecid (established low-toxicity comparators). Cellular injury was assessed via evaluation of cell death. Drug-treated organoids showed dose-dependent increases in cell death, with significantly more death under the two high-toxicity drugs than the two low-toxicity ones — the expected ranking, on a reference set deliberately chosen because that ranking was already known. The authors explicitly call for "further optimization of both organoid maturation and toxicity assays" before the platform can be trusted for screening.
