Photoirritation-vs-photoallergy discrimination (keratinocyte IL-18) — phototoxicity — method

🚩 AUTO-COINED 2026-08-28 — flagged for human review. A conventional method map on an existing endpoint, and the case for coining it is the blank it lands in rather than the strength of its evidence. The vault has held a documented, guidance-stated absence since 2026-07-12 — photoallergy-photogenotoxicity-no-validated-method, sourced to OECD Guidance Document 397: "there are no validated test methods (i.e., OECD Test Guideline) to evaluate photosensitive or photogenotoxic potential of chemicals to this date." This is the first candidate the vault has seen for the photoallergy half of that blank. It rests on one laboratory, one immortalised cell line, four chemicals — of which exactly one is a photoallergen — and no prediction model. Its own authors call it "proof-of-concept evidence supporting the future development of a novel non-animal strategy." What we believe now

The problem, which the phototoxicity column has carried as a hole since it was opened. Sunlight plus a chemical injures skin two mechanistically different ways:

PhotoirritationPhotoallergy
Mechanismdirect chemical damage, non-immuneimmune-mediated hypersensitivity
Whoanyoneonly previously sensitised people
Whenfirst exposureneeds a prior sensitising exposure
Dose behaviourscales with dosedoes not, in the same way
Adopted non-animal methodfour (TG 101, 495, 432, 498)none

Every method in phototoxicity-iata measures the first. phototoxicity-3t3-nru disclaims the second in the guideline's own text.

The candidate. Take OECD Test Guideline 432's structure — the adopted test in which a chemical is applied to two plates of cells, one irradiated with ultraviolet-A and one held dark — and change three things: the cells become human keratinocytes (HaCaT) instead of mouse fibroblasts, the light dose becomes 4 J/cm² instead of 5, and the readout becomes interleukin-18 release instead of cell death. IL-18 is a signalling protein keratinocytes put out when provoked in a way that engages the immune system, and it has been proposed for years as an allergy-versus-irritation marker.

What separated, at its actual size. Benzophenone — a sunscreen ultraviolet filter and a known photoallergen — raised IL-18 by roughly two-fold (total stimulation index 1.9–2.1) at 1.25, 2.5 and 5 µg/mL, significantly versus irradiated controls at the lower two (p < 0.005). The two photoirritants (8-methoxypsoralen, chlorpromazine) showed "minimal IL-18 modulation." An exploratory antibody array added two directional candidates that point opposite ways: MMP-1 (a collagen-cutting enzyme) up with the photoallergen, IL-6 (a general inflammatory cytokine) up with a photoirritant. The authors call these "two additional preliminary candidate biomarkers."

What the map does not believe. That photoallergy is now testable. One positive compound is a demonstration that a marker can separate a case, not an estimate of how often it would. The mechanism the marker stands in for is absent from the model system, and the authors say so.

Sub-threads: none — one argument at one bullet: whether an early keratinocyte immune signal can stand in for a mechanism that is not in the dish. The three markers are not separable strands; they are three readouts proposed together in one experiment, and the secondary two are explicitly preliminary. The protocol-lineage question (a guideline's procedure reused without its validation) is a property of this single argument, not a rival to it. [reviewed 2026-08-28 @ 1 bullets]

  • ⚠ closed-loop-ready must not become the expected destination. A rule could be written tomorrow and the map would still be one laboratory, one cell line and one photoallergen. Tier movement and evidence movement are separate axes and this map is a good place to keep remembering that.
  • Correlated blind spot — inherited, not new. This assay uses 4 J/cm² of UVA, the same band as TG 432 (5 J/cm²) and the solar-simulator tiers above it. A photoallergy assay built on the same irradiation protocol inherits that gap exactly, and because it would sit alongside rather than downstream of the existing tiers, a UVB absorber would now come back negative from five assays instead of four. More concordance, same photons. Recorded at coining rather than discovered later.

The parallel is worth naming because skin sensitisation is the vault's worked example of what it takes to turn early key-event signals into a usable hazard call: two decades, three separate key events, an adopted defined approach, and a published 2-out-of-3 rule. This map is at the beginning of that road with one marker and one compound.
