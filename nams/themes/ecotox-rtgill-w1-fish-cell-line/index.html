RTgill-W1 fish cell line acute toxicity (OECD TG 249) — aquatic ecotoxicity — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-11) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 249, adopted 14 June 2021), with an OECD validation report (ENV/CBC/MONO(2021)9) and demonstrated inter-laboratory reproducibility underneath. Adoption earns consensus deterministically. The second NAM in the aquatic column, and the second aimed at the same in-vivo test — TG 203, the adult acute fish lethality study.

What we believe now

Confluent monolayers of RTgill-W1 — a permanent cell line from the gill filaments of rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss), itself one of TG 203's standard species — are exposed for 24 h in a defined, protein-free, animal-component-free medium (L-15/ex). Viability is read three ways on the same cells: resazurin (metabolic activity), CFDA-AM (plasma-membrane integrity), neutral red (lysosomal integrity). All three EC50s are reported; the lowest is taken as the conservative estimate and becomes the predicted 96 h LC50 for fish.

The regulatory ambition is deliberately smaller than the FET's, and the guideline sets it that way. TG 249's stated purposes are (i) predicting fish acute toxicity in product testing, (ii) range-finding and pre-screening ahead of a full fish test, and (iii) hazard evidence "in combination with other lines of evidence" (QSAR, weight of evidence) inside an ITS/IATA. It does not claim to replace TG 203 outright — unlike fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet (TG 236), which is positioned to substitute for the adult test for many substances. Two adopted NAMs, same target, different ambition; the vault should not flatten them into "the fish alternatives."

The guideline reports EC50s in "excellent agreement" with in-vivo LC50s across a wide chemical range but gives no single headline sensitivity/specificity/R² figure in its text. Recorded as a gap, not filled from a secondary source. What it does quantify — unusually, and to its credit — is the applicability domain: water solubility 10⁻³ to 10⁶ mg/L, log KOW −4 to 7, log Henry's law constant 0 to −13. Effect concentrations should rest on measured exposure concentrations.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — Three machine-read viability endpoints (resazurin, CFDA-AM, NRU) and an explicit rule: take the lowest of the three EC50s. But see the blind spot — it fails on the same chemicals as the FET, for the same reasons.

  • Domain gate: bounded to log KOW −4 to 7 — a loop must check the applicability domain before trusting the decision rule. Extended 2026-08-16: that domain is written entirely in physicochemical terms and carries no mechanistic dimension. For a compound acting through species-specific biology, which fish the cells came from can decide the result — and the guideline's own reference-toxicant check passes either way, so the loop cannot detect the carve-out from inside the assay.
  • Correlated blind spot: shares a failure mode with [fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet]. In a battery these are one vote, not two — and an automated loop trusting the decision rule will compound the error rather than catch it.

  • 2026-08-16 — ⚠ domain_gate EXTENDED — the applicability domain is written in physical chemistry and the thing that decided the answer was which fish the cells came from. Status HELD at consensus, loop_readiness HELD at closed-loop-ready. Ingested moriyama-2026-tire-chemicals-fish-cell-lines: six fish cell lines — four salmonid, two distantly related — against two chemicals, 24 h, machine-read viability. (1) The calibration chemical says the assay family is sound. 3,4-dichloroaniline, this guideline's own reference toxicant, gave EC50 ≈ 50 mg/L in all six lines, against TG 249's published 43.6 ± 6.1 mg/L for RTgill-W1. No marked species difference anywhere. That is a clean external corroboration of the guideline's own number from an unrelated laboratory. (2) The chemical anyone would want to screen split the panel apart. 6PPD-quinone — the tyre-rubber breakdown product identified in 2021 as the cause of coho salmon dying within hours of rainstorms in urban streams — gave 8.54 µg/L in coho salmon cells, 37.8 µg/L in rainbow trout cells, and no significant cytotoxicity at all in chinook salmon, sockeye salmon, bluegill and fathead minnow cells up to 500 µg/L. Four of six lines, including two salmon species, return nothing on a chemical that empties rivers. → the-reference-chemical-agreed-and-the-real-one-did-not (3) Why this is a domain_gate move and NOT a status move. The guideline's own cell line, RTgill-W1, was not among the six tested — so TG 249 was not run, its decision rule was not applied, and nothing here is a validation failure. What the study measures is the assumption the guideline rests on: that one fish cell line stands in for fish. The existing gate (log KOW −4 to 7, water solubility and Henry's-law bounds) is stated entirely in physicochemical terms, and 6PPD-quinone sits inside it. The gate now also records the mechanistic carve-out: for a compound whose toxicity runs through species-specific biology — prior work points at mitochondrial dysfunction and interspecies differences in metabolic processing — the cell line's species is a determinant of the result, and no physicochemical screen detects that in advance. (4) The loop-relevant statement, which is the reason this is worth a bullet at all. A loop reading this assay cannot distinguish "clean flat line because the chemical is safe" from "clean flat line because this cell line cannot see this mechanism." Both pass every quality control the guideline specifies, including the reference-toxicant check — which passed here, on all six lines, while four of them were blind to the real chemical. A reference chemical certifies that the assay is being performed correctly; it cannot certify that the assay's biology contains the mechanism the next chemical will act through. closed-loop-ready is unchanged and remains correct: the readout is instrument-read and the rule is fixed. What is sharper is the standing caveat on that tier — trust the rule inside the domain, and the domain has a dimension the guideline does not enumerate. (5) An inter-laboratory disagreement inside the assay family, recorded rather than smoothed. In RTG-2 (rainbow trout), this study measured EC50 37.8 µg/L; Greer et al. 2023 reported no effect to 100 µg/L in the same line with the same viability chemistry. The authors attribute it to culture conditions and close by calling harmonised protocols "an urgent need." Recorded alongside the vault's standing reproducibility thread (validation-paradigm-nam-confidence). (6) Brakes. Single laboratory, technical note, two chemicals, n = 4, nominal concentrations not analytically confirmed — TG 249 requires measured ones, and 6PPD-quinone adsorbs and hydrolyses, so true potency may be higher than reported. The lines differ in tissue of origin (embryonic, epithelial), and the authors note gill-derived cells may be the more sensitive choice — an argument that TG 249's own choice of a gill line is the right one, untested here. Bias watch: partly funded by Bridgestone Corporation, a tyre manufacturer, on the toxicity of a tyre-derived chemical; the disclosure is upfront, the institution is a Japanese national research institute, and the direction is mixed — extreme coho sensitivity is confirmed, which is inconvenient to the funder, while "four of six lines see nothing" is not. evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, regulatory_status: none. Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • THE finding of this ingest — a shared blind spot, i.e. correlated failure, not redundancy: TG 249 documents that it under-predicts for (a) neurotoxic chemicals acting "through specific ion channels or receptors typical of brain tissue" (a gill monolayer has no brain) and (b) metabolism-dependent toxicity — the worked example is allyl alcohol, under-estimated because the cells cannot convert it to the far more toxic acrolein (acrolein itself is predicted very well: the toxicology is fine, the metabolic competence is not). fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet carries the same documented under-prediction domain — neurotoxic, metabolism-dependent, low-uptake chemicals. So the aquatic column now holds two adopted NAMs, both displacing TG 203, that fail on the same chemicals for the same structural reasons. They are not independent lines of evidence. A weight-of-evidence battery that stacks them looks like two votes and is closer to one — and the chemicals it misses are, in both cases, the neurotoxic and the bioactivation-dependent. Flagged, not resolved: covering that domain needs something with a nervous system or metabolic competence (the FET embryo has some; neither has much), and nothing in the vault supplies it. Recorded on both maps.
  • Redundancy (partial, and worth stating): the two methods overlap heavily in what they predict (acute fish lethality) while differing in what they are (an isolated gill epithelium vs a whole developing embryo). The honest read is partial redundancy with a correlated blind spot — the worst combination for a battery, and the reason this is flagged rather than celebrated as coverage.
  • In-silico edge (composes-with): the guideline supplies a QSAR for range-finding — log EC50 (mM) = −0.96(±0.09)·log KOW + 1.57(±0.28) — to predict an EC50 and guide concentration selection. A concrete, adopted-guideline instance of computation feeding a wet assay → in-silico-qsar-read-across.
  • Commercial-accessibility axis (cross-cutting, a counter-example): funding_flag: none. The cell line comes from recognised public depositories, the medium is defined and animal-component-free, a comparable assay exists as ISO standard 21115, and there is no proprietary kit or licensed test system anywhere in the guideline. Set against the vault's run of commercially-gated human-health methods — HepaRG™ (metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg), Corrositex® (skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435), EpiDerm™/KeraSkin™ (phototoxicity-rhe-pt), EpiOcular™/SkinEthic™ (eye-irritation-rhce-no-category) — TG 249 is the cleanest commercially-unencumbered adopted method the vault holds. It shows the third axis (practical accessibility) is not inevitable: an OECD TG can be built on open materials. Sharpens the axis named on 2026-07-10; still not modelled.
  • Dose bridge: none. This is an ecotoxicological endpoint — it predicts hazard to fish, not to humans — so pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics (human QIVIVE) does not apply. Same boundary the FET map draws.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No edge to any human-health column — the readouts (resazurin, neutral red) are the same generic viability chemistry used across the vault, but a shared assay reagent is not a shared claim, and the vault does not cross-link on that basis. Ecotox remains a structurally separate column with exactly one internal edge: the FET, above.
  • Added 2026-08-12 — same cells, richer readout, one rung down (composes-with, not redundant): phenotypic-profiling-cell-painting holds the human-cell version of the identical idea — stain many organelles, photograph the plate, extract a high-dimensional fingerprint — and sits at automatable-open-loop because converting a fingerprint into a hazard classification is an unwritten weight-of-evidence judgement. tome-2026-high-content-imaging-rtgill-w1 brings that technique onto the TG 249 cell line and inherits the same blocker. The edge is a warning, not a battery: these are the same measurement philosophy applied to different tissue, so a battery composing them would be another correlated vote rather than an independent one, and the vault does not treat "more features" as "more evidence."
  • Added 2026-08-12 — the rail's cause acquires a candidate fix and a fresh worked case, and NEITHER clears this row: metabolism-biotransformation-metabolite-generation (coined tonight) proposes supplying metabolic competence as a modular upstream step — the shape of fix the rail's remedy calls for, with no results behind it — and liang-2026-bht-metabolites-cardiotoxicity-zebrafish supplies a second worked case of the failure itself, alongside this guideline's own allyl-alcohol/acrolein example. The correlated_blind_spot on this map is unchanged: a proposed fix never run against a bioactivation-dependent chemical closes nothing.
