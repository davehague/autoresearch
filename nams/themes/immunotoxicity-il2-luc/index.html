IL-2 Luc / IL-2 Luc LTT (OECD TG 444A) — immunotoxicity — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-09) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 444A, published 25 June 2025). Adoption is what earns consensus (deterministic per the evidence model). The qualifier that travels with it is role, not standing: the guideline itself frames the method as a battery component, not a stand-alone replacement for the in-vivo immunotoxicity study. Structurally the same shape as genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa — adopted, and narrow on purpose.

What we believe now

The IL-2 Luc assay uses a stably transfected human T-lymphoblastic reporter cell line (the Multi-ImmunoTox Assay / MITA platform) carrying luciferase under cytokine gene promoters (IL-2, IFN-γ) plus a constitutive GAPDH control reporter. After chemical exposure, the cytotoxicity-corrected ratio of cytokine-promoter to constitutive luciferase flags suppression or activation of T-cell cytokine transcription — a mechanistic proxy for immunomodulatory potential. The IL-2 Luc LTT variant adds a leukocyte-toxicity readout to improve predictive capacity.

This is the first adopted OECD Test Guideline for a non-animal immunotoxicity method, and it opens the immunotoxicity endpoint in this vault. Underneath the adoption sits real independent reproducibility: JaCVAM-coordinated inter-laboratory validation plus the 2024 OECD validation report of the IL-2 Luc LTT assay — that, not a developer's self-validation, is what earns formally-validated beneath the TG.

We record no sensitivity/specificity numbers: the adoption materials carry none in the sources reached. Output is a screening call (immunomodulatory / not), not a quantitative apical immune-function prediction. (The TG PDF was archived 2026-07-09 — the earlier pdf_unavailable was a rung-1 stop, not a paywall. The archived bytes carry a 2 July 2026 cover date, i.e. a newer edition than the 25 June 2025 publication this map describes; the performance question above has therefore not yet been re-checked against the current text. Logged, not assumed away.)

The load-bearing caveat is scope. TG 444A reads one node — T-cell cytokine transcription — of a much larger immune system. It does not, alone, classify a chemical's systemic immunotoxic hazard, and it does not replace the T-cell-dependent antibody response (TDAR) nested within the in-vivo TG 443 EOGRTS.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — Luminescence ratio — machine-readable, no agreed call rule recorded.

  • Blocker: no published threshold
  • Correlated blind spot (added 2026-08-26, structural, unmeasured): shares a failure mode with immunotoxicity-human-pbmc-model. Both read isolated immune cells in suspension with no lymphoid architecture and no metabolic competence — a reporter T-cell line here, primary blood mononuclear cells there — so neither can produce a T-dependent antibody response (the whole-organism functional endpoint that regulation actually uses), and neither can see immunotoxicity that requires a metabolite or a germinal centre. In a battery these are closer to one vote than two, and an automated system reading their agreement as confirmation compounds the shared gap rather than catching it. ⚠ Argued from what each method contains, not measured — no study has run both on a shared chemical set, and this row carries no concordance statistic.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Battery composition (composes-with, by the guideline's own words; partly mapped 2026-07-10): TG 444A is written as a component of a battery to determine immunotoxic potential, not a stand-alone test. Its myelotoxicity CFU-GM neighbour is now mapped — immunotoxicity-myelotoxicity-cfu-gm — covering a second immune compartment (myeloid progenitor viability) alongside this map's adaptive T-cell cytokine signalling. Dendritic-cell and macrophage function assays remain unmapped (open gap, plain text, not a wikilink). No defined approach exists that composes these two; the vault flags candidate batteries rather than adopting them.
  • Cross-endpoint confound with the glucocorticoid receptor (flagged 2026-07-10, a genuine ambiguity): endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation (TG 454A) reads GR activation, and glucocorticoids classically suppress IL-2 transcription. A chemical that is a GR agonist would be expected to depress this assay's IL-2 promoter signal. So a positive TG 444A immunosuppression call and a positive TG 454A agonist call may be the same mechanism read twice, filed under two different endpoints. Neither guideline addresses the other. This is the vault's first candidate cross-endpoint confound — surfaced for a human, not resolved here.
  • Adopted-but-role-bounded sibling (structural, not biological): genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa is the vault's other freshly-adopted TG that is explicitly a follow-up / interpretation layer rather than a primary hazard call. Same two-axis shape, same honest scoping — worth reading together as the pattern "adopted ≠ stand-alone."
  • Immune-cell methodology family (composes-with, distinct biology): the skin-sensitization battery already reads immune-cell biology — skin-sensitization-hclat scores dendritic-cell activation (KE3) and skin-sensitization-keratinosens the keratinocyte ARE-Nrf2 response (KE2). These are immunological assays for a local, hapten-specific adaptive response (allergic contact dermatitis). TG 444A reads systemic, antigen-independent T-cell cytokine modulation. Adjacent cell biology, different question — cross-linked as a family, not redundant, and not composable into one call.
  • ⚠ Correlated blind spot (ADDED 2026-08-26 — the rail): immunotoxicity-human-pbmc-model. See the Loop-readiness section above for the argument. The consequence for battery design is the one the rail always states: two assays that fail on the same chemicals for the same structural reason cast one vote, not two, and the concordance a closed loop would read as confirmation is an artifact of the shared gap. Flagged on both maps; unmeasured on both.
  • Dose bridge (composes-with): like every in-vitro potency method here, an IL-2 Luc bioactive concentration needs pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics (OECD GD 331) to become a human-equivalent dose. The screening-call output makes this a weaker bridge than for quantitative assays, but the hub edge holds.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No edge to the local skin/eye irritation & corrosion maps, the hepatotox MPS map, the genotox detection battery (Ames / micronucleus / mammalian gene mutation), the carcinogenicity CTA map, the DART maps, inhalation ALI, FET ecotox, or the in-silico hub — immunotoxicity is a genuinely new column, and its only real edges are the three above. Nothing in the vault is duplicated or refuted by it.
