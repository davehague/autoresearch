Eye-hazard defined approaches (serious eye damage / eye irritation) — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-05) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 467). The vault's second adopted defined approach and its first for the eye endpoint — the DA pattern generalising beyond skin sensitisation.

What we believe now

OECD TG 467 codifies two defined approaches that combine non-animal information sources — BCOP (TG 437), EpiOcular EIT (TG 492), SkinEthic time-to-toxicity (TG 492B), in-vitro depth-of-injury, EyeIRR-IS — through a fixed data-interpretation procedure to assign the three-way UN GHS eye-hazard class (Cat 1 serious eye damage / Cat 2 irritation / No Category) without expert judgment or the Draize rabbit eye test. On an agrochemical set the DAs reproduced the majority in-vivo GHS call for 27 of 29 formulations. Applicability-domain-bounded.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — TG 467 is a defined approach in the same sense as GL 497 — a fixed data-interpretation procedure over validated component methods, applied without expert judgment.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-25 — This map gets its actual performance numbers, a rival at its own decision point, and a hub above it. From OECD GD 263 (3rd ed., 2024), eye-hazard-iata-gd263. (1) The numbers, which the vault did not hold: across the two defined approaches, "The rate of true positives varies between 56.3 and 69.2 %. Between 18.3% and 30.4 % are overpredicted as Cat1, whereas between 0.9 and 13.6 are false negatives (underpredicted as NoCat)." Read plainly: it gets the right answer on somewhere between a half and two-thirds of chemicals, over-labels up to 30% as blinding when they are not, and in the worst permutation calls up to 13.6% of genuinely hazardous chemicals unclassified. The vault's previously-recorded "27/29 majority-GHS concordance" was one favourable chemical set, not the DA's performance. (2) A rival at the same decision point: GD 263 routes suspected Category 2 chemicals to "either the Defined Approaches of TG 467 … or the time-to-toxicity test of TG 492B," and names both as "stand-alone methods that can predict three UN GHS categories." eye-irritation-rhce-time-to-toxicity-tg492b does with one assay what this DA does with two — at 74.4% balanced accuracy against this DA's 56–69% true-positive rate, though the two figures are not directly comparable. (3) ⚠️ And DAL-1's own independence is now questionable: it composes TG 492 (RhCE) with TG 437 (BCOP, excised cow eyes), and GD 263 reports that BCOP "underpredicts 14-47% of the in vivo Cat. 1 chemicals … mostly those inducing persistent effects" — so the DA's serious-damage arm rests on a method with a named, structural, temporal blind spot. Status and loop tier both hold at consensus / closed-loop-ready; what changed is the honesty of the numbers attached to them. → bcop-and-ice-share-a-persistence-blind-spot · regulatory-sensitivity-demand-caps-specificity-at-60-80-percent
  • 2026-07-05 — Ingested OECD TG 467 (adopted June 2022). Coins this map (new endpoint: eye irritation / serious eye damage). 27/29 majority-GHS concordance on the agrochemical set; adopted on both axes → consensus.
  • 2026-07-05 — Domain-widening move (Jan 2025 update): applicability extended from nonsurfactant chemicals to surfactants, plus Phase-3 data — the same maturation arc DAs follow across endpoints.
  • 2026-07-11 — This hub gains its first mapped component. Status holds at consensus. eye-irritation-rhce-no-category (OECD TG 492, corrected edition 25 June 2025) maps the RhCE information source this DA consumes — and the pairing sharpens what a defined approach actually adds. TG 492 alone yields one bit (tissue viability > 60% → UN GHS No Category; ≤ 60% → keep testing) and the guideline states flatly it "cannot be used on [its] own to replace the in vivo rabbit eye test." This DA, over the same kind of inputs, returns the full three-way call. The DA's value is therefore the data-interpretation procedure, not the assays — the same conclusion the skin-sensitisation column reached, now confirmed on a second endpoint. The component's numbers (sens 95–99%, spec 63–72%) also explain why: it is deliberately asymmetric, allowed to over-call because a positive routes onward instead of classifying.
  • 2026-07-15 — The hub gains its second mapped component, and it is mechanically independent of the first. Status holds at consensus. eye-macromolecular-ordered-matrix-tg496 (OECD TG 496, adopted 25 June 2024, corrected 2 July 2026) maps the acellular macromolecular information source — an in-chemico corneal-protein-matrix denaturation readout (OD at 405 nm → IDE score). The finding is not the assay, it is the pairing: this DA now composes two components that read the same endpoint by orthogonal mechanisms (protein denaturation in a tube vs living-tissue viability), which is the counter-design to a correlated blind spot — see the connect note below. The eye column now matches the skin-sensitisation column in shape and improves on the aquatic/endocrine columns in independence.
  • 2026-07-22 — regulatory-move, and a coverage gap it exposes. The UK Replacing animals in science strategy (regulatory-acceptance-uk-animal-replacement-strategy) puts eye irritation in basket 1 with a "by the end of 2026 we will aim to" target, and makes a claim the vault should have on record: of the nine non-animal OECD eye methods, only two — OECD TG 492B and OECD TG 467 (this map) — can fully identify all categories of eye hazard; the rest are partial and must be combined per OECD GD 263, the eye IATA. The vault's four eye maps are otherwise all partial-category methods, and it holds no map for TG 492B and none for GD 263 — the document that composes the partial ones into an answer. Logged as an open gap. Status and axes hold; the target is an aim, not a mandate. → uk-three-basket-readiness-taxonomy

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Sibling defined approach — the key global-ripple edge. Same composition logic as skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach: a fixed recipe of validated in-vitro methods + a rule-based data-interpretation procedure yielding a regulatory GHS classification, expert-judgment-free. Two adopted DAs across two endpoints now anchor the defined-approach pattern in the vault.
  • Two components now mapped (composes-with): eye-irritation-rhce-no-category (TG 492, RhCE tissue viability) and eye-macromolecular-ordered-matrix-tg496 (TG 496, acellular macromolecular denaturation) are both information sources in this DA's recipe — the eye column now has a hub-plus-two- components shape, deeper than the sensitisation column's hub-plus-three. Still to ingest (open gaps, plain text): TG 437 (BCOP), TG 438 (ICE), TG 460, TG 491.
  • Third component mapped 2026-07-19 (composes-with): eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494 (TG 494, Vitrigel-EIT — barrier function read as transepithelial electrical resistance) closes the last of the 2 July 2026 eye batch. The eye column is now hub plus three components, the deepest battery structure in the vault. But see the blind-spot note immediately below — component count and independent-vote count have stopped tracking each other here.
  • ⚠️ The two mapped components are mechanically INDEPENDENT — the good case for the blind-spot rail. TG 496 reads protein-matrix denaturation in a cell-free tube; TG 492 reads living-tissue metabolic viability. These fail differently, so within this DA they are closer to two independent votes than one — the opposite of the aquatic pair (fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet ↔ ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line) and the endocrine MMTV panel. This is the automation-tox21-qhts-platform counter-design (an orthogonal measurement, not a bigger battery) already built into an adopted regulatory defined approach. Caveat, so the independence is not over-sold: both components lack metabolism, so both are blind to metabolite-mediated eye hazard — independent on what they read, jointly blind on what neither reads.
  • ⚠️ THE THIRD COMPONENT IS NOT A THIRD VOTE (2026-07-19) — and this hub is where the distinction becomes legible. eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494 reads barrier integrity of a living human corneal epithelium; eye-irritation-rhce-no-category reads metabolic viability of a living human corneal epithelium. TG 494's own text states that irritants destroy barrier function and "subsequently induce epithelial cell death" — the guideline asserts that one component measures the precursor of what the other component measures. correlated_blind_spot is now set on both. This is the vault's first blind-spot pair stated by a source rather than inferred by the vault. So this battery now demonstrates both halves of the Tox21 counter-design at once: TG 496 (acellular, in a tube) fails differently from the two tissue methods; TG 494 fails similarly to TG 492. Three components, roughly two independent mechanisms. For a defined approach whose whole value proposition is composing independent information sources under a fixed rule, that gap between component count and independent-vote count is the number that actually matters — and no axis in the vault records it. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md.
  • Shared component technology: its EpiOcular component is a reconstructed-human-tissue model of the same class as skin-irritation-rhe (RhE/RhO viability) — adjacent local-irritation endpoints. As of 2026-07-11 that technology family carries four adopted OECD methods (TG 439 skin irritation, TG 431 skin corrosion, TG 498 phototoxicity, TG 492 eye) — see phototoxicity-rhe-pt. Named as a concentration risk: a systematic flaw in reconstructed-tissue models would ripple across four adopted TGs at once.
  • Global-ripple: distinct endpoint from the skin-sensitisation mechanism maps; the connective tissue is the DA pattern, not a shared assay.
