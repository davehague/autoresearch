Human Liver-Chip / MPS — hepatotoxicity (DILI) — method

Status: emerging (2026-07-06) — split axes, bias-flagged. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method (one sizeable blinded study) but regulatory_status: under-validation (FDA ISTAND pilot, not adopted), and the key validation is developer-run (funding_flag: industry / method-developer). The vault's first entry for the hepatotoxicity endpoint, previously a headline coverage gap.

What we believe now

The human Liver-Chip is a perfused microphysiological system (organ-on-chip) co-culturing primary human hepatocytes with non-parenchymal cells to reconstitute liver metabolism and microenvironment. In the largest single reported Organ-Chip evaluation to date (Ewart et al. 2022, PMID 36473994), it reached 87% sensitivity and 100% specificity for drug-induced liver injury (DILI) on a blinded 27-drug panel (870 chips, 3 donors). The eye-catching specificity is real but the panel is curated and the study was run by the platform's developer (Emulate) — a developer validation, not an independent ring trial. Kept emerging: promising, single-study, developer-led, regulatorily unadopted.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — The organ-chip's readouts are instrument-based, but nothing fixes how they combine into a call.

  • Blocker: multi-parametric readout with no fixed data-interpretation procedure

  • 2026-07-10 — An adopted human-liver TG arrives — for a different question. Status holds at emerging. metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg (OECD TG 445A) is the first adopted OECD in-vitro method using a human hepatic model — the HepaRG™ line this map already named among the hepatic systems to watch. It does not validate, refute, or supersede the Liver-Chip: TG 445A reads a mechanistic metabolic perturbation (CYP induction) in a static monolayer, while this map reads apical liver injury (DILI) in a perfused multi-cell system. The pairing is the vault's cleanest demonstration that the axes are orthogonal, not correlated: the Liver-Chip has a real apical predictivity number (87% / 100%) and no adoption; TG 445A has no predictivity number and full adoption on both axes. Neither fact bears on the other.

  • 2026-07-27 — The wet work of a 3D liver assay gets automated end to end, and this map's blocker does not move a millimetre. Status HELD at emerging; loop_readiness HELD at automatable-open-loop. Ingested hellmold-2026-mobot-3d-liver-spheroid-automation (Sci Rep 16(1); all twelve authors employed by mo:re GmbH, the company selling the robot — funding_flag: [method-developer, industry], the automation-hype variant the evidence model names explicitly). Every improvement claimed is a variance improvement — more uniform spheroids, better viability, less hands-on time — and none is quantified in the abstract.   • 2026-07-29 — The cheap comparator for drug-induced liver injury got measured, and it is chemical structure. Status HELD, loop_readiness HELD. Ingested xu-2026-tox21-expanded-panel-dili-cardiotoxicity (Environ. Health Perspect. 134:361-374, PMID 42428255, DOI 10.1021/ehp.6c00052; US federal intramural funding, funding_flag: none). On the same endpoint this map addresses — drug-induced liver injury — machine-learning models built on chemical structure alone reached 0.73 ± 0.03 AUC-ROC, against 0.74 ± 0.03 for structure combined with a 49-target robotic assay panel. Wet confirmation: 121 of 201 predicted-hepatotoxic compounds (60%) produced reactive oxygen species in HepaRG liver cells. Why this map does not move: a microphysiological liver system is a different instrument answering a different question — perfused, multicellular, reading apical injury over days — and nothing here tests one. Why it is recorded anyway: this map's case rests on physiological realism buying predictive accuracy the cheap methods cannot reach, and the vault now holds a number for what the cheap method reaches unaided. A liver chip's value proposition has to be stated against 0.73, not against zero. No source in the vault reports a head-to-head. → the-robot-half-added-one-point-of-auc-over-drawing-the-molecule

  • 2026-08-15 — A domain_gate is SET on a variable the whole field treats as known: the dose. Status HELD at emerging; loop_readiness HELD at automatable-open-loop. Ingested hartauer-2026-rifampicin-liver-chip-induction (Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics, DOI 10.1002/cpt.70418; UNC Eshelman School of Pharmacy + Javelin Biotech, five of ten authors employed by the chip's vendor) ran a perfused human liver tissue chip for seven days on three donors with 10 μM rifampicin and measured the intracellular drug concentration alongside the induction endpoints. It "differed substantially" from the concentration applied to the medium. → the-dose-the-loop-controls-is-not-the-dose-the-cell-receives (1) Why this earns a modifier rather than a bullet. A closed loop's control variable is concentration: the model picks a dose, the robot delivers it, and everything the loop learns about potency is a function of that number. If the map from nominal to actual is compound-specific and time-varying, the loop is learning a composition of two functions and crediting all of it to the first. On one compound that is a calibration offset; across a structurally diverse campaign it distorts the potency ranking, which is precisely what an active-learning loop optimises against when choosing where to sample next. (2) The coupling is the sharp part. Rifampicin induces the very transporters that govern its own intracellular concentration (this map's sibling finding on metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg). Exposure is not merely uncertain — it is a feedback term of the response being measured. A loop assuming dose is exogenous has assumed the feedback away. (3) The counterweight the chip is owed. The only reason anyone knows this is that the group measured it. A static 96-well plate has the same problem in unknown size and does not look. Reporting an inconvenient measurement is the behaviour the bias watch is meant to reward — recorded alongside, not instead of, the funding_flag: [industry, method-developer]. (4) What is unread and is not being filled in. The abstract gives no ratio, no direction and no donor spread. Whether intracellular exposure ran higher than nominal (accumulation) or lower (efflux and binding) changes what a loop should do about it, and the vault does not know which. Subscription publisher, full text unread.

  • 2026-08-17 — The chip beats both standard prediction tools on the hardest drug-interaction case, and the margin is behind a paywall. Status HELD at emerging; loop_readiness HELD at automatable-open-loop; domain_gate unchanged and now pointed straight at this study. mettler-2026-liver-chip-cyp3a4-ddi-net-effect (Drug Metabolism and Disposition 100359, PMID 42537548, DOI 10.1016/j.dmd.2026.100359 — eight of ten authors Novartis, a ninth a university microphysiological-systems head; funding_flag: [industry, method-developer]) ran six drugs that both block and induce CYP3A4, the liver's workhorse drug-metabolising enzyme, through a perfused liver-chip and measured midazolam clearance as the probe. (1) The problem is genuinely hard and the result is genuinely on it. Blocking makes a second drug accumulate; induction makes it vanish; the net effect can point either way and cannot be read off either mechanism alone. Against clinical reference values, "liver-chip predictions showed closer alignment and higher accuracy" than both a mechanistic static model and full physiologically based simulation. Enzyme activity held for three days and stayed inducible by rifampicin. (2) The mechanistic detail a screening loop would get wrong. Most precipitants raised CYP3A4 messenger RNA — the cheap, scalable, gene-expression signal that says "induction" — while the functional net effect came out as induction for some drugs and inhibition for others. The readout that automates and the readout that decides point different ways on part of this set. (3) The abstract's own counterweight, which is why it is credited. "Although constant exposures offered the strongest quantitative performance, the ability to apply dynamic PK profiles represents a distinctive feature of this platform." The flat condition predicted better than the platform's selling point, and they put that in the abstract. (4) Nothing moves, for three reasons. No effect size of any kind is available — paywalled, confirmed oa_status: closed, no free deposit, body unread. "Closer than the comparator" is a comparison, not a threshold, so the map's loop_blocker (multi-parametric readout, no fixed data-interpretation procedure) is untouched. And a company evaluating a platform for its own internal decisions is not an independent validation. → the-chip-beat-the-static-model-and-the-margin-is-unread (5) ⚠ The unread fact that matters most is this map's own domain_gate.
