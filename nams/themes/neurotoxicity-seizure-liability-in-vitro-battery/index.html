What we believe now

The endpoint. A seizure is a burst of runaway electrical activity in the brain, and drug-induced seizure is one of the commonest reasons a candidate drug is abandoned. The pre-first-dose regulatory expectation is currently an animal study with a human scorer at the end: the central-nervous-system leg of ICH S7A (the International Council for Harmonisation guideline requiring the heart, lungs and nervous system to be assessed before a human is first dosed) is a functional observation battery — a trained person handling a dosed rodent and scoring movement, behaviour, coordination and reflexes. See safety-pharmacology-ich-s7a-core-battery.

The method. Two instrument layers, no animal in either.

Layer 1 — the channel panel. Nerve cells fire by moving charged particles through pores called ion channels. Four human channels, each a known route to seizure, are expressed in engineered cell lines and read by automated electrophysiology — a robotic patch clamp that measures the electrical current through the channel directly and reports an IC₅₀, the concentration blocking half its activity.

Layer 2 — the neuron plate. Human induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC)-derived neurons grown on a microelectrode array: a well plate whose floor is a grid of electrodes, so the neurons' firing is recorded electrically rather than watched by anyone.

What the source demonstrates. 88 compounds — 10 parents plus 78 structural derivatives pulled from the Enamine REAL on-demand synthesis catalogue by pharmacophore similarity to known actives — screened across all four channels. Statistical analysis tied 12 pharmacophore features to α4β2 nicotinic activity, 4 of them also to K_V2.1. Then the coherence check: three parents (amoxapine, diphenhydramine, quetiapine) and their analogues on the neuron plate, where the seizure-like firing phenotype changed in the direction the channel IC₅₀ values predicted.

Read that result precisely. It is internal consistency between two in-vitro layers — a genuine and useful thing to show, and not a comparison against a person or an animal. There is no clinical case set, no concordance figure, and the compound set was built for structure-activity learning rather than for error estimation. The paper demonstrates that the two layers tell the same story; it does not demonstrate that the story is true. → a-non-animal-seizure-assay-arrives-with-numbers-and-no-threshold

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — the deterministic ladder in shared/evidence-model.md, in order:

  1. A physical assay exists → not computational-connective.
  2. Does the method's own model consume an in-vivo study? No. This is the entire reason this map exists separately from neurotoxicity-cns-safety-nams-ai, which fails at exactly this rung.
  3. Is human judgment load-bearing in the call? No. An IC₅₀ off an automated patch clamp and burst statistics off a microelectrode array are numbers. No morphology, no histology, no trained scorer.
  4. Machine-readable readout, no fixed data-interpretation procedure → rung 4.

Two separate missing rules, and neither is a machine-vision problem.

  • No integration rule. Nothing says how four IC₅₀ values combine into one call. One potent hit and three clean channels versus four moderate hits — the paper does not adjudicate, because its purpose was to steer medicinal chemists away from substructures, not to classify compounds.
  • No margin rule. An IC₅₀ is a concentration in a dish; the regulatory question is what a patient's brain is exposed to. That conversion is the subject of pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics and it is unusually hard here, because of the blood-brain barrier — the lining that keeps most of the bloodstream out of brain tissue. model-disagreement-as-a-machine-checkable-refusal-rule prices the gap: a consensus permeability model applied to 6,031 chemicals a screen had already called active against a brain receptor predicted 27% would never reach it in a person.

A machine could run every step of this tonight and return a clean table. Nobody has written down what the table means. The vault's dominant blocker, arriving on a brand-new 2026 method rather than inherited as historical debt — the same shape recorded for the kidney in the-kidney-arrives-with-a-plate-reader-and-no-rule.

Loop-ready is not endorsement. Automatability here says nothing about whether the screen is fit to decide anything about a human brain, and the blind spot below is the reason to be careful about exactly that.

⚠️ Correlated blind spot — intra-battery, two gaps

The channel panel and the neuron plate are not two independent votes:

  • Neither metabolises. No liver enzyme system in either. A parent compound clean at all four channels whose metabolite blocks one passes both legs, concordantly.
  • Neither has a blood-brain barrier. Compound is applied directly to cells in both. Chemistry that could never reach a person's brain is flagged as hazardous by both.

The two gaps push in opposite directions — confident negatives on bioactivation-dependent risk, confident positives on brain-excluded chemistry — which is what makes the compounding hard to notice: the battery's internal agreement statistics improve as a loop drifts into both errors. → the-channel-panel-and-the-neuron-plate-share-two-gaps
