Human PBMC immunotoxicity model — immunotoxicity — method

🚩 AUTO-COINED 2026-08-26, flagged for human review. A conventional method map on a conventional toxicological endpoint — but built on one source, one laboratory, six chemicals, and a method its own authors describe as having "the potential to be refined and incorporated into a robust new approach method." It is coined because the immunotoxicity column had no primary-human-cell entry at all, not because the evidence is strong. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md. Git is the undo.

Status: emerging (2026-08-26). One source, evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, regulatory_status: none, no prediction model, no reference chemical set, full text unread.

What we believe now

The problem this exists for. Immune suppression is among the most sensitive effects of PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances — the "forever chemicals"), and it is the endpoint that has "been used to inform regulatory guidance and health-based values." There are "more than 14,000 PFAS." The reference test — the T-dependent antibody response, in which an animal is immunised and its antibody output measured — cannot be run 14,000 times. This is a throughput argument before it is a 3Rs argument.

The method. Primary human peripheral blood mononuclear cells (the immune cells in a tube of donor blood: T cells, B cells, natural killer cells, monocytes) provoked with two standard stimuli — a bacterial cell-wall molecule (LPS) for the fast innate arm, and a plant protein (PHA) that forces T cells to divide, standing in for the adaptive arm. Six PFAS analogues. Readouts: proliferation, cytokine secretion, transcriptomics.

⚠ What it found, and the reason this map exists. "Several PFAS analogues enhanced T-cell proliferation following PHA activation, while a non-inclusive but overlapping subset of analogues suppressed cytokine secretion." Gene expression agreed: B-cell identity and antibody genes down, T-cell activation genes up. The proposed mechanism is "a dysregulated coordination between T- and B-cell responses" — not T cells failing, not B cells failing, but the handoff between them.

What it does not measure. An antibody. The regulated endpoint is a molecule the organism manufactures after immunisation, which needs the architecture of a lymph node — naive B cells meeting helper T cells in a germinal centre over days. A tube of blood has the cast without the stage. The measured readouts are key events on an adverse outcome pathway, and the link from those key events to the antibody outcome is currently a hypothesis, not a concordance statistic.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop. Proliferation, cytokine release and transcriptomics are all instrument-readable — plate readers, flow cytometers, sequencers. Rule 4 of the deterministic ladder fires: machine-readable readout, no fixed data-interpretation procedure.

But record why there is no rule, because it is not the usual reason. The vault's standard case is an administrative gap — the measurement is fine and nobody has set the cut-off (Test Guideline 493). Here the gap is upstream of any cut-off: the readouts have no agreed sign. One went up, one went down, both are adverse, and the compound sets only overlap. A threshold rule if x > t presupposes that larger x is worse; this endpoint has not established which direction is worse for which readout.

Why that is specifically dangerous in a loop. A loop does not just classify, it optimises — it picks the next experiment to maximise something. Point an active-learning selector at T-cell proliferation and it walks uphill toward the compounds that proliferate hardest, which on this evidence are not the compounds suppressing cytokines. Fast, confident, and possibly climbing the wrong axis. If the coordination hypothesis is right, the sign of the proliferation readout may even be inverted relative to the intuitive reading.

  • Correlated blind spot (flagged, structural, unmeasured): shares a failure mode with immunotoxicity-il2-luc. Both read isolated peripheral immune cells with no lymphoid architecture and no metabolic competence — so neither can produce a T-dependent antibody response, and neither can see immunotoxicity that requires a metabolite or a germinal centre. In a battery these are closer to one vote than two. ⚠ The overlap is argued from what each method contains, not measured — no study has run both on a shared chemical set. This map therefore carries the vault's standing complaint against itself: no correlated_blind_spot row in this vault yet carries a measured agreement statistic, and this row does not either.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Battery composition (composes-with, and the blind-spot pair): immunotoxicity-il2-luc is OECD Test Guideline 444A — cytokine-promoter luciferase reporters in an immortalised human T-cell line, adopted, and framed by its own guideline as a battery component rather than a stand-alone replacement for the in-vivo immunotoxicity study. This map is the obvious partner leg: primary cells with a broader cell mix against a defined reporter line. They are also the vault's newest correlated_blind_spot pair — both are peripheral cells in suspension with no lymphoid architecture and no metabolism, so a battery of the two is nearer one vote than two on any chemical whose immunotoxicity needs either.
  • Third leg, and a different limitation: immunotoxicity-myelotoxicity-cfu-gm covers bone-marrow toxicity and is graded bench-bound: animal-input because its prediction model consumes a mouse maximum tolerated dose. So the immunotoxicity column now holds one adopted reporter line, one validated-but- animal-dependent marrow assay, and one exploratory primary-cell model — and none of the three produces an antibody. The endpoint's coverage blank is the functional antibody response itself.
  • Endpoint-coverage gap (the valuable output): no non-animal method in this vault measures a T-dependent antibody response. Added to the open gaps as plain text rather than a link, per the index contract.
  • Chemical-class edge (composes-with): PFAS appear elsewhere in the vault as a steatosis problem (hepatotoxicity-steatosis-battery, via the PFAS/transcriptomics AOP source) and as a cardiac problem in the newer literature. This map adds the immune arm. Worth stating as a live cross-endpoint observation rather than a battery: PFAS are one chemical class that lands in at least three different endpoint columns, and no defined approach in the vault integrates across columns.
