DPRA — in chemico peptide reactivity (skin sensitisation KE1) — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-05) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 442C). A component of the 2-out-of-3 defined approach, not a stand-alone test.

What we believe now

The DPRA scores the molecular initiating event (KE1) of the skin-sensitisation AOP — covalent peptide/protein binding — via HPLC depletion of cysteine and lysine peptides. It calls GHS Cat 1 sensitiser vs non-sensitiser at 80% accuracy (N=157), 80% sensitivity, 77% specificity, ~85% within / ~80% between-lab reproducibility. It is one input to the 2o3 and ITS defined approaches — used in combination, not alone. False-negative modes: pre/pro-haptens needing activation, 220-nm co-elution.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — In-chemico peptide reactivity read as HPLC % depletion against banded thresholds — chemistry, not biology, and fully deterministic.

Through-line

  • 2026-07-05 — Ingested OECD TG 442C. Coins this map as a component of the 2o3 battery (KE1 slot). 80/80/77 vs reference; adopted on both axes → consensus. Honest caveat: single-KE assay, not a stand-alone replacement.
  • 2026-07-10 — Now a scored leg of a third DIP; and the KE1 slot is no longer DPRA's alone. Status holds at consensus. skin-sensitization-potency-its-da (GL 497 Part II) converts DPRA's % depletion into a 0–3 score — banded, not binary — which is summed with a KE3 score and an in-silico score into a 0–7 potency total. Read from the 2026 guideline: the ITS bands DPRA at Cys/Lys mean depletion ≥6.38% (score 1), ≥22.62% (2), ≥42.47% (3). The same edition admits ADRA as an alternative KE1 source in both the 2o3 and the ITS. So this assay now feeds three different data-interpretation procedures at three different resolutions — binary (2o3), banded score (ITS), continuous (Part III PoD) — and can be substituted out of all three.
