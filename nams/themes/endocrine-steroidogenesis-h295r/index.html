H295R steroidogenesis assay (OECD TG 456) — endocrine / steroidogenesis — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-07) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 456), with formally-validated inter-lab reproducibility underneath (OECD validation programme Phases 1–3). Opens the endocrine / steroidogenesis endpoint — a mechanistic route into reproductive toxicity, the field's last fully-empty human-health headline blank. The caveat is scope, not status: this is a Level-2 mechanistic screen, not a definitive fertility/multigenerational reproductive test.

What we believe now

The H295R steroidogenesis assay exposes the human adrenocortical carcinoma line NCI-H295R — which carries the full steroidogenic enzyme complement and makes both androgens and estrogens — to ≥7 concentrations of a chemical for 48 h, then measures testosterone (T) and 17β-estradiol (E2) in the medium (± viability control to separate specific effects from cytotoxicity). The 2022 data-interpretation procedure calls a positive at ≥1.5-fold induction/inhibition vs solvent control. Output is a screening/prioritisation call (inducer / inhibitor / no-effect on steroidogenesis), not an apical potency or reproductive-outcome prediction. Adopted as OECD TG 456 (2011; DIP revised 2022; corrected/updated 25 June 2025), sitting at Level 2 of the OECD endocrine Conceptual Framework. Trustworthy for what it is — a mechanistic steroid-synthesis screen — and read within a battery (receptor binding/transactivation

  • 2026-07-10 — The endocrine column moves outside EATS for the first time. Status holds at consensus. endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation (OECD TG 454A, adopted 2 July 2026) is the first adopted OECD in-vitro method for a non-EATS endocrine mechanism. This map is the S of EATS (Steroidogenesis); TG 454A reads receptor transactivation at the glucocorticoid receptor — stress, inflammation, immune function, metabolism. The two are complementary Level-2 mechanism screens on different nodes and duplicate nothing. Nothing in TG 454A bears on H295R's own performance or scope.   • 2026-08-03 — Named as the Tier 1 hormone-disruption screen in the EU's flagship chemical-risk programme. Status and loop_readiness both HELD. THE PAPER REPORTS NO RESULTS — design only, results due Q1 2027 — so nothing about this assay's performance changes.   • 2026-08-07 — ⚠️ A SCOPE CLAIM THIS MAP WAS MAKING IS PUSHED BACK ON, MECHANISTICALLY. Status HELD at consensus; evidence_tier, regulatory_status and loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready ALL HELD; a domain_gate is ADDED. Ingested rehurkova-2026-testicular-steroidogenesis-in-vitro-models (Archives of Toxicology 100(8):3211–3229, PMID 42047694, open access; RECETOX / Masaryk University, a public Czech research infrastructure; funding_flag: [none] — reviews other groups' models and concludes essentially all of them are inadequate, including any the authors could have a stake in). A semi-systematic review over 1,533 studies. (1) The objection is mechanistic, not rhetorical. H295R cells "lack a fully functional LHR/LHCGR signaling pathway" — the luteinising-hormone receptor, which is how the pituitary actually instructs a testicle's Leydig cells to make testosterone. A cell that cannot receive that signal cannot be stimulated the way testicular models are, so an entire class of endocrine disruption is invisible to this assay: anything acting on the hormonal command to produce testosterone rather than on the synthesis enzymes themselves. Add the provenance: "fundamental differences between adrenal and gonadal cells, female and male physiology." h295r-cannot-receive-the-signal-that-drives-a-testicle (2) Why NOTHING downgrades. OECD Test Guideline 456 never claimed to be a male-gonadal assay. It is a Level-2 mechanistic screen for interference with steroid synthesis, validated as exactly that, with a published ≥1.5-fold call rule and a machine-readable hormone readout — all of which remain true. The rebuttal is to a USE, and the use was partly this vault's own: this map has said since 2026-07-07 that the assay opens "a mechanistic route into reproductive toxicity." That route is now qualified — adrenal, female-derived, and blind to gonadotropin signalling. Recorded as a domain_gate, which is the schema's slot for "trust the decision rule, inside the domain." (3) The loop consequence, stated because closed-loop-ready is the tier most easily misread as endorsement. An automated endocrine battery that counts TG 456 as its steroidogenesis vote is not getting a testicular-steroidogenesis vote, and a loop trusting the battery's decision rule will not discover that on its own. Same logic as the correlated-blind-spot rail, one assay short of triggering it. (4) It reinforces the fertility anti-edge rather than weakening it. This map has recorded since 2026-07-07 that four adopted endocrine screens do not add up to a fertility test. This is a fifth reason, specific to the male half: one of the four is not reading the right organ's biology. See reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams and the new reproductive-testicular-steroidogenesis-leydig-models.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — the EATS core is now three-quarters mapped, and this map is the one that is structurally different. With endocrine-er-transactivation (TG 455, E), endocrine-ar-transactivation (TG 458, A) and this map (TG 456, S), only Thyroid (T) is missing — now the endocrine column's highest-value gap. But note what separates H295R from its new siblings: they read receptor transactivation through a luciferase reporter; H295R reads actual hormone production (testosterone, 17β-estradiol) by intact steroidogenic cells. It therefore does not share the reporter-crosstalk confound documented in steroid-receptor-crosstalk-shared-reporter-confound — there is no shared response element for a competing receptor to fire. Along with endocrine-er-binding-hrer (cell-free binding), H295R is one of only two endocrine methods in the vault that is structurally immune to that confound, which makes it a genuinely independent vote in an endocrine weight-of-evidence assessment rather than a correlated one.
  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — the fertility anti-edge is reinforced, not weakened. Kumbhar 2026 names TG 456 alongside TG 455/458/493 as screens measuring "receptor activation and hormone synthesis, not fertility." Now that all four are mapped, the point this map made on 2026-07-07 stands more sharply: four adopted endocrine screens do not add up to a fertility test. See reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams.
  • DART / reproductive mechanism siblings (composes-with, NOT redundancy): three DART-relevant mechanism screens now sit in the vault reading different biology — this endocrine/steroidogenesis screen (sex-steroid synthesis), the developmental zebrafish teratogenicity assay developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity (structural malformation), and the neuro DNT-IVB battery developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb (neurodevelopmental-process disruption). They are complementary, not overlapping. Scope honesty: H295R opens the endocrine mechanism arm feeding reproductive toxicity; the definitive reproductive (fertility / multigenerational) endpoint remains unmapped (candidates in Open gaps: ER/AR transactivation TG 455/458, hESC DevTox, ReProTract, Leydig/Sertoli and follicle assays).
  • Toxicokinetic dose-anchoring (composes-with): a cell-based potency screen reports effects at nominal medium concentrations, so it composes-with the PBK/QIVIVE layer pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics for any in-vitro-to-human-dose extrapolation — the same dependency the other cell-based batteries carry.
  • Global-ripple: distinct endpoint/mechanism from the genotox maps, the skin/eye local-toxicity maps, the carcinogenicity map, and the systemic (hepatotox / repeat-dose / inhalation) maps. A coverage-breadth addition opening the endocrine column — no confirm/refute edge, one methodological-family note: like the Liver-Chip and RhE models it is a human-cell in-vitro mechanistic model, part of that technology family.

Added 2026-08-07 (the male-gonadal scope limit)

  • Sibling map coined: reproductive-testicular-steroidogenesis-leydig-models. The testis-derived model family (primary Leydig cells, MA-10, TM3, mLTC-1, R2C, BLTK-1, explants) now has its own map, emerging / automatable-open-loop, regulatory_status: none. This map and that one disagree, and the disagreement is the finding: the adopted assay is the wrong organ for male gonadal steroid synthesis, and the right-organ alternatives are 1,533 papers deep with four usable reference chemicals out of 23 and no formal validation. Neither map can be read as covering the other.
  • The endocrine column now has a named coverage hole. The adopted screens this vault maps cover receptor binding (endocrine-er-binding-hrer), receptor activation (endocrine-er-transactivation, endocrine-ar-transactivation, endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation) and adrenal-line hormone synthesis (this map). Male gonadal hormone synthesis has no adopted assay. For an automatable endocrine battery that is a coverage gap, not a redundancy — and it is the kind of gap a loop cannot detect from inside, because every assay it runs will return a valid number.
