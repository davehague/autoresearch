H295R steroidogenesis assay (OECD TG 456) — endocrine / steroidogenesis — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-07) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 456), with formally-validated inter-lab reproducibility underneath (OECD validation programme Phases 1–3). Opens the endocrine / steroidogenesis endpoint — a mechanistic route into reproductive toxicity, the field's last fully-empty human-health headline blank. The caveat is scope, not status: this is a Level-2 mechanistic screen, not a definitive fertility/multigenerational reproductive test.

What we believe now

The H295R steroidogenesis assay exposes the human adrenocortical carcinoma line NCI-H295R — which carries the full steroidogenic enzyme complement and makes both androgens and estrogens — to ≥7 concentrations of a chemical for 48 h, then measures testosterone (T) and 17β-estradiol (E2) in the medium (± viability control to separate specific effects from cytotoxicity). The 2022 data-interpretation procedure calls a positive at ≥1.5-fold induction/inhibition vs solvent control. Output is a screening/prioritisation call (inducer / inhibitor / no-effect on steroidogenesis), not an apical potency or reproductive-outcome prediction. Adopted as OECD TG 456 (2011; DIP revised 2022; corrected/updated 25 June 2025), sitting at Level 2 of the OECD endocrine Conceptual Framework. Trustworthy for what it is — a mechanistic steroid-synthesis screen — and read within a battery (receptor binding/transactivation

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — the EATS core is now three-quarters mapped, and this map is the one that is structurally different. With endocrine-er-transactivation (TG 455, E), endocrine-ar-transactivation (TG 458, A) and this map (TG 456, S), only Thyroid (T) is missing — now the endocrine column's highest-value gap. But note what separates H295R from its new siblings: they read receptor transactivation through a luciferase reporter; H295R reads actual hormone production (testosterone, 17β-estradiol) by intact steroidogenic cells. It therefore does not share the reporter-crosstalk confound documented in steroid-receptor-crosstalk-shared-reporter-confound — there is no shared response element for a competing receptor to fire. Along with endocrine-er-binding-hrer (cell-free binding), H295R is one of only two endocrine methods in the vault that is structurally immune to that confound, which makes it a genuinely independent vote in an endocrine weight-of-evidence assessment rather than a correlated one.
  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — the fertility anti-edge is reinforced, not weakened. Kumbhar 2026 names TG 456 alongside TG 455/458/493 as screens measuring "receptor activation and hormone synthesis, not fertility." Now that all four are mapped, the point this map made on 2026-07-07 stands more sharply: four adopted endocrine screens do not add up to a fertility test. See reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams.
  • DART / reproductive mechanism siblings (composes-with, NOT redundancy): three DART-relevant mechanism screens now sit in the vault reading different biology — this endocrine/steroidogenesis screen (sex-steroid synthesis), the developmental zebrafish teratogenicity assay developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity (structural malformation), and the neuro DNT-IVB battery developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb (neurodevelopmental-process disruption). They are complementary, not overlapping. Scope honesty: H295R opens the endocrine mechanism arm feeding reproductive toxicity; the definitive reproductive (fertility / multigenerational) endpoint remains unmapped (candidates in Open gaps: ER/AR transactivation TG 455/458, hESC DevTox, ReProTract, Leydig/Sertoli and follicle assays).
  • Toxicokinetic dose-anchoring (composes-with): a cell-based potency screen reports effects at nominal medium concentrations, so it composes-with the PBK/QIVIVE layer pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics for any in-vitro-to-human-dose extrapolation — the same dependency the other cell-based batteries carry.
  • Global-ripple: distinct endpoint/mechanism from the genotox maps, the skin/eye local-toxicity maps, the carcinogenicity map, and the systemic (hepatotox / repeat-dose / inhalation) maps. A coverage-breadth addition opening the endocrine column — no confirm/refute edge, one methodological-family note: like the Liver-Chip and RhE models it is a human-cell in-vitro mechanistic model, part of that technology family.
