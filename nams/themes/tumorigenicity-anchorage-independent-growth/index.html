Anchorage-independent growth (SACF / GILA) — tumorigenicity of gene-edited cells — method

Status: consolidating (2026-07-31) — 🚩 auto-coined, needs a human verdict. Four independent laboratories reproduced it closely, which is the consolidating bar; its applicability domain is one cell line and one engineered knockout, and its predictivity has never been tested. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method, regulatory_status: none, loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop. Opens a new context of use inside the existing carcinogenicity endpoint group: cell and gene therapy products, rather than chemicals.

What we believe now

Two dish assays score anchorage-independent growth — the ability of a cell to grow while attached to nothing, one of the oldest laboratory signatures of a cancer-like transformed state.

  • SACF (soft agar colony formation) — cells suspended in soft agarose for four weeks; colonies counted by high-content imaging and analysis software.
  • GILA (growth in low attachment) — two weeks on an attachment-resistant surface; survivors measured as total ATP by a bioluminescent glow.

The intended use is tumorigenicity risk assessment for CRISPR/Cas9-edited cell therapies, where the standard test is injecting the cells into immunocompromised mice and watching for six to twelve months — a test whose own human-cell engraftment rate is documented as unreliable.

The evidence is a four-laboratory HESI Global multi-site study on a spike-in model: MCF10A human breast epithelial cells seeded with a controlled fraction of cells carrying a PTPN12 tumour-suppressor knockout. Limit of detection 0.8% edited cells at most sites — roughly one transformed cell in 125. SACF inter-laboratory Spearman rank correlation 0.988–1 (intra-laboratory 0.952–1) against a stated random-data threshold of 0.64; SACF's dynamic range is ~200-fold against GILA's ~15-fold. GILA's agreement was below the noise threshold until saturating concentrations above 12.5% were excluded, after which it reached 0.9–1.

What was established is transfer, not truth. One test article, no negatives, no reference set — the study demonstrates the protocol works in four hands and makes no predictivity claim. Every experimental author works for a pharmaceutical company that would otherwise pay for the twelve-month mouse study.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — Instrument-read end to end (imaging colony count; bioluminescent ATP), plate-based, and demonstrably transferable between laboratories. No published procedure turns the number into a tumorigenicity call.

  • Blocker: nothing states what colony count in a real edited product constitutes a concern. The limit of detection is derived per site by statistical test against a spiked positive control, which exists only because this was a spike-in study; a therapeutic product arrives without one. The paper deliberately positions the assays as "development-stage characterization tools contributing to tumorigenicity evidence generation … rather than as a standalone determinant."
  • ⚠ A hand-tuned step inside the automated readout. After an incubator fault changed colony sizes at one site, the imaging acquisition and analysis settings had to be adapted, and the authors generalise: "the imaging settings used herein need to be re-evaluated for each experiment." The 0.988–1 agreement was obtained with humans tuning the analysis per experiment. → the-imaging-settings-were-retuned-for-each-experiment
  • Promotion path. A published, product-agnostic threshold (colony count per seeded cell against a defined untreated control) plus frozen analysis settings would move this map to closed-loop-ready. Neither exists.
  • ⚠ Loop-ready is not endorsement, and here the caution is sharp: a machine could run this assay end to end today and it has never been shown to predict a tumour in anything.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Same endpoint, two loop tiers apart (added 2026-07-31). carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay (SHE and Bhas 42 morphological transformation) has better regulatory standing — two OECD Guidance Documents, an EURL ECVAM/ESAC-endorsed validation — and sits at human-in-the-loop, because its transformed foci are judged by eye and the guidance names that subjectivity as a known weakness. This map has no regulatory standing at all and is two tiers higher, purely because a colony is counted and a focus is judged. The axes disagree cleanly, in both directions, on one endpoint.
  • ⚠ Correlated blind spot with that map, and the shared cause is the concept, not the cell. Both measure transformation phenotype in a cultured cell line — anchorage-independent growth here, morphological focus formation there. Neither has an immune system, a stroma, a blood supply or time. A transformed phenotype that a body would clear, and a genuine carcinogenic mechanism that needs tissue architecture to express, are invisible to both. In a weight-of-evidence package that cites both, their agreement is close to one vote. For a loop: an automated system reading concordance between two transformation assays as corroboration is compounding a shared architectural gap, fast.
  • ⚠ And the rail fires INSIDE this map. SACF and GILA measure the same property — anchorage-independent growth — in the same cell line under the same manipulation, differing only in the substrate and the readout chemistry. Running both is close to one measurement reported twice. Same shape as the two five-readout single-cell-line batteries the vault coined on 2026-07-28.
  • Composes-with the genotoxicity column, and does not duplicate it. genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation and genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus detect DNA damage from chemicals. Neither addresses the question here, which is whether a deliberate edit at an unintended site produced a growth-competent cell.
  • Reference-standard tension, shared with validation-paradigm-nam-confidence. The in-vivo mouse test this method is an alternative to is also the reference this method would be scored against, and it is documented as unreliable in the same paper. The vault holds the mirror image on 2026-07-29, where the EU's validation laboratory had to build a reference chemical list by expert committee because the animal endocrine tests could not supply one.
