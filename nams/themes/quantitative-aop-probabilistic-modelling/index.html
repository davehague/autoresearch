Flagged for human review in docs/open-gaps-log.md. Git is the undo.

What we believe now

An adverse outcome pathway (AOP) is toxicology's causal diagram: a chemical perturbs a molecule (the molecular initiating event), which changes something in a cell, then in a tissue, ending in visible harm (the adverse outcome). The intermediate steps are key events. AOPs are chemical-agnostic — they describe the road, not the traffic — and in their ordinary form they are qualitative: boxes and arrows.

A quantitative AOP (qAOP) puts numbers on the arrows. How much of key event 1 produces how much of key event 2, and therefore how much of a measurement in a dish implies how much risk of the actual disease.

That object is the vault's single largest missing piece, described from the other direction. Nineteen maps sit at automatable-open-loop because they produce clean instrument numbers that no published procedure converts into a hazard call. A working qAOP is such a procedure — one that was fitted rather than voted on.

The first entrant, and its shape

Durnik et al. 2026 build a Bayesian qAOP for liver fibrosis — the scarring that follows sustained liver injury. The cells that do the scarring are hepatic stellate cells, normally quiet fat-storing cells that switch into scar-producing mode under repeated damage; αSMA and COL1A1 (the gene for collagen's main building block) are the standard markers of that switch. The model returns "the probability of the expression fold change" for those two markers "given the effects on tissue injury", and from that estimates a COL1A1 fold-change range associated with high fibrosis odds.

Two properties matter more than the endpoint:

(1) The training material is the published record. Inputs came from the in-vitro toxicology literature and from Open TG-GATEs, a large public Japanese toxicogenomics database. No ring trial. No committee-assembled reference chemical set. The rule was mined, not commissioned — which is the only one of the three known routes to a decision rule that a machine could execute. → a-decision-rule-assembled-from-other-peoples-published-data

(2) Continuous variables, not bins. The stated advance over earlier qAOPs is modelling key events as continuous quantities rather than chopping them into low/medium/high buckets first. Binning destroys exactly the gradation an optimiser needs to distinguish a small effect from a large one. → a-continuous-probability-is-not-a-hazard-class

⚠ What it is not

It is not a hazard classification. The model outputs a probability over a continuous fold change and an associated "biologically relevant range." Somewhere between that range and a regulatory decision sits a cut-off, and nobody has set it. The committee step is displaced, not removed.

This lands the map in the same position the vault assigned phenotypic-profiling-cell-painting on 2026-07-26 — good for the exploration leg of a loop (which experiment next, how much uncertainty remains), not yet an answer for the decision leg (what does a regulator receive). Two methods have now arrived at that position independently, from opposite directions: one with an endpoint and no rule for converting it, one with a rule-shaped output and no endpoint.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: computational-connective — scored off the ladder. This is not a physical assay; it is the model layer a closed loop would update, which is the definition of the tier.

Its relationship to the loop is unusually direct, and worth stating carefully because it cuts both ways:

  • The upside. Every other computational-connective map in the vault answers a bridging question — what would the dose be in a human (pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics), what would a related chemical do (in-silico-qsar-read-across), what happened between the instrument and the number (nam-data-processing-pipeline), was the modelling reported well enough to reproduce (ml-model-reporting-reproducibility). This one answers the decision question itself, in a form that is differentiable in spirit and updatable by construction — a Bayesian model is designed to absorb the next experiment.
  • The downside, and it is the vault's own rail. A rule fitted to the published literature inherits that literature's systematic properties — favoured cell lines, favoured concentration ranges, the documented preference for publishing positive results — as if they were biology. And the failure is invisible in the usual way: a rule fitted to the literature will agree with the literature, and that agreement is what the failure looks like. Same structure as models-trained-on-hit-calls-learn-the-assay (2026-07-27), one layer up: there, a hazard model learned how the assay behaved; here, a decision rule could learn how the field published.

No performance number is reported — no accuracy, no calibration, no held-out test, no independent-dataset comparison. A Bayesian model with no reported calibration is a hypothesis about a rule, not a rule. Nothing here promotes any method map.

  • Composes-with the two AOP-designed batteries, and completes their design pattern. nephrotoxicity-ciptec-aop-battery (2026-07-28) and hepatotoxicity-cholestasis-battery (2026-07-28) both chose their key events from a published adverse outcome pathway rather than from assay availability — a pattern two independent groups landed on in one night. Both then stopped at the same place: measurements against a pathway, and no rule for combining them. The nephrotoxicity authors declined to publish a rule on principle. This map is the missing half of that design pattern — the thing that turns "we measured five key events of an AOP" into "and therefore the odds of the adverse outcome are this." Cross-linked in both directions.
  • Composes-with pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics — and the pairing is what a loop would actually need. A qAOP says what a given in-vitro concentration implies about harm; QIVIVE says what human dose produces that in-vitro concentration. Neither alone gets from a plate to a decision. Tonight's other ingest (jackson-2026-dnt-ivb-contextualize-in-vivo-apical) is a worked case where the toxicokinetic half was load-bearing and the qAOP half was absent — the call was made by expert judgement in the gap.
  • Composes-with hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps and hepatotoxicity-cholestasis-battery on the liver. Liver fibrosis is a distinct injury from cholestasis (bile flow failure) and from acute drug-induced liver injury, so this is a coverage addition to the hepatotoxicity group rather than a competitor to either map. No status ripple.
  • Extends in-silico-qsar-read-across without duplicating it. Both are model-layer objects and they consume different things: (Q)SAR predicts hazard from chemical structure; a qAOP predicts an outcome from measured biology. On 2026-07-29 Tox21's expanded panel scored 0.74/0.82 AUC-ROC on liver and heart injury against 0.73/0.80 from structure alone — a result that prices every wet method against a cheap structural comparator. A qAOP is not exposed to that comparison in the same way, because its inputs are mechanistic measurements rather than a fingerprint; whether it beats structure alone is unknown and untested, and is exactly the head-to-head the vault logged as missing on 2026-07-29.
