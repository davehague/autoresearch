CYP induction in differentiated human hepatic cells (OECD TG 445A) — biotransformation / metabolism — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-10) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 445A, published 2 July 2026). Adoption is what earns consensus (deterministic per the evidence model), and underneath it sits formally-validated reproducibility from an independent EURL ECVAM validation (2014) with ESAC peer review (October 2014). The interval between those two facts — twelve years — is this map's most transferable finding.

What we believe now

TG 445A measures whether a chemical induces the catalytic activity of three inducible human cytochrome P450 enzymes — CYP1A2, CYP2B6 and CYP3A4 — in cryopreserved, differentiated HepaRG™ human hepatic cells. Induction is initiated when a ligand binds one of three nuclear receptors: AhR (→ CYP1A family), CAR (→ CYP2B), PXR (→ CYP3A). Results are expressed as n-fold induction against vehicle control, and the prediction model is a four-way call: Positive (≥2-fold at ≥2 concentrations), Equivocal (≥2-fold at one concentration), Borderline positive (≥2-fold only at the highest concentration), Negative (all other cases).

The protocol reads enzyme activity, not mRNA, and says why: "Measuring mRNA does not necessarily" track induced catalytic activity. It also states the human-relevance argument negatively — rodent AhR, CAR and PXR differ from human in ligand specificity and in chemical potency, so "species differences in liver metabolism present a challenge when extrapolating to humans." This method removes the extrapolation. It does not put an adverse-outcome call in its place.

This is the vault's first adopted in-vitro biotransformation method, and it opens the metabolism/biotransformation column. Scope is a mechanistic call about metabolic competence and its perturbation, plus a regulator-recognised drug–drug interaction input (US FDA 2017; EMA 2012). The protocol names the adversities induction can drive — "inflammation, cholestasis, steatosis, hepatotoxicity and carcinogenesis and thyroid disruption" — but the assay predicts none of them. No sensitivity/specificity or apical-predictivity figures appear in the sources reached.

Access is a real constraint, recorded not laundered. HepaRG™ is a patented cell line (PCT/FR02/02391, 8 July 2002) licensed to Biopredic International, which supplied cells, medium and supplements in every cited validation study. The protocol notes "other cell line providers are currently available." An adopted, freely-citable method can still be gated behind a commercial supplier — the vault now holds three instances of this pattern (see connect, below).

Loop readiness

  • Access gate: HepaRG(TM) is patented, single-licensor — bears on whether the loop can be stood up outside the lab that built it.

Two ingests, one conclusion. (1) ali-2026-chemically-defined-media-heparg-huh7 (Arch. Toxicol., DOI 10.1007/s00204-026-04502-z, PMID 42446672; TU Berlin + the German Federal Institute for Risk Assessment, funding_flag: none) sequenced this guideline's cell line in a chemically defined medium against the usual fetal-bovine-serum medium. HepaRG turned up exactly the programmes TG 445A exists to interrogate — xenobiotic metabolism, response to xenobiotic stimulus, and transport — and on hepatic-identity and detoxification gene panels sat closer to real pooled primary human hepatocytes than serum-grown HepaRG did. The comparison line HuH7 responded through sterol biosynthesis and stress instead, with "limited improvement." Nothing moves. This is messenger RNA, not enzyme activity: no CYP1A2/2B6/3A4 catalytic measurement and no n-fold induction figure was produced, so the guideline's actual readout was never run in either medium. Directional signal, not validation. (2) ⚠ THE RAIL, NOW WITH A NUMBER. The same paper finds cell-line identity is the dominant source of transcriptomic variance in hepatic models — ≈12,600–14,100 genes between lines or against primary hepatocytes, against 1,000–2,300 for swapping the entire culture medium. This map has carried a correlated_blind_spot against hepatotoxicity-cholestasis-battery since 2026-07-28 on the argument that a shared cell line is a shared substrate. The argument now has a measurement behind it, and the measurement says the shared substrate is the largest term. Tonight also opens hepatotoxicity-steatosis-battery on the same line, so three of the vault's four hepatic maps run on HepaRG. A hepatic battery composing them reads one cell line's biology three times and counts it as three independent measurements; anything HepaRG expresses atypically is wrong in all three, in the same direction, and they will agree with each other while being wrong. For a loop that is the worst version of the rail — concordance read as corroboration, decision rule trusted, error compounded at speed. (3) A quieter loop point. A chemically defined medium makes the culture input machine-checkable: every component a named molecule at a stated concentration, so a loop can assert its input contract instead of assuming it. Two laboratories running TG 445A on licensed HepaRG in different serum lots are running the same guideline on measurably different cells. The 3Rs argument for defined media and the automation argument for defined media are the same argument, and neither literature cites the other — the second time the vault has recorded that convergence (first: 2026-07-20). → chemically-defined-medium-moves-heparg-toward-primary-hepatocytes · cell-line-identity-outweighs-culture-medium-in-hepatic-transcriptomes

This vault records the shared metabolic gap as the cause in four of seven rows of the correlated-blind-spot rail; this is the first source showing a real regulatory-facing programme designing around it from the start. Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Dose bridge — the strongest edge in the vault (composes-with): pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics (OECD GD 331) is the toxicokinetic hub that turns an in-vitro bioactive concentration into a human-equivalent dose. PBK models require metabolic clearance and enzyme-induction parameters as inputs. TG 445A is the first adopted OECD method that generates one of those inputs. Where every other method in this vault consumes the PBK bridge, this one feeds it. That is a different kind of edge, and it is why a metabolism column matters more than its endpoint coverage suggests.
  • Same tissue, opposite question (composes-with, not redundant): hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps reads apical liver injury (DILI) in a perfused organ-on-chip; TG 445A reads a mechanistic metabolic perturbation in a static hepatic monolayer. Both are human liver in-vitro models; neither substitutes for the other. Note the technology contrast that matters for the two axes: the Liver-Chip has a real apical predictivity number (87% sens / 100% spec on a blinded 27-drug panel) and is not adopted; TG 445A has no predictivity number and is adopted. Read together they are the vault's sharpest illustration that the axes are orthogonal, not correlated. The Liver-Chip map also names HepaRG among the hepatic models to watch — the same line, here in its adopted role.
  • Mechanistic upstream of carcinogenicity and thyroid disruption (open edge, deliberately not adopted): the protocol names carcinogenesis and thyroid disruption among induction-driven adversities. The vault holds carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay (non-genotoxic carcinogens — the class the genotox battery misses; CAR/PXR activation is a classical non-genotoxic rodent-liver-tumour mechanism). This is a plausible mechanistic bridge, flagged not asserted — no source read in this run tests it. Logged as an open gap.
  • Adopted-but-commercially-gated (structural pattern, third instance): with skin-sensitization-potency-its-da (Derek Nexus, license-gated) and skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435 (Corrositex®, one vendor's kit). Three adopted OECD methods whose scientific standing is unimpeachable and whose practical accessibility is bounded by a commercial licence. This is a third axis the vault's two-axis model does not currently capture, and it is now visible enough to name.
  • ⚠️ CORRELATED BLIND SPOT — added 2026-07-28, and it is a first for this map. hepatotoxicity-cholestasis-battery, coined tonight, measures five cholestasis key events and measures all five in HepaRG — the same immortalised human liver line this adopted Test Guideline runs in. TG 445A's own protocol names hepatotoxicity, cholestasis and steatosis among the adversities that enzyme induction can drive, so these two are natural battery partners, and that is precisely why the shared cell line matters. A chemical whose effect depends on something HepaRG expresses atypically is missed by the induction assay and by all five cholestasis readouts, and the battery reports six-way concordant safety. Six measurements, one cell line, one vote. The vault's remedy is unchanged: the fix for a correlated blind spot is a measurement that fails differently — a second cell system, or primary hepatocytes from several donors — not a bigger panel. Recorded on both maps. Nothing demotes: this is a statement about battery composition, and TG 445A's own standing as an adopted, closed-loop-ready method for its own question is untouched. → a-battery-inside-one-cell-line-is-not-five-independent-votes
