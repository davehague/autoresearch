What we believe now

The AOP-Wiki is the public, OECD-endorsed database where Adverse Outcome Pathways (AOPs — documented causal chains from a chemical's first molecular interaction to a health outcome) are curated and stored. Every AOP-based battery-design decision this vault has recorded — the kidney and cholestasis batteries' Key Event selection, quantitative-aop-probabilistic-modelling's attempt to put numbers on the arrows — has implicitly relied on this database being complete and consistently structured, without this vault previously treating the database's own curation process as something with a bottleneck of its own.

AOP-Wiki EMOD 3.0 (Hench et al. 2026, arXiv preprint) proposes a third-generation data model for that database: restructuring entries to be FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) and explicitly AI-readable, strengthening the structural links between AOP Key Events and the NAMs that test them, and using agentic AI — software agents that read source literature and populate the Wiki's structured fields themselves, rather than relying solely on human curators — as the curation mechanism. The stated destination is quantitative AOPs (qAOPs), for which a consistently structured, machine-readable knowledge base is a prerequisite.

What is not yet known. No quantitative result for either the data-model restructuring or the agentic curation pipeline's accuracy was recoverable from the abstract-level material this run's tooling could read. This is a proposal, evaluated (per its own title) by a "content evaluation framework" whose specific metrics and scores against real AOP-Wiki content were not accessible this run.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: computational-connective — first-match on the deterministic ladder: this is knowledge infrastructure, not a physical assay, and is scored off the loop ladder the same way nam-data-processing-pipeline and the vault's (Q)SAR/read-across and PBK/QIVIVE entries are.

Why it belongs in the vault anyway. A closed loop that updates its own decision rule needs some structured account of the causal pathway it is testing. A knowledge base only a human curator can keep current is a bottleneck on how fast that reasoning layer can grow — a knowledge-layer cousin of the hardware "reality gap" automation-self-driving-labs already tracks for physical instruments (unilabos-hardware-interoperability-kernel).
