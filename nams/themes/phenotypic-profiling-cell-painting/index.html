Phenotypic profiling / Cell Painting — broad bioactivity profiling — method

🚩 NEW MAP, auto-coined 2026-07-26 — and it opens a new endpoint group, broad-bioactivity-profiling. Every other method map in this vault is built to answer one named toxicological question. This one is built to answer none of them and produce a point of departure anyway. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md. Git is the undo.

Status: consolidating (2026-07-26). Over 1000 chemicals tested, multi-site reproducibility reported, consortium-scale datasets (JUMP, OASIS) covering "hundreds of thousands of small molecules, industrial / agricultural chemicals or genetic perturbations", and no live rebuttal. Not consensus — adoption is what earns consensus, and the source states plainly that "Standard CP would need to formally demonstrate their applicability for regulatory purposes in comprehensive validation studies, a first key step towards their implementation as an OECD TG." evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method, not formally-validated, and the reason is one clause in the reproducibility claim — see below. regulatory_status: none.

What we believe now

Cell Painting inverts how a toxicology assay is built. Every other method here starts with an endpoint — is this a skin sensitiser? an eye irritant? a mutagen? — and is meaningless against any other question. Cell Painting starts with the cell.

Cells are stained with a fixed panel of fluorescent dyes marking different compartments — nucleus, mitochondria, cytoskeleton, membrane, endoplasmic reticulum — imaged by automated high-content microscopy, and reduced by machine learning to a morphological fingerprint: hundreds to thousands of numbers describing how the cell's shape and internal organisation changed. Run a concentration series and the point at which the fingerprint departs from control is a point of departure, derived without naming a mechanism.

"Phenotypic profiling is an emerging in vitro method in which cells are fluorescently labelled to visualise the morphological changes that a perturbagen (such as a chemical compound) induces … the generated microscopy images are used to extract many different image features using Machine Learning (ML) approaches … and more recently using DL."

Multi-site reproducibility"a robust and reproducible method with high and cross-platform interoperability, even if each laboratory needed to adjust some assay parameters"
Regulatory statusnone; comprehensive validation studies named as the prerequisite for a Test Guideline

Read the 68% honestly. "Similar or more conservative" for two-thirds of chemicals means the cell-based number was in the right place or erred toward caution. It also means that for roughly a third it did not — and the source does not say which way those went, on which chemicals, or against which animal studies. A third being less conservative would be a serious problem; a third being much more conservative would be a different and milder one. The source does not distinguish them. The vault records the number as reported and builds nothing on it.

Read the reproducibility claim honestly too, because it sets the tier. "Reproducible … even if each laboratory needed to adjust some assay parameters" describes a method that works everywhere after local tuning. The rung that separates formally-validated from peer-reviewed-method on this vault's ladder is inter-laboratory reproducibility — working on unfamiliar equipment in unfamiliar hands without adjustment. That is exactly the property eight-methods-that-broke-when-a-second-lab-ran-them (ingested the same night) says is the only one that catches the failures nobody predicted. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop. Machine-readable readout, no fixed data-interpretation procedure.

  • Everything about the generation half is machine-shaped. Fixed dye panel, automated microscope, feature extraction by trained model, no human scoring anywhere in the chain. It already runs at consortium scale. As a data generator it may be the most loop-native method in this vault.
  • Blocker: there is no decision rule, and unlike the vault's other 14 open-loop maps, nobody is writing one. The other fourteen are assays with a defined regulatory question awaiting a published procedure — a domain_gate away from promotion. Here the absence is architectural: the method does not target a classification, so there is nothing for a data-interpretation procedure to interpret into. A fingerprint is a description, not a verdict.
  • ⚠ And this is the interesting half. A profiling method that yields a point of departure without needing an endpoint is, structurally, an objective function — precisely the scalar that the-loop-closes-where-the-objective-is-a-number identifies as the property that let a closed loop work in protein synthesis and that hazard classification does not supply. Cell Painting is the closest thing in this vault to a machine-computable "is something happening here?" signal. What it cannot do is tell you whether the something is harmful. It is a candidate for the exploration leg of a loop, not the decision leg — and confusing the two would be exactly the error shared/evidence-model.md warns about when it says loop-ready is never an endorsement.
  • domain_gate — and note where it points. Every named source of variability (cell density, DMSO percentage, dye stability, microscope model, analysis software) is a property of the laboratory, not of the chemical. This is the vault's first domain_gate that constrains the operator rather than the test item, and it is a level-zero/level-II problem in the sense of nam-data-processing-pipeline.

Ingested zhang-2026-cp-agent-cell-painting-moa-reasoning (Zhang et al., CP-Agent, arXiv:2606.03435, stated as an ICLR 2026 conference paper; University of Hong Kong with two of six authors at the Nvidia AI Technology Center — funding_flag: [industry]). Retrospective, on ~1.9 million image-and-context pairs from three public archives (BBBC021, CPJUMP1, RxRx3). (1) The perception layer, measured, and general-purpose models fail it outright. Asked which compound produced a Cell Painting image, "All models fell below random baseline, except for Grok-4, which slightly exceeded it", with "near-zero F1 scores"; they were merely weak on the easier context tasks (F1 0.526 cell line, 0.628 channel). A purpose-trained encoder fitted to 1,846,436 pairs reached F1 0.896. This map's "no human scoring anywhere in the chain" now needs a qualifier — the chain contains a trained model doing the seeing, and the untrained version is worse than chance. (2) The reasoning layer does not close the blocker, and the reason is structural. The pipeline's first stage retrieves the "Most Matched Experimental Context", which includes the compound's known mechanism of action; the reports then open "The specimen is hypothesized to reflect microtubule stabilization by taxol … Below, I assess whether the observed morphology aligns with this hypothesis." The mechanism is an input. A decision rule has to run unknown-chemical-in, verdict-out. (3) The evaluation measures plausibility, not correctness: 11 experts rated 40 reports (10 image pairs × 4 models) on a 1–7 scale across ten language- and reasoning-quality criteria. No accuracy figure exists, because on all ten pairs the answer was supplied. (4) What it does establish: feature ranking was stable across repeated runs, and the learned embeddings showed monotonic dose-response structure — which supports the exploration-leg candidacy this map already flags, and not the decision leg. (5) Zero-shot generalisation is reported as cosine similarity 0.432 vs 0.286 for baseline CLIP — an embedding-geometry measure, not an accuracy. → frontier-multimodal-models-score-below-chance-on-a-cell-painting-plate · an-agent-that-explains-the-answer-it-was-handed

Added 2026-08-03 — the decision leg, attempted

Composes-with automation-self-driving-labs, and the edge is new. This map's blocker and that map's emptiest component are the same hole seen from two sides: here it is "no rule converts the fingerprint into a call"; there it is "the decision leg has an architecture and no error rate." CP-Agent is the first artefact the vault holds that sits in both — an agentic pipeline built specifically to interpret this assay's output. It does not close the hole, and reading why is the useful part: it was given the mechanism and asked to justify it.

Added 2026-08-12 — the same technique lands on an adopted guideline's cell line, and imports this map's blocker with it

Ripple only. Status HELD at consolidating; loop_readiness HELD at automatable-open-loop; no field changes; no source filed here. tome-2026-high-content-imaging-rtgill-w1 (Environ. Sci. Technol. 60:21402-21416, PMID 42579109) applies image-based phenotypic profiling — the identical philosophy this map holds, multiple organelle dyes plus automated microscopy plus dimensionality reduction — to RTgill-W1, the rainbow trout gill cell line behind OECD Test Guideline 249. Ninety-four morphological features resolved a herbicide's concentration- and time-dependent response, with lysosomal redistribution and mitochondrial fragmentation as early sub-lethal indicators, and the pipeline proved robust to processing choices.

⚠ The instructive part is the contrast with the assay it sits beside, and it belongs on this map because it generalises this map's blocker. ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line is closed-loop-ready for a reason that has nothing to do with biology: an instrument-read output paired with a written, deterministic rule — take the lowest of three viability midpoints. Bolt a phenotypic profile onto the same cells and the new readout arrives with no rule at all, exactly as this map's does. This map's loop_blocker records that its missing decision rule is a consequence of the method's design rather than a gap in a guideline. Tonight is the evidence that the property travels with the technique rather than with the endpoint — move phenotypic profiling onto an adopted guideline's cell line and the blocker moves with it.
