Reagent provenance — the animal inside the non-animal method — input-quality map

🚩 CONVENTION DEPARTURE — flagged for human review. Not a method map, not a framework map, not an infrastructure map. An input-quality map: its subject is neither an assay nor a regulator but the material supply chain every wet assay in this vault silently depends on. Its endpoint (method-input-quality) is not a toxicological endpoint. The vault's fifth non-method map, and the closest sibling is ml-model-reporting-reproducibility — which is also a quality record of a whole class of methods rather than a method. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md. Git is the undo.

Status: emerging (2026-07-20). One source, a review, no primary data → every concept strength: suggestive. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method records the standing of the sourcing instrument only; the vault's ladder still has no rung for a review — the seventh instance of that schema gap.

Why this map exists

Every method in this vault is here because it replaces an animal. Nobody had checked what is still inside them.

Spänle, Haiber & Seeger read the materials lists of the seven human-cell-based assays in the developmental neurotoxicity in-vitro battery. All seven have removed fetal bovine serum (FBS) — the component everyone knows about, the one Good Cell Culture Practice names. All seven still contain animal-derived material.

What we believe now

The finding is a variability channel, not an ethics complaint — though the paper makes both cases and the ethical one is real.

"Animal-derived matrices introduce significant limitations, including batch-to-batch compositional and mechanical variability and undefined growth factor content, which can confound lineage commitment and reproducibility."

Undefined growth factor content means the plate contains biologically active molecules nobody has enumerated, at concentrations nobody has measured, varying by lot. In a differentiation assay — which most of these are — that is not an impurity; it is an uncontrolled arm of the experiment.

The guidance gap: OECD GIVIMP (Good In Vitro Method Practices) and GCCP (Good Cell Culture Practice) "promote replacing undefined components such as FBS, but ECM and BSA are less explicitly addressed." EURL ECVAM encourages animal-free antibodies "where scientifically feasible." The authors ask for the guidance to name ECM and BSA alongside serum, and for manufacturers to standardise the labels "serum-free," "animal-free," "xeno-free," "chemically defined" — which today do not mean what a buyer assumes.

The fixes exist and are unevenly priced. Recombinant human serum albumin costs €0.0022 more per 5 mL of medium than BSA — effectively free. Recombinant laminin runs ~2× (LN521 €622/mg vs EHS laminin €296/mg). Synthetic PEG hydrogels and recombinant phage-display antibodies are available. But: "Current evidence is largely based on proof-of-concept studies, and systematic comparisons between animal-derived and fully defined alternatives across DNT-IVB endpoints remain scarce," and swapping is not consequence-free — "Changes in baseline phenotype, sensitivity, or readouts, have been reported even when technical adaptation is successful." Removing the mouse tumour may change the answer, and nobody has systematically measured by how much. The real barrier is revalidation effort and incumbency, not price.

And the field's knowledge of the problem is structurally suppressed: "Potential limitations such as reduced cell adhesion or altered differentiation efficiency are likely underreported, as negative or non-optimizing results are rarely published."

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: not-applicable — this map grades no assay. It records something that sits underneath the axis: what the assays are made of.

But the loop consequence is the reason the map exists, and it is a vault inference.

⚠️ The source never mentions AI, automation, closed loops or active learning. This reading is the vault's.

A closed loop's founding assumption is that a change in the readout means a change in the chemistry. An undeclared lot-to-lot shift in a mouse-tumour extract violates that assumption silently. A human operator eventually acquires the folklore — the cells went strange after the new Matrigel lot — and discounts it. An active-learning loop has no representation for reagent lot at all: it sees a shifted dose–response, treats it as information, and updates on it. Speed converts an unmodelled input into a confidently learned artefact, which is the blind-spot rail's mechanism arriving through the supply chain instead of through a battery.

The remedy the paper argues for on 3Rs grounds — chemically defined components — is also exactly what would make the input contract machine-checkable. Two literatures, one remedy, no citation between them.

(1) The measurement. On 2026-07-20 this map recorded that "systematic comparisons between animal-derived and fully defined alternatives … remain scarce" and that removing the animal component may change the answer with nobody having measured by how much. For hepatic models, here is the number. Swapping fetal bovine serum for a chemically defined medium changed 2,272 genes in HepaRG cells and 1,017 in HuH7 (adjusted P < 0.05, |log2 fold-change| > 0.5). Real, not negligible. (2) ⚠ AND IT IS SECOND-ORDER. The contrast between cell lines, and between either line and real primary human hepatocytes, runs to ≈12,600–14,100 genes — 6× to 14× the medium effect. Cell identity is the primary driver of transcriptomic variance; medium is a model-dependent secondary effect. The authors call this "identity-first, medium-modulated" and explicitly decline the alarming reading: adopting defined media is "not … a disruptive break from serum-based literature, but rather a controlled refinement." The 2026-07-20 concern survives, resized. The variability channel is real and its rank is lower than the decision — which cell line — that every assay makes silently before the reagent question is ever asked. (3) The direction is model-dependent, which is worse for a loop than random noise. In HepaRG the defined medium lifted xenobiotic metabolism and transport programmes — the machinery deciding whether a chemical is activated or detoxified. In HuH7 it lifted sterol biosynthesis and stress responses instead. A systematic, model-specific shift does not average out across a campaign the way random error does. (4) ⚠ THE INPUT A LOOP CAN NEVER LEARN ABOUT. A loop never varies its cell line. Model identity is fixed on day one, contributes the largest share of the variance, and therefore never appears in the loop's data at all. This is the 2026-07-30 generalisability finding arriving through the cell bank: one laboratory running very fast produces structurally zero evidence about the input that matters most, because it holds it constant. Not a defect of any platform — it is what a closed loop is. (5) A caveat the vault must carry with the number. The defined media were each cell-line-optimised, so this is not clean serum-removal: "medium-associated effects cannot be attributed solely to the absence of serum." And "'CDM' formulations are not interchangeable." n = 3, one laboratory, two cell lines, one tissue — nothing here licenses the same conclusion for the neural assays that raised the question. → cell-line-identity-outweighs-culture-medium-in-hepatic-transcriptomes · chemically-defined-medium-moves-heparg-toward-primary-hepatocytes
