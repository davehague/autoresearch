2-out-of-3 defined approach (skin sensitisation) — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-04) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted. The clearest "this NAM is ready" case in the vault: a fixed combination of three individually adopted assays, codified in OECD Guideline 497.

Sub-threads: none — one argument, held together by the object at its centre: OECD Guideline 497's data-interpretation procedure and the admissible information sources it names. Every arrival is a claim about that same object — how well it predicts, on which chemicals, under which substitutions, and where its concordance means less than it looks. The pro-hapten and mixtures questions are not separate strands; both are boundaries of the same rule's applicability, argued in the same terms. [reviewed 2026-08-27 @ 10 bullets]

What we believe now

For skin-sensitisation hazard (sensitiser vs non-sensitiser), the 2-out-of-3 (2o3) defined approach — two concordant results among DPRA, KeratinoSens, and h-CLAT — is an OECD-adopted replacement for the animal LLNA within its chemical applicability domain. It predicts human outcomes at 72–97% balanced accuracy, 84–97% sensitivity, 50–100% specificity. High sensitivity (rarely misses a sensitiser) is the strength; specificity is the soft axis (as low as 50% — it can over-call non-sensitisers). It is a hazard call; GHS potency sub-categorisation is a separate DA (the ITS) in the same guideline.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — Three machine-readable component assays (HPLC peptide depletion, luciferase, flow cytometry) feeding a data-interpretation procedure the guideline itself calls "a transparent, rule-based approach requiring no expert judgment." The vault's flagship loop-ready case.

  • Domain gate: chemical applicability domain of GL 497 — a loop must check the applicability domain before trusting the decision rule.

Swapping the key-event-3 assay from the h-CLAT to U-SENS™ (skin-sensitization-u-sens, coined this run) gives 71% balanced accuracy against the mouse LLNA (n=168) and 77% against human data (n=66) — at or below the bottom of this map's incumbent 72–97% human-data range. It now needs the qualifier that a component's threshold has a measured band (128–176% stimulation index) in which applying the rule is uninformative, holding 15–17% of chemicals — and the procedure contains no representation of that band.   • 2026-08-20 — ⚠ THE FLAGSHIP'S DECISION RULE WAS VALIDATED ON ONE CHEMICAL AT A TIME, AND A CONSORTIUM WITH REGULATORS AT THE TABLE SAYS SO. Status HELD at consensus. evidence_tier and regulatory_status HELD. loop_readiness HELD at closed-loop-ready — deliberately, see (3). domain_gate MOVED. Ingested krzykwa-2026-botanicals-dermal-nams-mixtures (Pharmaceutical Biology 64(1):922–948, DOI 10.1080/13880209.2026.2717803, PMID 42610438, published online 18 August 2026; gold open access, full text read). The Dermal Working Group of the Botanical Safety Consortium, which exists under a Memorandum of Understanding between the US FDA, NIEHS and HESI (MOU 225-19-032) with NIEHS intramural support. funding_flag: [industry] — six authors are employed by personal-care or dietary-supplement companies, a contract research organisation that sells this testing is a participant, and direct funding comes from an industry consortium's corporate sponsors.

    (1) What a botanical is, and why it breaks the assumption. A botanical is a plant-derived ingredient — an extract, an essential oil, ground plant material — and it is not a chemical. It is a variable mixture of hundreds of things whose composition shifts with growing conditions, cultivar, plant part and extraction route. The paper's own example: bergamot oil in two lots, one cold-pressed (retaining the light-activated furanocoumarins) and one steam-distilled (which strips them). Same name on the label, different hazard in the bottle.

    (2) ⚠ The finding. "Because these approaches were primarily developed and validated using single chemicals, the extent to which concordant results across key events support hazard identification for complex botanical mixtures remains an important area of investigation." The 2-out-of-3 rule is a function over three binary inputs and nothing else. It cannot ask whether two agreeing assays agreed for a reason connected to sensitisation or because they share an interference — and for a mixture that question is live. Each component hits a stated bound: DPRA cannot be made up to its prescribed 100 mM stock without a molecular weight (gravimetric workaround with "preliminary success"), and tannins and other plant phenolics deplete its peptides non-specifically, which is the assay's own positive signal; KeratinoSens is interfered with at the readout rather than the biology, since phytoestrogens at high concentration inhibit the luciferase enzyme per Test Guideline 442D itself; h-CLAT may false-negative above log Kow 3.5 per Test Guideline 442E, and essential oils are exactly that lipophilicity class. Published discordance runs both ways on the same assay — Kolle 2023 saw botanical extracts induce CD54 where five were non-sensitisers in vivo, Shiraishi 2021 saw propolis negative in h-CLAT but a moderate sensitiser in the mouse lymph-node assay. → the-two-out-of-three-rule-was-built-for-one-chemical-at-a-time

    (3) Why loop_readiness HOLDS, and why that is the honest call rather than the flattering one. The readouts are still instrument-read. The rule is still published, fixed and deterministic. No human judgment step is introduced, so demoting the axis for a finding about what the rule was validated on would be inferring one axis from another — the thing this vault exists not to do. What moves is the domain_gate, and the consequence is stated exactly: a machine could run this battery on a plant extract today, execute correctly, apply the rule correctly, and return a category whose meaning has not been established. The rule does not know what it was validated on. This is the cleanest example the vault holds of closed-loop-ready not being an endorsement.

    (4) ⚠⚠ A SECOND correlated-blind-spot route on this battery — different mechanism, and this one has a citation. The 2026-08-01 connect pass flagged the pre-hapten / pro-hapten metabolic gap here as a vault inference, with correlated_blind_spot deliberately unset pending a source. This is not that finding, and it does not close it. It is a second one in the same shape: for both KeratinoSens (KE2) and h-CLAT (KE3) — the two cell-based key events — the consortium names "the risk that cytotoxic components may mask responses induced by sensitizing constituents." Both assays are read against viability (KeratinoSens requires >70%; h-CLAT stains viability alongside its markers and runs a cytotoxicity dose-finder first), so a mixture carrying something merely poisonous at the concentration where a different component would have registered returns two quiet negatives out of three — which the rule scores as a pass. A third, smaller shared route runs cross-endpoint: the MTT viability dye is used in both Test Guideline 439 skin irritation and KeratinoSens, and plant constituents reduce MTT directly while plant pigments absorb at its measurement wavelength. Both guidelines carry controls for it (killed- tissue and colorant controls with corrections; treatment removal before the viability read; neutral red or PrestoBlue as substitutes) — which is what a mature adopted method looks like, and is recorded here because an automated system that skipped the colorant control would return two wrong answers from one cause. → cytotoxic-masking-is-a-second-shared-failure-route-through-the-battery

    (5) correlated_blind_spot is STILL NOT SET on this map, and the reasons are stated so a human can close it. (a) It is a different mechanism from the one flagged on 2026-08-01, so it does not answer the question that was asked. (b) No experiment has been run — this is a study-design paper; the masking risk is a stated technical expectation, not a measured false-negative rate, and eleven reference botanicals have been selected, not tested. (c) It is bounded to mixtures — for a single defined chemical "a cytotoxic constituent masks a sensitising constituent" has no referent — which makes it a domain_gate finding first and a correlated_blind_spot finding second. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md and docs/backlog.md.

    (6) The asymmetry a loop should act on. Every shared failure mode now recorded on this battery — metabolic, cytotoxic-masking, solubility, the log Kow threshold — pushes toward the negative. The 2-out-of-3 rule is symmetric; the failure modes are not. On an undefined mixture, a concordant negative is a weaker signal than a concordant positive, and nothing in the procedure encodes that.

    (7) Bias watch, in an unusual direction. The commercial interest here points toward "the existing non-animal tests work on our ingredients." The paper argues the harder case anyway, in a consortium with two US regulators at the table. That is worth saying plainly and it does not retire the flag — and the supporting literature was compiled non-systematically with study quality not formally assessed, by the authors' own statement.

    (8) Global-ripple: walked every map. No Status and no loop tier moves anywhere. The same source ripples onto skin-irritation-rhe, phototoxicity-3t3-nru, phototoxicity-rhe-pt, skin-sensitization-dpra, skin-sensitization-keratinosens, skin-sensitization-hclat and mixture-toxicity-in-vitro-nams — recorded as a shared domain_gate on the local-dermal column rather than as evidence about any one method's performance. Back- edge written into 2o3-da-skin-sensitization-predictivity, whose 72–97% range is now explicitly scoped to single chemicals.

  • 2026-08-27 — ⚠ THE 2026-08-01 PRO-HAPTEN FLAG NOW HAS A NAMED CANDIDATE REPAIR IN THE OECD PIPELINE, AND correlated_blind_spot STAYS UNSET. Status HELD at consensus; all three axes unchanged; nothing on this map or its component maps was edited beyond this bullet. A new map was coined tonight for a fourth non-animal skin-allergy assay — skin-sensitization-sens-is — which runs on lab-grown human skin tissue rather than on a cell line or in a test tube, and whose draft OECD Test Guideline states in para. 11 that reconstructed human epidermis "includes metabolic systems inherent to such skin models", so that pro-haptens — chemicals that only become allergens after a skin enzyme transforms them — "can be detected by the test method", with pre-haptens (air-oxidised) detected too. Its developer's own 50-chemical blinded study reports "the majority" of a pre-/pro-hapten-enriched set identified.

    That is precisely the class the 2026-08-01 connect pass flagged as this battery's candidate shared blind spot (restated in full so this bullet stands alone: DPRA is a cell-free peptide-reactivity measurement with no enzymes at all; KeratinoSens and h-CLAT run on immortalised HaCaT and THP-1 lines with limited skin-relevant metabolism; so a pro-hapten can be missed three times for one structural reason and the 2-out-of-3 rule then reports two concordant negatives out of three, which is a pass). The flag was raised as a vault inference with no citation, and the field was deliberately left unset pending one.

    This is still not that citation, and the four reasons are recorded rather than glossed — reconstructed-epidermis-metabolic-competence-as-pro-hapten-answer. (1) Para. 11 asserts something about its own tissue and never measures DPRA, KeratinoSens or h-CLAT; nobody has run this battery and SENS-IS on a shared pro-hapten set and reported the disagreement, and the vault will not manufacture that comparison by placing two sources side by side. (2) The evidence is the vendor's, on the chemical class its competitors are said to fail: the declaring authors are ImmunoSearch co-founders and shareholders, and ImmunoSearch owns the assay, the tissue and the guideline submission. (3) There is no number — "the majority", "broadly consistent with LLNA EC3 trends", and no sensitivity, specificity, accuracy or per-chemical table anywhere in the retrievable text of a 50-chemical blinded study. (4) The developer's own data undercuts the clean version: for aromatics needing heavy metabolism, the standard epidermis-only tissue under-called potency and needed a thicker full-thickness model, so reconstructed epidermis's metabolic competence is real and partly insufficient for the very class it is credited with solving.

    What HAS changed is the shape of the open question, and it is worth a human's attention. It was: is the vault's inference right, and will anyone cite it? It is now: this is the second candidate repair to a correlated_blind_spot row whose entire evidence base belongs to the party selling the repair. The first was HepaRG cells against the micronucleus assay's false-positive rate (logged 2026-07-27, still awaiting a policy decision the rails files do not cover). Two instances is a pattern and a predictable one: the organisation with the strongest incentive to demonstrate that an incumbent battery has a shared blind spot is the organisation selling the alternative. Neither claim is thereby false. But the rail's repairs will systematically arrive pre-loaded with the exact bias the rail exists to resist, and an automated system that adopts a repair on the strength of the repairer's own evidence has automated a conflict of interest. Logged to docs/backlog.md as an extension of the 07-27 decision, not as a new one.

⚠️ Added 2026-08-01 (monthly connect pass) — CANDIDATE CORRELATED BLIND SPOT, FLAGGED, NOT ADOPTED

This is a vault inference produced by the monthly cross-method sweep, not a finding from an ingested source, and the correlated_blind_spot frontmatter field has deliberately NOT been set. Stage 07's rule is flag, never auto-adopt. It needs a human verdict.

The connect pass asked its standard question of every battery — do two components fail on the same chemicals for the same structural reason? — and the answer for this one looks like yes, on a battery the vault has been holding up for three weeks as its cleanest closed-loop-ready example.

Pre-haptens and pro-haptens are chemicals that are not themselves reactive but become skin sensitisers after chemical oxidation in air (pre-) or after enzymes in the skin metabolise them (pro-). The vault already records these as a false-negative mode for DPRA (skin-sensitization-dpra) — "pre/pro-haptens needing activation" — where it is logged as one assay's limitation.

It is not one assay's limitation. DPRA is a purely chemical peptide-reactivity measurement with no cells and therefore no metabolism at all. skin-sensitization-keratinosens and skin-sensitization-hclat are immortalised cell lines whose skin-relevant metabolic competence is limited. None of the three can reliably activate a pro-hapten, so a chemical that only becomes a sensitiser after metabolism has three chances to be missed for one shared reason — and the 2-out-of-3 rule then reports two concordant negatives out of three, which is a pass.

This is exactly the shape of the ROS-assay ↔ 3T3-NRU pair in the phototoxicity IATA and the FET ↔ RTgill-W1 pair in ecotoxicology: a battery casting one vote while appearing to cast several.

⚠️ Why it matters more here than anywhere else in the vault. This is the flagship. GL 497's fixed 2-out-of-3 rule is the vault's standing answer to what would make a method loop-ready, and this battery and the phototoxicity IATA are the only two batteries in the vault that clear end to end — every component closed-loop-ready, a published deterministic rule at the top. Both of them carry a shared-metabolism blind spot. An automated system running this battery would read two negatives as corroboration, apply the rule correctly, and pass a pro-hapten — fast, repeatably, and with every error pointing the same way. The two things an autonomous lab could actually run today are the two things whose concordance means least.

What a human needs to decide. (1) Whether the vault's own reading is right — the metabolic-competence claim about KeratinoSens and h-CLAT is drawn from this vault's existing notes, not from a source read this run, and needs a citation before the field is set. (2) Whether GL 497's stated chemical applicability domain already carves pro-haptens out, in which case this is a domain_gate the vault has under-described rather than a new blind spot.
