2-out-of-3 defined approach (skin sensitisation) — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-04) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted. The clearest "this NAM is ready" case in the vault: a fixed combination of three individually adopted assays, codified in OECD Guideline 497.

What we believe now

For skin-sensitisation hazard (sensitiser vs non-sensitiser), the 2-out-of-3 (2o3) defined approach — two concordant results among DPRA, KeratinoSens, and h-CLAT — is an OECD-adopted replacement for the animal LLNA within its chemical applicability domain. It predicts human outcomes at 72–97% balanced accuracy, 84–97% sensitivity, 50–100% specificity. High sensitivity (rarely misses a sensitiser) is the strength; specificity is the soft axis (as low as 50% — it can over-call non-sensitisers). It is a hazard call; GHS potency sub-categorisation is a separate DA (the ITS) in the same guideline.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — Three machine-readable component assays (HPLC peptide depletion, luciferase, flow cytometry) feeding a data-interpretation procedure the guideline itself calls "a transparent, rule-based approach requiring no expert judgment." The vault's flagship loop-ready case.

  • Domain gate: chemical applicability domain of GL 497 — a loop must check the applicability domain before trusting the decision rule.

  • 2026-07-05 — Ingested the three component Test Guidelines as their own maps (TG 442C DPRA / KE1, TG 442D KeratinoSens / KE2, TG 442E h-CLAT / KE3). This map is now the battery hub with live component cross-links (closes the "component assays" open gap). The single-assay numbers explain the battery: h-CLAT carries the sensitivity (93%), DPRA/KeratinoSens carry the specificity — the 2o3 combines them to beat any one alone.
  • 2026-07-09 — Parent guideline moved; this map's Status holds. GL 497 was revised (current edition 2 July 2026), and the vault had been reading the 2021 edition without knowing it — surfaced when the PDF archival pass found the OECD content/dam URL serves whichever edition is current. Two changes matter here. (1) The guideline gained a Part III: two adopted DAs deriving a quantitative point of departure — now skin-sensitization-quantitative-pod-da. Critically, the guideline states Part III DAs cannot be used for the Part I hazard call, so this is a sibling that does not chain into the 2o3; the 2o3's hazard claim is untouched. (2) The guideline now admits additional information sources into its DAs — ADRA and kDPRA (TG 442C), LuSens and EpiSensA (TG 442D), U-SENS (TG 442E) — which is the "KE2/KE3 new entrants" watch item arriving inside the guideline. Not re-graded from the 2026 text: the 2o3 performance figures above were read from the 2021 edition and have not been re-verified against the current one. Re-reading Part I of the 2026 edition is an explicit open item, not a silent assumption that nothing changed.
  • 2026-07-10 — The triad is complete, and Part I of the 2026 text was read. Ingesting skin-sensitization-potency-its-da (Part II) closes the potency gap open since 2026-07-04. Status holds at consensus — the ITS answers a different question and does not bear on this hazard call. Two things the 2026 Part I text settles. (1) The 2o3 DIP is unchanged: two concordant results among assays addressing at least two of KE1–KE3, "a transparent, rule-based approach requiring no expert judgment." (2) The "new entrants" are confirmed inside the DA — Part I admits ADRA beside DPRA (KE1), and EpiSensA and LuSens beside KeratinoSens™ (KE2) — producing 2o3 permutations, each with its own performance table in Annexes 2 and 9 of the Supporting Document. The guideline also warns that its reference data are imbalanced (more Cat. 1 than NC), so "measures of balanced accuracy are more uncertain, particularly in the case of the human data comparison" — which bears directly on the 72–97% BA range above. The performance figures in "What we believe now" remain 2021-sourced; the per-permutation 2026 tables were not transcribed this run. Still an open item.
  • 2026-07-22 — regulatory-move (national, hedged). The UK Replacing animals in science strategy (regulatory-acceptance-uk-animal-replacement-strategy) puts skin sensitisation in basket 1 and names the whole stack this map sits on — TG 442C (peptide binding), TG 442D (keratinocyte), TG 442E (dendritic-cell activation) and TG 497 including SARA-ICE — with the target "By the end of 2026 we will aim to apply only validated alternative methods…". It also states the residual honestly: animal tests are used "only… if these alternative methods do not provide enough data for decisions on the risk of skin sensitisation to be made confidently." That sentence is the live boundary of this map's defined approach, written by a regulator. Status and axes hold; the target is an aim, not a mandate (uk-2026-targets-are-aims-not-mandates). → uk-three-basket-readiness-taxonomy

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — independently confirmed by a US regulator, in a different sector. regulatory-acceptance-fda-nam-framework (FDA CDER draft guidance, March 2026) states that CDER "has … accepted NAMs using in vitro reconstructed human epidermis assays to predict risk for human skin sensitization to replace the use of animals (e.g., mice in the local lymph node assay)," and cites OECD Guideline 497 and Kleinstreuer et al. 2018 by name. Two things make this worth recording rather than filing as an echo of the OECD: (1) it is a US drug regulator, independent of the OECD process, and (2) it is for pharmaceuticals — a sector outside the cosmetics/REACH context where the 2o3 battery is usually discussed. The LLNA-replacement claim this map has carried since 2026-07-04 now has corroboration from a second regulatory system.
  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — and CDER says why, which tells the vault what the other endpoints lack. CDER attributes the acceptance of skin-sensitisation NAMs to there being "well-defined adverse outcome pathways" plus in-vitro and in-silico strategies to evaluate prediction of human responses. That is the clearest published statement of the precondition — and it is the diagnostic for every endpoint in this vault that has not achieved stand-alone acceptance. Skin sensitisation has a complete AOP (KE1→KE2→KE3), and it is the only endpoint here whose battery composition falls out of the AOP structure rather than being assembled around an animal test. Compare phototoxicity-iata (AOP-informed, guidance only) and developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb (17 assays, no proof-of-principle against in-vivo). AOP completeness looks like the gating variable, and this map is the existence proof.
  • Composes a testing battery (now wired). This DA is built from three component assays, each ingested as its own map and each an AOP key event: skin-sensitization-dpra (KE1, peptide reactivity), skin-sensitization-keratinosens (KE2, keratinocyte activation), skin-sensitization-hclat (KE3, dendritic-cell activation). The three are complementary, not redundant — the canonical IATA/defined-approach composition edge the connect layer exists to capture. (Closes the component-assay open gap logged 2026-07-04.)
  • Sibling DA — different endpoint. eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da (OECD TG 467) is the same defined-approach pattern — fixed recipe of validated methods + rule-based data-interpretation procedure → a GHS classification — applied to the eye endpoint. Two adopted DAs now anchor the DA pattern in the vault.
  • Sibling DA — potency (ingested 2026-07-10). skin-sensitization-potency-its-da is Part II of this same guideline: a 0–7 score summed from KE1 + KE3 + an in-silico prediction, assigning GHS 1A / 1B / NC. It skips KE2, which this DA requires — so the two are not nested, and the ITS is not "the 2o3 plus potency." A distinct potency claim, not a duplicate of this hazard call. Its honest headline: against human potency data it is LLNA-equivalent (54–75% vs the LLNA's 60%), not superior, and two of ten permutations under-predict 1A as 1B.
  • Sibling DA — quantitative PoD (ingested 2026-07-09), and an explicit NON-composition edge. skin-sensitization-quantitative-pod-da is Part III of this same guideline and reads the same three key-event assays — but the guideline forbids using it for the Part I hazard call. One battery, three regulatory questions (hazard here, potency in the ITS, dose in Part III), three distinct data-interpretation procedures. This is the vault's clearest demonstration that a defined approach is an object in its own right, separable from the assays it consumes: swap the DIP, get a different kind of answer from identical inputs.
  • A defined approach is an object, not a recipe (the 2026 reading). As of the 2 July 2026 edition, what GL 497 adopts is a data-interpretation procedure plus an admissible set of information sources, not a fixed triple of assays. Alternative sources are permitted at every key event, generating a space of permutations evaluated separately. Combined with the three-DIP triad above, the object that carries the regulatory standing is clearly the DIP, and the assays are interchangeable within a characterised set.
  • Global-ripple: distinct mechanism/endpoint from skin-irritation-rhe (RhE viability, skin irritation) — no confirm/refute edge, only the DA-pattern kinship with the eye DA above. Outward, the endpoint's only real edges are the dose bridge (pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics) and — new this run — the computational hub in-silico-qsar-read-across, which supplies the ITS's scored in-silico leg.
