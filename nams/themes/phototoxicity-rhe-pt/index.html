RhE phototoxicity test (OECD TG 498) — phototoxicity — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-11) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 498; first adopted 2021, current edition 2 July 2026, one of the seven Test Guidelines in that batch). Adoption earns consensus deterministically. The topical, human-tissue confirmation tier of the new phototoxicity column.

What we believe now

A chemical is applied topically to reconstructed human epidermis — differentiated, multilayered, with a functional stratum corneum. Half the tissues receive 6 J/cm² of simulated sunlight, half stay dark. Viability is read by MTT. The prediction model is one threshold: a ≥ 30% drop in relative viability in the irradiated arm, at any concentration, means phototoxic; if no concentration reaches 30%, not phototoxic. Borderline (within 5% of the cutoff) or discordant results trigger a second and possibly third run.

The barrier is the whole point. Where the screening tier (phototoxicity-3t3-nru) exposes naked cells, here the chemical must cross skin before it can be photo-activated in a living cell — which is what makes the result relevant to a real topical exposure, and what makes this the tier that adjudicates the screen's positives.

The guideline is, by its own account, built from two methods this vault already maps. It "combines the basic principles" of TG 439 — the RhE tissue and MTT readout, skin-irritation-rhe — with the standardised irradiation procedures of TG 432, phototoxicity-3t3-nru. The vault has not previously held a method that is, by construction, two of its own maps welded together.

The validation base is thin, and it is the guideline that says so. Both figures below are real; they are also not the same kind of figure, and collapsing them would be the error this vault exists to avoid.

A later literature-derived database of 60+ chemicals in the EpiDerm™ model performed better than the pre-validation figures — but the TG states plainly those studies, "whilst using the same SOP, were not conducted as formal validation studies." So the formal evidence under the adopted reference method is a ten-chemical set from 1999. The KeraSkin™ numbers are an independent check that a second tissue matches the first; they are not a fresh, first-in-class validation of the underlying prediction model.

This is the vault's first adopted method whose formal validation base is visibly thin — and the reason is more interesting than the fact. For this endpoint there is no validated in-vivo reference method at all (photoallergy-photogenotoxicity-no-validated-method), so no large animal-derived reference set can be assembled; the benchmark is scarce human clinical data. The thinness is a property of the endpoint, not a lax guideline. It still means "adopted on both axes" and "extensively validated" are different claims here — which is precisely the distinction the two-axis model exists to keep visible, and the first time in this vault that the gap runs in this direction (regulatory acceptance ahead of a deep validation base, rather than the usual reverse).

Scope, per the TG against itself: topical exposure only; it "is not designed to predict other adverse effects that may arise from the combined action of a chemical and light (e.g., it does not address photo-genotoxicity, photoallergy, or photocarcinogenicity)." No metabolic activation. MTT-reducing or coloured chemicals interfere and need adapted controls or an HPLC/UPLC readout.

funding_flag: method-developer — a test-system dependency, not sponsored science: both validated tissues are proprietary (EpiDerm™, MatTek; KeraSkin™, validated via KoCVAM, the Korean validation centre — an independent body, which is what carries the weight on the me-too). There is no generic RhE model in this TG.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Battery composition (composes-with, by the OECD's own guidance): the top tier of the phototoxicity IATA, phototoxicity-iata (OECD GD 397) — it adjudicates positives from TG 432, which in turn sits below the MEC gate (TG 101) and the ROS assay (TG 495), both still to ingest (open gaps, plain text).
  • Parent methods (composes-with, structural — the vault's first "method built from two of its own maps"): TG 439 (skin-irritation-rhe) supplies the tissue and the MTT viability readout; TG 432 (phototoxicity-3t3-nru) supplies the irradiation procedure. Recorded on all three maps.
  • RhE tissue-technology family (composes-with, not redundancy): the reconstructed-human-epidermis platform now carries three adopted, mechanistically distinct OECD methods in this vault — skin irritation (TG 439, skin-irritation-rhe), skin corrosion (TG 431, skin-corrosion-rhe), and now phototoxicity (TG 498). Same tissue, same MTT readout, three different hazards, distinguished purely by exposure protocol (duration, and now irradiation). The technology family also extends to the cornea-like epithelium of eye-irritation-rhce-no-category (TG 492). A striking amount of the vault's adopted local/topical coverage rests on one platform — a concentration risk worth naming: a systematic flaw in RhE models would ripple across four adopted TGs at once.
  • Redundancy check vs the screening tier — negative, and instructively so: TG 432 and this map read the same biology with the same light/dark logic, yet are not substitutes; the discriminator is the barrier (intrinsic photo-reactivity vs whether enough chemical crosses skin to matter). See phototoxicity-3t3-nru.
  • Dose bridge (composes-with, weak): the output is a binary hazard call, not a potency estimate, so the pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics hub edge is real but thin — there is no bioactive concentration to convert into a human-equivalent dose. Named for completeness; not load-bearing.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No edge to the sensitisation battery (see the photoallergy note below), the genotox battery, DART, immunotox, hepatotox, metabolism, inhalation, ecotox, repeat-dose or the in-silico hub. Two near-edges named and refused: the skin-sensitisation battery reads hapten-driven immune activation and is mechanistically adjacent to photoallergy — but no photoallergy method is validated, and the sensitisation assays have not been validated with an irradiation arm; likewise the genotox battery does not cover photogenotoxicity. Both are recorded as documented blanks in photoallergy-photogenotoxicity-no-validated-method, not as coverage.
