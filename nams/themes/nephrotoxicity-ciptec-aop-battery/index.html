AOP-guided ciPTEC nephrotoxicity battery — nephrotoxicity — method

🚩 AUTO-COINED 2026-07-28 — flagged for human review, and it opens a NEW ENDPOINT GROUP. The vault's 63rd map and its first for the kidney. Nephrotoxicity was previously a headline endpoint-coverage gap — no non-animal kidney method had been ingested at all. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md. Git is the undo.

Status: emerging (2026-07-28). evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, regulatory_status: none. One laboratory, three compounds from one chemical class, no negative controls, no sensitivity or specificity computed or computable. A proof-of-concept, and the paper says so.

What we believe now

The kidney is among the organs drugs damage most often, and the proximal tubule — the stretch that reabsorbs most of what the kidney filters — takes most of the chemical punishment. This platform grows ciPTEC (conditionally immortalised human proximal tubule epithelial cells) and measures five key events drawn from published kidney adverse outcome pathways:

Key eventAssayWhat the instrument reads
Mitochondrial depolarisationJC-10 dyefluorescence, 490/520 and 540/590 nm
Reactive oxygen speciesCM-H₂DCFDAfluorescence, 485/535 nm
Cell viabilityPrestoBluefluorescence, 560/590 nm
Membrane rupturelactate dehydrogenase (LDH) releaseabsorbance, 490 nm
Lysosomal injuryβ-N-acetylglucosaminidase (NAG) activityabsorbance, 405 nm

Three platinum chemotherapy drugs, ≥3 biological × 3 technical replicates. The concentration that halves cell survival (IC₅₀ — lower means more toxic) came out at 26.3 µM for cisplatin, 113 µM for oxaliplatin and 529 µM for carboplatin at 24 hours; 10.5 / 45.5 / 97.0 µM at 72 hours. So cisplatin is roughly four times more potent than oxaliplatin and twenty times more potent than carboplatin in the dish — the same rank order as the clinical record.

The genuinely useful result is the ordering of the readouts, not of the drugs. Mitochondrial depolarisation fired first and most consistently, at lower concentrations than anything else, for all three compounds. Reactive oxygen species were compound-dependent — strong for cisplatin, moderate for oxaliplatin, minimal for carboplatin. The late markers (LDH leakage, NAG release, outright viability loss) needed higher concentrations. For anyone choosing one readout to run at scale, that is actionable: measure the mitochondria.

That the drug ranking matches the clinic is a sanity check, not a validation. Three structurally related compounds of known and graded severity is the easiest possible test, and there are no negatives to be wrong on.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — by the deterministic ladder in shared/evidence-model.md:

  • Readout? Fully instrument-readable. Fluorescence at four wavelength pairs, absorbance at two. No microscope and no human scoring anywhere — which clears the hurdle that blocks the micronucleus assay, the cell transformation assays and zebrafish teratogenicity.
  • Decision rule? No. Nothing converts five curves into a hazard call, and the authors decline to propose one. → rung 4.
  • Inputs? No in-vivo study consumed. Not bench-bound.

A brand-new endpoint arrives already sitting in the vault's largest blocker category, and it arrives there by a route the other eighteen did not take. The usual story is historical debt — an assay adopted decades ago under a guideline that never specified interpretation. This method was built in 2026, designed from a causal pathway, with readouts chosen for quantification, and its authors declined to write a rule on principle, judging three compounds insufficient to justify one. That is the correct scientific call, and it illustrates why the vault's habit of calling this blocker "cheap to fix" needs a qualifier: the rule cannot honestly be written until there is evidence to write it against, and writing it is what would make the method usable enough to generate that evidence. → the-kidney-arrives-with-a-plate-reader-and-no-rule

Loop-readiness is not endorsement, and this is a clean example. A machine could run this assay end to end today and return five curves. Nothing about that says the screen is fit to decide anything about a kidney.

⚠️ Correlated blind spot — intra-battery

All five readouts sit in one immortalised cell line. Five agreeing measurements sharing one biological substrate are not five independent votes: any nephrotoxic mechanism depending on a transporter, enzyme or structure that ciPTEC expresses atypically fails in all five at once, and the battery reports concordant safety.

The authors disclose the underlying limitation honestly — the line "cannot fully recapitulate the structural complexity, vascularisation, and multicellular interactions of the kidney in vivo" — but that is a statement about biological realism, not about vote independence. The second claim is this vault's, and it is the one that matters to an automated system reading concordance as confidence.
