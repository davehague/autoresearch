Mammalian cell gene mutation (OECD TG 476 / TG 490) — genotoxicity — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-07) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 476 + TG 490). The mammalian gene-mutation arm of the in-vitro genotoxicity battery — the piece flagged "still to ingest" since Ames (TG 471) and micronucleus (TG 487) were mapped. With this ingest the core in-vitro genotox battery now spans bacterial gene mutation (Ames), mammalian gene mutation (TG 476/490), chromosome damage (TG 487) and a mechanistic MoA interpretation layer (ToxTracker TG 446A).

What we believe now

The in-vitro mammalian cell gene mutation tests expose mammalian cells to a chemical (± S9), let induced mutations fix and express, then select for mutants (colony counting → mutant frequency). TG 476 uses the Hprt (X-linked) or xprt (gpt transgene, AS52) locus with 6-thioguanine selection — detecting base substitutions, frameshifts and small indels, but not large chromosomal deletions. TG 490 uses the thymidine kinase (Tk) locus in L5178Y tk+/− (mouse-lymphoma assay) or TK6 tk+/− cells with trifluorothymidine selection; the Tk locus tolerates larger lesions, so colony sizing (small vs large) lets TG 490 detect both gene mutations and chromosomal events — a wider spectrum than TG 476, overlapping into the clastogenicity endpoint. Originally a single 1984 guideline, split 28 July 2015 into TG 476 + TG 490. Trustworthy for its endpoint (mammalian gene mutation), read within a battery.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Battery composition (composes-with): the mammalian gene-mutation complement to the bacterial gene-mutation Ames test genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation — TG 476/490 catch mammalian-specific gene-mutation events (and, for Tk, chromosomal-scale lesions) that a bacterial assay cannot fully represent. Together with the chromosome-damage micronucleus test genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus the three cover the battery's mutational endpoints; TG 490's Tk locus overlaps into the micronucleus endpoint (chromosomal damage), so the two are complementary with a deliberate overlap, not redundant.
  • Mechanistic follow-up (composes-with): genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa (OECD TG 446A) is the MoA layer above the whole battery — a positive/equivocal TG 476/490 call is exactly what ToxTracker's DNA-reactive-vs-indirect discrimination is meant to interpret.
  • Carcinogenicity bridge (composes-with, complementary): the genotox battery detects genotoxic carcinogens via a mutation route; the cell-transformation assays carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay catch non-genotoxic carcinogens the battery misses — complementary halves of the carcinogenicity picture, not redundant.
  • Global-ripple: distinct endpoint from the skin/eye local maps, the systemic maps (DNT, hepatotox, repeat-dose, PBK, inhalation), the ecotox map, and the new endocrine map — a within-endpoint deepener, not a new column.
