KeratinoSens — ARE-Nrf2 keratinocyte activation (skin sensitisation KE2) — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-05) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 442D). A component of the 2-out-of-3 defined approach; the validated reference method for the ARE-Nrf2 key-event slot.

What we believe now

KeratinoSens reads the keratinocyte-activation key event (KE2) via an ARE-Nrf2 luciferase reporter. It calls sensitiser vs non-sensitiser at 77% accuracy (155/201), 78% sensitivity, 76% specificity vs LLNA, ~85% within/between-lab reproducibility. One input to the 2o3/ITS defined approaches — used in combination. Cytotoxicity can confound the signal; specificity (~76%) is middling.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — ARE-Nrf2 luciferase induction read as luminescence against EC1.5/IC50 thresholds.

Ingested krzykwa-2026-botanicals-dermal-nams-mixtures (Pharmaceutical Biology 64(1):922–948, DOI 10.1080/13880209.2026.2717803; Botanical Safety Consortium Dermal Working Group under a US FDA / NIEHS Memorandum of Understanding; funding_flag: [industry]). (a) The readout itself can be inhibited. Per Test Guideline 442D, this method's own guideline, "phytoestrogens at high concentrations can interfere with the luciferase enzyme" — the light-producing protein the entire measurement depends on. That is not the cell responding wrongly; that is the ruler being bent, and it is a different category of failure from an applicability-domain miss. (b) ⚠ A shared failure route with the battery's other cell-based key event. For this assay and for skin-sensitization-hclat, the consortium names "the risk that cytotoxic components may mask responses induced by sensitizing constituents" — both are read against viability (this one requires >70%), so a mixture carrying something merely poisonous at the concentration where a sensitiser would have registered returns a negative from both. Two of three key events, one mechanism, and the 2-out-of-3 rule scores two concordant negatives as a pass. (c) The MTT viability dye is a third, cross-endpoint route: plant constituents reduce MTT directly and pigments absorb at its wavelength — shared with skin-irritation-rhe. Mitigations exist in the guideline (treatment removed before the viability read; neutral red uptake or PrestoBlue as substitute endpoints).   • Composes the 2o3 battery. The KE2 component of skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach, alongside skin-sensitization-dpra (KE1) and skin-sensitization-hclat (KE3) — complementary key events, not redundant.
  • The potency DA does NOT use this assay (an anti-edge, recorded 2026-07-10). skin-sensitization-potency-its-da (GL 497 Part II) reads KE1 + KE3 + in-silico and skips KE2 entirely. So of GL 497's three data-interpretation procedures, KeratinoSens feeds the hazard call (2o3, as KE2) and the dose call (Part III, via EC1.5/IC50) but not the potency call. This is worth stating rather than leaving implicit: the "one battery, three questions" framing is true of the guideline, not of every assay in it. The KE2 slot is the one the potency DA judged dispensable.
  • Intra-slot redundancy (flagged, and now confirmed inside the DAs): TG 442D houses several methods for the same KE2. Read from the 2026 GL 497 text, Part I's 2o3 admits EpiSensA and LuSens beside KeratinoSens™ as alternative KE2 sources — the 2025 α-Sens/EpiSensA watch item landing not as new standalone TGs but as substitutable information sources inside an adopted DA. Redundancy here is by design (interchangeable within the slot), unlike the cross-KE complementarity above.
  • ⚠️ Designed redundancy is not a correlated blind spot — but three of the five are still one measurement (added 2026-07-18, a VAULT INFERENCE). Five interchangeable methods in one key-event slot is substitutability by design: the guideline offers alternative information sources for the same KE2 call, which is categorically different from the vault's blind-spot rail (two assays that look like independent votes and are not). The rail does not fire here, and saying so is part of applying it honestly. However, the guideline's own taxonomy shows KeratinoSens, LuSens and α-Sens all read ARE-Nrf2 luciferase induction — the same reporter mechanism, differing in cell line and protocol. A lab swapping among those three gains availability, cost and supply-chain independence; it does not gain mechanistic independence. Swapping to EpiSensA/Epi²SensA — RT-qPCR gene expression in 3D reconstructed human epidermis — genuinely changes what is measured. For a loop this matters concretely: an automated system told "any TG 442D method satisfies KE2" will treat those five as equivalent, and for the purpose of covering a shared ARE-Nrf2 failure mode, three of them are one. Flagged as a vault inference from the guideline's structure; TG 442D makes no such claim.
  • Global-ripple (re-walked 2026-07-18): confirms/composes the 2o3 hub; no refutation edges. The 2026 edition does not change the 2o3, ITS or quantitative-PoD data-interpretation procedures themselves — it widens the admissible source set at one slot. skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach, skin-sensitization-potency-its-da (which skips KE2 entirely) and skin-sensitization-quantitative-pod-da all hold their Status and loop tiers.
  • 2026-08-09 — ⚠️ A REDOX GROUP READ THE SAME MOLECULAR EVENT AND DECLINED TO CALL IT SENSITISATION — which supplies a candidate mechanism for a specificity gap this map has carried as a bare number since 2026-07-05. Status HELD at consensus. loop_readiness HELD at closed-loop-ready. No domain_gate is set. Ingested armouch-2026-r3act-oxygen-controlled-redox-nam (NAM Journal 2026, article 100117, PMID 42542580) — a four-material, single-laboratory, developer-authored proof of concept for a new oxidative-stress platform, oxidative-stress-redox-nrf2-profiling, coined tonight. Its authors observed salicylic acid activate Nrf2 in human keratinocytes under physiological oxygen and interpreted it "as antioxidant-response engagement rather than skin-sensitization classification." That is the identical molecular event, in the identical cell type, that OECD Test Guideline 442D converts into the positive call — and all five methods now in the KE2 slot read it. The mechanism it proposes. Nrf2 is the cell's general chemical-stress response; skin sensitisation is one of several things that trigger it. Anything engaging the antioxidant response — an oxidant, a redox-active particle, a mild irritant, a chemical perturbing the peroxide balance without ever haptenating a protein — produces the reading the guideline calls "allergen." This map has held 76% specificity (84/110 non-sensitisers correctly called) and the phrase "specificity is middling" for a month with no account of what the misses have in common. A structural, directional, perfectly reproducible over-call on redox-active chemicals is a candidate account, and it is the kind an automated system would never surface: it repeats identically in every lab and every one of the five methods, so reproducibility cannot detect it, and a laboratory running two KE2 methods for confidence has run one measurement twice. ⚠ Why NOTHING moves, stated explicitly because the temptation to move it is real. (a) Status aggregation is highest-tier-wins: a four-material developer proof of concept is single-lab-exploratory, the lowest wet rung, against an adopted guideline with formal validation and ~85% inter-laboratory reproducibility. (b) Salicylic acid was never run in TG 442D and no comparison against the guideline's thresholds is reported — two different assays, two different purposes, and reading one as refuting the other exceeds the evidence. (c) The guideline already says this: TG 442D describes a single key event, explicitly not a stand-alone hazard call, and exists to be combined with the other two — the over-call is a known property of a screen designed for use inside a battery, which the 2-out-of-3 defined approach already guards against by requiring three different key events. A domain_gate was considered and DECLINED. "Redox-active chemicals" would be an applicability-domain carve-out a loop must check first, and it is the right shape — but nobody has tested how much of the 24-point specificity gap is redox-driven, and a carve-out written from one anecdote plus a plausible mechanism would be the vault laundering a hypothesis into a rule. Recorded as an open question in docs/open-gaps-log.md instead.
