RhCE "No Category" eye test (OECD TG 492) — eye irritation / serious eye damage — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-11) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 492, adopted 14 June 2019, corrected 25 June 2025 — the vault is reading the current edition, which now carries four validated test systems). Adoption earns consensus deterministically. The first component method mapped underneath the eye defined approach (eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da, TG 467).

What we believe now

A chemical is applied to a reconstructed human cornea-like epithelium; viability is read by tetrazolium (MTT for the two reference methods, WST-8 / WST-1 for the me-toos) against a negative control. The output is one bit: mean tissue viability > 60% → UN GHS No Category; ≤ 60% → not a classification, but a referral onward to another in-vitro method "or, as a last option, in rabbits."

The specificity looks bad and it does not matter — a design decision, not an excuse. Sensitivity 95–99%; specificity 63–72%, i.e. up to a 37% false-positive rate. The guideline's position is that these FP rates "are not critical in the context of this Test Guideline," and structurally it is right: a positive is not a call. Only the negative is a regulatory decision ("a negative result should be accepted for not classifying a chemical"). Anything at or below the cutoff merely costs more testing. The assay is deliberately asymmetric — allowed to be wrong in the expensive direction, never in the dangerous one. Reading its specificity as a weakness is reading the wrong assay.

The false-negative side is where the real finding is, and the guideline hands over the number. FN rates "are within the in vivo Draize eye test within-test variability of 12%." The reference standard is itself that irreproducible. This is the sharpest statement the vault has ingested of a theme it keeps circling — from the h-CLAT's 66% specificity to the ITS's LLNA-equivalence — that a NAM's apparent inaccuracy is partly the animal test's own noise, here measured rather than asserted. When a method's error rate sits inside the reference test's variance, "as good as the animal test" has stopped being a coherent target.

Scope, hard. TG 492 "cannot be used on [its] own to replace the in vivo rabbit eye test." It does not separate Cat 1 (serious, irreversible damage) from Cat 2 (reversible irritation), nor 2A from 2B. It is the initial step of a Bottom-Up approach (chemicals expected not to need classification) or a late step in a Top-Down approach.

funding_flag: method-developer — all four validated systems are proprietary reconstructed-tissue products (EpiOcular™, SkinEthic™ HCE, LabCyte CORNEA-MODEL24, MCTT HCE™). A test-system dependency, not sponsored science; the validations were independently peer-reviewed.

Added 2026-07-19 (TG 494 ingest) — ⚠️ this map acquires a correlated blind spot

correlated_blind_spot: [eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494], set on both maps. No status moves, no loop tier moves — this map's science and automatability are exactly what they were. What changed is what a concordant result from this map and its new sibling is worth.

eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494 (OECD TG 494, ingested tonight) reads barrier integrity of a living human corneal epithelium via transepithelial electrical resistance. This map reads metabolic viability of a living human corneal epithelium via MTT/WST. Different instruments, different numbers — the same tissue type, and by TG 494's own account two points on one causal chain: irritants "first destroy tear film and epithelial barrier function of the eye, subsequently induce epithelial cell death."

The event this map measures is downstream of the event TG 494 measures — and the guideline says so. That makes this the vault's first blind-spot pair asserted by a guideline rather than inferred by the vault. Every earlier pair (FET ↔ RTgill-W1, the endocrine MMTV panel, TTR-ANSA ↔ TTR-FITC) was a vault inference from shared structure. This one is in the source text.

Consequence, stated for a loop: a chemical damaging the cornea by a route neither model supports — most obviously one requiring metabolic bioactivation, which neither tissue has — reads clean on both, and reads clean concordantly. An automated system scoring that agreement as independent replication is raising confidence on what is close to a structural artefact.

The counterweight, and it is genuine. eye-macromolecular-ordered-matrix-tg496 (TG 496, acellular protein-matrix denaturation) remains mechanically independent of both living-tissue methods. The eye battery therefore now demonstrates both halves of the Tox21 counter-design in one adopted guideline: TG 494 added a component that fails similarly to this one, while TG 496 is the component that fails differently. Three components, roughly two independent votes. See eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494 for the full analysis.
