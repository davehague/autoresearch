ROS assay for photoreactivity (OECD TG 495) — phototoxicity IATA tier 2 — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-12) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 495, adopted 18 June 2019). funding_flag: none — commodity reagents, no proprietary cell line, no licensed kit, no commercial test system.

What we believe now

TG 495 is tier 2 of the phototoxicity IATA (phototoxicity-iata, OECD GD 397): TG 101 (UV-VIS absorption / MEC gate) → TG 495 (this) → TG 432 (3T3 NRU screen) → TG 498 (RhE topical confirmation). It is in chemico — no cells at all. A chemical is irradiated with simulated sunlight and its reactive-oxygen-species generation is read through two colorimetric probes:

  • type II photochemistry (energy transfer → singlet oxygen) — bleaching of p-nitrosodimethylaniline (RNO);
  • type I photochemistry (electron/hydrogen transfer → superoxide anion, free radicals) — reduction of nitroblue tetrazolium (NBT).

It measures photoreactivity, which is not phototoxicity. The guideline is disciplined about this: "the ROS assay was designed for qualitative photoreactivity assessment of chemicals." Photoreactivity is the molecular initiating event; phototoxicity is the adverse outcome. A TG 495 positive says the molecule makes ROS in the light. Whether that harms skin is what tiers 3 and 4 exist to answer. Only the IATA turns this result into a classification.

The numbers — and why 42% specificity is the design, not the defect

Multi-laboratory validation, 2 standards + 42 coded chemicals (23 phototoxins, 19 non-phototoxic), two different solar simulators. The assay "provided no false negative predictions upon defined criteria as compared with the in vitro/in vivo phototoxicity."

MetricValue
Sensitivity100%
Specificity (per laboratory)42–82%
Positive predictivity75–92%
Negative predictivity100%

Intra- and inter-day precision for the quinine positive control exceeded 90%; the guideline reports "high inter-laboratory reproducibility."

A 42% specificity in a stand-alone classifier would be damning — more than half the safe chemicals flagged. TG 495 is never a stand-alone classifier. It sits at a tier where a negative closes the assessment and a positive only routes the chemical onward. With 100% sensitivity and 100% NPV, the only claim the tier actually makes — "not photoreactive; stop here" — was never wrong on the validation set. Its false positives cost downstream testing. They do not cost a missed hazard. A rule-out tier is graded by its NPV, not its specificity.

Stated limits, from the guideline

  1. No metabolism. "The test has not been designed to address indirect mechanisms of phototoxicity, such as effects of metabolites of a test chemical." A photo-inert parent with a photo-reactive metabolite is invisible.
  2. Solubility-bounded applicability domain — restricted to chemicals meeting the protocol's solubility criteria (DMSO or sodium phosphate buffer); insoluble chemicals are unsuitable without solubility enhancers, which are not yet characterised.
  3. A negative predicts a negative in vivo; a positive requires further data.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — Cell-free colorimetric ROS probes against defined criteria; 100% sensitivity and NPV. But see the blind spot — like 3T3 NRU it cannot biotransform, so a photo-inert parent with a photo-reactive metabolite passes the whole IATA.

  • Correlated blind spot: shares a failure mode with [phototoxicity-3t3-nru]. In a battery these are one vote, not two — and an automated loop trusting the decision rule will compound the error rather than catch it.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Composes-with the IATA hub (battery composition). phototoxicity-iata (GD 397) tiers this method at position 2. TG 495's negative terminates the assessment; its positive routes to phototoxicity-3t3-nru (TG 432, the cell screen) and then phototoxicity-rhe-pt (TG 498, the RhE confirmation). The column is now three of four tiers mapped — only TG 101 (UV-VIS / MEC gate) remains, and it is a physico-chemical measurement rather than a toxicology assay.
  • ⚠️ CLOSES THE VAULT'S OWN OPEN CORRECTION — specificity must be read against battery position. On 2026-07-11 the vault recorded, and flagged as not-yet-retro-applied: "stop reading standalone specificity as method quality and start reading it against the assay's position in its battery." That was inferred from two cases: eye-irritation-rhce-no-category (TG 492 — 95–99% sensitivity, 63–72% specificity, where a positive "never classifies") and skin-sensitization-hclat (85/93/66 — the sensitisation battery's most sensitive, least specific member). TG 495 is the third instance and the decisive one, because the guideline supplies the 100% NPV that makes the argument arithmetic. The pattern now holds across three endpoints (eye, skin sensitisation, phototoxicity) and should be treated as a property of tiered batteries in general. Recommend the vault retro-apply it to TG 492 and h-CLAT.
  • ⚠️ Absent biotransformation — the vault's most repeated failure mode, now in a third column. TG 495 cannot see metabolite-mediated phototoxicity. That is the same structural gap as the aquatic correlated blind spot: fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet (TG 236) and ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line (TG 249) both under-predict bioactivation-dependent chemicals because neither has full biotransformation (TG 249's allyl-alcohol→acrolein case being the cleanest demonstration). It is also why metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg (TG 445A) matters beyond its own endpoint. Lack of metabolic competence is now a named, cross-column, correlated blind spot spanning ecotox, phototoxicity and — by construction — most cell-free and simple in-vitro methods in this vault. Flagged as a vault-wide pattern, not a per-method caveat.
  • Refines, does not close, the photoallergy blank. phototoxicity-iata records from GD 397 that photoallergy and photogenotoxicity have no validated test method. TG 495 adds a shade: photochemical assays (ROS, UV/VIS) "can predict photoallergenic potential … although there is still a substantial risk of false positive predictions." So the blank is a validation blank, not an absence of signal. Narrower, still open.
  • Commercially unencumbered (the counter-example, third instance). With phototoxicity-3t3-nru (TG 432) and ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line (TG 249), TG 495 is an adopted OECD method with no proprietary test system at all. Against endocrine-er-transactivation's unobtainable cell line and metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg's patented one, this keeps the vault's practical-accessibility worry honest: gated access is a choice the human-health methods have made, not a law of the field.
  • In-chemico methodology family. With skin-sensitization-dpra (TG 442C, peptide reactivity) and skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435 (TG 435, synthetic membrane) — cell-free chemistry standing in for a biological event. All three are cheap, fast, reproducible screening tiers that sit below a cellular or tissue confirmation, and all three earn their place by sensitivity, not specificity. That is now a recognisable archetype in the vault, and TG 495 is its clearest member.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No edge to the endocrine column, genotox battery, carcinogenicity, hepatotox, DNT, inhalation, repeat-dose, in-silico, or PBK/QIVIVE beyond the generic dose bridge. Nothing refuted; nothing duplicated.
