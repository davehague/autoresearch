Estrogen receptor transactivation (OECD TG 455) — endocrine, the "E" of EATS — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-12) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 455, a Performance-Based Test Guideline; adopted 14 June 2021, corrected 2 July 2026). Underneath sits independent ICCVAM / NICEATM validation of the VM7Luc method and the OECD peer-review route for the STTA method.

⚠️ Adopted, validated — and one of its two founding reference methods currently cannot be run at all. See below. The Status stays consensus because the guideline and the endpoint are intact; the defect is at the method level, and the vault's two axes cannot see it.

What we believe now

Estrogens bind the estrogen receptor (ERα, and less so ERβ); the receptor–ligand complex binds estrogen response elements (EREs) in DNA and transactivates transcription. TG 455 places a firefly luciferase reporter downstream of EREs in a stably transfected line and reads luminescence as ER transactivation — in agonist mode (chemical alone) and antagonist mode (chemical against a fixed agonist). It is the E of the EATS modalities (Estrogen / Androgen / Thyroid / Steroidogenesis), and the modality the whole OECD endocrine-disrupter programme was built around when it began in 1998.

A Performance-Based Test Guideline, and that architecture is load-bearing. TG 455 defines the endpoint plus performance standards, and admits any method meeting them. Three currently qualify:

The decision rule ships with the guideline (unlike its binding sibling, TG 493): an agonist call is positive if the RPC_Max reaches or exceeds 10% of the 1 nM 17β-estradiol positive-control response in two of two, or two of three, independent runs. 14 proficiency substances for the agonist assay, 10 for the antagonist.

Scope, in the guideline's own words. The methods "are being proposed for screening and prioritisation purposes, but can also provide mechanistic information that can be used in a weight of evidence approach", and "results should not be directly extrapolated to the complex signaling and regulation of the intact endocrine system in vivo." Conceptual Framework Level 2 — a mechanism screen. It says a chemical engages the ER; it does not say the chemical causes endocrine harm, and it says nothing at all about fertility.

No sensitivity/specificity against a reference chemical set appears in the guideline text reached. The reference-chemical accuracy lists (22 chemicals for ER agonist accuracy) live in the Performance Standards, not the TG body. Recorded as a gap, not filled.

The defect: an adopted method nobody can run. The entire content of the 2 July 2026 correction is a note, printed twice:

"Users of Test Guideline 455 should be aware that the VM7Luc ER TA assay is currently not implementable because the cell line used is not available from the source mentioned. Actions are underway to remediate this situation."

evidence_tier does not move. regulatory_status does not move. The number of laboratories that can perform this fully validated, formally adopted OECD reference method is zero. The PBTG architecture contains the damage — STTA and ERα CALUX remain runnable, so the ER TA endpoint is fully testable today — which is a real argument for performance-based guidelines. But the vault named a third axis (practical accessibility) on 2026-07-10 and declined to model it; this is the case that says model it. Here the third axis does not bound access, it zeroes it, on a method scoring maximally on both tracked axes.

And the same cell line was misidentified. DNA testing (Li et al. 2014) showed the line used to develop the assay "was not the BG1 human ovarian carcinoma cell line, but was instead a variant of the MCF7 human breast cancer cell line" — hence the 2016 rename BG1Luc4E2 → VM7Luc4E2. OECD's position: this "does not affect published validation studies nor the utility and application of this assay." Defensible — validation here is an empirical performance record, not a derivation from assumed biology — and also a reproducibility failure that passed development, formal ICCVAM validation and OECD adoption before anyone ran DNA authentication.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — Luminescence against an explicit rule (RPC_Max >=10% of the positive control in 2/2 or 2/3 runs). The access gate is the story here: adopted, validated, loop-ready — and nobody can run the reference method, because the cell line cannot be had. Automatability means nothing without reagents.

  • Access gate: the VM7Luc4E2 reference cell line is currently unobtainable from its source — bears on whether the loop can be stood up outside the lab that built it.
  • Correlated blind spot: shares a failure mode with [endocrine-ar-transactivation, endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation]. In a battery these are one vote, not two — and an automated loop trusting the decision rule will compound the error rather than catch it.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Completes the EATS core, with one gap left (composes-with). With this map the endocrine column holds E (TG 455), A (endocrine-ar-transactivation, TG 458), S (endocrine-steroidogenesis-h295r, TG 456) — plus the non-EATS glucocorticoid receptor (endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation, TG 454A). Thyroid (T) is the only EATS modality still unmapped, and is now the endocrine column's highest-value gap.
  • Binding vs activation — composes-with, NOT redundant. endocrine-er-binding-hrer (TG 493) reads the same receptor one step earlier: does the chemical bind ERα. TG 455 reads what the receptor does afterwards. A binder may be an agonist, an antagonist, or silent — TG 493 cannot tell them apart and TG 455 can. The connect verdict is composition, and the warning is the inverse of a redundancy warning: a TG 493 positive is not an ER-activity finding. Also: TG 455 ships a decision table; TG 493 ships none.
  • ⚠️ Correlated confound — the shared MMTV promoter (see steroid-receptor-crosstalk-shared-reporter-confound). VM7Luc4E2 places its EREs upstream of the MMTV LTR — the same promoter endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation (TG 454A) uses to read the glucocorticoid receptor, and the same promoter endocrine-ar-transactivation's 22Rv1/MMTV method uses to read the androgen receptor. One viral promoter, three adopted guidelines, three receptors; each argues the crosstalk down to "minimal" and none cites the others. The endocrine Level-2 panel's four results look like four independent votes and are less independent than they look. Second instance of the vault's correlated-blind-spot pattern (after FET / RTgill-W1, 2026-07-11) — that one was biological, this one is molecular.
  • Not fertility (an anti-edge, stated to prevent the vault's most repeated category error). reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams exists because adopted endocrine mechanism screens keep being mistaken for fertility tests. TG 455 is Level 2. It measures reporter transactivation. Kumbhar 2026 names TG 455 specifically among the screens that measure "receptor activation and hormone synthesis, not fertility." This map does not narrow the fertility blank.
  • Reporter-gene methodology family (technology, not biology): with endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation, immunotoxicity-il2-luc and genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa — stably transfected reporter lines sharing the failure mode TG 454A documents: reporter interference and cytotoxicity masquerading as signal, especially in antagonist mode.
  • Practical-accessibility axis (the third axis, now unavoidable): joins metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg (HepaRG™, patented, licensed to Biopredic), skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435 (Corrositex®, the sole validated kit) and in-silico-qsar-read-across (Derek Nexus, license-gated). Those bound access. TG 455's VM7Luc zeroes it. endocrine-er-binding-hrer adds a third species of the constraint — a radioisotope permit. Flagged for a human: the vault should decide whether to model this axis.
  • Regulatory-acceptance ripple: regulatory-acceptance-fda-nam-framework — CDER's draft guidance is the counterpoint. TG 455 is the maximally adopted case (a TG, validated, harmonised) and is still partly unusable; the FDA framework describes methods with no TG that may still be usable. Adoption and usability are coming apart from both ends at once.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. No edge to the skin/eye local maps, the genotox battery, carcinogenicity CTA, hepatotox MPS, DNT, zebrafish teratogenicity, inhalation ALI, phototoxicity column, the aquatic maps, or PBK/QIVIVE beyond the generic dose bridge every in-vitro potency method needs. Nothing in the vault is refuted or duplicated by TG 455.
