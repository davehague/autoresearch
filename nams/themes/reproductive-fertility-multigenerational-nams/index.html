Fertility / multigenerational reproductive toxicity — NAM coverage — method

Status: emerging (2026-07-09) — and the Status describes the endpoint's NAM coverage, not a single method, because there is no method to describe. This is the vault's one endpoint-level map, coined deliberately. Flagged as a departure from the one-map-per-method convention so a human can rule on whether the pattern earns its keep.

No validated non-animal replacement exists for fertility or multigenerational reproductive toxicity. That is a property of the field in 2026, not a gap in this vault's reading. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method (the strength of the negative claim, from a systematic review — not the strength of any method) and regulatory_status: none. Highest-tier-wins therefore yields emerging: the best fertility-specific science available is single-lab and unvalidated.

What we believe now

The endpoint has two tiers, and neither one closes it.

A mature, adopted MECHANISM tier — which answers a different question. OECD TG 455 (stably transfected ER transactivation), TG 458 (AR transactivation), TG 493 (hrER binding) and TG 456 (H295R steroidogenesis, mapped at endocrine-steroidogenesis-h295r) are all adopted and formally validated. Every one measures receptor activation or hormone synthesis. None measures fertility, fecundity, litter size, or a multigenerational outcome. They belong to endocrine disruption; filing them here would overstate them. A pointed detail: H295R — the only validated steroidogenesis assay — is a female adrenal carcinoma line and reads male gonadal (Leydig-cell) steroidogenesis poorly, so even the mechanism tier has a hole on the male-fertility axis.

An unvalidated FERTILITY tier — right question, no validation. Encapsulated in-vitro follicle growth (eIVFG) is the strongest science: alginate-encapsulated follicles that mature, ovulate, and yield developmentally-competent oocytes, used as a tiered ovotoxicity screen (Xu 2020, PMID 32697846; NIEHS-funded). Its own authors frame it as a pre-screen to reduce, not replace, in-vivo testing. No OECD/ECVAM/ICCVAM validation activity. The male side — testicular organoids, Leydig/Sertoli co-cultures, testis-on-chip — is fragmented across competing unvalidated platforms, one with a lapsed commercial spinout.

Why this has not moved in sixteen years. ReProTect (Schenk 2010, PMID 20493943) was a 33-partner EU consortium that assembled a 14-assay battery across germ cells, fertilisation, implantation and embryo development, and blind-ring-trialled it against ten reference chemicals with results largely matching known in-vivo profiles. It never became a Test Guideline; component assays proved poorly transferable between laboratories. "A good battery exists" and "a replacement exists" are different sentences. The apical in-vivo tests — TG 443 EOGRTS, TG 421/422 — remain the standard, and OECD's November 2024 draft revision of TG 443 refines cohorts and dose selection rather than replacing the whole-animal design.

What would move this map. An OECD or ECVAM/ICCVAM validation programme opening on a fertility-specific assay; eIVFG entering the OECD Test Guidelines work plan; or the EU PARC hiPSC spermatogonia/oogonia work graduating past proof-of-concept. Absent one of those, the honest read stays none on the regulatory axis.

Scope discipline. devTOXqP (Stemina, commercial) predicts developmental toxicity, not fertility, and must not be filed here if a later sweep surfaces it.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: bench-bound — The endpoint-level version of bench-bound, and a different problem from CFU-GM's: the apical standard is whole-animal multigenerational testing and the one candidate (eIVFG) is single-lab and unvalidated. Fixable by assay development, not by a new decision rule. The vault's last human-health blank.

  • Blocker: no validated non-animal replacement exists for the endpoint at all
  • Bench-bound because: no-nam-exists

  • ⚠️ ADDED 2026-07-12 — the blank is UNCHANGED, and this run is the strongest test it has faced. The vault today ingested three adopted OECD endocrine mechanism screens — endocrine-er-transactivation (TG 455, ER transactivation), endocrine-ar-transactivation (TG 458, AR transactivation) and endocrine-er-binding-hrer (TG 493, ER binding) — which, with endocrine-steroidogenesis-h295r (TG 456), are precisely the four guidelines Kumbhar 2026 names when it says the adopted screens measure "receptor activation and hormone synthesis, not fertility." The vault now holds all four. They do not narrow this blank by one inch. This map exists to prevent exactly the inference that a fuller endocrine column implies progress on fertility. A completed EATS panel says a chemical perturbs a hormone receptor or a hormone synthesis pathway. It says nothing about whether an organism can conceive, gestate, or produce viable offspring across generations. Each of TG 455/458/493 restates this in its own words ("should not be directly extrapolated to the complex in vivo situation"; Conceptual Framework Level 2). The more complete the mechanism panel becomes, the more tempting the category error gets — and the more load this map has to carry.
  • ADDED 2026-07-12 — one axis did move, and it is not the science. regulatory-acceptance-fda-nam-framework (FDA CDER draft, March 2026) describes a route by which an unvalidated NAM may support a submission if it is fit-for-purpose for a defined context of use. That does not create a fertility NAM — the fertility-specific tier (eIVFG, testis organoids) remains unvalidated and ReProTect's 14-assay battery is still not a TG, exactly as recorded. But it does mean the honest phrasing of this blank shifts slightly: from "no validated non-animal replacement exists and therefore none can be used" to "no validated non-animal replacement exists — and under FDA's proposed route, an unvalidated one could still be argued into a specific submission." The scientific blank is untouched. The regulatory consequence of the blank is now less absolute than the vault has been stating it.
  • Adjacent DART arms (composes-with, distinct biology): the vault's other two DART maps read different things and neither is fertility — developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity reads structural malformation, developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb reads neurodevelopment. Together with this map, DART is now: developmental seeded, neuro seeded, reproductive/fertility documented as empty.
  • Mechanism arm next door (composes-with, must not be conflated): endocrine-steroidogenesis-h295r is the endocrine screen that feeds reproductive concern without measuring reproduction. This map exists partly to hold that distinction, which two prior runs blurred by listing TG 456 as a candidate reproductive gap-closer.
  • Ecotox false friend (explicit non-edge): fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet (TG 236) is zebrafish acute aquatic lethality — same organism as the teratogenicity assay, different biology again, and unrelated to mammalian fertility.   • Dose bridge (weak, hypothetical): pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics would be needed to make any future in-vitro fertility assay's bioactive concentration a human-equivalent dose. Noted as the shape of a future edge, not a current one.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. This map refutes nothing and validates nothing — it is the first map in the vault whose content is a documented negative. Its one real effect on the rest of the vault is corrective: it removes the standing implication that TG 456, zebrafish teratogenicity, or the DNT battery were partial answers to the fertility question. They were never that.

Added 2026-07-19 — the blank holds against a machine-learning attempt

No status moves. bench-bound / no-nam-exists unchanged. deep-learning-dart-prediction-recall-gap (Ahn et al. 2026) is the first source the vault has ingested that aims a model directly at this endpoint: graph neural networks over 23 US EPA ToxCast bioassays pre-mapped to DART mechanisms, aggregated by majority vote, validated against the ECVAM ReProTect reference chemicals — whose labels come from in-vivo evidence for developmental toxicity, male fertility and female fertility.

It misses 43% of them (recall 0.57), and on a set that is 83% positive its F1 of 0.68 sits below the ≈0.91 of a model that simply calls everything toxic (vault-computed from the paper's reported class balance; the paper states no baseline).

The reason is this map's own finding, arriving in a new medium. The authors scope it themselves: "the model does not predict DART outcomes directly." It predicts mechanism-linked bioactivity. Kumbhar 2026 said the same thing about the adopted screens — TG 455/458/493/456 measure "receptor activation and hormone synthesis, not fertility." A model trained on mechanism screens inherits the gap between mechanism and apical outcome; it does not close it. Training data cannot contain an endpoint nobody measured.

The negative is now better-evidenced than it was, and that is a real update even though nothing moves. Before tonight this map recorded a documented blank — regulators and reviewers stating that NAMs are not accepted as standalone replacements for apical DART endpoints. It now also holds a measured one: someone pointed the current best computational tooling at the endpoint, on public data, with no commercial interest, and reported a number. The blank is not merely un-filled for want of trying.
