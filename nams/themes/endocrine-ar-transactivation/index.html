Androgen receptor transactivation (OECD TG 458) — endocrine, the "A" of EATS — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-12) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 458, adopted 26 June 2020, corrected 4 July 2023). Three fully validated reference test methods; validation run by national public bodies (NIFDS Korea for the 22Rv1 method; the JaCVAM lineage for AR-EcoScreen™), not by developers alone.

What we believe now

Androgens (testosterone; the more potent 5α-dihydrotestosterone, DHT) bind the androgen receptor (AR); the complex translocates to the nucleus, binds androgen response elements (AREs), and transactivates a firefly luciferase reporter. Agonist mode reads induction; antagonist mode reads inhibition of a fixed agonist reference (500 pM DHT in AR-EcoScreen™), with hydroxyflutamide as reference antagonist. This is the A of EATS.

Three methods, three architectures:

AR-EcoScreen™CHO (hamster ovary)transfected human AR cDNAheat-shock promoter, 4×C3-ARE → luc; SV40-Renilla for cytotoxicity
AR-CALUX®U2-OS (human osteosarcoma)transfected human AR cDNATATA promoter, 3×ARE → luc

Only the third reads an endogenous, natively regulated AR in a cell type that actually depends on androgen signalling; the other two install the receptor in a heterologous host (a hamster ovary line, a bone-tumour line). A legitimate design — it controls receptor level and maximises signal-to-noise — but worth naming: two of the three adopted methods for androgen biology read a transfected receptor in a cell with no androgen biology of its own. Controls also differ: Method 1 has a specificity control for the agonist assay but not the antagonist; Methods 2 and 3 have one for the antagonist "to give assurance that what is measured is a competitive antagonist."

Scope, in the guideline's words: it "exclusively addresses transcriptional activation and inhibition of an androgen-regulated reporter gene," and "the results … should not be directly extrapolated to the complex in vivo situation of androgen regulation of any cellular or physiological processes." Conceptual Framework Level 2 — a mechanism screen, like TG 455, TG 493 and TG 454A. No sensitivity/specificity against a reference chemical set appears in the guideline text reached — recorded as a gap, not filled.

The finding that outgrew the endpoint: receptor selectivity here is engineered, not natural. All three methods had to suppress a response to a different receptor — the glucocorticoid receptor. The guideline states why, in Annex E:

"The glucocorticoid receptor (GR) is expressed in 22Rv1 origin cells alongside AR, endogenously. The minimal GR-mediated response can interfere with the AR-mediated response because the GR is structurally similar to the AR and shares hormone response elements that exhibit cross-talk with the AR. To eliminate GR expression in cells, a GR-knockout 22Rv1/MMTV cell line was developed using the … CRISPR/Cas9 system."

And its own comparison table shows three different engineering answers to that one confound: AR-EcoScreen™ — "minimal GR crosstalk due to the selection of an appropriate androgen responsive element"; AR-CALUX® — "no or little GR, ER and PR expression"; 22Rv1 — "GR knock-out."

The steroid receptors (AR, GR, PR, MR) are structural relatives binding overlapping response elements. A reporter downstream of such an element does not know which receptor fired it. Selectivity is not something these assays have — it is something their developers built.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚠️ THE EDGE THAT MATTERS — TG 458 must delete the receptor TG 454A reads. endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation (TG 454A, adopted 2 July 2026) reads the glucocorticoid receptor through an MMTV-luciferase reporter. TG 458's Method 3 reads the androgen receptor through an MMTV LTR reporter — in a cell line where the GR has been knocked out by CRISPR precisely because it would otherwise contaminate the signal. endocrine-er-transactivation's VM7Luc4E2 reads the estrogen receptor through EREs upstream of the same MMTV promoter, and reassures the reader the promoter shows "only minor cross-reactivity with other steroid and non-steroid hormones." One viral promoter; three adopted OECD guidelines; three different receptors; zero cross-citation. Each guideline solved the confound for its own case. Nobody has stated it jointly. Cross-linked in all three maps; logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md as an open question for a human.
  • Consequence for battery reasoning (composes-with, but not independently). A chemical screened across the endocrine Level-2 panel — TG 455 (ER), TG 458 (AR), TG 454A (GR), TG 456 (steroidogenesis) — produces four results that look like four independent mechanistic votes. Three of them read reporters on overlapping response elements, separated by cell-line engineering rather than by biology. Promiscuous steroid-receptor ligands are exactly the chemicals where the votes correlate, and exactly the chemicals the panel most needs to get right. This compounds the artefact TG 454A already documents (interfering/cytotoxic chemicals mimicking competitive antagonists): the endocrine panel now carries two named, shared, structural false-signal mechanisms.
