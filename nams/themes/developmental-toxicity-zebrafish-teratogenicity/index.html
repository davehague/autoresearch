Zebrafish embryo developmental-toxicity assay (teratogenicity) — method

Status: emerging (2026-07-07) — single-lab, developer-run, unqualified. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method (published whole-organism assay with a defined applicability domain and internal optimisation) but regulatory_status: none (used for IND submissions, not formally qualified under ICH S5(R3), not recognised by China's drug agency). Opens the developmental-toxicity (teratogenicity) endpoint — the developmental arm of DART, previously unmapped. Bias flag is load-bearing: developer self-validation (Hunter Biotechnology).

What we believe now

The zebrafish embryo/larval developmental-toxicity assay exposes AB-strain embryos ~4–6 hpf → 5 dpf and scores morphological malformations (pericardial/renal edema, bradycardia, jaw malformation, yolk-sac delay) and embryo-fetal lethality; a teratogenic index (TI = LC25/NOAEL) distinguishes true teratogens from merely generally-toxic compounds. Song et al. (2021) report, post-optimisation, 90.3% sensitivity / 85.7% specificity / 88.9% concordance on 45 ICH S5(R3) reference compounds — strong for a whole-organism NAM, but from a single laboratory (inter-lab study only "being planned") and a method developer. Domain is bounded: three antimetabolite positives (cytarabine, ribavirin, hydroxyurea) were missed. The high numbers are held at arm's length pending independent inter-lab validation — the same discipline applied to the developer-run Liver-Chip (hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps).

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: human-in-the-loop — Same organism and life stage as the FET, a different tier — because this reads a spectrum of malformation types rather than near-binary apical endpoints. The teratogenic index formula is arithmetic, but it is arithmetic over human-scored inputs.

  • Blocker: graded morphological malformation typing (edema, jaw defects) is scored by eye.
  • Escape hatch, now demonstrated but not walked through (2026-07-15). Sivaprasad et al. 2026 (zebrafish-machine-vision-malformation-scoring) train a Vision Transformer on time-lapse embryo microscopy that detects developmental malformations at 92% accuracy (and fertility at 98%) on a research benchmark. That is the first concrete evidence the eye-scoring blocker is learnable — but it is a single unvalidated study, still slower than a human, and a binary call, not the graded typing the regulatory readout needs. The tier does NOT move: a trained human is still load-bearing in the actual call. What moves is the blocker's status — from "architecturally scored by eye" to "scored by eye, with a demonstrated machine-vision path that no one has validated." Loop-ready is a validated claim; this is a proof-of-concept.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Same organism, different biology (contrast — NOT redundancy): this assay and the FET/TG 236 acute-toxicity test fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet both use zebrafish embryos, but read different endpoints — here, human developmental hazard / teratogenicity (structural malformation, TI); there, aquatic apical lethality (ecotox). The shared model must not collapse the two: same fish, different question.
  • DART sibling (composes-with, methodological complement): covers the developmental/teratogenicity arm of DART that the neuro-specific DNT battery developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb does not — the two are complementary DART maps (structural malformation vs neurodevelopmental process disruption), not overlapping.
  • Toxicokinetic latent edge: a whole-organism assay reads an internal exposure directly, so it does not yet invoke the PBK/QIVIVE dose-anchoring layer pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics the cell-based batteries need — flagged as a future edge if quantitative potency extrapolation is attempted.
  • Global-ripple: distinct endpoint from the skin/eye local maps and the genotox/carcinogenicity maps — a coverage-breadth addition that opens the developmental-toxicity column. Reproductive (fertility / multigenerational) toxicity remains unmapped — this closes the developmental, not the reproductive, blank.
  • ⚠️ The machine-vision escape hatch is a cross-map lever, not a one-map fact (2026-07-15). This is one of three human-in-the-loop maps whose sole blocker is a human eye scoring images — genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus (micronuclei down a microscope) and carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay (morphological foci) are the other two. Sivaprasad 2026 demonstrates the class of solution (a spatiotemporal image model learning the score) on zebrafish; whichever of these three attracts a validated automated scorer first is the vault's next likely human-in-the-loop → automatable-open-loop promotion. Watch for it, per stages/01-sweep.
  • ⚠️ Reads directly against the agenda-drift finding. automation-self-driving-labs warns that automation flows to what is already automatable and away from the rest. The eye-scoring maps are exactly the "rest" — and this paper is a datapoint that the frontier is (slowly, from academia rather than the regulatory-tox field) reaching toward them. The finding cuts both ways: the escape hatch is being built, but by an AI-security lab benchmarking a dataset, not by anyone trying to close a regulatory tox loop — the crossover automation-self-driving-labs says has not yet happened.
