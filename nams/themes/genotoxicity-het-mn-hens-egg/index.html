What we believe now

HET-MN is not a primary genotoxicity test — it is a follow-up. When a chemical tests positive on a standard in vitro assay (classically genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus, OECD TG 487), HET-MN asks whether that positive survives inside a whole, metabolically intact organism. Fertilised chicken eggs are dosed at the shell membrane on day 8 of development (not legally an animal experiment under EU law); blood is drawn on day 11 and micronucleus frequency scored in circulating erythrocytes. Reisinger et al. 2022 report the formal three-laboratory validation: 34 chemicals, 123 coded experiments, 29 chemicals in the predictivity calculation (two excluded for unconfirmed tissue exposure due to poor solubility). Against historical reference data: 84% sensitivity (range 67–89% across the three labs), 98% specificity, 91% accuracy, 92% within-lab and 87% between-lab reproducibility.

The mechanistic point, and why this vault cares beyond one assay's numbers. The standard in vitro genotoxicity battery's dominant known failure mode is false positives from cells that lack metabolic competence — the same gap thakkar-2026-fragrance-genotoxicity-false-positives put a number on the previous night (up to 93% false-positive rate for cosmetic/fragrance ingredients). HET-MN's whole-embryo format has working ADME that a culture well does not, which is exactly why its specificity (98%) so outperforms the in vitro micronucleus assay's own cited specificity against in vivo data (30.8%, different cell line, not a clean head-to-head, but the direction is the point). HET-MN does not fix the upstream battery's blind spot — it is a downstream check that catches some of what the blind spot lets through, after the chemical has already triggered a follow-up.

Brakes. Sensitivity varies 67–89% across the three validating labs — wider than the headline reproducibility statistics suggest. funding_flag: [industry] — Cosmetics Europe funded the validation phase that produced these numbers and selected the test chemicals; several authors carry Henkel affiliations; the paper's "None declared" COI statement is recorded as stated, not as resolving the tie. The exposure route (shell-membrane dosing) is explicitly described by the authors as closer to intravenous than oral administration — an applicability caveat, not a flaw, but one a reader should not round off.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: human-in-the-loop. The readout (micronucleus count) requires a trained person at a microscope; no automated or machine-vision scoring is discussed in the founding validation. This is the same shape as genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus's own blocker before that map's machine-vision escape hatch arrived (imaging flow cytometry + deep learning, three labs, 2026-07-16) — no equivalent evidence has been shown yet for whole-embryo erythrocyte scoring specifically, and the vault should watch for whether that escape hatch transfers here.

⚠ Correlated blind spot check

None set, and worth stating why explicitly rather than leaving it implicit. HET-MN is purpose-built to escape the metabolic-competence blind spot that genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation and the standard in vitro micronucleus assay share (both can miss or wrongly flag compounds depending on bioactivation) — it adds ADME, it does not lack it. A correlated-blind-spot flag would be the wrong call here: this assay's whole job is to be the thing that does NOT share that failure mode with its upstream battery.
