Macromolecular ordered-matrix eye test (OECD TG 496) — eye irritation / serious eye damage — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-15) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 496, adopted 25 June 2024, corrected 2 July 2026 — the vault is reading the current edition, confirmed from the archived PDF). Adoption earns consensus deterministically. The second component method mapped underneath the eye defined approach (eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da, TG 467) — and the first eye method in the vault that uses no cells and no tissue at all.

What we believe now

A freeze-dried macromolecular reagent (proteins, glycoproteins, carbohydrates, lipids) is rehydrated and self-assembles into an ordered matrix that mimics the transparent, highly-organised structure of the cornea. A test chemical is applied; how much it denatures that matrix is read as optical density at 405 nm, converted to an Irritection Draize Equivalent (IDE) score, and compared to fixed prediction-model cut-offs. This is in chemico — a test-tube protein-chemistry reaction, no biology.

Two validated methods sit in the guideline, doing two different jobs:

The honest weak spot is OI®'s sensitivity. As a positive detector of true eye hazards, OI® misses nearly half (54% sensitivity) — which is exactly why the guideline never lets it stand alone and frames it as a rule-out / initial-step method inside a Bottom-Up strategy, not a classifier. OptiSafe, in its No-Category rule-out role, is doing that one job well: 100% sensitivity in-set (no false negatives), ~18% of safe chemicals over-called onward — the cheap direction. Both methods, like the RhCE test, are graded by the reliability of the negative call, not the specificity of the positive.

Scope, hard. "Cannot be used on their own to replace the in vivo Draize eye test." Neither resolves Category 2 (reversible irritation) at all — a No-Category call clears a chemical; anything else routes onward. Gases and aerosols are outside the applicability domain.

funding_flag: method-developer — both are proprietary commercial kits (Ocular Irritection®; OptiSafe™ EIT, Lebrun Labs). A test-system dependency, not sponsored science; OptiSafe's inclusion was independently peer-reviewed by the OECD.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — optical density at 405 nm against a fixed IDE cut-off. One of the purest loop-ready shapes in the vault: no cells, no scoring, no judgment — the same clean arithmetic as skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435 (breakthrough time) and phototoxicity-ros-assay (in-chemico ROS). Carries a domain_gate: the acellular matrix has no metabolic competence, so a loop must not trust a clean IDE score on a chemical whose hazard is metabolite-mediated; and gases/aerosols are out of domain.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Component of the eye defined approach (composes-with): TG 496 is a second information source under eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da (TG 467), joining eye-irritation-rhce-no-category (TG 492). The eye column now has a hub + two components shape, deepening the pattern the skin-sensitisation column set (a DA hub over TG 442C/D/E).
  • ⚠️ The GOOD case for the blind-spot rail — orthogonal readouts in one adopted battery. TG 496 reads protein-matrix denaturation in a tube; TG 492 reads living-tissue metabolic viability (MTT/WST). These are mechanically independent ways to fail — a chemical that denatures corneal protein and a chemical that kills corneal cells need not be the same chemical. So unlike the aquatic pair (fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet ↔ ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line, shared missing organs) or the endocrine MMTV panel (shared reporter backbone), the two eye components are closer to two independent votes than one. This is the counter-design automation-tox21-qhts-platform found in production stated as a design principle: the remedy for a correlated blind spot is a measurement that fails differently, not a bigger battery — and here an adopted regulatory battery is already built that way.
  • The shared limitation, stated so the independence is not over-sold: both eye components are bench chemistry/tissue with no metabolic competence and no model of the intact eye's inflammatory/repair cascade. Their votes are independent on the mechanism they each read; both are still blind to metabolite-mediated eye hazard and to delayed effects. Independence is real but not total — a loop should treat concordant No-Category calls as strong for direct-acting chemicals and weak for bioactivation-suspect ones (domain_gate).
  • In-chemico loop-ready family (composes-with, cross-endpoint): joins phototoxicity-ros-assay (TG 495) and skin-corrosion-membrane-barrier-tg435 (TG 435) as adopted, cell-free, OD/threshold methods that are closed-loop-ready by construction. The vault's cleanest evidence that the escape from human interpretation runs through chemistry, not biology — the fewer living components, the more deterministic the call.
  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. Refutes nothing, duplicates nothing, moves no other method's status or loop tier. Its only edges are internal to the eye column plus the in-chemico loop-ready family.

Added 2026-07-19 (TG 494 ingest) — this map's independence claim gets a control group

No status moves, no loop tier moves, no frontmatter change. But the 2026-07-15 finding recorded here — that TG 496 and TG 492 are "closer to two independent votes than one" — was, until tonight, a claim with nothing to contrast against inside its own battery. It now has one.

eye-irritation-vitrigel-tg494 joined the eye column as a third component: barrier integrity (TEER) in a living human corneal epithelium. It is correlated with TG 492 — same tissue, and TG 494's guideline states that barrier loss "subsequently" causes the cell death TG 492 measures. correlated_blind_spot is set on both of them, and not on this map.

That contrast is the useful thing. The eye battery now contains, side by side, a component pair that fails similarly and a component (this one) that fails differently — the bad case and the good case of the rail, under one adopted defined approach, distinguishable by exactly the property the rail names: not what the assay is made of, but whether its failure mode is shared. TG 496 is independent of the tissue methods because it has no tissue at all — the in-chemico escape recorded here on 2026-07-15, now with the counterfactual visible.

The vault's cleanest statement of the principle to date, and it is the number a loop should carry: the eye defined approach composes three components and roughly two independent mechanisms. Battery size and battery independence moved in opposite directions the moment a third component was added. The unchanged honest caveat still binds this map too — all three components lack metabolic competence, so their independence is real on what each reads and jointly absent on metabolite-mediated eye hazard.
