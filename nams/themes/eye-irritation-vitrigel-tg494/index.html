Vitrigel-Eye Irritancy Test (OECD TG 494) — eye irritation / serious eye damage — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-19) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 494, current edition corrected 2 July 2026, read from the archived PDF). Adoption earns consensus deterministically. The third component method mapped under the eye defined approach (eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da, TG 467), closing a gap that has stood as plain text in vault/index.md since 2026-07-11.

What we believe now

Human corneal epithelial (hCE) cells are grown on a collagen vitrigel membrane into a tissue with a working barrier. A chemical is applied for three minutes while Transepithelial Electrical Resistance (TEER) — how strongly the tissue resists a small electric current — is measured continuously. An irritant breaks the barrier and the resistance falls; the readout is the shape of the falling curve over time.

The guideline's mechanistic case for measuring barrier rather than viability matters, and it comes back below: irritant chemicals "first destroy tear film and epithelial barrier function of the eye, subsequently induce epithelial cell death, and finally produce stromal degeneration."

The decision rule, complete. Three indexes from the mean TEER curve — time lag (t1), intensity (−[P2 − P1]/[t2 − t1]), plateau level (100 − P2):

CriteriaPrediction
time lag > 180 s AND intensity < 0.05 %/s AND plateau ≤ 5.0 %No Category — "no further testing … is considered necessary"
any one of those fails"No Stand-alone Prediction Can Be Made" — routes onward

A rule-out only. TG 494 "is not intended to identify nor differentiate between UN GHS Category 1 and UN GHS Category 2," nor 2A from 2B. A negative clears a chemical; anything else is not a positive call at all. Same one-directional shape as TG 492 and the TG 496 methods.

Reproducibility: 80–100% within-laboratory across three labs, 92% between-laboratory.

Read the 83% → 96% honestly: the assay did not improve, the question got smaller. A third of chemicals (32%) were removed by pre-test before those figures were computed. Both surviving false negatives were Cat. 2B — mild irritants, the cheapest class to miss. And the guideline's verdict on the unfiltered configuration is unusually candid: it "would not be the method of choice because of its limited predictivity (i.e., low specificity)." Specificity of 61–71% means roughly one safe chemical in three is pushed onward for more testing — the cheap direction for a rule-out tier, but not free.

funding_flag: method-developer — proprietary test system (the Vitrigel hCE model, from Yamaguchi & Takezawa) and a named reference instrument. The counterweight is real: validation was run by a JaCVAM-organised management team with ICATM across three Japanese laboratories and independently peer-reviewed by an international panel. A national validation body, not a developer marking its own work.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: closed-loop-ready — electrodes in, curve out, three arithmetic indexes, three fixed thresholds, one boolean AND. No human judgment enters the call at any point. Joins the vault's twenty other top-tier maps.

But the tier is not the finding. The vault has twenty closed-loop-ready methods; a twenty-first is not news. What is new is the domain_gate.

Every other domain gate in the vault is a fact the loop must be told — TG 435's pH window, TG 249's log KOW bounds, TG 496's "no metabolism," TG 495's solubility limit. TG 494's is a pre-test the same robot can run: measure pH, watch for phase separation, take an absorbance reading at 0 and 3 minutes, compare to stated numbers. A machine can determine by measurement on the sample in front of it that this assay is not competent to answer for this chemical.

shared/evidence-model.md warns that "a closed-loop-ready tier does not mean 'trust the DIP on any chemical' — it means trust it inside the domain." That has always been an obligation on whoever builds the loop, discharged by knowing things. TG 494 puts the check inside the protocol, where a loop cannot skip it by not knowing. See machine-checkable-applicability-domain-gate.

The honest limit on that praise. A machine-checkable gate covers the failures someone anticipated. TG 494 is equally blind to metabolite-mediated eye hazard, and no pre-test detects that — it is carried in the domain_gate field the old way, as a caveat. And a loop reporting "96% sensitive" without also reporting that it refused 32% of chemicals is misrepresenting itself. No OECD Test Guideline template requires a refusal rate to be reported. (Vault observation, flagged as one.)

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Third component of the eye defined approach (composes-with). Joins eye-irritation-rhce-no-category (TG 492) and eye-macromolecular-ordered-matrix-tg496 (TG 496) under eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da (TG 467). The eye column now has a hub + three components shape — the deepest battery structure in the vault, ahead of skin sensitisation's hub + three.

  • ⚠️ THE RAIL FIRES — and this is the sharpest version of it the vault has recorded. correlated_blind_spot: [eye-irritation-rhce-no-category], set on both maps.

    TG 492 reads metabolic viability of a living human corneal epithelium (MTT/WST; > 60% viability → No Category). TG 494 reads barrier integrity of a living human corneal epithelium (TEER). Different instruments, different numbers — the same tissue, and, by the guideline's own account, two points on one causal chain. TG 494's rationale states that irritants destroy barrier function and "subsequently induce epithelial cell death."

    Every previous blind-spot pair in this vault was a vault inference from shared structure. FET ↔ RTgill-W1: the vault reasoned that neither has a nervous system or metabolism. The endocrine MMTV panel: the vault noticed three guidelines sharing a promoter backbone, none citing the others. TTR-ANSA ↔ TTR-FITC: the vault read a shared molecular initiating event out of a taxonomy.

    Here the guideline says it. TG 494 asserts that the event TG 492 measures is downstream of the event TG 494 measures. That is not two independent ways to be wrong about an eye — it is one mechanism sampled twice, early and late. A chemical that damages the cornea by a route neither model supports (most obviously, one requiring metabolic bioactivation — neither tissue has metabolic competence) reads clean on both, and reads clean concordantly.

    For a loop this is the exact failure mode the rail exists for. An automated system running the eye battery sees TG 492 and TG 494 agree, scores that as replication, and raises its confidence — when the agreement is close to structural. Speed converts the bias into a compounding one.

  • ⚠️ And the good news sits right beside it — the battery is 3 components but roughly 2 votes. eye-macromolecular-ordered-matrix-tg496 (TG 496) reads denaturation of an acellular protein matrix in a tube — no cells, no tissue, no viability, no barrier. It is mechanically independent of both living-tissue methods, which is why the vault recorded it on 2026-07-15 as the good case: the automation-tox21-qhts-platform counter-design ("the remedy for a correlated blind spot is a measurement that fails differently, not a bigger battery") already built into an adopted guideline.

    Tonight sharpens that finding rather than softening it. The eye battery now demonstrates both halves of the principle in one place: adding TG 494 added a component that fails similarly to one already present, while TG 496 remains the component that fails differently. Battery size and battery independence moved in opposite directions. A loop counting components counts three; a loop counting independent mechanisms counts about two. This is the vault's cleanest demonstration that those are different numbers — and it is now visible inside a single adopted regulatory battery rather than argued across two.

    Honest caveat, unchanged from 2026-07-15: TG 496 is independent on the mechanism it reads, and all three components are jointly blind to metabolite-mediated eye hazard. Independence is real, not total.

  • In-domain rule-out family (composes-with, cross-endpoint). Its shape — a one-directional negative call, high sensitivity, mediocre specificity, "positives" that are not classifications — matches eye-irritation-rhce-no-category (95–99% sens / 63–72% spec), phototoxicity-ros-assay (100% sens / 42–82% spec), and TG 496's OptiSafe (100% sens / 82% spec). Grade a rule-out tier on the reliability of its negative call, not the specificity of its positive — the vault's 2026-07-11 correction, holding for a fourth method.

  • ⚠️ Composes-with deep-learning-dart-prediction-recall-gap — the same idea, arrived at from the model side the same night. That study carries a computed applicability domain (nearest-neighbour distance on molecular fingerprints, threshold mean + 0.5σ) and excludes out-of-domain predictions from evaluation. TG 494 measures its domain with a pH probe and a plate reader. A wet assay and a machine-learning model, from different continents and different literatures, independently building the same capability: the ability to determine that you are not entitled to answer. For an autonomous lab that may be the most transferable design pattern in tonight's run — a loop that cannot decline is a loop that will confidently answer questions it has no standing on. (Vault inference; neither source frames it this way.)

  • Global-ripple: walked every other map. Refutes nothing, duplicates nothing, and moves no other method's status or loop tier. Its edges run to the eye column (hub + two siblings), the cross-endpoint rule-out family, and the domain-gate pattern. It does not touch the skin, genotoxicity, endocrine, DART, phototoxicity or ecotox columns.
