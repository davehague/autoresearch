What we believe now

  • 2026-08-31 — MAP OPENED. aim-mash-first-fully-qualified-ai-drug-development-tool. FDA's Biomarker Qualification Program granted full qualification — not a Letter of Intent — to AIM-MASH AI Assist (PathAI), an AI tool scoring liver biopsy images for MASH (metabolic dysfunction–associated steatohepatitis) disease activity: steatosis, hepatocellular ballooning, lobular inflammation, fibrosis. Qualified Context of Use: enrollment and endpoint assessment in Phase 2/3 MASH clinical trials. Backed by a validation study across >1,400 clinical-trial biopsies reporting non-inferiority to individual pathologist reads (steatosis, fibrosis), superiority (ballooning, inflammation, reproducibility). EMA had already qualified the same tool earlier in 2025 — dual Western-regulator acceptance before this US announcement (9 December 2025). A pathologist's accept/reject of every AI score remains a required step; the tool is not qualified for diagnostic use.

    Why this earns a tenth map rather than folding into regulatory-acceptance-fda-istand-ddt-qualification: that map is scoped specifically to FDA's ISTAND pathway (novel methodologies with no existing qualification home), currently holding two Letter-of-Intent-stage instances with no public performance data. This is a different FDA program (the Biomarker Qualification Program, an established track for imaging/biomarker tools) reaching completion, not entry. Filing it under ISTAND would misname the mechanism; the vault's regulatory-acceptance group already distinguishes gates by shape (policy/framework/plan/strategy/list/route/receipt/pathway), and "an established program's first completed AI qualification" is a shape none of the other nine holds.

    Composes with, does not duplicate: regulatory-acceptance-fda-istand-ddt-qualification — same underlying thesis (FDA treats AI models as reusable regulatory tools), different program, opposite end of the maturity spectrum (this one closed its file; ISTAND's two instances just opened theirs). regulatory-acceptance-ai-model-credibility — the FDA/EMA AI Guiding Principles this instance concretely satisfies: a mandatory human accept/reject step is a textbook case of "human-centric by design" and assessing "the complete system including human–AI interactions."

    What this is explicitly NOT: an animal-replacement instance. MASH biopsy scoring assesses human-disease severity in a human clinical trial; it retires no animal toxicology study. Held in the regulatory-acceptance group for the mechanism, not for touching the vault's usual scope.
