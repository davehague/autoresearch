What we believe now

Steatosis is fat accumulating inside liver cells. It is the first stage of fatty liver disease — the most prevalent liver pathology there is — it is usually silent, and evidence increasingly implicates chemical exposure in causing and worsening it. It is not the same failure mode as the two the vault already tracks: not cells dying (drug-induced liver injury) and not bile backing up (cholestasis). A battery built for those two does not see it.

The method has three layers and is anchored in an adverse outcome pathway network — a published causal chain from molecular trigger to organ harm — which is why its readouts are the ones they are rather than whatever was convenient to measure.

  1. In silico — predict the trigger. A tiered computational workflow combining (Q)SAR (predicting activity from chemical structure), read-across (inferring from structurally similar chemicals with data) and molecular docking (simulating whether the molecule physically fits a receptor's binding site) predicts whether the chemical perturbs the ligand-activated transcription factors that switch fat-handling gene programmes on and off. These are the molecular initiating events.
  2. In vitro — measure the consequences. HepaRG human liver cells are exposed and measured on seven key events downstream: lipid accumulation, fatty-acid uptake, mitochondrial β-oxidation (fat burning), lipid secretion, mitochondrial dysfunction, endoplasmic-reticulum stress, oxidative stress.
  3. Integrate — one number. A "steatogenic score" combining biological relevance and response magnitude across all endpoints.

What was shown: seven reference steatogenic chemicals — the epilepsy drug sodium valproate, the antibiotic tetracycline, the heart-rhythm drug amiodarone, the pesticide cyproconazole, and three plasticisers of which two are PFAS — scored above two known-clean controls (minocycline, tartaric acid), at three concentrations each.

What that is worth: the method is not broken. That is the honest ceiling on nine pre-labelled compounds. Two negative controls cannot establish a false-positive rate; seven positives cannot establish a false-negative rate; and nothing here defines an applicability domain. The vault has a live cautionary example — developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb's roughly 60% specificity, about two in five clean chemicals wrongly flagged, which only became visible at scale.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop. Every readout is instrument-readable — plate reader or high-content imaging — and the in-silico layer is software by construction. The pipeline runs end to end in machines and produces a number.

Then it stops at the same place nineteen other maps stop. "Clearly distinguished" describes a figure, not a decision procedure. No cut-off is published, no performance against reference chemicals is published, and without those an automated system can compute this score and cannot act on it.

One structural feature is genuinely different, and it is not an improvement. The in-silico layer decides what the wet layer measures — a model selecting the experiment, inside a regulatory-toxicology method rather than a materials-chemistry platform. That is the front half of a closed loop, arriving natively in this literature, which is the crossover this vault has watched for since 2026-07-12. It is a weak instance: the tiering is fixed, nothing updates on the wet result, there is one iteration and no adaptivity. And selection is a blind-spot generator — if the in-silico layer is systematically wrong for a chemical class it will systematically decline to measure the key events that would have caught it, and unlike a battery that runs everything, a pipeline that selects produces no evidence of what it skipped. See in-silico-layer-chooses-what-the-wet-layer-measures.

⚠️ Correlated blind spot — three liver methods, one cell line

The vault now holds three hepatic methods, and HepaRG is inside all of them: this one, metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg (OECD Test Guideline 445A, adopted, closed-loop-ready) and hepatotoxicity-cholestasis-battery (which already carries the intra-battery form of this flag).

Tonight the rail acquires a measured basis rather than an argued one. cell-line-identity-outweighs-culture-medium-in-hepatic-transcriptomes finds that in hepatic models, cell-line identity is the dominant source of transcriptomic variance — roughly 12,600–14,100 genes separate one line from another or from real human liver cells, against 1,000–2,300 for swapping the entire culture medium. Choosing the cell line is a bigger decision than any of the reagent decisions the vault was previously worried about, and it is made once, silently, before any assay is designed.

So a hepatic testing battery that composes steatosis with cholestasis and CYP induction has not taken three independent readings of liver biology. It has taken one cell line's biology, three times. Anything HepaRG expresses atypically — a transporter, an enzyme, a nuclear receptor — is wrong in all three at once, in the same direction, and the three will agree with each other while being wrong.

For a loop this is the worst version of the rail. An automated system reading three concordant hepatic results will treat the concordance as corroboration, trust its decision rule, and iterate fast in the wrong direction. Speed converts a shared substrate into a compounding error. Flagged on all three maps.
