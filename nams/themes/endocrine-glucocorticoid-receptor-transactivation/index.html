Human glucocorticoid receptor transactivation (OECD TG 454A) — endocrine (non-EATS) — method

Status: consensus (2026-07-10) — regulatorily adopted on both axes. evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 454A, published 2 July 2026). Underneath the adoption sits formally-validated reproducibility from an independent blinded four-laboratory ring trial (Rivero-Arze et al. 2025, PMID 40683478) and an OECD peer review of that validation (October 2024 – February 2025).

What we believe now

The glucocorticoid receptor (GR) is a ligand-activated nuclear-receptor transcription factor governing stress response, inflammation, immune function and metabolism. Bound by cortisol or a synthetic glucocorticoid, it homodimerises, binds glucocorticoid response elements (GREs) in target-gene DNA, and modulates transcription. TG 454A's test system is HMLN-hGR: an immortalised human HeLa line stably transfected with an MMTV-luciferase GRE reporter and a GRα expression plasmid (pSG5-hGR). Luminescence reports transactivation in agonist mode (chemical alone) and antagonist mode (chemical against a fixed agonist concentration, commonly ~80% of maximal induction). Reference chemicals, from the guideline: dexamethasone (strong agonist; positive control and proficiency chemical), cortisol (natural ligand, proficiency chemical), mifepristone (antagonist, proficiency and reference chemical).

Why this is a new column and not a deeper one. OECD endocrine testing is organised around the EATS modalities — Estrogen, Androgen, Thyroid, Steroidogenesis. This vault's endocrine entry has been the S: endocrine-steroidogenesis-h295r (TG 456). The guideline states the GR "was identified as playing a key role in several non-EATS endocrine pathways" and records that OECD recommended GR transactivation assays for its Conceptual Framework as early as 2012. TG 454A is the first adopted OECD in-vitro method for a non-EATS receptor. It sits at Level 2 of the Conceptual Framework — "in vitro assays providing data about selected endocrine mechanism(s)/pathway(s)" — i.e. a mechanism screen, not an apical determination.

Read the numbers for what they are. The ring trial gave 97% agreement between all four laboratories on activity classification and 99.6% within-laboratory concordance (one deviating triplicate in 272), across 34 chemicals. These are the tightest reproducibility figures in this vault. They are not sensitivity/specificity against a reference set of known human GR-active chemicals, and not predictivity against any apical in-vivo endocrine outcome. Reproducibility is a precondition for predictivity, not evidence of it. No predictivity figure exists in the sources reached. Treating 97% as an accuracy would be exactly the two-axis error this vault exists to prevent.

A documented false-positive mechanism, in the guideline's own words. "Some chemicals suspected to have an antagonistic effect were shown to be interfering chemicals and not specific GR antagonists." Luciferase inhibition or plain cytotoxicity depresses the signal exactly as a competitive antagonist does; the method requires an explicit discrimination step. The antagonist call is structurally weaker than the agonist call.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop — Luminescence is machine-readable, but discriminating real antagonism from cytotoxic interference is a judgment call the guideline does not reduce to a rule.

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • ⚠️ ADDED 2026-07-12 — the vault's other endocrine guidelines have to DELETE this receptor to work. endocrine-ar-transactivation (TG 458) reads the androgen receptor, and its Method 3 uses the 22Rv1/MMTV_GR-KO line — a prostate carcinoma line whose glucocorticoid receptor has been knocked out by CRISPR/Cas9. TG 458 states why: "the GR is expressed in 22Rv1 origin cells alongside AR, endogenously. The minimal GR-mediated response can interfere with the AR-mediated response because the GR is structurally similar to the AR and shares hormone response elements that exhibit cross-talk with the AR." All three TG 458 methods engineer the GR response away, by three different routes. And the reporter backbone in Method 3 is the MMTV LTR — the same promoter this map's HMLN-hGR line uses to read the GR, and the same promoter endocrine-er-transactivation's VM7Luc4E2 line uses to read the estrogen receptor. One viral promoter, three adopted OECD guidelines, three different receptors, zero cross-citation. This map's Status does not move — TG 454A remains consensus, and its own validation is unaffected — but its specificity claim is now contextualised: GR/AR/ER reporter assays are separated by cell-line engineering, not by biology, and the endocrine Level-2 panel is less independent than four results make it look. See steroid-receptor-crosstalk-shared-reporter-confound. This compounds the antagonist-mode interference artefact already documented below: the panel now carries two named, shared, structural false-signal mechanisms. Logged in docs/open-gaps-log.md for a human.
  • Endocrine sibling, different modality (composes-with, not redundant): endocrine-steroidogenesis-h295r (TG 456) measures hormone synthesis (testosterone, 17β-estradiol) in a female adrenocortical line — the S of EATS. TG 454A measures receptor transactivation at the GR — outside EATS entirely. Two Level-2 mechanism screens on different nodes of endocrine biology; they compose into a broader Conceptual-Framework Level-2 panel and duplicate nothing. Together they now give the vault a synthesis arm and a receptor arm.
  • Immune/inflammatory overlap (composes-with, flagged as a genuine ambiguity): GR is a master regulator of inflammation and immune function, and immunotoxicity-il2-luc (TG 444A) reads T-cell cytokine transcription (IL-2, IFN-γ) — a process glucocorticoids classically suppress. A chemical that is a GR agonist would be expected to suppress IL-2 promoter activity. So a positive TG 444A immunosuppression call and a positive TG 454A agonist call may be the same mechanism read twice, in two different endpoints. Neither guideline addresses the other. This is the vault's first candidate cross-endpoint confound rather than a battery composition — flagged for a human, not adopted, and logged as an open gap.
  • Not fertility (an anti-edge, stated to prevent a category error): reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams exists precisely because adopted endocrine mechanism screens keep getting mistaken for fertility tests. TG 454A is a non-EATS receptor screen; it is even further from a fertility endpoint than TG 455/458/493/456 are. It must not be filed as narrowing the fertility blank. Recorded here because the same error was made about TG 456 and corrected on 2026-07-09.
  • Reporter-gene methodology family (technology, not biology): immunotoxicity-il2-luc (cytokine promoter → luciferase), genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa (GFP reporters in mESC) and this map (GRE → luciferase) are all stably transfected reporter lines. They share a failure mode worth naming once: reporter interference and cytotoxicity masquerading as a real signal, which is exactly the antagonist-mode artefact TG 454A documents. Cross-linked as a family.
  • Metabolic overlap (weak, real): GR agonism perturbs metabolism, and metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg (TG 445A) reads PXR/CAR/AhR-driven CYP induction. Nuclear-receptor cross-talk between GR and PXR is known in the literature but is not tested by any source read in this run — noted as a plausible edge, not asserted.
