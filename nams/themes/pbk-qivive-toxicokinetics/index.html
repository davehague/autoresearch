PBK modelling / QIVIVE — ADME & toxicokinetics — method

Status: consolidating (2026-07-06) — split axes. evidence_tier: formally-validated (mature, internationally-agreed OECD framework) but regulatory_status: under-validation (adopted guidance document, accepted case-by-case per submission — not a blanket-adopted method with its own TG). The vault's first entry for the ADME / toxicokinetics endpoint — the "connective tissue" that makes in-vitro potency human-relevant, previously a headline coverage gap and the flagged next-sweep priority.

Sub-threads: none — one argument at 10 dated bullets: this is the dose bridge, and every bullet measures how well it carries a load or names what a body will let it carry. The apparent split between "regulators adopting it" and "studies benchmarking it" is not two arguments — the regulatory bullets exist because the benchmarks came out the way they did, and separating them would file the acceptance without its evidence. The endpoint-by-endpoint accuracy spread (liver good, development catastrophic) is the argument's content, not a strand of it. [reviewed 2026-08-08 @ 10 bullets]

What we believe now

PBK models represent absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion across physiological compartments; QIVIVE uses them (forward or reverse dosimetry) to translate an in-vitro bioactive concentration into a human-equivalent external dose. OECD GD 331 (Series No. 331 / ENV/CBC/MONO(2021)1) is the adopted framework for characterising, validating and reporting these models, explicitly supporting models parameterised from in-vitro/in-silico data. It is an enabling layer, not a hazard assay — it certifies how a model is built and defended, with acceptance decided per submission. Adoption momentum is forward: 2021 GD → 2025 QIVIVE principles.

Loop readiness

loop_readiness: computational-connective — Not an assay. This is the model that updates — PBK/QIVIVE is built to be reparameterised as new in-vitro data arrives (TG 445A now feeds it directly), which is precisely the model updates leg of the loop. It is also the dose bridge: it converts an in-vitro concentration into a human dose, without which a closed loop produces numbers nobody can act on.

  • 2026-07-07 — Regulatory-axis datapoint (a body picks it up for a real programme): EFSA's PPR Panel applied PBK/QIVIVE reverse dosimetry to DNT-IVB data for pesticide active substances (Scientific Opinion, 2025) — unbound in-vitro Cmax → PBK-predicted plasma Cmax/Css → oral equivalent dose.   • 2026-07-10 — A method that FEEDS this hub, rather than consuming it. Status holds at consolidating. metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg (OECD TG 445A, adopted 2 July 2026) is the vault's first adopted in-vitro biotransformation method. PBK models require metabolic clearance and enzyme-induction parameters as inputs; every other map here consumes the dose bridge, and this one supplies a girder for it. GD 331 explicitly supports models "parameterised from in-vitro/in-silico data" — TG 445A is now an adopted OECD route to one such parameter. The hub's own regulatory_status is untouched (still guidance, case-by-case), but the in-vitro parameterisation story it rests on just gained a Test Guideline underneath it.

  • 2026-07-22 — A regulator states this map's ceiling in its own words. The UK Replacing animals in science strategy (regulatory-acceptance-uk-animal-replacement-strategy) puts dedicated pharmacokinetic studies in basket 2 — alternatives exist but are not validated enough — and says flatly: "There are currently no alternative methods which fully predict in vivo pharmacokinetics." The target is a reduction, not a replacement: "reduce the use of dogs and non-human primates in dedicated PK studies for human medicines by at least 35% by 2030." Nothing here moves this map's axes; the value is external corroboration, from a government rather than from the modelling community, that the computational-connective layer is not yet standing alone on kinetics. → uk-three-basket-readiness-taxonomy

  • 2026-07-24 — The bridge gets measured against the regulator's own published numbers, and its accuracy is set by the endpoint. Status HELD at consolidating; loop_readiness HELD at computational-connective. Ingested Chang, Allen, Kleinstreuer & Mumtaz 2026 (Toxicological Sciences 209(3):kfag017, PMID 41703654) — 35 priority chemicals, curated ToxCast/Tox21 activity concentrations (EPA invitrodb v3.5) run backwards through the httk PBTK model in NICEATM's Integrated Chemical Environment to an equivalent administered dose, benchmarked against in-vivo points of departure and ATSDR minimal risk levels. (1) The split is the finding: liver 9/10 within 15-fold; nervous system 8/16 within 10-fold; developing fetus 1/6 within 10-fold, the misses 83–250-fold out. Against the published guidance values the spread runs to 5,745-fold (neuro), 2,556-fold (hepato) and 17,042-fold (developmental). The abstract's "PoDs for a majority of chemicals can be predicted" is defensible for liver, arguable for nerve, and false for development; the vault grades on the results section. The developmental result compounds a blank the vault already carries — reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams (bench-bound), developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb (contested), developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity (emerging) — and it is an independent failure, at the extrapolation layer rather than the assay layer. (2) The coverage ceiling is upstream of this map: "over 50% of tested chemicals lacked cHTS data or failed quality control" — thirteen years after automation-tox21-qhts-platform's robot started running, half the priority chemicals still have no usable screening data. (3) ⚠️ The design hits the ceiling named two days earlier: every success criterion here is agreement with an animal result, which is exactly what next-generation-validation-replaces-the-ring-trial proposed replacing with mechanistic anchoring — and Kleinstreuer is a co-author of both. Vault inference, flagged: concordance with the rodent is the only large labelled signal this field has, so it is what a self-improving loop would actually optimise, including the rodent's species-specific errors. funding_flag: none commercially; the authorship is NICEATM (the validation body) with ATSDR (the agency publishing the benchmark values), which is well-placed and directionally interested. 35 chemicals, 6 of them developmental — a signal, not a verdict. → ivive-reproduces-the-animal-answer-for-liver-and-not-for-development, ivive-benchmarked-against-the-animal-answer-hits-the-named-ceiling

  • 2026-07-31 — A regulator gives this model layer a job and refuses it another, in writing. Status and tier both HELD. efsa-2026-waiving-dog-studies-agrochemicals places PBK modelling at the centre of a published procedure for waiving a legally required animal study: the flowchart's terminal step is an interspecies comparison of internal exposure supported by PBK, and if the dog adds nothing there, the study may be waived. That is the most consequential regulatory job this map has been given — a model deciding an experiment does not need to happen. And the same opinion draws the boundary immediately: PBK is "a tool and not for the derivation of HBGVs. The application of standard regulatory uncertainty factors remains unchanged." A model may say which experiment to skip; it may not say what dose is safe. For anyone reasoning about how much of the regulatory stack a model layer can occupy, that line is drawn by a regulator, in 2026, well short of the answer — and it is a sharper statement of the same proportionality logic regulatory-acceptance-ai-model-credibility carries from the US and EU medicines regulators. Second, smaller ripple, same night: transcriptomic-point-of-departure converts a dish concentration to a human dose through in-vitro-to-in-vivo extrapolation, so its headline concordance with chronic animal studies (~50%/~70% within one order of magnitude) is a joint result of the measurement and this map's model layer, undecomposed in the published abstract. A loop consuming that number inherits both uncertainties and can see neither. → a-regulator-published-the-procedure-for-deleting-an-animal-study

  • 2026-08-03 — The bridge was run end to end on 41 chemicals and reached the far side for 9 of them. Status HELD at consolidating, tier HELD at computational-connective. Ingested chiu-2026-human-cells-machine-pod-comparison (Chiu, Roe & Rusyn, Regul. Toxicol. Pharmacol. 169:106104, PMID 41997412; NIEHS + California EPA funding, funding_flag: none). In-vitro-to-in-vivo extrapolation from ToxCast bioactivity — this map's machinery, applied to the largest robotic screening dataset in existence — produced a chronic oral point of departure for 9 of 41 US EPA-assessed substances (22%), the lowest coverage of the four routes compared. This is the first coverage figure the vault holds for the dose bridge itself, as distinct from its accuracy, which the vault already tracks (~50%/~70% within an order of magnitude on the transcriptomic route; the UK strategy's stated ceiling). Accuracy answers "when it runs, is it right"; this answers "how often does it run at all". The abstract does not decompose the 32 failures — a chemical inactive across the panel, a chemical outside the platform's solubility domain, and a chemical with no measured clearance or protein-binding parameter to feed the model are all consistent with the number, and only the last is a defect of this map's layer. Recorded without attribution; the full text is GATED (Elsevier, HTTP 403 at the browser rung) and logged. Nothing demotes — a coverage figure on one application is not a statement about the models' validity, and the same paper's finding that stacked computational routes exceed 90% coverage cuts the other way (the-cheapest-routes-cover-the-most-chemicals). → the-robot-battery-produced-a-dose-number-for-one-chemical-in-five

  • 2026-08-06 — The largest single-campaign clearance dataset the vault holds arrives, and it is an input to this hub rather than an application of it. Status HELD at consolidating, tier HELD at computational-connective. Ingested ellison-2026-ittc-hepatocyte-clearance-207-chemicals (Toxicological Sciences advance article kfag086, PMID 42544817; funding_flag: [industry] — Procter & Gamble, the Research Institute for Fragrance Materials and the American Chemistry Council). (1) 207 chemicals, in-vitro hepatic intrinsic clearance at two concentrations, generated explicitly as PBK model input. That is the same inbound relationship metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg has to this hub — a supplier of girders, not a consumer of the bridge — and it is the second such source here. (2) The distribution is the finding: 68% of chemicals at 0.1 µM and 62% at 1 µM cleared below 30 µL/min/10⁶ cells, against a full range of 0–4,294. For roughly two-thirds of this set the liver is not what keeps internal concentration down, so a model defaulting to brisk hepatic clearance under-predicts internal exposure for the majority of it. (3) Concentration dependence is real and bounded: R = 0.8 between the two test concentrations (~64% shared variance) — good agreement, not interchangeability, and a clearance value quoted without its test concentration is missing information the model needs. (4) The purpose is a new consumer of this hub: an industry-convened effort to build an internal threshold of toxicological concern, which needs this bridge to convert historical oral no-effect doses into internal concentrations. Coins risk-assessment-ttc tonight. (5) Full text GATED (OUP HTTP 403 at the browser rung), so the chemical-selection criteria and species split are unread — if the 207 came from fragrance and consumer-product inventories, "two-thirds clear slowly" is a fact about those inventories. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method, strength: single-study. → two-thirds-of-a-consumer-chemical-set-is-slowly-cleared

  • 2026-08-08 — The price of going fully in-silico on the kinetics finally has a number, on a clinical endpoint. Status HELD at consolidating, tier HELD at computational-connective. geci-2026-dili-in-vitro-pbk-integration (Archives of Toxicology 100(5):2029-2046, open access) predicts drug-induced liver injury from three in-vitro potencies divided by peak plasma concentration, and runs the comparison twice — once with the concentration measured in humans, once with it simulated end-to-end by this hub's machinery (PK-Sim, parameterised entirely from in-silico property predictions: ADMETLab for lipophilicity, solubility, unbound fraction and permeability; ADMET Predictor for intestinal-fluid solubility; PKsmart for clearance). Substituting the simulation for the human measurement costs about five points of discrimination — ROC AUC 96% → 91% — and changes 13.94% of individual classifications. That is the cleanest published price this vault holds for removing the last organism-derived input from a hazard pipeline, and it is a small price. What it is not: validation of this hub. The comparison is against a clinical outcome on 241 drugs, a chemical space (marketed pharmaceuticals, well-characterised ADME) about as favourable to a PBK model as any that exists, and the authors say the potencies cluster in narrow ranges that limit transfer to novel candidates. Read beside the 2026-08-06 entry above, the pattern holds: this bridge performs where the compounds are well behaved. Coins hepatotoxicity-dili-in-vitro-pbk-integration tonight as a downstream consumer. → a-fully-in-silico-dili-rule-that-never-false-alarms-and-misses-half

Cross-intervention / connect (§6.5)

  • Enabling/connective (composes-with — the hub edge): PBK/QIVIVE is the dose-anchoring layer for every in-vitro potency method. Explicit composes-with edges this run: developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb (QIVIVE principles use it as the worked example) and repeat-dose-systemic-toxicity-nam (its tiered framework uses PBK for plasma-Cmax bioavailability tiers).
  • Upstream parameter supply (composes-with, and it points the other way — added 2026-07-10): metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg (TG 445A) measures induction of CYP1A2/2B6/3A4 activity in human hepatic cells. That is an input to a PBK model, not an output of one. This is the hub's first inbound edge: an adopted OECD method generating a parameter the hub needs. Worth noting what is still missing — induction is only one metabolic parameter; intrinsic clearance, protein binding and transporter kinetics have no adopted in-vitro TG (logged as an open gap, deliberately as plain text, not a wikilink).
  • The local-endpoint kinship, now resolved (2026-07-10). This clause previously flagged the GL 497 potency ITS DA as a future edge that might need internal dose. Both it and Part III are now ingested — and the answer is no. skin-sensitization-potency-its-da returns a GHS potency class, needing no internal dose; skin-sensitization-quantitative-pod-da returns a dermal dose directly from key-event data, with no PBK step at all. So the mature local endpoint reached a quantitative PoD by bypassing this hub, not by using it. That is a genuinely interesting negative result for a hub whose premise is that dose-anchoring runs through PBK.
  • Global-ripple: not a hazard method, so no confirm/refute edge with any existing map — a distinct, cross-cutting endpoint whose value is compositional.
  • Composes-with automation-self-driving-labs — this hub is now something a proposed orchestrator drives (added 2026-07-21). ToxMCP (Djidrovski 2026) wraps the Open Systems Pharmacology PBPK suite as a typed tool an LLM agent calls under provenance and critical-action guardrails; its one worked demonstration is a PBPK simulation (an acetaminophen brain-to-plasma AUC ratio, which the paper itself labels not validated). The PBK/QIVIVE layer this map grades is one of the five kernels that orchestration layer orchestrates. A one-author preprint with no executed evaluation — nothing about this hub's science or standing moves.
