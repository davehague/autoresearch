A test that answers before you ask the question

What it is

Every other method in this vault starts with an endpoint. Is this a skin sensitiser? An eye irritant? A mutagen? The assay is built to answer that question and is meaningless against any other.

Cell Painting inverts that. Cells are stained with a fixed panel of fluorescent dyes that light up different cell compartments — nucleus, mitochondria, cytoskeleton, membrane, endoplasmic reticulum — imaged by high-content microscopy, and reduced by machine learning to a morphological fingerprint: hundreds to thousands of numbers describing how the cell's shape and internal organisation changed. Run a concentration series and you get a point of departure — the concentration at which the cell's overall appearance starts to depart from control — without ever having named a mechanism.

"Phenotypic profiling is an emerging in vitro method in which cells are fluorescently labelled to visualise the morphological changes that a perturbagen (such as a chemical compound) induces … the generated microscopy images are used to extract many different image features using Machine Learning (ML) approaches … and more recently using DL."

The numbers, as reported

Multi-site reproducibility"a robust and reproducible method with high and cross-platform interoperability, even if each laboratory needed to adjust some assay parameters"
Scale of the consortiaJUMP, OASIS — "hundreds of thousands of small molecules, industrial/agricultural chemicals or genetic perturbations"
Regulatory status"Standard CP would need to formally demonstrate their applicability for regulatory purposes in comprehensive validation studies"

Read the 68% honestly. "Similar or more conservative" for two-thirds of chemicals means the cell-based number was in the right place or erred on the side of caution. It also means that for roughly a third, it did not — and the paper does not say which way those went, on which chemicals, against which animal studies. A one-third rate of less conservative would be a serious problem; a one-third rate of much more conservative would be a different, milder problem. The source does not distinguish them, and the vault will not guess.

And read the reproducibility claim honestly too. "Reproducible … even if each laboratory needed to adjust some assay parameters" describes a method that works everywhere after local tuning. That is not the property that separates formally-validated from peer-reviewed-method on this vault's ladder — that rung requires the method to work on unfamiliar equipment in unfamiliar hands without adjustment, which is the whole point of eight-methods-that-broke-when-a-second-lab-ran-them. evidence_tier: peer-reviewed-method.

Named sources of variability

The paper lists them, and every one is a level-zero or level-II problem in the sense of six-data-levels-sit-between-the-plate-and-the-call: "cell culture conditions (e.g., cell density at the imaging stage), solvent percentage (e.g., different amount of DMSO), staining procedure (e.g., dye signal stability over time), and finally from the use of different microscopy systems and data analysis software and approaches."

Why it matters to the loop, and where it stops

Everything about this method is machine-shaped. Fixed dye panel, automated microscope, feature extraction by trained model, no human scoring anywhere. It runs at consortium scale already — hundreds of thousands of perturbations. As a generator it is arguably the most loop-native method in the vault.

And it has no decision rule. There is no published procedure that converts a morphological fingerprint into a regulatory call, because there is no regulatory call it is designed to make. It produces a point of departure and a similarity structure; what to do with those is a weight-of-evidence judgement. That places it squarely in the vault's largest and most stubborn category: automatable-open-loop — the fifteenth map with the field's dominant blocker, and the same state Tox21 has been in since 2013.

There is a real prize here and it is worth naming plainly. A profiling method that produces a point of departure without needing an endpoint is a natural objective function for an autonomous system — the scalar that the-loop-closes-where-the-objective-is-a-number says closed loops require and hazard classification does not supply. What it cannot do is tell you whether the number means the chemical is safe.
