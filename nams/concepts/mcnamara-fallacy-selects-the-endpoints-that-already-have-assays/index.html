The loop will work where the data already is, and this vault knows exactly where that isn't

The fallacy. Bisht et al.'s framing: institutions facing complex problems "measure what is easy to measure, disregard what cannot be quantified, presume the unmeasured is unimportant, and finally declare that the unmeasured does not even exist." Their argument for why AI amplifies it: "AI techniques rely on numerical signals such as losses, rewards, or fitness values for optimization and evaluation of the final system, which naturally pushes research projects involving AI toward problems that can be quantified." Plus a data constraint — ML needs a lot of examples in a machine-manipulable format, "further reducing the span of problems amenable to AI solutions."

The cited measurement (Hao et al. 2026, across 41.3 million research papers, recorded as this paper's citation rather than as the vault's own source): AI-augmented scientists publish 3.02× more papers and receive 4.84× more citations, while AI adoption shrinks the collective volume of scientific topics studied by 4.63% and reduces scientist-to-scientist engagement by 22%. More output, faster, over a measurably narrower field. Their reading: "The tools accelerate established fields leading to a narrower set of questions being asked, while genuinely hard, data-sparse problems go untouched." And the diagnosis: "The current AI deployment in science seems almost entirely technology-push" — the tool sets the agenda, rather than the need.

Why this lands hard in this vault specifically. This vault is a map of exactly which parts of toxicology are data-rich and which are blank, and the blanks are catalogued:

EndpointState
Reproductive / fertilityNo non-animal method exists at all — bench-bound: no-nam-exists
Photoallergy, photogenotoxicity"no validated test methods … to this date", per OECD guidance
Thyroid disruption67 in-vitro methods, zero adopted Test Guidelines; the animal test is still the answer
Repeat-dose systemic toxicity12-chemical proof of concept, no concordance figure
CarcinogenicityGuidance documents only, no TG, scored by eye
Skin sensitisationThree adopted assays, three adopted decision rules, a Bayesian dose model
Skin / eye / phototoxicityTen-plus adopted plate-reader methods with published cut-offs

The bottom two rows are where a loop can act. The top five are where the answers are missing. An acquisition function scores candidate experiments by expected information gain, uncertainty, or improvement — all of which require a model, which requires data, which exists for the bottom rows and does not exist for the top ones. A loop cannot compute the expected value of an experiment in a domain where no assay produces a number. It will therefore not propose building one; it will propose the 400th chemical through the skin-sensitisation battery.

This is not hypothetical drift — the vault has already measured a version of it. On 2026-07-24 the vault ingested FDA CDER's fifteen-year submission archive: 93% of all non-animal-method submissions are in silico (49%) and in vitro (44%), with the microphysiological systems that carry most of the field's attention among the rarest things actually filed (regulatory-acceptance-fda-nam-framework). The vault flagged that as "consistent with Hartung's agenda drift having already happened," with cheapness and regulatory familiarity as uncontrolled confounds. Bisht et al. supply the mechanism that would produce the same pattern, from an entirely different literature, with no knowledge of toxicology. Two independent derivations of one structural claim — the same pattern the vault noted on 2026-07-13 when Hartung and the Tox21 paper converged on the blind-spot remedy.

What a loop-builder should take from it. The endpoints with no NAM are precisely the endpoints where a closed loop would be most valuable and where it is structurally least likely to go. Prioritisation of loop infrastructure by "where can we automate today" — which is what this vault's loop-readiness table invites — reproduces the fallacy. The complementary question, which the table does not ask, is "where is the absence of a method costing the most animals," and its answers are reproductive toxicity and repeat-dose systemic toxicity, both bench-bound.
