The skin-allergy assay that arrived with its decision rule already written

The single most decisive question this vault asks of any method is whether the guideline publishes a fixed, deterministic procedure for converting the measurement into the call. Not whether the readout is a number — most are. Whether anyone has agreed, in writing, what the number means.

Most of the vault's assays fail on exactly that. The estrogen-receptor binding assay (OECD Test Guideline 493) is fully quantitative and sits one rung down the loop-readiness ladder because the guideline itself states that no agreed interpretation method exists. That is the vault's dominant blocker, and it is administrative, not technical.

The SENS-IS draft does not have that problem. Every step from raw instrument output to a GHS hazard category and a potency sub-category is arithmetic, and all of it is in the public consultation text:

StepRuleJudgement required
Is the tissue alive enough to read?cycle count for HSP90AA1 ≥ 0.9× vehicle controlsnone
Is this concentration merely irritating?≤ 20 of 23 IRRITATION genes over-expressednone
Is a gene "over-expressed"?fold-increase over calibrator > 1.25none
Is the chemical an allergen?≥ 7 of 21 SENS-IS genes and/or ≥ 7 of 17 REDOX genes, in two replicates on two tissue batchesnone
How strong an allergen?lowest concentration still positive, banded 100/50 → weak, 10 → moderate, 1 → strong, 0.1 → extremenone
GHS sub-category?strong+extreme → 1A; positive at 10% and above → 1Bnone

Two properties compound. The readout is RT-PCR cycle counts — an instrument number, not a picture somebody scores. And the arithmetic is not merely specified in prose: para. 35 says the values "are pasted in a dedicated spreadsheet program" that computes the verdict, and that "the spreadsheet document is an integral part of the SOP." The guideline ships an implementation.

Why this is unusual in the endocrine, genotoxicity and imaging columns of this vault, and not in this one. The skin-sensitisation endpoint is already where the vault's clearest loop-ready cases live: OECD Guideline 497's 2-out-of-3 defined approach carries the guideline's own description of itself as "a transparent, rule-based approach requiring no expert judgment." SENS-IS is the fourth assay in this column to arrive with a computable rule, and the first single assay that produces hazard and potency together rather than needing a second defined approach for the dose question.

The reproducibility numbers deserve reading at two resolutions, because they say different things. Five laboratories, 26 chemicals: 96% between-lab agreement on the yes/no allergen call, and 80.8% on the three-way strength call across all 26 chemicals (the draft's headline 84% figure covers 21 of them). Translated: run a chemical past five different labs and they will nearly always agree whether it is an allergen, and about one chemical in five will come back with a different strength label depending on who ran it. The hazard call is reproducible enough to automate. The potency call — the one that decides what concentration is safe in a product — is where the labs still disagree, and no amount of automation fixes disagreement about the answer.

This is not an endorsement, and three qualifications travel with it permanently. The validation study was "coordinated by ImmunoSearch (test developer)" — the company that sells the assay and the tissue — and only then peer-reviewed by an independent panel. The draft's own para. 7 declines to present it as a stand-alone test: "it is recommended that the SENS-IS assay be used in conjunction with other sources of information." And it is a draft out for consultation, not an adopted Test Guideline: regulatory_status: under-validation, and the vault will not read it as adopted until an adopted text exists.

Rebutted by: sens-is-prediction-model-parameters-are-user-retunable — the guideline permits the user to change the 1.25-fold threshold, the gene identities and the required gene count with justification, and calibrates the threshold to one commercial tissue brand, so the rule above is fixed for a configuration rather than a constant of the method. Also full-thickness-escalation-is-an-unruled-pre-analytical-choice — the arithmetic sits downstream of an unruled expert choice of tissue depth whose default under-calls potency for metabolically demanding aromatics, so a complete decision rule is not the same as a complete procedure.
