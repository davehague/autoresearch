A receptor-proximal signal that doesn't need the receptor to bind — a blind spot named for the adopted assay, not yet acted on

endocrine-er-transactivation is this vault's consensus, closed-loop-ready OECD Test Guideline 455 map — a reporter-gene transactivation assay whose entire mechanism depends on a chemical binding the estrogen receptor and driving it to activate transcription of a luciferase reporter. cipolletti-2026-eralpha-phosphorylation-biomarker-ed (Cipolletti, Campesi, Pellegrini, Fiocchetti, Acconcia & Marino, 2026, Environmental Toxicology and Pharmacology 122:104949, University of Roma Tre, open access CC-BY, funding_flag: none) reports that bisphenol A and ten further chemicals increase ERα phosphorylation — a non-genomic, extra-nuclear signaling step upstream of and separable from transcriptional activation — in human monocytes and breast-cancer cell lines, and that for several of the ten chemicals this happens "often independently of direct receptor binding."

Why this is a limit, not a new validated method

If a chemical can trigger ERα-pathway signaling without binding the receptor the way the adopted assay's mechanism requires, the adopted transactivation assay has a structural blind spot for exactly that mechanism — not because the assay is run wrong, but because its decision mechanism (ligand binding → transcriptional reporter) is not the only route into the pathway. This is a different kind of limitation from the correlated_blind_spots this map already carries (the shared MMTV promoter the ER/AR/GR transactivation family must each engineer away) — this is a mechanism gap, not a cross-reactivity gap, and the schema has no field for it; recorded in prose.

The honest ceiling

This is one academic group, primary monocytes from an unspecified small donor pool plus two breast-cancer cell lines, eleven chemicals total, no inter-laboratory reproduction, and no comparison against the adopted assay's own call on the same compounds — so whether any of the eleven chemicals that phosphorylate ERα without binding it would also test negative on OECD TG 455 is not established, only implied by the mechanism. evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory; regulatory_status: none. Nothing on endocrine-er-transactivation moves — status, evidence_tier, regulatory_status and loop_readiness all HOLD. The finding is logged as an open question about the adopted assay's coverage, not a result that changes its grading.
