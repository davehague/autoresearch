A model that publishes the arithmetic for saying "I don't know"

This vault's third axis turns on one question more than any other: does the method publish a fixed, deterministic procedure for turning its numbers into a call? Roughly two dozen maps here are stuck at automatable-open-loop because the answer is no — the instrument produces a clean number and nothing converts it into a decision without a person. The adopted estrogen-receptor binding test is the vault's sharpest case: fully quantitative, regulatorily adopted, and the guideline itself says no agreed interpretation method exists.

So a method that publishes one is worth reading closely, and this is a slightly unusual one, because what it publishes is a rule for not answering.

The rule

Seven models — three descriptor families crossed with two classical algorithms, plus a graph neural network — each vote permeable or not. The spread of the votes is the confidence measure:

Vote splitSpread
7–0 (unanimous)0.00
6–10.35
5–20.45
4–30.49

Threshold: below 0.46, report the prediction. At or above it, return "Inconclusive." In practice that means up to two dissenting models is tolerated and a near-tie is refused.

It is arithmetic, it is published, it is reproducible from the paper, and — the part that matters for a closed loop — it has a third output. Not permeable, not impermeable, but outside the domain where this model has earned the right to speak. An autonomous system consuming this model does not have to invent a confidence policy; the method ships one.

What refusing costs and buys

Buys: sensitivity 86% → 89%, specificity 76% → 79% on the honest split (scaffold splitting, where molecules sharing a core skeleton are kept out of the training set so the test chemistry is genuinely unfamiliar).

Costs: 12% of the test set gets no answer. In the application case study, 1,080 of 6,031 chemicals — 18% — came back Inconclusive.

That is the whole trade, stated in numbers a person can hold: for every hundred chemicals, hand back twelve to eighteen unanswered, and be right about three more of the rest. The vault has an article on a method that makes the same bargain in the wet lab (eye-irritation-rhce-no-category and its one-directional rule-out); this is the computational version, and its threshold is public where most confidence scores are not.

The weakness the paper does not raise

The seven models are not seven opinions. They are trained on one curated dataset of 3,968 compounds, and three of them share the same physicochemical descriptor family while two more share fingerprint representations. Their agreement is correlated by construction, and how correlated is never measured. When they all vote the same way, some unknown fraction of that unanimity is "the training data says so" rather than "the chemistry says so."

This is the vault's correlated-blind-spot rail, one layer up from where it usually fires. The rail's canonical form is two wet assays sharing a metabolic gap and therefore casting one vote rather than two. Here it is seven models sharing a dataset. The consequence is the same and slightly worse: the ensemble's agreement is being used simultaneously as the prediction and as the confidence in the prediction, so a systematic gap in the training set silently produces a confident answer rather than an Inconclusive one — the refusal branch fires on disagreement, and shared blindness does not disagree.

Tonight's companion finding from the screening layer, two-metrics-related-by-a-transform-cast-one-vote, is the extreme case of the same principle: agreement is evidence only in proportion to the independence of what is agreeing.

The brakes

  • Computational only, single study, no regulatory standing (regulatory_status: none).
  • The labels are not a gold standard. The paper itself notes the standard high-throughput permeability assay is variable and lacks the active transport mechanisms that are the barrier's biological point.
  • The threshold 0.46 is a choice, not a derived quantity — it sits between the 5–2 spread (0.45) and the 4–3 spread (0.49) and is not justified against any external criterion.
