The same company that serves a proprietary cardiac model also publishes a paper built entirely on someone else's open one

The finding, plainly

Unlike this map's other two computational cardiotoxicity sources — the map's own headline unpublished classifier, and nursyafi-2026-cardiosim-tox-multitask-qsar's structure-only QSAR served from a login-gated commercial platform — this source is not a proprietary black box. It benchmarks Paci2020, a human iPSC-cardiomyocyte electrophysiological simulation model published independently of any company named in this paper, and its contribution is a biomarker-selection methodology: systematic combinatorial testing of ten candidate electrophysiological biomarkers via an interpretable ordinal logistic regression, converging on a four-biomarker signature (qNet, Ca_resting, dVm/dtmax, Cycle_length) that meets the CiPA initiative's own published acceptance criteria on held-out compounds.

Why this is the same vendor's second paper on this map, and why that matters differently here

nursyafi-2026-cardiosim-tox-multitask-qsar — already on this map, ingested 2026-08-14 — carries a corresponding author employed by Meta Heart Co., Ltd, serves its model from metaheart.kr/login, and carries a competing-interest statement the vault already flagged as internally contradictory (declaring the employment and denying any commercial conflict in the same paragraph). This source's senior/corresponding author (K.M.L.) is employed by Meta Heart Inc — the same company — and its competing-interest statement follows the identical pattern. This is the map's first instance of one company appearing on two separate papers, which sharpens rather than repeats the 2026-08-14 finding: the company's output on this endpoint is not one product but a small research program — one paper predicting ion-channel blockade from structure alone (a proprietary model), and this one refining biomarker selection on a public, third-party simulation model (an open methodology). Whether Meta Heart's proprietary classifier (Cardiosim-Tox) internally uses or benchmarks against findings like this paper's four-biomarker signature is not stated in either source, and the vault does not assume a connection it cannot verify.

Honest ceiling

Twenty-eight compounds — the field's standard fixed CiPA reference panel — means this result, like the map's other CiPA-panel sources, says nothing about generalization outside that chemical space. One laboratory, no interlaboratory replication of this specific four-biomarker combination. evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, strength: single-study. regulatory_status: none — CiPA-criteria satisfaction is a benchmarking claim against the initiative's own published bar, not an independent regulatory review.
