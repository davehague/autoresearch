A domain_gate this map didn't have: the chorion decides who never reaches the test compound

fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet is graded consensus / automatable-open-loop on the strength of OECD TG 236's adopted status and near-binary apical endpoints — but its frontmatter carries no domain_gate. Pamanji (2026) names one directly: TG 236 must begin exposure by the 16-cell stage, while the egg is still enclosed in its chorion, and the chorion "may function as a selective permeability barrier" — the review's own priority table ranks this among the two most consequential, near-term-actionable gaps across the entire zebrafish OECD Test Guideline portfolio, specifically for high-molecular-weight compounds. A compound that cannot cross the chorion in appreciable quantity within the 96-hour exposure window will read as non-toxic in TG 236 regardless of its true hazard — a false-negative mechanism the assay's own adopted status says nothing about, because regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted measures whether a Test Guideline exists, not what its applicability domain excludes.

Why this is a domain_gate, not a correlated_blind_spot. The vault's existing correlated_blind_spot between fish-embryo-acute-toxicity-fet and ecotox-rtgill-w1-fish-cell-line is a mechanistic gap shared by both assays (neither has a nervous system or full xenobiotic metabolism, so both miss neurotoxicants and bioactivation-dependent compounds). The chorion barrier is a physical gap unique to the whole-embryo, chorion-intact format — RTgill-W1 is a dissociated cell line with no chorion at all, so this does not extend the existing correlated pair. It is graded here as a domain_gate: a carve-out the loop must check before trusting TG 236's decision rule, exactly the shape the vault's domain_gate modifier defines for TG 435's pH bound and TG 249's log KOW bound (shared/evidence-model.md).
