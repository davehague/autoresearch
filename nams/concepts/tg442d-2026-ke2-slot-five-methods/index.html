TG 442D's KE2 slot grows to five methods — a regulatory-move, watch item resolved

What moved. On 2026-07-05 this map logged a watch item: OECD had run Nov–Dec 2025 public consultations to extend TG 442D with a serum-free α-Sens ARE-Nrf2 method and an EpiSensA/Epi²SensA update, and the KE2 slot was "accreting new validated methods — watch for adoption next cycle." That cycle has arrived. The 2 July 2026 edition of TG 442D adopts both: α-Sens as a new Appendix ID, and Epi²SensA as an update to the epidermal-sensitisation appendix. Both went through a formal review path before landing in guideline text — Epi²SensA through a Performance-Standard-based validation study plus independent peer review, α-Sens through OECD public consultation plus independent peer review — the same evidentiary bar the original KeratinoSens method cleared via EURL ECVAM/ESAC. This is why evidence_tier holds at regulatory-adopted and regulatory_status holds at OECD-TG-adopted: the bar for entry into this guideline did not lower, the roster of methods clearing it grew.

Two axes hold; only acceptance moves. direction: regulatory-move is deliberate, not loop-move or a validity claim. Neither α-Sens nor Epi²SensA changes what a KE2 test measures or how automatable it is — they change how many OECD-sanctioned ways exist to measure it. loop_readiness on skin-sensitization-keratinosens holds at closed-loop-ready: all five methods in the slot (two RT-qPCR, three luciferase) are instrument-read against a fixed, guideline-specified decision procedure — a human is not load-bearing in any of the five calls. Status holds at consensus — this is an already-adopted slot gaining adopted alternatives, not a slot newly earning adoption.

The mechanism split, read from the guideline's own taxonomy. Three of the five methods — KeratinoSens, LuSens, and α-Sens — are ARE-Nrf2 luciferase reporter assays: they all read the same light- output signal from the same stress pathway, in monolayer cell lines. The other two — EpiSensA and Epi²SensA — read gene expression by RT-qPCR (counting transcribed copies of stress-response genes) in reconstructed human epidermis, a multi-layer 3D tissue model, not a flat cell line. This is a vault inference drawn from how the guideline itself groups the five methods, not a claim the guideline states outright: a lab choosing among KeratinoSens, LuSens, and α-Sens is choosing among protocol variants of the same measurement principle, not gaining mechanistic independence. Real independence inside this KE2 slot only shows up when choosing EpiSensA/Epi²SensA over the luciferase trio — a different biological signal, a different assay format, a different failure mode profile. Five interchangeable methods in one key-event slot is designed redundancy (substitutable information sources by construction), which this vault treats as categorically different from the correlated_blind_spot rail (two assays that appear independent and are not) — but the sub-grouping above shows the redundancy is not evenly distributed across the five.

The FBS-free detail is worth keeping visible. Fetal bovine serum (FBS) — a growth supplement harvested from bovine fetal blood — is the default nutrient additive for most cultured animal cell lines, including the cell lines that run luciferase-reporter KE2 assays. A "non-animal method" that depends on an animal-derived culture reagent is a real and rarely-discussed inconsistency in the NAM space. α-Sens's FBS-free protocol closes that gap for one ARE-Nrf2 method — it does not establish that KeratinoSens or LuSens use FBS (this vault has not verified their culture protocols), only that an FBS-free version of this assay type now exists as adopted guideline text.

What is honestly unknown. No sensitivity/specificity/accuracy numbers for α-Sens or Epi²SensA are recorded here — the source consultation PDFs did not text-extract this run, and this note does not estimate or infer them. Anyone reading this concept note should not treat the KeratinoSens 77/78/76 figures as applying to the two new entrants; they are cited in the source digest strictly as the slot's incumbent baseline, carried forward from oecd-tg442d-keratinosens-skin-sensitisation.
