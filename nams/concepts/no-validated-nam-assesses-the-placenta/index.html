An endpoint where the animal test does not work either

This vault records coverage gaps constantly, and they almost all have the same shape: the animal test is the standard, a non-animal replacement does not exist yet, and the work is to build one. That is what bench_bound_reason: no-nam-exists means, and it is what reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams has said about fertility since 2026-07-09.

The placenta is a different and worse shape, and the vault did not have it.

The consortium's own words: "Currently, neither reproductive/developmental in vivo test guidelines nor validated NAMs can adequately assess the impact of EDs on the placenta." Both halves. The regulatory animal tests for developmental and reproductive toxicity do not assess placental effects adequately, and no validated non-animal method does either — and "attempts to validate in vitro placental models have failed."

The stated reason the animal side fails is worth keeping, because it is the reason a replacement is hard rather than merely absent: "consistent interspecies differences in placental transfer present major challenges for extrapolation." Placentas differ between species more than most organs do. A rat's placenta is not a small human one.

Why this is structurally harder than a normal gap

Validating a new method means demonstrating it agrees with something. The vault's whole evidence_tier ladder assumes there is a reference answer somewhere — a ring trial reproduces it, an OECD guideline adopts it, predictivity is measured against known outcomes.

For the placenta there is no reference answer to validate against. The animal study is not a usable standard because of the species difference; human data is observational and confounded. So the ordinary route — build the assay, run the reference chemicals, compare against the in-vivo result — is unavailable at the first step.

This is the same structure the vault recorded on 2026-07-28 from a very different direction: a kidney method whose authors declined to publish a decision rule on principle, because three compounds are not enough evidence to write one against, and writing it is what would make the method usable enough to generate the evidence. Here the circularity is one level up — a validation with nothing valid to validate against.

For a loop

bench-bound with bench_bound_reason: no-nam-exists is the parent map's grade, and it holds. But the two sub-cases inside it are not the same investment, and the vault should keep them apart the way it already keeps animal-input apart from no-nam-exists:

  • Fertility — an assay does not exist yet; there is a reference standard (the multigenerational animal study) to validate one against. Fixable by assay development.
  • Placenta — an assay does not exist, a previous attempt to validate one failed, and the standard it would be validated against does not answer the question either. Fixable by assay development plus establishing what "right" means.

An autonomous laboratory can be pointed at the first. It cannot be pointed at the second, because there is nothing for it to optimise toward.

Limits

A project description, not a study. Fifty-plus authors describing what they intend to build; no data, no chemicals, no performance number anywhere in the paper. The gap statement carries weight because it argues against the authors' interest — a consortium seeking to demonstrate progress is stating that a previous validation failed and that the regulatory standard is inadequate. The capability claims in the same paper carry no such weight and are not recorded as findings. Two authors hold equity in a company selling developmental-neurotoxicity battery services; that conflict is declared in the paper and recorded in the source digest.
