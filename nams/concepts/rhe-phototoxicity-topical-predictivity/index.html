RhE phototoxicity test (OECD TG 498) → topical phototoxicity

A chemical is applied topically to reconstructed human epidermis — a differentiated, multilayered keratinocyte tissue with a functional stratum corneum. Half the tissues get 6 J/cm² of simulated sunlight; half stay dark. Viability is read by MTT. A ≥ 30% viability drop in the irradiated arm at any concentration means phototoxic; nothing reaching 30% at any concentration means not phototoxic. Borderline results (within 5% of the cutoff, or discordant replicates) go to a second and possibly third run.

Both axes adopted: evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 498, first adopted 2021; the archived bytes are the current 2 July 2026 edition, declassified by the Chemicals and Biotechnology Committee on 15 June 2026 — one of the seven TGs in that batch). It is the topical confirmation tier of the phototoxicity IATA (phototoxicity-iata-composition), sitting above the barrier-free 3T3 screen (3t3-nru-phototoxicity-screen).

The method is two of this vault's existing maps welded together. The guideline says so explicitly: it "combines the basic principles" of TG 439 — the RhE tissue and the MTT viability readout (skin-irritation-rhe) — with the standardised irradiation procedures of TG 432 (phototoxicity-3t3-nru). The barrier is what it buys: unlike a bare fibroblast monolayer, the chemical must cross a stratum corneum before it can be photo-activated in a living cell, which is what makes the result relevant to a topical exposure and what makes TG 498 the tier that adjudicates the screen's positives.

The validation base is thin, and the guideline is the one that says so. The Validated Reference Method, EpiDerm™ PT, carries sensitivity 86.7% / specificity 93.3% — from a 1999 pre-validation study, Phase III, on ten chemicals tested twice. A later literature-derived database of 60+ chemicals in the EpiDerm™ model performed better than that, but the TG states those studies, "whilst using the same SOP, were not conducted as formal validation studies." So the formal evidence under the adopted reference method is a ten-chemical set from 1999. The KeraSkin™ PA figures added in the 2026 edition (100% / 98.6% / 99.3%) are excellent but are "me-too" figures: a similar method validated against an already-fixed prediction model and Performance Standards, independently peer-reviewed as meeting them. That is a real, independent check on equivalence to EpiDerm™; it is not a fresh, first-in-class validation of the underlying prediction model, and the two numbers must not be read as if they were the same kind of thing.

This is the first adopted method in the vault whose formal validation base is visibly thin — and the reason matters more than the fact. For this endpoint there is no validated in-vivo reference method at all (photoallergy-photogenotoxicity-no-validated-method), so no large animal-derived reference set can be assembled to validate against; the benchmark is scarce human clinical data. The thinness is a property of the endpoint, not evidence of a lax guideline. It still means "adopted on both axes" and "extensively validated" are, here, different claims — which is exactly the distinction this vault exists to keep visible.

Scope, stated by the TG against itself: it "is not designed to predict other adverse effects that may arise from the combined action of a chemical and light (e.g., it does not address photo-genotoxicity, photoallergy, or photocarcinogenicity)." Topical exposure only. No metabolic activation. MTT-reducing or coloured chemicals interfere and need adapted controls or an HPLC/UPLC readout.

funding_flag: method-developer — a test-system dependency, not sponsored science: both validated tissues are proprietary products (EpiDerm™, MatTek; KeraSkin™, validated via KoCVAM, the Korean validation centre — an independent body, which is what carries the weight on the me-too). There is no generic RhE model in this TG. Same family as HepaRG™ under TG 445A (cyp-induction-heparg-biotransformation) and Corrositex® under TG 435 (membrane-barrier-corrosion-subcategorisation) — the practical-access axis the vault named on 2026-07-10 and still does not model.
