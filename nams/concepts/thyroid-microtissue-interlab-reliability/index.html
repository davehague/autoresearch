Interlaboratory reliability of the human thyroid microtissue assay → thyroid hormone synthesis disruption

endocrine-thyroid-disruption-nams has rested, since 2026-07-13, on one source: Vergauwen 2024's finding that "validated Level 2 assays for THSD are still completely lacking today" — 67 in-vitro thyroid methods, zero adopted Test Guidelines, all routed through EU-NETVAL. Deisenroth et al. 2026 is a second, independent validation track on the same endpoint: US EPA/NICEATM, not EU-NETVAL, and a structurally different assay — a 3D human thyroid microtissue built from primary donor cells, which measures thyroid hormone synthesis directly, rather than the mechanism-fragment readouts (thyroperoxidase inhibition, transthyretin displacement) the eight EU-NETVAL methods use.

What was shown. Across 3 laboratories and 3 independent human thyroid donors, the assay transferred reliably — new labs, following the study's SOPs, reproduced its effects on thyroid hormone synthesis. On 4 blinded reference chemicals (3 true positive, 1 true negative), bioactivity was consistent across labs and donors, and dynamic range, precision, and screening-quality metrics met the study's pre-set acceptance criteria. An independent international validation management team monitored the whole study, alongside the EPA/NICEATM coordination.

Why evidence_tier: formally-validated, not higher. This is an ECVAM/ICCVAM-style interlaboratory reproducibility study — exactly what the formally-validated tier is defined for in shared/evidence-model.md. It is not regulatory-adopted: no Test Guideline exists, so regulatory_status stays under-validation, one rung below OECD-TG-adopted. The two axes move independently here, as the rail requires — the science just cleared a real reproducibility bar; the regulatory apparatus hasn't moved at all.

What this is NOT — the honesty constraint that matters most. This is a reliability validation, not a predictivity one. No sensitivity, no specificity, no apical concordance figure is reported. Four reference chemicals is a very small set for any predictivity claim even if the study had made one. strength: suggestive, not strong — this establishes that labs agree with each other, not that the assay's calls agree with reality.

Why loop_readiness on the map does not move. No data-interpretation procedure — no fixed positive/negative threshold or decision algorithm — is described. Per the deterministic ladder in shared/evidence-model.md, a machine-readable readout without a fixed decision rule stays at automatable-open-loop; this result adds reliability evidence to one candidate assay, it does not supply the missing decision rule the whole thyroid column is blocked on. The map's loop_blocker — "nothing to interpret WITH" — is unchanged.

funding_flag: [industry], recorded plainly, not laundered. Two of the three named collaborators, Bayer Crop Science and Corteva Agrisciences, are agrochemical manufacturers whose own compounds get screened for thyroid disruption by methods like this one — a direct interest in an assay that judges their products. The counterweight: EPA/NICEATM coordinated the study, and an independent international validation management team monitored it throughout. Both facts are recorded; neither cancels the other.

Why this matters beyond one assay. The thyroid column now has two parallel validation jurisdictions — EU-NETVAL's eight mechanism-fragment methods, and this EPA/NICEATM functional-readout track — approaching the same endpoint from different biology and different regulators. That is new territory for the vault: every other endpoint with more than one validation effort has them within a single regulatory jurisdiction. Worth tracking whether the two tracks converge on the same chemicals, or diverge.
