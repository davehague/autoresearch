ASPA's first applied case study still runs on expert judgement, end to end

leist-2026-aspa-ngra-workflow published ASPA as a flowchart grammar with a reserved slot for automated ("A-DP") and iterative-loop ("L-DP") decision points — and reported zero of either in its published v1.9/S15 schema, with the explicit disclaimer that ASPA "is NOT a one-click risk assessment." islam-2026-nam-read-across-conazole-case-study is the first source this vault has found that runs ASPA on a concrete regulatory question rather than describing it: difenoconazole (target compound) read across from eight other conazole fungicides and two metabolites, using the same EU Horizon grant (964537, RISK-HUNT3R) and overlapping authorship (Leist M. on both).

Every judgement call in the published case study is made by a person, not a rule:

  • Source-compound selection combined structural (Tanimoto) and biological-response (CALUX, ToxProfiler, Cell Painting PLUS, primary-human-hepatocyte transcriptomics, H295R steroidogenesis) similarity — the paper states plainly that "a consensus on combining these complementary in silico data streams is not yet available."
  • Cross-platform disagreement was resolved by a human, not a rule. Prothioconazole clustered with the negative control in one assay (Cell Painting PLUS) and with the target compound in another (hepatocyte transcriptomics); the paper's own response is "a broader panel provides better coverage" — more data fed to a person's judgement, not a tie-breaking procedure.
  • Which of several computed numbers to trust was also a judgement call, not a rule: direct kNN read-across, reverse-dosimetry kNN, and a worst-case-source-compound route produced LOAEL/HED estimates that disagreed with each other by up to roughly 17-fold (see biological-similarity-read-across-matches-loael-but-not-via-every-route for the numbers); the paper picks among them by domain reasoning about where PBK-based reverse dosimetry is known to compound uncertainty, not by a published selection rule.

The paper's own conclusion names the gap directly: "consensus on the minimum assay set, biological coverage requirements, and concordance criteria for regulatory decision-making are still lacking." That is a restatement of this map's standing finding — the integration layer has no agreed interpretation rule — from inside an applied case study rather than a framework description. No A-DP or L-DP is exercised here either. ASPA's reserved automation slot remains empty in its first published application, not just in its first published specification.

Bias watch. Lead author Islam and co-author Gardner are employed by Certara Predictive Technologies (may hold shares) — a commercial PBK/read-across modelling vendor with a direct stake in NAM-based workflows being seen as regulator-ready. Co-author Bothe is employed by, and holds equity in, Bayer AG — a manufacturer of conazoles, the exact chemical class this case study assesses as having conservative (i.e. favourable) NAM- derived safety margins. funding_flag: [industry].
