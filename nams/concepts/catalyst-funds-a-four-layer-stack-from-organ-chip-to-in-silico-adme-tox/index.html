This is the vault's first named instance of a federally funded program explicitly designing a pipeline that runs organ-chip hardware into a computational model rather than treating the two as competing NAM categories. hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps already tracks organ-chip data as a standalone automatable-open-loop method; pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics already tracks computational ADME modeling as a mature computational-connective method with its own OECD framework. Why this belongs on the loop-infrastructure map rather than either endpoint map. No model exists yet to grade: there is no readout to classify as instrument-readable or not, and no decision rule to test for fixed vs. expert-judged, because nothing has been built. Filing it on automation-self-driving-labs alongside TSAssistant, ToxMCP and the STP roundtable keeps the endpoint maps clean of pre-validation architecture announcements, per this map's own standing convention.
