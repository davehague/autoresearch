The model layer's access-versus-accountability trade-off, resolved in one direction by a source with no platform to sell

strength: suggestive — this note records the paper's own account of regulatory integration and download counts, not an independent audit of CERAPP/CoMPARA/CATMoS predictive accuracy, which is not reported in the reached text. regulatory_status: under-validation rather than OECD-TG-adopted: CATMoS is described as integrated into OPERA and having "achieved regulatory acceptance," which is a real but narrower claim than a Test Guideline — logged as the paper states it, not upgraded.

The trade-off this map has been tracking, resolved differently here

in-silico-qsar-read-across carries a standing access_gate (set 2026-08-14): a model served only from a vendor's login-gated web endpoint cannot be version-pinned, re-run against a fixed reference set, or audited for which applicability-domain stratum a prediction came from — the gate is about the ability to check, not the cost. durai-2026-toxiverse-bioprofiling-modeling-platform (ToxiVerse) showed the opposite failure: open-source and free, but with no accuracy, no cross-validation, no benchmark, no applicability-domain policy reported anywhere — accessible and unaccountable. OPERA and its named endpoint models are the first source on this map that is credibly both at once: downloadable as a standalone desktop app, Docker container, or embeddable library (so it can be version-pinned and re-run independently, unlike a vendor's web endpoint), and built to the five OECD (Q)SAR validation principles with named regulatory integrations (EPA's Endocrine Disruptor Screening Program citing CERAPP by name; CATMoS folded into OPERA with stated regulatory acceptance) rather than an unaudited claim of usefulness.

Why funding_flag: none carries more weight here, not less

Every other "free public button" this map has recorded — ToxiVerse, the six-tool aquatic-UV-filter consensus — came with a funding_flag bias-watch attached (method-developer or industry). This one is a US federal intramural program describing its own government-built, government-maintained tooling, with no commercial platform and no licensing revenue at stake, which is the closest this map gets to a disinterested account of what "the model layer became a free public button" can look like when accountability is designed in rather than retrofitted.
