A fragrance-industry review names the standard genotox battery's own metabolic-competence gap, and proposes a four-method fix

Thakkar, Botelho & Api (2026, RIFM) review why standard in-vitro genotoxicity assays — the Ames bacterial reverse-mutation test (genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation, TG 471), in vitro micronucleus (genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus) and chromosomal aberration — routinely call fragrance/cosmetic materials genotoxic when the finding does not reflect real human hazard, citing false-positive rates "up to 93%." The attributed cause is structural, not chemical-specific: these test systems lack sufficient phase II metabolic competence (the enzymatic machinery that bioactivates or detoxifies a compound the way a human body would), so a compound can register as DNA-damaging in the assay purely as an artefact of an under-metabolizing test system.

The correlated-blind-spot angle. Three of this vault's consensus/regulatory-adopted genotoxicity methods — Ames, the standard in-vitro micronucleus test, and chromosomal aberration — share this same root cause for a false positive: none of them, run without S9 metabolic activation or an equivalent system, can distinguish "this chemical needs metabolism to become genotoxic and the test lacks that metabolism" from "this chemical is genuinely inert." A battery combining several of these assays on the same chemical gets concordant false positives, not independent confirmation — the shape this vault's rail exists to catch. This is a scope-condition on already-adopted methods (none of their evidence_tier/regulatory_status moves), not a validation failure.

The proposed fix composes four non-animal methods into a tiered strategy specifically to recover metabolic competence: genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa (mechanistic follow-up), genotoxicity-3d-skin-micronucleus-rsmn (dermal-relevant, some metabolic capacity), chicken-egg-based assays (CEGA/HET-MN — no map yet, logged as an open gap) whose fetal-chicken system supplies metabolic capacity the standard battery lacks, and in silico metabolism prediction (OASIS TIMES, connects to in-silico-qsar-read-across) as a cheap plausibility pre-filter.

Why it matters for a loop. An automated system trusting a battery of Ames + micronucleus + chromosomal aberration as independent lines of evidence would, on a metabolically-dependent chemical, see three concordant positives and treat that agreement as confirmation — when all three share the identical blind spot.
