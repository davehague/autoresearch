No validated method for photoallergy or photogenotoxicity → a documented blank

The vault's second ingested absence, and it is recorded for the same reason as the first (no-standalone-fertility-nam-replacement, the fertility/multigenerational blank): a gap the primary guidance states is real is a different object from a gap the vault merely hasn't read yet. This one is stated by an OECD Guidance Document, in one sentence:

"there are no validated test methods (i.e., OECD Test Guideline) to evaluate photosensitive or photogenotoxic potential of chemicals to this date."

GD 397 divides light-activated chemical hazards into three, and covers one:

HazardWhat it isValidated method
Phototoxicity (photoirritation)acute, non-immune, light-induced skin responseYes — TG 101, TG 495, TG 432, TG 498, composed by this IATA
Photoallergyimmune-mediated; light structurally alters the chemical so it acts as a haptenNone
Photogenotoxicitygenotoxic response via direct photoexcitation of DNA, or indirect excitation of a photoreactive chemicalNone

The guidance does not paper over the hole — it scopes itself around it, and says why: an IATA assembled over non-validated methods "would be difficult to use in regulatory contexts." So GD 397 restricts itself to phototoxicity and leaves the other two named and empty. That is an unusually clean statement of the field's own boundary, and the reason regulatory_status: none and stance: open-question are recorded here against an otherwise regulatory-adopted-tier source: the source is adopted guidance; the finding is that two hazards have nothing.

The two blanks are not equally orphaned, and the vault can already see why. Photoallergy is a hapten-driven, immune-mediated reaction — mechanistically the same class of event as skin sensitisation, where the vault holds its single most mature battery: the GL 497 defined approaches over DPRA (KE1, peptide haptenation), KeratinoSens (KE2) and h-CLAT (KE3) — see skin-sensitization-2o3-defined-approach. The photoallergy AOP is, in effect, the skin-sensitisation AOP with a photochemical step bolted onto the molecular initiating event. That the field has an adopted, validated battery for one and nothing for the other is a real and interesting asymmetry — flagged here as an open question, not as a claim that the sensitisation assays can be extended (they have not been validated for it, and this vault does not invent applicability).

Photogenotoxicity is similarly adjacent to a mature vault column: the genotox battery (genotoxicity-ames-bacterial-reverse-mutation, genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus, genotoxicity-mammalian-gene-mutation) covers gene mutation and chromosome damage, and genotoxicity-toxtracker-reporter-moa adds mechanistic mode-of-action discrimination — but none is validated with an irradiation arm. Again: adjacency is not applicability.

And a third absence, which reframes the whole column. TG 498's proficiency table records that its in-vivo reference classifications "were derived from the validation studies of the 3T3 NRU PT test (OECD TG 432) and were mostly based on clinical human data as no validated in vivo method is available for this endpoint." So phototoxicity has no animal incumbent — the NAMs here are not replacing a Draize or an LLNA, they are the method, benchmarked directly against humans. Every other endpoint in this vault asks "is the NAM as good as the animal test?" This one cannot, and does not.
