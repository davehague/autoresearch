The loop is refereed now. It is still pointed at yeast.

What changed. The vault's automation-self-driving-labs map has been assembled almost entirely from preprints: LAP (arXiv, unimplemented), ProtoPilot (arXiv), ToxMCP (bioRxiv, single author), Pipette (arXiv, simulation only), the Bayesian experiment-design paper (bioRxiv). Every honest caveat the vault wrote about this column included the words "not peer reviewed."

This one is, in the Journal of the Royal Society Interface, with the wet work done and two novel biological results reported. It is the second system in the vault demonstrated running end-to-end with physical execution (after ProtoPilot) and the first of the two to be refereed.

What did not change. There is no toxicology in it. No hazard endpoint, no OECD Test Guideline, no regulatory question, no chemical safety assay. The organism is Saccharomyces cerevisiae — baker's yeast — and the findings are metabolite interactions: glutamate inhibiting the growth of spermine-treated cells, aminoadipate partially rescuing cells from formic-acid stress.

The diagnosis the vault settled on 2026-07-20 holds, and this sharpens it. The gap is no longer architectural, it is domain. Loops work. They work in chemistry (ChemCrow's organosilicon syntheses), in molecular biology (ProtoPilot's plasmid construction), and now — refereed — in systems biology. What a toxicology loop still lacks is unchanged: a validated assay, a checkable applicability domain, a fixed decision rule, and a willing regulator. Fourteen maps in this vault are stuck on the third of those.

And there is a distinction this source makes clearer than the others: a discovery loop is not an evidence-generating loop. Every closed loop the vault holds is optimising for finding something new — a new material, a new hypothesis, a new metabolite interaction. Nobody has to accept the result. A regulatory toxicology loop has the opposite job: it must produce a defensible answer to a question somebody else asked, under a decision rule agreed in advance, that a regulator will act on. Those are different engineering problems wearing the same architecture, and success at the first says less about the second than the shared vocabulary implies. The hard part of the tox loop was never the loop.

Honest brakes. No reliability rates of any kind are reported (see symbolic-scaffolding-is-the-proposed-fix-for-llm-unreliability); the yeast findings are unreplicated; and this is the builders reporting their own system. funding_flag: none — public Swedish and UK research funding, no commercial sponsor. Status on the map holds at emerging: a single demonstration in an unrelated domain does not move a method's evidence grade, and there is no method here to grade.
