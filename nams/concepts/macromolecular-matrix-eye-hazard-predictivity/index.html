Macromolecular ordered-matrix eye test (OECD TG 496) → eye irritation / serious eye damage

A freeze-dried macromolecular reagent — proteins, glycoproteins, carbohydrates, lipids — is rehydrated and self-assembles into an ordered matrix that mimics the transparent, highly-organised structure of the cornea. A test chemical is applied; the extent to which it disrupts/denatures that matrix is read as optical density at 405 nm, converted to an Irritection Draize Equivalent (IDE) score, and compared to fixed prediction-model cut-offs. No cells, no tissue, no metabolism — pure chemistry, which is what "acellular biochemical" / "in chemico" means here.

Both axes adopted: evidence_tier: regulatory-adopted, regulatory_status: OECD-TG-adopted (OECD TG 496, adopted 25 June 2024, corrected 2 July 2026). Two validated methods live in the guideline — Ocular Irritection (OI®) and the OptiSafe Eye Irritation Test™ (OS) — both proprietary kits, hence funding_flag: method-developer (a test-system dependency; OptiSafe's inclusion was independently peer-reviewed by the OECD).

Two methods, two different jobs. Both call UN GHS No Category (a one-directional rule-out, the initial step of a Bottom-Up strategy). Only OI® additionally calls serious eye damage / UN GHS Category 1 without further testing — "At this time, only OI® can be used to identify chemicals that have the potential to induce serious eye damage."

The numbers, honestly. OI® overall against the rabbit Draize is 75% accuracy, 81% specificity, and only 54% sensitivity — as a positive detector it misses nearly half of true eye hazards, which is exactly why the guideline forbids it as a stand-alone and frames it as a rule-out / initial-step method. OptiSafe in its No-Category rule-out role is stronger for that job: 100% sensitivity (31/31), 82% specificity, 90% accuracy — no false negatives in-set, ~18% of safe chemicals over-called onward (the cheap direction). Read these the way the vault reads every rule-out tier: judge the negative call by its reliability, not the positive call by its specificity.

Scope, hard. The methods "cannot be used on their own to replace the in vivo Draize eye test." Neither resolves Category 2 (reversible irritation). Gases and aerosols are outside the applicability domain. Being acellular, they carry no metabolic competence — an inert parent with a corrosive metabolite is invisible, the same biotransformation blind spot the vault tracks in the aquatic and phototoxicity columns.

Where it sits in the vault. The second component method under the eye defined-approach hub (eye-irritation-serious-eye-damage-da, TG 467), joining the RhCE viability test (eye-irritation-rhce-no-category, TG 492). The two components read the same endpoint by mechanically independent routes — protein-matrix denaturation in a tube (here) vs living-tissue metabolic viability (TG 492). That independence is the good case the correlated-blind-spot rail exists to distinguish from the bad ones: an orthogonal measurement that fails differently, which is precisely the counter-design automation-tox21-qhts-platform demonstrated in production. On the loop axis, an OD-at-405 nm readout against a fixed IDE cut-off is one of the purest closed-loop-ready shapes in the vault — the same clean arithmetic as TG 435 (membrane barrier) and TG 495 (ROS photoreactivity).
