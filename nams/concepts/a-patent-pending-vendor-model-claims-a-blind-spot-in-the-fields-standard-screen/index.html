A vendor names a real gap in the field's standard screen, and is also the only party who has measured whether their fix works

The finding, plainly

This map already carries the field's premise that a single-channel (hERG-only) acute pore-block screen is an incomplete surrogate for arrhythmia risk — that is the entire reason the CiPA (Comprehensive in vitro Proarrhythmia Assay) initiative and this map's own multichannel hiPSC-cardiomyocyte assay exist. This source names a narrower, mechanistically distinct incompleteness inside hERG assessment itself: even a multichannel acute-block screen still assumes the number of channels present at the cell surface is fixed, and does not ask whether a drug reduces channel trafficking — folding, ER export, membrane delivery — which can prolong the QT interval by shrinking functional channel density with zero acute pore-blocking activity. That is a real, named mechanism (drug-induced hERG trafficking disruption is independently documented in the pharmacology literature the paper cites) and a genuine blind spot claim against the field's standard practice.

Why the vault does not credit the fix without the caveat

The paper's author is the founder, CEO, and a beneficial owner of Biosentry, the company selling the model, with disclosed patent filings on the described technology. No independent laboratory, no blinded evaluation, and the evaluated compound set (31 total, with the headline metric computed on a 21-compound CiPA subset at a fixed recall of 0.500) is small enough, and the reported metric narrow enough, that the paper's own framing — proof-of-concept requiring independent validation — is the honest read. A fixed recall of 0.500 by construction means the model misses half its true positives in this evaluation; the reported "1.000 specificity" describes performance on the half of true positives the model does flag, not overall accuracy, and citing it without that qualifier would overstate the result.

Relevance to the map's standing pattern

This map's 2026-08-14 entry already names a pattern: two unrelated vendors (NEXEL's classifier, Meta Heart's Cardiosim-Tox) each hold a private cardiac decision rule on this endpoint, and reads that as "cardiac safety is the highest-value early screen … so the remaining scarce good is the interpretation … and a product is not a publication." This source is a third instance, from a third company, and adds a wrinkle: unlike the other two, Biosentry's paper states its own limitation candidly (proof-of-concept, needs independent validation) rather than presenting the model as a finished product. The vault records that candor as a point in the paper's favor without letting it substitute for the validation itself.

Honest ceiling

Single author, single company, single un-replicated evaluation on 31 compounds. No interlaboratory comparison, no wet-lab confirmation of the trafficking-pathway mechanism in the cells this paper's own model is meant to stand in for. evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, strength: single-study. regulatory_status: none.
