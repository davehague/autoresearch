AR transactivation (OECD TG 458) → endocrine disruption, the "A" of EATS

With TG 455 (E) and TG 456 (S) already mapped, TG 458 gives the vault the A. The endocrine column now holds three of the four EATS modalities plus a non-EATS receptor (TG 454A, glucocorticoid). Thyroid (T) remains the one EATS gap.

The mechanism read. Androgens (testosterone, and the more potent 5α-dihydrotestosterone / DHT) bind the AR; the complex translocates to the nucleus, binds androgen response elements (AREs), and transactivates a firefly luciferase reporter. Agonist mode reads induction; antagonist mode reads inhibition of a fixed agonist reference (500 pM DHT in AR-EcoScreen™), with hydroxyflutamide as the reference antagonist.

Three methods, three architectures — and the differences are not cosmetic:

Only Method 3 reads an endogenous, natively regulated androgen receptor in a cell type that actually depends on androgen signalling. Methods 1 and 2 install the receptor in a cell that has no particular reason to have one — a CHO ovary line and a bone-tumour line. That is a legitimate design (it maximises signal-to-noise and controls receptor level), but it is worth naming: two of the three adopted methods for androgen biology read a transfected receptor in a heterologous cell. The guideline's own scope caveat — "should not be directly extrapolated to the complex in vivo situation" — is doing real work here.

Scope. OECD Conceptual Framework Level 2, exactly like TG 455, TG 493 and TG 454A: a mechanism screen for screening, prioritisation and weight-of-evidence input. It reports that a chemical activates or blocks a reporter gene downstream of the AR. It does not report androgenic harm, and it is not a fertility or reproductive-toxicity test — see reproductive-fertility-multigenerational-nams.

Its larger contribution is the confound it documents. All three methods have had to engineer away a response to the glucocorticoid receptor — see steroid-receptor-crosstalk-shared-reporter-confound, which is the finding that actually matters from this source.

funding_flag: method-developer records that all three cell lines are access-gated (MTA + licence with the JCRB Cell Bank for AR-EcoScreen™; licence with BioDetection Systems for AR-CALUX®; MTA with the Korean Collection for Type Cultures for 22Rv1/MMTV_GR-KO). The validations were run by national public bodies (NIFDS Korea; the JaCVAM lineage), not by the developers alone.
