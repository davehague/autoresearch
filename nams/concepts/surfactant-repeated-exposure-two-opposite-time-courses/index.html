The repeated-dose protocol this map has been missing arrives, and it immediately produces two incompatible shapes

This map's standing blocker — named independently by three reviews (organ-chip-readiness-tracks-the-evidence-not-the-biology, no-decision-rule-without-a-standardised-measurement, ali-user-group-names-sop-and-prevalidation-gap) — is no standardised dose characterisation, no agreed repeated-exposure design, for the airway ALI tissue family. This source is the first on this map to actually build and run a repeated-exposure protocol rather than argue for one. It is a direct continuation of bmd-mppd-modeling-gives-airway-irritation-a-standardized-dose-extrapolation — same program (PETA Science Consortium / VITO), same two surfactants (oleoyl sarcosine, Triton X-100), same MucilAir tissue — moving from "what single dose reaches the tissue" to "what happens over repeated daily doses."

The two surfactants do not just differ in potency — they differ in shape. Oleoyl sarcosine's effect grows day over day, the pattern a cumulative-damage model would expect. Triton X-100's effect is loudest on day 1 and quiets down afterward — consistent with either the tissue mounting a repair/adaptive response or the day-1 mechanism depleting whatever it was acting on. The abstract does not say which, and this vault cannot adjudicate that from a structured abstract alone. This is the map's fourth instance of two models or two chemicals on this map disagreeing with each other on a ranking or kinetic question — after Hopkins et al.'s vehicle-delivery artefact, the Kim/Johansson lung-chip contrast, and the 2026-10-19 single-dose potency-ranking reversal between BEAS-2B and MucilAir for these same two surfactants.

What does not move. No numeric cutoff is proposed, so loop_readiness stays closed-loop-ready on this map exactly as scoped by the standing domain_gate — to the Kim et al. 2026 lung-chip configuration only. This repeated-exposure protocol, like every other model on this map outside that one domain_gate, still has no published decision rule. regulatory_status holds at under-validation — this paper does not add a new regulatory action, it strengthens the evidence base inside the program that already moved the axis.

Open gap. The abstract gives no specific readout name (TEER, viability, a cytokine panel) and no numeric values, so this cannot yet be checked against the map's own 12%/55% viability-loss cutoffs or compared quantitatively to the single-dose companion study's BMD/MPPD-derived human equivalent concentrations.
