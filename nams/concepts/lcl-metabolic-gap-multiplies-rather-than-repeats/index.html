The rail, applied to a multiplier instead of a vote

The shared cause. Lymphoblastoid cell lines are immortalised white blood cells. They do not have a liver's enzyme complement, and the authors say so: the "limited metabolic capacity of lymphoblast cell lines" is why "the chemicals selected for this study were generally considered direct acting." No S9 fraction, no hepatocyte co-culture, no metabolic prosthesis of any kind was used here.

The vault already carries this exact gap in the same cell lineage. The in-vitro micronucleus assay (genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus) and the TGx-DDI biomarker (genotoxicity-tgx-ddi-transcriptomic-biomarker) both run in TK6 — itself a human lymphoblastoid line — and both bolt on rat liver S9 precisely because the cells have no native metabolism. That pairing is the vault's sixth correlated blind spot. Metabolism is now the shared cause in four of seven rows.

But this is a different shape of failure, and the difference is the point. Every previous row on the rail is about two assays voting on the same question: FET and RTgill-W1 both stand in for the fish test; the ROS assay and 3T3 NRU both sit in the phototoxicity battery; TG 494 and TG 492 both read the eye. Two votes that are really one vote. The error is additive — you thought you had two independent pieces of evidence and you had one.

A variability factor is not a vote. It is a multiplier applied to somebody else's answer: a hazard assay produces a point of departure, and the variability factor divides it to produce a limit protective of sensitive individuals. If the hazard assay is blind to a pro-toxicant (because it runs in TK6 with rat S9 that doesn't perform the relevant conversion) and the variability factor for that chemical was derived in cells that are blind in the same way, then the wrong dose is being divided by the wrong factor, in the same direction, for the same reason. The errors do not sit side by side; they compose. That is worse than a correlated vote, and no existing row on the rail has this structure.

A concrete way it fails. A chemical requiring bioactivation reads weakly cytotoxic in the LCL panel, so its measured spread across 131 donors is narrow, so it receives a low variability factor — "people don't differ much on this one." The real inter-individual variation for that chemical may live almost entirely in the metabolic step the assay does not perform, and the paper's own genome-wide association result points straight at it: the loci driving variability were "primarily containing transporter and metabolism genes." The assay found that metabolism genes drive variability, in a cell system that barely does metabolism. Whatever variability lives in full hepatic activation is not in these numbers, and the method returns a confident small factor rather than an abstention.

Flagged as a vault inference, and the composition point emphatically so. The paper never mentions TGx-DDI, never mentions the micronucleus assay, and never discusses autonomous systems. What is sourced is the metabolic limitation and the GWAS result; the cross-method pairing and the multiplicative-compounding argument are this vault's, and are labelled as such. Both maps carry correlated_blind_spot.

What a loop should do about it. The same remedy the Tox21 counter-design implies and the vault has now repeated four times: not another cell assay on the same metabolic assumption, but a measurement that fails differently — metabolically competent cells (HepaRG, primary hepatocytes) for the variability panel, or an explicit gate routing suspected pro-toxicants out before any factor is derived. A bigger donor panel adds statistical precision to a systematically shifted number.
