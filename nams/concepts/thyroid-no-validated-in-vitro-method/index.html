Thyroid: 67 in-vitro methods, zero Test Guidelines — the vault's third ingested absence

"validated Level 2 assays for THSD are still completely lacking today." — Vergauwen et al. 2024 (PMID 39931575) (THSD = thyroid hormone system disruption. "Level 2" = the OECD Conceptual Framework's in-vitro mechanistic tier — the rung where TG 455, TG 458, TG 493 and TG 456 all sit.)

The gap the vault named, and what closing it revealed

On 2026-07-12 the vault wrote: "Thyroid (T) — the last EATS modality, and now the endocrine column's highest-value single gap." It has been closed — and the gap was never in the vault.

EATS is the four-modality core of endocrine screening: Estrogen, Androgen, Thyroid, Steroidogenesis. The vault holds the other three as adopted OECD Test Guidelines, all with in-vitro methods and published decision rules:

Thyroid is not a hole in the map. It is a hole in the world.

The biology is not what's missing

The inventory organises Level 2 into eight biological blocks that walk the entire thyroid axis — central regulation (TRH/TSH receptors); synthesis (thyroperoxidase, the sodium-iodide symporter, IYD); transport (displacement from transthyretin and thyroxine-binding globulin); metabolism (deiodinases DIO1/2/3, glucuronidation, sulphation); cellular uptake (MCT8, OATP1C1, OAT4); receptor response (thyroid receptor TRα/TRβ transactivation); plus microtissue and integrative assays.

That is a mechanistically complete panel. Sixty-seven methods exist and people run them. What does not exist is validation — the independent, multi-lab demonstration that a method gives the same answer in someone else's hands. Eight methods are in the EU-NETVAL pipeline. One has already failed: the TPO luminol assay, rejected as non-specific to thyroperoxidase. That is the status lifecycle moving both ways, recorded in a source, on an endpoint the vault has never held before.

The inversion — and it is the sharpest thing in this note

Thyroid does have adopted OECD Test Guidelines. They are animal tests: the Amphibian Metamorphosis Assay (TG 231), XETA (TG 248), LAGDA (TG 241), plus — since 2016 — thyroid endpoints written into the standard in-vivo rodent guidelines.

So the regulatory answer to "is this chemical a thyroid disruptor?" is, today, still: dose an animal.

Estrogen and androgen got validated in-vitro Test Guidelines from 2009 onward. Thyroid got none. The endocrine column's other three modalities and its fourth are separated by roughly fifteen years of validation effort that thyroid never received — not by any difference in how well the biology is understood.

Set that against phototoxicity-iata, the vault's other structural oddity, which has NAMs and no animal test at all (its reference standard is human clinical data). Thyroid is the exact mirror: an animal test, and no validated NAM. Two endpoints, opposite pathologies, and both of them break the vault's default frame — "is the NAM as good as the animal test?" — from opposite directions.

The in-silico arm has the same shape

Twelve thyroid (Q)SAR models are statistically validated — and none has been "put forward for addition to the OECD QSAR Toolbox, where there is some regulatory peer review." Validated by their authors; never shown to a regulator. The same two-axis split the vault has documented everywhere else (in-silico-qsar-read-across), on a column with no wet-lab guideline to fall back on.

Loop reading

The eight EU-NETVAL methods are plate-based, machine-readable assays — fluorescence (TPO-AUR's Amplex UltraRed), fluorescent-probe displacement (TTR-ANSA/TTR-FITC), luciferase reporters (TR-CALUX), cell proliferation (T-screen). A robot could run every one of them tomorrow.

And it would not matter, because there is no agreed decision rule and no validated method to apply one to. This is automatable-open-loop in its purest form: the wet work is loop-shaped and the interpretation layer does not exist — the same diagnosis as developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb, and the same one tox21-robots-solved-humans-still-interpret finds at Tox21's scale. Automating thyroid screening would produce a great deal of data that nobody has agreed how to read.

Honest caveats

This is a catalogue, not an experiment. It reports no new performance data; its authority is the completeness of its survey. Several co-authors develop assays that appear in it, which would ordinarily earn a method-developer flag. It is not applied as a certainty-inflating one, because the paper's headline runs against that interest: a developer bias pushes toward claiming readiness, and this paper announces that nothing is ready. That is the same triangulation the vault used on Kumbhar 2026 for the fertility blank — and it is the reason this is graded strong rather than suggestive.
