The fatty-liver blank starts closing, on nine chemicals with known answers

Steatosis is fat building up inside liver cells — the first stage of fatty liver disease, the most common liver pathology there is, usually silent, and increasingly linked to chemical exposure. Until this note, the vault's liver coverage was cells dying (hepatotoxicity-liver-chip-mps) and bile backing up (hepatotoxicity-cholestasis-battery). Steatosis is neither of those, and a battery built for them does not see it. This is a coverage blank, and it starts closing here.

What the method does

It is anchored in an adverse outcome pathway network — a published causal chain from the molecular trigger to the organ-level harm — which is why the readouts are the ones they are rather than whatever was convenient.

Predict the trigger, in software. (Q)SAR (activity from chemical structure), read-across (inference from structurally similar chemicals), and molecular docking (simulating whether the molecule fits a receptor's binding site) together predict whether a chemical perturbs the ligand-activated transcription factors that switch fat-handling gene programmes on and off.

Measure the consequences, in cells. HepaRG human liver cells, then seven readouts along the pathway: lipid accumulation, fatty-acid uptake, mitochondrial fat-burning, lipid secretion, mitochondrial dysfunction, endoplasmic-reticulum stress, oxidative stress.

Integrate into one number. A "steatogenic score" combining biological relevance and response magnitude across all endpoints.

What it showed, stated at the strength it actually has

Seven chemicals already known to cause steatosis — including the epilepsy drug sodium valproate, the heart-rhythm drug amiodarone, the antibiotic tetracycline, and three plasticisers of which two are PFAS — scored above two chemicals already known not to.

"These scores clearly distinguished steatogenic from non-steatogenic chemicals."

Nine compounds with known labels cannot establish a false-positive rate, a false-negative rate, or an applicability domain. They can establish that the method is not broken, which is a real and necessary result and is exactly why this map opens at emerging rather than higher. Two negative controls is enough to show a score is not saturating; it is not enough to say anything about specificity. The vault has a live example of why that matters — the developmental-neurotoxicity battery's roughly 60% specificity, meaning about two in five clean chemicals wrongly flagged, only became visible at scale.

The loop reading

Every readout is instrument-readable — plate reader or high-content imaging — and the first layer is software by construction. The pipeline runs end to end in machines and produces a number.

And nothing published says where to cut the number. "Clearly distinguished" describes a figure, not a decision procedure. An automated system can compute this score today and cannot act on it, which is automatable-open-loop exactly.

One structural feature is worth separating out, because it is unusual and it is not about the blocker: the in-silico layer decides what the wet layer measures. A model choosing the experiment, inside a regulatory toxicology method, is the front half of a closed loop. It is a weak instance — the tiering is fixed, nothing updates on the wet result, there is no feedback edge — but it is the crossover shape this vault has been watching for since 2026-07-12, and it appeared in toxicology rather than in materials chemistry. Recorded separately in in-silico-layer-chooses-what-the-wet-layer-measures.

The brakes

  • Nine chemicals, all pre-labelled, single laboratory, no independent replication. evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory.
  • The developers evaluated their own method. This is the ONTOX consortium assessing an ONTOX workflow, and two co-authors are at ProtoQSAR S.L., a commercial QSAR firm supplying the kind of in-silico layer it sells. The declared statement is no competing interest; the vault grades funding_flag: [method-developer, industry] regardless, per its bias-watch rail.
  • The decision rule is unread — the weighting inside the steatogenic score is the whole procedure and it is behind Elsevier's paywall. Logged.
  • HepaRG only, and that is not a free choice. One immortalised, single-licensor cell line, shared with metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg and hepatotoxicity-cholestasis-battery. Per cell-line-identity-outweighs-culture-medium-in-hepatic-transcriptomes, cell-line identity is the dominant source of transcriptomic variance in hepatic models — so three liver methods on one line are not three independent windows onto the liver. Flagged as a correlated_blind_spot on all three maps.
  • regulatory_status: none. No Test Guideline, no validation-pipeline entry. Per the FDA CDER framework that does not mean unusable — CDER will accept a fit-for-purpose method with no guideline — but it is where this sits tonight, and this method is a long way from fit-for-purpose evidence.
