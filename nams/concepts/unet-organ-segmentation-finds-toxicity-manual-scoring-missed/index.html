A segmentation model, not a classifier — the escape hatch broadens to "finding what the human missed"

This map's four prior machine-vision entries (Sivaprasad, Al-Absi, Pei, FINS — see developmental-toxicity-zebrafish-teratogenicity through-line) all frame the automation question the same way: can a model replicate the human's named-malformation call, faster or at scale? Wang & Wang 2025 answer a different question. Their U-Net does pixel-level segmentation and morphometric quantification of four internal tissue layers (photoreceptor cell layer, inner plexiform layer, skeletal muscle, spinal cord) in zebrafish exposed to Ag⁺, 15 nm AgNPs, and 100 nm AgNPs — and the paper's headline claim is not "matches the expert" but that the automated readout surfaced size-dependent, organ-specific toxicity disparities that conventional analytical approaches failed to resolve.

Why this belongs in the ranking, but does not move the tier. The map's actual loop_blocker is the regulatory readout: graded malformation typing (edema, jaw defects, spinal curvature → teratogenic index) on the ICH S5(R3) reference-compound set. This paper does not touch that task — it works on a different chemical class (silver, not ICH S5(R3) drugs), a different measurement (sub-organ morphometry, not gross-malformation classification), and reports no EC50 or teratogenic index. It extends the general claim that zebrafish morphology is machine-scoreable (now a fifth demonstration, after Sivaprasad, Al-Absi, Pei, FINS) without directly attacking the specific blocker this map tracks. loop_readiness HOLDS at human-in-the-loop.

The nuance worth keeping. Every prior escape-hatch attempt on this map is a race against a human's existing judgment — can the model call it the way the expert calls it, or faster. This is the first instance where the automated method's value proposition is explicitly finding something the manual method could not see at all (a resolution/throughput argument, not an agreement argument). That is a genuinely different shape of evidence for what an autonomous loop could add beyond replacing a scorer — and it is exactly as unvalidated as the rest of the thread: single lab, single paper, no accuracy figure against its own training masks, no independent replication. funding_flag: none — academic group (City University of Hong Kong), no vendor claim, so this is not the automation-hype variant.

Training-data caveat, consistent with the vault's standing observation. The U-Net is trained on manually-drawn masks — like the Bhas 42 focus classifier (carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay) and the DNT battery's CNNs (dnt-scoring-is-already-a-neural-network-and-the-blocker-holds), it learns to reproduce a human's segmentation rather than independently verifying it. The organ-specific disparities it surfaces are real signal only to the extent the training masks and thresholding-method comparison are themselves accurate — unmeasured here beyond a qualitative "robustness testing" claim in the code repository.
