"One Exposome" names the vault's own closed-loop question as a governance proposal, with no system behind it yet

The finding, plainly

This vault exists to ask which NAM endpoints could run in a closed loop. Coecke & Hartung name substantially the same target — a "recursive, self-correcting loop" / "self-improving toxicology ecosystem" where experiments update models and models choose the next experiment — as the destination for the whole NAM/AI field, not one method. It extends Hartung's own earlier solo proposal already on this map (hartung-2026-evidence-to-decision-agentic-ai, 2026-07-31: GRADE Evidence-to-Decision as the decision-leg template) by wrapping it inside a wider exposure-surveillance and digital-twin architecture. Nothing here is built. The paper is explicit that its value "is a hypothesis to be tested," proposes measurable testable propositions (Section 7.5) for future work, and names human oversight and final regulatory decision authority as structural requirements, not concessions.

Where it fits, and where it does not fit cleanly

automation-self-driving-labs's current declared sub-threads are execution (can a machine physically carry out an experiment) and verification (is the measuring stick sound) — carved apart from experiment selection, which forked to automation-experiment-selection on 2026-08-12. This source is conceptually closest to the deciding leg (the Evidence-to-Decision layer, the self-correcting loop concept), the same category the pre-fork Hartung EtD source already sits in on this map. It is recorded here, alongside that prior source, rather than forced into either of the two current strands or moved to the forked child map — consistent with how the 2026-07-31 Hartung entry was already treated, and flagged for the standing coherence-stale re-read rather than silently absorbed.
