"Level 4 autonomy" is not one measurement — two ladders, two verdicts, same underlying systems

automation-self-driving-labs ingested Cheng et al. 2026 four nights ago (a-pharma-rd-head-writes-the-industrys-own-autonomy-ladder-and-still-finds-no-level-4) and recorded, as a second independent framework-level confirmation of this map's recurring finding, that "fully demonstrated Level 4 autonomy therefore remains an open frontier." This source complicates that read: it is a different, earlier autonomy ladder (predating Cheng's, adapted from Tom et al. and SAE self-driving-car levels) that defines Level 4 more loosely — autonomous protocol generation, execution, analysis, and results-driven hypothesis adjustment, with human intervention needed only for anomalies — and under that definition, Adam (yeast gene-function hypothesis testing), Eve (malaria hit-compound screening), and MicroCycle have already reached it.

Neither claim is false. Adam and Eve really did run closed-loop hypothesis-to-test cycles largely unattended (clearing Tobias & Wahab's bar). Neither one publishes machine-verifiable failure-recovery guarantees or audit trails (failing Cheng's additional, stricter bar). The ladders measure different things under the same name. This is a methodological finding the vault should carry forward on its own terms: any claim that cites "a system has/hasn't reached Level N autonomy" needs its ladder's definition attached, or the claim is not comparable across sources — exactly the kind of silent-collapse-of-a-distinct- axis this vault's evidence model exists to prevent, one level removed (here it's two infrastructure papers disagreeing with each other, not a toxicology finding disagreeing with another).

Addendum, 2026-10-05 — a third data point, from the primary source this time

Cheng et al.'s "L3-to-narrow-L4 at best" verdict named three systems, one of them "GPT-5-Ginkgo." This vault had only Cheng's secondhand characterization until tonight, when gpt5-ginkgo-primary-source-shows-human-reengineering-between-automated-steps ingested the primary paper directly. The primary source confirms Cheng's reading from the inside: the paper's own body text describes substantive human diagnosis and workflow re-engineering interleaved between GPT-5's automated optimization rounds — exactly the kind of failure-recovery-by-a-human, not by-the-system, gap that keeps a demonstration below Cheng's stricter Level-4 bar even as it would plausibly clear Tobias & Wahab's looser one (closed-loop hypothesis-to-test cycling, human intervention for anomalies — which the reagent-stock and plasmid-redesign interventions arguably still are). This doesn't resolve the ladder disagreement; it adds a third, primary-source-verified data point on the same side of it as Adam/Eve: a system that clears a loose bar and not a strict one, with the gap located in the same place (humans recovering from failures the system surfaced but could not itself fix).
