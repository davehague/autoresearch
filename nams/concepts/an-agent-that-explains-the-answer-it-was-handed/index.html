It writes the rationale. The verdict came with the retrieval.

phenotypic-profiling-cell-painting carries the sharpest version of this vault's dominant blocker. Cell Painting is fully machine-read end to end — fixed dye panel, automated microscope, trained feature extractor, no human scoring anywhere — and it is stuck at automatable-open-loop because no rule converts its output into a call, and unlike the vault's other open-loop maps, nobody is writing one. The method deliberately targets no endpoint, so there is nothing for a data-interpretation procedure to interpret into.

CP-Agent is the closest thing the vault has seen to someone attacking that gap. It is worth being precise about what it attacks.

The direction of inference

CP-Agent chains six sub-agents: retrieve experimental context, segment channels, extract cell features, rank features, synthesise statistics, generate a report. Its output is a paragraph of prose linking measured morphological changes to a biological mechanism.

The first stage — context retrieval — matches the image against the archive and returns what the paper calls the "Most Matched Experimental Context". That block contains the cell line, the culture medium, the imaging settings, the compound, and the compound's mechanism of action.

The reports then begin, verbatim from the paper's own worked examples:

"The specimen is hypothesized to reflect microtubule stabilization by taxol leading to bundled tubulin and mitotic arrest. Below, I assess whether the observed morphology aligns with this hypothesis…"

"Given the proposed MoA of PI3K class I inhibition, it is known to alter cellular metabolism and survival signaling … which should cause mitochondrial dysfunction, cell cycle arrest, and cytoskeletal disruption; this would present as enlarged irregular cells with disorganized mitochondria (observed: +64% texture contrast, +41% cell area, reduced form factor)."

The mechanism is the input. The report is an argued verdict on whether the picture fits it.

Why that is not the missing rule

A decision rule for a hazard method has to run in one specific direction: unknown chemical in, verdict out. Everything upstream of the verdict is allowed to be automated, and in Cell Painting's case already is. The step nobody has written is the one that says this fingerprint means this chemical is a problem, for a chemical whose answer is not already in the archive.

CP-Agent runs the other direction. Given the chemical and its known mechanism, it produces a defensible account of why the picture is consistent with that. In drug discovery — the paper's actual target, and it is an ICLR paper, not a toxicology one — that is a real and valuable job: it makes a screening result legible to a chemist and flags where the evidence is thin. The reports do that visibly well, including the one on sorbinil, where the agent "flagged ambiguity and suggested further validation" rather than overclaiming.

For a regulatory loop it is the wrong direction, and being unimpressed by it would be a category error rather than a criticism of the work.

The evaluation measures plausibility, not correctness

"Experts (N = 11) … rated 40 reports (10 pairs × 4 models) on a 1–7 scale across ten criteria … covering language quality and reasoning quality."

Eleven raters. Ten image pairs. A subjective 1-to-7 scale. The reported outcome is that most metrics scored high and that GPT-5 reasoned best.

Nothing in that design can produce a correctness rate, because on all ten pairs the mechanism was already known and supplied to the system. The quantity measured is how convincing eleven people found the prose.

This vault has a standing reason to be careful with that specific quantity. A fluent, well-structured, appropriately-hedged rationale for a supplied conclusion is the easiest artefact a language model produces and the hardest one for a reader to discount — and an automated loop that consumes such rationales as evidence will find every one of them persuasive. That is the same failure shape as the correlated-blind-spot rail: speed converting a known weakness into a compounding one.

What would move the axis

A published procedure that takes a Cell Painting fingerprint for a chemical with no prior data and returns a classification, with a stated accuracy against a reference set. Not a rationale. A rule, and a number for how often it is right.
