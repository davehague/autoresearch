Machine-vision scoring of zebrafish developmental abnormalities → developmental toxicity (teratogenicity)

The zebrafish developmental-toxicity assay is stuck at human-in-the-loop for one specific reason: a trained human scores morphological malformations by eye (pericardial/renal edema, jaw malformation, spinal curvature) to build the teratogenic index. This is a loop-move claim about that exact bottleneck: Sivaprasad et al. (2026) show a Vision Transformer, trained on time-lapse embryo microscopy, can learn the scoring step — 98% accuracy on fertility classification, 92% on detecting developmental malformations from toxic exposure (toxicant: 3,4-dichloroaniline, the FET's own reference chemical).

Why this does not move the map to loop-ready — three reasons, all recorded.

  1. Single study, research benchmark. evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, regulatory_status: none. One toxicant, one dataset, a model reported once — not validated, not ring-trialled, not regulatorily recognised.
  2. Still slower than a human. ~3.4 h (model) vs ~1.3 h (expert) for fertility; the authors flag an early-detection accuracy gap and future work. The task is shown learnable, not solved.
  3. Binary, not graded. 92% "malformation present/absent" is a long way from the graded malformation typing (which organ, how severe → teratogenic index) the regulatory readout requires.

What it does change: the reading of the blocker. "Scored by eye" is no longer a permanent architectural fact of this assay — it now has a demonstrated, if immature, machine-vision path. The disciplined move is to keep loop_readiness: human-in-the-loop (a human is still load-bearing in the regulatory call) while annotating the blocker to record that the escape hatch exists. This is the exact pattern stages/01-sweep names as high-value: a machine-vision method for an assay currently scored by eye.

funding_flag: none — public AI-research funding (Image-Tox; ELSA; German Federal Ministry of Education and Research), an academic computer-vision group, not a vendor selling an automated scorer. So the automation claim is graded as an honest single-study result, not the "fully automated platform" hype variant the bias watch flags. The same escape hatch is the thing that would move genotoxicity-in-vitro-micronucleus and carcinogenicity-cell-transformation-assay — the vault's other two human-in-the-loop maps — if it ever lands there.
