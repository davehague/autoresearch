Tox21's mechanism screens correlate with in-vivo DART outcomes, but nobody has turned the correlation into a rule

automation-tox21-qhts-platform's standing finding is that Tox21's instrument-readable output has no fixed, published procedure converting it into an apical hazard call — loop_readiness: automatable-open-loop, blocked by the missing decision rule rather than by robotics. This source tests that gap against a column the map's Tox21 through-line had not yet reached: developmental and reproductive toxicity. It finds the same shape of answer as every other Tox21-downstream source on the map (Xu 2026 on DILI/cardiotoxicity, Chiu 2026 on point-of-departure derivation): real, statistically detectable structure in the data, and no rule that converts it into a classification a loop could apply.

Why direction: extends-applicability rather than regulatory-move or loop-move. Nothing here moves Tox21's loop_readiness tier — it was already automatable-open-loop and remains so, for the same reason. What extends is the map's evidence base: a fully independent group (no ScitoVation-style method-developer stake, no NCATS/EPA institutional interest in Tox21's own success) reaches the platform's central finding — mechanistic screening data needs an interpretation layer that does not yet exist — from a DART angle the map had not covered.

The PR (progesterone receptor) reversal is worth keeping. Within TG 421, PR agonism associated in the opposite direction from every other significant MIE, and the authors could not explain why from their data alone — a reminder that "significant association" in a Fisher's-exact screen is a purely statistical enrichment claim, not a validated mechanistic signal, and a loop consuming these associations uncritically would have no way to distinguish the PR reversal from a real finding.

Honest ceiling, and a caught hallucination. Fisher's-exact enrichment against a reference database is a different, weaker claim than a trained classifier's held-out predictive accuracy, and this paper never reports the latter — no AUC, sensitivity, specificity, or accuracy figure appears anywhere in the full text. An earlier automated summary of this paper's abstract fabricated an "AUC 0.7–0.98" range; that figure does not exist in the source and is explicitly excluded from this note (see vault/sources/kwon-2025-tox21-oecd-dart-mechanistic-associations.md). Binary TG-positive/negative classification discards dose-response and potency information by the authors' own admission, and chemicals lacking a definitive TG call or CAS number were dropped from analysis without a reported effect size for that exclusion.
