The uncertainty estimate held, on real biology — this map's own standing skepticism gets one of its first positive instances

automation-experiment-selection's own declared single sub-thread has, since it was forked out of automation-self-driving-labs on 2026-08-12, carried one recurring finding underneath nearly every bullet: "the uncertainty estimate the selection rests on is the part that has not been shown to hold." MOMbot is one of the clearer positive instances this map has logged of that estimate actually holding, on real tissue rather than a simulator with a known ground truth: the active learner's own uncertainty model predicted where the interesting region of the search space was (centered on the eventual logistic-curve inflection point), and the interventions it chose to run there, chosen without knowing the true ID50 in advance, did in fact land closer to the number the full 121-organoid dataset later revealed than did interventions a human picked manually or interventions generated at random.

Why this is biology, not chemistry or materials, and why that matters for this vault's crossover thesis. Every prior selection-mechanism source on this map — the simulated 45-task benchmark, Qin's real pipetting robot matching food-dye absorbance spectra, MoLRSI's antibiotic-screening agent, Che's wastewater decolorization loop — either ran in a chemistry/materials domain or on a computational simulator. Brunnsåker (2026-11-09) was this map's first real biology-domain closed loop, on yeast metabolism. MOMbot is this map's second, and it is closer to the vault's own subject matter than the first: the biological material is whole-organism/organoid/stem-cell developmental biology, including Xenopus laevis embryos — the same species regulatory developmental-toxicity testing already uses (the Frog Embryo Teratogenesis Assay–Xenopus, FETAX) — though MOMbot's own demonstrated loop (thermal control of developmental rate) is not itself a teratogenicity assay, so this is organism kinship, not an actual toxicology crossover.

The honest ceiling, stated by the authors and worth repeating rather than smoothing over. The result is reported on one intervention modality (electrical field duration) and one organoid system; the authors' own stated scope is that the loop converges well "after an investigator has pre-determined a space of interventions… and is willing to provide biological targets" — a human still frames the question. Whether the same uncertainty estimate holds on the other three demonstrated-but-not-closed-loop modalities (chemical, thermal, mechanical vibration), or on more complex biological targets, is explicitly named by the authors as future work, not shown here.
