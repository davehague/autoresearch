Another self-driving-lab safety claim that is more honest in its limitations section than its abstract

automation-self-driving-labs's execution strand already tracks LabShield (diagnostic gap between what an agent knows and does), Safe-SDL (an architecture for the gap), LabGuard (the first measured before/after pipeline), AEGIS (a live physical-robot demonstration on an OT-2), SafeLab (Safe Success Rate trailing Success Rate by 30+ points) and PREVENT (pre-execution hazard gating, simulation-only). Chemist Eye adds a camera-and-vision-language-model layer meant to watch a whole lab — PPE compliance, worker medical emergencies, fire — and steer mobile robots or alert humans in response.

The abstract's 97%/95% headline does not survive the paper's own tables. The 97% figure is LLaVA-Phi3's best per-question result (97.5% PPE, 97.0% accident) on a 2,000-image manually-labelled set, not an overall system accuracy, and at least one decision-making configuration the text cites as 10/10 shows 3/10 in the paper's own table, unreconciled. The authors say so themselves, more plainly than the abstract implies: the VLMs are "not yet trustworthy" for autonomous safety decisions and belong at the human-alerting layer, not the autonomous-decision layer — with hallucination rates reaching 93% (LLaVA-Phi3) and 41% (LLaVA-7B) on specific questions, and, without added scene context, a tendency to move robots closer to a simulated fire rather than away from it (the most common decision error logged).

Deployment, stated precisely. The sensing hardware is real (the Cooper Group's Automation Chemistry Lab, three mobile robots), but the evaluation is a simulated replay of recorded ROS bag files, not live closed-loop robot control — a materially weaker deployment claim than AEGIS's physical-robot demonstration already on this map, and worth distinguishing from it precisely for that reason.

Bias watch, and why this one reads differently. funding_flag: [none] — ERC, EPSRC, Leverhulme and Royal Society funding, no commercial platform sold, no vendor stake in the outcome. The builders still evaluate their own system (the standing caveat on every execution-strand source here), but the automation-hype discount this vault applies to vendor "fully automated" claims applies less to this one — and the paper's candor about its own system's untrustworthiness is itself worth crediting, consistent with how this map has treated self-reported limitations elsewhere (e.g. the Alyagoub hERG-trafficking source on cardiotoxicity-hipsc-cm-proarrhythmia).

Zero toxicology or chemical-hazard content. General lab-safety infrastructure, not a tox assay — filed for the crossover pattern, not as a toxicological finding. No correlated_blind_spot — no chemical panel.
