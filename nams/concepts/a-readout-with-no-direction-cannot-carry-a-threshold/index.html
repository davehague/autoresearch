A readout with no direction cannot carry a threshold

The vault's loop ladder has a hidden prior step

The third axis grades a method on two questions: is the readout instrument-readable, and does a fixed, deterministic data-interpretation procedure exist. The vault has repeatedly found the second question to be the binding one — Test Guideline 493 is fully quantitative and still automatable-open-loop because the guideline says no agreed interpretation method exists.

It is natural to read "no agreed decision rule" as an administrative gap: nobody has convened the committee that would set the cut-off. This source shows a case where the gap is scientific and upstream of any cut-off.

What happened

Primary human blood immune cells, two standard provocations — a bacterial cell-wall molecule for the fast innate arm, a plant protein that forces T cells to divide for the adaptive arm — and six PFAS.

  • T-cell proliferation went UP in several analogues.
  • Cytokine secretion went DOWN in a "non-inclusive but overlapping subset" of analogues.
  • Gene expression showed the same split: antibody genes and B-cell identity down, T-cell activation up.

All of it is reported, correctly, as immune dysregulation. Immunotoxicity genuinely runs in both directions — suppression makes you fight infection worse, activation drives inflammation and autoimmunity — and both are adverse.

Why that is a decision-rule problem and not just a messy result

A threshold rule is if x > t then hazard. It presupposes that larger x means more harm. Give it a readout where both larger and smaller are harmful and the rule has no expressible form; you would need at minimum a two-sided rule, a per-readout sign, and an account of what to do when two readouts in the same assay disagree — which is exactly the observed case here, because the compound sets only overlap, they are not the same.

A closed loop makes this worse rather than better, and it is worth being precise about why. A loop does not merely classify; it optimises. It selects the next experiment to maximise something. Point an active-learning selector at "T-cell proliferation" and it will happily walk uphill toward compounds that proliferate hardest — which, on this evidence, is a different set of compounds from the ones suppressing cytokines, and there is no reason yet to believe the first set is the more dangerous one. The loop would be fast, confident and searching the wrong axis.

This is a different failure from the vault's correlated-blind-spot rail, and worth keeping distinct. The rail is about two measurements that agree for a bad reason. This is about one measurement whose sign is undefined. A loop compounds both, but you fix them differently: the rail needs an independent second method, and this needs an anchoring study that says which direction predicts the human outcome.

What it would take to give the readout a sign

Something outside the assay has to establish which direction predicts harm in people. The candidate anchor already exists and is named in the same abstract: human studies report "decreased vaccine antibody titres" after PFAS exposure. That is a real, directional, human outcome. Nothing in this work connects the in-dish directions to it quantitatively — the connection offered is a mechanistic hypothesis, that PFAS disrupt the coordination between T cells and B cells, which would explain how T-cell activity could rise while antibody output falls.

If that hypothesis is right, the sign of the T-cell readout is not merely unknown — it is inverted relative to the intuitive reading. More T-cell proliferation would be a marker of worse antibody outcomes. That is a testable claim and this study does not test it.

Evidence weight

One government laboratory, six compounds out of a class the same abstract sizes at "more than 14,000", no prediction model, no threshold, no reference chemical set, no independent replication, subscription-only full text unread. evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, strength: single-study. The direction observation is robust to the missing numbers — it is a statement about which way things moved, which the abstract states — but everything about magnitude, potency and donor variability is absent.
