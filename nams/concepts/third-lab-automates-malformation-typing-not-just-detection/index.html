The machine-vision escape hatch, attempt three: categorical typing plus an automated EC50, not just a yes/no call

This map's loop_blocker names graded morphological malformation typing scored by eye as the reason it sits at human-in-the-loop. The two prior machine-vision attempts on record here answered a coarser question than that: zebrafish-machine-vision-malformation-scoring (Sivaprasad 2026) detected malformation-present-or-not as a binary call; al-absi-2026-zebrafish-automated-embryo-sorting's commercial classifier's category labels were statistically unrelated to real embryo outcomes. Pei et al. (2026) is the first to train on the actual named phenotype categories the regulatory readout is built from — head hemorrhage, jaw malformation, swim bladder absence, pericardial edema, yolk edema, bent spine — with per-category accuracy reported rather than one pooled number, and to close the loop through to an automated dose–response fit.

Why this is a genuine step closer, not a repeat. The 2026-08-15 bullet on this map named the remaining gap precisely: "the regulatory readout is graded malformation typing — which organ, how severe." This source addresses "which organ" directly (eight anatomically-named categories, individually scored) for the first time. It does not address "how severe" — the categories are presence/absence per phenotype, with no severity grading within a category (e.g., degree of spinal curvature), so the blocker survives in a narrower form.

Why the EC50 check is the most persuasive piece here. 120 images from two exposure groups never used in training were scored end-to-end by the model, and its automatically-fitted EC50s landed within roughly 2-9% of independently, manually-calculated values. This is a real held-out check within a single study — stronger than a training-set accuracy figure — though the held-out compounds (CAL, DCAL) were still among the four the model was trained on, just unseen images of them, not unseen toxicant classes.

Why the tier does not move. Single lab, no ring trial, no independent replication — the same ceiling every prior instance on this map has hit. The compound domain (industrial/environmental disinfection by-products) differs entirely from the ICH S5(R3) reference-compound set the map's own regulatory-validity claim (Song 2021) is built on, so this evidence extends the machine-vision argument without touching the assay validity argument. funding_flag: none — Chinese NSFC-funded academic group, no vendor involvement, not the automation-hype variant this vault watches for.
