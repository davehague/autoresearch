A non-animal seizure assay arrives with numbers and no threshold

What was missing until now

The vault opened its adult central-nervous-system column on 2026-08-13 with neurotoxicity-cns-safety-nams-ai — and that map is bench-bound / animal-input, because the assessment strategy it describes names "traditional toxicology findings" (the in-vivo study) as one of its three inputs. Rung 2 of the ladder, first match wins. See an-integration-that-consumes-an-animal-study-is-bench-bound-at-the-top.

Worse, the automation on that endpoint was pointed at the animal: sensors plus AI for "continuous, non-invasive monitoring of animal behavior" (the-ai-on-this-endpoint-was-pointed-at-the-animal-not-at-its-replacement), and the guideline that requires the study reported "state-of-the-art functional in vivo approaches" among its modernisation pillars (the-modernisation-pillars-name-a-better-animal-study-and-a-human-judgement).

So the column had a strategy that consumed an animal study, and no assay that didn't. This is the assay that doesn't.

What it measures

Nerve cells fire by moving charged particles through pores called ion channels. Four of them, each a known route to a seizure, were measured by automated electrophysiology — a robotic patch-clamp that reads the electrical current through the channel directly and reports an IC₅₀, the concentration at which half the channel's activity is blocked:

ChannelWhat it doesSeizure route
K_V2.1potassium channel; resets the neuron after firingblock it → firing does not damp down
Na_V1.2sodium channel; starts the firing signalinterfere → firing destabilises
GABA_A (α1β2γ2)the brain's main brake receptorblock the brake → excitation runs away
α4β2 nicotinicexcitatory receptor (nicotine's target)activate → extra excitation

Then a second layer: human induced pluripotent stem cell-derived neurons grown on a microelectrode array — a well plate whose floor is a grid of electrodes, so the neurons' firing is recorded electrically rather than watched by anyone. The reported result is that the seizure-like firing phenotype changed with the derivatives in the direction the channel IC₅₀ values predicted.

That is a coherence check between two in-vitro layers, and it should be read as one. It is not a comparison against a person, or against an animal. It says the two measurements tell a consistent story about the same chemistry — a genuine and useful result, and a different thing from being right.

Why it lands at automatable-open-loop

Apply the deterministic ladder in shared/evidence-model.md:

  1. Is there a physical assay? Yes — not computational-connective.
  2. Does the method's own model consume an in-vivo study? NO. This is the difference from the strategy map next door, and it is the whole reason a separate map exists.
  3. Is human judgment load-bearing in the call? No. An IC₅₀ off an automated patch clamp and burst statistics off a microelectrode array are instrument numbers. There is no morphology, no histology, no trained scorer — none of the blockers that hold the micronucleus assay, the cell transformation assay or zebrafish teratogenicity at human-in-the-loop.
  4. Machine-readable readout with no fixed data-interpretation procedure → rung 4.

And rung 4 is where it stops, for two separate missing rules:

  • No integration rule. Nothing published says how four IC₅₀ values combine. Is a compound with one potent channel hit and three clean worse than one with four moderate hits? The paper does not say, because its purpose was to guide chemists away from substructures, not to classify compounds.
  • No margin rule. An IC₅₀ is a concentration in a dish. The regulatory question is whether a patient's brain ever sees that concentration. Nothing here relates the two. This is the same gap the-dose-the-loop-controls-is-not-the-dose-the-cell-receives and pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics exist to name, and it is a hard gap for the brain specifically, because of the blood-brain barrier — see model-disagreement-as-a-machine-checkable-refusal-rule, where a consensus model applied to 6,031 screening actives predicted 27% would never reach the brain receptor they scored against.

So: a machine could run every step of this assay tonight and return a clean table of numbers, and nobody has written down what the table means. That is the vault's single most common blocker, arriving on a brand-new 2026 method rather than inherited from a decades-old guideline — the same shape recorded for the kidney on 2026-07-28 in the-kidney-arrives-with-a-plate-reader-and-no-rule.

The bias watch, and it is load-bearing

Nine of eleven authors are ApconiX, a contract research organisation whose commercial business is ion-channel screening and safety pharmacology. The assay is theirs ("we previously described an integrated in vitro liability assay for seizure"), the structure-activity knowledge produced is a saleable asset, and the other two authors are at a computational medicinal-chemistry consultancy. There is no academic, regulatory or public-body co-author, and no competing-interest statement in the accepted-manuscript record. funding_flag: [industry, method-developer] — the exact case shared/evidence-model.md names: a method developer reporting on the performance of its own method.

Two design facts limit what the numbers can mean, independently of who paid. First, the 88 compounds were chosen for similarity to known actives — the correct design for building a structure-activity relationship, and the wrong one for estimating error rates, because the set is enriched in positives and contains no representative negatives. Second, four channels out of many dozens implicated in seizure. A seizure mechanism running through a channel not on the panel is invisible here by construction.

evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, regulatory_status: none, strength: single-study. This is a promising screen from one company, not a validated method, and nothing in it is accepted anywhere.

The structural sibling worth noticing

This is the same instrument stack as the vault's heart map: automated electrophysiology on engineered channel-expressing cell lines, plus human iPSC-derived cells on a microelectrode array. See cardiotoxicity-hipsc-cm-proarrhythmia and three-cardiac-channels-predicted-jointly-beat-three-predicted-alone. The cardiac version of this argument is roughly a decade ahead — it has an international initiative behind it, a named channel panel, and an agreed direction of travel. The brain version has one company and 88 compounds. If a loop is going to be built on this instrument class, the heart is where the decision rule will be written first, and the brain will inherit the pattern.
