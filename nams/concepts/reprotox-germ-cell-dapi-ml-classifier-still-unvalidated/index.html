A third unvalidated male-fertility-tier platform arrives, measuring germ-cell cytotoxicity rather than steroidogenesis — and the blank still does not close

This map's own language since 2026-07-09 is that the male side of fertility-tier coverage is "fragmented across competing unvalidated platforms." ReproTox adds one more, and it is a genuinely different read from the others already on the vault's books: a direct germ-cell cytotoxicity screen (hiPSC-derived male gamete progenitors, DAPI + single-cell transcriptomics, IC50s against known spermatotoxicants), not a hormone-synthesis mechanism screen. That makes it a different question from reproductive-testicular-steroidogenesis-leydig-models (Leydig-cell steroidogenesis) — composes with, does not duplicate it; both remain upstream, single-cell-type reads, the same category this map's founding systematic review already discounts from closing the endpoint (fecundity and multigenerational outcome, not cell-level toxicity, is what "fertility" means here).

The one new wrinkle: an ML shortcut, not a validation step. The project trained a classifier to call germ-cell identity from DAPI staining alone, replacing an antibody-based immunofluorescence step — a workflow efficiency gain, not a toxicological decision rule, and no performance metric for the classifier is reported. The project states plainly it has not yet begun OECD-guideline validation, naming that as future work contingent on funding not yet secured. This is a negative claim argued without any obvious incentive to understate progress (the report's own stated interest is a commercialization route), which is the same evidentiary shape this map has leaned on before (Kumbhar 2026, the PARC consortium's own "not yet" on spermatogonia/oogonia work).

Does not move Status or loop_readiness. The endpoint remains bench-bound / no-nam-exists: a validated upstream cytotoxicity screen, however automated its cell-counting step becomes, is not a fertility assay, and no apical reproductive outcome is measured here or anywhere else on this map's books.
