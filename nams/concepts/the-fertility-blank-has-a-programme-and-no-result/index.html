A named watch-item comes back, and the answer is "not yet"

Fertility and multigenerational reproductive toxicity is this vault's largest human-health blank: no validated non-animal method exists for the endpoint at all. The map recording that has, since it was coined, named exactly what would change it. One of the three items was the European PARC partnership's stem-cell-derived germ-cell work reaching more than proof-of-concept.

This paper is PARC describing that work, and it has not.

That is worth recording as a finding rather than as an absence of news, for a reason specific to how this vault grades. The map's claim — no replacement exists — has been resting on a 2026 systematic review and on the vault not having found a counter-example. Those are different kinds of confidence. A consortium publishing what it is building, with no results in it, converts "we have not found one" into "the group most likely to have one says it does not exist yet."

What is actually being built

Human induced-pluripotent-stem-cell-derived spermatogonia and oogonia — the precursor cells that become sperm and eggs. Primary human testis organoids. iPSC-derived Leydig-like cells, the cells that make testosterone. Mouse embryonic-stem-cell-derived germ cells. Transcriptomic and epigenetic profiling across all of it. A prostate cell assay covering oestrogen, androgen and retinoid pathways. Computational structure-activity models for two retinoid receptors, trained on around 10,000 substances from the US Tox21 screening collection.

That is a serious programme. It is also, on the evidence of this paper, entirely prospective: of the stem-cell-derived germ cells the consortium writes that "their responses to chemical exposure remain largely unexplored."

The related state statements, which matter for adjacent maps

  • Steroidogenesis. OECD Test Guideline 456 — the H295R assay — is "the only validated in vitro method" for steroidogenesis and its "adequacy in testing for effects on gonad steroidogenesis is frequently questioned" because H295R is a female adrenal carcinoma line, not gonadal tissue. This independently corroborates, from a fifty-author consortium, the applicability-domain limit the vault set on endocrine-steroidogenesis-h295r on 2026-08-07 from a single unrelated review.
  • Non-EATS pathways. OECD's endocrine framework covers oestrogen, androgen, thyroid and steroidogenesis. "OECD validated test methods for non-EATS mechanisms remain lacking." The vault's counter-example is endocrine-glucocorticoid-receptor-transactivation — OECD Test Guideline 454A, adopted 2 July 2026, the first adopted in-vitro method for a receptor outside that set.
  • Blood–brain barrier. Identified by OECD itself as a gap in the developmental-neurotoxicity in-vitro battery, and "endocrine disruption in the context of DNT remains a significant regulatory gap." Relevant to developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb.

Nothing moves, and that is the correct outcome

status: emerging holds. loop_readiness: bench-bound holds, with bench_bound_reason: no-nam-exists. A plan is not a method, and this vault does not promote on announced intent — that discipline is the whole reason the map is trustworthy about an absence.

Bias note. The paper is co-authored by two shareholders of DNTOX GmbH, a company that sells developmental-neurotoxicity battery services, on a document naming that battery's gaps and proposing the programme to fill them. The journal records the conflict; the vault records it too. It does not touch this note's claim, which is a statement of what has not been demonstrated.
