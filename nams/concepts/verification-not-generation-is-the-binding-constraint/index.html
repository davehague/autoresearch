The field says the bottleneck is checking, not producing

This vault's standing answer to its own question — why is there no closed loop on a regulatory toxicology assay? — has been: the loop has nothing to optimise against. The robots work, the models work, and what is missing is a defensible procedure that converts the instrument's number into a call the loop can be right or wrong about.

The autonomous-science community has now written down the same structure, about chemistry and materials, in a roadmap that mentions no toxicology at all:

"Producing a candidate discovery is no longer the hard part, but verifying it is, and this asymmetry now limits autonomous science more than raw model capability."

Two literatures, no citation between them, one shape. The vault treats independent derivation as the strongest form of support it recognises, and this is the second time it has happened on this map — the first being the correlated-blind-spot rail, argued from the assay side by this vault's evidence model and from the AI side by Hartung's "opaque AI pipelines risk magnifying the problem."

The three counter-currents the roadmap reports

The claim is not abstract. The document lists what went wrong in the year since its predecessor:

  • A corrected flagship discovery result. (Not named in the abstract; recorded as stated.)
  • Agents that rival experts on closed-ended questions complete only a fraction of open-ended research. Good at questions somebody already knows the answer to; much worse at the ones that matter.
  • Fabricated citations surfacing at leading venues. Machine-generated references to papers that do not exist, in refereed proceedings.

Every one of those is a verification failure, not a capability failure. The systems produced plausible output and the checking layer did not catch it.

Toxicology's version is strictly worse, and the vault should keep saying so

In materials, verifying a proposed structure means trying to make it. The answer is unambiguous and it arrives. This map already carries the field's own number for how often anyone bothers: DeepMind's GNoME proposed 380,000 stable crystal structures and fewer than 5% have been independently experimentally validated — in the domain where checking is easiest.

In regulatory toxicology the ground truth is human harm. It arrives late, it arrives confounded, it cannot be produced on demand, and for most chemicals it never arrives at all. Whatever verification scaffolding the autonomous-science community builds in year one, it will be built for a domain where the answer is obtainable — and the transfer to a field where it is not is exactly the step nobody has taken.

Honest ceiling. No data, no system, no measurement. A roadmap by 28 authors describing their own field while that field is being funded by a national programme the abstract names. Graded in-silico-mechanistic, strength: suggestive. The counterweight, recorded: the headline claim is that the field cannot check itself, which is argued against interest.
