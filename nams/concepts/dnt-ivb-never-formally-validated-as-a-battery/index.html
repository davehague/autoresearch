The DNT battery was never formally validated as a battery — and the OECD says so

On 2026-07-06 this vault graded developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb as evidence_tier: formally-validated, with the stated justification "multi-lab, multi-party standardised battery with a defined process-coverage domain." On 2026-07-07 an EFSA depth check held that grade.

It was wrong, and the correction comes from the body that would have had to run the validation.

"no formal validation procedure has yet been completed for the DNT-IVB as an integrated battery."

Why the vault got it wrong — the inference that looked safe

shared/evidence-model.md defines formally-validated as "ECVAM/ICCVAM ring trial, inter-lab reproducibility." The vault saw a battery standardised across EFSA, the US EPA, NIEHS and two European academic institutes, endorsed by the OECD's Working Party of National Coordinators, and read multi-party standardisation as satisfying inter-lab validation.

Those are different things, and the gap between them is the entire content of this note. Many labs agreeing on how to run a set of assays is a protocol-harmonisation achievement. A formal validation asks a different question — does the assembled battery, run blind, give the right answer? — and it has an apparatus: reference chemicals, coded samples, pre-set acceptance criteria, an independent validation management team. That apparatus was applied to the human thyroid microtissue assay in Deisenroth 2026 (endocrine-thyroid-disruption-nams, ingested 2026-07-18) and the vault correctly moved that map up for it. It has not been applied to the DNT battery.

What this does and does not say

It does not say the battery is bad science. It is used constantly and at scale — ~650 chemicals across three agencies, harmonised concentration–response modelling, centralised databases. Individual assays have met published test-readiness criteria. Nothing here is a failed validation.

It says the vault claimed a tier the evidence does not reach. formally-validated sits one rung below regulatory-adopted and is the tier the vault reserves for ring-trialled methods. The honest tier for a battery with no completed formal validation, substantial peer-reviewed method development and a defined coverage domain is peer-reviewed-method.

Why the source's own posture makes this stronger, not weaker

Read the byline. The first author is OECD Secretariat staff in the division that runs the Test Guideline programme. The second is at NICEATM, the US validation centre. The third is at EFSA, one of the agencies running the case studies. The paper calls the battery "a flagship OECD project."

These are the three institutions with the strongest reason to present the battery as further along than it is, and they volunteered the gap in their own limitations section. funding_flag: none on the commercial axis; the institutional-self-description flag is recorded in the source digest. When a body describing its own flagship project publishes the thing that most undercuts it, that claim is about as load-bearing as this vault's evidence gets — which is why strength: strong on a review with no primary data.

Consequence for the map

evidence_tier formally-validated → peer-reviewed-method. Status consolidating → contested: per shared/evidence-model.md, consolidating requires "no live rebuttal," and a rebuttal of the map's central evidence claim, from the guideline body, is exactly that. regulatory_status holds at under-validation — document 377 and the Initial Recommendations are real regulatory standing and nothing here touches them. loop_readiness holds at automatable-open-loop; see dnt-defined-approach-is-an-aspiration-not-a-deliverable.

Status moves both ways, and tonight it moved down on the vault's own highest-leverage map. The gap between the 2026-07-06 grading and this correction is the clearest instance so far of the failure mode shared/evidence-model.md exists to prevent: inferring one axis from adjacent evidence rather than reading what the source actually certifies.
