Nine machine-runnable components, assembled by a human

The vault now holds two eye/skin-class IATAs and they land in different loop tiers. That is the loop_readiness axis doing exactly what it was built for, so it is worth being precise about why.

The phototoxicity IATA (GD 397) is closed-loop-ready. It is a sequential cascade: TG 101 (spectrum) → TG 495 (ROS) → TG 432 (3T3 NRU) → TG 498 (RhE). Each rung has a gate. A negative at a rung terminates; a positive routes to the next rung. There is nothing to decide — the flowchart is the decision procedure, and a controller can execute it.

The eye IATA (GD 263) is not. Its architecture is:

  1. Part 1 — gather everything: human data, rabbit data, in-vitro data from nine adopted TGs, non-OECD data, skin-corrosion data, physico-chemical properties (pH, acid/alkaline reserve), and non-testing predictions ((Q)SAR, read-across, mixture bridging).
  2. Part 2 — "a weight of evidence (WoE) approach." Module 9's subsections are: place/role of WoE in the IATA, coverage of relevant sources of information, assessment of data quality, adequacy of information, non-testing data, existing human data, evaluation of consistency of the data, assessment of the coverage of relevant parameters and observations, conclusions of WoE. Those are headings for a thought process, not operations on numbers. Annex I supplies an "example of matrix"; Annex II supplies "examples of WoE evaluations." Examples, not an algorithm.
  3. Part 3 — new testing, entered "if the WoE analysis is inconclusive."

And the branch selection is itself a judgement. Which ladder you climb — top-down (start with a Cat 1 method) or bottom-up (start with a No-Cat method) — depends on a hypothesis the assessor forms first: "All available information and the WoE assessment should be used to formulate a hypothesis of the most likely eye hazard potential of the test chemical … This hypothesis and the regulatory context under which a decision must be taken should then guide the choice of the prospective testing approach." The bottom-up route "should be followed only when all available collected information and the WoE assessment result in a high a priori probability that the test chemical does not require classification." A prior, set by a person, selects the experiment. There is no rule for computing it.

So: loop_readiness: human-in-the-loop, `loop_blocker: the integration step is an unspecified weight-of-evidence judgement, and the choice of testing route depends on an assessor-formed prior.

This is not the usual human-in-the-loop shape and the distinction matters for anyone allocating effort. The vault's four other human-in-the-loop maps are blocked at the scoring step — a trained eye counting micronuclei or grading foci down a microscope — and for those, machine vision is a demonstrated escape hatch. The eye IATA is blocked after every scoring step. All nine of its components produce clean instrument numbers; several are closed-loop-ready on their own. No image classifier reaches this blocker. What would move it is a defined approach — a published data-interpretation procedure over the module outputs — which is exactly what TG 467 is for a fixed pair of assays, and exactly what the DNT battery has been waiting for with no date (developmental-neurotoxicity-dnt-ivb). The vault made this same distinction on 2026-07-20 and it holds here: same-looking blocker, entirely different investment.

The one thing that partially escapes. GD 263 routes suspected Category 2 chemicals to TG 467 or TG 492B, both of which do have fixed rules returning all three categories. So the IATA contains a fully-specified sub-path — and that sub-path is closed-loop-ready. A loop could run the machine-runnable middle of this guidance and would still need a human for the parts that decide whether to enter it, and for the parts where the answer comes out inconclusive.

Honest note on what is not knowable from the document. GD 263 reports no performance figure for the IATA as a whole. Every number in the guidance belongs to a component. There is no measurement of how well the assembled strategy classifies chemicals, and therefore no baseline against which an automated version could be compared. Recorded as a gap, not filled.
