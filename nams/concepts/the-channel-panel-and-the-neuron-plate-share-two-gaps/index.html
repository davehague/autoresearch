Two legs, two shared gaps — the seizure battery casts one vote

The rail, applied on arrival

shared/evidence-model.md states the rule: when two assays in one battery fail on the same chemicals for the same structural reason, that battery casts one vote, not two. The vault applies it to the fish embryo test and the RTgill-W1 fish-cell assay (neither has a nervous system or full metabolism), to the reactive-oxygen photoreactivity assay and the 3T3 neutral red uptake test (neither biotransforms), and to the two skin-genotoxicity assays in two-skin-genotox-assays-that-fail-on-the-same-chemistry.

The seizure battery in neurotoxicity-seizure-liability-in-vitro-battery has two legs, and they share two gaps.

Gap one — neither leg metabolises

The channel panel is engineered cell lines expressing one human ion channel each. The second leg is human iPSC-derived neurons. Neither has a liver. Neither carries the cytochrome P450 enzyme system that turns a drug into its metabolites in a person, which is exactly the competence this vault tracks as a standalone method class (metabolism-cyp-induction-heparg, metabolism-biotransformation-metabolite-generation).

So the failure mode is precise: a compound that is clean at all four channels, and quiet on the neuron plate, and whose liver metabolite is a potent channel blocker, passes both legs and the battery agrees with itself. The vault has already recorded this exact chemistry happening in a different organ — the-parent-compound-tested-clean-and-the-metabolites-did-not. The reason a metabolism gap is dangerous is not that it exists; it is that when every leg shares it, the battery's internal agreement carries no information about it.

Gap two — neither leg has a blood-brain barrier

The blood-brain barrier is the tight lining of the brain's blood vessels that keeps most of the bloodstream out of brain tissue. It is the reason many chemicals that are violently active on isolated neurons are harmless to a person's brain: they never arrive.

Neither leg has one. Both apply the compound directly to the cells. So a compound excluded from the brain in life is scored as a seizure hazard by the channel panel and by the neuron plate, and the two agree — for a reason that has nothing to do with the brain.

This one has a number attached, from elsewhere in the vault. model-disagreement-as-a-machine-checkable-refusal-rule records a consensus permeability model applied to 6,031 chemicals a high-throughput screen had already called active against a brain receptor: it predicted 27% would never reach that receptor in a person. If roughly a quarter of "brain-active" screening hits are unreachable, a two-leg battery with no permeability filter in either leg is producing a false-alarm rate of that order — concordantly.

Why this is worse for a loop than for a person

A toxicologist reading two agreeing in-vitro results knows, from training, to ask "would this ever get there, and does the body make something worse out of it?" An automated system reading the same two results sees agreement and treats it as confirmation. It then selects its next experiments in the neighbourhood the agreement pointed at.

The two gaps push in opposite directions, which makes the compounding harder to notice:

  • The metabolism gap produces confident negatives — bioactivation-dependent seizure risk called safe, twice.
  • The permeability gap produces confident positives — brain-excluded chemistry called hazardous, twice.

A loop tuned on this battery therefore drifts toward chemistry that is inert in a dish and away from chemistry that is merely impermeable, and its own concordance statistics improve while it does so. Speed converts a known bias into a compounding one — the standing statement of the rail.

What would break the correlation

Neither fix is exotic, and neither is present here:

  1. A metabolic-activation arm — an S9 liver fraction, a hepatocyte co-culture, or explicit synthesis and testing of the major metabolites. This is the same fix logged as effective for the micronucleus assay in heparg-metabolism-fixes-the-micronucleus-false-positives, and it is the modular upstream step described in metabolic-competence-as-a-modular-upstream-step — with the caution from the-metabolic-fix-was-tested-without-the-metabolism-case that adding the arm is not the same as demonstrating it catches the cases it was added for.
  2. A permeability gate in front of the battery — a structure-based blood-brain-barrier filter run before the plate, so a compound the model says never arrives is either excluded or flagged. That is a computational-connective fix, it is cheap, and it is the piece in-silico-qsar-read-across already holds.

Recorded as an open question, not a refutation. The paper does not claim independence between its two legs and does not report either gap; the claim that the legs are correlated is this vault's, made from their construction. correlated_blind_spot is set on the map with a description rather than a sibling map slug — the same schema departure already logged for the ciPTEC kidney battery (a-battery-inside-one-cell-line-is-not-five-independent-votes), because the correlated pair is two legs inside one method rather than two maps.
