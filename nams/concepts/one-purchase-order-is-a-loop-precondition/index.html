The two things a loop needs that nobody puts on the loop's list

Read the autonomous-lab literature this vault holds and the bottlenecks are always the same three: can the robot execute the step, can the agent choose the next experiment, can anything interpret the result. See automation-self-driving-labs.

Here is a real multi-country testing programme, and the first two things it spent money on were buying all the chemicals from one place and agreeing how to dissolve them.

Why that is not trivial

Every in-vitro toxicity result is a statement about a concentration in a well. Getting a chemical to that concentration means dissolving it, usually in DMSO, usually near the edge of what it will actually dissolve at. Get it wrong and the assay reports a clean negative for a chemical that was never in solution at the dose on the label.

The vault already records this as a domain_gate in the strong form. The robotic screening platform's domain gate is exactly this: "Poorly-soluble, volatile, and unstable compounds are outside the domain the platform can honestly assay — a loop must check solubility before trusting any negative" (automation-tox21-qhts-platform). And Cell Painting's entire list of named variability sources — cell density, DMSO percentage, dye stability, microscope model, analysis software — is a property of the laboratory, not the chemical (phenotypic-profiling-cell-painting).

PARC's answer is to remove both variables by fiat:

"All compounds were centrally ordered and distributed to ensure batch consistency and facilitate inter-laboratory comparability."

"A harmonized SOP was developed and distributed across laboratories to standardize stock preparation, solubility testing, and dilution procedures…"

Plus radiolabelled standards for most substances, purchased specifically so solubility and stability could be verified rather than assumed.

The second thing: bioactivation is an endpoint, not an afterthought

The vault's sharpest standing finding is the correlated blind spot: two assays in one battery that fail on the same chemicals for the same structural reason cast one vote, not two. The shared cause in four of the rail's seven rows is the same — the cells cannot metabolise. A chemical that is harmless as drawn and harmful once the liver has worked on it walks through a metabolically incompetent battery untouched, and an automated loop trusting the battery's agreement compounds the error rather than catching it.

PARC does not treat metabolism as a caveat. It is one of the five endpoints, with its own workstream:

  • Phase I (the oxidation step, mostly cytochrome P450 enzymes) in HepaRG and HepG2 human liver cells, in liver, kidney and intestinal sub-cellular fractions, and in CYP-specific supersomes.
  • Phase II (the conjugation step — glucuronidation and sulfation, which tag a chemical for excretion) using recombinant enzymes.
  • Kinetic parameters (Vmax, Km, clearance) extracted and fed into PARC's physiologically-based kinetic modelling, which is what converts a dish concentration into a body dose (pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics).

And the project's integration logic names "bioactivation potential" as a mechanistic alert alongside endocrine activity, neurodevelopmental perturbation, immune modulation and genotoxic signals — not as a qualifier on them.

What this adds to the vault

The vault has recorded the metabolic blind spot repeatedly as a defect. This is the first source showing a real regulatory-facing programme designing around it from the start, and the design is the obvious one: if none of your hazard assays can metabolise, run a separate metabolism arm and read the two together.

That is an architectural answer, and it costs a workstream. It is also, notably, an answer that a fully closed loop would have to encode as "never trust a negative from these assays until the metabolism arm has reported" — a cross-assay precondition, not a per-assay threshold. Nothing in the vault's loop_blocker vocabulary can currently express that shape.

Limits

  • Design only, no results. Nothing here says the metabolism arm will work, or that the harmonised SOP will produce comparable exposures. Results due Q1 2027.
  • Nothing moves on any map's loop_readiness or Status.
  • strength: single-study — one programme, one paper, no independent account of it.
