The context of use is much smaller than the headline

Liver-on-a-chip is sold, in the general press and in vendor material, as the technology that replaces the animal liver study — a human-relevant system that tells you in advance whether a molecule will damage a liver.

The first serious regulatory evaluation of eight of them is not asking that question. Its context of use is: a human trial has already produced a raised liver-enzyme reading, and the chip is one input into deciding whether to keep dosing. Retrospective. Diagnostic. Advisory. One line of a weight-of-evidence argument about a signal that already exists.

Why the narrowness is the point, not a disappointment

A context of use is a contract, and narrow contracts are what get qualified. ICCVAM's framework — the US machinery that owns the evidence axis — states that "the COU determines the detailed criteria and implementation of the key concepts." There is no fixed bar; the bar is set by what you claim. FDA's ISTAND route works the same way: you qualify a tool for a stated use, not in general.

So the eight vendors and CDER have chosen the claim they think eight blinded compounds can actually support. That is good regulatory practice and it is also a measurement of how much confidence the field believes it can currently buy: enough to help interpret a signal, not enough to predict one.

What it means for anyone reading the eventual result

When this study reports, the number will be about retrospective interpretation. It will not license the sentence "liver chips predict drug-induced liver injury with X% accuracy," and it will be reported as though it does. The vault's job is to hold the distinction: a validated context of use is validated for that use, and carrying a number outside its context is the commonest way non-animal methods get oversold.

The loop reading

An automated system cannot infer a context of use; it has to be told one, and then constrained to it. This is the same object as a domain_gate — a carve-out the loop must check before trusting the decision rule — except that it constrains the question rather than the chemical. The vault has no field for it, and the loop consequence is identical: a system that runs the assay outside its context of use produces a confident, well-formed, inapplicable answer.
