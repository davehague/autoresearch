The phototoxicity IATA (OECD GD 397) → how the phototoxicity methods compose

The composition layer for a column whose individual assays are all adopted but none of which is, alone, a photosafety assessment. GD 397 was proposed by Japan to the OECD Working Party on Hazard Assessment and maps the methods onto the Adverse Outcome Pathway for photo-irritation: absorption of light energy by a photoreactive chemical → photochemical reaction → cell damage → skin response.

The tiers, cheapest gate first:

TierMethodReads
1TG 101 — UV-VIS absorption spectraMEC < 1,000 L mol⁻¹ cm⁻¹ → very unlikely to be photoreactive
2TG 495 — ROS assay (in chemico)photo-generated reactive oxygen species; ICH S10 names it an optional initial screen for pharmaceuticals
3TG 432 — 3T3 NRUlight/dark cytotoxicity ratio, no barrier → sensitive screen (3t3-nru-phototoxicity-screen)
4TG 498 — RhE PTtopical confirmation in human epidermis, with a barrier (rhe-phototoxicity-topical-predictivity)

Two axes, and they do not agree — which is the point of tracking them separately. evidence_tier: formally-validated (every method it composes is an adopted, validated TG, and the AOP grounding is sound), but regulatory_status: proposed — GD 397 is a Guidance Document, applied case-by-case, not a Test Guideline. This is the same shape as OECD GD 331 (pbk-qivive-toxicokinetics): WNT-approved guidance composing adopted parts, without itself being a binding test method. The vault's third guidance-level hub.

The gate is a filter, not a verdict. The guidance is explicit that a low MEC does not close the question — photolabile chemicals can still be captured downstream — so TG 101 narrows the workload rather than issuing an exoneration.

What makes this IATA structurally different from every other battery in the vault. The skin-sensitisation 2-out-of-3 DA, the eye DA, the DNT battery and the repeat-dose framework are all built to displace an existing animal test — the LLNA, the Draize, the in-vivo DNT study, the 90-day rodent study. This one is not, because for phototoxicity there is no validated in-vivo test to displace (photoallergy-photogenotoxicity-no-validated-method). The IATA is therefore not a replacement strategy; it is the primary regulatory strategy for the endpoint, benchmarked against human clinical data. It is the vault's first look at what an integrated testing approach designs like when regulators build one NAM-native from the ground up rather than retro-fitting one around an animal incumbent — and the answer is: a cheap physico-chemical gate, an in-chemico reactivity screen, a sensitive cell screen, and a human-tissue confirmation, in that order.
