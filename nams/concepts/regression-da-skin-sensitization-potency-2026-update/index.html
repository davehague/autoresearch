The developer of an already-adopted tool publishes an update, and the numbers behind the update are unread

This note exists to mark a dated pointer, not a performance claim. The regression-based Defined Approach is already closed-loop-ready and consensus on skin-sensitization-quantitative-pod-da under OECD Guideline 497 Part III. This 2026 paper, by the same author who originated the tool (Natsch & Gerberick 2022), is explicitly aimed at TG 497's 2026 implementation cycle and reports a larger evaluation set (203 chemicals) than the vault's existing figure for OECD EG DASS's independent 196-chemical evaluation — but whether the 203 is a superset, a refresh, or a different cut of the reference data, and whether the accuracy band or the under-prediction tail moved, could not be established: ScienceDirect returned HTTP 403 and only a truncated abstract was recoverable via NCBI eutils / Europe PMC.

stance: open-question, deliberately not confirms or extends. Confirming would require the percentages; extending applicability is plausible (a larger reference set generally would) but unverified. The honest position is that this is evidence the tool is under active iteration by its developer ahead of a live regulatory deadline, and nothing more, until a future ingest obtains the full text.

Bias watch. Sole-author, sole-employer (funding_flag: [industry, method-developer]) — the same developer-origin discount the vault already applies to this tool's 2022 baseline. The competing-interests statement recovered ("no known competing financial interests") is the author's self-report; it does not change the structural fact that he is reporting on his own employer's method.
