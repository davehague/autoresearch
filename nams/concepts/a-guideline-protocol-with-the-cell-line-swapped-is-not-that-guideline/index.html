"Adapted from Test Guideline 432" inherits the protocol, not the evidence

What was adapted. The source builds its assay on OECD Test Guideline 432, the adopted 3T3 Neutral Red Uptake phototoxicity test — chemical applied to two plates of cells, one irradiated with ultraviolet-A and one kept dark, the answer being how much more the cells die in the light. It keeps the two-arm structure and changes two things:

TG 432 as adoptedThis study
CellsMouse Balb/c 3T3 fibroblastsHuman HaCaT keratinocytes
UVA dose5 J/cm²4 J/cm²
ReadoutNeutral Red Uptake (viability)IL-18 release (immune signalling)

Why this is worth a note rather than a footnote. The guideline is consensus and closed-loop-ready in this vault (phototoxicity-3t3-nru) because it is regulatorily adopted, and its prediction model — the PIF and MPE thresholds that turn the two curves into a verdict — was calibrated in those mouse cells, at that light dose. Change the cell and the dose and you have changed the biology the thresholds were fitted to. The bridging evidence offered is that TG 432's positive control (chlorpromazine) and a negative (sodium lauryl sulfate) behaved as expected. That is two chemicals, and it establishes the system is not broken, not that it is equivalent.

To be fair to the study: it is not claiming to be TG 432. It reads a different endpoint — an immune signal rather than cell death — so equivalence to the guideline's prediction model was never the goal. The guideline's role here is as a standardised irradiation and exposure protocol, which is a legitimate and common way to use one.

The schema strain this exposes

The vault's three axes have no field for "built on an adopted guideline's protocol, carrying none of its validation." evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory is correct and says nothing about the lineage. regulatory_status: none is correct and says nothing about the lineage. A reader scanning either axis sees a proof-of-concept assay with no relationship to an adopted method — and a reader scanning the paper sees "adapted from OECD Test Guideline 432," which reads, wrongly, like borrowed standing.

This is a recurring shape, not a one-off. phototoxicity-rhe-pt records the inverse case done properly: TG 498 was built by combining TG 439's tissue-and-MTT readout with TG 432's standardised irradiation, and it went through adoption in its own right, with its own performance standards and its own "me-too" validation route for a second tissue. The difference between TG 498 and this assay is not the engineering — both are recombinations of adopted parts — it is that one was validated as the new thing it became.

What it means for a loop

An automated system reading method metadata would see the string "OECD TG 432" attached to a protocol whose cell line, species and light dose are all different from the guideline's. Protocol provenance and validation provenance are two different facts and only one of them travels with the protocol text. A loop that treats "conforms to TG 432's procedure" as a proxy for "carries TG 432's evidence" will over-trust every adapted method it encounters — and adapting a guideline's procedure is the single most common way new assays get built.
