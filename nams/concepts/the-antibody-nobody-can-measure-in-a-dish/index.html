The antibody nobody can measure in a dish

What the regulation is actually built on

Immunotoxicity's reference test is the T-dependent antibody response (TDAR). Immunise an animal with something novel; measure how much antibody it produces. It is T-dependent because making that antibody requires two cell types to cooperate — a T cell recognises the threat and licenses a B cell to manufacture antibody. The readout is a product: a molecule that did not exist before and that the organism built.

In people, the same signal appears as vaccine antibody titres, and this source's opening cites exactly that: epidemiological studies reporting "decreased vaccine antibody titres" after PFAS exposure. That human-side agreement is why immunotoxicity became, in the authors' words, "one of the most sensitive endpoints for PFAS exposure" and why it "has been used to inform regulatory guidance and health-based values."

So the endpoint that sets PFAS exposure limits is a functional output of a whole immune system.

What the dish can offer instead

The PBMC model measures three things: how much T cells divide, how much cytokine gets secreted, and which genes change. All three are states, not products. None of them is an antibody titre.

This is not an oversight — it is close to a structural limit of the format. Manufacturing a T-dependent antibody response needs the architecture of a lymph node: naive B cells meeting antigen-presenting cells and helper T cells in a germinal centre, over days, with cells migrating between compartments. A tube of peripheral blood mononuclear cells has the cast but not the stage. It is the immune system's cells without the immune system's geography.

The move the field makes, and its exact cost

Toxicology's answer is the adverse outcome pathway: if you cannot measure the harm, measure a key event on the causal chain leading to it. That is precisely how this paper positions itself — the model "is a cost-effective and ethical method for identifying and characterizing key events (KEs) in the adverse outcome pathway (AOP) for PFAS-induced immunotoxicity."

The cost of that move is a link that must be established separately: how strongly does the key event predict the adverse outcome? For this method that link is currently a hypothesis — an attractive one, that PFAS disrupt "coordination between T- and B-cell responses", which would explain a falling antibody titre without either cell type failing on its own. It is a good hypothesis. It is not a concordance statistic.

Nothing read here reports how well the in-dish readouts rank the same compounds as a TDAR does. For a method whose eventual purpose is to triage thousands of chemicals, that number is the method's whole value proposition, and it does not yet exist.

Why the vault records this as a coverage statement, not a criticism

The honest summary is not "the assay is inadequate." It is: the immune endpoint that regulation uses has no non-animal equivalent, and this work is an attempt to build the first leg of one. The vault should file that next to its other coverage blanks rather than as a deficiency of this paper — it is the same shape as the phototoxicity endpoint having no validated in-vivo reference at all, and the reproductive/fertility endpoint having no non-animal method whatsoever.

And there is a real prize behind it. The motivating arithmetic is "more than 14,000 PFAS" against an animal test that cannot be run 14,000 times. Even a key-event assay with an unquantified link to the adverse outcome is more useful than testing nothing, provided the ranking it produces is honest about being a ranking and not a hazard call. That distinction is exactly the one this vault exists to keep.

Evidence weight

Single study, one government laboratory, six compounds, no prediction model, no reference set, no independent replication. Full text subscription-only and unread; the compounds are not named and no effect size is reported. evidence_tier: single-lab-exploratory, strength: single-study. The claim in this note is structural — it follows from what each method measures — and does not depend on the unread numbers.
