A sixth voice on "the procedure," and the first from a regulated manufacturer rather than a validation body or academic

What this adds

validation-paradigm-nam-confidence's "the procedure" sub-thread already holds ICCVAM's five-step framework (and its automation exemption), EURL ECVAM's account of what a validation set actually is, and Zhu & Locke's mis-ordering argument. This source's distinguishing feature is who is arguing, not what: a cosmetics manufacturer for whom NAM validation is existential (the EU's cosmetics animal-testing ban leaves no fallback to animal data), rather than a validation body, an academic ring-trial designer, or a policy commentator. Its diagnosis — no single cross-industry standard exists — is consistent with everything else on this map's procedure strand, but it proposes no new mechanism: no rubric, no data-sharing protocol, no named standard-setting body, unlike ICCVAM's already-concrete five-step framework already on this map. Read next to that existing machinery, the "call to action" sits behind the field's own state of the art in specificity, not ahead of it.
