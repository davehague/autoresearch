The tier follows the readout, and here is the cleanest possible demonstration

The vault's rule, restated

shared/evidence-model.md says it flatly: "Readout objectivity drives the tier, never assay format." Its existing illustration is the fish embryo acute toxicity test (OECD TG 236) versus zebrafish teratogenicity — "the same organism at the same life stage" landing in different tiers because one reads near-binary survival endpoints and the other reads a spectrum of malformation types.

Tonight's source makes the demonstration tighter still, because the two assays are not merely the same organism — they are frequently the same plate, imaged and filmed in the same run by the same laboratories.

Why the difference is real and not an artefact of who happened to build what

It would be easy to assume the behavioural assay is automated because someone bothered to automate it, and the morphology assay is not because nobody has. That is half right and misses the structural half.

Behaviour reduces to one continuous number per larva per time bin. Distance moved. There is no taxonomy — nothing to name, nothing to categorise, nothing to decide the boundary of. A camera and a tracking algorithm produce it, and two laboratories' cameras produce comparable numbers.

Malformation is a taxonomy. "Pericardial edema, moderate" is a type and a grade, drawn from a list a person learned. Automating it means teaching a classifier the list — which is exactly what zebrafish-machine-vision-malformation-scoring records being attempted (a Vision Transformer at 92% on a research benchmark; single-lab, slower than a human, binary rather than graded).

One is a measurement. The other is a judgement rendered by a trained eye. That is the whole axis, and this pair is the cleanest instance of it the vault holds — cleaner than the FET comparison, because the two assays here are not merely the same species but routinely the same experiment.

The practical consequence, which is a warning

Anyone building a loop will reason about test systems — "we have zebrafish larvae in plates, therefore zebrafish work is automatable." That reasoning is wrong at exactly the wrong granularity. The same larvae in the same plate are automatable for one question and not for another, and which is which is a property of the question, not of the fish, the plate, or the robot.

The generalisation to check on every future map: the unit that carries loop_readiness is the readout, not the test system. Where one test system supports several readouts, the tier belongs to each readout separately, and a map that covers a test system rather than a readout will average two honest answers into one misleading one.

Not an endorsement

automatable-open-loop on the behavioural assay says a machine can make the measurement. It does not say the measurement is a good proxy for human developmental neurotoxicity, and this source does not claim it is — the assay is positioned as a first tier that prioritises compounds for the mechanistic in-vitro battery and for mammalian studies, not as a replacement for either. A changed swimming pattern says something is wrong with the animal. It does not say what, or where.
