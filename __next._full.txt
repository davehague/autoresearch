1e:Tc25,2026-07-22 — [[maps/genotoxicity-tgx-ddi-transcriptomic-biomarker]] → **consolidating** (auto-coined) · **the genotoxicity column's first `closed-loop-ready` map, and it qualifies the tier on arrival** (Wang & Crute et al. 2025, *Toxicological Sciences* 208(2):233–243, PMID 41031489, DOI 10.1093/toxsci/kfaf138). A **64-gene expression signature** in human TK6 cells, read by digital transcript counting, classifying chemicals as DNA damage-inducing or not. **Four independent laboratories, 14 blinded compounds, 47 valid tests: 100% sensitivity (95% CI 82–100%), 86% specificity (95% CI 67–96%), 91% accuracy, inter-lab Kappa 0.61–1.0** → `evidence_tier: formally-validated`, a bar few sources here clear. **The specificity is the contribution, not the sensitivity:** it exists to fix the in-vitro micronucleus assay's **30–65% specificity** — an adopted, required test that wrongly flags a third to two-thirds of harmless chemicals, each false alarm historically buying a follow-up animal study. **(1) Loop:** `closed-loop-ready` — machine-readable readout **and** a published rule (three classifiers, positive if any fires). **⚠️ But the rule is *fixed* without being *sample-independent*:** two of the three legs (PCA, hierarchical clustering) are **unsupervised and batch-relative**, so a compound's call can depend on what else was analysed with it.
