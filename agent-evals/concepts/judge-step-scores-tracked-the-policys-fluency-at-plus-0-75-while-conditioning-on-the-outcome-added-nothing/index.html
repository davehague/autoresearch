Judge step scores tracked the policy's fluency at +0.75 while conditioning on the outcome added nothing

Grounded in credit-without-ground-truth-executed-replay-audit; belongs to evals-that-localize-the-fix.

The audit does not stop at "no signal" — it names the mechanism. "Implicit credit echoes the policy's fluency (median rank correlation +0.75, replicating at +0.70 in a second family under a corrected instrument), while conditioning on the outcome adds no causal information (partial correlation −0.004, Qwen)."

Two findings, and the second is sharper than the first.

Fluency. A model asked whether a step was good produces something that correlates 0.75 with how confident and well-formed the step looked. That is what is visible in a transcript. It is not what the step did. This is now the third instance of the same shape in this vault: showing a judge the final state instead of the transcript moves the reported rate about ten points and flips the label on up to a fifth of cases; giving a judge the run directory and code instead of the transcript is worth 17.2 points of recall and takes κ from 0.53 to 0.75. Transcripts carry style; environments carry consequence. Stated at the step level, the rule is: an attributor that reads only the trajectory is reading fluency, and its output will rank the articulate steps first.

Outcome conditioning. The intuitive repair is to tell the instrument how the run ended — you know it failed, so weight steps by their relation to the failure. Partial correlation −0.004. Knowing the ending contributed nothing about which step caused it. Any failure-attribution pipeline built on "we know this run failed, ask the model which step did it" is built on exactly this.

Why it matters beyond credit assignment. This vault's localization instruments are almost all transcript-reading judges with the outcome supplied — that is the standard construction for machine error attribution, and it is the construction with a measured null and a named confound. The fix direction is already indicated elsewhere in the vault and is the same one: give the instrument the artifact and the environment rather than the story of what happened.

Bounds. One environment, two 7–8B policies, small samples, and the fluency correlation is reported as a median rank correlation across trajectories rather than with an interval. The −0.004 partial correlation is reported for one family. "Fluency" is operationalized by the paper's own measure and a different operationalization might weaken or strengthen the association. The replication at +0.70 is in a second family under an instrument the authors had already had to correct once.
