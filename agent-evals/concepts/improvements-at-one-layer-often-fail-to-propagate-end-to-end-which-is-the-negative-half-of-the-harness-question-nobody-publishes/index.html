Improvements at one layer often fail to propagate end to end which is the negative half of the harness question nobody publishes

Grounded in engineering-reliable-coding-agents-system-around-the-model; belongs to evals-that-localize-the-fix.

"Across this evidence, many apparent model failures originate elsewhere in the system, while improvements at one layer often fail to propagate to end-to-end outcomes." Synthesised from 164 scholarly works, 100 practitioner records, 29 benchmark records and 17 author-system case records.

Every harness result in this theme is a delta that propagated, because a layer change that moved nothing does not get written up. The table reads 13.7 points on Terminal-Bench, 15.36 on tabular analytics, 7.5 to 28.9 from a loop harness, 12.4 from a learned harness, ~11 across nine harnesses. The literature is selected for propagation. This is the first source here to state the non-propagating case as common, and it is stated over a corpus that includes practitioner records — which is where the failures that never became papers would live.

It makes the defect-mass mechanism general rather than a fact about self-evolution. harness-gains-track-recoverable-execution-defect-mass-so-where-there-is-no-defect-there-is-no-gain came from one team holding one recipe fixed across eight languages, and a-harness-gain-reported-beside-a-92-9-percent-drop-in-the-defect-it-compensates-is-the-first-independent-corroboration-of-the-defect-mass-mechanism corroborated it once. Both say a layer improvement shows up end-to-end only where that layer was binding. A literature-scale statement of non-propagation is the same claim observed at the population level, which makes three arrivals and one instruction: identify which layer is binding before you improve one, and the cheap local version of that is counting recoverable execution defects in your own traces.

It also reframes the model-or-harness argument's failure mode. This theme has treated the problem as attribution — the effect exists, whose is it. Non-propagation is a different problem: an effect that exists at one layer and disappears by the time you measure the outcome, which is what final-answer-correctness-spans-eleven-points-across-nine-harnesses-while-planning-tool-use-and-efficiency-vary-much-more-so-the-headline-axis-is-where-the-harness-effect-hides measured on a single axis and this generalises across layers. The practical read is unchanged in direction and sharper in emphasis: a tie on the outcome axis is not evidence a layer does not matter, and the trajectories that would say otherwise are already in the traces.

What it does not do is the thing the theme is blocked on. No apportionment. Six teams have built grids capable of separating model from harness variance and none has reported the numbers; a review of 164 works plus 29 benchmark records does not produce the decomposition either. That is consistent with this theme's 2026-08-08 conclusion that the apportionment is an analysis nobody's reviewers ask for, and it retires the hope that a synthesis would find one hiding in the literature.

Bounds. Single-author monograph, "structured rather than exhaustive" by its own description, with 17 of the case records being the author's own systems. The central claim is qualitative — "many," "often," no effect sizes, no counts of how many of the 164 works reported non-propagation. Coding agents specifically. Treat the direction as corroboration of three prior sources and the magnitude as absent.
