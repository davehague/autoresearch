An agent's recorded plan recovers only part of the true prerequisite structure, so grading against the plan grades the narration rather than the work

Grounded in loopsbench-loop-engineering-benchmark; belongs to evals-that-localize-the-fix.

The benchmark holds a prerequisite DAG recovered from a project's real build history, and separately captures what the coding agent wrote down as its own plan. The recorded plans recovered only part of the source-recovered structure.

Why this extends. It closes a shortcut that the localizing thread otherwise walks straight into. Everything this vault has collected argues for scoring the trajectory rather than the end state, and the agent's own plan is the most convenient trajectory artifact available — structured, already emitted, free to parse. This says the plan is a partial and self-reported account of what the work depended on, so a grader built on it scores the agent's description of its reasoning rather than the reasoning. The ground truth has to come from outside the run.

The generalization past coding. The same trap is waiting anywhere an agent emits a plan, a rationale, or a set of subgoals and the eval treats those as structure. Plan-adherence metrics, subgoal-completion rates, and self-reported step labels all inherit this: they measure consistency with a self-authored artifact, which an agent can be consistent with while being wrong about. It is the trajectory-side version of outcome-based-grading-requires-transcript-auditing-as-its-counterweight-or-it-certifies-shortcuts-as-successes — there, outcome grading certifies shortcuts; here, plan grading certifies narration.

What it costs to do right. Independently recovering prerequisite structure is the expensive half of a-dependency-dag-of-separately-testable-units-with-completed-nodes-held-as-regression-obligations-scores-a-long-run-continuously, and this claim is the reason the expense is not optional. It also lands on the pattern the vault keeps hitting from cumulative-retrieval-recall-tracks-answer-quality-better-than-search-effort-so-effort-metrics-mislead-as-progress-signals: the informative signal is the one that needs work to obtain, and the free one is already on the dashboard.

Why single-study. One benchmark, one team. "Recovers only part" is reported qualitatively rather than as a coverage fraction, so the size of the gap is unknown — it could be a modest shortfall or a near-total mismatch, and those imply different levels of caution.

What would move this. A reported overlap statistic between agent-authored plans and independently recovered dependency structure. Or evidence from a domain where plans track structure well, which would make this a property of long-horizon software work rather than of agent self-reports generally.
