Routing every harness edit through a typed failure signal and a falsifiable contract keeps a self modifying harness attributable

Grounded in one-recipe-many-harnesses-what-self-evolution-encodes; belongs to evals-that-localize-the-fix.

The evolution recipe is instrumented rather than opaque: "The recipe routes every edit through a typed failure signal and records it as a falsifiable contract, making each modification attributable after evolution."

It answers, from an unexpected direction, the open question opened on 2026-08-14. a-harness-that-rewrites-itself-from-rollout-feedback-removes-harness-held-fixed-as-an-available-control recorded that a self-improving harness destroys the assumption every comparison in this vault rests on — that the harness is fixed for the duration of the measurement — and offered no repair beyond "report a learning curve." This is a better repair, published two days earlier by a different group: stop trying to hold it fixed and make each change accountable instead. A typed failure signal says which failure class triggered the edit; a falsifiable contract says what the edit claims to fix. Together they mean a reader can ask of any evolved harness which of its modifications earned their place, which is strictly more than a curve can tell you.

It is the same move this vault has already endorsed one level up. assigning-each-failure-mode-to-a-component-edge-and-a-fault-side-turns-an-eval-score-into-a-repair-owner turns a score into a repair address. This turns a repair into a checkable claim. The pair closes a loop the theme has been circling: eval localizes the fault, harness edit claims to fix it, contract makes the claim falsifiable, next rollout tests it.

Practical consequence for anyone adopting a self-evolving harness. The artifact to demand is not the harness and not the score — it is the edit log with its contracts, because that is the only thing that distinguishes a scaffold shaped by real failures from one that drifted into benchmark-specific tuning. The paper's own framing is exactly this: a "legible compensation layer" rather than "an opaque benchmark-tuned scaffold." Legibility is the deliverable.

Bounds. One recipe, one benchmark family, and the instrumentation is the authors' design — nobody has shown that a typed signal chosen by the same team that writes the edits catches edits it was not built to see. The method makes edits attributable; it does not make them correct, and the paper does not report how many contracts were later falsified, which is the number that would turn this from a good design into a measured result.
