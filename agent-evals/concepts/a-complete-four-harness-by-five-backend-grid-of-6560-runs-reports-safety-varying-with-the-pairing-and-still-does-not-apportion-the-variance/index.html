A complete four-harness by five-backend grid of 6,560 runs reports safety varying with the pairing, and still does not apportion the variance

Grounded in agents4d-lifecycle-runtime-safety-harness-grid; belongs to evals-that-localize-the-fix.

All 20 combinations of four agent harnesses (Hermes, OpenClaw, Claude Code, Codex) and five model backends (GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, MiniMax-M3, Qwen3.7-Plus) were run against 328 risk-injected cases: 6,560 runs, a complete factorial. The reported result is that observed safety "varies with both its harness-LLM pairing and how risk is introduced." No variance apportionment — not to harness, not to backend, not to their interaction.

Why this extends rather than confirms or pushes back. It is the fifth independent target-agent measurement of the model-versus-harness question, and like EduClaw-Bench it lands on interaction rather than on either side. That makes the tally four families pointing at the harness or at an interaction against one optimizer-role study pointing at the model. But the extension that matters is not the tally, it is what the grid demonstrates about why the number never arrives.

The inference this licenses. Five teams have now built apparatus capable of a two-way or three-way decomposition. Zero have reported one. Until tonight the strongest version of that observation was four teams whose grids were incidental to their claims. AgentS4D's grid is not incidental — the full cross product is one of the paper's contributions, it cost 6,560 runs, and it still yields one qualitative sentence. That moves the read from "these papers are not built for it" to something stronger: the decomposition is not what anyone is being rewarded for reporting. evals-that-localize-the-fix has been waiting for a benchmark to answer this incidentally. Five grids in, that wait should be declared over.

What it also adds that nobody else has. The risk carrier moved behaviour independently of the harness and the backend. That is a property of the environment the agent is pointed at, not of either factor in the argument, and it joins reasoning effort (reasoning-effort-moved-the-score-as-much-as-a-cross-family-harness-swap-so-the-model-versus-harness-frame-is-missing-a-third-factor) and the environment parameters in seven-unreported-environment-parameters-sit-underneath-every-agentic-search-result-so-the-environment-is-a-fourth-term-beside-model-harness-and-inference-settings on a growing list of terms the two-factor frame has no slot for.

Why single-study. One benchmark, one construction team, one task family, and safety signals rather than task accuracy as the dependent variable — a configuration could be safer because it is less capable, and nothing here separates those. The claim it supports well is the meta-claim about reporting practice, which does not depend on the dependent variable at all.

What would move this. A study whose stated purpose is the decomposition, reporting variance owned by model, by harness, by environment, and by their interactions as numbers on one task family. Nothing in this vault is that study, and the incentive analysis above says one is unlikely to arrive as a side effect of a benchmark paper.
