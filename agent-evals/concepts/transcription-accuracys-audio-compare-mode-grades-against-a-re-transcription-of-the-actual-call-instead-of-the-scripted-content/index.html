Transcription Accuracy's new audio-compare mode grades against a re-transcription of the actual call instead of the scripted content

Grounded in deepeval-unreleased-transcription-accuracy-audio-mode-and-audio-glitch-metric; belongs to the-judge-as-a-maintained-dependency and build-vs-adopt-eval-tooling.

TranscriptionAccuracyMetric originally graded a voice provider's transcript against spoken — text reconstructed from the test case's own scripted user turns. A new compare_against="audio" mode instead re-transcribes each turn's actual audio clip with a configurable speech-to-text model (defaulting to an OpenAI model prompted to transcribe verbatim, including fillers and non-speech noise, since a cleaning-oriented transcription would erase the very defects the check exists to catch) and grades the provider's transcript against that independent re-transcription.

Why this extends the theme. deepeval-ships-a-judge-based-transcription-accuracy-metric-with-no-validation-number-the-same-pattern-as-trajectory-g-eval logged, on 10-05, that the metric's comparison never touched the actual recording — it checked a provider's transcript against the test's own script, which cannot catch a case where the script itself misstates what was actually said. This mode closes exactly that gap by pinning the reference to the audio rather than to a self-reported script, the same addressable-dependency move design-for-verifiability has tracked in other judges that moved from a frozen reference to a live lookup — except here the lookup is pinned to a recording rather than left open to the web.

What it does not settle. The judge's verdict-generation step is unchanged by this mode; it still produces a verdict and a P(yes) with no published human-agreement number, for either comparison mode. Grading against better evidence does not retire the-judge-as-a-maintained-dependency's standing demand for a validated judge — it raises the floor on what the judge is shown, not on what the judge has been shown to get right.

Why single-study. One PR, beta, Python-only, no tagged release, no validation corpus for the audio-compare judge path.
