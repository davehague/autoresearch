A read-derived coverage score returned a perfect 1.000 in the condition where nothing was available because the agent filled the hole itself

Grounded in working-set-of-a-coding-agent-coherence-debt; belongs to evals-that-localize-the-fix.

"A measure built on read events therefore looks for a hole the agent has already filled. The failure is behavioral rather than statistical, so averaging does not remove it."

The numbers behind it. The score credits the agent's own writes: "Under deletion this returned 1.000, a perfect score, in the condition where nothing was available. Disqualifying agent-authored paths from the numerator corrects it and makes the same cell return 0.000. Any event-derived coverage measure needs this exclusion, and to our knowledge it is not standard." Reads rise as availability falls: reads of the withheld file go "from 5.5 to 10 to 13 as more facts are removed. The score holds up or climbs precisely as the quantity it estimates collapses." And the mechanism: an agent asked to act acts — a missing fact produces wrong work rather than absent work, and how often a model reports being blocked instead "ranges from every trial to none."

This is an instrument-validity finding aimed at the class of instrument most diagnostic work in this vault is built on. Trajectory-derived measures — what the agent read, how many tool calls it made, how much it searched — underpin separating-retrieval-gaps-from-utilization-gaps-turns-a-wrong-answer-into-a-repair-address, cumulative-retrieval-recall-tracks-answer-quality-better-than-search-effort-so-effort-metrics-mislead-as-progress-signals and every failure-attribution result here. The claim is not that those measures are noisy. It is that the quantity they estimate is destroyed by the behaviour they are watching: the agent responds to a shortfall by generating something, so the shortfall enters the tool stream as more activity, and a coverage score reads that as coverage.

The failure direction is the one this vault keeps finding survives. a-shipped-similarity-gate-caught-zero-of-fifty-six-meaning-breaking-edits-and-never-exceeded-0-700-balanced-accuracy-across-ninety-cells approved an instruction reversal at cosine 0.9608; this returns 1.000 where nothing was available. A broken instrument reading low eventually gets investigated. A broken instrument reading perfect gets cited.

One correction is concrete enough to apply tonight, and the authors say it is not standard practice. Exclude agent-authored paths from the numerator of any event-derived coverage measure. The same cell moved from 1.000 to 0.000. If you compute anything of the form "did the agent see what it needed", check whether the agent wrote what it needed first.

What to measure instead, per the authors: nothing derived from the event stream. On workloads whose coupling they authored, the required facts are known and the outcome is exact — "that instrument produced the zero-deviation table in Section 4.3, and it needs no graph, no window, no event stream." The proxy is kept only for historical corpora "where nothing better is recoverable", with its limits stated rather than defended. That is the trade this vault has been recording from the other direction: constructing the ground truth is what buys a measurement, and reading it off logs is what you do when you cannot.

The proxy is weak on its own terms too. Import edges recover 40% of the authored edges at 40% precision. Making the graph directed removes every spurious edge at no cost to recall, so the symmetric convention is "pure loss". Resolving the identifiers an edit uses reaches perfect recall at 4% precision, which makes it a candidate generator rather than a measure. And the score exists only where the agent edits, so under full withholding roughly 60% of trials contribute nothing and the survivors are selected by the behaviour under study.

Bounds. Single paper. The sharpest supporting contrast is underpowered and the authors say so: withholding four of eight facts gives AUC 0.59 with a 95% interval of [0.36, 0.81] over fourteen trials per arm, and they refuse the 1.00 from full withholding as an artifact of attrition. The 1.000-to-0.000 correction is one cell of one workload. Six of seven workloads are migration-shaped. The mechanism — compensate rather than abstain — is model-dependent by the paper's own report, ranging from every trial to none, so a harness built on a model that abstains reliably has a different problem.
