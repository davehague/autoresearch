A shipped similarity gate caught zero of fifty-six meaning-breaking edits and never exceeded 0.700 balanced accuracy across ninety cells

Grounded in similarity-gates-approve-reversals; belongs to design-for-verifiability.

"The production drift guard we audited caught 0 of 56 meaning-breaking mutations, and one approved item, 'withhold the study drug' -> 'administer the study drug', came in at cosine 0.9608. We observed five shipped operating points, and balanced accuracy across 90 configuration-threshold-task cells never exceeded 0.700 (median 0.525)."

This is the vault's largest standing gap answered on a second population, and this population contains no model at all. nine-hundred-thirty-one-human-annotated-trajectories-scoring-six-published-judges-is-the-first-population-level-grader-validation-here found on 2026-08-16 that nobody had checked the LLM judges already in circulation. This does the same job for the deterministic tier — deduplication filters, semantic caches, drift guards and answer grader gates, all shipped as framework features — and the result is structurally worse. A judge that disagrees with a human is miscalibrated and can be corrected at the threshold; a gate whose score is monotone in surface overlap rather than in meaning is wrong at every threshold, which is what a median balanced accuracy of 0.525 across 90 cells means.

It relocates the cheap tier of the grader ladder from "trustworthy but limited" to "trustworthy only if you checked." an-llm-judge-costs-a-hundred-labels-plus-weekly-maintenance-so-it-is-a-dependency-rather-than-an-artifact orders evaluators by price: cheap assertions and reference checks, medium structural validation, expensive judges. Embedding cosine reads as a cheap reference check and is the least reliable instrument measured anywhere in this vault. The ordering by price was never an ordering by fidelity and this is the first source to show the two coming apart at the cheap end — so cheapness bought the appearance of a check rather than a check, and the ladder needs a validation line at every tier rather than only at the judge.

The failure direction is the part that makes it invisible. fixing-grading-bugs-moved-one-model-from-42-to-95-percent-so-the-measuring-instrument-can-own-more-of-a-score-than-anything-it-measures noted that a broken grader producing a low score eventually gets investigated because someone doubts the number. This one produces a high score — 0.9608 on a semantic reversal — so the gate passes, the dashboard is green, and nothing prompts an investigation. Same trap as a-tool-that-writes-the-rubric-and-then-scores-against-it-removes-the-only-step-where-a-human-would-notice-the-rubric-was-wrong, reached from the opposite end: there the criterion was never read, here the criterion is readable and measures the wrong quantity.

The action it licenses is narrow and immediate. Not "stop using embeddings" — the strongest two of nine configurations separated reversal from paraphrase at matched overlap at AUROC 0.79–0.90, and the author's position is that "a valid instrument is buildable." It is: before trusting any similarity gate, run a matched-pair audit — a handful of single-word reversals against a handful of full rephrasings — and look at whether the scores order the way you assumed. That costs an afternoon, like the known-good-output check, and belongs in the same place in the construction skeleton.

Bounds. Single author, single paper, no replication. The 0-of-56 figure is one production drift guard. "Balanced accuracy" across 90 cells pools configurations and thresholds that are not interchangeable, so the median summarises a grid rather than estimating one instrument. Nothing here touches this vault's other cheap-tier checks — execution, span provenance, per-check assertions — whose graders do not depend on a learned representation.
