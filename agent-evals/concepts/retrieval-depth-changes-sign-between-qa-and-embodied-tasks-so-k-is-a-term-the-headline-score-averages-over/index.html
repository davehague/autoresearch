Retrieval depth changes sign between QA and embodied tasks so k is a term the headline score averages over

Grounded in harness-the-memory-substrate-evaluation; belongs to what-the-headline-number-averages-away.

Eleven memory substrates under one harness, three backbones, four benchmark suites. Increasing retrieval depth helps long-context factual QA and hurts sequential decision-making: "On ALFWorld the picture inverts… task success falls with k… M7 drops from 32.1% at k=1 to ∼25% at k=5."

The mechanism is measured rather than asserted, which is what makes it portable. Attention mass at increasing k moves toward the retrieved block and away from the prompt's working region — "LoCoMo: →0.10 [context] vs. →0.66 [retrieved]; ALFWorld: →0.17 [context] vs. →0.44 [retrieved]." The difference between the two regimes is where the answer lives. On QA "answers live in retrieved text"; on ALFWorld "the load-bearing region is the observation and admissible-action list." Retrieval competes with the observation for attention, and on an action-taking task the observation is the thing you cannot afford to dilute.

k is normally configuration, not a term under test, and that is the finding. This vault has spent considerable effort arguing that the harness, the environment, the serving stack and the inference settings are unreported variables underneath published scores. Retrieval depth is another one, and it is worse behaved than most: it does not merely shift a score, it reverses direction between task families. A team that tunes k on a QA suite and ships it into an agent loop has optimized against the wrong sign, and no aggregate over the two families would reveal it — the mean of a rising curve and a falling one is flat.

The experiment is a config sweep and belongs in the same afternoon as the observation-space sweep. Same system, k at 1 and 5, one retrieval-flavoured task and one action-taking task, four cells. It sits directly beside changing-only-the-observation-and-action-space-inside-one-harness-moved-success-34-2-points: both hold everything fixed and vary one channel that the eval treats as plumbing, and both find the channel worth more than the things that get reported.

It also fits the theme's harder claim. what-the-headline-number-averages-away has been arguing that mixing components which are not substitutes produces a number nobody can act on. Here the components are not even the same sign, which is the strongest form of that argument to arrive so far — an average across regimes is not merely lossy, it is pointing at zero.

Bounds. Single study, code "available upon acceptance" so nothing is reproducible today. The ALFWorld figure is one substrate (M7 Distilled Strategies) on one backbone (Qwen3-32B-AWQ) and the k=5 value is read off a curve as "∼25%" rather than tabulated. The attention-mass numbers are single values from a figure with no variance reported, and attention mass is a suggestive proxy for causal influence rather than a demonstration of it. Only two task families are contrasted, so "QA versus embodied" may be standing in for some other property — task length, action space size, or how much of the prompt is load-bearing. Three backbones, none from the GPT family.
