The harness moved the nanoGPT record less than experiment noise while changing how the agent worked sixfold

Grounded in prime-agent-self-improving-rlm-harness; belongs to what-the-headline-number-averages-away.

Same paper, different benchmark, opposite result. On the nanoGPT speedrun — three models, each run against an alternative harness, 18 runs — "we find that the choice of harness has little effect on final records compared to the noise of the experiment."

The process difference is enormous and invisible in the record. Under Prime Agent the models ran experiments outside the benchmark's training script: simulating a candidate optimizer on synthetic gradients, numerically optimizing update-rule coefficients before committing to a training run. "The effect is largest for DeepSeek V4 Pro, which created roughly six times more such experiments per training run under Prime Agent than under Claude Code." Kimi K3 "defined a probe function through which it ran roughly ninety screening experiments and all 19 of its validated records, whereas the same model on its own CLI performed every operation through direct file edits and built no such machinery."

This is the theme's second-axis argument arriving from the null side. Everywhere else here, a headline number hides a difference that would have shown up on a second axis — final-answer-correctness-spans-eleven-points-across-nine-harnesses-while-planning-tool-use-and-efficiency-vary-much-more-so-the-headline-axis-is-where-the-harness-effect-hides, harness-configurations-that-all-pass-every-test-differ-12-8-times-in-tokens-so-at-ceiling-the-only-remaining-question-is-the-bill. Here the headline is not hiding a smaller effect; it is reporting zero while the working method changed completely. An eight-seed-verified training record is a hard outcome metric with a noise floor, and it absorbed a sixfold change in how the agent got there.

The operational reading is a warning about harness selection. A team that chooses a harness on a saturated or noise-limited benchmark will find no difference and conclude there is none. The same team on ARC-AGI-3 would find a difference spanning most of the scale. Whether a harness is worth points is a property of the benchmark's headroom, not of the harness — which makes "we A/B'd two harnesses on our eval and saw nothing" an uninformative result unless the eval's noise floor is published beside it, per three-run-verdict-agreement-of-74-7-percent-published-beside-a-harness-comparison-is-the-noise-floor-this-vault-has-been-asking-for.

Bounds. "Little effect ... compared to the noise" is a qualitative statement; no variance figure is given, no per-model records are tabulated, and the experiment counts are "hand-classified from complete traces" with denominators "audited where available and otherwise estimated from launch commands." Three models, 2–3 seeds per harness.
